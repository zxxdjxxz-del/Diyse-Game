# P01 — CONVOY / OPENING AMBUSH

### [AUTHORED OPENING — WESTWAYS CONVOY]

The convoy is moving along the Westways. Civilians travel under escort; the recovery wagon is more heavily guarded. The recovered Card remains inside its damaged recovery casing and is inert.

The convoy officer and Cyanis may both be present for the opening exchange.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Dovaren. East side. Keep the travelers clear of the recovery wagon.

**CYANIS:** Already moved them down the shoulder.

A beat.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** I hadn't finished.

**CYANIS:** Were you going to say something different?

The officer looks at him.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** No.

**CYANIS:** Good.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Recovery team says the casing shifted again.

**CYANIS:** Seal holding?

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Holding.

**CYANIS:** Then it can stay mysterious until Caelora.

The conversation is about to end.

Cyanis notices the road ahead.

**CYANIS:** Sir.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** What?

**CYANIS:** No return traffic.

A beat.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Could be nothing.

**CYANIS:** Could be. Hold the column anyway.

The officer looks toward the empty bend, then makes the call.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Column, halt!

The ambush begins before the convoy can fully settle.

Black Host fire and war-sorcery break the road formation and separate Cyanis onto the east side with civilians and wounded escorts.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Dovaren! East side! Get them down!

**CYANIS:** Stone line! Move!

The Card does not react.

### [AUTHORED COMBAT — CYANIS SOLO]

**Combat 1:** Black Host Raider + Black Host Crossbowman + Ruin Shieldbearer.

The fight is story-authorized tutorial pressure, not an ordinary random encounter.

After the first formation breaks, the second authorized pressure arrives.

**Combat 2:** Beast Handler + Convoy Rift Hound.

No concealed Ruin Vanguard and no Riftmaw appear here.

### [HANDOFF]

The convoy is badly damaged but not destroyed. Cyanis is still separated on the east side. The Card remains cased and inert.

Player control moves into the Wreck Field.

# P02 — WRECK FIELD

### [GAMEPLAY — SURVIVOR WORK]

Ordinary wreck-field traversal shows Cyanis only.

A story stop triggers near wounded convoy personnel.

**WOUNDED ESCORT:** Dovaren.

**CYANIS:** Where?

**WOUNDED ESCORT:** Two by the axle. One under the canvas.

**CYANIS:** Can you stand?

**WOUNDED ESCORT:** Enough.

**CYANIS:** Get the two by the axle behind the stone line. Don't lift anyone you can't.

**WOUNDED ESCORT:** The one under the canvas?

**CYANIS:** I'll get them.

Player control resumes for the short rescue route.

At a damaged wagon, a civilian is trying to reach a trapped relative.

**CIVILIAN:** My brother's under there.

**CYANIS:** Don't get under the frame.

**CIVILIAN:** He's right there.

**CYANIS:** I know. Help me pull him clear.

The rescue is resolved with one simple authored interaction/state change. The dialogue script does not prescribe a rope puzzle, lifting animation chain, or detailed wagon choreography.

Afterward:

**CIVILIAN:** He's breathing.

**CYANIS:** Keep him on his side. If that changes, shout.

**CIVILIAN:** You're leaving?

**CYANIS:** Not far.

### [STONE-LINE ROUTE]

The shorter center route is visibly unstable. Cyanis redirects survivors along the safer lower stone line.

**WOUNDED ESCORT:** Middle road's faster.

**CYANIS:** Not if that wagon comes down.

**WOUNDED ESCORT:** Stone line, then.

**CYANIS:** Stone line.

### [AUTHORED HOUND PRESSURE]

A lone Convoy Rift Hound threatens the survivor route under current encounter authority.

**CYANIS:** Behind the wagon. Now.

**AUTHORED COMBAT — CYANIS SOLO / CONVOY RIFT HOUND.**

No additional creature form or hidden story meaning is added.

### [NORTH WITHDRAWAL]

After the encounter, the convoy officer reaches the east-side wreck line.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** North side's pulling back.

**CYANIS:** I see it.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Looks like they're breaking.

**CYANIS:** Maybe.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Look at the cut.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** What about it?

**CYANIS:** Everything else is choked. That one's clear.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** You think it's bait.

**CYANIS:** I think they want us looking north.

The officer does not treat the guess as proof.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Relay. Bring me the field when you get there.

**CYANIS:** On my way.

Player control resumes toward the Evacuation Relay.

# P03 — EVACUATION RELAY DECISION

### [STORY TRIGGER — RELAY]

Cyanis reaches the damaged but usable relay. Survivors are being consolidated toward it.

The officer arrives for the field report.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Report.

**CYANIS:** Several can't move without help. We don't have enough clean fighters to chase north and hold this place.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Recovery wagon?

**CYANIS:** Still ours. East approach is open. Center road isn't safe for casualties.

A beat.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** If that north group reaches the ridge intact, they can reform and hit us again.

**CYANIS:** They can.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Then take whoever can still move fast and drive them north before they settle.

**CYANIS:** No, sir.

A beat.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Dovaren.

**CYANIS:** We chase now, we split damaged defenders and leave the wounded, the wreck, and the recovery wagon behind us.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** If they're actually running, there isn't a second fight.

**CYANIS:** Then holding ten minutes costs us ten minutes.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** If that cut is bait, chasing costs us the field.

The officer considers it.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Your alternative.

**CYANIS:** Pull the casualties into the relay. Reform escorts around the wreck and recovery wagon. Watch north. Nobody goes into that cut until we can hold without them.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** And if they come back?

**CYANIS:** They come back to a line.

A beat.

The officer accepts the field argument and issues the order.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Relay first. Reform on the wagon. Keep eyes north.

**CYANIS:** Yes, sir.

Cyanis starts to go.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Dovaren.

**CYANIS:** Sir?

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Next time, give me the field before you give me "no."

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Fair.

The Card remains inert.

# P04 — FIELD TRIAGE CAMP / ILYRA / FIRST INCOMPLETE RESPONSE

### [TRIAGE CAMP]

The relay-side defensive area is now a working triage camp. Relevant wounded, civilians, escorts, Cyanis, Ilyra, and the officer may be visible when the authored scene is active.

Ilyra is already working when Cyanis enters.

Her first line is work.

**ILYRA:** Walking wounded by the blue cloth. If you can stand, help somebody who can't. Nobody moves her yet.

Cyanis needs the same lane for incoming survivor movement.

**CYANIS:** I need this lane clear.

**ILYRA:** Move the crates. She stays.

**CYANIS:** Why?

**ILYRA:** Her breathing gets worse when we move her. Give me a stable surface and a few minutes.

Cyanis checks the rest of the lane.

**CYANIS:** All right. Crates move.

They work separately for a short stretch.

Later:

**CYANIS:** East side's thin. How much space do you need behind the wagon?

**ILYRA:** Eight paces.

**CYANIS:** Seven keeps the west corner covered.

**ILYRA:** Seven.

A beat.

Ilyra notices him more closely.

**ILYRA:** Your arm's shaking.

**CYANIS:** Still works.

**ILYRA:** I didn't ask whether it works.

**CYANIS:** I'm upright.

**ILYRA:** For now.

Renewed Black Host pressure cuts the exchange off.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** East ridge!

**CYANIS:** Get everyone behind the relay wall.

**ILYRA:** Two can't move yet.

**CYANIS:** That wall won't wait for them.

**ILYRA:** Neither will their lungs.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** What do you need?

**ILYRA:** Half a minute. And something between them and that ridge.

Before Cyanis can answer, light wakes inside the damaged recovery casing.

Fragmented green-and-gold geometry reaches through weak points in the treatment position and briefly stabilizes failing cover and movement space.

Nobody intentionally activates it.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Dovaren—?

**CYANIS:** Wasn't me.

Ilyra sees Cyanis's reaction to the flare.

**ILYRA:** Your pulse changed when that started.

**CYANIS:** Can you keep me standing?

**ILYRA:** For now.

**CYANIS:** Then move them.

Ilyra immediately uses the opening to finish moving the threatened patients.

Once the immediate movement crisis is resolved, the green-and-gold geometry **fully collapses**.

The recovery casing and Card go inert again.

There is no lingering protection into P05.

Ilyra checks Cyanis once more.

**ILYRA:** Still with me?

**CYANIS:** Yes.

**ILYRA:** Good. Don't improve on that answer.

Hostile pressure remains outside the treatment line.

**CYANIS:** I'm holding the front.

**ILYRA:** Not alone.

**CYANIS:** You have patients.

**ILYRA:** And they need this ground to stay ours.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Your call.

**ILYRA:** It is.

Ilyra joins the defense by her own decision.

**ACTIVE COMBAT PAIR NOW: CYANIS + ILYRA.**

### [PERIMETER PRESSURE]

The first Card flare is over. The Card and casing are dark. No special protection is active.

After a short normal defensive interval, an armored Black Host Ruin Vanguard approaches the perimeter alone.

Writer-facing only: this is Seyrik Rell. Neither Cyanis, Ilyra, nor the player-facing script identifies him.

The figure does not stop to deliver a villain speech.

**CYANIS:** Host.

**ILYRA:** I see him.

**CYANIS:** Camp stays behind us.

**ILYRA:** Agreed.

**AUTHORED ENCOUNTER — CYANIS + ILYRA VS. RUIN VANGUARD PURSUER.**

The Card remains inert throughout.

At the encounter-authorized threshold, the Pursuer disengages and withdraws alive.

### [POST-ENCOUNTER]

**CYANIS:** He's leaving.

**ILYRA:** Let him.

**CYANIS:** Wasn't planning to chase.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** Good.

The scene does not declare what the withdrawal means.

Player control returns to the defended triage area for a real breathing interval before P06.

Cyanis and Ilyra remain active together. The Card stays inert. Treatment and perimeter work continue without another mandatory dialogue scene.

# P06 — RIFTMAW + WAR-SORCERER / FINAL BROKEN CONVOY CONFRONTATION

### [FINAL PUSH]

The War-Sorcerer advances on the treatment line with **Riftmaw**, visibly a Black Host war-beast asset in restraint/harness context.

This is one combined final boss encounter.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** East cut! Riftmaw!

Ilyra sees the second green-and-gold flare begin inside the damaged recovery casing.

**ILYRA:** Cyanis.

**CYANIS:** I know.

**ILYRA:** Same pattern.

The fragmented protection forms again around the defended opening.

**CYANIS:** Use it while it's here.

**ILYRA:** Planning to.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Sorcerer's behind it!

**CYANIS:** Keep everybody behind stone. Nobody follows us out.

**ILYRA:** Keep the lane behind me clear.

**CYANIS:** You have it.

**BOSS COMBAT BEGINS — CYANIS + ILYRA VS. RIFTMAW + CONVOY WAR-SORCERER.**

Encounter authority owns the exact mechanics. The second incomplete flare provides its currently authorized opening protection for Rounds 1–3. It is not selectable and is not a Prime activation.

Riftmaw has one HP bar and does not transform. No support wave or injured Iron Cohort Soldier is added.

### [SCRIPTED BATTLE EVENT — CASING BREAK]

After the second flare is already active, a heavy impact reaches the recovery position behind Cyanis and Ilyra.

The already-damaged recovery casing is knocked loose and breaks open on the stone.

The **Card skids free intact**, still lit green and gold.

The fight does not pause for dialogue or explanation.

From this moment onward the casing is finished. The Card itself remains the physical artifact.

### [POST-BOSS]

Riftmaw and the War-Sorcerer are defeated in the same encounter.

Remaining organized Black Host pressure breaks contact.

The second green-and-gold response collapses completely. The Card goes dark.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** They're pulling back.

**CYANIS:** Let them.

Ilyra immediately checks Cyanis rather than celebrating.

**ILYRA:** Look at me.

**CYANIS:** I am.

**ILYRA:** Good. Stay there a second.

**CYANIS:** Camp?

Ilyra checks the treatment line.

**ILYRA:** Still ours.

A few feet away, the intact Card lies beside the broken casing.

Once Ilyra is satisfied Cyanis is stable enough to move, Cyanis picks up the Card itself.

It stays dark in his hand.

# P07 — AFTERMATH / SURVIVOR RECOVERY / OVERNIGHT CAMP

### [FIELD STATUS]

The crisis closes through work, not celebration.

The casing is gone. Cyanis now carries the Card itself.

The convoy officer checks the field and medical state.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Dovaren. Amarin. Field.

**CYANIS:** East cut's clear. Nobody chased.

**ILYRA:** Walking wounded can move. Two patients still can't.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Missing?

A wounded escort answers from the recovery group.

**WOUNDED ESCORT:** Don't know. Rear roster went with a wagon.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Then we sweep the wreck line.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Brackenwall can send a fresh detail at first light.

**CYANIS:** If somebody's pinned, first light is late.

**ILYRA:** If somebody's bleeding, it's later.

The officer considers the remaining light and personnel.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** One sweep. No chase beyond the wreck markers.

**CYANIS:** Understood.

**ILYRA:** Agreed.

### [THE CARD]

The officer looks at the Card in Cyanis's possession.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** That stays with you until Brackenwall.

**CYANIS:** Happily.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Nobody experiments with it on the road.

**ILYRA:** No argument.

Nobody proposes another separation test here.

### [ILYRA STAYS]

The officer turns back to the evacuation problem.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Your medical group moves with the first wounded column.

**ILYRA:** They should.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** And you?

**ILYRA:** I'm staying tonight.

The officer waits for the reason.

**ILYRA:** That thing reacted twice. His condition changed both times, and I'm the one who's been treating it.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** Separating them now would be stupid.

**CYANIS:** You don't have to stay because I got dragged into this.

**ILYRA:** I'm not.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** I'm staying because I'm the person who's seen what it does to you. And we still have a sweep.

Cyanis accepts that.

**CYANIS:** All right.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Finish the sweep. Brackenwall afterward.

**ILYRA:** Understood.

### [SURVIVOR SWEEP / CONVOY DEPARTURE]

The bounded final survivor sweep occurs inside the wreck/search line. It does not become a pursuit sequence.

Most surviving convoy personnel, wounded, civilians, and usable wagons continue onward once the immediate recovery work is stable.

Cyanis and Ilyra remain overnight with the Card and minimal practical guard/support.

The Card remains inert.

This is still **Chapter 0**.

# OPTIONAL C01 — SIX MINUTES

### [OVERNIGHT CAMP]

The camp is substantially quieter now.

Because this is camp, Cyanis and Ilyra may both be visibly present as field models. Portraits and the dialogue box carry the scene.

The Card is nearby under conservative watch and remains inert.

Cyanis has made the mistake of trying **bitterleaf** as though it were something worth drinking.

Ilyra notices after the damage is done.

**ILYRA:** What are you drinking?

**CYANIS:** I was hoping you knew.

Ilyra looks at the cup.

**ILYRA:** Bitterleaf.

**CYANIS:** That explains the name.

**ILYRA:** You drank it?

**CYANIS:** One sip.

**ILYRA:** Why?

**CYANIS:** It was hot and in a cup.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** Those aren't qualifications.

**CYANIS:** I know that now.

Ilyra takes the cup out of consideration. No elaborate prop comedy is required.

A little quiet follows.

**ILYRA:** Have you eaten?

**CYANIS:** Have you?

She looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Right.

A simple camp ration is enough for the scene. They eat because both of them have gone too long without doing so.

For a while, neither speaks.

Then:

**ILYRA:** I forgot how hungry I was.

**CYANIS:** Same.

More quiet.

**CYANIS:** This is better than the bitterleaf.

**ILYRA:** An extraordinary achievement.

**CYANIS:** I thought so.

They keep eating.

After a while Ilyra starts to shift back toward work.

**CYANIS:** No.

**ILYRA:** No what?

**CYANIS:** Whatever useful thing you were about to do.

**ILYRA:** There are several.

**CYANIS:** Of course there are.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** You were about to get up too.

**CYANIS:** I was adjusting.

**ILYRA:** Toward standing.

**CYANIS:** Very judgmental.

**ILYRA:** Accurate.

Neither gets up.

A little more silence passes.

**CYANIS:** Five minutes.

**ILYRA:** For what?

**CYANIS:** Nothing.

**ILYRA:** That's not a task.

**CYANIS:** Exactly.

She thinks about it.

**ILYRA:** Six.

**CYANIS:** Why six?

**ILYRA:** I don't know.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Six minutes, then.

**ILYRA:** Six.

They sit there and do nothing useful.

The scene ends before either of them turns that into a conversation about what it means.

C01 does not advance the chapter.
