### [OVERNIGHT CAMP — OPTIONAL CHARACTER-LIFE TRIGGER]

Late evening after P07.

Most of the surviving convoy has already moved toward Brackenwall. Cyanis and Ilyra remain at the quieter field camp with the intact Card and minimal practical support.

Because this is camp, both Cyanis and Ilyra may be visibly present as field models. Portraits and the dialogue box carry most of the scene.

The Card is dark and inert.

No plot reveal occurs here.

---

### [BITTERLEAF]

Cyanis has a cup in hand.

He takes a drink.

Stops.

**ILYRA:** Bad?

**CYANIS:** Depends what it was supposed to be.

Ilyra looks at the cup.

**ILYRA:** Where did you get that?

**CYANIS:** Pot by the fire.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** That's bitterleaf.

**CYANIS:** I gathered that much.

**ILYRA:** It's for compresses.

Cyanis looks at the cup again.

**CYANIS:** Ah.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** You drank it.

**CYANIS:** Some of it.

**ILYRA:** Why?

**CYANIS:** Hot. Cup.

Ilyra stares at him.

**CYANIS:** In my defense, I'm tired.

**ILYRA:** That's not a defense.

**CYANIS:** It's all I have.

She takes the cup away from him.

No elaborate prop routine is needed.

---

### [FOOD]

A little silence follows.

**ILYRA:** Have you eaten?

**CYANIS:** Have you?

Another silence.

**ILYRA:** That's irritating.

**CYANIS:** I thought so.

They end up with ordinary camp food.

For a while, neither speaks.

They are both hungrier than either realized.

Eventually:

**ILYRA:** I forgot I was hungry.

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** This is much better than the compress water.

**ILYRA:** An achievement.

**CYANIS:** A low one.

**ILYRA:** Still counts.

They keep eating.

The silence is not awkward, but it is still the silence of two people who met today.

---

### [NEITHER OF THEM STOPS]

When the food is nearly gone, Ilyra shifts as if she's about to get back up.

**CYANIS:** No.

**ILYRA:** No what?

**CYANIS:** Whatever you're about to go do.

**ILYRA:** I have patients to check.

**CYANIS:** They're settled.

**ILYRA:** I know.

**CYANIS:** Then sit down for a minute.

Ilyra looks at him.

**ILYRA:** You were about to stand up too.

Cyanis pauses.

**CYANIS:** Different.

**ILYRA:** How?

He thinks about it.

**CYANIS:** I hadn't decided what I was doing yet.

**ILYRA:** You were looking at the perimeter.

**CYANIS:** Could've been admiring it.

**ILYRA:** At night.

**CYANIS:** Very scenic.

Ilyra almost smiles.

Neither gets up.

---

### [SIX MINUTES]

A little time passes.

**CYANIS:** Five minutes.

**ILYRA:** For what?

**CYANIS:** Nothing useful.

Ilyra looks at him.

**ILYRA:** Six.

**CYANIS:** Why six?

**ILYRA:** Five sounds planned.

Cyanis considers that.

**CYANIS:** Six minutes of aggressively unplanned nothing.

**ILYRA:** Don't make it sound like work.

**CYANIS:** Right.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Six minutes.

**ILYRA:** Six.

They sit.

For once, neither of them tries to make themselves useful.

No confession follows. No relationship summary. No discussion of destiny, the Card, or what the day meant.

The scene ends in the quiet.

---

## C01 function

- gives Cyanis and Ilyra their first ordinary post-crisis Character-Life scene;
- keeps their ease early and tentative rather than instantly intimate;
- lets the bitterleaf mistake provide mundane comedy without a prop-performance sequence;
- shows that both neglect food/rest while taking care of other people;
- lets them notice the pattern in each other without turning the scene into therapy;
- ends with a small, arbitrary shared rule — six minutes of doing nothing useful;
- Card remains inert;
- C01 does not advance Chapter 0.
