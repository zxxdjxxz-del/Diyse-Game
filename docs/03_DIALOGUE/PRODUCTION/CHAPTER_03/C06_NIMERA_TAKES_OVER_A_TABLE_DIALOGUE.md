### [OPTIONAL CHARACTER-LIFE TRIGGER — CRESTHAVEN RECORDS / MAP ROOM]

Available during the Chapter-3 cleanup window after Beat 15.

The records room is usable but newly reopened.

One central table was cleared for shared work.

Nimera has occupied most of it.

Not messily.

Aggressively, meticulously, defensibly.

Books, copied notes, labeled scraps, ink, and archive tools sit in distinct working zones.

Nimera is writing.

Cyanis and Torren enter already halfway through an argument whose origin no longer matters.

**CYANIS:** I bet you use that cape to sneak up on the goats you fuck.

**TORREN:** You look like a walking dick in armor.

Nimera's pen stops.

She looks up.

Then laughs hard enough to put the pen down.

**NIMERA:** Oh, good. Shit, that's a relief.

**CYANIS:** Good?

**NIMERA:** I was worried you'd both become respectable indoors.

**TORREN:** No danger.

**CYANIS:** He started it.

**TORREN:** Didn't.

**NIMERA:** I don't care. Continue. I want enough evidence to assign blame properly.

**CYANIS:** Absolutely not.

**NIMERA:** Coward.

Torren reaches the table with a rolled route map.

He stops.

**TORREN:** Where am I putting this?

**NIMERA:** Anywhere free.

Torren looks across the table.

**TORREN:** There isn't anywhere free.

Nimera looks up, offended.

**NIMERA:** That's not true.

Cyanis points to a narrow clear patch.

**CYANIS:** There.

**NIMERA:** Fuck no. That's allocated.

**TORREN:** To what?

**NIMERA:** Cross-reference stack.

**TORREN:** Where is it?

**NIMERA:** Not there yet.

Cyanis looks at Torren.

**CYANIS:** Future paper.

**NIMERA:** Yes. Time proceeds forward. Occasionally I prepare for it.

Torren starts lowering the map onto another clear patch.

**NIMERA:** Not there either. Wet ink.

Torren checks.

She's right.

**TORREN:** Damn it.

**CYANIS:** Don't encourage her.

**NIMERA:** He's recognizing objective reality.

Torren looks at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** He's right about the armor, though.

**CYANIS:** Keep talking, you mud-caked old bastard wearing half a hedge.

**TORREN:** Better than looking like a silver-plated cock with command authority.

Nimera laughs again.

### [THE TABLE, APPARENTLY]

Cyanis starts inspecting the table like an enemy position.

**CYANIS:** Notes. Books. Ink. Your cup has territory.

**NIMERA:** Liquid touching paper is how civilization shits itself. That's basic order.

**TORREN:** Strong argument.

**CYANIS:** Whose side are you on?

**TORREN:** Table's.

Nimera points at him.

**NIMERA:** See? Field judgment. Crude, occasionally useful.

Cyanis points to another clear patch.

**CYANIS:** Fine. There.

**NIMERA:** Lamp.

**CYANIS:** There is no lamp.

**NIMERA:** There will be when it gets dark.

Cyanis stares at her.

**CYANIS:** Future lamp.

**NIMERA:** Correct.

**TORREN:** Future lamp outranks future paper?

**NIMERA:** Obviously. Fixed requirement versus expected work product.

**CYANIS:** I regret asking anything.

### [COMMUNAL]

Cyanis folds his arms.

**CYANIS:** This is the communal table.

**NIMERA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** You have taken over the communal table.

**NIMERA:** I've taken over most of the usable surface while using it for communal work.

**TORREN:** So mostly took it over.

Nimera turns to him.

**NIMERA:** Mostly is not the same word.

**TORREN:** Knew you'd like that.

Cyanis points to a thin pile beneath one book.

**CYANIS:** What's that?

**NIMERA:** Notes I haven't decided are useful.

**CYANIS:** Rubbish.

**NIMERA:** Unclassified.

**CYANIS:** Rubbish with ambition.

Nimera looks at the pile.

**NIMERA:** Shit. That's better.

**CYANIS:** Thank you.

**NIMERA:** Stay humble.

### [THE PRACTICAL RESULT]

Torren finally unrolls the map against his own chest.

**CYANIS:** You're using yourself as the table now.

**TORREN:** Works.

**NIMERA:** Adaptation. Problem solved.

Cyanis reaches for a chair.

Two books occupy it.

He looks at Nimera.

**NIMERA:** In use.

He looks at the next chair.

Her satchel is on it.

**CYANIS:** How much of this room are you using?

Nimera genuinely considers the question.

**NIMERA:** Currently, or in the planning sense?

Torren immediately heads for the door.

**TORREN:** Don't ask.

**CYANIS:** Coward.

Nimera calls after him.

**NIMERA:** Your map can go on the window ledge if you put something clean under it.

Torren stops.

**TORREN:** Knew there was space.

Nimera freezes.

Cyanis smiles.

**CYANIS:** He got you.

**NIMERA:** Window ledge is auxiliary surface. Different jurisdiction.

Cyanis starts walking out.

**CYANIS:** Absolutely not.

From the doorway:

**TORREN:** Future explanation.

Nimera throws a blank scrap at him.

It lands several feet short.

Torren leaves.

Cyanis laughs and follows.

Nimera retrieves the paper.

**NIMERA:** Cowardly bastards.

She returns to work.
