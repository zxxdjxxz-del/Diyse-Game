### [STORY TRIGGER — CAELORA GATE]

**Player control pauses.**

The checkpoint is routine.

Maevra is recognized as a senior Yahtrean commander returning on Crown business. The group clears normal entry procedure for the royal district.

No one attempts to confiscate Cyanis's Card. No one treats the party as detainees or suspects.

Once they are through:

**MAEVRA:** Palace first.

Cyanis looks past her toward the city.

**CYANIS:** I can see three places selling food from here.

**MAEVRA:** Then you can admire them on the way uphill.

**TORREN:** Palace is uphill.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** I hadn't asked you.

**TORREN:** Still true.

**ILYRA:** Devastating. Beaten by fucking topography.

**CYANIS:** You're enjoying this.

**ILYRA:** More than I expected.

**MAEVRA:** Keep moving.

### [GAMEPLAY — FIRST CAELORA STRETCH]

**Player control resumes.**

Let the player actually see modern Caelora before the Wayfinder comparison lands.

Partway through the controlled first stretch, Torren slows and looks along the inside face of the wall.

**TORREN:** Wayfinder had more city than this.

Maevra looks over.

**MAEVRA:** How much more?

**TORREN:** Enough that this wall would've been inside it. Not near the edge. Road alignment's changed, and the Wayfinder is broken through the middle of the old capital cluster, so I can't lay one over the other cleanly. But larger isn't a guess.

Cyanis looks back along the wall.

**CYANIS:** So the modern wall sits inside the old footprint.

**TORREN:** Yeah. How far inside, I can't defend from the copy.

**MAEVRA:** Say that upstairs.

**TORREN:** Was planning to.

Ilyra glances at him.

**ILYRA:** You look offended she thought you might overstate it.

**TORREN:** Bad measurements piss me off.

**CYANIS:** There he is.

A short silence while the city continues around them.

**CYANIS:** Still fucking hungry.

**TORREN:** Palace is still uphill.

**ILYRA:** I think that's becoming his answer to you.

**CYANIS:** I fucking regret this group.

**MAEVRA:** No, you don't.

**CYANIS:** Not yet.
