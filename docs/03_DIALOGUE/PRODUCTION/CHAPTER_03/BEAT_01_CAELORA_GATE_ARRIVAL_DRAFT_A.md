### [GAMEPLAY — CAELORA APPROACH]

**Player control is active.**

Caelora is visible ahead before the party reaches the gate.

Let the capital get a clean visual read: functioning walls and gate traffic, ordinary Crown movement, civilian and trade traffic, and a skyline materially larger than the settlements the party has recently crossed.

Do not front-load dialogue over the reveal.

During the final road approach, ordinary traversal remains Cyanis-only on the field.

No combat is required.

---

### [STORY TRIGGER — CAELORA GATE]

**Player control pauses.**

Maevra, Ilyra, and Torren may appear with Cyanis for the routine checkpoint scene.

The checkpoint is deliberately uneventful.

Maevra is recognized as a senior Yahtrean commander returning on Crown business. The group is entered through normal gate procedure and cleared for the royal district.

No one attempts to confiscate Cyanis's Card. No one treats the party as detainees, suspects, or a containment problem.

Do not create a separate gate-officer subplot merely to give the checkpoint dialogue.

Once entry is cleared:

**MAEVRA:** Palace first.

**CYANIS:** Before food?

**ILYRA:** You ate.

**CYANIS:** Hours ago.

**ILYRA:** I know.

**TORREN:** He'll live.

**CYANIS:** Good. Medical consensus.

**ILYRA:** That's not what that was.

**MAEVRA:** Keep moving.

### [GAMEPLAY — FIRST CAELORA STRETCH]

**Player control resumes.**

Caelora is a town/capital space, so relevant party members may be visibly present according to the current town presentation rule. Keep staging simple.

Maevra is actively taking the group toward the palace, so one short route-guidance exchange is legal while the player moves through the controlled first stretch.

Give the player time to see the modern city before the mural comparison lands.

**TORREN:** Mural had more city.

**MAEVRA:** How much more?

**TORREN:** Enough that this wall would've been well inside it.

**ILYRA:** You're sure?

**TORREN:** About that. Not the exact boundary.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** Say it exactly like that upstairs.

**TORREN:** Was going to.

A short silence while the city continues around them.

**CYANIS:** Still hungry.

**MAEVRA:** I heard you the first time.
