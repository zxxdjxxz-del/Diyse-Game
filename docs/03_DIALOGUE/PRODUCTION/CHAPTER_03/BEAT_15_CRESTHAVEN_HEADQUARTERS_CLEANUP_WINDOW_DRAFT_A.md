### [TRAVEL CUT — OUTSIDE CRESTHAVEN]

The skipped road journey ends outside Cresthaven.

The fort sits inside the forest as a visibly abandoned Crown outpost being forced back into use rather than restored to grandeur.

Work is already underway.

Crew members are:
- clearing debris from the gate and yard;
- checking roof and wall damage;
- carrying in food, water, bedding, lamps, and basic medical supplies;
- opening rooms that can be made safe quickly;
- moving tables / boards into a records and map room;
- marking doors and lower spaces that remain unsafe or sealed.

Older construction shows beneath and through the Crown fort: oversized fitted stone in the foundations, sealed lower arches, dark metal worked into masonry that does not match the later fort, and sections whose proportions clearly belong to something older.

Mirena and Maevra are already there with the crew.

Cyanis takes in the amount of work already happening.

**CYANIS:** You beat us here.

**MIRENA:** Apparently.

Torren looks up toward the roofline.

**TORREN:** Been here a while.

**MIRENA:** Long enough to find three leaks and a floor nobody is allowed to stand on.

From farther inside the yard, Maevra looks over from whatever practical work she is helping coordinate.

**MAEVRA:** Four leaks.

Mirena looks toward her.

**MIRENA:** Excellent. It's improving already.

Maevra goes back to the work instead of joining the conversation.

### [FIRST LOOK INSIDE]

Mirena leads them through the main usable section while the crew continues working around them.

This is a genuinely guided handoff, so brief movement dialogue is allowed here.

They pass:
- a room being cleared for sleeping;
- supplies being stacked in a dry store;
- a small treatment room receiving clean cloth, water, a cot, and basic medical stock;
- a larger room where an old table and wall space are being cleared for maps, records, and investigation material.

Mirena does not narrate each function.

Nimera slows near a lower arch partly exposed behind removed debris.

The stone beneath the Crown wall is older, larger, and fitted differently.

**NIMERA:** That's not Crown work.

**TORREN:** No.

Cyanis looks at the sealed lower opening.

**CYANIS:** Another old door.

**ILYRA:** Don't.

**CYANIS:** I didn't do anything.

**ILYRA:** That's why I'm getting ahead of it.

Mirena glances at the sealed arch.

**MIRENA:** Anything still sealed stays that way until somebody has a reason to open it.

Nimera keeps looking at it.

**NIMERA:** Define reason.

**MIRENA:** Something better than curiosity.

Nimera looks faintly disappointed.

### [THE HANDOFF]

They reach the room being cleared for maps and records.

A crew member carries a damaged chair out past them.

Mirena looks at the four permanent party members.

**MIRENA:** While you're working with me, this is your base. Operationally. I am not giving you a fort; the paperwork alone would make me hate you.

Cyanis looks around the half-clean room.

**CYANIS:** Wasn't asking for one.

**MIRENA:** Good.

Torren checks the nearest wall with one hand.

**TORREN:** Dry enough.

**NIMERA:** Inspiring standard.

**ILYRA:** Better than the last place we slept underground.

Nobody argues with that.

The surrounding work already establishes what is usable without a tutorial speech:
- beds / rest space;
- supplies;
- records / map workspace;
- basic treatment space;
- a functioning gate / travel point.

Other rooms remain dusty, sealed, damaged, or under active repair.

### [ONE MORE THING — OLD CITY ACCESS]

Mirena is about to turn back toward the yard when she remembers something.

**MIRENA:** One more thing. The Archive crew opened a side passage after you left yesterday—lower side of the working collection. They've marked the entrance. They got the closure open, then stopped, and I don't know what's in there.

Nimera straightens slightly.

**NIMERA:** Was it there when we went down?

**MIRENA:** Not accessibly.

**ILYRA:** Stopping at the threshold was sensible.

**MIRENA:** I occasionally employ sensible people.

**NIMERA:** Deliberately?

Mirena gives her a look.

**MIRENA:** You're settling in quickly.

Cyanis looks back toward the direction of Caelora as if he can somehow see the Archives from here.

**CYANIS:** You want us to check it.

**MIRENA:** When you're ready. Not before.

That is the entire in-world unlock.

No one names the Archive Judgment Engine.

### [MIRENA AND MAEVRA LEAVE]

Back in the yard, the initial reopening work is stable enough to continue without them.

Maevra crosses over while tightening a glove / carrying a practical work item / finishing whatever task she was already doing.

She looks at Torren, then at the west side of the fort.

**MAEVRA:** West wall's soft under the moss.

**TORREN:** Saw it.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

That is all she needs to contribute.

Mirena looks at the four.

**MIRENA:** If something changes the investigation, send word. Otherwise, try not to discover anything impossible until tomorrow.

**CYANIS:** And if we find another Warden?

**MIRENA:** Consider not waking it.

Ilyra looks at Cyanis.

**ILYRA:** We can do that.

**CYANIS:** Why are you looking at me?

**NIMERA:** Pattern recognition.

Torren almost smiles.

**NIMERA:** Also, the impossible part is not really under our control.

**MIRENA:** I was afraid you'd say that.

Mirena and Maevra leave for Caelora.

They do not become default residents or traveling companions.
