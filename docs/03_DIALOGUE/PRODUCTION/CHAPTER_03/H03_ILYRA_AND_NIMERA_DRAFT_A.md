### [OPTIONAL CHARACTER-LIFE — CRESTHAVEN SUPPLY ROOM]

Available during the Chapter-3 cleanup window.

The supply room is usable but unfinished. Several Crown crates still carry hurried temporary labels from the reopening crew.

Ilyra is checking a shelf.

Nimera is staring at a wooden crate marked in thick charcoal:

**MISC.**

**NIMERA:** That's not a category.

Ilyra glances over.

**ILYRA:** It is if you stop caring.

Nimera slowly turns toward her.

**NIMERA:** No. That's fucking surrender disguised as handwriting.

**ILYRA:** It's a box.

**NIMERA:** Boxes can participate in bad systems.

**ILYRA:** Brave position.

Nimera opens it.

Inside: spare cord, candle stubs, buttons, chalk, wax, a hinge pin, two unmatched buckles, cloth, and one spoon.

She picks up the spoon.

**NIMERA:** Why is there a spoon?

**ILYRA:** Eating.

**NIMERA:** I know what a spoon does.

**ILYRA:** You asked.

**NIMERA:** I asked why it's in here with cord, wax, hardware, chalk, and apparently whatever else somebody lost patience with.

**ILYRA:** Maybe they were tired.

Nimera pauses.

**NIMERA:** Annoyingly plausible.

She puts the spoon aside.

**ILYRA:** Put it back.

**NIMERA:** Absolutely not. Now I need to know whether it's an accident or evidence of a worse sorting principle.

**ILYRA:** You're investigating the spoon.

**NIMERA:** I'm correcting the box.

### [THE SORT]

Nimera starts making small piles on the floor.

Ilyra watches for a moment, then reaches down and takes the cord.

Nimera's head snaps toward her.

**NIMERA:** What are you doing?

**ILYRA:** Taking cord.

**NIMERA:** I just placed that.

**ILYRA:** I noticed.

**NIMERA:** Then why did you move it?

**ILYRA:** I need cord.

A beat.

**NIMERA:** You're contaminating the sort.

**ILYRA:** It's string.

**NIMERA:** Cord.

**ILYRA:** Somehow worse.

Ilyra starts away with it.

**NIMERA:** Wait. All of it?

Ilyra holds up what she took.

**ILYRA:** No.

**NIMERA:** Fine. Good. Otherwise I'd have to decide whether an empty cord category should remain represented.

Ilyra looks at her.

**ILYRA:** You'd make a memorial for string.

**NIMERA:** Cord. And no. Probably.

Ilyra smiles.

**NIMERA:** You're doing this on purpose now.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

Nimera smiles back despite herself.

**NIMERA:** Good. I was worried you were actually like this.

**ILYRA:** I can be both.

**NIMERA:** Deeply inconvenient.

### [THE UNKNOWN PIECE]

Nimera digs farther into the crate and pulls out a small bent brass fitting.

She turns it over.

**ILYRA:** What's that?

**NIMERA:** Don't know. Manufactured fitting, probably retaining something, but the wear is asymmetric and this bend might be damage. Don't let me call it a bracket yet.

**ILYRA:** Miscellaneous.

Nimera looks at her.

**NIMERA:** Fuck no.

**ILYRA:** Seems accurate.

**NIMERA:** Unknown function is not miscellaneous. `Unknown` describes our knowledge. `Miscellaneous` describes a collection strategy, usually a shitty one.

**ILYRA:** It came from the miscellaneous box.

**NIMERA:** Which is already wrong.

**ILYRA:** According to you.

**NIMERA:** According to language, evidence, and basic respect for ever finding anything again.

Ilyra raises an eyebrow.

Nimera stops.

**NIMERA:** Fine. That was too much confidence.

**ILYRA:** I didn't say anything.

**NIMERA:** Your face did. I don't know who labeled the box or why. They could have been tired, rushed, inheriting somebody else's disaster—

**ILYRA:** You're defending them now.

**NIMERA:** I'm correcting myself. Don't make it sentimental.

Ilyra laughs.

**NIMERA:** There. You know I'm right.

**ILYRA:** No. I'm enjoying you prosecuting and acquitting a stranger over charcoal.

**NIMERA:** The fucking evidence changed.

**ILYRA:** The evidence was my eyebrow.

**NIMERA:** Hostile witness.

### [BAD CATEGORIES]

Ilyra picks up one of the unmatched buckles.

**ILYRA:** Clothing?

**NIMERA:** Hardware.

**ILYRA:** Belt.

**NIMERA:** Could be harness.

**ILYRA:** Clothing adjacent.

Nimera stares at her.

**NIMERA:** Absolutely not. That's how categories rot.

**ILYRA:** Starting to think you hate categories.

**NIMERA:** I love categories. That's why bad ones make me fucking furious.

A beat.

Ilyra smiles.

Nimera hears herself.

**NIMERA:** All right. That sounded a little unwell.

**ILYRA:** No. That sounded exactly like you.

Nimera laughs.

**NIMERA:** Fuck you.

**ILYRA:** Better.

### [UNRESOLVED]

Nimera finds a small empty box and places the brass fitting inside.

She writes:

**UNRESOLVED — FUNCTION UNKNOWN**

Ilyra reads it.

**ILYRA:** Miscellaneous with more steps.

Nimera's pen stops.

**NIMERA:** It is a bounded unknown. Future me knows exactly what I failed to establish instead of inheriting `who fucking knows` in polite charcoal.

**ILYRA:** Longer miscellaneous.

**NIMERA:** Get out.

Ilyra heads for the doorway with the cord.

**ILYRA:** Don't overthink the spoon.

Nimera looks at the spoon still sitting beside the crate.

**NIMERA:** It is presently an unresolved storage anomaly.

Ilyra stops at the doorway.

**ILYRA:** Spoon.

**NIMERA:** That's still just a noun.

**ILYRA:** Very exclusive category.

Nimera throws a candle stub at her.

Ilyra steps aside. It misses.

**ILYRA:** Candles go over there.

She points to the pile Nimera made.

Then leaves.

Nimera looks at the candle on the floor.

She picks it up and puts it in the correct pile.

Then looks at the spoon.

A beat.

**NIMERA:** Fucking miscellaneous.

She drops it back into the original crate.

Immediately regrets it.
