### [OPTIONAL CHARACTER-LIFE — CRESTHAVEN SUPPLY ROOM]

Available during the Chapter-3 cleanup window.

The supply room is usable but unfinished. Several Crown crates still carry hurried temporary labels from the reopening crew.

Ilyra is checking a shelf.

Nimera is staring at a wooden crate marked in thick charcoal:

**MISC.**

**NIMERA:** That's not a category.

Ilyra glances over.

**ILYRA:** It is if you stop caring.

Nimera slowly turns toward her.

**NIMERA:** No. That's bullshit surrender disguised as handwriting.

**ILYRA:** It's a box.

**NIMERA:** Boxes can participate in bad systems.

**ILYRA:** Brave position.

Nimera opens it.

Inside: spare cord, candle stubs, buttons, chalk, wax, a hinge pin, two unmatched buckles, cloth, and one spoon.

She picks up the spoon.

**NIMERA:** Why is there a spoon?

**ILYRA:** Eating.

**NIMERA:** I know what a spoon does.

**ILYRA:** You asked.

**NIMERA:** I asked why it's in here with cord, wax, hardware, chalk, and whatever else somebody got sick of sorting.

**ILYRA:** Maybe they were tired.

Nimera pauses.

**NIMERA:** Annoyingly plausible.

She puts the spoon aside.

### [THE SORT]

Nimera starts making small piles on the floor.

Ilyra watches, then reaches down and takes the cord.

**NIMERA:** What are you doing?

**ILYRA:** Taking cord.

**NIMERA:** I just placed that.

**ILYRA:** I noticed.

**NIMERA:** Then why did you move it?

**ILYRA:** I need cord.

A beat.

**NIMERA:** You're contaminating the sort.

**ILYRA:** It's string.

**NIMERA:** Cord.

**ILYRA:** Somehow worse.

Ilyra starts away with it.

**NIMERA:** Wait. All of it?

Ilyra holds up what she took.

**ILYRA:** No.

**NIMERA:** Fine.

Ilyra smiles.

**NIMERA:** You're doing this on purpose now.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

Nimera smiles back despite herself.

**NIMERA:** Good. I was worried you were actually like this.

**ILYRA:** I can be both.

### [THE UNKNOWN PIECE]

Nimera digs farther into the crate and pulls out a small bent brass fitting.

She turns it over.

**ILYRA:** What's that?

**NIMERA:** Don't know. Some kind of manufactured fitting. Bend might be damage.

**ILYRA:** Miscellaneous.

Nimera looks at her.

**NIMERA:** Fuck no.

**ILYRA:** Seems accurate.

**NIMERA:** Unknown function means I don't know what the damn thing is. Miscellaneous means somebody stopped sorting.

**ILYRA:** It came from the miscellaneous box.

**NIMERA:** Which is already an accusation against the box.

Ilyra raises an eyebrow.

Nimera catches herself.

**NIMERA:** Fine. Against whoever labeled the box. Maybe they were tired, rushed, inheriting somebody else's disaster—

**ILYRA:** You're defending them now.

**NIMERA:** I'm correcting myself. Don't make it sentimental.

Ilyra laughs.

**NIMERA:** There. You know I'm right.

**ILYRA:** No. I'm enjoying you prosecuting and acquitting a stranger over charcoal.

**NIMERA:** The evidence changed, you smug asshole.

**ILYRA:** The evidence was my eyebrow.

**NIMERA:** Hostile witness.

### [BAD CATEGORIES]

Ilyra picks up one of the unmatched buckles.

**ILYRA:** Clothing?

**NIMERA:** Hardware.

**ILYRA:** Belt.

**NIMERA:** Could be harness.

**ILYRA:** Clothing adjacent.

Nimera stares at her.

**NIMERA:** Absolutely not. That's how categories rot.

**ILYRA:** Starting to think you hate categories.

**NIMERA:** I love categories. Bad ones piss me off.

Ilyra smiles.

**NIMERA:** All right. That sounded a little unwell.

**ILYRA:** No. That sounded exactly like you.

Nimera laughs.

**NIMERA:** Fuck you.

**ILYRA:** Better.

### [UNRESOLVED]

Nimera finds a small empty box and places the brass fitting inside.

She writes:

**UNRESOLVED — FUNCTION UNKNOWN**

Ilyra reads it.

**ILYRA:** Miscellaneous with more steps.

Nimera's pen stops.

**NIMERA:** It says what I failed to establish instead of leaving future me with `who the hell knows` in polite charcoal.

**ILYRA:** Longer miscellaneous.

**NIMERA:** Get out.

Ilyra heads for the doorway with the cord.

**ILYRA:** Don't overthink the spoon.

Nimera looks at the spoon beside the crate.

**NIMERA:** Spoon.

Ilyra stops at the doorway.

**ILYRA:** Very exclusive category.

Nimera throws a candle stub at her.

Ilyra steps aside. It misses.

**ILYRA:** Candles go over there.

She points to the pile Nimera made.

Then leaves.

Nimera picks up the candle and puts it in the correct pile.

She looks at the spoon.

A beat.

**NIMERA:** Miscellaneous. Son of a bitch.

She drops it back into the original crate.

Immediately regrets it.
