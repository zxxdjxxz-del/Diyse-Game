### [STORY TRIGGER — ROYAL VERIFICATION ROOM]

The investigation moves into the palace verification room.

The Queen's physical seal is produced with its custody record.

Lysara looks at it.

**LYSARA:** Mine.

Othmar checks the casing and record.

**OTHMAR:** No visible substitution. No reported damage. Custody record is continuous.

**LYSARA:** Test it.

A legitimate recent order Lysara remembers authorizing is tested.

The seal answers normally.

**OTHMAR:** Normal.

The suspect Card-transport order is tested again.

It produces the same recognized core authority response.

Silence.

**CYANIS:** That's inconvenient.

**MIRENA:** Very.

Lysara looks to Othmar.

**LYSARA:** What does this actually prove?

**OTHMAR:** Your seal is here, intact, and functioning normally. The transport order still carries the same recognized authority response. Our ordinary verification accepts both. I cannot yet tell you how both facts are true.

**LYSARA:** Then don't pretend you can.

Torren looks at the two results.

**TORREN:** Honest contradiction.

Lysara glances at him.

**LYSARA:** Preferable to a false answer.

### [CUSTODY]

Mirena has the ledger open.

**MIRENA:** No missing interval?

**OTHMAR:** None recorded.

Maevra looks up.

**MAEVRA:** Recorded isn't the same as impossible.

**OTHMAR:** Correct. The ledger does not prove covert access happened. It also does not prove it could not have happened.

Lysara folds her hands.

**LYSARA:** And we do not know physical access was required at all.

No one answers.

**LYSARA:** Good. Then both remain possibilities, not facts.

That closes the speculation.

### [NEXT EXPERT]

Mirena leans back from the ledger.

**MIRENA:** Crown procedure has told us exactly what Crown procedure can tell us.

**CYANIS:** Which is?

**MIRENA:** That something impossible-looking passed every normal test.

She taps the suspect order.

**MIRENA:** I want someone who works where normal classification stops being useful.

Othmar knows the answer.

**OTHMAR:** Pellan.

**CYANIS:** Who?

**MIRENA:** Nimera Pellan. Card scholar. Old City archives.

**TORREN:** Good?

Mirena considers the word.

**MIRENA:** Annoyingly.

**ILYRA:** That's usually promising.

Lysara returns to the practical question.

**LYSARA:** What does she need?

**MIRENA:** Authenticated copies, the verification notes, and access to the Card as it currently is.

**OTHMAR:** Originals and the physical seal stay here.

**LYSARA:** Agreed.

She looks to Cyanis.

**LYSARA:** The Card remains with you. Pellan may inspect it. Nobody separates it from you or tests it against another authority object.

**CYANIS:** Good.

### [THE SPLIT]

Mirena gathers the Crown-side records.

**MIRENA:** Maevra, stay with me. I want the transport chain pulled apart beside the field procedure it was imitating.

**MAEVRA:** Happily. Somebody made a false order look boring enough to work. I want to know how.

**OTHMAR:** Boring is one of administration's more dangerous qualities.

**MAEVRA:** See? Fun group.

Cyanis looks at the ledgers.

**CYANIS:** I think our definitions have separated.

Lysara looks to Cyanis, Ilyra, and Torren.

**LYSARA:** Take the Card and copies to Pellan. Mirena's authorization opens the archive route you need. Nothing more.

**TORREN:** Understood.

**MIRENA:** Show her what we know. Also what we don't.

**ILYRA:** I suspect she'll notice if we cheat.

**MIRENA:** Immediately.

The investigation splits.
