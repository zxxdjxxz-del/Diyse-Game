# 3. Repeated-exposition / evidence-discipline audit

**PASS after trims.**

The chapter was repeating variants of the same principle too often:
- record ≠ reality;
- attempt ≠ success;
- connected questions ≠ proven same actor;
- evidence should not be made to prove more than it actually proves.

Those principles remain important to the cast and mystery, but they no longer need to be verbally restated every time evidence appears.

Targeted corrections:
- Beat 6 / Beat 7 — minor duplicate-firewall wording trimmed while preserving character voice and behavior;
- Beat 12 — the seal-copying distinction now lands once: the practitioner clearly copied the mark and clearly attempted something more, but nothing proves the genuine royal response was reproduced;
- Beat 13 — the Caelora debrief now reports the consequential result instead of repeating Beat 12's entire epistemic argument.

The environment and prior scenes are now trusted to carry more of the player's understanding.
