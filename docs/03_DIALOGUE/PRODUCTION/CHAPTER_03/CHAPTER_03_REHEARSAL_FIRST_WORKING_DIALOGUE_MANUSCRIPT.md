# BEAT 1 — CAELORA GATE / ARRIVAL

### [GAMEPLAY — CAELORA APPROACH]

Player control is active.

Caelora is visible ahead before the party reaches the gate. Let the functioning capital get a clean visual read before dialogue begins.

During the final road approach, ordinary traversal remains Cyanis-only on the field.

### [STORY TRIGGER — CAELORA GATE]

The routine checkpoint is deliberately uneventful. Maevra's real authority and Crown business are sufficient for lawful entry. No one detains the party or attempts to confiscate Cyanis's Card.

Once entry is cleared:

**MAEVRA:** Palace first.

**CYANIS:** Before food?

**ILYRA:** You ate.

**CYANIS:** Hours ago.

**ILYRA:** I know.

**TORREN:** He'll live.

**CYANIS:** Good. Medical consensus.

**ILYRA:** That's not what that was.

**MAEVRA:** Keep moving.

### [GAMEPLAY — FIRST CAELORA STRETCH]

Player control resumes. Caelora uses town/hub presentation, so relevant party members may be visibly present according to implementation needs.

Maevra is actively taking the group toward the palace, allowing one short route-guidance exchange.

After the player has had time to read the modern city:

**TORREN:** Mural had more city.

**MAEVRA:** How much more?

**TORREN:** Enough that this wall would've been well inside it.

**ILYRA:** You're sure?

**TORREN:** About that. Not the exact boundary.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** Say it exactly like that upstairs.

**TORREN:** Was going to.

A short silence.

**CYANIS:** Still hungry.

**MAEVRA:** I heard you the first time.

The first controlled Caelora stretch ends at the palace / royal-district transition.

# BEAT 2 — ROYAL AUDIENCE / THE CHAPTER-2 REPORT

### [STORY TRIGGER — WORKING ROYAL AUDIENCE]

Player control pauses for the formal report.

This is a working royal audience, not a ceremonial spectacle. Queen Lysara, Crown Princess Mirena, Chancellor Othmar Calder, Maevra, Cyanis, Ilyra, and Torren are present with only the clerical support needed for records and maps.

Simple field placement only. Portraits + dialogue box carry the conversation.

**LYSARA:** Commander. Start with the people.

**MAEVRA:** The prisoners still in the Bastion are out. The corridor is open again.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** Rhazek is alive.

**MIRENA:** He got away.

**MAEVRA:** With what remained organized enough to move.

**OTHMAR:** Direction?

**MAEVRA:** None I can defend.

**LYSARA:** Earlier transfers?

**MAEVRA:** Gone before we reached them.

**ILYRA:** Prisoners said groups had already been moved out.

**LYSARA:** Did anyone know where?

**ILYRA:** No one I spoke to.

**MIRENA:** Condition of the people you did recover?

**ILYRA:** Mixed. Exhausted. Some injured badly enough that the route we used wasn't an evacuation route.

**MIRENA:** So reopening the road mattered.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

**OTHMAR:** The route you used began in the Old Waterworks?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**TORREN:** Then it became older construction.

**OTHMAR:** Black Host work?

**TORREN:** No.

**OTHMAR:** Certain?

**TORREN:** About that, yes. Their work sits on it. It didn't make it.

**MAEVRA:** The old route eventually opened into foundations beneath the Bastion. That's how we got inside without taking the occupied road.

**LYSARA:** And nobody knew it connected.

**TORREN:** Not on any route map I've seen.

A beat.

**MIRENA:** Show us the mural copy.

Torren's existing Chapter-2 copy is put where the room can examine it. No bespoke map animation is required.

**TORREN:** I copied placement, route relationships, and the marks that mattered. Not every line on the wall.

**OTHMAR:** How exact is the scale?

**TORREN:** Good enough to compare positions. Not good enough to survey from.

Mirena studies the copy.

**MIRENA:** This is where Caelora is now.

**TORREN:** Same location.

**LYSARA:** The city shown there is much larger.

**TORREN:** Much.

A brief silence.

**MIRENA:** And this structure north of it.

**TORREN:** Separate from the city on the mural.

**MIRENA:** That's our Crest.

**LYSARA:** It is.

**OTHMAR:** No ambiguity in the copy?

**TORREN:** None about the mark.

**MIRENA:** On something that old.

**TORREN:** That's what was on the wall.

**LYSARA:** Do you know what the structure was?

**TORREN:** No.

**LYSARA:** Good.

Torren looks at her.

**LYSARA:** You know what you copied. You don't know what it meant. Keep those separate.

**TORREN:** Was planning to.

**MIRENA:** If any of the buried collections under Caelora survived intact, the Old City may have something comparable.

**OTHMAR:** Some of the archives do survive. What they contain is another question.

**LYSARA:** Then it remains a question until we have a reason to answer it properly.

The report moves on rather than turning the mural into a solved history lesson.

**MAEVRA:** There's one more part of this.

**LYSARA:** The Card.

**MAEVRA:** Recovered from the Diysean ruins in the Westways. It was already being moved toward Caelora under what we understood to be Crown authority.

Lysara's attention shifts to Cyanis.

**LYSARA:** It remains with you?

**CYANIS:** Yes.

**LYSARA:** By necessity or preference?

**ILYRA:** Necessity is safer.

**CYANIS:** I was going to say preference.

**ILYRA:** You would've been wrong.

A beat.

**LYSARA:** What happens when it leaves him?

**ILYRA:** We only tested separation once on purpose. His pulse went bad. Tremor. The Card answered green and gold toward him. Returning it stopped the response.

**CYANIS:** We decided curiosity had limits.

**MAEVRA:** It has stayed with him since.

**OTHMAR:** And the transport toward Caelora was formally authorized?

**MAEVRA:** That's what the chain said.

**LYSARA:** Do we have the order?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

Lysara looks to the attending clerk.

**LYSARA:** Bring the original authorization and every document attached to that transport.

**OTHMAR:** Including dispatch routing and expenditure records.

**LYSARA:** All of it.

The audience shifts from field report to document verification.

Cut before anyone performs the Beat-3 authentication or states the Queen's prior knowledge position.
