# Mirena's Concern / Cresthaven Lead

### [DIRECT CUT — PRIVATE ROOM, CAELORA]

Leaving the Sealwright Chamber ends the Old City field section.

Cut directly to a private palace meeting room in Caelora.

Present:
- Cyanis;
- Ilyra;
- Torren;
- Nimera;
- Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth;
- Maevra.

Queen Lysara is **not** present.

Mirena has Crown records and seal-custody material already spread across the table from the work she and Maevra continued while the party was below.

Do not stage a formal audience.

Mirena looks at the four of them.

**MIRENA:** What changed?

Cyanis does not start at the beginning.

**CYANIS:** Someone's been using the Old City.

Mirena's expression sharpens.

**MIRENA:** Using it how?

**NIMERA:** Reading first.

A beat.

**NIMERA:** Then practicing.

Maevra looks at her.

**MAEVRA:** Practicing what?

**CYANIS:** The royal seal.

Mirena goes still.

**MIRENA:** The Crest?

**CYANIS:** Yes.

Nimera takes over only far enough to make the distinction that matters.

**NIMERA:** The room had copies. Older Diysean forms first. Then older Yahtrean seals. Then royal-document seals.

**TORREN:** Work gets cleaner as it goes.

**NIMERA:** Eventually the Crest.

**ILYRA:** Repeatedly.

Mirena looks between them.

**MIRENA:** A visual copy?

**CYANIS:** Late ones were good.

**NIMERA:** The mark was good.

Mirena catches the wording.

**MIRENA:** The magic wasn't.

**NIMERA:** I can't prove that.

A beat.

**NIMERA:** I can prove somebody used enough magic trying to do something beyond the impression that they scorched work surfaces built for sealcraft.

**MIRENA:** Did any of them answer like the Queen's?

Nimera shakes her head once.

**NIMERA:** Nothing we found proves that.

**MIRENA:** But somebody was trying.

**NIMERA:** Yes.

### [THE CROWN-SIDE PROBLEM HAS NOT BECOME EASIER]

Mirena glances at the custody material already on the table.

**TORREN:** Any luck up here?

**MAEVRA:** No gap. No theft window.

Mirena looks back at the party.

**MIRENA:** Nothing useful.

A beat.

**MIRENA:** So we have a seal that should not have been used, an order carrying the real authority response, and somebody below the city spending serious magic learning to reproduce the Crest.

**CYANIS:** Trying to reproduce it.

Mirena looks at him.

**MIRENA:** Good correction.

A beat.

**MIRENA:** I hate it.

### [THE OTHER THING THAT CHANGED]

Mirena notices Cyanis has been keeping one hand near the Card without handling it.

**MIRENA:** And the Card?

Cyanis takes it out normally.

The deep Ruby state is stable.

Mirena has not seen it in this state before.

**MIRENA:** That's new.

**CYANIS:** Very.

**NIMERA:** It happened after a Diysean Warden tried to kill us.

Mirena looks at her.

**MIRENA:** I was hoping that sentence improved.

**NIMERA:** It does not.

Cyanis gives the important sequence without turning it into a theory.

**CYANIS:** It assessed all four of us first. Then attacked.

**MIRENA:** Assessed how?

**ILYRA:** Old ward-work. Light across the whole group. Deliberate.

**CYANIS:** After we beat it, it stayed active long enough to give us two messages.

Mirena waits.

**CYANIS:** `PREVIOUS ERROR.`

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Then `LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED.`

The room goes quiet.

**MIRENA:** Exact wording?

**NIMERA:** As close as the surviving language allows. The order matters more than any prettier translation.

**MIRENA:** And then the Card changed.

**CYANIS:** After the Warden went completely dead.

Mirena looks at the Ruby Card again.

**MIRENA:** Do we know what Last Sentinel is?

**NIMERA:** No.

Mirena accepts the answer immediately.

**MIRENA:** Good.

Nimera looks faintly offended.

**NIMERA:** That's my line.

**MIRENA:** You left it unattended.

### [MIRENA'S CONCERN]

Mirena stops looking at the Card and returns to the table.

Her attention is now on the shape of the problem rather than any one artifact.

**MIRENA:** Someone knew enough to find material most people don't know exists.

No one interrupts.

**MIRENA:** Someone reached restricted collections beneath my capital.

She looks at the copied seal evidence.

**MIRENA:** Someone had enough magic to keep trying this after ordinary failure should have stopped them.

A beat.

**MIRENA:** Which means I don't know which part of this is access, which part is magic, and which part is both.

A beat.

**MIRENA:** More importantly, I don't know which door they came through.

Torren glances toward the custody records.

**TORREN:** Physical or political?

**MIRENA:** Yes.

That answer lands badly enough that nobody jokes about it.

**MIRENA:** Until I know more, I don't want this passed through every office that would normally be entitled to hear it.

**CYANIS:** You think someone's inside the Crown.

**MIRENA:** I think somebody has meaningful access to things they should not.

She meets his eyes.

**MIRENA:** Those are not the same accusation yet.

**CYANIS:** Yet.

**MIRENA:** Exactly.

### [THE REQUEST]

Mirena looks at all four of them rather than only Cyanis.

**MIRENA:** I want you to keep working this with me.

A beat.

**MIRENA:** Directly.

Cyanis looks once at Ilyra, Torren, and Nimera.

Nobody performs a vote.

**CYANIS:** All right.

**NIMERA:** You do understand I already followed them into a sealed dead civilization and fought its furniture.

Mirena looks at Cyanis.

**MIRENA:** Is she always going to be like this?

**CYANIS:** I met her today.

**NIMERA:** Promising start, though.

Ilyra almost smiles.

**ILYRA:** We keep going.

That is enough.

### [THE OLD MAP COMES BACK]

Torren is still looking at the evidence spread across the table.

**TORREN:** There's one other thing.

He brings out / retrieves the existing copy made from the Chapter-2 Ancient mural / route evidence.

This is **not new evidence**. It is the same old material already reported in Caelora:
- the enormous Ancient city corresponding to modern Caelora;
- a separate structure farther north;
- the exact Crest of Yahtrea marked on that structure.

Mirena looks at the copied map again.

**MIRENA:** I still hate that.

**NIMERA:** The Crest?

**MIRENA:** The fact that the Crest of a kingdom that did not exist is sitting on a map older than the kingdom.

**TORREN:** That's the part.

Mirena studies the geography rather than the symbol now.

**MIRENA:** Show me the scale again.

Torren does.

He keeps the uncertainty intact rather than pretending the Ancient map is perfectly modern.

**TORREN:** This is the best fit. Mountains here. Old route comes out of Caelora on this side. Position's approximate.

Mirena traces the modern relationship mentally / against a current Crown map already available in the room.

She stops.

**MIRENA:** Wait.

Nobody speaks.

She checks it again.

**MIRENA:** I think I know where that is.

**CYANIS:** Where?

Mirena looks up.

**MIRENA:** Cresthaven.

A beat.

**TORREN:** The old forest fort?

**MIRENA:** If your placement is close, yes.

Torren looks at the two maps.

**TORREN:** Close enough to check.

**NIMERA:** And the Crest?

Mirena looks back at the Ancient copy.

**MIRENA:** Still impossible.

A beat.

**MIRENA:** But now it has an address.

### [BEAT END]

Cresthaven is now the next practical lead.

Do not explain:
- why the Crest of Yahtrea appears on an Ancient source;
- Cresthaven's full Ancient history;
- anything farther north;
- the recent reader / seal practitioner's identity;
- the mechanism behind Lysara's impossible seal response;
- what `LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED` ultimately refers to.

Transition into:

**Beat 14 — Rest in Caelora / Morning Departure.**

## Beat-13 production notes

- The Old City debrief is selective rather than chronological. Mirena asks what changed and follows the implications that affect current decisions.
- Maevra is present because she remained in Caelora with Mirena, but her dialogue density is deliberately low. She supplies only the concise Crown-side custody result and does not act as a co-debriefer or bridge narrator.
- the seal-copying evidence is framed correctly: somebody practiced increasingly accurate copies culminating in the Crest of Yahtrea and used substantial magic, but nothing proves a successful duplicate of the genuine royal authority response.
- the Warden report preserves exact sequence and timing without classifying Last Sentinel or the Ruby Card.
- Mirena's concern emerges from her political brain: she focuses on access, power, institutional exposure, and who can be trusted with the investigation.
- Mirena asks the four-person party to continue directly with her; the group agrees without a loyalty speech or four separate confirmations.
- the Cresthaven lead comes from revisiting the existing Chapter-2 Ancient map/mural copy brought back into the discussion by the field party. Beat 13 does not invent a new map.
- Mirena identifies the marked northern structure as **probably Cresthaven** by comparing the old geography to current Crown knowledge. The exact ancient meaning of the Crest remains unresolved.
- Beat 14 owns the explicit instruction to rest for the night and the next-morning departure structure.
