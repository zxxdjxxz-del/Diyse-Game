## Person-Brain performance standard

The target is not equal line length and not generic concision.

- speakers hold the floor as long as their actual thought requires;
- interruptions come from motive, urgency, impatience, expertise, humor, or relationship;
- characters may self-correct, restart, trail off, swear, answer the wrong part, argue over wording, or keep going past the first technically complete sentence;
- different people keep different rhythms;
- exposition remains evidence-owned and reveal-safe;
- the Dialogue Editor must not flatten a Person Agent into concise generic competence.
