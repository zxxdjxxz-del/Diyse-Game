## Person-Brain performance standard — 2026-09-12

This pass is not a generic “make dialogue longer” pass.

The target is:
- speakers hold the floor for as long as their thought and personality naturally require;
- interruptions come from another character's motive, urgency, impatience, expertise, humor, or relationship—not from a line-length quota;
- characters may self-correct, restart, trail off, swear, answer the wrong part, argue over wording, or continue past the first technically complete sentence;
- different people keep different rhythms rather than converging on equal-sized turns;
- exposition remains evidence-owned and reveal-safe;
- the Dialogue Editor may shape timing, but it must not flatten a Person Agent into concise generic competence.

### Nimera-specific active interpretation

Nimera's current character authority is:
- **Face:** Memory;
- **Base:** Cardweaver;
- **Subclass:** Proofhunter.

`Resource` and `Sixfold Knight` are retired for Nimera and must not be restored.

Her current performance behavior includes:
- correcting imprecise words even when the practical point is already understood;
- distinguishing evidence from inference and one kind of uncertainty from another;
- revising her own statement aloud when she catches herself overclaiming;
- keeping the floor through multi-step reasoning when interested;
- swearing frequently and naturally inside ordinary speech and technical explanation;
- getting interrupted by people who have an actual reason to stop her;
- objecting to the interruption, sometimes claiming she was almost done, and occasionally continuing anyway;
- becoming socially embedded through mundane argument, teasing, work habits, and callbacks rather than explicit belonging speeches.

This does **not** mean every Nimera line is long or profane. Vulnerability may simplify her language; true fury may become cleaner and more formal; urgent danger may require very short speech.

### Ensemble contrast retained

The performance pass must preserve contrast:
- **Cyanis** may keep talking through a judgment or argument when he is actually working something out; he is not restricted to leader one-liners.
- **Ilyra** may finish the practical or medical consequence she owns, interrupt unsafe behavior, and use dry humor without becoming a party mother.
- **Torren** remains naturally terse much of the time, but may hold longer turns on terrain, routes, field evidence, or judgment he genuinely owns. He must not be lengthened merely to match Nimera.
- **Maevra** is socially quick, practically useful, and capable of mischief; she must not become a co-exposition narrator.
- **Mirena/Lysara** are allowed complete authority turns: decisions, boundaries, questions, and orders do not need to be artificially distributed across the room.

## Spoken-dialogue / narration rule remains in force

The governing rule remains:

> **The environment shows. Evidence owners interpret. Authority figures decide. Characters react. Nobody recites the scene back to the player.**

The Person-Brain performance pass does **not** undo the prior spoken-dialogue / narration audit. Longer dialogue is earned by character thought, conflict, uncertainty, humor, or decision-making—not by restating visible information.

Current interpretation:
- **Lysara converts information into judgment, boundaries, questions, or orders.**
- **Mirena converts evidence into investigation choices and trust boundaries.**
- **New-listener briefings stay compressed** and expand only through questions the new listener genuinely needs answered.
- **The environment carries visible progression.** Dialogue adds interpretation, uncertainty, decision, or reaction.
- **Scholars and technical owners may explain evidence** when interpretation itself is their job; narration reduction is not a universal brevity rule.
