# Chapter 1 — Dialogue Polish Pass 01

**Status:** CURRENT PASS RECORD — standalone scene drafts are current authority  
**Scope:** full-chapter read across Beats 1–15 + C03/C04/C05 after the rehearsal-first rebuild and field-presentation normalization.

## Purpose

This pass reads Chapter 1 as one continuous chapter rather than evaluating each beat in isolation. It targets repeated phrasing, writer-facing relationship commentary, knowledge-firewall speech, premature relationship language, redundant transition dialogue, and optional dialogue that weakens later Character-Life progression.

This is a **polish pass**, not a new structural rewrite. Current Chapter-1 story order, geography, encounter placement, Maevra injury continuity, Junction monument continuity, random-encounter rules, Card continuity, and gameplay presentation remain unchanged.

## Changes made

### Beat 3 — Greenhollow / Torren
- `Twenty-five years and you're still wrong about this` changed to `All these years and you're still wrong about this`.
- Reason: Ilyra is present in Beat 3, while C05 later has her naturally ask Maevra how long she has known Torren and receive the exact twenty-five-year answer. The old line made that later question a continuity error.
- Torren's Card observation no longer falsely says the inert Card is visibly glowing. He reacts to Cyanis carrying unusual old metal before Maevra gives the travel-relevant brief.
- one repeated `Good enough` was changed to `Works for me`.

### Beat 4 — Hollow Watch Approach
- removed Cyanis's explicit `I'm starting to understand the relationship` exchange.
- Reason: Maevra/Torren familiarity already reads through their speech; Cyanis does not need to narrate the relationship state for the audience.
- varied repeated `Good enough` phrasing.

### Beat 6 — Black Host Excavation / Lower Access
- removed the second `subtle` joke in the short Hollow Watch dungeon run.
- removed the repeated `Ready? / Yes / Go` threshold exchange.
- the lower-junction approach now ends on Torren's warning and silence.

### Beat 7 — Lower Junction / Six Channels
- rewrote the relief discussion to remove the old `maybe / could be / could still be / could be both` interpretation ladder.
- characters now react to visible facts: litters, children, movement in both directions, carts, animals, six passages, and heavy civilian use.
- the original function remains unresolved without making the cast sound like they are policing the knowledge firewall.
- shortened the route-onward scene and removed another repeated readiness roll call.

### Beat 8 — Forced Inner Section
- removed the optional post-random-encounter bark containing `I liked you better before the fort / You liked me for about six minutes`.
- Reason: it pushed Cyanis/Torren into their later sharp-friendship register too early and weakened C04's role as an early accidental boundary break.
- tightened the forced-door and Castellan-threshold dialogue.

### Beat 10 — Post-Castellan Mural
- removed the `could be related / could've moved people / or just gotten them under cover` theory ladder.
- the cast now notices the visual relationship between the six-passages discovery and the regional descents, then lets the image sit.
- the mural continues to carry the major discovery rather than the dialogue explaining every possible interpretation.

### Beat 11 — Torren Recruitment
- removed Cyanis's `You're all very comfortable already` line.
- Reason: it was writer-facing relationship commentary. The group shorthand reads without explanation.

### Beat 12 — Southern Briar
- varied the departure phrasing so the chapter no longer repeats `Good enough / Inspiring` in multiple route scenes.
- Briarhide timing and all Chapter-1 route cuts remain unchanged.

### C03 — Torren's Version of Dinner
- removed `That's the most Torren answer possible` and similar character-sheet commentary.
- shortened the `technically right / only technically / important distinction` ladder.
- added more actual dead air and a mundane `Pass the salt` beat so the meal feels less like continuous joke construction.
- softened Cyanis/Torren's post-meal exchange so C03 does not require their later insult register.
- Torren still smokes only after everyone finishes eating.

### C05 — Not Professionally
- retained the user-directed cast: Ilyra + Maevra only.
- retained the splint change and restrained Warden magic that eases pain but cannot mend the fracture.
- rebuilt the Torren conversation so it emerges from quiet rather than functioning as an interview checklist.
- removed the redundant `Were you together? / Are you still together? / Were you lovers? / Recently?` sequence.
- Ilyra now learns the exact twenty-five-year history here, asks naturally about what happened and the present relationship, and eventually asks whether Maevra still loves Torren.
- Maevra says yes and says Torren knows; the scene does not claim Torren's private feelings for him.
- the ending returns to the splint and the `professional` joke rather than becoming a relationship-resolution speech.

## Scenes deliberately not substantially rewritten

Beats 1, 2, 5, 9, 13, 14, and the core of Beat 15 survived the chapter-wide read without a strong enough reason for another dialogue rewrite. Their current strengths outweigh the value of cosmetic churn.

C04 also survives substantially intact. Its `old slut / old cut` misunderstanding still works as the first conspicuous accidental jump into Cyanis/Torren's sharper shared humor, especially now that the premature Beat-8 bark has been removed.

## Current chapter-level read

The chapter now has a cleaner progression:
- Cyanis/Ilyra begin with established practical ease;
- Maevra enters as injured but still active and decisive;
- Torren/Maevra familiarity is visible immediately without giving away the exact private history;
- Torren earns trust through route knowledge and Hollow Watch action;

- Cyanis/Torren's personal humor stays relatively restrained until optional C04;
- the post-climax camp scenes finally let the four characters exist as people rather than continuing the story briefing.
