# BEAT 1 — BRACKENWALL / PROTOCOL

### [GAMEPLAY — BRACKENWALL ARRIVAL]

**Player control is active.**

Brackenwall is working.

Not peaceful, exactly.

But working.

The fort's outer yard has absorbed the Broken Convoy survivors without becoming a disaster scene. Wagons are being inventoried. Damaged harness is stacked for repair. A pair of soldiers argue over a cracked axle while a clerk writes down numbers neither of them likes.

Cyanis Dovaren and Ilyra Amarin enter through the inner gate with the sealed recovery casing under escort.

The artifact remains dark.

No green-and-gold geometry appears.

A Brackenwall custody officer is waiting beside a reinforced intake table.

---

### [STORY TRIGGER — FORMAL INTAKE]

**Player control pauses.**

The custody officer looks at the casing.

Then Cyanis.

Then Ilyra.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** This is the recovered piece?

**CYANIS:** That's the one.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Stayed sealed?

**CYANIS:** Yes.

Ilyra looks at him.

Cyanis catches it.

**CYANIS:** The casing stayed sealed.

The officer looks between them.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Different answer.

**ILYRA:** Twice.

The officer takes out a small intake sheet.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Start with what it actually did.

Ilyra answers first.

**ILYRA:** Green and gold light. Fragmented geometric patterning. It stabilized damaged structures around us for a short period.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Around the artifact?

**ILYRA:** Around him.

She indicates Cyanis.

The officer looks at him.

**CYANIS:** I wasn't doing it on purpose.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** I wasn't accusing you.

**CYANIS:** Good. I don't have a defense prepared.

The officer writes.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Physical effect on you?

Cyanis glances at Ilyra.

**CYANIS:** Apparently I'm not allowed to answer that one alone anymore.

**ILYRA:** Correct.

To the officer:

**ILYRA:** Rapid uneven pulse. Tremor. Dizziness. Magical response rose with the light. Both times the effect ended when the geometry collapsed.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Lasting symptoms?

Ilyra looks at Cyanis's hand.

He notices.

**CYANIS:** Less than yesterday.

**ILYRA:** Which is not the same as none.

The officer writes that down too.

Cyanis watches the pen.

**CYANIS:** I liked it better when people only argued with me verbally.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Paper is more patient.

A beat.

Cyanis considers that.

**CYANIS:** Unfair advantage.

The officer sets the sheet aside.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** All right. Normal intake first. We move the casing into the warded store, keep it sealed, and nobody opens anything until Caelora authorizes it.

Ilyra nods.

**ILYRA:** Sensible.

**CYANIS:** That's new.

Ilyra looks at him.

**CYANIS:** For the artifact.

The custody officer gestures to two reinforced carrying handles.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Set it here.

Cyanis does.

Nothing happens.

The officer waits a moment.

Then takes one handle and signals an escort to take the other.

They lift the sealed casing.

Still nothing.

The officer starts toward the warded interior door.

One step.

Two.

Three.

Cyanis's expression changes.

He does not move.

Ilyra sees it immediately.

**ILYRA:** Stop.

The custody officer stops.

The artifact gives a single hard metallic knock inside the casing.

Everyone looks at it.

A thin green line appears beneath one damaged seam.

Not a flare.

Not protection.

Just light.

Cyanis grips the edge of the intake table.

Ilyra is beside him before anyone asks.

**ILYRA:** Cyanis.

**CYANIS:** I'm fine.

She gives him a look.

**CYANIS:** Sorry. Habit.

**ILYRA:** Better.

She takes his wrist.

The custody officer does not move the casing farther.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** What changed?

**ILYRA:** His pulse.

The green line brightens once.

Cyanis winces.

Not dramatically.

Enough.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Bring it back.

The escort looks at the officer.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Slowly.

They reverse direction.

One step.

The light dims.

Another.

Cyanis exhales.

Ilyra keeps counting.

The casing reaches the intake table.

The green line disappears.

The artifact is dark again.

Silence.

The custody officer looks at the warded-store door.

Then at Cyanis.

Then back at the casing.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** We're not doing that again.

**CYANIS:** Strongly support the new procedure.

**ILYRA:** So do I.

The officer turns to the nearest runner.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Find Commander Solmar.

The runner leaves.

---

### [GAMEPLAY — BRACKENWALL INTAKE HOLD]

**Player control returns briefly.**

The casing remains on the intake table under guard.

Cyanis is not required to hold or touch it.

Ilyra stays near enough to monitor him without hovering.

The player may look around the working Brackenwall yard while the formal escalation occurs.

Background life continues:
- convoy survivors being reassigned to bunks;
- damaged wagons being inspected;
- ordinary fort traffic moving around the anomaly instead of stopping to stare;
- no one treats the artifact as a public spectacle.

At the progression point, Maevra Solmar enters the intake area with the custody officer.

---

### [STORY TRIGGER — MAEVRA]

**Player control pauses.**

Maevra approaches the casing first.

Does not touch it.

She looks at Cyanis.

**MAEVRA:** Dovaren.

Then Ilyra.

**MAEVRA:** Amarin.

**CYANIS:** Commander.

**ILYRA:** Commander.

Maevra looks to the custody officer.

**MAEVRA:** Tell me what happened here, not what happened on the road.

The officer gestures toward the warded-store door.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Standard sealed transfer. No attempt to open it. Three paces from Dovaren, the casing lit and he became symptomatic.

**MAEVRA:** Three exactly?

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Approximately.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

The officer raises an eyebrow.

**MAEVRA:** Good that you said approximately.

She looks at Ilyra.

**MAEVRA:** Same symptoms as the convoy?

**ILYRA:** Similar. Faster onset. We stopped before it developed as far.

**MAEVRA:** And when they brought it back?

**ILYRA:** The light stopped. His pulse settled.

Maevra looks at Cyanis.

**MAEVRA:** Anything they missed?

Cyanis thinks.

Actually thinks.

**CYANIS:** Pressure behind the eyes.

Ilyra looks at him.

**CYANIS:** You didn't ask that one yet.

**ILYRA:** I am now.

**CYANIS:** It's gone.

**ILYRA:** Good.

Maevra looks at the casing again.

**MAEVRA:** So we have an object nobody here can identify, two field incidents nobody can reproduce on purpose, and now it objects to ordinary custody.

**CYANIS:** That's one interpretation.

**MAEVRA:** Do you have a better one?

**CYANIS:** No.

**MAEVRA:** Excellent. Saves time.

She turns to the custody officer.

**MAEVRA:** It does not go into stores.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Agreed.

**MAEVRA:** It stays sealed. Nobody tests distance. Nobody tests touch. Nobody tests whether the third time is funnier.

Cyanis glances at Ilyra.

**CYANIS:** I wasn't going to suggest that.

**ILYRA:** You were thinking something adjacent.

**CYANIS:** Unprovable.

Maevra's mouth moves very slightly.

Almost a smile.

Then she is back on the problem.

**MAEVRA:** Brackenwall can't answer this.

She looks at Cyanis.

**MAEVRA:** Caelora might.

**CYANIS:** That's where it was supposed to go in the first place.

**MAEVRA:** The artifact was.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** Now you are too.

Cyanis looks at the casing.

**CYANIS:** Comforting distinction.

Ilyra folds her arms.

**ILYRA:** He's not traveling without medical observation yet.

Maevra looks at her.

**MAEVRA:** Were you planning to stay here?

**ILYRA:** No.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

No ceremony.

Decision made.

Maevra walks to a route board mounted beside the intake workspace.

The road south and east is marked in several hands and several ages of ink.

She taps Brackenwall.

Then the Briar routes.

Then Greenhollow.

**MAEVRA:** I can get us to Greenhollow.

Cyanis looks at the board.

**CYANIS:** And after that?

**MAEVRA:** After that I want Torren Harth.

Ilyra studies the routes.

**ILYRA:** Guide?

**MAEVRA:** Route specialist.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** Also a guide, when he feels like admitting that's what he's doing.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** Friend of yours?

Maevra keeps looking at the map.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

The answer is simple enough that Cyanis does not push it.

Maevra traces the route from Brackenwall toward Greenhollow.

**MAEVRA:** Briar Passage changes faster than the reports do. The Greenhollow road is usable. Deeper south, I want someone who knows what changed this week, not what somebody copied onto a board last season.

**CYANIS:** Fair.

**ILYRA:** How soon?

Maevra looks at Cyanis.

Then the sealed casing.

Then Ilyra.

**MAEVRA:** Once you've eaten.

Cyanis opens his mouth.

Ilyra turns her head toward him.

He closes it.

Maevra notices.

**MAEVRA:** Good. We're learning.

**CYANIS:** Apparently as a group.

# BEAT 2 — BRIAR PASSAGE / FIRST TRAVERSAL

### [GAMEPLAY — LEAVING BRACKENWALL]

**Player control remains active.**

The maintained ground outside Brackenwall narrows gradually rather than ending at a hard boundary.

Packed road becomes root-broken road.
The roadside ditch becomes intermittent.
Old marker stones begin appearing at uneven distances, some cleaned recently, some half-swallowed by moss.

Maevra Solmar is actively leading Cyanis Dovaren and Ilyra Amarin to Greenhollow.
She travels with them but does not join the combat formation.

The sealed Card remains with Cyanis.
It stays inert.

A short distance into the trees, the road divides around a low ridge and joins again beyond it.

Maevra takes the left side without stopping.

Cyanis follows.

**CYANIS:** Confident.

**MAEVRA:** About this part.

**CYANIS:** Reassuring qualifier.

**MAEVRA:** You'll hear worse ones before Caelora.

Ilyra looks at the parallel track through the trees.

**ILYRA:** What's the other one?

**MAEVRA:** Same road if nobody's let it rot.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** Which is why we're taking this one.

The exchange ends with the route decision.

**Player control continues.**

---

### [ROUTE STOP — FIRST MARKER]

At a weathered marker where several narrower trails peel away from the main route, Maevra stops.

One face of the stone has been cleaned recently.
Another still carries older scoring beneath lichen.

She reads the ground as much as the marker.

Recent wheel ruts bend east.
Foot traffic has worn the inside edge of the turn.
A broken branch has been cut cleanly instead of left hanging.

**CYANIS:** Greenhollow?

**MAEVRA:** East.

She points down the used road.

**ILYRA:** The marker says that too.

**MAEVRA:** Today.

Ilyra looks at her.

**MAEVRA:** Marks get moved. Roads get renamed. Somebody clears the wrong branch because their grandfather called it the old east road and now everyone gets to suffer.

Cyanis looks at the stone.

**CYANIS:** You sound personally offended.

**MAEVRA:** Roads should know what they are.

**ILYRA:** That's a difficult standard for a road.

**MAEVRA:** Yet I maintain it.

Maevra starts east again.

**MAEVRA:** This way.

**Player control resumes.**

### [GUIDED MOVEMENT — AFTER THE FIRST HOSTILE STRETCH]

The main road has fractured into several used traces around fallen timber and heavy growth.

Maevra does not stop. She chooses one of the traces and leads the party through it.

**CYANIS:** Still know where we are?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** Immediate answer. Very convincing.

**MAEVRA:** We're north of a drainage cut and west of Greenhollow.

She steps over a churned patch in the road.

Cyanis does not.

**MAEVRA:** And you're stepping in horse shit.

Cyanis looks down.
Steps sideways.

Ilyra keeps walking.

**ILYRA:** Useful local knowledge.

**CYANIS:** I see why we brought her.

Maevra points ahead where the traces converge again.

**MAEVRA:** Greenhollow gets enough traffic that people keep finding it. This is the easy half.

**CYANIS:** Easy compared to what?

As they pass an older southern trace descending through brush, Maevra indicates it without stopping.

**MAEVRA:** Deeper south, one road becomes three, three become service cuts, and half the useful markers assume you already know which hill they mean.

**ILYRA:** And Torren does?

**MAEVRA:** Torren knows which parts are still true.

The route rejoins.
The exchange ends.

**Player control continues without additional travel chatter.**

### [POST-ENCOUNTER STOP]

After a normal field fight, the party pauses inside the normal post-battle grace window.

Ilyra catches Cyanis before he starts moving again and checks a shallow cut on his forearm.

**CYANIS:** It's nothing.

**ILYRA:** It is, in fact, a cut.

**CYANIS:** A very small cut.

**ILYRA:** Good. Then this will be a very small argument.

She tightens the wrap once.
Lets go.

Cyanis flexes his hand.

**CYANIS:** Done?

**ILYRA:** Already was.

Maevra waits a few paces ahead rather than continuing without them.

**MAEVRA:** Beautiful. At this pace we might reach Greenhollow before you both die of professionalism.

**CYANIS:** She's been with us one day and she's already taking sides.

**ILYRA:** There are sides?

**MAEVRA:** Apparently.

The stop ends.
Maevra resumes leading.

**Player control returns.**

### [ROUTE STOP — SOUTHERN TURN]

Later, the eastward Greenhollow road crosses a broader old junction.

Maevra stops long enough to verify the branch.

The Greenhollow route continues through visibly used ground.
A southern road descends between thicker trees.
Its first marker still stands, but growth has crowded the path beyond it.

Cyanis looks down the southern branch.

**CYANIS:** That's our later road?

**MAEVRA:** One of them.

**CYANIS:** Looks passable.

**MAEVRA:** Passable isn't the question.

She looks at the branch points beyond the first bend.

**MAEVRA:** Which one still connects is.

Ilyra studies the older marker.

**ILYRA:** And that's where Torren earns his food.

**MAEVRA:** Among other things.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** Expensive man?

**MAEVRA:** Worse.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** He knows when he's right.

Cyanis looks at Ilyra.

**CYANIS:** I already know one of those.

Ilyra glances at the fresh wrap on his arm.

**ILYRA:** Your arm is still wrapped.

**CYANIS:** Point withdrawn.

Maevra laughs once.

Then points east.

**MAEVRA:** Greenhollow.

They move on.

**ILYRA:** No.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** That would require planning.

**CYANIS:** That was unnecessary.

**ILYRA:** Still true.

# BEAT 3 — GREENHOLLOW / TORREN

### [GAMEPLAY — GREENHOLLOW]

**Player control is active.**

Greenhollow is built around movement.

Carts arrive muddy and leave patched.
Harness hangs from repair rails.
Travelers stop at the route board long enough to read the latest corrections before choosing a road.

The board is not decorative.
Old marks have been scraped away, overwritten, crossed out, and replaced.

A broad-shouldered man stands in front of it with a small piece of chalk in one hand.

He removes one route mark.
Studies the board.
Puts it back slightly lower.

Maevra stops when she sees him.

**MAEVRA:** Torren.

He looks over.

His expression changes by very little.

Enough.

**TORREN:** Maevra.

A beat.

He looks past her at Cyanis and Ilyra.

Then at the sealed casing traveling with Cyanis.

**TORREN:** You brought work.

**MAEVRA:** I missed you too.

**TORREN:** Didn't say I didn't.

Maevra's mouth pulls sideways.

Not quite a smile.

She steps up to the board.

---

### [STORY TRIGGER — ROUTE BOARD]

**Player control pauses.**

Maevra nods toward Cyanis and Ilyra.

**MAEVRA:** Cyanis Dovaren. Ilyra Amarin.

Then to Torren:

**MAEVRA:** Torren Harth.

Torren gives them each a short nod.

**TORREN:** Heard about the convoy.

**CYANIS:** Good things?

**TORREN:** No.

Cyanis accepts that.

Torren looks back at the board.

**MAEVRA:** We need Caelora.

Torren taps the direct Greenhollow–Dunmere line.

A heavy closure mark crosses it.

**TORREN:** Not on this road.

**CYANIS:** Black Host?

**TORREN:** Enough of them.

**ILYRA:** How bad?

**TORREN:** Bad enough I wouldn't use it.

Maevra looks at the older lines running back through Briar Passage.

**MAEVRA:** South through Briar?

Torren studies the board for a moment.

**TORREN:** Parts.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Parts?

**TORREN:** I know the region. I don't know every road today.

Maevra nods once.

That is the answer she wanted.

**TORREN:** I can get you started. After that I check the ground before I promise anything.

**CYANIS:** You found your man.

**MAEVRA:** I know.

Torren gives her a look.

**TORREN:** Don't make it strange.

**MAEVRA:** You started with "you brought work."

**TORREN:** Accurate statement.

**MAEVRA:** So was mine.

Ilyra looks between them once.

Says nothing.

---

### [CARD BRIEF]

Maevra turns slightly toward the sealed casing.

**MAEVRA:** The other problem is this.

Torren looks at it.

Does not touch it.

**MAEVRA:** Recovered Card. Stayed sealed. It behaves abnormally around Dovaren.

**TORREN:** How abnormally?

**MAEVRA:** Enough that Brackenwall tried to transfer custody and moving it away made him sick.

Torren looks at Cyanis.

Then Ilyra.

**TORREN:** Can he travel?

Ilyra answers.

**ILYRA:** Yes. Monitored.

**TORREN:** Then that's what I plan for.

Cyanis raises one hand.

**CYANIS:** That's all you need?

Torren looks at the casing again.

**TORREN:** For a road?

A beat.

**TORREN:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** I like him.

**MAEVRA:** Give it time.

Torren ignores both of them and moves to the north side of the board.

---

### [HOLLOW WATCH]

A route climbs away from Greenhollow toward Hollow Watch.

Torren taps it once.

**TORREN:** I'm not taking you south yet.

Maevra does not look surprised.

**MAEVRA:** Hollow Watch.

**TORREN:** Hollow Watch.

Cyanis looks at the northern route.

**CYANIS:** What do you know?

Torren folds his arms.

**TORREN:** Black Host took the fort about a week ago.

**ILYRA:** Confirmed?

**TORREN:** Enough traffic went up. None of it looked friendly.

He taps the spur below the fort.

**TORREN:** Since then, digging gear keeps going this way.

**CYANIS:** What are they digging for?

**TORREN:** Don't know.

Immediate.

No embarrassment in it.

**MAEVRA:** Garrison?

Torren's expression hardens slightly.

**TORREN:** No reliable word.

**CYANIS:** You've been up there?

**TORREN:** No.

A beat.

**TORREN:** I've watched the road. Going closer alone would've been stupid.

Cyanis nods.

No challenge.

**MAEVRA:** And you're not leaving until you know more.

**TORREN:** Correct.

**MAEVRA:** Even with Caelora waiting.

**TORREN:** Caelora will still be there tomorrow.

He looks at the Hollow Watch mark.

**TORREN:** I'm not walking south while that sits north of Greenhollow and I don't know what they're doing there.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** You want to take the fort back?

Torren shakes his head.

**TORREN:** I want eyes on it.

Then:

**TORREN:** If we can clear it, we clear it. If we can't, we come back knowing why.

Cyanis gives him a small nod.

**CYANIS:** Good answer.

**TORREN:** Usually is.

Maevra turns her head toward him.

**TORREN:** Usually.

That satisfies her.

---

### [DECISION]

Maevra looks to Cyanis and Ilyra.

**MAEVRA:** I'm going.

**CYANIS:** Same.

Ilyra looks toward the north route, then at Cyanis.

**ILYRA:** If he goes, I go.

Cyanis looks at her.

**ILYRA:** Medical observation.

**CYANIS:** I wasn't arguing.

**ILYRA:** You were about to.

**CYANIS:** Apparently everyone can do this now.

Torren looks between them.

**TORREN:** Do what?

**MAEVRA:** Tell him what he's about to say.

**TORREN:** Useful skill.

**CYANIS:** I'm starting to dislike Greenhollow.

**TORREN:** You've been here five minutes.

**CYANIS:** Strong start.

Torren takes a small field case from beneath the route board and slings it at his hip.

He checks the fastening on his bow without looking down.

Then turns the Hollow Watch route marker so its warning face is visible to anyone approaching the board.

**TORREN:** We don't take the obvious road up.

**MAEVRA:** Of course we don't.

**TORREN:** Host owns the fort. No reason to walk up and introduce ourselves.

He looks to Cyanis and Ilyra.

**TORREN:** When you're ready, meet me at the north edge.

**CYANIS:** You leading?

**TORREN:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** Good. I was hoping one of us knew where we were going.

**TORREN:** I know the first part.

Cyanis pauses.

**CYANIS:** Somehow that's more reassuring.

**TORREN:** Should be.

Maevra starts away from the board.

Torren watches her go for half a beat.

Then looks back at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** She still do that?

**CYANIS:** I've known her about a day.

Torren considers this.

**TORREN:** Right.

A beat.

**TORREN:** You'll catch up.
