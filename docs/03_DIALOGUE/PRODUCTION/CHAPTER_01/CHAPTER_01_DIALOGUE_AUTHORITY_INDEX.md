## Maevra / Torren Chapter-1 reveal boundary

The full Maevra/Torren history may exist inside their character brains, but **Chapter 1 dialogue is not allowed to expose it yet**.

Player-facing Chapter-1 read:
- they have obviously known one another for a long time;
- they argue with practiced, old-married-couple ease;
- it may initially seem as though they do not get along particularly well;
- both are stubborn: Maevra around duty/responsibility, Torren around ground truth/practical judgment;

Do **not** reveal in Chapter 1:
- the exact duration of their history;
- their enlistment ages;
- that they were lovers or romantically involved;
- sexual history;
- breakup history;
- repeated separation/reunion history;
- current romantic status;
- Torren's private romantic feelings.

Canonical C04 `Not Professionally` may reveal only a little more: Torren is more than an ordinary colleague to Maevra and he matters to her personally. That is the chapter ceiling.

## Person-Brain interpretation

The performance pass does not mean making every speaker longer.

- Cyanis can think through a full field judgment when pressure allows it instead of feeding one clue per turn.
- Ilyra's turn ownership comes from the whole character, not her job. She may finish a medical consequence when one truly belongs to her, but medicine is not her default conversational function.
- Torren remains comparatively sparse, but routes, terrain, maps, weather, animals, field maintenance, and practical efficiency can naturally pull much longer turns out of him.
- Maevra remains socially quick, argumentative, warm, mischievous, and competent rather than functioning only as command exposition.
- silence remains valid when the scene earns it, especially around death, danger, and discoveries that do not need immediate interpretation.
- interruptions, callbacks, unfinished thoughts, profanity, and longer floor-holding should arise from character behavior rather than being added mechanically.
