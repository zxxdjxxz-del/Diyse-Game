## Maevra / Torren Chapter-1 reveal boundary

The full Maevra/Torren history may exist inside their character brains, but **Chapter 1 dialogue is not allowed to expose it yet**.

Player-facing Chapter-1 read:
- they have obviously known one another for a long time;
- they argue with practiced, old-married-couple ease;
- it may initially seem as though they do not get along particularly well;
- both are stubborn: Maevra around duty/responsibility, Torren around ground truth/practical judgment;
- they know one another's habits and likely reactions unusually well.

Do **not** reveal in Chapter 1:
- the exact duration of their history;
- their enlistment ages;
- that they were lovers or romantically involved;
- sexual history;
- breakup history;
- repeated separation/reunion history;
- current romantic status;
- Torren's private romantic feelings.

Canonical C04 `Not Professionally` may reveal only a little more: Torren is more than an ordinary colleague to Maevra and he matters to her personally. That is the chapter ceiling.

Beat 11's stale explicit `twenty-five years` exchange has been removed. `All this time` preserves the long-history feel without disclosing the chronology.
