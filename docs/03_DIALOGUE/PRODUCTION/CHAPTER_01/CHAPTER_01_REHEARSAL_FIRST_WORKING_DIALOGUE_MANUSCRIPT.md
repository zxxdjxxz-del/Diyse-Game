# BEAT 1 — BRACKENWALL / PROTOCOL
## Rehearsal-first Draft C — awaiting user approval

### [GAMEPLAY — BRACKENWALL ARRIVAL]

**Player control is active.**

Brackenwall is working.

Convoy survivors are being assigned bunks. A cracked wagon wheel has been leaned against a wall with three different chalk marks on it. Somebody is boiling something that smells better than it looks.

Cyanis Dovaren and Ilyra Amarin enter through the inner gate under escort.

Cyanis is carrying the recovered Card itself. Its protective recovery casing broke during the Chapter-0 final boss fight and is gone.

The Card is dark.

A custody officer waits beside a reinforced intake table with an open ledger and a cup that has gone cold.

---

### [STORY TRIGGER — FORMAL INTAKE]

**Player control pauses.**

The custody officer nods at the Card.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** That's it?

**CYANIS:** That's it.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** This was inside the recovery casing?

**CYANIS:** Until the fight.

**ILYRA:** Card survived. Casing didn't.

The officer looks at the Card again.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Better than the other way around.

**CYANIS:** Considerably.

The officer opens the ledger.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** What did it do?

Ilyra leans one hip against the table.

**ILYRA:** Green and gold light. Geometry. It held damaged structure together around us for a few seconds.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Around the Card?

Ilyra points at Cyanis.

**ILYRA:** Him.

The officer looks at Cyanis.

**CYANIS:** I wasn't helping.

**ILYRA:** You were present.

**CYANIS:** I did that very well.

The officer writes.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** And afterward?

**ILYRA:** Bad pulse. Tremor. Dizzy.

**CYANIS:** Briefly.

Ilyra looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Mostly briefly.

The officer keeps writing.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Anything now?

Cyanis flexes one hand.

**CYANIS:** Head's clear.

Ilyra does not contradict him.

That is enough to make him glance at her anyway.

The custody officer closes the ledger halfway.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Fine. Warded store until Caelora decides what to do with it.

**CYANIS:** Works for me.

The officer gestures to the table.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Set it down.

Cyanis does.

Nothing happens.

The officer waits a moment, then picks up the Card and starts toward the interior door.

One pace.

Two.

Cyanis's hand catches the edge of the table.

Ilyra sees him before he says anything.

**ILYRA:** Stop.

The officer stops.

A thin green-gold line wakes along the Card's face.

Another line reaches from it across the floor toward Cyanis.

Cyanis shuts his eyes for a second.

Ilyra is already at his wrist.

**CYANIS:** Don't.

**ILYRA:** I'm counting.

**CYANIS:** That's somehow worse.

His pulse jumps beneath her fingers.

The line brightens.

The custody officer looks from Cyanis to the Card in his hand.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Coming back.

He reverses course slowly.

The green line retracts as the Card comes closer.

Cyanis breathes out through his nose.

The officer sets the Card back on the intake table.

Dark again.

Ilyra keeps hold of Cyanis's wrist for two more beats.

Then lets go.

The custody officer stares at the warded-store door.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Right.

He pulls the ledger back toward himself.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** That's finished.

**CYANIS:** Good.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Wasn't asking.

**CYANIS:** Still good.

The officer points at a runner.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Get Solmar.

The runner goes.

---

### [GAMEPLAY — BRACKENWALL INTAKE HOLD]

**Player control returns briefly.**

The Card stays on the intake table under guard.

Cyanis is free to move around the immediate yard, but the Card does not leave the intake area.

Ilyra does not hover. She does, however, hand Cyanis a cup of water without asking.

He takes it.

Around them, Brackenwall keeps working:
- a quartermaster argues over missing harness;
- two convoy survivors are shown toward sleeping space;
- someone tries to roll the marked wagon wheel away and is immediately shouted back;
- nobody forms a crowd around the Card.

At the progression point, Maevra Solmar enters with the custody officer.

---

### [STORY TRIGGER — MAEVRA]

Maevra walks straight to the intake table.

She looks at the Card.

Then Cyanis.

**MAEVRA:** Dovaren.

**CYANIS:** Commander.

She looks at Ilyra.

**MAEVRA:** Amarin.

**ILYRA:** Commander.

The custody officer points toward the warded-store door.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Tried to take the Card inside. He went pale, it lit. Brought it back, both stopped.

Maevra looks at Cyanis.

**MAEVRA:** How bad?

Cyanis opens his mouth.

**ILYRA:** Bad enough.

Cyanis turns to her.

**CYANIS:** I am standing here.

**ILYRA:** I know.

Maevra looks between them.

**MAEVRA:** Any reason to try it again?

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** No.

**ILYRA:** No.

**CYANIS:** Very much no.

**MAEVRA:** Great. Love consensus.

She pulls the ledger closer and reads the last few lines.

**MAEVRA:** It stays with Dovaren for now. Nobody gets curious with the distance.

The custody officer nods.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Fine by me.

Maevra shuts the ledger.

**MAEVRA:** Brackenwall isn't equipped for this.

**CYANIS:** Caelora.

**MAEVRA:** Caelora.

He looks at the Card.

**CYANIS:** That was the plan before it developed opinions.

**MAEVRA:** Now the plan has supervision.

**CYANIS:** Mine or its?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

Ilyra almost smiles.

Cyanis sees it.

**CYANIS:** That's not helpful.

**ILYRA:** I wasn't helping.

Maevra turns toward the route board mounted beside the intake workspace.

**MAEVRA:** We don't go straight from here.

Cyanis joins her at the board.

**CYANIS:** Why not?

**MAEVRA:** Because I can get us to Greenhollow without gambling.

She taps the eastern route.

Then the older southern lines beyond it.

**MAEVRA:** After Greenhollow, I want Torren Harth.

**ILYRA:** Guide?

Maevra thinks about the word.

**MAEVRA:** When he's being paid, yes.

**CYANIS:** And when he isn't?

**MAEVRA:** Annoyingly similar.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** You trust him.

Maevra keeps her eyes on the map.

**MAEVRA:** With roads.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** Most other things too.

She moves on before Cyanis can decide whether that was an invitation.

**MAEVRA:** Briar changes. Reports don't keep up. Greenhollow first, then Torren, then south.

Ilyra looks at Cyanis.

**ILYRA:** You're traveling?

**CYANIS:** Apparently under supervision.

**ILYRA:** Good.

**CYANIS:** You were coming anyway.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

There is nothing dramatic in it.

Maevra straightens the route sheet she has just knocked crooked.

Then notices Cyanis is still holding the untouched cup of water.

**MAEVRA:** Drink that.

Cyanis looks down at it.

**CYANIS:** This is becoming a hostile workplace.

**ILYRA:** Drink.

He does.

Maevra points toward the mess hall with two fingers.

**MAEVRA:** Eat too. We leave after.

Cyanis starts to say something.

Ilyra looks at him.

He thinks better of it.

Maevra is already walking away.

**CYANIS:** I didn't say anything.

**ILYRA:** I know.

She takes her own cup and follows.

# BEAT 2 — BRIAR PASSAGE / FIRST TRAVERSAL
## Rehearsal-first Draft C — awaiting user approval

**Walking-dialogue lock:** limited moving dialogue is legal here only while Maevra Solmar is genuinely leading Cyanis and Ilyra to Greenhollow. Ordinary traversal remains gameplay and silence.

### [GAMEPLAY — LEAVING BRACKENWALL]

**Player control remains active.**

Brackenwall's maintained road thins gradually into Briar Passage.

Packed earth gives way to roots and older repair stone. Ditches appear, vanish beneath growth, then return. Marker stones stand at uneven distances and in visibly different states of maintenance.

Maevra leads.

Cyanis carries the recovered Card itself. It remains inert.

A short distance into the trees, the road divides around a low ridge and rejoins beyond it.

Maevra takes the left trace without slowing.

Cyanis looks at the right one.

**CYANIS:** Not even considering it?

**MAEVRA:** Considered it yesterday.

**CYANIS:** And today?

**MAEVRA:** Still has a tree across it.

Ilyra looks through the brush.

There is, in fact, a tree across it.

**ILYRA:** Strong case.

Cyanis follows Maevra.

The conversation ends with the route choice.

---

### [ROUTE STOP — FIRST MARKER]

At a weathered marker where several narrower trails peel away, Maevra stops.

She looks at the road before she looks at the stone.

Recent wheel ruts bend east. The inner edge of the turn is worn by foot traffic. Someone has cut a hanging branch cleanly within the last few days.

**CYANIS:** Greenhollow?

Maevra points east.

**MAEVRA:** That way.

Ilyra reads the marker.

**ILYRA:** Stone agrees.

**MAEVRA:** Good for it.

She rubs moss away from an older score beneath the newer route mark.

**MAEVRA:** Last time I came through, somebody had turned this face around.

**CYANIS:** Accident?

**MAEVRA:** He swore his grandfather called the other road east.

Ilyra looks at the two roads.

**ILYRA:** Was his grandfather right?

Maevra stares at her.

**MAEVRA:** That's not the point.

Cyanis smiles.

**CYANIS:** It absolutely is now.

Maevra points east again.

**MAEVRA:** Roads should know what they are.

**ILYRA:** Roads are famously difficult that way.

Maevra gives her a betrayed look.

Then starts moving.

**MAEVRA:** Both of you can walk east in silence.

They do.

For a while.

---

### [HOSTILE FIELD GAMEPLAY]

Normal Briar Passage exploration resumes.

- Random encounter pressure is active.
- Combat party: **Cyanis + Ilyra**.
- Maevra remains with the traveling group but is never represented in battle.
- Side paths may contain ordinary exploration rewards, route history, or lived-world details.
- No major Ancient-story discovery occurs here.

No general walk-and-talk layer is used.

---

### [GUIDED MOVEMENT — FRACTURED ROAD]

After the first hostile stretch, the maintained road breaks around fallen timber into several equally plausible traces.

Maevra chooses one and keeps moving.

Cyanis follows for a few steps.

**CYANIS:** Still Greenhollow?

**MAEVRA:** Unless somebody moved it.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** That sounded less reassuring than you meant it to.

**MAEVRA:** It sounded exactly right.

She steps over a churned patch.

Cyanis steps directly into it.

Maevra glances down.

**MAEVRA:** Horse shit.

Cyanis freezes.

Ilyra keeps walking.

**CYANIS:** You could've led with that.

**MAEVRA:** I did. With my feet.

Ilyra looks back once.

**ILYRA:** Very clear demonstration.

Cyanis scrapes his boot against a root and catches up.

A few yards later an older southern trace appears through the brush, then breaks into several smaller cuts.

Maevra points at it while they pass.

**MAEVRA:** That's why I want Torren.

**CYANIS:** Because of the horse shit?

Maevra looks at him.

**MAEVRA:** Because he knows which bad road is still a road.

Ilyra looks down the disappearing traces.

**ILYRA:** Useful distinction.

**MAEVRA:** Very.

The traces converge again. The exchange ends.

---

### [GAMEPLAY — BRIAR NETWORK]

A substantial uninterrupted exploration section follows.

The route should read as a network rather than a corridor:
- old service cuts crossing the main road;
- elevation changes;
- marker stones from different periods;
- former road beds half-reclaimed by roots;
- side paths still maintained for local use;
- small repair piles and trimmed growth showing that people still work this landscape unevenly.

Do not restore the false trail, route-choice puzzle, damaged crossing, separate Old Waystone, or early Briarhide confrontation.

---

### [POST-ENCOUNTER STOP]

After a normal field fight, the group stops inside the post-battle grace window.

Cyanis is already checking the road ahead when Ilyra catches his forearm.

**CYANIS:** I'm fine.

She turns his arm enough to expose a shallow cut.

**ILYRA:** Didn't ask.

**CYANIS:** It's tiny.

Ilyra takes out a short wrap.

**ILYRA:** Excellent. Hold still for a tiny amount of time.

Cyanis does.

Maevra is several paces ahead looking at a marker and leaves them to it.

Ilyra ties the wrap.

**CYANIS:** Done?

She has already put the rest of the bandage away.

**ILYRA:** Keep up.

Cyanis looks at the wrapped arm.

**CYANIS:** Brutal bedside manner.

**ILYRA:** We're in a forest.

She starts after Maevra.

Cyanis follows.

---

### [ROUTE STOP — SOUTHERN TURN]

Later, the Greenhollow road crosses a broader old junction.

The eastward route is visibly used. A southern road descends between thicker trees, then fragments beyond the first bend.

Maevra stops and looks down it.

Cyanis follows her gaze.

**CYANIS:** Later?

**MAEVRA:** Later.

**CYANIS:** Torren?

**MAEVRA:** Torren.

Ilyra studies the older marker at the mouth of the southern road.

**ILYRA:** You really don't want to take it without him.

**MAEVRA:** I really don't want to spend half a day proving I chose the wrong branch.

Cyanis looks at the disappearing road.

**CYANIS:** Sensible.

Maevra turns toward him.

**MAEVRA:** Don't sound surprised.

**CYANIS:** I was being sincere.

**MAEVRA:** Worse.

Ilyra starts east.

**ILYRA:** Greenhollow?

Maevra points after her.

**MAEVRA:** See? She listens.

**CYANIS:** This has become unnecessarily personal.

Maevra walks east.

**MAEVRA:** Keep up.

---

### [FINAL APPROACH — GAMEPLAY]

The forest opens gradually.

Cart traffic becomes heavier. Cut brush and small repair piles appear beside the road. The first Greenhollow structures show between the trees.

No conversation is required.

Let the settlement arrive visually.

---

### [ENTRY THRESHOLD — GREENHOLLOW]

At the settlement edge, Maevra finally stops.

**MAEVRA:** Greenhollow.

Cyanis looks at the first cluster of workshops, carts, route boards, and people moving through them.

**CYANIS:** That's it?

Maevra looks at him.

**MAEVRA:** Were you expecting trumpets?

**CYANIS:** A sign would've been nice.

Ilyra points to a large painted board ten feet away.

`GREENHOLLOW` is written across it.

Cyanis looks at the board.

Then at Ilyra.

She says nothing.

**CYANIS:** I meant earlier.

Maevra starts into town.

**MAEVRA:** Of course you did.

Cyanis follows, muttering something that does not need a subtitle.

# BEAT 3 — GREENHOLLOW / TORREN
## Rehearsal-first Draft C — awaiting user approval

### [GAMEPLAY — GREENHOLLOW]

**Player control is active.**

Greenhollow is busy in the unremarkable way useful places are busy.

A wheelwright has half a cart lifted on blocks. Two travelers are arguing over whether a red chalk mark means closed or merely unpleasant. Somebody carries a crate through the argument without asking either of them to move.

The route board is covered in fresh corrections layered over older ones.

A broad-shouldered man stands in front of it with chalk between his fingers.

He wipes out one line.

Looks at the board.

Looks past it toward the road outside town.

Draws the line back differently.

Maevra stops.

For the first time since Brackenwall, she looks pleased before she says anything.

**MAEVRA:** Torren.

He turns.

The change in his face is small and immediate.

**TORREN:** Maevra.

She walks up to him.

He looks at Cyanis and Ilyra behind her, then at the Card Cyanis is carrying.

**TORREN:** You brought trouble.

**MAEVRA:** I brought three people.

Torren looks at the Card again.

**TORREN:** Four things, then.

Maevra laughs once through her nose.

**MAEVRA:** There he is.

Torren puts the chalk down on the board ledge.

---

### [STORY TRIGGER — ROUTE BOARD]

**Player control pauses.**

Maevra gestures between them.

**MAEVRA:** Cyanis Dovaren. Ilyra Amarin.

Then, almost unnecessarily:

**MAEVRA:** Torren Harth.

Torren gives Cyanis and Ilyra a nod.

**TORREN:** Heard about the convoy.

**CYANIS:** We made an impression.

**TORREN:** On several wagons.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** That's cold.

**TORREN:** They were burning when I heard about them.

A beat.

Ilyra looks away so nobody can accuse her of smiling.

Maevra points at the board.

**MAEVRA:** We need Caelora.

Torren takes the chalk back up and draws a short mark over the direct road southeast.

**TORREN:** Not that way.

**CYANIS:** Host?

**TORREN:** Road's theirs right now.

Maevra leans closer to the board.

**MAEVRA:** Dunmere still reachable through Briar?

Torren runs one finger along several older route lines without answering immediately.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

Then he taps two places where the route markings thin out.

**TORREN:** Probably here. Maybe here.

Maevra looks at him.

**MAEVRA:** You're selling it beautifully.

**TORREN:** You came to me.

**MAEVRA:** I did.

He gives her the chalk.

**TORREN:** Then stop complaining.

She takes it.

Cyanis watches the exchange.

**CYANIS:** You two always like this?

**MAEVRA:** Worse when we're tired.

**TORREN:** She's tired now.

Maevra turns toward him.

**MAEVRA:** Careful.

Torren's expression does not change.

**TORREN:** See?

Ilyra has moved to the side of the board and is reading the current closure notes.

**ILYRA:** How recent is this one?

Torren looks where she points.

**TORREN:** This morning.

**ILYRA:** Yours?

**TORREN:** No. Miller came in from the south before breakfast.

He points to another mark.

**TORREN:** That one's mine.

**CYANIS:** Yesterday?

**TORREN:** Three days.

Cyanis looks at the dense board again.

**CYANIS:** So the map's mostly an argument.

**TORREN:** Good maps are.

---

### [THE CARD]

Maevra hands Torren the chalk back, then nods toward Cyanis.

**MAEVRA:** There's another complication.

Torren looks at the Card.

**TORREN:** Guessed.

**CYANIS:** Rude.

**TORREN:** It's glowing ancient metal in your hand.

The Card is not glowing.

Cyanis looks down at it.

Then back at Torren.

**CYANIS:** It isn't glowing.

Torren looks at the Card.

**TORREN:** Hm.

**CYANIS:** You don't get to win that anyway.

Maevra cuts in before the argument can improve itself.

**MAEVRA:** Recovered Card. Abnormal response around him. Brackenwall tried moving it into storage. He got sick.

Torren looks at Ilyra.

Not because she is standing nearest. Because she is the Warden.

**TORREN:** He good to travel?

**ILYRA:** Yes.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** If he stops pretending small things don't count.

**CYANIS:** That condition was added later.

Torren nods once.

**TORREN:** Fine.

**CYANIS:** That's it?

**TORREN:** You want me to diagnose the old metal?

**CYANIS:** No.

**TORREN:** Then that's it.

Maevra's eyes flick to Cyanis as if to say *this is why I came here.*

She does not say it.

---

### [HOLLOW WATCH]

Torren steps around the board and points north.

A smaller road climbs away from Greenhollow toward Hollow Watch.

**TORREN:** Before south, we go up there.

Maevra follows his hand.

**MAEVRA:** Hollow Watch.

Torren nods.

The ease drops out of him.

**CYANIS:** What's happened?

**TORREN:** Host took it last week.

A cart rattles past behind them.

Nobody in the scene looks at it.

**TORREN:** Been sending digging gear up since.

**MAEVRA:** Garrison?

Torren looks at the road instead of her.

**TORREN:** Haven't seen anybody come down.

That sits for a moment.

**ILYRA:** You went up?

**TORREN:** Far as the lower bend.

**MAEVRA:** Alone?

**TORREN:** Obviously.

Maevra stares at him.

**TORREN:** I came back.

**MAEVRA:** That was not the part I objected to.

**TORREN:** I know.

Cyanis looks toward the northern road.

**CYANIS:** And you're staying here until somebody checks it.

Torren looks at him.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** All right.

Torren studies him for half a second, perhaps expecting more.

There isn't any.

**MAEVRA:** That's it?

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** He has a fort full of Host soldiers uphill from his town.

Torren's eyebrow moves slightly.

**CYANIS:** I'd want it checked too.

**MAEVRA:** I wasn't disagreeing.

**CYANIS:** You sounded disappointed.

**MAEVRA:** I expected at least one argument.

**TORREN:** Give him time.

Ilyra looks at Cyanis.

**ILYRA:** He'll recover.

Cyanis exhales.

**CYANIS:** Excellent. Everyone's settled in.

The humor dies when Torren looks north again.

**TORREN:** Road up is watched.

He takes a small field case from beneath the board.

Checks the strap.

**TORREN:** There's another way to the ridge.

**MAEVRA:** You taking us?

**TORREN:** That's the idea.

**CYANIS:** You fight too, or are we carrying you when this goes badly?

Torren looks at the bow on his back.

Then at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** You can try.

Cyanis grins despite himself.

**CYANIS:** Good enough.

---

### [DEPARTURE SETUP]

Torren turns the Hollow Watch marker so the warning face points outward toward incoming travelers.

A local route worker passing behind him notices and stops.

**ROUTE WORKER:** You're going up?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

The worker looks at Maevra, Cyanis, and Ilyra.

**ROUTE WORKER:** With them?

**TORREN:** That's usually how groups work.

**ROUTE WORKER:** That's not what I meant.

Torren picks up the chalk again and presses it into the worker's hand.

**TORREN:** If Miller comes back, ask about the wash south of marker twelve.

The worker's face says this is not the answer he wanted.

**ROUTE WORKER:** Torren.

Torren is already fastening his field case.

**TORREN:** Ask him.

A beat.

The worker gives up.

**ROUTE WORKER:** Fine.

Torren looks at Maevra.

**TORREN:** Need anything before we go?

**MAEVRA:** Food.

**TORREN:** You ate.

**MAEVRA:** I can need food twice.

**TORREN:** That's not how need works.

**MAEVRA:** Twenty-five years and you're still wrong about this.

Torren looks at Cyanis and Ilyra.

**TORREN:** North edge. Ten minutes.

**CYANIS:** Is she getting food?

**TORREN:** Yes.

**MAEVRA:** See?

Torren walks away before she can enjoy it.

Ilyra watches him go.

**ILYRA:** I understand why you wanted him.

Maevra looks pleased again.

**MAEVRA:** Don't tell him that.

**CYANIS:** I was going to.

**MAEVRA:** Especially you.
