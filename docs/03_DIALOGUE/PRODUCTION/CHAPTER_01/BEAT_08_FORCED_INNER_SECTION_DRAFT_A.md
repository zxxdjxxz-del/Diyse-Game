### [STORY STOP — FORCED DOOR]

When the player reaches the damaged fitted doorway, the party stops.

Cyanis looks at the intact center of the door.

Then at the hole beside it.

**CYANIS:** They didn't open it.

**MAEVRA:** No.

Torren looks through the broken wall beside the frame.

**TORREN:** Went around it.

Ilyra looks at the damage.

**ILYRA:** Subtle.

Cyanis glances at her.

**CYANIS:** I think we've established subtle isn't their thing.

Maevra looks through the breach.

**MAEVRA:** They got through.

That ends the discussion.

No one explains what the machinery in the wall does.

**Player control returns.**

### [OPTIONAL POST-RANDOM-ENCOUNTER BARK]

This exchange is optional. It may fire after an ordinary random encounter in the protected inner section. The story does not require the player to hear it.

Ilyra looks over Cyanis and Torren before checking herself.

Cyanis notices.

**CYANIS:** You first.

Ilyra looks at him.

**ILYRA:** I'm standing.

**CYANIS:** So am I.

**ILYRA:** Unfortunately, that has never been your standard.

Torren checks himself.

**TORREN:** She's got you there.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** I liked you better before the fort.

**TORREN:** You liked me for about six minutes.

Maevra looks between them.

**MAEVRA:** Finished?

**ILYRA:** Nearly.

Cyanis looks at Torren.

**CYANIS:** You good?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** Good. Apparently I ask now.

Ilyra gives him a look.

Cyanis catches it.

**CYANIS:** Don't enjoy this.

She does not answer.

The bark ends there and control remains with the player.

### [THRESHOLD STOP — CASTELLAN CHAMBER]

The party stops at the chamber entrance.

The room beyond is substantially more intact than the forced route behind them.

A large shape is integrated into the far architecture.

Do not identify it yet.

The Black Host route ends here.

Cyanis looks into the chamber.

**CYANIS:** This where they stopped?

Maevra looks at the lamps and damage ending at the threshold.

**MAEVRA:** Looks like it.

Torren checks the first stretch of floor.

**TORREN:** Floor's good.

Cyanis looks at the massive shape in the far wall.

**CYANIS:** Reassuring.

**TORREN:** Didn't say the room was.

Ilyra adjusts her grip on her Wardrod.

**ILYRA:** Anything moving?

Cyanis watches the far structure.

**CYANIS:** Not yet.

Maevra shifts behind the combat line.

**MAEVRA:** Get ready before it does.

Cyanis brings his shield forward.

Torren nocks an arrow.

Ilyra settles beside Cyanis.

No one mentions the Card.

Nothing activates yet.

The beat ends before the party crosses far enough into the chamber to trigger the Castellan.

**Knowledge:** nobody identifies the machinery, protected chamber, Castellan, Host objective, or later mural information early.

**Walking dialogue:** none. Nobody knows this route well enough to be an active guide.

**Reductive production:** reuse the existing protected-inner corridor/chamber kit, one damaged-door/wall variant, existing Black Host lamps/braces, ordinary encounter assets, B00 blocking, portraits, and restrained camera/light/sound. No ordinary on-map enemy staging, aid station, special barricade room, bespoke machinery set piece, extra clue props, or one-use cinematic build is required.
