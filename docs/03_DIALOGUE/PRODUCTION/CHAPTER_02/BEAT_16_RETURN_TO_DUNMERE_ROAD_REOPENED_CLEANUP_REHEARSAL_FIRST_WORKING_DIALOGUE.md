## [BACK IN DUNMERE]

Dunmere is functioning around the return rather than staging a formal reception. Rescued people are being reunited, seated, fed, checked, or helped farther into town as needed.

The elder meets Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren, and Maevra once the immediate movement has settled enough to speak.

**ELDER:** Everyone made it back?

Ilyra looks toward the rescued group.

**ILYRA:** Everyone we brought out.

The elder hears the distinction.

**ELDER:** Everyone you brought out.

Cyanis nods.

**CYANIS:** There were others before them.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** They'd already been moved.

**ELDER:** Where?

**CYANIS:** We don't know.

**ILYRA:** The prisoners didn't know either.

The elder's expression hardens, but does not turn into a speech.

**ELDER:** So they're still missing.

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

---

## [WHAT HAPPENED AT THE BASTION]

**ELDER:** And the Old Bastion?

Torren answers first.

**TORREN:** Host pulled out.

**ELDER:** Completely?

**TORREN:** Organized force did. Rhazek ordered the withdrawal.

The elder looks at Cyanis.

**ELDER:** Rhazek was there himself?

**CYANIS:** Commanding it.

**MAEVRA:** The kidnappings too.

**CYANIS:** He admitted the order was his.

A beat.

**ELDER:** Is he dead?

**CYANIS:** No.

**CYANIS:** We beat him. He withdrew with what was left of his people.

The elder absorbs that without treating survival as failure.

**ELDER:** But he lost the fort.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** Here, he lost.

The elder nods once.

**ELDER:** Good enough for the road.

---

## [THE ROAD]

The elder looks toward the route out of town.

**ELDER:** People are already asking if they can move again.

Torren answers carefully.

**TORREN:** Bastion isn't controlling the corridor anymore. Gate's open. Their checkpoints are gone.

**ELDER:** Greenhollow road?

**TORREN:** Usable.

A beat.

**TORREN:** Normal caution. But yeah.

The elder nods.

**ELDER:** Then we reopen it.

Maevra looks toward the waiting wagons that had been stalled when the group first arrived.

**MAEVRA:** They'll like that.

Cyanis follows her look.

**CYANIS:** I like that.

Torren glances at him.

**TORREN:** Food again?

**CYANIS:** Among other things.

Ilyra looks at Cyanis.

**ILYRA:** Mostly food.

**CYANIS:** I've had a difficult day.

Maevra smiles.

**MAEVRA:** Heroically hungry.

**CYANIS:** Exactly.

The humor is brief and ordinary. It does not erase the unresolved missing people.

---

## [NOT THE END OF IT]

The elder looks back to the group.

**ELDER:** You said the Bastion wasn't where they kept everyone.

Torren shakes his head.

**TORREN:** Holding point.

**CYANIS:** People came in. Some got moved somewhere else.

**ELDER:** And Rhazek wouldn't say where.

**CYANIS:** No.

A short silence.

**ILYRA:** We got the people who were still there.

**MAEVRA:** And opened the road.

Cyanis looks toward the rescued townspeople.

**CYANIS:** The rest doesn't disappear because we did that.

**ELDER:** No.

A beat.

**ELDER:** But neither does this.

Cyanis looks at them.

The elder does not turn the moment into a ceremony.

**ELDER:** Get some food. Get dry. Sleep if you're capable of it.

Torren looks at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** He won't be.

**CYANIS:** I can sleep.

**ILYRA:** Eventually.

**MAEVRA:** After he remembers three more things he needs to do first.

Cyanis looks between them.

**CYANIS:** I hate all of you.

**TORREN:** No you don't.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Unfortunately.
