# BEAT 2 — DUNMERE ARRIVAL / MISSING TRAVELERS

**Draft:** A — current rehearsal-first working dialogue

### [ARRIVAL]

Dunmere is functioning. Shops are open, people are working, and ordinary town life continues. The first thing the group sees is not a disaster scene.

The party enters the town proper.

**CYANIS:** Food?

**ILYRA:** That was fast.

**CYANIS:** I've been thinking about it for miles.

**MAEVRA:** He has.

**TORREN:** Inn's farther in.

**CYANIS:** See? Useful town already.

Torren slows.

A number of loaded wagons are standing where outbound traffic would normally be moving through. Teamsters are tending animals, shifting cargo, or waiting rather than leaving.

**TORREN:** Hm.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** That's never reassuring.

**TORREN:** No.

Maevra follows his attention to the wagons.

**MAEVRA:** Loaded.

**ILYRA:** And staying loaded.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

A nearby **CARTER** hears them.

**CARTER:** If you're waiting on the road, don't.

**CYANIS:** We weren't.

**CARTER:** Good. Saves time.

**MAEVRA:** What's wrong with it?

The carter gives her a tired look.

**CARTER:** Depends how badly you want to get where you're going.

**TORREN:** Caelora traffic?

**CARTER:** Mostly stopped.

**CYANIS:** Stopped by what?

**CARTER:** People leave. People don't arrive.

The easy humor drops out of the group.

**ILYRA:** How long?

**CARTER:** Long enough that I'm not taking another wagon out there.

**CYANIS:** Bandits?

**CARTER:** Wish it was.

A voice from nearby:

**RETURNED GUARD:** They're not missing.

The group turns.

A road guard is sitting near one of the wagons. He is bruised and scraped but already bandaged; nothing requires Ilyra to seize control of his care.

**CARTER:** You should be lying down.

**RETURNED GUARD:** Did that.

**ILYRA:** You saw what happened?

The guard looks at her, then at the others.

**RETURNED GUARD:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** Who took them?

**RETURNED GUARD:** Black Host.

A beat.

**MAEVRA:** Where?

**RETURNED GUARD:** The Old Bastion.

Torren's expression hardens slightly.

**TORREN:** Bastion's occupied?

**RETURNED GUARD:** Has been for a while. Wasn't stopping everybody before.

**MAEVRA:** It is now.

The guard nods.

**RETURNED GUARD:** They came out fast. Didn't want the wagon. Didn't want the cargo.

**CYANIS:** People.

**RETURNED GUARD:** Yeah.

A beat.

**RETURNED GUARD:** Took the others. I got clear.

Nobody congratulates him for surviving and nobody asks him to justify it.

**ILYRA:** You get hit in the head?

**RETURNED GUARD:** No.

**ILYRA:** Lose consciousness?

**RETURNED GUARD:** No.

Ilyra gives him a short nod and leaves it there.

**CARTER:** He's not the only one who came back with the same story.

**CYANIS:** And people who didn't come back?

The carter looks toward the waiting wagons.

**CARTER:** That's why we're waiting.

### [WHAT THE BASTION MEANS]

The party steps slightly aside from the wagon traffic. This is a short authored stop inside a functioning town, not a long strategic council.

**CYANIS:** Old Bastion.

**TORREN:** Old fort on the corridor.

**CYANIS:** Controls the road?

**TORREN:** If somebody holds it properly, yeah.

**MAEVRA:** Black Host does.

**TORREN:** Sounds like it.

**ILYRA:** And they're taking travelers, not supplies.

**MAEVRA:** Deliberately.

Cyanis looks back toward the returned guard and the wagons that are not leaving.

**CYANIS:** So we're not continuing through that.

**TORREN:** Wasn't going to recommend it.

**CYANIS:** Good.

A beat.

**CYANIS:** Who knows the Bastion best from this side?

The carter answers without being drawn into the group's private discussion.

**CARTER:** Elder's been collecting everything people bring back.

**MAEVRA:** Where?

The carter points farther into town.

**CARTER:** You'll find them.

Torren looks at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** Elder first.

**CYANIS:** Elder first.

**Objective:** Speak with the Dunmere elder.

Beat ends with normal town control returning to the player.

### Beat-2 knowledge checkpoint
- Dunmere is inhabited and functioning.
- Outbound travel toward Caelora has sharply reduced because people have repeatedly failed to arrive.
- A direct survivor establishes that **Black Host soldiers are taking people**, not merely robbing travelers or blocking the road.
- **The Old Bastion** is occupied by the Black Host and is being used to control the corridor.
- The party does not yet know the waterworks approach, Prisoner Galleries, holding/transit function, Rhazek's presence, or the destination of anyone taken.
- Next objective is to speak with the Dunmere elder for actionable local knowledge.

# BEAT 3 — DUNMERE ELDER / HIDDEN APPROACH

**Draft:** A — current rehearsal-first working dialogue

### [THE ELDER]

The group finds the Dunmere elder in town. The scene remains a practical local briefing rather than a ceremonial council.

**ELDER:** You're the ones asking about the Old Bastion.

**CYANIS:** We are now.

The elder looks over the four of them.

**ELDER:** Then I'll save you the first bad idea. Don't take the road to it.

**CYANIS:** Wasn't planning to.

**TORREN:** Why?

**ELDER:** Because the fort was built to watch that road. Clear approach, good walls, and enough height to see trouble coming before trouble sees them.

**MAEVRA:** So a frontal approach tells them exactly when we're arriving.

**ELDER:** And gives them time to decide what to do with the people they've taken.

Ilyra's expression tightens.

**ILYRA:** Other approaches?

**ELDER:** Bad ground. Less direct. Still ends under their walls.

Torren thinks for a moment.

**TORREN:** Anything under them?

The elder looks at him.

**ELDER:** Maybe.

**CYANIS:** That's promising.

**TORREN:** Depends on the maybe.

### [THE WATERWORKS]

The elder indicates an old municipal route on a local map or equivalent field reference.

**ELDER:** Dunmere's old waterworks used to run farther than the current system. One service branch goes out toward the Bastion's lower foundations.

**TORREN:** Into the fort?

**ELDER:** I didn't say that.

**TORREN:** Good.

The elder raises an eyebrow.

**ELDER:** Good?

**TORREN:** Means you're telling me what you know.

A beat.

**ELDER:** It runs toward the lower foundations. That's all I know.

**CYANIS:** Used recently?

**ELDER:** Not the far branch. Newer works bypass it. Nobody has reason to maintain what they don't use.

**ILYRA:** Flooded?

**ELDER:** Parts of it can be.

**MAEVRA:** Collapsed?

**ELDER:** Parts of it can be.

Cyanis looks at Torren.

**CYANIS:** You look delighted.

**TORREN:** Better than the road.

**MAEVRA:** That is a very low standard.

**TORREN:** Still clears it.

**CYANIS:** Any sign the Host uses the waterworks?

**ELDER:** None anyone here has seen. That isn't the same as none.

Torren nods once.

**TORREN:** Better answer.

**ILYRA:** If they aren't watching it, we can get close before they know we're there.

**MAEVRA:** If it still goes where the old plans say.

**TORREN:** I'll know soon enough.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Can you get us through?

**TORREN:** Haven't seen it.

**CYANIS:** Right. Stupid question.

**TORREN:** Little bit.

Maevra almost smiles.

**CYANIS:** Can you find out if it gets us through?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** Better question.

### [COMMITMENT]

The elder looks between them.

**ELDER:** If the branch is blocked, come back. Don't turn a bad route into a grave because you already walked half of it.

**TORREN:** Wasn't planning to.

**ELDER:** Good.

**CYANIS:** Where's the entrance?

The elder gives the group the practical location of the old waterworks access. Exact map geometry and access mechanism belong to area design.

**ELDER:** You'll know it when you see it. After that, you're in older works. I can't tell you what shape they're in.

**CYANIS:** You've told us enough.

**ILYRA:** More than we had.

**MAEVRA:** And more useful than walking up the road.

Torren looks toward the direction of the waterworks.

**TORREN:** Let's go see how bad it is.

**CYANIS:** That's your optimistic version, isn't it?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**Objective:** Enter the Old Waterworks.

Beat ends with normal Dunmere control before departure toward the waterworks entrance.

### Beat-3 knowledge checkpoint
- A direct road assault on the Old Bastion is tactically poor because the fort was built to dominate that approach.
- The elder knows an **old, disused Dunmere waterworks service branch** runs toward the Bastion's lower foundations.
- The elder does **not** claim the branch enters the fort and does not know what lies beneath or beyond the old municipal works.
- The far branch is no longer maintained; some flooding or collapse is plausible.
- No local evidence shows the Black Host routinely using the waterworks, but the elder does not guarantee it is empty.
- Torren judges the uncertain covert route worth testing.
- The party commits to the waterworks infiltration.
- The party still knows nothing about the Sunken Archive, western mural, Archive Leviathan, secret passage, Prisoner Galleries, Rhazek's presence, or the masked officer.

All three are still looking at him.

**TORREN:** Don't.

**CYANIS:** Didn't say anything.

**ILYRA:** Neither did I.

Maevra smiles.

**MAEVRA:** Coward.

Torren points at her.

**TORREN:** Fuck you.

**MAEVRA:** There he is.

The room settles.

The window stays open.

Nobody goes immediately back to bed.

Cyanis takes the chair by the wall. Ilyra stays sitting against the bedframe. Maevra shifts until the splinted arm is comfortable. Torren remains on the floor beneath the window.

For a little while they complain about the cold instead.

Then that runs out too.

The room becomes quiet again.

After a few minutes, Cyanis closes his eyes where he is sitting. Ilyra does the same. Maevra lies back down.

Torren looks once at the ash cup.

He reaches toward the pouch beside him.

Three voices, eyes still closed:

**CYANIS:** No.

**ILYRA:** No.

**MAEVRA:** Don't you fucking dare.

Torren's hand stops.

A beat.

He moves the pouch farther away.

**TORREN:** Buzzkills.

Maevra laughs into her pillow.

Fade.

> **END C06 — STILL BURNS**
