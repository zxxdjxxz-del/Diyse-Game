*Staging: Late evening at the Field Triage Camp. Ilyra sits at a makeshift table cleaning the last instruments and finishing patient labels. Most of the camp has settled.*

*Staging: Cyanis approaches carrying two bowls of camp stew and two pieces of coarse bread.*

*Staging: He nods toward the stack of clean cloth beside Ilyra's elbow.*

## C02_B005

## C02_B006
**ILYRA:** I'm almost finished.

## C02_B007

## C02_B010
**CYANIS:** I brought two bowls.

## C02_B011
**ILYRA:** That was not the question.

## C02_B012
**CYANIS:** I noticed.

## C02_B013
**ILYRA:** Cyanis.

## C02_B014
**CYANIS:** Not since before the attack.

## C02_B015

## C02_B016
**CYANIS:** I brought two bowls.

**ILYRA:** Sit.

## C02_B023

## C02_B024
**CYANIS:** You haven't eaten yet.

## C02_B025
**ILYRA:** Neither have you.

## C02_B026

## C02_B031
