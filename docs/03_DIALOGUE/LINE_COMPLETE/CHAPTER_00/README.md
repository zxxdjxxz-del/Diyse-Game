### Current Chapter-0 two-flare Card-response rule
Historical S004/S005 staging must not be read as one continuous protection state.

Current authority is:

**First flare — S004 / triage crisis**
- fragmented green-and-gold geometry appears for the first time while the treatment space is threatened;
- it briefly stabilizes weak cover / movement space long enough to help move patients;
- Cyanis is visibly strained and Ilyra can observe that his condition is involved;
- the phenomenon remains unidentified;
- **the first flare fully subsides before concealed Seyrik appears**.

**Between flares**
- concealed Seyrik is fought with **no Card-derived protection**;
- Riftmaw is fought with **no Card-derived protection**;
- the Card remains inert through the noncombat intervals between those encounters.

**Second flare — S005 / final War-Sorcerer push**
- when the final organized Black Host assault again threatens the defended treatment / evacuation space, the same fragmented phenomenon returns;
- this is a second distinct response, not the first flare continuing;
- current encounter authority gives Cyanis + Ilyra **Defense +15% / Spirit +15% for Rounds 1–3** during this second flare;
- after the final confrontation, the second flare fades completely and the Card returns to inert state.

Dialogue-generation firewall:
- nobody intentionally activates either flare;
- nobody identifies Prime, Might, Last Sentinel, bearer status, or ancient-weapon meaning;
- dialogue may recognize that the phenomenon happened twice, but cannot correctly explain why it happened or how to reproduce it;
- do not write dialogue implying the Card protected the party continuously through Seyrik and Riftmaw.
