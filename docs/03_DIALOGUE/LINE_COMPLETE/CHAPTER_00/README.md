## Current two-flare Card-response rule

The two Chapter-0 responses are **separate events**.

**First flare — P04**
- fragmented green-and-gold geometry appears for the first time while the treatment space is threatened;
- it briefly stabilizes weak cover / movement space long enough to help move patients;
- Cyanis is visibly strained and Ilyra can observe the correlation;
- the phenomenon remains unidentified;
- the first flare fully subsides before concealed Seyrik appears.

**Between flares**
- concealed Seyrik is fought with **no Card-derived protection**;
- the Card remains inert through the noncombat reset after P05.

**Second flare — P06**
- the same fragmented phenomenon returns during the combined Riftmaw + War-Sorcerer final push;
- it is a second distinct response, not continuation of the first;
- current encounter authority gives Cyanis + Ilyra Defense +15% / Spirit +15% for Rounds 1–3;
- during this fight the recovery casing breaks and the intact Card comes free;
- after the confrontation, the second flare fades completely and the Card returns inert.

### Dialogue-generation firewall
- nobody intentionally activates either flare;
- nobody identifies Prime, Might, Last Sentinel, bearer status, ancient-weapon meaning, or the Entity;
- dialogue may recognize that the phenomenon happened twice but cannot correctly explain why or how to reproduce it;
- do not write dialogue implying continuous protection through P05.
