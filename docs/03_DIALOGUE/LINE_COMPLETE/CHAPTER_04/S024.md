### MANDATORY BOSS — REGULATION CRUCIBLE

#### FORM I — DIVIDED REGULATION CRUCIBLE
- Four elemental chambers around the central engine: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Earth.
- Exactly two chambers are active/targetable at once; the other two are dormant and untargetable.
- Central core is always a legitimate target.
- Player may rush the core or spend actions dismantling chambers.
- Destroyed chambers stay destroyed and never respawn.
- Authored intact-chamber rotation: **Fire/Ice → Lightning/Earth → Fire/Lightning → Ice/Earth**.
- If one scheduled chamber is already destroyed, its slot stays empty while the surviving scheduled chamber activates.
- If both scheduled chambers are destroyed, that rotation is a core-only window.
- What survives becomes part of Form II.
- Former Wind speed/cadence inheritance and Water Barrier/restoration/stabilization inheritance are removed and not reassigned.

#### FORM I DEFEAT / GENUINE TRANSFORMATION
Core reaches zero. No damage spills forward. Battle UI withdraws for transformation staging.

Vaelira sees it.

**VAELIRA:** Back.

Party withdraws within the existing stage.

Surviving chamber lights feed inward; the central silhouette changes with the prepared state swap.

**NIMERA:** It's combining them.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**TORREN:** We just killed it.

**VAELIRA:** We killed the divided system.

New body forms.

**CYANIS:** Different thing?

**VAELIRA:** Different problem.

#### FORM II — THE SEVENTH REACTION / UNIFIED ELEMENTAL BODY
Fresh full HP / fresh authored MP.

Inherited traits come only from Form-I chambers still intact:
- Fire — stronger Fire/Burn pressure
- Ice — stronger Ice/Freeze pressure
- Lightning — stronger Lightning/Stun/disruption pressure
- Earth — stronger Earth/Staggered/defensive pressure

Destroyed chamber traits stay gone. No chamber respawns. There is no third form.

Because this is a genuine fresh-HP boss form, the current global **Prime availability refresh** rule applies at transformation.

No exact historical Form-II named command list has been recovered. Do not invent one as part of this four-element conversion.
