# 5. BEFORE THE ARENA

The party reaches the observation station.

Vaelira checks the controls.

**VAELIRA:** That's strange.

**NIMERA:** Chapter's favorite sentence.

Vaelira ignores her.

**VAELIRA:** The lockout is gone.

**CYANIS:** We knew that.

**VAELIRA:** The test cycle isn't.

Torren looks through the observation glass.

**TORREN:** Something moving?

Vaelira checks another reading.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**NIMERA:** Is it supposed to?

**VAELIRA:** No.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** Which I would like everyone to appreciate is a different answer from “I don't know.”

Nimera nods.

**NIMERA:** Growth.

Vaelira looks at her.

**VAELIRA:** Don't.

Cyanis looks through the glass.

**CYANIS:** Can we shut it off from here?

Vaelira tries.

Nothing.

**VAELIRA:** Apparently not.

Torren:

**TORREN:** There's the chapter I remember.

# 10. DEFEAT

Crown Prototype reaches zero HP.

Its final attack posture stops.

The body/frame powers down rather than exploding.

Battle UI clears.

Silence.

Torren looks at it.

**TORREN:** Dead?

Vaelira checks the nearest instrument.

**VAELIRA:** Inactive.

Torren looks at her.

**TORREN:** Didn't ask that.

**VAELIRA:** Then ask a more precise question.

Nimera:

**NIMERA:** Oh, she's definitely staying.

Vaelira looks at the powered-down Prototype.

**VAELIRA:** It's not restarting.

**TORREN:** Better.

# 11. THE CARD

With the test cycle disabled, a previously inaccessible storage/recovery compartment in the arena releases.

Simple mechanical state change.

No magical creation event.

Inside is a pre-existing Card.

Cyanis picks it up.

Might coloration: **Ruby**.

Nimera examines it without taking it from him.

**NIMERA:** Might.

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

Vaelira looks from the Card to the disabled Prototype.

**VAELIRA:** That was already in there.

**NIMERA:** Looks like.

**VAELIRA:** Why?

Nimera looks around the old prototype chamber.

**NIMERA:** Don't know.

Beat.

**NIMERA:** I'm getting surprisingly comfortable with that sentence.

Vaelira:

**VAELIRA:** I'm not.

**NIMERA:** Give it time.

# 12. AFTERMATH

Vaelira looks over the dormant apparatus.

**CYANIS:** You going to take it apart?

**VAELIRA:** No.

Cyanis looks surprised.

**VAELIRA:** Someone from the Annex is going to take it apart.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** Carefully.

**NIMERA:** Inspirational.

**VAELIRA:** I can still leave you here.

**NIMERA:** You'd miss me.

Vaelira starts walking.

**VAELIRA:** I absolutely would not.

Nimera follows.

**NIMERA:** That was very fast.

**VAELIRA:** Because the answer was easy.

Torren passes Cyanis.

**TORREN:** Here we go again.

Cyanis:

**CYANIS:** Could be worse.

**TORREN:** Still got time.

From ahead:

**NIMERA:** Stop fucking saying that.
