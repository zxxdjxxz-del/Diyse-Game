# EXPERIMENTAL C01 — SIX MINUTES

### [OPTIONAL INTERACTION — OVERNIGHT CAMP]

The camp is finally small enough to hear individual noises again.

Canvas shifts in the wind.
A guard coughs somewhere beyond the relay.
The last wagon light is already far down the road.

Cyanis is sitting beside a low brazier.

He has a cup.
A packet of dried leaves.
And the expression of a man who believes this constitutes a plan.

Ilyra approaches with a folded strip of clean cloth tucked under one arm.

She slows three steps away.

Sniffs once.

Stops completely.

**ILYRA:** What is that smell?

Cyanis looks into the cup.

**CYANIS:** Encouragingly, I was about to ask you the same thing.

Ilyra looks at the open packet beside him.

Her eyes narrow.

**ILYRA:** Give me the packet.

Cyanis hands it over.

She reads the label.

Then looks at the cup.

Then at him.

A long beat.

**CYANIS:** That's not tea.

**ILYRA:** No.

Another beat.

**CYANIS:** How far from tea?

**ILYRA:** In every useful direction.

Cyanis lifts the cup and smells it again.

His face answers before he does.

**CYANIS:** I thought it was just very serious tea.

Ilyra takes the cup from him.

**CYANIS:** I hadn't actually tried it.

She looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Much.

Ilyra closes her eyes for one second.

**ILYRA:** Bitterleaf is for poultices.

**CYANIS:** It was with the dried plants.

**ILYRA:** So is half the medicine.

**CYANIS:** That storage system has weaknesses.

Ilyra pours the cup into the dirt away from the brazier.

Cyanis watches the loss with genuine disappointment.

**CYANIS:** Is one cup dangerous?

**ILYRA:** Probably not.

Cyanis points at the damp patch in the dirt.

**CYANIS:** Then that feels premature.

**ILYRA:** It numbs skin.

A beat.

Cyanis touches his tongue against the inside of his cheek.

Ilyra notices.

**ILYRA:** You did drink it.

**CYANIS:** In my defense, the mistake had already been made.

**ILYRA:** That is not a defense.

**CYANIS:** It was structurally complete.

Ilyra stares at him.

Then, despite herself, laughs once.

Short.
Tired.
Real.

Cyanis points toward her with an empty hand.

**CYANIS:** There. Morale improved.

**ILYRA:** Through medical negligence.

**CYANIS:** Still improved.

Ilyra crouches beside the supplies.

She finds the actual tea tin almost immediately.

Cyanis watches.

**CYANIS:** Where was that?

Ilyra holds it up.

It was directly behind the cups.

Silence.

**CYANIS:** That's hostile design.

**ILYRA:** It's a tin.

**CYANIS:** A smug tin.

Ilyra opens it.

**ILYRA:** You're not touching this one.

**CYANIS:** I've learned.

**ILYRA:** You learned twelve seconds ago.

**CYANIS:** Efficiently.

She starts making the drink correctly.

Cyanis leans back on one hand.

Something wrapped in cloth shifts beside Ilyra's knee.

A field ration slides out.

Still sealed.

Cyanis catches it.

Looks at the seal.

Looks at Ilyra.

Ilyra keeps working on the tea as if this has nothing to do with her.

**CYANIS:** Is this yours?

**ILYRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** Recent?

**ILYRA:** Define recent.

Cyanis looks down at his own belt.

His ration is still there too.

He stops.

Ilyra follows his eyes.

Now they are both looking at his ration.

A beat.

**ILYRA:** Excellent.

**CYANIS:** I was busy.

**ILYRA:** I am thrilled for you.

Cyanis hands her ration back.

**CYANIS:** Open yours.

**ILYRA:** Open yours.

**CYANIS:** I asked first.

**ILYRA:** You are not my commanding officer.

**CYANIS:** This is becoming unnecessarily constitutional.

Ilyra finally looks at him.

**ILYRA:** Eat.

Cyanis opens his ration.

**CYANIS:** Tyranny.

Ilyra opens hers.

**ILYRA:** Miraculous recovery.

They start eating while the tea steeps.

For a little while, neither says anything.

Cyanis shifts against the relay stone.

Metal catches awkwardly against it.

Ilyra looks at the armor still on him.

**ILYRA:** Why are you still wearing all of that?

Cyanis looks down.

**CYANIS:** Habit.

**ILYRA:** You've been sitting here long enough to poison yourself recreationally.

Cyanis considers the argument.

**CYANIS:** Fair.

He starts undoing a shoulder piece.

Ilyra reaches for the clean cloth beside her.

Refolds one corner.

Cyanis sees it.

Says nothing.

She folds the other corner.

He watches.

Ilyra feels the attention and looks over.

**ILYRA:** What?

Cyanis points at the cloth.

**CYANIS:** Wasn't that already folded?

**ILYRA:** Not properly.

He removes the shoulder guard.

Sets it down.

**CYANIS:** Dangerous.

**ILYRA:** What is?

**CYANIS:** Leaving a rectangle unsupervised around you.

Ilyra looks at the cloth.

Then puts it down.

Deliberately.

**ILYRA:** There.

**CYANIS:** Brave.

She pours the tea.

Hands him a cup.

Cyanis smells it before drinking.

Ilyra watches him do it.

**ILYRA:** Progress.

**CYANIS:** Trust but verify.

**ILYRA:** You trusted bitterleaf.

**CYANIS:** We are moving past that.

**ILYRA:** I haven't agreed to that.

Cyanis takes a careful sip.

Waits.

**CYANIS:** My mouth still works.

**ILYRA:** Inspiring.

He takes another sip.

Ilyra finally sits fully rather than hovering on the edge of another task.

The camp is quiet enough that the brazier can be heard.

Cyanis looks toward the dark road.

Then at the half-finished ration in his hand.

**CYANIS:** How long before one of us gets up and finds something to fix?

Ilyra thinks about it.

**ILYRA:** Two minutes.

**CYANIS:** That's bleak.

**ILYRA:** Accurate.

Cyanis looks at her cup.

Then his.

**CYANIS:** Make it five.

Ilyra raises an eyebrow.

**ILYRA:** You negotiated me down from nothing to five minutes of rest.

**CYANIS:** Strong leadership.

**ILYRA:** Six.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** Why six?

Ilyra settles back against the stone.

**ILYRA:** Because you said five like it was difficult.

A beat.

Cyanis nods.

**CYANIS:** Cruel.

**ILYRA:** Sit there.

They do.

No one speaks for a while.

Neither reaches for the armor.
Neither touches the cloth.
Neither looks at the sealed recovery wagon.

The cups stay warm in their hands.

Six minutes is not enough to fix anything.

For once, that is the point.
