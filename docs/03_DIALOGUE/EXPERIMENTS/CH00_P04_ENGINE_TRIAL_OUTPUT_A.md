# EXPERIMENTAL P04

### [GAMEPLAY — RELAY-SIDE TRIAGE]

**Player control is active.**

The relay has stopped looking like a defensive position and started looking like a place people are trying not to die.

Blankets cover the ground between old stonework and wagons.
A broken door has become a treatment board.
Someone has tied blue cloth around a post so incoming survivors know where to go.

The sealed recovery wagon sits inside the perimeter.
Dark. Quiet.

A blonde Warden is already working near the center of the lane.

She points without looking up.

**ILYRA:** If you're walking, keep walking. Blue cloth first.

A civilian starts to kneel beside a wounded escort.

**ILYRA:** You—stay with him.

The civilian freezes.

**CIVILIAN:** What do I do?

Ilyra presses two fingers to the escort's neck, then shifts the civilian's hand into place.

**ILYRA:** Feel that?

The civilian nods.

**ILYRA:** Tell me if it changes. Don't decide what it means. Just tell me.

The civilian nods again, more firmly this time.

Ilyra moves to the next patient before the answer is finished.

Cyanis enters from the survivor route.

He takes in the lane, the wounded, the open eastern approach.

Then the stretcher team trying to squeeze through the same space Ilyra is using.

### [STORY BEAT — TWO DIFFERENT EMERGENCIES]

**CYANIS:** I need a corridor through here.

Ilyra looks up.

Not at him first.

At the route he means.

**ILYRA:** Not yet.

Cyanis steps closer and sees the patient lying partly across the lane.

**CYANIS:** I've got another group coming in behind me.

**ILYRA:** And I've got one here who gets worse when we move him.

Cyanis crouches just enough to see the patient's position without getting in her workspace.

**CYANIS:** How long?

Ilyra checks the splinting beside her.

**ILYRA:** Two minutes if nobody "helps."

One of the escorts who had been reaching toward the patient slowly withdraws their hands.

Cyanis notices.

**CYANIS:** All right.

He looks across the cluttered edge of the lane.

Points.

**CYANIS:** We move the supply crates. Stretcher line goes around the stone until you're clear.

Ilyra follows the route with her eyes.

**ILYRA:** That works.

Cyanis turns to the nearby escorts.

**CYANIS:** You heard her. Nobody touches him. Crates first.

The escorts move.

Ilyra returns to the patient.

Cyanis starts to leave.

**ILYRA:** Warden Amarin.

He looks back.

She is still working.

**ILYRA:** Since we're apparently coordinating now.

A small beat.

**CYANIS:** Cyanis Dovaren.

**ILYRA:** I know.

He waits.

Ilyra finally glances up.

**ILYRA:** People have been yelling it for the last hour.

Cyanis considers the wreck field behind him.

**CYANIS:** Fair.

**Player control returns.**

### [GAMEPLAY — WORKING CAMP]

The player helps move supplies away from the treatment lane and checks the relay-side perimeter.

Ilyra remains in motion behind Cyanis.

No one stops working because the protagonists have met.

On the next pass through the treatment space, Ilyra catches Cyanis using his shield hand to retighten a strap because his left hand misses the buckle once.

Then twice.

**ILYRA:** Dovaren.

He looks over.

She points at his hand.

**ILYRA:** Do that again.

Cyanis flexes it.

**CYANIS:** That?

**ILYRA:** The part where it ignored you.

He looks at the hand.

**CYANIS:** It's had a difficult morning.

**ILYRA:** So has everyone here. Most of their hands still know where the buckle is.

Cyanis exhales through his nose.

Not quite a laugh.

**CYANIS:** I'm functional.

Ilyra looks at the people on the ground around them.

Then back at him.

**ILYRA:** Conveniently, that isn't the same as well.

She returns to the patient before he can turn it into a longer conversation.

**ILYRA:** You're behind them unless something changes.

Cyanis nods once.

**CYANIS:** Works.

A shout comes from the east perimeter.

---

### [STORY TRIGGER — PRESSURE RETURNS]

**CONVOY OFFICER:** East approach! Move!

Cyanis is already turning.

A pulse of war-sorcery flashes beyond the relay stones.

The first impact strikes short.

Dust shakes loose across the treatment lane.

Cyanis points toward the inner wall.

**CYANIS:** Walking wounded inside! Clear the outer edge!

People begin moving.

Ilyra does not.

Two patients remain near the exposed side.

Cyanis sees them.

**CYANIS:** Amarin.

**ILYRA:** I see it.

**CYANIS:** That position won't hold.

**ILYRA:** Neither will his airway if they lift him like that.

Another distant discharge builds.

Cyanis looks from the ridge to the patient.

**CYANIS:** Give me a number.

Ilyra checks the work in front of her.

**ILYRA:** Twenty seconds.

**CYANIS:** You'll have ten before the next hit.

**ILYRA:** Then make ten useful.

Cyanis turns toward the outer lane.

Shield up.

**CYANIS:** Stretcher team ready on her call. Nobody improvises.

The next spell comes down.

It lands beside the recovery wagon.

The sealed casing jumps against its restraints.

A thin green line appears along one broken seam.

Then gold.

Cyanis stops mid-step.

The sound drops under a low crystalline hum.

### [ECONOMICAL HD-2D STAGING]
- camera lowers slightly behind Cyanis rather than cutting to a bespoke animation;
- portrait/UI can disappear for the first visual beat;
- green/gold line-light travels across existing relay stone and shield props;
- no humanoid figure, summon, or attack effect appears.

The light reaches the ground around Cyanis.

It does not form a complete symbol.

Angles begin and stop.
Circles fail to close.
Lines double back through empty space.

Then the fragments jump outward.

A cracked relay block settles instead of falling.

A shield brace that had started to buckle locks against the ground.

The exposed route between Ilyra and the inner wall holds.

Everyone sees it.

No one understands it.

The convoy officer looks toward Cyanis.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** Dovaren?

Cyanis is staring at the same thing.

**CYANIS:** Wasn't me.

Ilyra looks at the artifact.

Then Cyanis.

Then back to the pattern.

His breathing catches on the next pulse of light.

Ilyra notices.

She does not explain it aloud.

She turns to the stretcher team.

**ILYRA:** Now.

The team moves.

**ILYRA:** Keep him level. Slow at the shoulder—slow—good.

Cyanis stays at the threatened edge while the geometry shivers around his boots.

A second patient clears the lane.

The light pulses again.

Cyanis's shield drops half an inch.

He corrects it immediately.

Ilyra sees that too.

**ILYRA:** Cyanis.

No answer.

Not because he is ignoring her.

His eyes have narrowed on the line ahead.

She steps into his sightline rather than grabbing him.

**ILYRA:** Look at me.

He does.

**ILYRA:** Can you hear me normally?

**CYANIS:** Yes.

**ILYRA:** Any numbness?

He opens and closes the shaking hand.

**CYANIS:** No.

**ILYRA:** Dizzy?

A beat too long.

**CYANIS:** A little.

Ilyra holds out her hand.

He gives her his wrist.

She counts.

The geometry brightens.

His pulse jumps under her fingers at the same moment.

Her expression changes.

Very slightly.

**ILYRA:** Again.

Another pulse.

Another jump.

**CYANIS:** That's bad?

**ILYRA:** That's connected.

Cyanis glances toward the sealed wagon.

**CYANIS:** Can you tell me how?

**ILYRA:** No.

Immediate. Certain only about the uncertainty.

Another impact flashes beyond the held line.

**ILYRA:** Breathe on my count.

Cyanis follows.

Not perfectly.

Better on the second try.

The last stretcher clears the exposed route.

The green-and-gold pattern loses one segment.

Then three.

The braced shield settles under its own weight.

The cracked stone holds without the light.

The geometry collapses inward in broken pieces and goes dark.

The sealed artifact is inert again.

Cyanis looks at the empty ground.

**CYANIS:** I preferred it when the cargo was just inconvenient.

Ilyra releases his wrist only after his pulse begins to come down.

**ILYRA:** Keep preferring that.

A new warning call cuts across the camp.

**CONVOY OFFICER:** East line! One coming through!

Cyanis lifts his shield.

No green light answers him.

He checks the lane behind them.

**CYANIS:** Your patients clear?

Ilyra looks once.

One stretcher has reached cover.
The second patient cannot be moved farther yet.

**ILYRA:** Clear enough to keep treating them.

Cyanis looks east.

**CYANIS:** Then I keep it that way.

He starts forward.

Behind him, Ilyra picks up her Wardrod.

Cyanis hears it leave the ground and looks back.

**CYANIS:** You're staying with them.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

She steps beside him.

Cyanis glances at the Wardrod.

Then at her.

Ilyra nods toward the perimeter.

**ILYRA:** Which requires us to still own this side of the wall.

A beat.

Cyanis accepts the logic exactly as she accepted his earlier route change.

**CYANIS:** All right.

Ilyra sets her stance.

**ILYRA:** Good.
