Once equipment access or an Ability is legally unlocked, it remains available regardless of currently selected Base/Subclass unless an explicit global rule says otherwise.

## 5. Global Ability weapon-independence — locked

**No Ability or Ultimate requires a particular equipped weapon.**

Once learned, every Ability and Ultimate remains usable with any otherwise-legal equipment loadout.

Equipment still matters for:
- ordinary Attack;
- equipment stats/defenses;
- Weapon / Secondary slot geometry;
- Relic / Legacy legality;
- visual presentation.

If a technique visually implies a weapon not currently equipped, presentation uses an authored manifested/projected equivalent rather than disabling the command.

Abilities retain their authored formula and hit package and do not inherit ordinary Weapon Attack hit patterns unless explicitly authored.

This supersedes any older Truthshot or Axiomblade weapon-gating draft.

## 10. Universal Synthesis architecture — locked

Synthesis is the ninth/final Mastery node.

A character may purchase Synthesis only when all are true:
1. Base Class CL13;
2. Subclass CL13;
3. all four Core Masteries purchased;
4. all four Subclass Masteries purchased;
5. authored resolution/integration story requirement completed;
6. 1 unspent MP available.

Purchase costs exactly **1 MP**.

Pair story gates may be shared, but Synthesis eligibility/purchase remains individual.

The story gate is mandatory authored continuity, not romance, affinity, dialogue choice, or permanently missable content.

Each Synthesis grants:
- one permanent character-specific Base/Subclass integration passive;
- reciprocal partner **Legacy eligibility** under Section 13.

Synthesis does not:
- create a third class;
- merge stat packages;
- activate both Traits simultaneously;
- add an ordinary action;
- add an equipment slot;
- add a Standard Card slot;
- add a personal gauge;
- copy a partner's whole kit;
- bypass two-slot geometry;
- create weapon requirements;
- bypass Prepared limits;
- refresh/add Prime uses/slots;
- award class levels or extra MP.

A learned Ability retains its source-class identity for Synthesis trigger checks regardless of selected class.

## 11. Final six Synthesis effects — locked

### Cyanis — Unified Crest
A selected/resolved Crest Knight Ability can establish existing **Crest Attunement** when absent. Consuming Crest Attunement with a legal Crest Field/Seal arms **+15% Power** for Cyanis's next damaging Crest Knight Ability before the end of the following round.

### Vaelira — Unified Spectrum
A damaging Axiomblade Ability against an Imprinted target may use one existing Imprint element without consuming it. Once per round, a matching Axiomblade hit extends that matching Imprint by **+1 round** up to authored maximum. Prism Cycle retains precedence. No automatic Composite Reaction is created.

### Ilyra — Mercy Unbroken
A qualifying non-Ultimate Blue Warden heal/cleanse on another ally arms **+10% Power** for the next damaging non-Ultimate Vowblade Ability before the end of the following round. A Vowblade Ability/Ultimate that restores HP to another ally arms **-3 MP** on the next non-Ultimate Blue Warden Ability, minimum 1 MP.

### Seyrik — Tempered Ruin
A damaging non-Ultimate Ruin Vanguard Ability arms **-3 MP** on the next non-Ultimate Ruin Warden Ability, minimum 1 MP. A Ruin Warden Ability/Ultimate that restores HP to another ally arms **+10% Power** for the next damaging non-Ultimate Ruin Vanguard Ability.

### Torren — Measured Passage
A damaging non-Ultimate War Archer Ability resolving against Hunter's Measure arms **-2 MP** on the next non-Ultimate Routeweaver Ability, minimum 1 MP. Establishing or legally extending a Torren Route Field arms **+10% Power** for the next damaging non-Ultimate War Archer Ability against Hunter's Measure.

### Nimera — Living Proof
A target satisfies the known-target condition when Diysean Appraisal has revealed its permitted combat data and Hunter's Measure is currently present. Once per round, Nimera's first damaging non-Ultimate Truthshot against that target gains **+10% Power**. A qualifying Truthshot/Final Annotation hit arms **+10 Base Hit/application reliability** for the next hostile Standard Card against that same enemy where relevant.

Cross-balance status: **PASS — no broad raw-number rebalance required**.

Normalized Synthesis boundaries from the cross-balance audit remain controlling, including one trigger per qualifying action, non-stacking same-direction stored bonuses, and no Synthesis Power/MP-discount application to Ultimates unless separately authored as a later trigger source.

## 15. Chapter-12 Cresthaven secured Legacy release — final locked flow

During the **Chapter 12 pre-point-of-no-return Cresthaven preparation window**:

For each Legacy independently:
1. the corresponding Character Quest must be completed and its Legacy Component earned;
2. the matching secured Legacy becomes ready for completion/release;
3. the component is integrated/registered in a short authored interaction;
4. the component leaves Key Items and the completed named Legacy equipment enters normal inventory;
5. the native owner may equip it immediately if ordinary family/slot legality permits;
6. the reciprocal partner may equip it only after purchasing that character's Synthesis and satisfying ordinary legality.

If the Character Quest is incomplete, the Legacy remains secured/incomplete and is not added to inventory as a dead shell.

Because Audit103 keeps Character Quests available until the Chapter-12 point of no return, the player may leave, complete the missing Character Quest, return to Cresthaven, and claim the corresponding Legacy before committing to the finale.

The six Legacies are therefore **optional endgame equipment**, consistent with the optional nature of Character Quests.

No Legacy is required for:
- mandatory story completion;
- Story Prime use;
- Final Severance;
- understanding required main-plot lore;
- accessing the ending.
