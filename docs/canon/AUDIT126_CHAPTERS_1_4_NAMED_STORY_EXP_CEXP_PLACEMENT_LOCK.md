# 1. Global placement rules

- Chapter-level totals from Audit125 remain unchanged.
- Mandatory named/story rewards are fixed first-clear or deterministic story-milestone packages.
- Optional Elites and Regional Hunts are not charged to the mandatory chapter pool.
- Nonlethal authored encounters may award full first-clear EXP/CEXP.
- A reward may resolve on successful encounter completion without implying a kill.
- Fixed authored packages are exempt from lower-level ordinary-enemy EXP diminishing returns.
- No new farmable lawful-authority encounter is created.
