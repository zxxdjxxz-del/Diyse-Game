# 10. Native Legacy completion architecture

A character's native Legacy project is a Base-class capstone project and does **not** require Synthesis or Subclass CL13.

Current requirements are:

1. **Base Class CL13**
2. all **4 Core Masteries**
3. that character's Character Quest completed / resolution satisfied
4. that Character Quest's unique **Legacy Component** obtained and registered
5. the character's unique **Legacy precursor** obtained and registered
6. **Legacy Gate A**
7. **Legacy Gate B**
8. valid Kessara project availability in the late-game returnable window

The Character Quest Legacy Component and the precursor are separate unique Project Items.

They may be obtained in either order.

# 15. Relic-copy forging

Kessara may reproduce an already-obtained Relic using:

> **1 matching-Face Relic-copy Forge Component**

Rules:

- the original Relic must already have been obtained;
- the forged copy is mechanically identical to the original;
- one Relic may be copied at most once;
- maximum owned quantity per Relic is **2**: one original + one forged copy;
- forged copies do not gain Legacy-only bonuses;
- Legacies remain unique and cannot be duplicated through this system.

This copy system supports simultaneous native-owner / linked-receiver Relic builds without creating a separate shared-Relic catalog.

Exact Kessara menu presentation and any service fee remain implementation/economy details, not locked here.

# 16. Legacy power-tier identity

The intended equipment hierarchy is:

> **Ordinary < Relic < Legacy**

Legacies are the final native equipment tier and should generally be modestly stronger than comparable Relics in broad raw power while preserving specialist Relic niches.

Legacy identity also includes capstone stat perks that ordinary/Relic equipment does not normally emphasize, including approved axes such as:

- **Max HP Up**
- **Max MP Up**
- **Accuracy Up**
- **Evasion Up**

These perks travel with the item when legally worn through donor Legacy access.

**Important:** the exact 17-piece Legacy raw-stat values and exact perk assignments are still **pending approval** and are **not promoted by Audit117**. The working v600 proposal remains non-canon until explicitly approved or revised.
