# Audit96 — Level Progression, Hunt Difficulty and Encounter Strength Closure

**Authority:** Diyse: HD-2D JRPG Clean Active Complete Master Canon **v1.81 / Audit96**  
**Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Status:** **LOCKED / CONTROLLING** for player-level progression, expected chapter bands, Regional-Hunt and Major-Hunt recommended player levels, encounter-strength equivalents, and relative combat-pressure tuning.

Audit96 inherits all compatible v1.80/Audit95, v1.79/Audit94, v1.78/Audit93, and earlier canon unless explicitly superseded.

## 0. Authority contract
- Expected player level is a readiness/progression target, not a hard access gate unless a separate story/system gate explicitly says otherwise.
- Enemy strength-equivalent level is an internal balance benchmark only. It is not a displayed enemy level, does not alter the player Level-60 cap, and may exceed 60.
- Enemies should generally feel numerically stronger than the party. The party wins through tactics, Cards, equipment, Subclasses, targeting, mitigation, party construction, and encounter knowledge rather than numerical equality.
- No dynamic level scaling is introduced. Encounters use fixed authored tuning; returning later can naturally make earlier optional content easier.
- Elite, Regional Hunt, Major Hunt, named miniboss, and mandatory story boss remain separate roles.
- Numeric tuning cannot invent extra boss forms or extra fresh HP bars.

## 1. Player progression spine
- **Chapter 0 has no player levels and no leveling progression.** It remains authored/tutorial combat outside the numbered level curve.
- **Chapter 1 begins at Level 1.**
- Chapters 1–4 use roughly even early-game growth; Chapter 5 onward uses larger gains.

Internal strength equivalents above 60 do **not** create Level 61+ player progression.

Baseline ordinary combat pressure:
- ordinary durability should generally prevent one comparable party member from comfortably deleting an enemy with one routine turn; starting durability may sit roughly 10–20% above that one-turn comfortable-burn target;
- routine ordinary attacks roughly 12–18% of an appropriately geared expected-level character’s HP before extraordinary mitigation;
- heavy actions roughly 22–30%; clearly telegraphed high-commitment attacks roughly 35–45%;
- formation synergy is preferred over HP inflation. Coordinated enemy turns may KO a character if the player ignores defense, targeting, healing, control, or telegraphed mechanics.

## 10. Player-facing difficulty intent
- Intended hierarchy: **Ordinary < strong ordinary / anchor < named miniboss < Elite < mandatory story boss < Regional Hunt < Major Hunt**.
- Reaching the recommended player level means the party is prepared to attempt the encounter; it does not mean the party has numerically caught up.
- The player should routinely feel that enemies are stronger on paper; victory comes from tactical superiority.
- Overleveling remains valid. Fixed tuning means returning later can make earlier optional fights easier.
