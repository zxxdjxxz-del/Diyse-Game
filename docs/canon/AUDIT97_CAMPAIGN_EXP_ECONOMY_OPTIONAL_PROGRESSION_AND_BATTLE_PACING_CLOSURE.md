## 1. Two-track progression model — LOCKED

Diyse now distinguishes two level references:

1. **Campaign-only level** — the level naturally produced by mandatory story content plus the normal campaign-route random encounters, with no Regional Hunts, Major Hunts, Hunt-route enemies, or other optional/backtracking combat.
2. **Recommended / optional-content level** — the readiness level a player can reach by engaging substantially with Hunts, return routes, optional areas, and backtracking combat.

Mandatory story content is balanced so a campaign-only player can finish the game. Optional content is never secretly required to clear the story. Optional progression is deliberately valuable and can create a real numerical advantage over mandatory encounters.

## 3. Campaign ordinary-battle baseline — LOCKED

The normal mandatory-story route targets **165 ordinary random battles across Chapters 1–12**.

This 165-battle campaign baseline **includes** ordinary battles occurring while traveling between mandatory story destinations as well as ordinary battles inside mandatory story areas/dungeons.

It **excludes**:
- Regional Hunt approach/return-route ordinary battles;
- Major Hunt destination/approach ordinary battles;
- other explicitly optional/backtracking combat;
- Elites, minibosses, mandatory named encounters, story bosses, Regional Hunt bosses, Major Hunt bosses, and Chapter 0 authored tutorial encounters.

Chapter 0 remains outside the level/EXP curve and uses authored tutorial combat only.

## 16. Mandatory campaign ordinary/Elite strength rebase — LOCKED

Audit97 further clarifies that **all mandatory campaign combat roles** use the campaign-only expected party as their balance reference at the authored encounter placement. This includes ordinary enemies, strong ordinary/formation anchors, named minibosses, Elites, and story bosses.

The controlling relative-pressure grammar is:
- ordinary enemy ≈ campaign-only expected party +1 equivalent;
- strong ordinary / formation anchor ≈ +2;
- named miniboss ≈ +2 to +3;
- Elite ≈ +3;
- mandatory story boss ≈ +4 to +5.
