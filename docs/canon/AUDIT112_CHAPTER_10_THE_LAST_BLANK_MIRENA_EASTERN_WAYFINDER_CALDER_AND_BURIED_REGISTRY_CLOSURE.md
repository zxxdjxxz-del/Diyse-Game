# 2. Mirena's investigation since Chapter 3 — LOCKED

After the Chapter-3 composite-order investigation, **Crown Princess Mirena continues reviewing Crown records for other strange orders and provenance irregularities**.

This work continues quietly through later chapters rather than beginning conveniently in Chapter 10.

Her review is not limited to literal copies of the Chapter-3 contradiction. She is looking for Crown actions where:
- the order itself may be authentic;
- seals and issuing authority may be valid;
- but provenance, supporting context, timing, attached authorities, or the surviving record trail does not make sense.

Most records she examines have ordinary explanations: incomplete filing, age, bad cataloguing, missing routine attachments, or other mundane administrative causes.

One older eastern research order remains unresolved.

### 2.1 The eastern research order

The accessible Crown record contains only two meaningful surviving points:

1. an authentic Crown order authorizing **research in an eastern area beyond / east of Cerythvale**;
2. a later authentic administrative notation that the research was **completed**.

The accessible chain contains **no meaningful surviving middle**:
- no field report;
- no findings report;
- no recovered-material inventory;
- no excavation summary;
- no explanation of what was discovered;
- no explanation of why the area warranted the scale of research ultimately found there.

The authorization itself is genuine. Mirena does **not** begin Chapter 10 believing it to be a forged order.

### 2.2 What Mirena does not know

At the start of Chapter 10, Mirena does **not** know:
- that Eastern Wayfinder exists;
- that the old research site was a Wayfinder;
- that the operation became a substantial excavation;
- that Calder's Prime research was involved;
- that the site contains the final eastern survey needed by the party's accumulated cartography;
- that a protected Ancient registry exists below the excavation.

Mirena sends the party to determine **what the Crown actually researched there and what happened between authorization and completion**.

### 7.1 No historical Prime activation

Hard firewall:

**No Prime had ever been successfully activated before the first modern activation in the game's present era.**

- documented previous Prime summon activations;

# 8. Calder and the Chapter-0 convoy — LOCKED

Before Chapter 0, Calder's Prime research program had already produced a **standing recovery/reporting directive**.

Recovered Diysean Cards matching the unusual criteria relevant to that research were to be reported upward and transported to **Caelora** for examination.

The recovered Card carried by the **Chapter-0 convoy** triggered that authority.

Therefore:

**Othmar Calder is the reason the recovered Card was ordered transported to Caelora, and that transfer order is the reason the Chapter-0 convoy Cyanis was assigned to protect existed.**

### 8.1 Limits on Calder's involvement

This does **not** mean Calder:
- chose Cyanis personally;
- arranged Cyanis's assignment specifically;
- caused the Chapter-0 attack / ambush;
- knew Cyanis would become central to later events;
- knew the recovered Card would successfully activate a Prime;
- orchestrated every downstream event.

Calder wanted the recovered Card brought to the capital under a standing research/custody process. Cyanis was a soldier assigned to protect the resulting convoy.

### 8.2 Lawful first action versus later abuse

The original Chapter-0 transfer order is **lawful**.

Calder possesses legitimate authority to order an important recovered Ancient Card brought to the capital for examination.

The later Chapter-3 seizure architecture is a separate escalation and must not retroactively make the Chapter-0 transfer itself fraudulent.

- lower access had become unsafe;
- personnel were ordered to withdraw / cease further work;

- the sealed structure lies directly beneath Eastern Wayfinder;

Crossing the threshold is the point where the party moves beyond reconstructing Calder's expedition and enters material Calder's team never reached.

# 12. Buried Registry — LOCKED

Beyond the Registry Warden is a compact untouched Ancient administrative space presently called the **Buried Registry**.

Calder's excavation team never reached this area.

### 12.1 Original function

The Buried Registry is a **regional custody / authority / responsibility registry physically paired with the Wayfinder geography above**.

It is **not**:
- a secret computer control room;
- a digital database;
- the full Underground Crest Network reveal;
- Crown Engine itself;
- a final explanation of Prime activation.

Physical presentation should use Ancient stone / metal administration and archaeology:
- relief geography / engraved location surfaces;
- record cabinets / racks / tablets;
- authority marks / seals;
- custody / jurisdiction / responsibility records;
- amendment / transfer records.

Avoid computer/network/database vocabulary.

### 12.2 What the party can understand

The core discovery is:

**The cartographic geography recorded by Eastern Wayfinder and the administrative/custody records in the Buried Registry were deliberately related parts of one larger Ancient arrangement.**

Character ownership:
- Torren recognizes locations / geographic correspondence.
- Nimera recognizes administrative organization, custody, responsibility, jurisdiction, and transfer.
- Vaelira recognizes the shared relational / spatial structure between the two bodies of material.

No single character suddenly possesses the whole explanation.

### 12.3 Prime references

Some registry records use the Ancient classification associated with **Prime Cards**.

This proves only that Prime-class Cards mattered enough to be distinguished within formal Ancient custody/administrative material.

It does **not** provide:
- previous successful Prime activations;
- Prime-wielder histories;
- a complete explanation of powers;
- an explanation of why Cyanis or anyone else can activate a Prime in the present era.

### 12.4 Transfer / continuity evidence

The Registry may visibly show that Ancient responsibilities and custodianship could be **transferred / amended between offices or bearers** while the protected place/object continued.

This is permitted as a restrained environmental/story seed.

Do **not** turn Chapter 10 into the complete philosophical rebuttal to Calder's later Living Anchor argument. The full Calder confrontation and interpretation belong to Chapter 11.

### 12.5 Caelora / Yahtrenhold convergence

The completed geographic reconstruction and the Buried Registry's administrative relationships both show disproportionate convergence toward the area corresponding to **Caelora in Yahtrenhold**.

The party may conclude:

**The area occupied by modern Caelora was unusually important to the Ancient arrangement.**

They may not yet conclude exactly why.

This is the final Ancient directional clue of Chapter 10.

### 12.6 Post-boss pacing

The Buried Registry is a **short, quiet post-boss discovery zone**, not another full dungeon layer.

No random encounters are required after the Registry Warden. The intended tone is silence / archaeological discovery after the combat climax.
