## Cross-chapter production locks

### Official production tiers

Use only:

- C0 Conversational
- C1 Staged
- C2 Dramatic

- V2 Face/class identity
- V3 Named signature

Earlier conversion-audit shorthand using `S0/S1/S2` is normalized to the C/V vocabulary without changing approved staging.

### Screen and battle grammar

- Master composition remains 16:9/1920×1080 reference framing, with wider Android screens revealing additional horizontal scenery rather than stretching critical composition.
- Field characters target ~80 px.
- Battle characters target ~200 px.
- Dialogue portraits remain large/high-resolution.
- Party uses the permanent staggered left-side formation; enemies remain right; open center is protected for actions/VFX.
- Undersized early parties occupy legal positions inside the same formation grammar.

### Environment and asset reuse

Approved durable families include:

- Edgelands regional environment kit;
- wet/water library;
- Southhold civic material family;
- Ancient Diysean civilization-level structural grammar with route/archive/command/regulation sublanguages;
- Ancient cartographic master system;
- Black Host environment family;
- Cresthaven evolving master hub;
- modular NPC/crowd body library;
- prop/environment state system;
- modular six-element VFX system;
- modular Face/Card/Prime VFX architecture.

### Nonlethal battle presentation

Create a reusable nonlethal-resolution presentation path for lawful confrontations, animal retreats, stabilization and similar outcomes. Suppress generic death dissolve, loot-show and victory-pose presentation where the authored outcome is nonlethal.

### Boss grammar

Use three project-wide categories:

1. same-body / same-HP escalation;
2. genuine new form when the encounter truly changes body/combat problem;
3. Prime-scale entity using the Prime presentation pipeline.

Do not manufacture new full forms merely to dramatize a threshold.

### Android cost discipline

Decorative particle count, reflections, weather density, secondary background motion, distortion and noncritical dynamic lights may scale down by device quality. Gameplay readability, target clarity, exact character identity, critical story VFX, battle timing and UI clarity may not.
