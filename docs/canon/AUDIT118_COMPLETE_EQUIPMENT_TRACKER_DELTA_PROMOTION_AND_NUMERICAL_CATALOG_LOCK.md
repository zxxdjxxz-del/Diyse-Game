# 7. Exact surviving Relic Trait rules

The following are the controlling Relic Trait definitions. References to Critical chance remain legal item text; the universal Critical payout remains separately open.

## Cyanis Weapons

### Opening Measure
Before the target's first action this round:
- **+10 Base Hit**
- **+6 percentage points Critical chance**

If the action is single-target:
- **+6% final damage**

### Crest Conduction
For an eligible attack that already has authored hybrid/off-axis scaling:
- add **8% of the lower relevant offensive stat** to the authored contribution where legal.

For a pure-axis action, no new hybrid profile is created. Only an explicitly authored opposite-stat secondary contribution may receive the smaller **+6%** support.

### Formation Cut
Against **2+ enemies**:
- **+8% final damage**
- **+5 percentage points applicable Defense penetration**

Against **4+ enemies**, penetration becomes **+8 percentage points**.

If the action already has a Staggered rider:
- **+5 percentage points** application chance at 2+ targets;
- **+8 percentage points** at 4+ targets.

## Cyanis Armors

### Answer the Blow
After taking direct Physical damage:
- **+10% Defense** and **5% less direct Physical damage** through the end of the following round.

If the triggering hit dealt at least **15% Max HP**, the Defense bonus becomes **+12%**.

Maximum once per round.

### Heavy Step
While above **50% HP**:
- **+8% action Speed**
- **+8 Base Hit**

After Defend/Guard, retain the Speed benefit through the following round.

### Warded Impact
After taking Magical damage of at least **12% Max HP**:
- **+10% Spirit**
- **+15 Status Resistance**

through the remainder of the current round and the next round. Maximum once per round.

## Ilyra Weapons

### Measured Correction
Eligible Wardrod control/debuff/status/utility actions gain:
- **+12 application reliability**.

If the effect successfully lands, Ilyra's next Wardrod action against that same target gains:
- **+5% final damage**.

### Vow in Motion
After a real heal, Barrier, or cleanse resolves:
- store one Vow in Motion charge.

The next Wardrod action gains:
- **+8% final damage**
- **+10 Base Hit**

Only one charge may be stored.

### Break the Posture
Against a target using Guard/Brace or another eligible ordinary defensive posture:
- **+8% final damage**
- **+15 percentage points applicable Defense penetration**

If the action already has a Staggered rider:
- **+5 percentage points** application chance.

No protected boss state is bypassed.

## Ilyra Armors

### Carry the Hit
At **50% HP or lower**:
- take **10% less direct Physical damage**.

After taking a Physical hit worth at least **15% Max HP**:
- **+10% Defense** through the end of the following round.

Maximum once per round.

### Warden's Quiet
- **+15 Status Resistance**
- **12% resistance to eligible ordinary negative-stat changes**

While protected by a Barrier:
- **+8% Spirit**.

### Answer Quickly
- **+10 Status Resistance**

Once per round, when an ally is at or below **30% HP**, Ilyra's next eligible support action gains:
- **+15% action Speed**
- **+8% heal/Barrier potency**.

## Torren Weapons

### Clean Shot
Single-target bow attacks gain:
- **+10 Base Hit**
- **+5 percentage points Critical chance**

If the target currently has no Evasion bonus:
- **+6% final damage**.

### Split the Field
Against **2+ targets**:
- **+7% final damage**
- **+10 Base Hit**

Against **4+ targets**:
- Base Hit bonus becomes **+15**.

### Known Line
Against a target whose relevant profile has already been revealed/established:
- **+12 Base Hit**
- **+10 percentage points applicable Defense penetration**

If the target is already affected by an eligible Evasion Down / Base-Hit-reducing state:
- Base Hit bonus becomes **+18**.

If the action already has a harmful-status rider:
- **+5 percentage points** application chance.

No hidden information is revealed.

## Torren Armors

### Good Footing
If Torren has not been directly hit this round:
- **+8 Base Hit**
- **+6% action Speed**

After Torren successfully hits an enemy, the benefit persists for the remainder of that round.

### Correct the Miss
After Torren misses an eligible attack:
- his next eligible attack gains **+15 Base Hit** and **+5% final damage**.

Maximum one stored correction; expires after the following round if unused.

### Read the Commitment
When an enemy directly targets Torren, until Torren's next action against that enemy:
- **+10 Base Hit**
- **+5 percentage points Critical chance**

Incoming Staggered application from that enemy is multiplied by **x0.80**.

The offensive read expires when used or at the end of the following round.

## Nimera Weapons

### Twin Pattern
Nimera's normal Attack becomes:
- **2 hits x 58% = 116% total listed Power**
- same target
- one selected action.

A harmful-status rider attached to the selected action checks once unless explicitly authored per hit.

### Written in Air
An eligible Conduit attack may use its better legally supported Magical scaling profile when the action supports that substitution.

When resolving as Magical:
- **+6 application reliability** to an already-existing chance-based rider.

No unsupported damage axis or hidden information is invented.

### Open Pattern
Nimera's normal Attack may target up to **3 enemies** at **75% Power per target**.

Cards are unaffected. No status rider is created automatically.

## Nimera Armors

### Rewrite Position
When Nimera changes target between consecutive eligible actions:
- **+8% Magic contribution**
- **+8% action Speed**

for the newly selected action.

### Hold the Pattern
Once per battle, extend one eligible beneficial temporary effect on Nimera by **one standard duration step**.

The same specific effect application may only be extended once. Protected/scripted states are excluded.

Also grants:
- **+10 Status Resistance**.

## Vaelira Weapons

### Chosen Element

- **+8% final damage**
- **+8 application reliability** to an already-existing chance-based elemental status rider.

### Refracted Sequence

### Held Charge

- **+10 Status Resistance**

until she casts her first damaging spell that round.

# 14. Relic-copy forging — exact core rule

Kessara's Relic-copy service uses:

> **1 matching-Face Relic-copy Forge Component**

to create one extra copy of an **already-obtained** Relic.

Rules:

- the original Relic must already be registered/obtained;
- the forged copy has identical stats, Trait, slot rules, and restrictions;
- an individual Relic may be duplicated **once**;
- maximum owned quantity for one Relic = **2**: one original + one forged copy;
- a forged copy cannot be used to create another copy;
- Legacies cannot be duplicated through this system;
- with 3 copy materials per Face, the player may duplicate at most **3 Relics from that Face** across the game.

The purpose is to support simultaneous native-owner / linked-receiver Relic builds after donor Relic permission without creating a fake shared-Relic catalog.

Exact service-menu timing, UI treatment, and any currency/service fee remain open.
