**Authority:** Diyse: HD-2D JRPG Clean Active Complete Master Canon **v1.78 / Audit93**  
**Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Status:** **LOCKED / CONTROLLING** for Chapters 0–12 enemy-production rosters, carryovers, authored/nonlethal handling, Elite/Hunt separation, mandatory bosses, boss-form architecture, production-family relationships, and final naming closure.

Audit93 inherits all compatible v1.77/Audit92, v1.76/Audit91, v1.75/Audit90, v1.74/Audit89, and earlier canon unless explicitly superseded.

## Global locks
- Area-first enemy ecology; no minimum species quota and no fixed random-battle quota.
- Earlier enemies may carry into later areas when ecology, faction presence, and story context fit.
- Shared rigs/materials/animation/VFX do not imply identical lore identity.
- Elite, Regional Hunt, mandatory named encounter, and mandatory story boss are separate roles unless explicitly locked otherwise.
- Lawful/coerced/victim-class humans use authored nonlethal handling unless voluntary hostile agency is explicit.
- Fresh HP bars are reserved for genuine locked transformations; state shifts/exposed states stay on the current bar unless otherwise authorized.
- `Sixfold` is reserved for party-linked concepts.
- Regional Hunts end at **#11 Throne of Emperor Vaelkor**. Chapter 12 has no Regional Hunt.

## Final naming locks
- Chapter 6 authored/special guard: **Weather Crown Shield Guard**.
- Chapter 11 dedicated conventional Elite: **Lord-Marshal Kharvek**.
- Chapter 12 final-domain manifestations: **Hunger Aspect, Ruin Aspect, Silence Aspect, Fear Aspect**; legacy Fragment labels are retired.

## Late-game encounter-role closure
- Chapter 9 Elite: **Ruin Breach Captain**; mandatory bosses remain Equal Mercy Arbiter and Commander Rhazek/Bastion Devourer; Hunt #9 is Mercyfallen Behemoth.
- Chapter 10 Elite: **Perfect Administrator**; Hunt #10 is Authority Remnant.
- Chapter 11 Elite: **Lord-Marshal Kharvek**; Varkesh and Vaelkor remain mandatory story encounters; Hunt #11 is Throne of Emperor Vaelkor.
- Chapter 12 Elite: **Devourer of Names**; Last Weapon Archon is a mandatory guardian; Reconstituted Entity → The Last Command is the final two-form boss; no Hunt.
