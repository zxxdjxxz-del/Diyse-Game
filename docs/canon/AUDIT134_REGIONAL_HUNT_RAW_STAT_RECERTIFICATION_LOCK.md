# 5. Difficulty hierarchy

Regional Hunts remain fixed optional challenge content above numbered-chapter Elites.

Current principle:

**Ordinary < Elite < mandatory story boss < Regional Hunt < Major Hunt**

Exact relative HP is not required to be monotonic across every encounter because:
- fresh-body count;
- support objectives;
- recovery;
- action taxes;
- capture/floor mechanics;
- damage profile;
- encounter knowledge

all contribute to effective challenge.
