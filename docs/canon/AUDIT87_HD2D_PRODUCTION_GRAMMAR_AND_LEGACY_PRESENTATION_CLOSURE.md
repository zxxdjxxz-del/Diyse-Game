## Locked production grammar

- Field characters target approximately **80 px**.
- Battle characters target approximately **200 px**.
- Dialogue uses large high-resolution portraits.
- Standard battle staging is **four active party members on the left, enemies on the right, open center action space**.
- Normal exploration uses authored layered environments, controlled camera framing, parallax/foreground/background depth and selective geometry rather than requiring fully physically modeled scenes.
- Exact approved visual masters control all derivative HD-2D sprites, portraits, cut-ins and other presentation assets.
- The exact approved Yahtrea world map remains spatially authoritative and must not be reinterpreted by HD-2D conversion.
- Android/APK remains the platform target.
- Runtime remains approximately **25 hours**.
- Absolute level cap remains **60**.
- Permanent battle commands remain **Attack / Ability / Card / Item / Defend**.
- Random encounters remain part of the game.

## Production-cost grammar

HD-2D should create depth through authored composition rather than brute-force simulation.

Prefer reusable environment families, layered static/authored artwork, modest parallax, selective animated elements, reusable character poses, portraits, prop-state swaps, before/after environment states, small battle-background families, lightweight particles, baked/precomposed lighting where practical, short loops and selective dynamic lighting.

Do not default to fully modeled cities, seamless giant dungeons, free-camera traversal, fluid simulation, crowd simulation, destruction physics, chain/cloth/hair simulation, one unique battle arena per formation, or one bespoke body animation per named Ability when the approved scene can be communicated more economically.

## Production tier vocabulary

Use the Audit87 presentation budget vocabulary consistently:

- **C0 — Conversational**
- **C1 — Staged**
- **C2 — Dramatic**
- **C3 — Spectacle**
- **V1 — Common**
- **V2 — Face/class identity**
- **V3 — Named signature**
- **V4 — Prime/boss spectacle**

Most scenes should remain C0–C1. C2 is selective. C3 is rare. V4 is reserved for true top-tier presentation events and must not be spent casually.
