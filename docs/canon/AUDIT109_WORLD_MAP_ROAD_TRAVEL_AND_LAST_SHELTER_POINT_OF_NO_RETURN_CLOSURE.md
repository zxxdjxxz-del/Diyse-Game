## 1. Base-map authority and bounded approved changes

The Audit108 1536 × 1229 Yahtrea/Blackstone map remains the **base visual/spatial authority**, but its exactness is now subject to the following approved bounded corrections.

### 1.1 Emberforge / Deepforge position swap — LOCKED

Swap only the identities/positions of **Emberforge** and **Deepforge**:

- **Emberforge** occupies the former, more southerly Deepforge site.
- **Deepforge** occupies the former, farther-north Emberforge site.

This directly supports the mandatory Chapter 5 progression:

**Stonewake → Emberforge → Deepforge**

No surrounding terrain, river, mountain, road, or other location moves as part of this swap.

### 1.2 Westreach ↔ Crownfall Redoubt military road — LOCKED

Add one visible military/logistics road between **Westreach** and **Crownfall Redoubt**.

- The road climbs north/northeast from the Westreach theater through the Westways foothills toward Crownfall's eastern/rear approach.
- It remains on the Yahtrean side of The Blackspine.
- It supports the post-Chapter-8 reoccupation and supply of Crownfall before the Chapter 9 invasion.
- It should not be drawn as a road through the center of Ashfrost Expanse.

### 1.3 Westreach ↔ Draevensreach Blackspine crossing — LOCKED

Add one major pass-road crossing through **The Blackspine** connecting the Westreach-side campaign corridor to **Draevensreach**.

- This is the principal late-campaign coalition crossing into Blackstone.
- No new settlement, fortress, or permanent map node is added inside the pass.
- Exact local pass name remains optional/open unless later explicitly named.

### 1.4 Blackstone imperial causeway — LOCKED

Add/clarify the principal Blackstone transport spine as:

**Draevensreach ↔ Vhalmarch ↔ Vorathen**

The Blackstone road should visually read as an engineered imperial transport causeway rather than simply copying Yahtrean road language.

### 1.5 No other spatial permission

Audit109 does **not** authorize moving any other existing location, road, river, coastline, mountain belt, canyon/scar, region, or landmark.

Until a revised map image containing the changes above is generated and explicitly approved, the current Audit108 image remains the visual base and **Audit109's text controls the four approved differences**.

## 2. Chapter-by-chapter travel audit — LOCKED

### Chapter 1 — Brackenwall and the Wayfinder

Controlling travel geography:

**Brackenwall → Hollow Watch → Greenhollow → Briar Passage → Wayfinder Junction → Dunmere**

- Hollow Watch remains an isolated ridge dungeon/landmark, not part of a prison district.
- Briar Passage is a traversal leg, not a settlement.
- Wayfinder Junction is the outdoor Ancient cartographic crossroads.
- No separate Waystone locations exist.

### Chapter 2 — The Drowned Oath

Surface anchor: **Dunmere**.

Chapter-local progression remains local/sub-map geography:

**Poisoned Waterworks → Sunken Archive → Prisoner Galleries → Red Transfer Bastion → Extraction Causeway**

No additional master-map road or permanent node is required.

### Chapter 3 — Old City / Cresthaven establishment

Hard geography remains:

**Caelora → Old City / Suppressed Archives → return to Caelora → separate Cresthaven**

Cresthaven is not a Caeloran district or Archive chamber.

### Chapter 4 — The Seventh Reaction

The mandatory movement into **Ivorybridge** is supported by the Crownhold road network.

Reaction Annex / Regulation Core spaces remain local and do not require master-map nodes.

### Chapter 5 — Greyspires forge progression

After the Emberforge/Deepforge swap, the controlling route is:

**Stonewake → Emberforge → Deepforge**

Deepforge is now the farther-north/deeper Greyspires destination and Chapter 5 climax. The prior workaround in which the party geographically passed Deepforge before reaching Emberforge is retired.

The Chapter 5 aftermath naturally supports onward travel toward **Frostmere**.

### Chapter 6 — Frostmere / Weather Crown

Controlling route:

**Frostmere → Weather Crown / Weather Core → Crimson Relay / Crimson Work → Saboteur Refuge → Witness Camp**

- Weather Crown remains east/east-northeast of Frostmere.
- Crimson Relay / Crimson Work remains west/southwest of Frostmere on the Blackspine-facing side.
- The party is **not required to return to Frostmere between Weather Crown and Crimson Relay**. A local mountain route may connect the Weather Crown descent toward the Crimson Relay side.
- No new master-map road is required for these local branches.

### Chapter 7 — The Prison of Names

Controlling route:

**Ashford → Veycross → western transfer corridor → Prison of Names**

The Prison of Names is northwest of Veycross toward the Blackspine-facing occupied interior, while remaining southeast/east of the later Westreach/Horizon Vault theater.

The transfer corridor is a chapter-revealed military/service route below master-map road scale.

### Chapter 8 — Westreach / Horizon Vault campaign

Controlling progression:

**Prison-of-Names aftermath → Horizon Vault / Western Rift Engine → Westreach → Varkesh Command → Coalition Camp**

- Horizon Vault remains in the Blackspine foothills immediately west/northwest of Westreach.
- Varkesh Command lies farther west/northwest toward the enemy-facing side of the theater.
- Coalition Camp is established on the safer east/southeast Yahtrean side of the reclaimed Westreach area.
- Horizon Vault, Varkesh Command, and Coalition Camp do not require permanent decorative master-map labels.

### Chapter 9 — Larkspire / Crownfall invasion

Controlling macro movement:

**Larkspire / Equal Mercy → urgent transit west → Westreach corridor → Crownfall Redoubt eastern/rear approach**

The new **Westreach ↔ Crownfall Redoubt military road** supplies and explains the recent reoccupation of Crownfall and gives the party a believable rear approach during Rhazek's invasion.

No direct Larkspire ↔ Crownfall road is required.

### Chapter 10 — The Last Blank

Controlling route:

**Cerythvale → eastern route → Eastern Wayfinder → return toward Caelora with the completed eastern cartographic evidence**

- No intermediary Waystone exists.
- Concordance Vault is not required transit and remains optional-major content.
- The Cerythvale/Eastern Wayfinder relationship requires no additional master-map road.

### Chapter 11 — Crown Engine

World-map anchor: **Caelora**.

Controlling movement is vertical/local:

**Caelora → sealed Crown/administrative descent → Crown Engine beneath Caelora**

The Crown Engine is not beneath Cresthaven and is not a separate surface map node.

Chapter 3's Old City/Suppressed Archives and Chapter 11's much deeper Crown Engine may occupy related buried historical layers but are **not the same complex**.

### Chapter 12 — The Reforged March

Controlling late-war geography:

**Westreach-side campaign corridor → Blackspine pass → Draevensreach → Vhalmarch → Vorathen → The Veiled Citadel**

- The Westreach ↔ Draevensreach pass is the major coalition crossing into Blackstone.
- **Draevensreach → Vhalmarch → Vorathen** follows the Blackstone imperial causeway.
- Vhalmarch becomes the temporary Forward Hub only after Varkesh is defeated/captured, preserving inherited timing authority.
- The Veiled Citadel remains inside Vorathen and is not another overworld node.

### Chapter 13 — The Last Command

The final operation is briefed/authorized at **Cresthaven**, but that briefing is **not** the true point of no return.

The final-domain surface access anchor is now locked as:

**Cresthaven final briefing → return to captured Blackstone → Vorathen → The Veiled Citadel → Vaelkor/Black Host excavation descent → The Deepest City**

The Veiled Citadel / Vaelkor excavation infrastructure provides the surface access into the deepest surviving Ancient Diysean domain. **Final Archive is not the mandatory finale entrance** and retains its separate optional-major identity.

The locked final-domain progression remains:

**The Deepest City → Deep City → Last Weapon Archive → Last Weapon Archon → Last Shelter → Reactor Galleries → Reactor–Crest Interface → Reconstituted Entity → The Last Command → Final Severance → ending**

## 3. Last Shelter = true point of no return — LOCKED

**Last Shelter is the final point of no return.**

The player may enter Chapter 13, explore the Deepest City / Deep City, complete the Last Weapon Archive sequence, defeat the Last Weapon Archon, and reach **Last Shelter** without yet being irreversibly locked into the ending.

Last Shelter functions as the final:

- recovery point;
- save/checkpoint opportunity;
- equipment/loadout preparation point;
- narrative quiet point;
- explicit chance to turn back and finish still-available world content.

The irreversible threshold is:

**Last Shelter → Reactor Galleries**

Before the player crosses that threshold, the game must clearly warn that proceeding will lock them into the endgame.

Once the player confirms and passes beyond Last Shelter:

- ordinary world return is disabled;
- unfinished optional content is no longer accessible for that playthrough;
- the remainder of the finale proceeds continuously through Reactor Galleries, Reactor–Crest Interface, Reconstituted Entity, The Last Command, Final Severance, and the ending.

Exact implementation of pre-threshold return travel—direct fast travel, a final-domain return route, or another approved system—remains a production detail, but it must preserve the rule that the player is **not locked in before leaving Last Shelter**.

### Supersession

This explicitly supersedes older authority stating that:

- launching the final operation at Cresthaven is the true point of no return;
- entering Chapter 13 immediately ends all world access;
- the cleanup window necessarily ends before the player reaches Last Shelter.

The Cresthaven launch remains the **start of Chapter 13/final operation**, not the irreversible lock.

## 4. Master-map versus local-route scale rule — reaffirmed

A route can be mandatory and playable without requiring a permanent decorative world-map road or label.

Master-map roads are reserved for large persistent interregional/logistical connections. Chapter-local service roads, military tracks, mountain cuts, old routes, transfer corridors, facility approaches, and dungeon approaches may exist at field/sub-map scale.

Do not add permanent master-map clutter merely because a chapter uses a local path.
