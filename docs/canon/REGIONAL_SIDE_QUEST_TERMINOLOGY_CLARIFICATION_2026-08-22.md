# Diyse — Regional Side-Quest Terminology Clarification

**Date:** August 22, 2026  
**Status:** LOCKED CLARIFICATION under v1.89 / Audit104 and inherited v1.88 / Audit103 quest architecture.

This clarification records the intended relationship between the existing **10 ordinary non-story/non-Hunt side quests** and the informal phrase **regional quests**.

## Locked clarification

The existing ten ordinary side quests **are the game's regional side-quest layer**.

They are not a separate system from “regional quests,” and no additional third quest category called `Regional Quests` is implied or required.

Current optional quest structure therefore remains:

1. **Character Quests** — six character-centered optional quests, one for each permanent party member.
2. **Regional side quests** — the existing ten ordinary side quests established by Audit103; these provide local/regional stories, civic texture, settlement problems, reconstruction concerns, and recurring-supporting-character material across Yahtrea.
3. **Hunts** — the separate Regional Hunt / Major Hunt combat structure already controlled by Hunt canon.

The phrase **regional quest** may be used conversationally or in design discussion to refer to one of the ten ordinary side quests when its function is primarily regional/local. It does **not** create an additional quest count, quest reward structure, progression track, or separate menu category unless one is explicitly approved later.

Audit103's count of **exactly 10 ordinary side quests** is unchanged.

The currently established ten remain:
- Edda Harth — **The Marks We Leave**.
- Dunmere neighborhood waterkeeper — **The Water Between Houses**.
- Crown Princess Mirena — **A Measure of Bread**.
- Ivorybridge bridgekeeper / lampwright — **The Dark Span**.
- Stonewake forge steward — **One Fire Burning**.
- Crown Princess Mirena — **The Crown's Debt**.
- Talia Rell — **The Third Caravan**.
- Talia Rell — **The Living List**.
- Edda Harth — **When the Roads Open**.
- Crown Princess Mirena — **What We Build After**.

No additional regional-quest set is added by this clarification.
