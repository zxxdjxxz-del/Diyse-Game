## 2. Crownfall Redoubt geography and reoccupation — LOCKED

Crownfall Redoubt is the correct site for Rhazek’s Chapter 9 invasion because it lies on Yahtrea’s western frontier near the eastern edge of the Black Mountains. Larkspire is too far inside Yahtrean territory to function as the believable target of this large Black Host offensive.

Crownfall history/state:

- Crownfall was historically abandoned/ruined.
- Yahtrea reoccupies it shortly before Chapter 9 after western Black Host activity escalates.
- The Army hastily restores only the strategically useful upper/outer military works before Rhazek attacks.
- The restored fortress should visibly read as incomplete: old Crown stone and damaged works mixed with new braces, fresh Army emplacements, field medical areas, supplies, temporary repairs, and recently reopened military spaces.

Chapter 9 active geography includes:

- eastern/rear approach;
- upper/outer walls and defensive works;
- barracks / command yard;
- gatehouse;
- western ramparts / front;
- inner barrier and gate machinery.

The **older deep Crownfall ruins remain geographically distinct**.

### 2.1 Major Hunt separation

The existing post-Chapter-5 Major Hunt at Crownfall remains valid:

- **Crownless Siege Marshal → Oathbound Colossus**
- unlock: after Chapter 5;
- route: deeper lower/buried Crownfall fortifications and ruins.

Chapter 9 does not overwrite or consume that Hunt route. Rhazek’s infiltration force may exploit an old lower access route connected to the ruined depths, but the mandatory Chapter 9 story path does not reuse the Major Hunt chamber itself.

## 3. S049 — Crownfall invasion — LOCKED

After the Equal Mercy / Last Sanctuary portion concludes, the party receives urgent word that Crownfall is under a major Black Host assault and that **Commander Rhazek has been positively identified**.

The attack is a genuine military invasion, not a raid or small covert strike.

Locked battlefield structure:

- Yahtrean forces are already fighting before the permanent party arrives.
- The heroes do not single-handedly create Crownfall’s defense.
- **Commander Maevra Solmar** is the senior Yahtrean field commander at Crownfall.
- Maevra has been tasked with turning the hastily reoccupied Redoubt into a functioning western defensive position.
- The party enters from the eastern/rear side while the western works are under heavy assault.
- S049 gameplay emphasizes battlefield progression, stabilizing failing defensive positions, rescuing cut-off soldiers, and fighting organized Black Host formations.
- Rhazek’s army should visibly use coordinated shield, ranged, breach, support, and command elements rather than a collection of unrelated monsters.

### 3.1 Seyrik / Rhazek tactical continuity

Seyrik increasingly recognizes Rhazek’s command doctrine during the battle. He can identify or predict rotations, feints, fallback behavior, pressure changes, and the moment when the Host stops trying to break through and begins deliberately fixing Crownfall’s defenders in place.

This is a direct payoff to Audit91’s Rhazek/Seyrik continuity without making Chapter 9 cease to be Ilyra’s thematic chapter.

### 3.2 Rhazek’s strategic objective

Rhazek attacks Crownfall now because Yahtrea’s reactivation of the fortress threatens Black Host movement from the Black Mountains into Yahtrean territory.

His objective is **not merely to occupy Crownfall**.

He intends to **permanently disable Crownfall’s inner barrier/gate machinery** so the western route cannot be properly sealed again without major reconstruction.

The large western assault is partly a diversion intended to pin Crownfall’s main defenders while Rhazek and a smaller elite infiltration team enter the fortress through an old lower/internal access route.

The critical S049 reveal is therefore:

- the western line is being held in place;
- Rhazek is already inside Crownfall;
- his infiltration team is moving toward the inner barrier machinery.

## 4. S050 — Mercy Is Not Surrender — LOCKED

S050 begins immediately after the S049 reveal with no world-map or location transition.

### 4.1 Pursuit structure

- Maevra remains with the larger Crownfall defense because abandoning the western line would turn Rhazek’s diversion into a genuine breakthrough.
- The permanent six pursue Rhazek’s infiltration force through damaged/compromised upper Crownfall spaces toward the inner gate/barrier machinery.
- The pursuit does not descend into or replay the deep Major Hunt dungeon.
- Seyrik’s knowledge of Rhazek’s doctrine remains tactically useful during the chase.
- Ilyra’s chapter theme continues through authored beats showing that helping wounded defenders/support personnel and completing the mission are compatible responsibilities.

### 4.2 Demolition objective

By the time the party reaches Rhazek, sabotage of the inner barrier has begun, but permanent destruction can still be prevented.

The immediate story objective is therefore:

**stop the demolition / preserve Crownfall’s ability to seal the western route.**

Rhazek’s defeat is important because of the mission, not because reducing his HP is the only narrative objective.

## 5. Commander Rhazek boss architecture — LOCKED

### 5.1 Phase 1 — disciplined commander

Rhazek’s first phase presents the commander at his most controlled and militarily competent.

Approved combat identity:

- coordinated elite support;
- battlefield positioning;
- target marking;
- formation management;
- command actions;
- counters and tactical pressure;
- finite support rather than endless disposable reinforcements.

The party disrupts/destroys the demolition network during this phase and causes Rhazek’s original strategic operation to fail.

### 5.2 Escalation after mission failure

Rhazek refuses to withdraw after the demolition plan is lost.

The Black Host brought a specialized **siege/breaching apparatus** intended to physically destroy fortifications if conventional demolition failed.

Rhazek forcibly couples/integrates himself with this apparatus even though it was not intended as a normal human combat system.

This produces the second-phase identity:

**BASTION DEVOURER**

The title now has literal military meaning: the apparatus is a fortification-breaking weapon, and Rhazek turns himself into a living siege engine.

### 5.3 Seyrik thematic parallel

The transformation deliberately parallels Seyrik’s Black Host arc:

- both men were shaped by the Black Host toward greater usefulness;
- Seyrik ultimately rejects that system;
- Rhazek voluntarily pushes the same logic to its extreme and turns himself into a weapon rather than accept failure.

### 5.4 Phase 2 — destructive siege form

Bastion Devourer is destructive/breaching-focused rather than command-tactical.

Its attacks threaten:

- the party;
- surviving Crownfall machinery;
- the physical arena / fortress structure.

As the siege form is damaged, the external Black Host material and Rhazek’s original armor progressively break away.

### 5.5 Final exposed Rhazek segment

The encounter ends with a **short final exposed/unarmored Rhazek segment**, using his established exposed visual state.

This is **not** another full fresh-HP boss form.

By this point:

- the demolition objective has failed;
- Crownfall’s inner barrier is saved;
- Rhazek no longer has a viable military objective;
- he continues fighting because he refuses to stop.

## 6. Ilyra / mercy thesis — LOCKED

Chapter 9’s controlling moral distinction is:

**Mercy does not mean permitting a fully aware aggressor to continue harming others.**

Equal Mercy establishes that sick, compromised, coerced, confused, wounded, or otherwise victim-class people are not automatically enemies to be destroyed.

Rhazek is the deliberate contrast:

- he is fully aware;
- he has chosen the invasion;
- he knowingly endangers the people behind Crownfall’s line;
- he continues escalating after his strategic mission has already failed.

Stopping Rhazek is therefore fully compatible with Ilyra’s mercy rather than a contradiction of it.

## 7. Crownfall outcome — LOCKED

- Rhazek is defeated.
- Crownfall’s inner barrier survives.
- The main Black Host force withdraws when the infiltration/demolition operation fails.
- Yahtrea’s Army is responsible for holding the fortress against the main invasion.
- The permanent party’s decisive contribution is preventing Rhazek’s internal sabotage from turning that defense into a strategic defeat.
- Crownfall survives badly damaged but remains a viable western defensive position.
- Chapter 9 closes at Crownfall rather than Caelora.

## 8. Regional Hunt #9 — Larkspire — LOCKED

The former post-Chapter-9 **Palace Emergency Marshal** Hunt is retired/superseded. It no longer belongs to Chapter 9 because the Caelora continuity-crackdown structure has been removed.

Regional Hunt #9 is now a **post-Chapter-9 return to Larkspire**.

Crownfall does **not** receive Regional Hunt #9; its Chapter 9 purpose is the Rhazek invasion story, while its separate post-Chapter-5 Major Hunt remains deeper in the ruins.

### 8.1 Hunt identity

**MERCYFALLEN BEHEMOTH** is the locked Regional Hunt #9 boss.

It is an ancient therapeutic/recovery organism or engineered medical beast whose regenerative purpose catastrophically overgrew after centuries of malfunction.

Chapter 9’s restoration/reactivation of Larkspire systems exposes, destabilizes, or breaks the old containment that had kept it isolated.

It is a dangerous optional organism/monster, **not** a human/patient nonlethal target. This keeps the chapter’s mercy rules clear.

### 8.2 Hunt route

Locked return route:

**Larkspire Lower Hospice → Abandoned Quarantine Walk → Recovery Reservoir → Mercyfallen Behemoth arena**

This branch expands Larkspire without replaying the mandatory Equal Mercy path as a second full story dungeon.

### 8.3 Boss mechanics

Mercyfallen Behemoth’s approved mechanical identity centers on:

- regeneration;
- reallocation of defenses/resistances;
- absorption of magical effects and/or adaptation to repeated damage patterns;
- opening vulnerable nodes/organs when power is redirected into recovery.

The Hunt uses **one sustained HP bar**. It does not refill into a fresh second bar.

### 8.4 First-clear reward

Regional Hunt #9 retains the pre-existing protected first-clear reward:

**PRISMATIC RESERVE — Resource Standard Card**

Its existing function remains compatible:

- party elemental protection;
- temporary Spirit increase.

The reward is a pre-existing Card released/recovered through the Hunt; the Behemoth does not create the Card.
