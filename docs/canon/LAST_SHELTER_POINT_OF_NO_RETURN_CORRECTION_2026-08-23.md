# Last Shelter Point-of-No-Return Correction — 2026-08-23

**Status:** LOCKED / BOUNDED CORRECTION TO FINAL-ACT AVAILABILITY AUTHORITY  
**Scope:** Chapter 13 point of no return and cleanup/return access only.

This correction inherits all compatible v1.93 / Audit108, v1.92 / Audit107, v1.74 / Audit89, and Audit84 authority, but explicitly supersedes the older rule that the deliberate launch of the final operation from Cresthaven is itself the true final point of no return.

## Approved point of no return

**Last Shelter is the final point of no return.**

The player may begin Chapter 13 / the final operation and progress through the early final-domain sequence without yet losing world-return access.

Controlling Chapter 13 progression remains:

**The Deepest City → Deep City → Last Weapon Archive → Last Weapon Archon → Last Shelter → Reactor Galleries → Reactor–Crest Interface → Crest Integration / The Last Command → Final Severance → aftermath/ascent/return.**

The lock occurs only when the player deliberately **passes beyond Last Shelter into the Reactor Galleries / final approach**.

## Availability before Last Shelter

Before crossing the Last Shelter exit threshold:

- the player may still retreat/return from the Chapter 13 final domain;
- eligible world cleanup and still-available optional content remain accessible;
- the Cresthaven / Vhalmarch Forward Hub return architecture remains usable where otherwise compatible;
- the player may revisit equipment, Cards, upgrades, Character-Life scenes, Hunts, optional bosses, and other still-eligible content;
- entering Chapter 13, entering The Deepest City, reaching Deep City, completing the Last Weapon Archive, and defeating the Last Weapon Archon do **not** by themselves close world access.

Exact return implementation may use established fast-travel / secured-route logic and does not require replaying the entire final-domain approach.

## Last Shelter function

Last Shelter is the final substantial recovery, loadout, save, and preparation space before the irreversible endgame sequence.

At the exit from Last Shelter, the game should present an explicit authored warning that proceeding will prevent return to the wider world until the ending.

Once the player confirms and leaves Last Shelter for the Reactor Galleries:

- world return / fast travel is disabled;
- unfinished optional content is no longer accessible for that playthrough;
- the remaining sequence is continuous endgame progression through Reactor Galleries, Reactor–Crest Interface, Reconstituted Entity, Crest Integration / The Last Command, Final Severance, and the ending/aftermath;
- no later restoration of ordinary free-roam occurs before credits/ending resolution.

## Supersession

The following older interpretations are superseded:

- `launch final operation from Cresthaven = true final point of no return`;
- `starting Chapter 13 closes all cleanup access`;
- `entering The Deepest City closes all cleanup access`.

The deliberate Cresthaven launch remains the **start of Chapter 13 / final operation**, but it is no longer the irreversible lock point.

**Final irreversible threshold:** **exit Last Shelter and proceed into Reactor Galleries.**
