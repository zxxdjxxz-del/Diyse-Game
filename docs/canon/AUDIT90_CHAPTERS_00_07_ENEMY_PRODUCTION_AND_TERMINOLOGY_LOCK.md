## Global enemy-production rules

- **Area-first ecology:** enemy identities are assigned to locations/ecologies first rather than spread uniformly across a chapter.
- **No species quota:** an area does not require a minimum number of unique species.
- **No fixed random-battle quota:** roster approval does not establish a preset battle count.
- **Carryover rule:** earlier enemies may return whenever ecology, faction presence, and story context still fit. Prefer a fitting carryover over a new bespoke enemy created only to inflate variety.
- **Upgraded-form rule:** stronger returning forms require a meaningful behavioral, equipment, targeting, formation, defensive, status, or support change; HP inflation alone is insufficient.
- **Asset reuse does not equal lore identity:** shared rigs/animation/material/VFX foundations do not collapse distinct creatures into one species.
- **Elite / Hunt / story-boss separation:** these are separate encounter roles unless a later explicit authority says otherwise.
- **Authored nonlethal boundary:** lawful personnel, coerced people, prisoners, patients, harmed researchers, uncertain turncoats, and other victim-class humans are not ordinary random-farm enemies. Use authored subdual, withdrawal, stabilization, rescue, exposure, capture, or comparable resolutions.
- **Boss-form discipline:** same-body escalation normally stays on one HP bar; fresh full-health forms are reserved for genuine physical/narrative transformations already authorized.

## Party-only “Sixfold” terminology

“**Sixfold**” is reserved exclusively for concepts directly tied to the permanent six and their shared party progression/identity.

Valid examples include **Sixfold Accord** and Nimera's **Sixfold Knight** subclass.

Non-party enemies, creatures, bosses, facilities, locations, experiments, constructs, and environmental systems may not use “Sixfold.”

## Approved production-family foundations

These are production/asset relationships, not necessarily literal in-world promotion chains.

- **Black Host infantry:** Black Host Raider → Brackenwall Reaver → Way-Fort Marauder → Black Host Sky Skirmisher → later occupation/spatial infantry such as Occupation Infantry and Rift Gate Trooper.
- **Black Host ranged:** Black Host Crossbowman → Bastion Crossbow Guard → Rift Boltman → Rift Artillerist.
- **Black Host defenders:** Ruin Shieldbearer → Bastion Shield Guard → Black Host Forge Guard; later heavy defenders may continue the foundation where fiction fits.
- **Black Host casters:** Transfer Adept → Black Host Ward-Sorcerer → Black Host War-Sorcerer → Red Brand Adept.
- **Heavy Ruin humanoids:** Ruin Vanguard Pursuer → related industrial/heavy forms such as Ruin Hammerman → Ruin Vanguard Reaper.
- **Rift Hound:** direct recurring family with context-appropriate tuning/equipment.
- **Archive constructs:** Memory Scribe / Vault Sentinel / Archive Duplicant establish the record-construct language; Archive Scribe Engine, Judgment Frame, Grand Inquisitor Frame, and Archive Judgment Engine extend it.
- **Hollow Watch constructs:** Hollow Watch Sentry + Hollow Watch Ballista → Watch Captain Frame → Hollow Watch Castellan language.
- **Natural predator line:** Greenhollow Stalker establishes a reusable wilderness-predator family; Briarhide Stalker and Elder Briarhide are named alpha/elder extensions.
- Reaction Annex elemental devices/creatures, Deepforge maintenance constructs, Weather Crown ecology, and Prison of Names identity systems remain locally coherent even when sharing underlying production technology.
