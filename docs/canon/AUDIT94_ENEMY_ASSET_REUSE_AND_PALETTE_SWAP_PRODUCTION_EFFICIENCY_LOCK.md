## Production-efficiency targets
- Whole-game chapter-production count basis: **160 combat identities**.
- Roughly **40–45** identities should be producible as true or near-true palette/material swaps.
- Roughly **60%** of the roster — about **96 identities** — should avoid requiring a completely new base sprite/rig through palette, material, gear, prop, VFX, animation, or modular-silhouette reuse.
- Only roughly **20–25** identities should read to the player as obvious palette swaps.
- Most reused identities should be visually differentiated cheaply through helmets/headpieces, shoulder silhouettes, weapons/props, cloaks/cloth, horns/crests, scale changes, glow patterns, particles/aura, material passes, idle poses, minor armor geometry, or one altered attack animation.
- Bespoke production spend is concentrated on bosses, Regional Hunts, major creatures, transformation bodies, and encounter-defining silhouettes.
- Shared production assets never imply shared lore identity.

These are production-budget/readability targets, not enemy-count quotas. They cannot override story identity, ecology, locked exact visual authority, or combat readability.
