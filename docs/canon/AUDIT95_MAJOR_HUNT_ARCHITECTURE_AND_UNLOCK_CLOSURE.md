## Global Major Hunt locks
- Exactly **6 Major Hunts**, distinct from exactly **11 Regional Hunts**.
- Current Hunt-class optional-combat total: **17 = 11 Regional + 6 Major**. Older 18-Hunt / 12-Regional language is superseded.
- All six Major Hunts are optional and deterministic/nonfarmable on first clear.
- Each awards one pre-existing optional Prime; bosses do not create Cards.
- The six Major-Hunt Prime rewards cover all six Faces exactly once.
- There is no secret seventh Major Hunt.
- Major Hunts are not required to launch Chapter 12.

## Major Hunt sequence
1. **Ashen Whitehorn** — Ashfrost Expanse; unlock only after Chapter 4 fully ends; one sustained HP bar; Dawn Shepherd Prime.
2. **Crownless Siege Marshal → Crownless War Engine** — deep old Crownfall ruins after Chapter 5; genuine fresh-HP second form; Oathbound Colossus Prime.
3. **Concordance Guardian** — Concordance Vault after Sixfold Accord; one bar with six-protocol mastery trial; Living Revision Prime.
4. **Worldscar Leviathan** — Worldscar Basin after Chapter 8; one bar with Prismatic Confluence / Scarwake / Worldscar Surge states; Prismatic Leviathan Prime.
5. **Final Archive Arbiter** — Final Archives after Chapter 10; one bar with Custody protocols; Sheltering Host Prime. Clear exposes the deeper sealed descent but does not by itself open Worldframe Depths.
6. **The Unfinished World → Worldheart** — Worldframe Depths inside Final Archives; genuine fresh-HP second form; Starfall Engine Prime; final/hardest Major Hunt.

## Worldframe dual gate
Worldframe Depths opens only when **both** conditions are true:
1. Final Archive Arbiter / Final Archives Major-Hunt route has been cleared; and
2. Emperor Vaelkor has been defeated at the end of Chapter 11.

Either condition may be completed first, but both are required. Worldframe remains available in the post-Vaelkor cleanup window before deliberate Chapter 12 launch.
