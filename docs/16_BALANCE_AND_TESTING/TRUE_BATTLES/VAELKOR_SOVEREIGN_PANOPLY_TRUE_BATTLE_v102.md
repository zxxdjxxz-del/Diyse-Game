# 4. Sovereign Overrun timing boundary

Mandatory Lv56 Vaelkor is normally faster than all four reference characters.

Therefore:
> pressing Defend immediately after Sovereign Overrun Preparation is **not** automatically sufficient.

Standard Guard lasts only from that character's Defend action through the end of the current normal round. If Vaelkor acts first next round, that Guard has already expired.

The warning window instead supports current legal responses such as:
- recovery / cleansing;
- persistent Defense/Spirit mitigation;
- Set the Pace or other legal Speed preparation for the next initiative setup;
- Staggered pressure;
- a legally Ready Prime invocation;
- a correctly timed Defend where relative initiative actually permits it.

The simulation does not secretly extend Guard duration.

# 9. Representative mandatory no-Prime flow

One median-length prepared run cleared in **21 rounds**, with no KO and one full Sovereign Overrun Preparation → Resolution sequence.

- **R1–R3:** Vaelira seeks Burn, Torren establishes Hunter's Measure/Throughline, Ilyra establishes Vow of Severance, and Cyanis begins the Crest Strike → Crest Rend cycle. Imperial Guard is treated as a real following-round defense state rather than ignored.
- **R4–R6:** Great Beast Falls and Arcanum Ascendant supply meaningful burst while ordinary MP pressure remains visible. Nullifying Seal answers Imperial Guard when preserving the next round is worth the action.
- **R7–R9:** healing/cleansing and low-cost offense continue while Form I approaches collapse.
- **R10:** the genuine fresh Sovereign Panoply body is active at full **20,200 HP**. Form-I target states do not carry over; party HP/MP do.
- **R10–R15:** Hunter's Measure, Throughline, Burn/Bleed opportunities, and Vow of Severance are rebuilt against the new body. Absolute Command and control pressure create real support costs.
- **R16–R18:** MP recovery becomes necessary while the party continues dealing direct damage rather than falling into an immediate item-only loop.
- **R19:** Sovereign Overrun Preparation begins below the authored threshold. Vaelira attempts Stonebreak, Torren uses Set the Pace, Ilyra maintains sustain, and Cyanis continues pressure.
- **R20:** the Speed setup allows Cyanis to act before Vaelkor. Sovereign Overrun still resolves and deals real party-wide pressure; the party survives without a hidden Guard-duration extension.
- **R21:** the Panoply remains on the same Form-II bar in Final Sovereignty and is finished before another body can appear.

This representative flow proves:
1. genuine fresh-body persistence without party-resource refresh;
2. target-state rebuilding after body replacement;
3. meaningful MP/item attrition;
4. Sovereign Overrun's warning window and response systems;
5. Final Sovereignty as a same-bar pressure state rather than a third form;
6. no need for a hidden defensive command or new resource system.
