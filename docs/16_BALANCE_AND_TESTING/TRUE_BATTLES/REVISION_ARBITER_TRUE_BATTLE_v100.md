# 3. PLAYER POLICY

The policy is smart/normal rather than perfect-information scripting.

## Closed Record
The first three distinct actions deliberately spend the 80%-reduced window on setup/defensive value where possible:
- Torren uses **Sizing Shot** to establish Hunter's Measure;
- Vaelira uses **Stonebreak** for low-cost elemental/status pressure;
- Cyanis may use **Crest Reprisal** instead of wasting a large direct hit into the gate;
- Ilyra uses support as needed or contributes ordinary damage once the third layer has fallen.

The gate therefore consumes action value without requiring the player to throw three strongest attacks into 80% reduction.

## Adjudication
- Torren maintains Hunter's Measure and favors Colossus Draw while MP permits;
- Cyanis uses Harmonized Crest sequencing between physical setup and Crest Rend;
- Vaelira alternates standard elements to benefit from Prismatic Flow;
- Ilyra heals/cleanses when thresholds justify it and otherwise uses Warden's Valor;
- a Revision Claim is answered by changing command category rather than blindly accepting the 25% damage tax;
- Balance Seal / MP recovery may be used when they also provide a meaningful command-category change.

## Open Revision
The two short reconciliation layers are stripped by distinct ordinary party actions. Smart play favors setup/support actions where practical rather than spending the highest-damage action into the 40% reduction.

# 8. REPRESENTATIVE STRICT NO-PRIME FLOW

One median-length strict run cleared in **11 rounds**:

- **R1:** Registry Pulse; Stonebreak + Sizing Shot + Crest Reprisal efficiently remove the opening Assertion Layers; Ilyra attacks after the gate opens.
- **R2:** first Revision Claim appears; the claimed character changes command category instead of accepting the ongoing damage penalty.
- **R3–R6:** normal Adjudication pressure; Stun/stat-down responses consume support value while Hunter's Measure / Crest sequencing / Prismatic Flow continue to reward correct class play.
- **R7:** Bleed pressure and normal damage drive the boss through 40% HP; Open Revision begins. Cyanis uses Crest Reprisal and Ilyra contributes the second distinct action, stripping the two Open Revision Layers without wasting both best damage actions into 40% reduction.
- **R8–R10:** Open Revision uses Final Reconciliation, Recursive Judgment, and Identity Severance while the party's MP pool becomes meaningfully thin.
- **R11:** Open Revision Lance lands; Vaelira resolves the final hit.

This flow demonstrates all three intended encounter lessons:
1. use setup/support actions intelligently during damage-reduction gates;
2. vary command category under Revision Claim;
3. survive late Open Revision pressure after resources have been taxed.
