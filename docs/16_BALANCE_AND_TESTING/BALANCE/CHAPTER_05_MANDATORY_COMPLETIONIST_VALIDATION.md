## 8. Regional Hunt #5 — Whitehorn Ravager
Current recommendation:
> **Lv26**

Current body:
- Lv26
- **8,678 HP**
- ATK104 / MAG74 / DEF70 / Spirit64 / SPD41
- one bar

Chapter-5 access does **not** mean Chapter-5-clear difficulty.

A mandatory chapter-clear party is roughly **Lv22**. A fixed-content completionist route before clearing Whitehorn is roughly **Lv23**, with incidental optional combat capable of pushing somewhat higher. Both are intentionally below the Hunt's recommendation.

At early Lv22–23 access:
- Snowbound Rush and Ravager Gore are dangerous but not automatic full-HP deletions;
- the 8,678-HP body creates a long endurance test;
- attempting the Hunt early is a legitimate challenge rather than the tuning benchmark.

At the proper **Lv26** benchmark, current body/Powers retain Regional-Hunt-scale endurance while still allowing strong party builds, Cards, and Prime use to produce a meaningful completionist advantage.

**Verdict: PASS / RETAIN Lv26 RECOMMENDATION AND 8,678 HP.**

Do not weaken Whitehorn Ravager to Lv22 merely because its optional branch exists in Chapter 5.
