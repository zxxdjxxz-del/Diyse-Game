# Diyse — Optional Content Balance

**Status:** ACTIVE OPTIONAL-CONTENT BALANCE FRAMEWORK  
**Quest authority:** `../../11_QUESTS/`  
**Progression authority:** `../../10_PROGRESSION_AND_EXP/`

Current optional authored content:
- **5** ordinary Side Quests;
- **6** Character Quests;
- **8** active Regional Hunts;
- **6** Major Hunts.

Exact EXP/CEXP/G rewards are not owned here and remain subject to their current rebuilds.

## Test goals

Optional content should:
- feel materially rewarding;
- accelerate or broaden progression without becoming mandatory for story completion;
- create an earned advantage in later fixed encounters;
- avoid requiring repetitive grind;
- respect current unlock/cutoff rules.

## Character Quests

Verify:
- correct unlock;
- optional status;
- current boss/no-boss architecture from the quest/enemy owners;
- current completion reward family;
- no required Story Prime or ending gate.

## Side Quests

Verify:
- current five-quest roster only;
- removed quests do not reactivate or pay rewards;
- no accidental unique-boss creep where current quest design does not call for one.

## Hunts

Verify:
- current active count/unlocks;
- first-clear/reward once-only behavior;
- fixed tuning rather than dynamic scaling;
- current reward family;
- Major Hunts remain above-level optional challenges.

Historical optional EXP totals and cap-proof packages are not current acceptance targets while progression/reward values are being rebuilt.
