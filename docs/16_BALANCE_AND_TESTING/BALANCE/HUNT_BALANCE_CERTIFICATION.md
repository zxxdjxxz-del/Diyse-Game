## Difficulty principles

- Hunts use fixed authored encounters; no dynamic player-level scaling.
- Regional Hunts should sit above ordinary/Elite content available around their access window.
- Major Hunts are intentionally above-level optional challenges.
- Completionist progression may create a substantial earned advantage.
- Recommended levels are **not locked here** while the Player EXP/CEXP model is being rebuilt.
- Enemy/Hunt action kits and direct-damage Powers remain subject to the current enemy-ability/difficulty revalidation frontier.

## Prime/form boundary

Boss-form changes do not restore spent Prime identities merely because a fresh HP body begins. Prime availability and post-dismissal spacing follow the current Prime-system owner.

## Recertification inputs

For each Hunt, validate against:
- current unlock timing;
- current mandatory/high-side Player Level and CEXP state;
- current fixed enemy body/form architecture;
- current action kit and raw stats;
- ordinary equipment/consumable assumptions;
- optional Card/Prime access where legal.

Record:
- temporary KOs;
- wipes;
- ending HP/MP;
- item/recovery pressure;
- mechanic-response pressure;
- duration secondarily.

Historical recommended-level ladders, Power-completeness PASS labels, and recertification stats remain Git-history evidence only.
