Current hierarchy:
> **Ordinary < Elite < mandatory story boss < Regional Hunt < Major Hunt**

This is a class-level design rule, not a requirement that every individual HP value monotonically exceed every earlier encounter.

Effective challenge includes:
- HP/body count;
- Defense/Spirit;
- Speed;
- Evasion;
- Status Resistance;
- supports;
- action taxes;
- status pressure;
- targeting;
- recovery;
- fresh forms;
- Prime refresh opportunities;
- knowledge/mechanical execution.

## Optional Elites
Exactly:
> **12 numbered-chapter Elites**

No approved Chapter-10 Elite.

Typical intended fight length:
> approximately **2–4 serious party rounds**

depending on build/level/resources/mechanics.

## Early mandatory bosses
Audit129 specifically keeps Chapters 1–4 complete main-boss encounters around:
> **6–9 effective combat rounds**

Shorter nonlethal/miniboss encounters may be faster.

Do not extrapolate that exact 6–9-round number as a hard universal rule for every late-game boss.
