# Diyse — Encounter Difficulty Hierarchy

**Status:** ACTIVE DIFFICULTY-TIER FRAMEWORK  
**Enemy hierarchy authority:** `../../09_ENEMIES_AND_ENCOUNTERS/README.md`

Current hierarchy:
> **baseline ordinary enemy < strong normal-pool enemy < mandatory story boss < Regional Hunt < Major Hunt**

There is **no separate Elite encounter category**. Stronger normal identities belong to the ordinary enemy pool.

This hierarchy is a design rule, not a requirement that every later encounter have more HP than every earlier encounter.

Effective challenge may include:
- HP/body count;
- Defense/Spirit;
- Speed;
- Evasion;
- Status Resistance;
- supports/components;
- action economy and action density;
- status pressure;
- targeting;
- recovery/resource pressure;
- fresh-body or same-bar mechanics;
- Prime availability already carried into the encounter;
- knowledge/mechanical execution.

Boss form/state changes do **not** restore spent Prime identities merely because a transition occurs.

## Hunts

Recommended level is preparedness guidance, not an access gate.

Tuning is fixed/authored:
> **no dynamic player-level scaling**

Major Hunts are intentionally above-level optional challenges at their relevant access windows.

## Acceptance boundary

Measure temporary KOs, wipes, ending HP/MP, recovery pressure, mechanic-response pressure, and duration against the current encounter owner. Round count is one observation, not a universal tier definition.
