# Diyse — Encounter Difficulty Hierarchy

**Status:** ACTIVE DIFFICULTY-TIER FRAMEWORK  
**Enemy hierarchy authority:** `../../09_ENEMIES_AND_ENCOUNTERS/README.md`

Current hierarchy:

> **baseline ordinary enemy < strong normal-pool enemy < mandatory story boss < Regional Hunt < Major Hunt**

There is no separate Elite encounter category.

Effective challenge may include:
- HP/body count;
- Defense/Spirit;
- Speed and TURN pressure;
- supports/components;
- action Potency and target coverage;
- status pressure and susceptibility;
- Delay/Interrupt behavior;
- targeting;
- recovery/resource pressure;
- fresh-body or same-bar mechanics;
- Prime availability entering the encounter;
- mechanic knowledge/execution.

Hunts use fixed/authored tuning by default rather than unrestricted player-level scaling.

Measure temporary KOs, wipes, ending HP/MP, recovery pressure, mechanic-response pressure, and timeline duration.
