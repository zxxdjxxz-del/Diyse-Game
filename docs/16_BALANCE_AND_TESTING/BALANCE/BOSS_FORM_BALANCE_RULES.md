## Same-bar state change
- one HP bar;
- no fake second reward;
- no Prime availability refresh;
- no refill;
- state mechanics may change.

## Genuine fresh form
- new full HP body/form;
- Prime availability refreshes;
- reward paid after final encounter completion;
- no intermediate victory payout.
