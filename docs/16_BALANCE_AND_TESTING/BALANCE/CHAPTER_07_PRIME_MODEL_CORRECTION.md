# Chapter 7 — Prime-Model Correction Handoff

**Status:** ACTIVE CORRECTION TO THE v83 CHAPTER-7 PAPER VALIDATION  
**Scope:** Prime restoration/refresh statements only. All other Chapter-7 validation claims remain unchanged unless separately revised.

The Chapter-7 validation predates the current persistent Prime-spend model and contains a stale statement in its Major Hunt #2 handoff.

## Retired statement
The old validation says that the genuine fresh **Crownless War Engine** body refreshes Prime availability once.

That is retired.

## Current rule
Under current Prime authority:
- a genuine fresh-HP boss form does **not** restore a spent Prime identity;
- an unused Ready Prime remains Ready normally;
- a Prime already spent earlier in the battle remains spent until a valid rest or explicitly authored restoration effect restores it;
- Emergency Kit may restore acquired Prime identities to Ready, but does not bypass the separate post-dismissal two-normal-round spacing gate.

Therefore the Crownless Siege Marshal → Crownless War Engine transition does **not** create a Prime refresh.

The current Major Hunt #2 raw stats, Power values, two-body architecture, unlock, and recommendation remain closed unless a later true-battle certification demonstrates an actual balance failure under the persistent-spend model.
