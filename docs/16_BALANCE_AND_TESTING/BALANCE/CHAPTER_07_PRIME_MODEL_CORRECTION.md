## Current rule

For Prime availability:
- each Prime identity has one use per enemy HP body unless explicitly restored;
- spent/Ready state is battle-scoped;
- same-bar state changes do not refresh Prime availability;
- a genuine fresh-HP boss form **does refresh Prime availability** when the new body becomes active;
- fresh-body refresh does **not** cancel or shorten the separate post-dismissal spacing gate;
- after a Prime dismissal, **3 full normal party rounds** must complete before another Ready Prime may be invoked;
- Prime invocation and Prime commands cost **0 MP**.

Therefore:

> **Crownless Siege Marshal → Crownless War Engine refreshes Prime availability when the fresh War Engine body begins.**
