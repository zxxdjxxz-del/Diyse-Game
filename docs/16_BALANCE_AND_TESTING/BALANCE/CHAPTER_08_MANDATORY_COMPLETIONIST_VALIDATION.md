# Chapter 8 — Mandatory vs Completionist Enemy/Boss Validation

**Version:** v84  
**Status:** **PASS / VALIDATED WITH EXPLICIT FORMATION/TIMING DEPENDENCIES**  
**Power-audit status:** **CLOSED — no direct-damage Power changed**

## Purpose
Validate Chapter 8 against the party state that actually exists at each point rather than treating **Lv37** as the whole-chapter baseline.

Chapter 8 is the first full main-story chapter after Sixfold Volition. Therefore:
- all six permanent characters are available;
- the active battle party remains **maximum 4**;
- Subclasses are legal for the full chapter;
- mandatory-route Subclass training is still comparatively fresh early in the chapter;
- completionist parties may have materially stronger Subclass training, optional gear, Cards, Primes, and level advantage;
- optional progression is allowed to make fixed encounters faster/easier and is not dynamically erased.

The global direct-damage Power audit remains closed. Existing Powers are retained unless a specific encounter fails its intended role.
