# Diyse — Encounter Pacing Test Plan

**Status:** ACTIVE ENCOUNTER-PACING QA FRAMEWORK  
**Encounter authority:** `../../09_ENEMIES_AND_ENCOUNTERS/`  
**Story/route authority:** `../../02_STORY/`

Encounter pacing targets are derived from current route geometry, current formations, and playable traversal rather than a fixed whole-campaign battle quota.

## Measure from current content

For each traversable route section record:
- time/steps between battles;
- number of ordinary battles actually encountered;
- formation size/distribution;
- back-to-back encounter annoyance;
- long dry stretches;
- post-boss grace;
- pre-boss resource drain;
- safe-hub boundaries;
- optional-route contamination;
- repeat traversal/backtracking burden.

## Acceptance principles

- encounter frequency should support exploration rather than dominate it;
- short connective legs should not overstay through random combat;
- denser/maze-like areas may support more encounter pressure than direct routes;
- route-specific authored no-encounter spaces remain protected;
- later enemy-introduction pacing should not force every identity into the earliest possible formation;
- chapter pacing is evaluated from the **current** formations and route geometry.

### Current early-game examples

- Chapter 1 Brackenwall → Greenhollow leg is intentionally short/straight enough that random encounters do not overstay.
- Southern Briar may carry materially more encounter density/formation variety than that opening leg.
- Chapter 4 must be validated from the current forest-shortcut → Crown-road → Ivorybridge → Reaction-Annex route after its enemy rework closes.

Final encounter-frequency targets should be locked only after current map lengths and formations are playable.
