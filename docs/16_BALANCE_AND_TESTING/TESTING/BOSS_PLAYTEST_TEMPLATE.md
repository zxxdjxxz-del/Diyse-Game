## Questions
1. Was the core mechanic legible?
2. Did a valid response exist?
3. Did raw damage overwhelm mechanics?
4. Did defenses make the fight inert?
5. Did status play matter without trivializing?
6. Did one class/loadout dominate for unintended reasons?
7. Did the boss use enough of its kit?
8. Did fresh/same-bar architecture resolve correctly?
9. Did rewards happen once at the correct endpoint?
10. Did the fight fail because of balance or because of poor communication?
