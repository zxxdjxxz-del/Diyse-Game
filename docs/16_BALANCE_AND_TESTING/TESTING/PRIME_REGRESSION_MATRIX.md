# Diyse — Prime Regression Matrix

**Status:** ACTIVE PRIME REGRESSION MATRIX  
**Prime authority:** `../../07_CARDS/PRIME_CARDS/PRIME_SYSTEM_RULES.md`

Required cases:

1. Unacquired Prime is unavailable.
2. Acquired Story Prime can occupy any legal permanent-character Prime slot.
3. Pre-Volition slot capacity is 1; post-Volition capacity is 2.
4. Invocation costs 0 MP and consumes the invoker's current command opportunity.
5. Recovered Story Prime performs one signature action and demanifests.
6. Recovered does not create an Awakened meter.
7. Awakened manifestation starts with 3 meter segments.
8. Basic / Medium / Heavy cost 1 / 2 / 3 segments.
9. Meter is spent only when the selected command successfully reaches EXECUTION.
10. Interrupted queued Prime command spends no meter.
11. Prime next TURN remains scheduled from command selection even when its queued EXECUTION is canceled.
12. Meter above 0 keeps manifestation active.
13. Meter reaching 0 enters Final Return.
14. Final Return keeps the Prime on field until its already-scheduled next TURN.
15. Legal Delay to that TURN delays Dismissal.
16. Automatic Dismissal resolves at Final Return TURN, then party returns.
17. Manual early dismissal does not trigger Dismissal ability.
18. Prime KO before normal Dismissal does not trigger Dismissal ability.
19. Forced removal does not trigger Dismissal ability.
20. Party TURNs, queued EXECUTIONs, statuses, buffs, and debuffs remain suspended/preserved during Awakened manifestation.
21. Enemy TURNs/EXECUTIONs/statuses continue during manifestation.
22. Prime ability may explicitly heal/revive/buff the suspended party.
23. Duration effects applied to suspended party begin ticking only after return unless explicitly overridden.
24. Queued hostile actions retarget correctly when Prime manifests.
25. Queued hostile actions retarget correctly when Prime demanifests.
26. Reference Level equals highest current Level among all recruited permanent party members.
27. Prime stats do not inherit invoker equipment/stats/state.
28. Spent identity persists across battle end.
29. Full rest/checkpoint/explicit restoration restores eligible spent identities.
30. Boss form/state transitions alone do not restore spent identities.
31. Same-battle restored Awakened Prime starts at full HP, fresh 3-segment meter, and no prior Prime-local temporary state.
32. After demanifestation, party-wide lockout is owned by the invoker.
33. Lockout ends after the owner processes 3 personal TURNs.
34. Owner hidden reserve TURNs count.
35. Other party members' TURNs do not reduce the lockout.
36. Restoration does not bypass an active lockout.
