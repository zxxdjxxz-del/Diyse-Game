- **Chapter 1:** direct Greenhollow–Dunmere road is unavailable because of Black Host control.
- **Chapter 2 opening:** party reaches Dunmere through Wayfinder / southern Briar instead.
- **Chapter 2 finale:** Bastion/Extraction progression exits back toward Greenhollow and breaks the obstruction.
- **Post-Chapter 2:** direct Greenhollow ↔ Dunmere road is reopened and available.
