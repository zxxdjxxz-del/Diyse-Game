## Modern people broadly know
- Ancient Diyseans existed and were human.
- Their civilization was vastly advanced.
- ruins survive above and below ground.
- substantial late-period Diysean activity/population moved underground.
- Cards came from the ancient final era.
- the Crest is a major green-and-gold symbol of modern Yahtrea.

## Modern people do NOT begin the story knowing
- a colossal physical Crest exists beneath Yahtrea;
- scattered underground sites form one integrated Underground Crest civilization;
- the full reactor architecture;
- the true Last Weapon mechanism;
- the six Story-Prime sacrifice-warrior truth;
- the Entity existed;
- one Entity fragment survived;
- Vaelkor is being covertly influenced by that fragment.

## Entity
At story start the Entity is:
- not an accepted historical enemy;
- not a known myth/religious figure;
- not a scholarly theory;
- not known by Yahtrea, Black Host authorities, Othmar, Vaelkor, Crest Knights or Card scholars.

## Ancient machinery
Use:
- survived;
- preserved;
- reactivated;
- functions now.

Do not casually claim:
> "this machine has been running continuously for thousands of years"

unless a specific later source proves it.

## Character expertise
Expertise allows characters to recognize what their actual professions support.

It does not grant plot omniscience.
