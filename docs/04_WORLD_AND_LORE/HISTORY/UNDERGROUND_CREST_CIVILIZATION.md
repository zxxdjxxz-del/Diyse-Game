## Origin
The underground system began as legitimate catastrophe-survival infrastructure.

It was **not** originally:
- an Entity prison;
- a Last Weapon;
- a one-person anchor system;
- a distributed magical consciousness.

## Physical infrastructure
It includes:
- underground cities;
- bunkers;
- protected transit;
- stockpiles;
- defenses;
- reactors;
- administrative/maintenance complexes.

The system is organized around one enormous buried regional magical Crest beneath modern Yahtrea and extending beyond modern borders.

## Power architecture
Multiple magic reactors inject power directly into the gigantic Crest.

Do not portray the system as:
- conventional city-to-city magical power cables;
- a magical internet;
- a planetwide hive mind.

## Modern knowledge
Modern people do not begin the story knowing the scattered sites are components of one integrated civilization-scale system.

Cyanis recognizes modern Crest geometry.
That does not grant knowledge of the buried physical Crest.
