## People
Ancient Diyseans were:
- human;
- globally advanced;
- users of mature magical science/engineering;
- substantially more technologically/magically capable than modern Yahtrea.

They are not a lost nonhuman species.

## Ancient civilization
Their mature magical science fulfilled functions analogous to modern:
- infrastructure;
- industry;
- transport;
- medicine;
- defense;
- administration;
- survival systems.

Above-ground ruins survive unevenly:
- heavy stone/foundations/masonry survive best;
- fine construction, furnishings, exposed machinery, records and organic materials are often damaged or lost.

## Underground transition
As catastrophe worsened, substantial populations/infrastructure moved underground.

Modern archaeology knows subterranean Diysean sites exist.

The secret is not "there are underground ruins."
The secret is the **scale, integration, purpose and true history** of the buried system.

## Ancient capital relationship
Modern Caelora stands atop/through the site of an Ancient Diysean capital.
