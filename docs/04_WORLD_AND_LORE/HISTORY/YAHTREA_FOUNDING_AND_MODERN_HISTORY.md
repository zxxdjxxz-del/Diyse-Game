**Status:** ACTIVE WORLD-HISTORY AUTHORITY  
**Authority:** current world/lore owner plus later explicit approved corrections.  
**Domain rule:** this folder owns geography, place terminology, factions at world level, ancient/modern history, world chronology, modern-knowledge firewalls, and map/travel authority. Story scene sequencing belongs in `02_STORY`; exact dialogue in `03_DIALOGUE`.


## Founding
Yahtrea was founded by:
> **King Yahtren**

Current founding model:
- Yahtren unified southern towns that developed into / are remembered through **Cerythvale, Ashford, Ivorybridge, and Larkspire**;
- Greyspires settlements traded with the emerging kingdom and were integrated during Yahtren's reign;
- westward expansion followed.

## Caelora
The capital:
> **Caelora**

is named for Yahtren's wife:
> **Caelora**

The city was built atop / through the site of an Ancient Diysean capital.

Early construction in Caelora and other sites exposed ancient inert artifacts/Cards before the modern activation era.

## Internal chronology
Use YF as the current calendar:
> **YF = Years since the Founding of Yahtrea**

Current approximate timeline:
- **0 YF** — Yahtrea founded
- early royal era — Caelora established as capital
- **~200 YF** — first modern Card activation/recovery era begins
- **~220 YF** — naturally magical births begin appearing
- **~250 YF** — magical creatures begin returning/awakening
- **713 YF** — present-day story

Therefore:
- first Card activation is roughly **513 years before the present**
- naturally magical births begin roughly **493 years before present**
- magical creatures return roughly **463 years before present**

## Causality boundary
The game does **not** need to explain why Cards began activating specifically around ~200 YF.

The causal/metaphysical mechanism for:
- delayed Card activation;
- later natural magical births;
- magical-creature return

remains intentionally unresolved unless separately approved.
