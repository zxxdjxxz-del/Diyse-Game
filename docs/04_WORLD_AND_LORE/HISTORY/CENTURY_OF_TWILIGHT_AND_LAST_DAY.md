## Century of Twilight
Traditional name for the Ancient Diysean terminal era.

It is **not** literally one century.

Authorial chronology:
- begins approximately **150 years before Diysean civilization ends**;
- starts with discovery/approach of a rogue star.

The rogue star does **not** collide directly with Diyse.

Its approach destabilizes the system and causes:
- impacts/celestial disturbance;
- tides;
- climate disruption;
- famine;
- disease;
- displacement;
- infrastructure loss;
- conflict;
- immense mortality.

The catastrophe worsens over generations.

## The Last Day
Late within the Century of Twilight:
> **The Last Day**

lasts roughly **30 years**.

`Long Day` is retired.

The Last Day includes:
- prolonged abnormal daylight/light conditions;
- enormous repeated heat waves;
- intensified water/crop/habitability collapse;
- growing dependence on underground survival infrastructure;
- the Entity's active emergence;
- the Ancient War;
- development/use of the Last Weapon;
- the end of the Ancient War.

The Entity does not become the active enemy before this late era.
