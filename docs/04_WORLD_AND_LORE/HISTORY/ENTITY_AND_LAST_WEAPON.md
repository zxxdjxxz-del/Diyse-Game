## Entity origin
The Entity predates its arrival on Diyse.

It had been imprisoned off-world around:
- a small moon; or
- comparable engineered minor world.

Diyse was never the Entity's prison world.

The rogue-star catastrophe damages that prison.
A prison fragment reaches Diyse carrying the wounded/dormant Entity.

That prison fragment is **not** the later sole surviving Entity fragment.

## Ancient War
During The Last Day the Entity:
- awakens;
- feeds on structured magic;
- contaminates parts of the energized giant Crest;
- corrupts systems and living conduits;
- creates an organized force.

The Diyseans turn their survival infrastructure toward resistance.

## Last Weapon
The Last Weapon is **not a separate giant gun**.

Ancient Diyseans temporarily force the reactor-supported giant Crest into an emergency weapon state.

Core kill logic:
1. converge the Entity's distributed corrupted presence toward central body/control;
2. compress/concentrate the gathered presence;
3. drive overwhelming magical discharge through that concentration.

Many verified living operators are required because corrupted automation cannot be trusted.

## Six sacrifices
The six warriors whose magical force later becomes the six Story Primes sacrifice themselves as part of the Last Weapon operation.

The Last Weapon:
- destroys the Entity's central body/control;
- devastates already damaged ancient infrastructure;
- helps end the Ancient War;
- leaves ancient civilization effectively finished.

Exactly **one** Entity fragment survives.

There is:
