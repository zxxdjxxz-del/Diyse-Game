## Historical-source rule
Retired terms may remain:
- in archived filenames;
- in quoted old records;
- inside supersession notes.

They must not leak into current-facing:
- dialogue;
- map labels;
- item sources;
- quest text;
- chapter prose;
- UI location names.

## `Sixfold`
`Sixfold` is reserved for concepts directly tied to the permanent six and their shared party progression/identity.

Do not use `Sixfold` as generic ancient/facility branding.
