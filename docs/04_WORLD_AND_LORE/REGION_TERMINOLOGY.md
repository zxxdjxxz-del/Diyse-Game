## Exact usage

### Yahtrenhold
Formal map label:
> **YAHTRENHOLD**

Do not write:
- The Yahtrenhold
- Crownhold
- The Crownhold

when referring to the current region.

### The Westways
Formal region:
> **THE WESTWAYS**

### The Greyspires
Formal region:
> **THE GREYSPIRES**

`highlands` may still be used as ordinary terrain description when literally appropriate, but not as the formal region name.

### Black Host Territory
Formal map/prose designation:
> **BLACK HOST TERRITORY**

It is a political/territorial control label rather than a conventional country name.

### The Blackspine
Still canonical:
> **The Blackspine**

It is the dark mountain range/frontier barrier, not the name of the Black Host homeland.
