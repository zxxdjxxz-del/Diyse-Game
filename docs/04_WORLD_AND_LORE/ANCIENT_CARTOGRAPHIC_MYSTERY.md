# Diyse — Long-Form Ancient Cartographic Mystery

**Status:** ACTIVE WORLD / LORE AUTHORITY  
**Authority:** current world/lore owner plus later explicit approved corrections; detailed story/dialogue mechanics defer to their numbered owners.  
**Domain rule:** this folder owns geography, place terminology, factions at world level, ancient/modern history, world chronology, modern-knowledge firewalls, and map/travel authority. Story scene sequencing belongs in `02_STORY`; exact dialogue in `03_DIALOGUE`.


The cartographic mystery is:
- physical;
- archaeological;
- cumulative;
- not a magical six-piece puzzle;
- not a six-piece collection puzzle and not organized as one map fragment per Face.

Hidden author truth:
- the large-scale Ancient magic/infrastructure pattern ultimately involves **all six Faces together** across the mapped region;
- Chapter 4 does **not** let the characters identify that truth.

3. Chapter 3 — ~500-year-old Yahtrean Westways recordbook contains a rough Wayfinder-derived regional copy; Mirena identifies the giant tower's modern location as Cresthaven
4. Chapter 4 — Vaelira compares the already-copied Wayfinder evidence against Reaction Annex behavior; the resemblance strengthens, but **no new cartographic transcription/chart is discovered**
5. Chapter 5 — Deepforge works survey / physical map
6. Chapter 8 — Westguard-area western survey material
7. Chapter 10 — final eastern survey at Eastern Wayfinder

## Chapter-5 correction
The party initially mistakes incomplete evidence around Ivorybridge for a terminus.

Deepforge proves:
- the route did not terminate at Ivorybridge;
- Ivorybridge lay along it;
- the route continued north into the Greyspires.

This is incomplete evidence corrected by later evidence, not prophecy.

## Chapter-8 result
Western/northern/central geography increasingly reconciles.
The east remains the major blind spot.

## Chapter-10 result
Eastern Wayfinder completes the physical picture enough to prove many separate surveys/routes are describing parts of one enormous connected Ancient geography.

The party still does **not** yet know the full historical purpose.

The completed map points investigation back toward Caelora.
