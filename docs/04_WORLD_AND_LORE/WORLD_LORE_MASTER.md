## Hidden ancient geography
Ancient Diysean subterranean infrastructure is real and extensive.
Modern political region boundaries do not define that ancient underground system.

Modern people know:
- ancient ruins exist;
- some Diysean sites are subterranean;
- late Diysean populations moved underground.

They do **not** begin the story knowing:
- those sites form one integrated Underground Crest civilization;
- a colossal physical Crest lies beneath Yahtrea;
- the Entity existed;
- the true Last Weapon mechanism.
