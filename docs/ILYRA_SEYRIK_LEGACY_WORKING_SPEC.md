## 2. Mercy's Boundary — Blue Warden Legacy Shield

Identity: mercy as a boundary that can be actively defended.

Visual direction:
- refined practical Blue Warden shield;
- ivory/silver body with antique-gold framing and light-blue inlay;
- blue-white ward geometry concentrated around a clear perimeter/boundary;
- no Black Host chains, ember-red glow, biomechanical ribbing, oily corruption, or predatory motifs.

Seyrik retains his own Ruin Vanguard/Ruin Warden visual language when equipping it.

Exact final Defense/Spirit/stat values and any passive remain for the item/equipment audit. Do not add automatic intercepts, free barriers, automatic healing, extra actions, or Ability gating.

## 3. Purposebound — Ruin Vanguard Legacy Two-Handed Sword

Identity: force controlled by purpose and a chosen endpoint.

Visual direction:
- long disciplined Two-Handed Sword with clean martial profile;
- dark gunmetal/ash-steel body and bright functional edge;
- one restrained violet Ruin channel that visually terminates before becoming uncontrolled;
- strong guard/ricasso geometry;
- no Black Host chains, ember-red glow, biomechanical segmentation, organic ribbing, or corruption smoke.

When Ilyra equips it, her Blue Warden/Vowblade identity remains her own.

Exact final Attack/Magic/Spirit/stat values and any passive remain for the item/equipment audit. Do not add self-damage, HP costs, execute rules, gauges, automatic conversion, or weapon-required Ability interactions.

## 4. Audit103 Character-Quest Component mapping — locked

- Ilyra's Character Quest **Mercy Has a Voice** grants the unique Legacy Component that completes **Mercy's Boundary**.
- Seyrik's Character Quest **The Name That Remains** grants the unique Legacy Component that completes **Purposebound**.

The Legacy Component is not the final equipment item. The physical masterwork already exists in secured Cresthaven inventory; the Character Quest supplies the unique missing keyed/core component required to complete/restore it for use.

This is fixed authored completion, not crafting.

---

## 5. Chapter-12 Cresthaven secured release — final flow

During the Chapter 12 pre-point-of-no-return Cresthaven preparation window:

1. the native owner's Character Quest must be complete and its Legacy Component earned;
2. the matching secured Legacy becomes ready for completion/release;
3. the component is integrated/registered in a short authored interaction;
4. the component leaves Key Items and the completed Legacy enters inventory;
5. the native owner may equip under normal family/slot legality;
6. the reciprocal partner may equip only after the relevant Synthesis is purchased and normal legality is satisfied.

If the Character Quest is incomplete, the item remains secured/incomplete and is not placed in inventory as a dead shell. The player may complete the quest and return before the point of no return.

**Keep Them Alive** remains item-reward-free.

No new dungeon, Hunt, boss, recipe, material grind, currency fee, random drop, side quest, or general crafting system is introduced.

## 6. Slot asymmetry / build boundary

- Mercy's Boundary gives Seyrik an optional defensive Secondary path.
- Purposebound gives Ilyra an optional aggressive two-slot path.
- Neither is mandatory and neither changes Ability legality.
