## 1. Pair Legacy rule

Ilyra and Seyrik receive **two physical Legacy-tier equipment items**, one belonging to each native Base tradition.

Synthesis does not create these items. It grants the reciprocal partner the legal Legacy-tier permission established by the universal Synthesis architecture.

The pair mapping is:

| Native tradition | Legacy item | Equipment family | Reciprocal Synthesis wearer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue Warden | **Mercy's Boundary** | Shield | **Seyrik** after purchasing Tempered Ruin / Synthesis |
| Ruin Vanguard | **Purposebound** | Two-Handed Sword | **Ilyra** after purchasing Mercy Unbroken / Synthesis |

The native owner remains naturally compatible with the item's equipment family under the native tradition. The reciprocal partner's cross-tradition Legacy legality requires that character's purchased Synthesis node.

Legacy permission does not bypass normal Weapon / Secondary geometry. **Mercy's Boundary** occupies Secondary normally. **Purposebound** occupies Weapon + Secondary as a Two-Handed Sword.

No Ability or Ultimate gains a weapon or Shield requirement from either Legacy item.

## 2. Blue Warden Legacy — Mercy's Boundary

**Mercy's Boundary** is the Blue Warden Legacy-tier Shield.

### Identity

The name expresses the pair's completed lesson without turning mercy into softness: mercy has a line, and that line can be actively defended.

The Shield represents preservation that is willing to define what must be protected and enforce that boundary. It belongs to the Blue Warden tradition, but its identity is especially legible when Seyrik equips it after learning how Ruin can operate inside a chosen limit.

It does not make Blue Warden abilities require a Shield and does not create a new protection command.

### Visual direction

- refined, practical Blue Warden shield rather than an oversized fortress shield;
- ivory / silver body with restrained antique-gold framing and light-blue inlay;
- blue-white ward geometry concentrated around a clearly defined perimeter or inner boundary line;
- visually strong enough to read in Seyrik's darker silhouette without inheriting Black Host design language;
- no chains, ember-red inner glow, biomechanical ribbing, oily corruption, or predatory Black Host motifs.

When Seyrik equips it, his Ruin Vanguard / Ruin Warden effects remain his own. The Shield does not recolor all Ruin effects blue or erase the controlled violet Ruin language of Ruin Warden.

### Mechanical-stat boundary

This pass locks **item identity, family, name, eligibility mapping, and acquisition presentation only**.

Exact Defense / Spirit / other numerical stats and any final item passive remain for the dedicated production item/equipment audit.

Do not give Mercy's Boundary an automatic intercept, free barrier, automatic healing trigger, extra action, or Ability-gating effect merely to make the Legacy feel special.

## 3. Ruin Vanguard Legacy — Purposebound

**Purposebound** is the Ruin Vanguard Legacy-tier Two-Handed Sword.

### Identity

The name captures Seyrik's completed Ruin lesson: force is not controlled merely by becoming smaller. It is controlled by knowing exactly what it is for and stopping when that purpose has been achieved.

Purposebound is a decisive Ruin Vanguard weapon, not a corrupted Black Host artifact and not a requirement for Ruin Vanguard or Vowblade techniques.

The weapon is intentionally legal for Ilyra after Synthesis because her Vowblade resolution is not about becoming cruel; it is about accepting that preservation can require a hard, deliberate act.

### Visual direction

- long, disciplined Two-Handed Sword silhouette with a clean martial profile;
- dark gunmetal / ash-steel body with a bright functional edge rather than a jagged or monstrous blade;
- one restrained violet Ruin channel running through the weapon, visually terminating before it becomes uncontrolled;
- strong guard / ricasso geometry that makes the weapon feel contained and deliberate;
- no Black Host chains, ember-red glow, biomechanical segmentation, organic ribbing, or corruption-smoke presentation.

When Ilyra equips it, her Blue Warden / Vowblade visual identity remains blue-white Grace dominant where authored. Purposebound does not turn Vowblade into Black Host Ruin or overwrite her existing restrained violet accents.

### Mechanical-stat boundary

Exact Attack / Magic / Spirit / other numerical stats and any final item passive remain for the production item/equipment audit.

Do not give Purposebound self-damage, HP costs, execute mechanics, a Ruin gauge, automatic damage conversion, or any weapon-required Ability interaction.

## 4. Acquisition presentation — shared Cresthaven secured-equipment release

**Mercy's Boundary** and **Purposebound** are acquired through the same deterministic **Cresthaven secured-equipment release during the Chapter 12 pre-point-of-no-return preparation window** established for the Cyanis/Vaelira Legacy pair.

Production rules:
- reuse the same existing Cresthaven armory / secured-equipment staging rather than creating another bespoke endgame location;
- no new dungeon, Hunt, boss, crafting recipe, currency, material grind, random drop, or side quest;
- both are physical equipment that already exists in the world; Synthesis does not conjure them;
- exact historical provenance remains for the later item/lore-description pass rather than inventing unsupported history here;
- the late-Chapter-11 **Keep Them Alive** resolution scene remains item-reward-free and is not rewritten into an equipment handoff.

### Player-facing sequence

1. The late-Chapter-11 Ilyra/Seyrik resolution has already occurred as mandatory continuity.
2. During the Chapter 12 Cresthaven preparation window, **Mercy's Boundary** and **Purposebound** become available as deterministic secured-equipment pickups alongside the already-approved Legacy-release structure.
3. The player may physically collect either item even if the reciprocal character has not yet purchased Synthesis.
4. If reciprocal Legacy eligibility is incomplete, the equipment UI shows the relevant Synthesis / Legacy requirement rather than hiding or deleting the item.
5. Once the character purchases Synthesis and otherwise satisfies equipment legality, the reciprocal Legacy becomes equippable immediately.

This preserves the universal separation between **physical acquisition** and **character eligibility**.

## 5. Why this pair uses one Shield and one Two-Handed Sword

The asymmetry is intentional and mirrors the relationship rather than forcing identical reward geometry.

- **Mercy's Boundary** is preservation expressed as a defended limit.
- **Purposebound** is destruction expressed as a deliberate, bounded act.
- Seyrik gains a true late-game defensive Secondary option through the Blue Warden Legacy when his loadout permits it.
- Ilyra gains a true late-game aggressive Two-Handed Sword option through the Ruin Vanguard Legacy, occupying both Weapon and Secondary as normal.
- Neither item changes Ability legality.
- The pair therefore expands two opposite build directions without making either one the mandatory endgame loadout.

This also preserves the global rule that **Abilities and Ultimates never require particular equipment**.
