## Category

**Regional Hunt #11**

This encounter is a Regional Hunt, **not** the Chapter 11 Elite and not a mandatory story boss.

Chapter 11's conventional Elite remains a separate optional encounter role.

## Apparent identity

The construct is known in the modern Black Host campaign as the **Throne of Emperor Vaelkor** and can initially read as one of Vaelkor's secret imperial war constructs.

## True identity

The core of the Throne is secretly a **corrupted ancient Diysean construct**.

The Black Host did not originally create it. Vaelkor's regime found/appropriated it and added Black Host machinery, containment, armor and imperial modifications around/into the ancient construct.

This allows the Hunt to bridge late Chapter 11 Black Host aesthetics with the deeper ancient-Diysean/corrupted-Crest material of Chapter 12 without making its optional revelation mandatory exposition.

## Form structure

Preserve the established two-form concept:

1. **Sealed Throne**
2. **Walking Throne**

The first form may emphasize the Black Host imperial containment/housing. The second exposes the ancient construct more clearly as it tears free/changes combat state.

The existence of two forms here is deliberate and does not imply all Hunts should have transformations.

## Story function

The Hunt demonstrates, for players who pursue it, that the Black Host has progressed beyond merely studying ancient ruins and has successfully activated/weaponized at least one ancient Diysean construct.

It therefore reinforces Queen Lysara's reason for authorizing the final offensive: the risk that Vaelkor's regime could gain access to increasingly dangerous ancient infrastructure is real.

The mandatory story must still provide enough evidence of Black Host excavation/experimentation that the plot works if this optional Hunt is skipped.

## Availability

The Hunt unlocks during the deeper post-Varkesh Chapter 11 campaign after the Forward Hub has been established and the party has penetrated far enough into Vaelkor's ancient-excavation sphere for the construct's existence/location to be discovered.

It is optional and does not gate Vaelkor.

If unfinished before Vaelkor's defeat, it remains available during the **post-Vaelkor cleanup/preparation window**.

The deliberate launch of Chapter 12 is the final point of no return and closes Chapter 11 Hunt access.
