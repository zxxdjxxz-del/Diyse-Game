## Category

**Regional Hunt #11**

The `#11` is the Hunt's sequence number and does **not** change when chapter numbering changes.

This encounter is a Regional Hunt, **not** the Chapter-12 Elite and not a mandatory story boss.

Chapter 12's conventional Elite remains a separate optional encounter role.

## Apparent identity

The construct is known in the modern Black Host campaign as the **Throne of Emperor Vaelkor** and can initially read as one of Vaelkor's secret imperial war constructs.

## True identity

The core of the Throne is secretly a **corrupted ancient Diysean construct**.

The Black Host did not originally create it. Vaelkor's regime found/appropriated it and added Black Host machinery, containment, armor, and imperial modifications around/into the ancient construct.

This allows the Hunt to bridge late Chapter-12 Black Host aesthetics with the deeper Ancient-Diysean/corrupted-Crest material of Chapter 13 without making its optional revelation mandatory exposition.

## Form structure

Preserve the established two-form concept:

1. **Sealed Throne**
2. **Walking Throne**

The first form may emphasize Black Host imperial containment/housing. The second exposes the ancient construct more clearly as it tears free/changes combat state.

The existence of two forms here is deliberate and does not imply all Hunts should have transformations.

## Story function

The Hunt demonstrates, for players who pursue it, that the Black Host has progressed beyond merely studying Ancient ruins and has successfully activated/weaponized at least one Ancient Diysean construct.

It reinforces Queen Lysara's strategic concern that Vaelkor's regime could gain access to increasingly dangerous Ancient infrastructure.

The mandatory story must still provide enough evidence of Black Host excavation/experimentation that the plot works if this optional Hunt is skipped.

## Availability

The Hunt unlocks during the deeper post-Varkesh **Chapter-12** campaign after **Vhalmarch** has been established as the Forward Hub and the party has penetrated far enough into Vaelkor's ancient-excavation sphere for the construct's existence/location to be discovered.

It is optional and does not gate Vaelkor.

If unfinished before Vaelkor's defeat, it remains available during the **post-Vaelkor Chapter-12 cleanup/preparation window**.

Beginning Chapter 13 does **not** by itself constitute the true point of no return under Audit109. The final irreversible threshold is **Last Shelter → Reactor Galleries**. Access/return implementation must preserve that rule.
