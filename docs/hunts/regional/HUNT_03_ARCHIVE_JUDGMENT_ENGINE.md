## 8. Combat concept

Core identity:

**evaluate → alter the terms → punish reliance on a favorable state**

The mechanic should foreshadow the protected first-clear reward **Reversal Engine** without duplicating First Command Warden's limited weaker-action reproduction gimmick.

Structure:
- one enemy;
- one HP bar;
- no transformation;
- no adds;
- no immunity puzzle;
- no mandatory character;
- no scripted-round sequence;
- no compulsory Prime;
- no action-copy mechanic.

### RECORD STRIKE
Basic non-elemental magical attack against one target.

### CLAIM REVIEW
The Engine marks one party member as **Reviewed**.

No damage.

Cheap geometric target icon.

### ADVERSE FINDING
If the Reviewed target has an eligible ordinary positive stat effect, the Engine reverses one eligible buff into its corresponding debuff.

Examples:
- Attack Up → Attack Down;
- Defense Up → Defense Down;
- Magic Up → Magic Down;
- Spirit Up → Spirit Down;
- Speed Up → Speed Down.

Only existing legal reversal pairs may be used.

It does **not** reverse:
- Prime states;
- Ultimate states;
- unique story effects;
- unremovable equipment passives;
- unrelated status categories.

If no eligible buff exists, Adverse Finding deals modest non-elemental damage instead.

### BURDEN OF PROOF
Heavy non-elemental magical strike against the Reviewed target.

Damage increases modestly if that character currently has a stat reduction.

Natural pressure sequence:

**Claim Review → Adverse Finding → Burden of Proof**

The sequence is not rigidly scripted every cycle.

### RECORD NULLIFICATION
Removes one eligible ordinary beneficial effect without reversing it.

Low/no damage.

Used less frequently than Adverse Finding.

### MAJORITY FINDING
Moderate party-wide non-elemental magical attack.

No status reversal attached.

Prevents the fight from becoming only single-target protection.

## 9. Player counterplay

The Hunt must not reduce to “never use buffs.”

Legal responses include:
- use buffs while accepting the reversal risk;
- protect the Reviewed character;
- cleanse resulting debuffs;
- deliberately use a less-important buff as reversal bait;
- debuff the Engine;
- race its action economy;
- defend before Burden of Proof;
- heal normally.

The mechanic should create tactical tension, not punish the use of the game's normal systems.
