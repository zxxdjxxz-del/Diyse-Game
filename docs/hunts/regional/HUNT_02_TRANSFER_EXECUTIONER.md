## 6. Transfer Executioner identity

The Transfer Executioner is a Black Host biomechanical security/enforcement construct assigned to the transfer system.

It is:
- not a named Black Host commander;
- not possessed;
- not an Ancient Diysean guardian;
- not a second Rhazek.

Visual language follows established Black Host authority:
- black / crimson / purple;
- alien biomechanical fantasy-knight construction;
- ribbed and segmented surfaces;
- chain/restraint motifs;
- ember-red interior glow;
- dark-purple energy.

Its function reads as brutally practical: restrain subjects who break transfer control and eliminate resistance that cannot be moved.

The name **TRANSFER EXECUTIONER** is the Hunt/UI designation. The protagonists do not need to know an official Black Host model name.

## 8. Combat identity

Core identity:

**mark → restrain → punish**

Structure:
- one enemy;
- one HP bar;
- no transformation;
- no adds;
- no scripted round progression;
- no immunity puzzle;
- no Prime;
- no mandatory character;
- no special Hunt command.

### INTAKE BRAND
Marks one party member as the current **Transfer Target**.

Cheap visible red/purple restraint glyph or targeting bracket.

The mark itself deals no damage.

### RESTRAINT CHAIN
Single-target physical attack.

Against the Transfer Target:
- modestly stronger damage;
- temporary Speed reduction.

Use a short chain lash/tether animation rather than simulated chain physics.

### COMPLIANCE BLOW
Heavy single-target physical strike.

Moderate chance to reduce Defense briefly.

### EXECUTION STROKE
Signature attack.

Powerful single-target physical/Ruin-inflected strike.

Strongly prefers the current Transfer Target.

Deals increased damage if the target currently has the Executioner's Defense or Speed reduction.

This is **not** an instant-kill move.

The player can respond through normal healing, defense, mitigation, debuffs, and damage racing.

### CHAIN SWEEP
Moderate party-wide physical attack.

Used occasionally to prevent complete trivialization through single-target protection.

### REASSIGNMENT
If the current Transfer Target is incapacitated or the mark expires, the Executioner can assign another target on a later action.

No free attack.

No extra turn.
