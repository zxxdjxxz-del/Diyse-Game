**Status:** APPROVED / LOCKED  
**Acceptance authority:** CH1-HUNT-A001  
**Window:** Post-Chapter 1, after S011  
**Placement:** Old cistern spur off the modern Dunmere route  
**Type:** Regional Hunt / optional elite  
**Protected unique first-clear reward:** None currently locked

---

- Do not add or rewrite mandatory Chapter 1 dialogue to foreshadow the Hunt further.

→ **overgrown old cut**

## 4. Old Cut

The player returns to the relevant section of the modern Dunmere route.

Torren's recovered route knowledge makes an overgrown opening interactable:

**OLD CUT**

The route is narrow and clearly abandoned.

Reuse the normal regional environment kit:
- old road stones under vegetation;
- broken drainage masonry;
- roots;
- shallow runoff;
- remains of an old retaining wall;
- one optional side pocket.

Random encounters are enabled where the actual implemented terrain supports them.

There is **no predetermined battle count**. Encounter exposure comes from traversable distance, exploration, side pockets, backtracking, and encounter-rate implementation.

The route should feel like a discovered place without becoming another full Chapter 1 dungeon.

---

## 5. Cistern approach

The old cut descends toward abandoned waterworks.

Environmental language:
- old stone water channel;
- collapsed maintenance stairs;
- standing water;
- moss;
- silt;
- broken sluice pieces;
- large scrape marks;
- evidence that something heavy moves between water and stone.

No Ancient super-lore reveal.

No Black Host equipment.

No Entity clue.

This is abandoned infrastructure that became habitat.

## 6. Pre-fight dialogue

Torren stops near the lower opening.

**TORREN:** Hold up.

Cyanis stops.

**CYANIS:** What?

Torren looks at the water.

**TORREN:** Something's using this.

Ilyra looks down at the disturbed silt.

**ILYRA:** Recently?

Torren crouches.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

Cyanis looks toward the dark entrance.

**CYANIS:** How big?

**TORREN:** Bigger than I'd like.

**CYANIS:** That's not a measurement.

**TORREN:** Didn't bring a ruler.

**ILYRA:** Both of you.

Cyanis looks at Torren.

**CYANIS:** Bigger than you'd like?

Torren draws his bow.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

## 9. Combat identity

The first Regional Hunt teaches that Hunts use the normal Diyse combat system but ask for better use of it.

Core identity:

**pressure → stagger a target → feed**

Structure:
- one enemy;
- one HP bar;
- no transformation;
- no adds;
- no immunity puzzle;
- no scripted rounds;
- no Prime requirement;
- no mandatory character.

### RENDING BITE
Strong ordinary single-target physical attack.

### SILT WASH
Party-wide low/moderate damage plus a temporary Accuracy penalty.

Cheap splash/silt VFX.

### CISTERN RAM
Heavy single-target physical impact.

Moderate chance to lower Defense briefly.

### DEVOURING LUNGE
Signature move.

Heavy physical attack.

If it successfully damages the target, the Devourer restores a **small portion of its HP**.

Enough to matter, not enough to reset or indefinitely prolong the fight.

### THRASH
Occasional party-wide physical pressure.

Prevents one defensive character from fully trivializing the encounter.

## 11. Defeat

At zero HP, the Devourer recoils toward the reservoir edge and collapses against the old stone.

No explosion.

The water settles.

Battle UI clears.

Torren looks at the creature.

**CYANIS:** So that's what was down here.

**TORREN:** Apparently.

Cyanis looks around.

**CYANIS:** Map could've mentioned it.

**TORREN:** Map mentioned water.

**CYANIS:** Very thorough.

Ilyra looks at the filthy cistern.

**ILYRA:** Neither of you is drinking any of that.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** Why would I—

Ilyra keeps looking at him.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** I wasn't going to.

Torren starts toward the exit.

**TORREN:** Sure.

**CYANIS:** Fuck you, Harth.

**TORREN:** Road's this way.
