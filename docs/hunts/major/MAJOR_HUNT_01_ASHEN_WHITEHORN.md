# MAJOR HUNT #1 — ASHEN WHITEHORN

**Status:** ACCEPTED / FULL PRODUCTION LOCKED

**Destination:** Ashfrost Expanse  
**Realm:** Edgelands  
**World-map unlock:** after Chapter 3  
**Final Whitehorn confrontation:** after S022  
**Boss:** Ashen Whitehorn  
**Protected first-clear rewards:** Dawn Shepherd + March of Blades

## 1. Major-Hunt identity

Ashen Whitehorn establishes that a Major Hunt is an expedition rather than a short regional return branch. Ashfrost Expanse is a separate world-map hostile wilderness/tracking destination with no town, market, or permanent settlement.

## 2. Post-Chapter-3 unlock

After Chapter 3, **NEW DESTINATION — ASHFROST EXPANSE** becomes available on the world map.

The player may enter immediately. This preserves the post-Chapter-3 optional structure of Cresthaven character-life/hub content, Archive Judgment branch activity, and a newly unlocked Major-Hunt destination.

No character knows a Prime is present. The expedition is motivated by dangerous territory, tracks, and reports/signs of an unusually large creature.

## 3. Ashfrost Expanse production layout

Three compact connected spaces plus the final arena:

**Ashfrost Verge → Whitewind Shelf → Whitehorn Range → final arena**

Visual language:
- grey-white hard ground;
- old ash mixed through snow/frost;
- black exposed rock;
- dead grass through thin ice;
- windblown pale vegetation;
- low visibility in exposed sections;
- animal tracks;
- broken trees;
- occasional frozen runoff.

No permanent blizzard simulation.

Random encounters emerge from actual route size, side exploration, and encounter rate. No predetermined fight count.

## 4. Tracking grammar

Tracking is environmental observation rather than a separate minigame.

Clues may include:
- huge hoofprints;
- scraped bark;
- broken branches;
- gouges in frozen soil;
- kicked-away ash/snow;
- pale coarse hair;
- feeding-site/carcass evidence where appropriate.

Torren owns the practical field-reading role.

No glowing footprint trail, detective UI, or tracking meter.

Wrong branches may contain ordinary encounters, materials, a pickup/chest, or a scenic overlook.

## 8. Ashen Whitehorn visual authority

Ashen Whitehorn is a massive quadrupedal Edgelands beast, not a corrupted monster.

Locked visual direction:
- broad, heavy body;
- extremely powerful front shoulders;
- ash-grey/pale hide with near-white winter-coat patches;
- dark legs and muzzle;
- one enormous primary white horn sweeping forward from the skull, visibly scarred from years of impacts;
- smaller secondary horn/ridge structure;
- old scars;
- frost and ash caught in the coat.

No Black Host language, biomechanics, Ancient machinery, demonic coding, or supernatural corruption.

It should read as a real animal made legendary by age, scale, and lethality.

## 10. Combat identity

Core identity:

**drive → disrupt formation → commit to a devastating charge**

Structure:
- one enemy;
- one HP bar;
- no adds;
- no elemental immunity puzzle;
- no scripted-round lock;
- no mandatory Prime;
- no mandatory character.

Major-Hunt pressure/durability is higher than a Regional Hunt while remaining inside normal Diyse combat rules.

### Horn Gouge
Strong single-target physical attack. May briefly reduce Defense.

### Whitehorn Sweep
Wide physical attack against the party. Moderate damage. Can temporarily reduce Speed.

### Ash Kick
Light/moderate party damage plus temporary Accuracy reduction.

### Breaking Charge
Clearly telegraphed severe physical charge against one target. High damage, never an instant kill. Normal Guard/healing/Barrier/Defense/debuff responses remain legal.

### Ridge Trample
Moderate party-wide physical attack used to prevent total single-target protection from trivializing the fight.

## 11. Signature behavior — Driven

Whitehorn periodically applies **DRIVEN** to one target.

While Driven:
- Whitehorn prefers that target;
- Horn Gouge gains priority;
- Breaking Charge is more likely to target them.

Driven is not a stun and does not remove commands. It is a readable focus-pressure marker.

## 12. Low-HP state — Last Run

No second HP bar, no mutant form, no HP refill.

**LAST RUN**:
- modest Speed increase;
- more aggressive Whitehorn Sweep;
- Breaking Charge somewhat more frequent;
- fewer Ash Kicks;
- no free threshold attack.

Same animal, same rules, more committed pressure.

## 15. First-clear reward lock

Ashen Whitehorn has two protected deterministic first-clear acquisitions:

- **Dawn Shepherd — Grace Optional Prime**
- **March of Blades — Might Standard Card**
