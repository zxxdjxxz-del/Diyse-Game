# Diyse — Encounter Auren Rewards

**Status:** EXACT ORDINARY-FORMATION AUREN AUTHORITY

`12_ECONOMY_AND_REWARDS` owns these Auren payouts. Enemy composition/difficulty remains owned by `09_ENEMIES_AND_ENCOUNTERS`; Player EXP/CEXP remains owned by `10_PROGRESSION_AND_EXP`.

## Rules
- Auren is awarded **once per defeated formation**, not by summing a per-enemy bounty.
- Formation size may influence payout, but large weak groups do not automatically become the best money farm.
- Summoned/generated support created during battle grants **0 additional Auren**; its pressure is already priced into the source formation.
- Elites, named/authored encounters, bosses, Regional Hunts, and Major Hunts use separately authored rewards.
- These values are normal-route income support, not a grinding invitation. Normal story participation must remain economically viable.
