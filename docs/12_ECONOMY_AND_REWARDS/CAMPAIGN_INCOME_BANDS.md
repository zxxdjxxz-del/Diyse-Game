## Premium boundary
Emergency Kit, Reservoir Tonic, and Emergency Rally remain optional premium emergency stock.
They are intentionally excluded from baseline routine-restock solvency.

The mandatory route is not required to fund buying every premium copy from every shop while also buying all desired upgrades.
