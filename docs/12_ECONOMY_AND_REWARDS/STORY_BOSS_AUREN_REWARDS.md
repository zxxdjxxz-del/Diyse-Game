# Diyse — Mandatory Story-Boss / Named Encounter Auren Rewards

**Status:** EXACT MANDATORY STORY-BOSS AUREN AUTHORITY

`12_ECONOMY_AND_REWARDS` owns these direct Auren rewards. Combat bodies, forms, nonlethal resolution, and encounter architecture remain owned by `09_ENEMIES_AND_ENCOUNTERS`.

## Core rules
- Payout is **per resolved encounter**, not per HP bar or form.
- A genuine fresh Form II does **not** generate a second Auren payout.
- Temporary/summoned/support bodies generate **0 additional Auren** unless separately authored.
- Protected nonlethal disengagement/stabilization encounters award **0 Auren** unless an external story payment is separately authored.
- Story bosses do not all have to pay Auren merely because they are bosses.
- The final story victory does not need a meaningless postgame cash award.

## Narrative accounting note
Auren displayed after an encounter may represent recoverable funds, authorized bounty/requisition credit, secured supplies converted to spendable value, or another context-appropriate economic handoff. It does not require every creature or construct to literally carry coins.

## Final-boss rule
Do not add a large final-boss Auren number merely for numerical symmetry. With no postgame economy, the reward would have no meaningful purchasing function.
