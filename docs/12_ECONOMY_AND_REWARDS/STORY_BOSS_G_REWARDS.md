## Core rules
- Payout is per resolved encounter, not automatically per HP bar or boss form.
- A genuine fresh Form II does not automatically generate a second G payout.
- Temporary/summoned/support bodies generate no separate G unless explicitly authored.
- **Protected/nonlethal resolution can and should receive G where the encounter is a meaningful resolved challenge.**
- Withdrawal, restraint, stabilization, capture, or survival does not automatically mean zero G.
- G may represent requisition credit, secured funds, bounty, recovered operational value, or another story-appropriate handoff; enemies do not need to literally carry coins.
- Final-boss G should only exist if it remains spendable/useful within the game's ending structure.
