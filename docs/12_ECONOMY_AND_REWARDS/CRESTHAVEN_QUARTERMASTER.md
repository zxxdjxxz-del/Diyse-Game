# Diyse — Cresthaven Quartermaster

**Status:** CURRENT LONG-TERM REQUISITION AUTHORITY

**Domain rule:** `12_ECONOMY_AND_REWARDS` owns G denomination, purchase/replacement prices, sell rules, stock progression, commerce endpoint roles, reward-value/scarcity rules, and non-EXP reward handoffs.

Cresthaven is:
> **THE LONG-TERM CONSOLIDATION / REQUISITION ENDPOINT**

It is not a Regional Market.

## Early establishment
When Cresthaven first becomes a functioning headquarters, the Quartermaster may carry:
- currently unlocked basic Consumables;
- currently authorized ordinary equipment;
- story-appropriate requisition stock.

When it first functions as a Consumable-selling endpoint, it also receives the standard limited premium stock:
- **1 Emergency Kit**;
- **1 Reservoir Tonic**;
- **1 Emergency Rally**;
- no automatic restock.

All prices use **G**.

It does not reveal future ordinary gear early.

## Consolidation state
From the established expansion point onward:
- previously unlocked normal-stock Consumables remain available;
- later normal-stock Consumables are added as they unlock;
- ordinary equipment can be registered for permanent requisition according to the registration rules;
- premium Consumables remain limited to their one-copy stock and do not become unlimited;
- Relics and Legacies do not become ordinary shop inventory.

## Ordinary-equipment backfill
Cresthaven is the permanent backfill authority for all 38 ordinary equipment identities once their respective registration conditions are met.

The player should never need to:
- preserve every starting item forever;
- farm a closed one-time dungeon;
- reload an earlier chapter;
- exploit vendor geography;

to configure a legal later build.
