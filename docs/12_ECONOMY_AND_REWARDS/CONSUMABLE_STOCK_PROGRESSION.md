## Stock rule
Normal purchasable consumables use:
> **UNLIMITED NORMAL STOCK ONCE UNLOCKED**

unless a specific authored story state temporarily restricts access.

Scarcity should come from:
- price;
- unlock timing;
- reward-only classification;

not from:
- random shop refreshes;
- stock lotteries;
- artificial one-copy-per-store limits.

## Chapter 0 authored field issue
Chapter 0 guarantees **3 Field Salves** to Cyanis as finite convoy field issue.

This is story-issued inventory, not normal shop stock.
The reliable normal-stock progression below still begins in Chapter 1.

## Reliable progression spine
### Chapter 1 / early core
Reliable core includes:
- Field Salve
- Rousing Salts
- Blinding Mist
- Trauma Remedy early enough for Bleed pressure

### By late Chapter 2
Add reliable access to:
- Restorative Salve
- Flow Tonic

### By Chapter 3
- Stability Remedy is available before Chapter-4 Freeze pressure.
- General Remedy enters the core/regional stock layer.

### Chapter 4–5
Add specialist utility:
- Null Seal
- Balance Seal
- Vital Salve by Chapter 5

### Chapter 6
Add:
- Deepflow Tonic
- Greater Rousing Salts

### Chapter 7–8
Add:
- Full Remedy

### Chapter 8
Add reliable normal-stock access to:
- Company Salve

Reward-only access begins for:
- Reservoir Tonic
- Emergency Kit

### Chapter 9
Larkspire Regional Market introduces:
- Grand Salve — 2,250 HP / 240 Auren
- Highflow Tonic is reliable late stock around this window

### Chapter 10+
No new ordinary normal-stock consumable tier is required.
Emergency Rally remains reward-only/endgame optional.

## Carry-forward
Once unlocked, normal-stock consumables carry forward to later full-service endpoints and Cresthaven consolidation.

Reward-only items never enter ordinary stock:
- Reservoir Tonic
- Emergency Kit
- Emergency Rally
