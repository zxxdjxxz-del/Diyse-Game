# Diyse — Consumable Stock Progression

**Status:** CURRENT STOCK AUTHORITY

## Stock classes
### Normal stock
Normal purchasable Consumables use:
> **UNLIMITED NORMAL STOCK ONCE UNLOCKED**

unless a specific authored story state temporarily restricts access.

### Limited premium stock
Reservoir Tonic, Emergency Kit, and Emergency Rally use a separate rule after Chapter 10:
> **2 copies per eligible shop, no restock**

They are purchasable, but intentionally scarce and expensive.

## Chapter 0 authored field issue
Chapter 0 begins with:
- **2,500 Auren**;
- **3 Field Salves** issued to Cyanis.

Reliable normal purchasing still begins in Chapter 1.

## Reliable progression spine
### Chapter 1 / early core
Reliable core includes:
- Field Salve
- Rousing Salts
- Blinding Mist
- Trauma Remedy early enough for Bleed pressure

### By late Chapter 2
Add reliable access to:
- Restorative Salve
- Flow Tonic

### By Chapter 3
- Stability Remedy is available before first Chapter-3 Stun pressure and remains reliably available for Chapter-4 Freeze.
- General Remedy enters core/regional stock.

### Chapter 4–5
Add:
- Null Seal
- Balance Seal
- Vital Salve by Chapter 5

### Chapter 6
Add:
- Deepflow Tonic
- Greater Rousing Salts

### Chapter 7–8
Add:
- Full Remedy

### Chapter 8
Add reliable unlimited normal-stock access to:
- Company Salve

Guaranteed authored copies of Reservoir Tonic and Emergency Kit may appear before their shop unlock.

### Chapter 9
Larkspire Regional Market introduces:
- Grand Salve — 2,250 HP / **2,400 Auren**
- Highflow Tonic — **3,600 Auren** around this late-stock window

### Chapter 10
No new unlimited normal-stock tier is required.

### After Chapter 10 / Chapter 11 onward — limited premium unlock
The following become purchasable:
- **Emergency Kit — 8,000 Auren**
- **Reservoir Tonic — 12,000 Auren**
- **Emergency Rally — 15,000 Auren**

Eligible shops at Chapter-11 unlock:
- all **9 Regional Markets** that are currently accessible;
- **Cresthaven Quartermaster**.

Each eligible shop receives:
> **2 copies of each premium identity**

Rules:
- quantities are per shop and per item;
- no automatic replenishment;
- buying one reduces that shop's stored quantity permanently;
- guaranteed authored pickups do not consume shop quantity.

### Vhalmarch
After Vhalmarch is captured/stabilized in Chapter 12, its Forward Supply endpoint receives:
> **2 Reservoir Tonics + 2 Emergency Kits + 2 Emergency Rallies**

These also do not restock.

## Carry-forward
Once unlocked, normal-stock Consumables carry forward to later full-service endpoints and Cresthaven consolidation.

Premium items do **not** become unlimited stock. Their 2-per-shop cap remains permanent.
