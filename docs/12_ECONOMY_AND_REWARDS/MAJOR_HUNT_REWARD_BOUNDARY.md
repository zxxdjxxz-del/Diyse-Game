# Diyse — Major Hunt Rewards

**Status:** EXACT MAJOR-HUNT AUREN AUTHORITY

Exactly **6 Major Hunts** exist.

## Guaranteed Prime reward architecture
Every Major Hunt awards its associated Prime:
> **already Awakened**

The Prime is the Major Hunt's dominant permanent reward. Prime identity/combat rules live in `07_CARDS_AND_PRIMES`; access/quest state lives in `11_QUESTS`; combat bodies live in `09_ENEMIES_AND_ENCOUNTERS`; Player EXP lives in `10_PROGRESSION_AND_EXP`.

## Reward philosophy
Major Hunts should feel economically stronger than Regional Hunts, but they should not stack maximum cash on top of an already-Awakened Prime and every other reward axis.

Direct Auren is therefore a meaningful first-clear bonus rather than the primary reason to complete the Hunt.

Where an additional permanent component/precursor or exceptional Consumable is already awarded, the cash share is reduced relative to an otherwise comparable Major Hunt.

## Major Hunt #6 economy rule
The Unfinished World is optional beyond the grind-free Level-70 proof.

Its **1,800 Auren** reward and Starfall Engine are completionist surplus and must never be assumed in baseline story-economy affordability.

## First-clear / form rules
- Auren is awarded once on Major Hunt completion.
- Fresh HP forms do **not** award a second Auren payout.
- Same-bar state escalations do not award additional Auren.
- Summoned/support bodies do not add separate Auren.
- Repeat clears, if a future implementation permits them, do not automatically repeat first-clear cash or permanent rewards.

## No automatic reward stacking
Do not add an ordinary equipment drop, Relic, random material bundle, or normal-stock Consumable bundle to every Major Hunt merely for symmetry.

Any future additional permanent reward requires the direct-Auren line to be reviewed as part of the complete reward package.
