# Diyse — Chapter-by-Chapter G Liquidity Validation

**Status:** MANDATORY-ROUTE PURCHASE / RESTOCK PRESSURE CERTIFIED

This validation checks whether the closed mandatory-route G economy provides enough usable money at the points where the player actually needs to spend it.

It is intentionally stricter than simply comparing whole-game income against whole-game prices.

## Validation question
At a normal chapter-scale commerce cadence, can a player who does **no optional money content** afford:
1. a meaningful ordinary-equipment purchase; and
2. a substantial routine Consumable restock;

without repeatedly exhausting the wallet or requiring grinding?

Result:
> **YES — PASS**

## Income basis
Mandatory-route expected direct G:
> **316,870 G**

This uses the current owner-domain chapter income map:
- starting wallet + Chapter-0 authored combat: **4,200 G before Chapter 1 commerce**;
- ordinary formations: expected 225-encounter route values;
- mandatory story-boss/named-encounter G;
- fixed authored-event G;
- mandatory non-battle G.

No optional Elite, Side Quest, Character Quest, Regional Hunt, Major Hunt, resale, or deliberate grinding income is included.

## Result
Under this conservative spending model:
- the wallet **never goes negative**;
- the player never needs optional-content money to maintain baseline readiness;
- the player can make a meaningful equipment purchase every chapter through Chapter 12;
- routine healing/MP/revive/status restocking remains affordable;
- the final modeled wallet still contains approximately **29,870 G**.

Tightest late-game point:
> **Chapter 10 — approximately 7,200 G remaining after modeled equipment + routine restock**

This is enough pressure to preserve purchase choice without creating a grind requirement.

## Premium Consumable boundary
The limited-premium items are:
- Emergency Kit — **8,000 G**;
- Reservoir Tonic — **12,000 G**;
- Emergency Rally — **15,000 G**.

Buying one of each costs:
> **35,000 G**

These items are intentionally **not part of baseline solvency**.

The economy does not promise that a mandatory-route player can buy every premium copy from every shop while also buying a major equipment upgrade and a full routine restock every chapter.

That is deliberate:
- premium stock is optional emergency insurance;
- guaranteed authored premium copies still exist;
- optional Hunts/quests/Elites create the surplus that makes aggressive premium purchasing comfortable;
- premium purchases should compete with other luxury/build spending rather than becoming assumed routine maintenance.
