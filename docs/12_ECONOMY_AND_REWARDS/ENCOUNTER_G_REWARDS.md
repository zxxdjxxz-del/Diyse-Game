**Status:** FORMATION STRUCTURE CURRENT / EXACT G VALUES REOPENED FOR RECALIBRATION

This file replaces the former `ENCOUNTER_AUREN_REWARDS.md` current-facing authority.

## Currency
Ordinary encounter currency is:
> **G**

The retired name **Auren** must not be used in current-facing economy text.

## Reward architecture — retained
- G is awarded once per resolved ordinary formation, not as a simple sum of per-enemy bounties.
- Formation size may influence payout, but large weak groups must not automatically become the best money farm.
- Summoned/generated support created during battle grants no separate G unless explicitly authored.
- Elites, authored encounters, story bosses, Regional Hunts, and Major Hunts use their own reward authorities.
- Normal story participation must support the mandatory economy without repetitive money grinding.

## Current numeric status
The old exact formation values are **provisional/retired as final numbers** because the economy has since changed to:
- tenfold displayed G scale;
- completionist direct-cash target around **650,000 G**;
- stronger Hunt payouts;
- G payouts for protected/nonlethal resolved encounters.

Do not copy the old values forward unchanged merely because the formation identities remain valid.

## Formation identity authority
The existing Chapter 1–13 formation identities and the 225-encounter planning spine remain structurally usable. Exact G per formation will be regenerated during the active payout recalibration.

## No-farm rule
The final G curve must preserve intended purchase pressure and must not create a dominant low-risk formation farming loop.
