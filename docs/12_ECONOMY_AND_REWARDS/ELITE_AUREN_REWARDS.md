## Reward rule
Optional Elites should feel economically worthwhile without becoming a mandatory money route.

Direct Auren is calibrated at roughly **4–5.5 current-chapter ordinary formations** for a typical Elite, then adjusted downward when the Elite already provides a high-value permanent authored reward.

Auren is granted once on first defeat/clear. An Elite cannot be repeatedly farmed for the listed first-clear payout unless a future explicit repeatable encounter rule says otherwise.

## Chapter 10
Current Chapter 10 intentionally has **no approved optional Elite**. Do not invent one for economy symmetry.

## Chapter 0
The Ruin Vanguard Pursuer is a protected tutorial/structural encounter that retreats rather than resolving as a normal optional Elite kill.

> **Auren reward: 0**

Do not turn the protected Chapter-0 encounter into an early money source.

## Full optional-Elite cash budget
Clearing all 12 numbered-chapter optional Elites yields:
> **3,890 Auren**

This is meaningful optional wealth but does not replace the normal story-route economy.

## Permanent-reward adjustment rule
If a future approved Elite gains a permanent authored equipment/Card/component reward, review its Auren against the whole reward package instead of automatically stacking full Elite cash on top.

Current explicit example:
- **Ruin Breach Captain → Black Harvest** on first clear; therefore its direct Auren is intentionally below the otherwise expected Chapter-9 Elite cash line.

## No duplicate bounty rule
Temporary/summoned support bodies within an Elite encounter grant **0 additional Auren** unless explicitly authored otherwise. The listed encounter reward already prices the complete fight.
