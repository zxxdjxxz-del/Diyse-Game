# Diyse — Reward-Only Consumable Placement

**Status:** EXACT FINITE-SUPPLY / PLACEMENT AUTHORITY

These three Consumables never enter normal Regional Market, Cresthaven Quartermaster, or Vhalmarch stock:
- **Reservoir Tonic**
- **Emergency Kit**
- **Emergency Rally**

They are:
- finite;
- non-sellable;
- non-random;
- never ordinary enemy drops;
- never replenished by shop restock.

Their Auren values are reward-equivalence values only.

# Emergency Rally — exactly 2
## Copy 1 — Major Hunt #5 / Final Archive Arbiter
> **additional first-clear reward**

- earliest guaranteed source;
- does not replace **Parallax Host**;
- remains a meaningful optional-content advantage because it is obtainable well before Chapter 13.

## Copy 2 — Chapter 13 / Last Shelter
> **final-operation mass-casualty reserve**

- guaranteed alongside the final preparation/recovery state;
- ensures a campaign-only player can possess one Emergency Rally for the final operation even if Major Hunt #5 was skipped;
- a player who completed Major Hunt #5 can enter the finale with up to two if neither was consumed.

No third Emergency Rally exists.

# Scarcity / inventory rules
- copies are one-time authored pickups/rewards;
- consuming one permanently reduces the player's remaining supply;
- selling is impossible;
- Kessara cannot copy Consumables;
- no vendor replenishes them;
- no enemy can randomly drop them;
- no New Game+/postgame replenishment is assumed because Diyse has no postgame campaign.

# Design intent
## Reservoir Tonic
Strongest dedicated MP-restoration item; rare enough that MP conservation still matters. Three total copies provide one midgame, one advanced, and one late-game opportunity.

## Emergency Kit
Broad recovery is powerful but does not revive. Four total copies allow actual tactical use without turning it into routine stock.

## Emergency Rally
Mass revival is encounter-swinging and remains the rarest emergency item. Major Hunt #5 grants early optional access; Last Shelter provides the only campaign-guaranteed final-operation copy.

# Reward-value accounting
The **4,120 Auren-equivalent** total is authored item value, not direct currency. Because all nine items are non-sellable, they do not increase the player's spendable-Auren calibration.
