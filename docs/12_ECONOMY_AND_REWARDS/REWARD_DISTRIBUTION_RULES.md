# Diyse — Reward Distribution Rules
**Migration baseline:** `Diyse_CURRENT_WORKING_TRACKER_CONSOLIDATED_2026-08-27_v85.md`  
**Current whole-project written authority:** **v2.20 / Audit135**, plus later explicitly accepted v85 tracker-level economy closures.  
**Frozen provenance source:** `Diyse_Item_Equipment_Catalog_Economy_Audit_ARCHIVE_FULL_v603.md` only where later authority does not supersede it.  
**Domain rule:** `12_ECONOMY_AND_REWARDS` owns Auren denomination, purchase/replacement prices, sell rules, normal-stock progression, commerce endpoint roles, reward-value/scarcity rules, and non-EXP reward handoffs. Item definitions remain in `08`; enemy bodies remain in `09`; EXP/CEXP remain in `10`; quest structure remains in `11`.


## 1. Reward value is multi-axis
A permanent equipment/Card/Prime/component reward already carries meaningful value.
Do not automatically add maximum Auren and maximum Consumables on top of it.

## 2. Guaranteed progression beats RNG farming
Core ordinary upgrades and exceptional authored acquisitions should use:
- shop choice;
- guaranteed route pickup;
- protected cache;
- guaranteed named-combat reward;
- quest/Hunt completion;

rather than low-probability farming.

## 3. Ordinary and exceptional economies stay distinct
Ordinary equipment can enter Cresthaven requisition after registration.
Relics/Legacies do not become ordinary vendor stock.

## 4. Reward-only Consumables stay finite
Reservoir Tonic, Emergency Kit and Emergency Rally do not become normal repeatable stock.

## 5. No hidden money tax on build experimentation
Older ordinary gear keeps its price when later equipment access broadens.

## 6. No mandatory optional-money loop
The story economy should remain viable without requiring Hunts/Side Quests solely to afford baseline progression.
Optional content can make the player richer and broaden build options.

## 7. No automatic currency after every story beat
Direct Auren should make narrative/economic sense rather than acting as a chapter-clear reflex.
