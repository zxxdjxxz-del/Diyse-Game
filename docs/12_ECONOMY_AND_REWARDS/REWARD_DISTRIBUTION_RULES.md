# Diyse — Reward Distribution Rules

**Status:** CURRENT REWARD-DISTRIBUTION AUTHORITY

**Domain rule:** `12_ECONOMY_AND_REWARDS` owns G denomination, purchase/replacement prices, sell rules, stock progression, commerce endpoint roles, reward-value/scarcity rules, and non-EXP reward handoffs. Item definitions remain in `08`; enemy bodies remain in `09`; EXP/CEXP remain in `10`; quest structure remains in `11`.

## 1. Reward value is multi-axis
A permanent equipment/Card/Prime/component reward already carries meaningful value.
Reward packages may still award strong G where appropriate, especially Hunts; permanent-item value is not a reason by itself to make an optional combat payout feel weak.

## 2. Guaranteed progression beats RNG farming
Core ordinary upgrades and exceptional authored acquisitions should use:
- shop choice;
- guaranteed route pickup;
- protected cache;
- guaranteed named-combat reward;
- quest/Hunt completion;

rather than low-probability farming.

## 3. Ordinary and exceptional economies stay distinct
Ordinary equipment can enter Cresthaven requisition after registration.
Relics/Legacies do not become ordinary vendor stock.

## 4. Premium Consumables use limited shop stock
Reservoir Tonic, Emergency Kit and Emergency Rally are purchasable from the beginning of the normal shop economy.

Every Consumable-selling shop carries:
> **1 copy of each premium identity from that shop's first accessible state.**

They do not become unlimited repeatable stock.
Shop copies do not automatically restock.
Guaranteed authored copies remain separate rewards and do not consume shop stock.

## 5. No hidden money tax on build experimentation
Older ordinary gear keeps its relative economy-unit price when later equipment access broadens.

## 6. No mandatory optional-money loop
The story economy should remain viable without requiring Hunts/Side Quests solely to afford baseline progression.
Optional content can make the player richer and broaden build options.

## 7. Protected / nonlethal resolutions may still award G
A protected, restrained, stabilized, captured, withdrawn, or otherwise nonlethal combat resolution may award G when the authored event supports an economic payout.

Do not equate:
> **nonlethal = 0 G**

The payout belongs to the resolved event/formation, not to looting a killed body.

## 8. Hunts keep strong G payouts
Regional and Major Hunts should award a good/strong amount of G regardless of separate Prime, Forge Component, item, or other permanent reward.

Do not use another reward axis as an automatic cash discount.

## 9. No automatic currency after every story beat
Direct G should make narrative/economic sense rather than acting as a chapter-clear reflex.
