# Diyse — Former Optional-Elite G Reward Retirement

**Status:** **SUPERSEDED AS A SEPARATE REWARD LAYER — 2026-09-22**

## Current rule
The former numbered-chapter optional-Elite category no longer exists as standalone optional combat.

Those enemy identities are now strong normal-pool enemies in their owning chapter/area. Therefore:
- their former one-time optional-Elite G bounties are **retired**;
- they do not grant a second first-clear G package merely because they were historically tagged Elite;
- when encountered through a normal formation, G is owned by the normal **formation-level** reward authority in `ENCOUNTER_G_REWARDS.md`;
- quest-owned combat, Regional Hunts, and Major Hunts remain governed by their own reward authorities.
