# Diyse — Mandatory Non-Battle Auren Reward Map

**Status:** EXACT MANDATORY NON-BATTLE AUREN DELIVERY AUTHORITY

This file owns the exact chapter-by-chapter delivery of the mandatory-route Auren that is not earned from ordinary formations or mandatory story-boss payouts.

## Calibration basis
Current mandatory battle income:
- ordinary formations: approximately **13,560 Auren**;
- mandatory story bosses / resolved boss encounters: **8,490 Auren**;
- battle-income subtotal: approximately **22,050 Auren**.

Mandatory non-battle Auren below:
> **8,080 Auren**

Approximate mandatory-route direct-currency total:
> **30,127 Auren**

Ordinary formations therefore remain approximately **45%** of the calibrated mandatory-route direct Auren.

## Delivery rules
1. These rewards are on the **mandatory route**. Baseline solvency does not depend on obscure optional rooms.
2. A delivery can be requisition/field-operation credit, secured hostile operational funds, liberated military reserve, mandatory-path administrative/expedition reserve, or a direct Auren cache where that presentation is appropriate.
3. The Auren amount is exact. Exact container art, interaction animation, and dialogue acknowledgment may be authored later without changing the amount.
4. Do not reinterpret every line as literal loose coins carried by a monster or Ancient machine.
5. A permanent equipment/Card/Prime/Forge Component reward does not automatically gain extra Auren merely because it shares the same area.
6. No delivery below is repeatable.
