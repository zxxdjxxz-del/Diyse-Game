## Placement principles
1. **Front-load usability, not wealth.** Early money should arrive before or around meaningful commerce checkpoints, not after the player has already passed the purchase decision.
2. **Do not hide baseline solvency.** Mandatory-route budget should not depend on obscure optional rooms.
3. **Exploration can improve efficiency.** Some mandatory-area side-path chests may carry money, but missing one should not collapse the economy.
4. **Use story context.** Military requisition, recovered supplies, liberated funds, archive reserves, and settlement payments are preferable to implausible coin piles when appropriate.
5. **Do not duplicate permanent rewards.** If a mandatory cache already awards a valuable equipment identity, Card, Prime, or other major progression item, do not automatically add a large Auren amount to make it 'complete.'
6. **Chapter 10 rise is intentional.** Ch10 has fewer ordinary encounters than surrounding late chapters while entering a high-price economy, so its mandatory non-battle budget is larger.
7. **Chapter 13 remains functional but restrained.** There is no postgame; do not dump large amounts of unusable money after the final purchasing window.
