## Prime slots
Current direct project lock:
- once Prime battle loadouts become available in **Chapter 4**, each permanent character has **1 Prime slot**;
- after **Sixfold Volition** at the end of Chapter 7, each permanent character has **2 Prime slots**;
- the second slot is an expansion of the same Prime-loadout system, not a new progression currency;
- Prime slots are separate from the 3 Standard Card slots;
- each Prime slot may hold any acquired Prime.

This restores the pre-Volition slot rule that was omitted during reorganization. It does not change Story-Prime acquisition/awakening timing.

## Story bearer association
Story Prime bearer/Face association is narrative and thematic.

Once a Story Prime has been obtained:
- it may be equipped/Invoked by any active permanent party member through the normal Prime-slot system;
- the associated character remains the narrative bearer for story acquisition/awakening.

## Major-Hunt Prime access
Major-Hunt Primes are not owner-locked.

## No duplicate progression
Equipping a Prime on another legal character:
- does not create a copy;
- does not level the Prime;
- does not alter its identity multipliers;
- does not change its command kit.
