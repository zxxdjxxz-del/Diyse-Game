# Diyse — Prime Loadout and Access

**Status:** ACTIVE PRIME AUTHORITY  
**Authority:** current Card/Prime-domain owner plus later explicit approved corrections.  

## Prime slots

- once Prime battle loadouts become available in **Chapter 4**, each permanent character has **1 Prime slot**;
- after **Sixfold Volition** at the end of Chapter 7, each permanent character has **2 Prime slots**;
- Prime slots are separate from the character's **4 Standard Card slots**;
- each Prime slot may hold any acquired Prime;
- Prime loadouts are assigned outside battle and cannot be changed mid-battle.

## Story bearer association

Story Prime bearer/Face association is narrative and thematic.

Once acquired, a Story Prime may be equipped/invoked by any eligible active permanent party member through the normal Prime-slot system. The associated character remains the narrative bearer for story acquisition/awakening.

## Major-Hunt Prime access

Major-Hunt Primes are not owner-locked.

## No duplicate progression

Equipping a Prime on another legal character does not create a copy, level the Prime, alter its progression state, or change its command kit.
