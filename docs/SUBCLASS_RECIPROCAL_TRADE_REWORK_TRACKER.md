# Diyse — Reciprocal Subclass Trade Rework Tracker

**Status:** ACTIVE WORKING DESIGN TRACKER — NOT YET MASTER-CANON PROMOTION  
**Parent authority:** v1.84 / Audit99 and all compatible inherited class/equipment canon.  
**Purpose:** Keep the current subclass/donor redesign coherent while preserving already-authored material wherever it still fits.

---

## 1. Core rework direction

The current leading subclass-training architecture is three **reciprocal trades** after the Sixfold Accord:

- **Cyanis ⇄ Vaelira**
- **Ilyra ⇄ Seyrik**
- **Torren ⇄ Nimera**

The subclass name is **not** a constraint on donor selection. Existing subclass names may remain, change, or be replaced after mechanics and story logic are settled.

The donor relationship represents genuine cross-training. A recipient eventually learns the donor Base tradition's **full ordinary equipment package** rather than a cherry-picked subset.

---

## 2. Ordinary donor-equipment unlock cadence

Current working decision for ordinary donor equipment:

- **Subclass CL1 — donor Primary weapon family**
- **Subclass CL3 — donor Armor family**
- **Subclass CL5 — donor Secondary family/families, where the donor tradition has a separate Secondary option**

All legally learned equipment remains usable afterward under the existing open-equipment rule.

A donor tradition whose Primary consumes both Weapon and Secondary does not invent a Secondary family just to fill CL5.

Relic and Legacy access remain **separate later progression systems** and are not part of the CL1/3/5 ordinary-equipment unlocks. Their exact placement must be reconciled against the new subclass level cap before canon promotion.

## 5. Ilyra / Blue Warden equipment clarification

Current working equipment identity:

- **Blue Warden Base Primary:** Wardrod
- **Blue Warden Base Secondary:** Shield **or** Focus
- **Blue Warden Base Armor:** Warding / Blue Warden armor family

Blue Wardens as a tradition do **not** use swords.

Ilyra herself **can** learn/use swords through later equipment access. Her subclass growth is explicitly allowed to take her beyond the normal boundaries of the Blue Warden tradition.

## 6. Torren ⇄ Nimera shared combat language

### Hunter's Measure

**Hunter's Measure is a shared War Archer-derived tactical state.**

- Torren remains the original War Archer expert.
- Nimera's Torren-derived subclass can also **apply Hunter's Measure**.
- Torren and Nimera may exploit Hunter's Measure applied by the other when both are active.

Do **not** create a redundant separate Nimera-only mark unless a later design problem requires one.

### Keep these states distinct

- **Hunter's Measure** — War Archer tactical quarry/target state.
- **Diysean Appraisal** — Nimera Base information-reveal ability; it does not create an "Appraised" status.
- **Indexed** — existing Sovereign Index state with its Card-specific payoff.

Nimera's new Archer subclass should not collapse these three mechanics into one generic analyzed-target system.

## 7. Nimera — Torren-derived Archer subclass

**Status:** ACTIVE PROVISIONAL DESIGN.  
**Old direction being replaced:** Cyanis-derived / Sixfold Knight framing.  
**Design goal:** Nimera genuinely learns Torren's War Archer discipline. Her subclass should be visibly Archer-based and draw from Torren's Base abilities, while Nimera's personality changes how those lessons are expressed.

### Design identity

Torren's source grammar:

- quarry assessment / Hunter's Measure
- reliable precision
- prepared interruption
- pinning/control
- penetration against hard targets
- repeated/multihit pressure
- exploiting learned target information

Nimera's personal translation:

- analytical
- argumentative / evidence-driven
- precise
- pattern-aware
- treats the target as a problem that can be demonstrated and solved

The subclass should be **less Face-based** than the retired Sixfold Knight direction. Face/Card complexity remains primarily in Cardweaver.

### Current provisional CL progression

**CL1 — Measured Shot** *(working name)*  
- Bow attack.
- Applies **Hunter's Measure**.
- May gain an accuracy/quality bonus if Nimera has already revealed the target through Diysean Appraisal.
- Must not create an "Appraised" state.

**CL4 — Held Argument** *(working name)*  
- Prepared bow response descended from Torren's Watchful Aim concept.
- Fires when the chosen enemy begins an eligible Interruptible action.
- Stronger/more reliable against Hunter's Measure.

**CL7 — Pin the Variable** *(working name)*  
- Bow control attack descended from Torren's pinning technique.
- Applies Speed Down or equivalent mobility/control pressure.
- Hunter's Measure improves reliability and/or penetration.

**CL9 — Structural Failure** *(working name)*  
- Heavy single-target Physical bow attack.
- Significant Defense penetration.
- Strong against armored/high-Defense enemies, targetable components, devices, wards, and similar structural targets.
- Hunter's Measure provides an additional payoff.

**CL11 — Corroboration** *(working name)*  
- Controlled multihit/repeated-shot technique.
- Represents Torren's sustained shooting translated through Nimera's evidence/proof personality.
- Current concept: later hits become more accurate and/or gain Critical Chance against Hunter's Measure.

### Trait — Applied Evidence *(working name)*

Three-rank Trait centered on exploiting **Hunter's Measure** rather than counting Faces.

Current concept:

- **Rank I / CL1:** improved Base Hit against Hunter's Measure.
- **Rank II / CL6:** subclass attacks also gain Critical Chance against Hunter's Measure.
- **Rank III / CL12:** subclass attacks additionally gain modest Defense penetration against Hunter's Measure.

No personal gauge and no new mark.

### Ultimate — Final Annotation *(working name)*

**CL13.**

Current concept:

- Massive single-target Physical bow attack.
- Very high Defense penetration and high Base Hit.
- Especially effective against large targets, Hunts, armored enemies, components, devices, wards, and similar hard points.
- Receives a major payoff against Hunter's Measure.
- Current preference: **does not consume Hunter's Measure**, allowing Torren/Nimera cross-synergy to continue.

### Provisional selected-subclass stat package

Current candidate only; not yet locked:

- **Attack +8%**
- **Accuracy +8%**
- **Speed +6%**
- **Defense −6%**

This replaces the old knight-coded package only if approved after full six-subclass balance review.

### Name status

No final subclass name is locked.

Recent working-name ideas included:

- Proof Archer
- Pattern Archer
- Threadshot

Do not promote any of these by assumption.

## 8. Torren — Nimera-derived subclass

**Current name:** Routeweaver.  
**Status:** PRESERVE-FIRST.

The existing Routeweaver concept already fits the reciprocal trade because Torren learns from Nimera's Cardweaver tradition.

Key preserved principles:

- Torren gains the actual Cardweaver/Conduit equipment tradition rather than a fake Torren-only copy.
- Routeweaver remains Torren's own tactical interpretation of Conduits and selective Card support.
- It must not become a second Cardweaver.
- Existing physical/magical hybrid targeting, protection, route-reading, and selective Card interaction should be retained unless a later audit finds a direct conflict.

Under the new equipment cadence:

- **CL1:** Cardweaver Primary / Conduit access
- **CL3:** Cardweaver armor access
- **CL5:** Cardweaver Secondary access, including Focus where legal

Exact Relic/Legacy timing must be reconciled to CL13.

## 9. Cyanis ⇄ Vaelira

### Vaelira → Cyanis

**Status:** PRESERVE-FIRST.

Cyanis's existing Vaelira-derived magical subclass mechanics are already substantially authored and should not be replaced merely because the donor architecture is being made reciprocal.

Under the new ordinary-equipment cadence, the Vaelira-derived package should unlock progressively:

- **CL1:** Green Arcanist Primary
- **CL3:** Green Arcanist armor
- **CL5:** Green Arcanist Secondary/Focus access

Exact item-family names must follow the final item/equipment audit rather than being invented here.

### Cyanis → Vaelira

**Status:** MAJOR REDESIGN PENDING.

Vaelira's old Torren-derived Archer subclass no longer matches the reciprocal-pair architecture.

The replacement must:

- derive from Cyanis's **Base Crest Knight** combat grammar, not from the old subclass name;
- fit Vaelira's personality and Elements identity;
- inherit Cyanis's complete ordinary Base equipment tradition on the CL1/3/5 cadence;
- preserve any old Vaelira subclass mechanics only where they still make sense independently of archery;
- receive five normal abilities, a three-rank Trait, and a CL13 Ultimate.

Do not design this from the old "Prism Archer" name. The final name comes after the mechanics.

## 11. Mastery / Relic / Legacy reconciliation still required

The old system was authored around a CL12 class structure and included Subclass Mastery nodes, Equipment Mastery, donor Relics, and Legacy gates.

Because Subclasses are now being redesigned around **CL13**, the following must be explicitly reconciled before canon promotion:

1. exact Mastery-node timing and prerequisites;
2. whether "Equipment Mastery" keeps its existing name/function;
3. donor Relic unlock timing under CL13;
4. donor/shared Legacy unlock timing under CL13;
5. old CL12 references in class documents;
6. any Synthesis conditions that explicitly require Base/Subclass CL12;
7. all old one-way donor references superseded by the reciprocal pairs.

Do not silently carry old CL12 gates forward.
