## Levels 1–17
`CumulativeEXP(L) = 100 × (L - 1)^2`

## Level 17 onward
For the level-up from Level `L` to `L+1`, `L >= 17`:

`BaseCost = 100 × (2L - 1)`

`Multiplier = 1 + 0.35 × (L - 17) / 42`

`LevelUpCost = round_to_nearest_100(BaseCost × Multiplier)`
