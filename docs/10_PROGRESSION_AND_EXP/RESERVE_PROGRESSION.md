# Diyse — Active / Reserve Progression

**Status:** ACTIVE STRUCTURAL PROGRESSION AUTHORITY  
**Authority:** current progression-domain owner plus later explicit approved corrections.  


For recruited permanent characters:
- active/reserve status does not reduce the character's CEXP package;
- CEXP applies only to that character's selected class;
- no passive split to the other class;
- no unapproved catch-up multiplier;
- no conversion to Mastery Points.

Late recruits enter with their explicit starting Base Class Level/CEXP.
