# Diyse — Mastery Progression

**Status:** ACTIVE STRUCTURAL PROGRESSION AUTHORITY  
**Authority:** current class/progression owners plus later explicit approved corrections.

This supersedes every former Mastery Point grant/spend schedule.

## Retired Mastery Point behavior
Do not implement:
- Lv5 / 10 / 15 / 20 point grants;
- Volition point;
- Lv40 / 50 / 60 point grants;
- banking;
- spending;
- refund/respec;
- point counters;
- a replacement skill/talent currency.

Sixfold Volition unlocks Subclasses but grants no Mastery resource.

Donor Relic eligibility begins at Subclass CL7, subject to actual item ownership.
Donor Legacy eligibility begins at Subclass CL11, subject to actual Legacy completion/ownership.

All eight Masteries are unlocked once Base CL12 and Subclass CL11 are reached.
Class progression still continues to CL13.
Player Lv60 has no special Mastery function.
