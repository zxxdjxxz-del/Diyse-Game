## 1. Pair thesis

Ilyra and Seyrik integrate the same relationship from opposite directions.

- **Ilyra:** preservation gives her permission to press the attack, and successful Vowblade mercy makes her next return to Blue Warden discipline more efficient.
- **Seyrik:** Ruin Vanguard offense creates the opening for preservation, and successful Ruin Warden preservation feeds disciplined force back into Ruin Vanguard.

The pair therefore creates two opposite combat cadences without simply granting either character more raw healing.

Neither Synthesis creates a new gauge, extra action, extra Trait, new weapon requirement, or new healing/status subsystem.

## 2. Ilyra Synthesis — Mercy Unbroken

**Mercy Unbroken** is Ilyra's permanent Synthesis passive after the universal Synthesis gate is met and the node is purchased.

### A. Blue Warden → Vowblade bridge

**Once per round**, after Ilyra resolves a **non-Ultimate Blue Warden Ability** that either:
- restores HP to another ally; or
- removes at least one ordinary harmful status from another ally,

her next **damaging non-Ultimate Vowblade Ability** before the end of the following round gains **+10% Power**.

Boundaries:
- self-only recovery does not qualify;
- one qualifying Blue Warden action arms one one-use bonus;
- the bonus does not stack with itself;
- it does not alter the Vowblade Ability's Hybrid formula, targeting, hit count, penetration, MP cost, or weapon legality;
- Blue Warden Ultimates do not arm this clause.

### B. Vowblade → Blue Warden bridge

**Once per round**, when a **Vowblade Ability or Ultimate** restores HP to at least one conscious ally other than Ilyra, her next **non-Ultimate Blue Warden Ability** before the end of the following round costs **3 less MP**, minimum 1 MP.

Boundaries:
- an action that heals multiple allies still arms only one discount;
- the discount does not stack with itself;
- the discount applies to the next legal non-Ultimate Blue Warden Ability only;
- the discount does not alter healing Power, status removal, revival value, target rules, or timing;
- Mercy in Steel's self-healing alone does not qualify because another ally must actually receive HP recovery.

### C. Intent

Ilyra's late-game integration loop becomes:

**preserve someone → press the attack → turn offensive mercy back into efficient preservation.**

This lets Blue Warden and Vowblade actively feed one another without turning Vowblade into a second pure-healing kit or allowing Blue Warden to inherit Ruin Vanguard's raw offense directly.

## 3. Seyrik Synthesis — Tempered Ruin

**Tempered Ruin** is Seyrik's permanent Synthesis passive after the universal Synthesis gate is met and the node is purchased.

### A. Ruin Vanguard → Ruin Warden bridge

**Once per round**, after Seyrik resolves a **damaging non-Ultimate Ruin Vanguard Ability**, his next **non-Ultimate Ruin Warden Ability** before the end of the following round costs **3 less MP**, minimum 1 MP.

Boundaries:
- one qualifying Ruin Vanguard action arms one discount;
- the discount does not stack with itself;
- it does not alter the Ruin Warden Ability's Power, healing, Drain, status removal, revival value, targeting, or weapon legality;
- Ruin Vanguard Ultimates do not arm this clause.

### B. Ruin Warden → Ruin Vanguard bridge

**Once per round**, when a **Ruin Warden Ability or Ultimate** restores HP to at least one ally other than Seyrik, his next **damaging non-Ultimate Ruin Vanguard Ability** before the end of the following round gains **+10% Power**.

Boundaries:
- an action that restores multiple allies still arms only one one-use bonus;
- the Power bonus does not stack with itself;
- Siphon Rune's self-recovery alone does not qualify;
- Stolen Grace, Restoring Ward, Withering Mercy, Reclaimed Breath, and Mercy Through Ruin may qualify when they actually restore HP to another ally;
- revival through Reclaimed Breath counts because the revived ally returns with restored HP;
- the bonus does not change damage type, hit count, self-drawback clauses, cooldowns, MP cost, target rules, or weapon legality.

### C. Intent

Seyrik's late-game integration loop becomes:

**use Ruin decisively → make preservation easier → let successful preservation discipline the next use of Ruin.**

He does not become gentler by ceasing to be a Ruin Vanguard. He becomes more controlled because Ilyra's Warden discipline gives him a deliberate way to move between destruction and preservation.

## 4. Pair balance boundaries

- Neither Synthesis grants flat permanent stats.
- Neither adds raw healing percentages or new revival rules.
- Neither grants extra ordinary actions, extra Card actions, extra equipment slots, extra Card slots, or a second Trait.
- The +10% Power bonuses apply only to the next qualifying **non-Ultimate** damaging Ability.
- The 3-MP discounts apply only to the next qualifying **non-Ultimate** Ability and cannot reduce cost below 1 MP.
- A qualifying trigger may arm only one copy of its effect; repeated triggers do not stack multiple future bonuses/discounts.
- No Ability or Ultimate gains a weapon requirement.
- No additional pair bonus is granted merely for fielding Ilyra and Seyrik together.

The effects are intentionally cadence-oriented rather than raw-stat-oriented because Synthesis already also grants paired Legacy eligibility.
