# Diyse — Cyanis ← Vaelira Subclass Working Spec

**Status:** APPROVED CLASS-REWORK DESIGN — NOT YET MASTER CANON  
**Parent trackers:** `docs/CLASS_REWORK_MASTER_TRACKER.md`, `docs/CLASS_CEXP_WORKING_MODEL.md`, `docs/CLASS_MASTERY_WORKING_MODEL.md`  
**Authority boundary:** v1.84 / Audit99 plus newer explicit class-rework decisions. On August 21, 2026 the user explicitly approved the class-rework proposals previously advanced under “Let’s continue.” Where an older provisional draft conflicts with a later refinement, the latest refinement below controls.

## 1. Approved identity

- **Subclass name: Crest Arcanist.** This permanently supersedes the prior **Crest Magus** working name.
- **Character/class pairing:** **Cyanis — Crest Knight / Crest Arcanist**.
- Cyanis learns Vaelira's Green Arcanist magical/elemental discipline and translates it through Crest spellcraft rather than becoming a copy of Green Arcanist.
- Colorless Crest magic remains his strongest native magical expression.
- Battlefield Fields, seals, anti-magic control, penetration, and structural disruption remain central.
- Arcane Staff / Focus permissions expand equipment options but do not redefine every Crest spell's formula.

| **CL10** | Equipment Mastery becomes eligible; purchasing it unlocks the Green Arcanist donor **Relic**. |
| Synthesis | Later paired Legacy access under the final Synthesis rule. |

## 3. Approved weapon / ability legality

Crest Arcanist abilities are **weapon-independent once learned** unless an individual ability explicitly says otherwise.

- **Arcane Staff is not required** to use Crest Arcanist spells.
- Legal Sword, Sword + Shield, dual-Sword, Arcane Staff, and Arcane Staff + Focus configurations do not alter an ability's authored formula unless the ability explicitly establishes a loadout branch.
- Crest Arcanist abilities do not inherit the equipped weapon's ordinary Attack hit pattern.
- Donor equipment therefore expands Cyanis's build options without turning Arcane Staff into a hidden key for using the Subclass kit.

## 4. Approved selected-Subclass stat package

- **MP +10%**
- **Magic +10%**
- **Spirit +6%**
- **Attack -6%**

Crest Arcanist is a deliberate magical specialization rather than a neutral upgrade to Crest Knight.

## 5. Approved Trait — Crest Resonance

### Rank I — CL1
The first MP-costing **Spell-tagged Crest ability** Cyanis uses each round costs **2 less MP**, minimum 1 MP.

### Rank II — CL6
Damaging **Colorless Magical Crest abilities gain 15% Spirit penetration**.

### Rank III — CL12
Cyanis-authored Crest Fields and Seals with durations last **+1 round**, up to the ability's approved maximum duration.

For **Crest Dominion**, the perfected Crest Field has a base duration of 3 rounds and an approved maximum of 4 rounds, so Rank III extends it to 4 rounds while Crest Arcanist is selected.

## 6. Approved five normal abilities

### CL1 — Arcane Lance
**16 MP · one enemy · 220 Colorless Magical Power · 25% Spirit penetration**

After dealing damage, Cyanis gains **Crest Attunement**.

### Crest Attunement
Crest Attunement lasts until the end of the following round. The next **Crest Field** or **Crest Seal** Cyanis uses during that window receives one enhancement and then consumes Crest Attunement:

- **Crest Field:** its initial application cannot be weakened by ordinary enemy resistance or ordinary Field-suppression effects.
- **Crest Seal:** gains **+15 Base Hit / application reliability** against its legal target.

Crest Attunement is not a global Prepared effect and does not compete with the one-armed Prepared-effect limit.

### CL4 — Warding Crest
**30 MP · battlefield · 3-round Crest Field**

- Allies gain one-step resistance to established **Ruin-, Rune-, Spell-, and Card-Seal** effect categories.
- Eligible tagged enemy direct damage is reduced by **15%**.

### CL7 — Nullifying Seal
**19 MP · one enemy or legal hostile entity · 175 Colorless Magical Power**

After damage:
- dispels up to **2 eligible buffs**; or
- deals **+25% final damage** to wards, manifestations, devices, and ordinary summons;
- may **Banish** an eligible ordinary summon at **25% HP or lower**.

### CL9 — Arcane Rupture
**29 MP · all enemies · 205 Colorless Magical Power**

After damage:
- removes **1 removable hostile Field**;
- applies **Minor Magic Down for 2 rounds**.

### CL11 — Elemental Convergence
**18 MP · one enemy · 230 Magical Power · 25% Spirit penetration**

- Cyanis chooses one of the six standard elements.
- After damage, Cyanis gains the matching established **Elemental Guard through the end of the following round**.
- Does not apply an Imprint.
- Does not trigger Composite Reactions.
- Does not create an Elemental Field.
- Does not copy Core Element Array / Shaping Element Array status riders.
- Because the attack is elemental rather than Colorless, Crest Resonance Rank II does not add its extra 15% Spirit penetration.

## 7. Approved Ultimate — Crest Dominion

**All enemies · 330 Colorless Magical Power · 35% Spirit penetration**

After damage:
- remove **1 removable hostile Field**;
- suppress **1 eligible hostile ward, seal, or Rune for 2 rounds**;
- create the **Dominion Crest Field** for **3 rounds**.

### Dominion Crest Field
- Allies gain one-step resistance to the same established **Ruin-, Rune-, Spell-, and Card-Seal** categories.
- Eligible hostile **Spell-, Ruin-, and Rune-tagged direct damage** is reduced by **25%**.

Approved Trait interactions while Crest Arcanist is selected:
- Crest Resonance Rank II adds its **15% Spirit penetration** because Crest Dominion is a damaging Colorless Magical Crest ability, producing **50% effective Spirit penetration**.
- Crest Resonance Rank III extends Dominion Crest Field from **3 rounds to 4 rounds**.

Crest Dominion does not add a generic cleanse, broad Defense/Spirit Up package, Imprint, or Elemental Field.

## 10. Cross-balance status

The August 21 six-Subclass cross-balance pass made **no raw Power/stat changes** to Crest Arcanist. Arcane Lance's unusually high CL1 Power remains intentional because it also carries the highest ordinary CL1 MP cost among the six current Subclasses and establishes Cyanis as the overt magical-specialist recipient.

Percentage direct-damage reduction in Warding Crest / Dominion Crest Field does not establish additive stacking with unrelated DR effects; final stacking follows the later normalized global combat-modifier rule.
