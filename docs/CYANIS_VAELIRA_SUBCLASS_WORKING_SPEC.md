# Diyse — Cyanis ← Vaelira Subclass Working Spec

**Status:** ACTIVE WORKING DESIGN — NOT MASTER CANON  
**Parent trackers:** `docs/CLASS_REWORK_MASTER_TRACKER.md`, `docs/CLASS_CEXP_WORKING_MODEL.md`, `docs/CLASS_MASTERY_WORKING_MODEL.md`  
**Authority boundary:** v1.84 / Audit99 plus newer explicit class-rework decisions. This file preserves the existing Vaelira-derived magical subclass direction for Cyanis while rebasing it to the CL13 subclass structure. Explicitly approved decisions are marked separately from provisional mechanics so working proposals are not accidentally promoted as settled canon.

## 1. Explicitly approved decisions

- **Subclass name: Crest Arcanist.** This replaces the prior **Crest Magus** working name.
- **Character/class pairing:** **Cyanis — Crest Knight / Crest Arcanist**.

No other naming or balance proposal in this file should be treated as explicitly approved unless separately marked.

## 2. Design identity

Cyanis learns Vaelira's Green Arcanist magical discipline and translates it through his own Crest practice. The subclass should feel like Cyanis becoming a more overt magical battlefield controller rather than simply copying Vaelira's Base kit.

Core identity:
- Colorless Crest magic remains Cyanis's strongest native magical expression;
- Vaelira-derived elemental understanding appears selectively rather than replacing Cyanis's Crest identity;
- battlefield Fields, seals, anti-magic control, penetration, and structural disruption remain central;
- abilities are Crest spells and do not require Arcane Staffs unless a specific ability explicitly says otherwise;
- learned Arcane Staff / Focus permissions expand Cyanis's equipment options but do not redefine every spell's formula.

## 3. Equipment progression

A donor passes the donor Base tradition's complete legal ordinary equipment package, staged by Subclass CL.

| Subclass CL | Equipment result |
|---:|---|
| **CL1** | Gain legal use of Vaelira's **Arcane Staff** family. |

## 4. Working selected-Subclass stat package

**PROVISIONAL — not yet explicitly approved:**
- MP +10%
- Magic +10%
- Spirit +6%
- Attack -6%

Intent: Crest Arcanist is a deliberate magical specialization rather than a neutral upgrade to Crest Knight.

## 5. Trait — current preserve-first mechanic

**Current working name remains provisional.** The prior name **Geometric Authority** is not considered locked and is expected to be revisited because the subclass naming direction is moving away from geometry-heavy vocabulary.

Preserve-first mechanics:
- **Rank I / CL1:** the first MP-costing Spell-tagged Crest ability Cyanis uses each round costs **2 less MP**, minimum 1.
- **Rank II / CL6:** damaging **Colorless Magical Crest abilities gain 15% Spirit penetration**.
- **Rank III / CL12:** Cyanis-authored Crest Fields and Seals with durations last **+1 round**, up to their authored maximum.

## 6. Five normal abilities — preserve-first plus CL11 expansion

### CL1 — prior `Geometric Lance`
**Working mechanics:** 16 MP; one enemy; **220 Colorless Magical Power**; **25% Spirit penetration**; applies the current working rider `Reinforced Preparation`.

The ability name and rider name are provisional and should be renamed away from geometry-heavy terminology.

### CL4 — prior `Crest Lattice`
**Working mechanics:** 30 MP; battlefield; 3-round Crest Field.
- Allies gain one-step resistance to established Ruin-, Rune-, Spell-, and Card-Seal categories.
- Eligible tagged enemy direct damage is reduced by **15%**.

Name remains provisional.

### CL7 — Nullifying Seal
**Working mechanics:** 19 MP; one enemy or legal hostile entity; **175 Colorless Magical Power**.
- Dispels up to **2 eligible buffs**; or
- deals **+25% final damage** to wards, manifestations, devices, and ordinary summons;
- may **Banish** an eligible ordinary summon at **25% HP or lower**.

`Nullifying Seal` remains a strong working name but is not separately locked.

### CL9 — prior `Pattern Collapse`
**Working mechanics:** 29 MP; all enemies; **205 Colorless Magical Power**.
- After damage, removes **1 removable hostile Field**.
- Applies **Minor Magic Down for 2 rounds**.

The name is provisional and should be revisited away from geometry/pattern vocabulary.

### CL11 — Vaelira-derived elemental Crest attack
**PROVISIONAL MECHANIC:** 18 MP; one enemy; **230 Magical Power**; Cyanis chooses one of the six standard elements; **25% Spirit penetration**.
- After damage, Cyanis gains the matching **Elemental Guard through the end of the following round**.
- Does not apply an Imprint.
- Does not trigger Composite Reactions.
- Does not create an Elemental Field.
- Does not copy Core Element Array / Shaping Element Array status riders.

The recent working name `Sixfold Refraction` / `Prismatic Theorem` is **not approved** and should be replaced during the naming pass.

## 7. Ultimate — prior `Perfect Geometry`

**PROVISIONAL PRESERVE-FIRST CL13 REBASE:**
- all enemies;
- **330 Colorless Magical Power**;
- **35% Spirit penetration**;
- remove **1 removable hostile Field**;
- suppress **1 eligible hostile ward, seal, or Rune for 2 rounds**;
- create the subclass's perfected Crest Field for **3 rounds**.

Perfected Crest Field working effect:
- allies gain one-step resistance to the same established Ruin-, Rune-, Spell-, and Card-Seal categories;
- eligible hostile Spell-, Ruin-, and Rune-tagged direct damage is reduced by **25%**.

The prior Ultimate name **Perfect Geometry** is not locked and should be renamed away from geometry-heavy terminology.

## 8. Reinforced Preparation — current exact-rule proposal

**PROVISIONAL:** after the CL1 lance deals damage, Cyanis gains a temporary sequencing state until the end of the following round.

The next Crest Field or Crest Seal ability he uses during that window receives one enhancement, then the state is consumed:
- Crest Field: its initial application cannot be weakened by ordinary enemy resistance or Field-suppression effects;
- Crest Seal: gains **+15 Base Hit / application reliability** against its legal target.

This is not a global Prepared effect and does not compete with Cyanis's reaction/prepared mechanics.

The name `Reinforced Preparation` is provisional and should be replaced during the naming pass.
