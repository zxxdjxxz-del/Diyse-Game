## Core environment method
Depth through:
- layered authored art;
- selective 3D geometry;
- foreground occluders;
- middle-ground play space;
- distant backgrounds;
- lighting;
- modest parallax;
- prop-state changes;
- restrained animation.

## Reuse
Build **families**, not single-use dioramas.

A family should support:
- traversal;
- dialogue;
- random encounter staging;
- before/after states;
- optional returns;
- localized variants.

## Material readability
Regional differences should come from:
- ground texture;
- stone/wood/metal language;
- vegetation;
- climate;
- architecture;
- signage;
- lighting;
- skyline/mountain relationship.

Do not rely only on palette swaps.

## Surface vs local-site scale
The world map controls macro spatial relationships.

HD-2D chapter environments may compress local travel for production/readability while preserving:
- directional logic;
- route order;
- key terrain relationship;
- landmark identity.

Compression is not permission to move canon locations on the master map.
