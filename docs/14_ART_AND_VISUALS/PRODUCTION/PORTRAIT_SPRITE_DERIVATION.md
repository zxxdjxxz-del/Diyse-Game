**Visual rule:** the newest explicitly approved visual reference for a character/location controls all derivative field sprites, battle sprites, portraits, cut-ins, model sheets, and promotional derivatives unless that exact visual is later revised.


## Source hierarchy
1. newest exact approved character master
2. approved turnaround/secondary master
3. approved portrait/expression sheet
4. derivative field/battle asset

A derivative never outranks its master.

## Field sprite
At ~80 px:
prioritize:
- silhouette;
- hair mass/color;
- primary costume blocks;
- iconic weapon/equipment;
- height/build differences;
- readable age category where possible.

Do not chase micro-jewelry at the cost of silhouette.

## Battle sprite
At ~200–220 px:
retain more:
- armor/clothing layering;
- weapon identity;
- scars/hair details;
- distinctive cape/panel shapes;
- key props.

## Portrait
Portraits carry:
- facial acting;
- eye color;
- scars;
- makeup;
- hair detail;
- age identity;
- emotional nuance.

Do not use a simplified field sprite as face-authority reference.

## Cut-ins
Named signature / Ultimate / Prime cut-ins should derive from:
- exact face;
- exact costume;
- current weapon/prop state;

without inventing a separate "super form" unless explicitly approved.
