## 3. Cyanis approval significance

Cyanis is the first real B00 anchor and now proves the character-side rendering target:
- mature seinen/anime proportions;
- graphic cel-informed values rather than soft-brushed rendering;
- strong clean silhouette;
- angular material separation between silver armor, royal-blue cloth, and black underlayers;
- restrained gold accents;
- deliberate line hierarchy;
- no random speckle/grunge dependency;
- no painterly direction.

The exact approved render is fingerprinted in `CYANIS_CURRENT_VISUAL_LOCK.md` so later work cannot silently drift back to earlier Cyanis variants.

## 4. New-style master requirements

Every remaining party master must establish:
- mature seinen/anime proportions and facial construction;
- approved identity without accidental age/face drift;
- visibly chaotic variable line weight;
- thick-to-thin tapered strokes;
- broken/disappearing contours where light/form supports them;
- heavier line buildup at weight, overlap, joints, compression, weapon intersections, and deep shadow;
- precise restrained facial linework;
- large readable hair masses with energetic tapered accents;
- **graphic cel-informed shading with roughly 2–4 dominant value groups**;
- controlled material separation between skin, hair, cloth, leather, metal, and magical elements;
- rich but controlled color;
- no painterly/soft-brushed rendering;
- no glossy mobile-gacha finish;
- no photoreal rendering;
- no sterile uniform digital outline;
- no flat low-budget cel shading;
- no generic AI-smooth or random-speckle surface treatment.

Character-specific redesigns are allowed only when explicitly approved during B00, as happened with Cyanis. Once approved, the new B00 design supersedes older outfit details for that character.

## 5. Scale derivatives

After each high-resolution master is approved, create style-faithful runtime derivatives rather than blind downscales.

### Battle target
Approximate target: **200–220 px character height**.

Preserve silhouette, major thick/thin line rhythm, face/hair read, weapon/class identity, strong shadow masses, and important costume/material divisions. Remove micro-line noise that aliases.

### Field target
Approximate target: **~80 px character height**.

Preserve silhouette, dominant palette blocks, hair/face read, iconic equipment, and a few deliberate heavy ink accents. Do not reproduce every portrait-scale mark.

## 6. Production sequence

1. approve a clean high-resolution new-style master;
2. fingerprint and document that exact render;
3. create battle-scale derivative;
4. create field-scale derivative;
5. place the character against representative B01/B03/B10 scene/material tests;
6. test a representative B06 VFX effect beside/around the character;
7. repeat through all six permanent characters;
8. only then declare B00 passed.

Generated character sheets, invented labels/stats, or multi-view presentation boards are not substitutes for the master. The preferred B00 review asset is a **single clean full-body character render** unless another view is specifically requested.

## 7. Cross-category validation

Once B00 passes, recheck:
- B01 stone versus character line hierarchy;
- B03 foliage versus player silhouette/readability;
- B10 props versus character material/rendering cohesion;
- B06 fire/VFX versus the anime/seinen character presentation;
- future enemy/NPC art against the same grammar.

Earlier material-family work is not automatically invalidated, but it must conform to the revised non-painterly visual authority before final acceptance.
