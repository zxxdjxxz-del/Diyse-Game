# Diyse — B00 Party Character Style Anchor v1

**Benchmark:** B00 — Permanent Party / Character Style Anchor  
**Status:** **REQUIRED / NEW-STYLE PARTY MASTERS NOT YET PRODUCED**  
**Style authority:** `../../DIYSE_VISUAL_STYLE_CANON.md`

## 1. Correction of scope

Diyse already has approved permanent-party **character designs and appearance references** for Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren, Nimera, Vaelira, and Seyrik.

Those approvals lock identity-level information such as face, age read, body type, hair, clothing, armor, equipment, palette, silhouette, and other character-specific design details.

They do **not** constitute finished character masters in the newly locked visual rendering style:

> **Seinen HD-2D Fantasy with Chaotic Variable Line Weight**

The existing character appearance references must therefore be treated as **identity/design authorities**, not as proof that the party has already been rendered in the new style.

## 2. Why B00 is required

Characters carry the strongest line identity in Diyse. Environment art, props, VFX, portraits, enemies, and runtime derivatives are supposed to echo or support that hierarchy.

Because the six permanent party members have not yet been rendered in the new style, the project is missing its primary visual anchor.

B00 must therefore precede any claim that the full benchmark set has been cross-category validated as one coherent game.

Existing B01/B03/B10 approvals remain valid **inside their own material/asset families**, but their final cross-game cohesion must be rechecked against B00 once the party masters exist.

B06 remains visual-style pending and should not be advanced until the character anchor gives its anime/seinen VFX target a concrete in-game reference.

## 3. Required party set

B00 covers all six permanent party members:

- Cyanis
- Ilyra
- Torren
- Nimera
- Vaelira
- Seyrik

No redesign is implied. The goal is to preserve each approved character identity while changing **how the design is drawn, shaded, inked, and presented**.

## 4. New-style master requirements

Each high-resolution party master must establish:

- mature seinen/anime proportions and facial construction;
- the already-approved character identity without age drift or costume redesign;
- visibly chaotic variable line weight;
- thick-to-thin tapered strokes;
- broken or disappearing contours where light/form supports them;
- heavier line buildup at weight, overlap, armor joints, fabric compression, weapon intersections, and deep shadow;
- precise restrained facial linework;
- large readable hair masses with energetic tapered accents;
- hybrid painterly/cel shading with roughly 2–4 dominant value groups;
- controlled material separation between skin, hair, cloth, leather, metal, and magical elements;
- rich but controlled color;
- no glossy mobile-gacha finish;
- no photoreal rendering;
- no sterile uniform digital outline;
- no flat low-budget cel shading;
- no generic AI-smooth surface treatment.

## 5. Scale derivatives

After a high-resolution master is approved, each character must receive style-faithful runtime derivatives rather than blind downscales.

### Battle target

Approximate target: **200–220 px character height**.

Preserve:
- silhouette;
- major thick/thin line rhythm;
- face/hair read;
- weapon/class identity;
- strong shadow masses;
- important costume/material divisions.

Remove micro-line noise that aliases at runtime scale.

### Field target

Approximate target: **~80 px character height**.

Preserve:
- silhouette;
- dominant palette blocks;
- hair/face read;
- iconic equipment;
- a few deliberate heavy ink accents.

Do not attempt to reproduce every portrait-scale mark.

## 6. Review sequence

B00 should be produced asset-first, not as a generated infographic.

Recommended sequence:

1. preserve the current approved design reference for a character;
2. create one clean high-resolution new-style character master;
3. review identity retention and line/shading language;
4. create battle-scale derivative;
5. create field-scale derivative;
6. place the character against representative B01/B03/B10 scene/material tests;
7. test one representative B06 VFX effect beside/around the character;
8. repeat across the full six-person party;
9. only then declare the character rendering language locked across the permanent cast.

The first character may be used as a pilot, but **B00 does not pass until all six permanent party members have approved new-style masters**.

## 7. Cross-category validation

Once B00 passes, recheck:

- B01 stone versus character line hierarchy;
- B03 foliage versus player silhouette/readability;
- B10 props versus character material/rendering cohesion;
- B06 fire/VFX versus the anime/seinen character presentation;
- future enemy and NPC art against the same character grammar.

This does not automatically invalidate earlier material-family work. It creates the missing character authority needed to judge whether those assets actually belong in the same game.
