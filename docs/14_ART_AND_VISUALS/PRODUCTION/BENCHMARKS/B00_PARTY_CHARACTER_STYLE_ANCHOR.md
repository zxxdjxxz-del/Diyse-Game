# B00 — Party Character Style Anchor

**Status:** CYANIS OUTFIT LOCKED / PARTY STYLE ANCHOR IN PROGRESS

B00 exists to establish the first true party-character rendering authority for the current Diyse visual direction. Older approved party renders remain design/identity references only unless explicitly promoted here.

## Current visual direction

Diyse uses a mature seinen-inspired HD-2D fantasy style with chaotic variable line weight, graphic anime-stylized rendering, shape-first readability, and cinematic atmospheric lighting. Painterly rendering language is retired from current-facing art direction.

## Cyanis — locked face and hair

The approved Cyanis face/hair is locked from the current B00 exploration:
- youthful late-20s appearance;
- clean-shaven face;
- short/tight sides;
- longer textured/tousled dark-brown top;
- sharp mature-seinen facial structure;
- hazel eyes;
- no return to the earlier facial-hair treatment.

## Cyanis — locked outfit

The latest approved full-body render is the outfit authority for Cyanis going forward.

Locked outfit traits:
- bilateral silver shoulder armor — armor on both shoulders;
- angular, segmented silver chest armor with a tapered V-like plate architecture rather than a single medieval breastplate;
- dark royal-blue structured longcoat/tabard with long split tails;
- fitted black underlayer/combat trousers;
- black leather used primarily at articulation, gloves, belts, and boots;
- silver articulated forearm armor on both arms;
- angular silver shin/knee armor over the dark leg layer;
- restrained gold piping/trim only;
- no shield;
- no chest crest;
- no back crest;
- no hip crest/amulet required;
- no excessive filigree, decorative clutter, tassels, or generic ceremonial-knight ornamentation;
- no painterly finish.

The silhouette should read as a mobile royal battle knight with clean anime shape language, not a walking suit of medieval plate armor.

## Superseded Cyanis outfit explorations

All earlier B00 outfit generations from this exploration are superseded for outfit design. They may not be used to reintroduce:
- one-shoulder armor;
- the older ornate breastplate silhouette;
- large crest medallions;
- character-sheet-only invented details;
- generic knight filigree;
- rogue/light-armor drift;
- shields or newly invented heraldry.

## Next B00 gate

Cyanis now serves as the first outfit-design lock, but B00 is not complete until the approved locked design is rendered and evaluated consistently under the final character rendering treatment at production/runtime scales, then used to establish the remaining five party-member style passes.
