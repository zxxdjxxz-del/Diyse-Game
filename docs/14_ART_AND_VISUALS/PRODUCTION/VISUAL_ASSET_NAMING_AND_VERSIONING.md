# Diyse — Visual Asset Naming / Versioning

**Status:** ACTIVE VISUAL-ASSET PRODUCTION RULE  
**Authority:** current visual owner domains plus later explicit approved corrections.  
**Visual rule:** the exact current approved character/location master controls derivative identity. Derivatives may include rigged models, portraits, cut-ins, icons, model sheets, promotional art, or other approved surfaces; none becomes a new master without explicit approval.


## Stable production naming
Active production assets should use:
- current character/location name;
- asset role;
- expression/state;
- stable semantic ID where useful.

Avoid embedding obsolete canon terms in new player-facing asset names.

## Historical assets
Old filenames may contain:
- Southhold;
- Crownhold;
- Blackstone;
- Westreach;
- Sixfold Annex;
- First Champion;
- old character ages.

These may remain in archive/reference storage.

Do not treat filename text as current canon.

## Master tagging
For locked appearance assets, maintain explicit metadata such as:
- MASTER
- LOCKED
- DERIVATIVE
- RETIRED
- ARCHIVE

Do not overwrite the only approved master while creating:
- optional 2D reductions/derivatives;
- recolor tests;
- expression variants;
- background-removal versions.

## Version control
A new derivative is not a new canon master unless explicitly approved as one.
