Standard elements exactly:
- Fire
- Ice
- Lightning
- Earth

## Linked harmful-status language
Where authored:
- Fire → Burn
- Ice → Freeze
- Lightning → Stun
- Earth → Staggered

## Production requirement
Each element should have a distinct readable:
- motion language;
- impact language;
- particle/shape family;
- sound handoff;
- icon/color treatment;

without requiring every spell to be visually unique.

## Retired
Do not build combat-element pipelines for:
- Wind
- Water

Ordinary:
- rain;
- wind;
- rivers;
- spray;
- mist;
- condensation

remain environmental visuals.

## Ruin
Ruin is:
- special affinity/school;
- not a fifth standard element.

Do not place Ruin in a five-element wheel.
