## Standard Cards
Visual goal:
- magical technique/action object;
- reusable Card identity;
- strong Face readability;
- not a deckbuilder hand-animation language.

No draw/discard/shuffle VFX grammar is needed.

## Prime Cards
Prime invocation must read as a much larger manifestation event than Standard Card use.

### Recovered Story Prime
- one signature action;
- immediate dismissal same ordinary round;
- strong but bounded manifestation.

### Awakened
- full direct-control Prime state;
- three Prime rounds;
- sustained manifestation identity.

### Major-Hunt Prime
- acquired Awakened;
- should visually communicate completed optional ordeal.
