## Visual causality
The finale should make the six roles readable as a sequence, not a simultaneous indistinguishable light explosion.

Suggested readability:
- HOLD — stabilizing/locking geometry
- DISTINGUISH — native vs corrupted magic separation
- MAP — trace/path identification
- PRESERVE — protected living/viable structure boundary
- CONTAIN — isolated boundaries around each identified trace
- END — simultaneous eradication of isolated Entity traces


Last Convergence's title does not mean the party literally repeats ancient convergence.
