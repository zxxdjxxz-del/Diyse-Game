# Global Chapter-0 enemy presentation

Chapter 0 visually reads as:
- night battle;
- wrecked convoy;
- field fire;
- smoke;
- panic;
- harsh military pressure.

Maintain strong visual separation between:
1. **Black Host military enemies**;
2. **Rift / warped war-beasts**;
3. **the concealed Ruin Vanguard Pursuer**.

Enemy silhouettes must remain readable at field and battle camera distance as well as in high-resolution concept/reference art.

## Black Host line troops
Shared visual language:
- disciplined and militarized;
- dark iron / dark steel;
- blackened leather and dark cloth;
- restrained crimson accents;
- practical issued war gear;
- severe imperial geometry;
- functional rather than ornamental.

Do not make Black Host line troops look like heroic knights, flamboyant fantasy villains, cultists, or generic glossy-gacha soldiers.

## Rift beasts
Shared visual language:
- visibly living creatures;
- recognizable animal/predator anatomy first;
- wrong or Ruin-adjacent characteristics second;
- not undead;
- not pure demons;
- not amorphous goo monsters;
- Black Host war-beast handling may leave practical restraint/harness evidence.

## Concealed elite threat
The Ruin Vanguard Pursuer is visually distinct from line troops:
- heavier;
- more individualized;
- more threatening;
- intentionally obscured enough to preserve Seyrik's Chapter-0 identity concealment.

# Black Host Raider

## Core visual identity
Frontline Black Host infantry built for fast, aggressive pressure.

## Silhouette
- medium-build adult human soldier;
- compact, forward-leaning profile;
- lighter than a full heavy knight;
- clearly melee-oriented;
- readable one-handed weapon silhouette.

## Body / proportions
- athletic military frame;
- not gigantic;
- not bulky like Seyrik;
- should read as a trained line soldier rather than a brute.

## Clothing / armor
- dark iron lamellar or segmented plate over black padded underlayers;
- angular plated shoulders without oversized pauldrons;
- black or charcoal cloth at the joints;
- dark leather straps and harness points;
- short military tabard or waist cloth with restrained crimson accent;
- practical hard-wearing boots.

## Head / face
- usually helmeted;
- helmet may be enclosed or partially enclosed;
- narrow eye slit or shadowed face opening preferred;
- if visible, the face remains limited and non-individualized.

## Weapon
- one-handed short military saber / arming blade / heavy war-falchion family;
- brutal and functional;
- not elegant;
- not oversized.

## Palette
- charcoal / black iron;
- dull gunmetal;
- muted crimson;
- black-brown leather;
- small pale-steel edge highlights.

## Signature read
- red military accent;
- severe state-issued geometry;
- issued equipment rather than personalized adventuring gear.

## Motion / animation
- aggressive advance;
- low guarded stance;
- direct slashing motion;
- soldier pressing an advantage, not a duelist performing flourishes.

## Must not
- bright gold ornament;
- heroic-knight styling;
- sleek pretty-boy combat styling;
- huge cape;
- wild barbarian language.

# Black Host Crossbowman

## Core visual identity
Ranged Black Host soldier providing controlled battlefield pressure.

## Silhouette
- medium human silhouette;
- slimmer/lighter than Raider;
- crossbow must read immediately;
- visibly ranged at battle distance.

## Body / proportions
- lean military frame;
- less armored than Raider;
- mobile but disciplined.

## Clothing / armor
- black or deep-charcoal military coat / half-coat;
- lighter chest protection;
- forearm guards suitable for firing;
- strapped bolt case/quiver;
- simple shoulder protection;
- more dark cloth than heavy metal.

## Head / face
- helmet or hooded military helm;
- lower face may be covered by cloth or a metal guard;
- anonymous and uniformed rather than individualized.

## Weapon
- compact Black Host military crossbow;
- angular, industrial construction;
- reinforced limbs;
- dark metal with dark wood or black composite structure;
- not ornate hunting gear.

## Palette
- dark steel;
- black cloth;
- restrained crimson;
- dark leather;
- small pale-metal highlights.

## Signature read
- visible bolt case and loading rig;
- immediately reads as the ranged member of an organized military line.

## Motion / animation
- planted feet;
- aim / snap-fire / reload posture;
- more methodical than the Raider.

## Must not
- longbow identity;
- rogue/thief styling;
- flamboyant sniper aesthetic;
- oversized siege-weapon silhouette.

# Ruin Shieldbearer

## Core visual identity
Heavy Black Host defensive infantry with a Ruin-affiliated edge.

## Silhouette
- broadest ordinary Black Host soldier in Chapter 0;
- heavy defensive profile;
- tower-like stance;
- shield defines the silhouette.

## Body / proportions
- stockier and more heavily built than Raider/Crossbowman;
- may read slightly taller or simply broader;
- planted, difficult-to-move presence.

## Clothing / armor
- denser plate coverage;
- heavier chest and shoulder armor;
- thick underpadding;
- blackened, scarred metal;
- heavier boots and gauntlets.

## Head / face
- full helm preferred;
- more enclosed than Raider;
- faceless military-enforcer read.

## Shield
- large, dark, angular military shield;
- oppressive and practical;
- slight Ruin-edged detailing allowed;
- must still look physically functional rather than like a magical prop.

## Weapon
- short thrusting weapon, mace, or compact spear-like sidearm;
- visually secondary to the shield.

## Palette
- black iron / dark steel;
- deeper crimson accent than lighter Black Host troops;
- slightly colder overall read.

## Signature read
- visually communicates “hold the line”;
- broad shield face;
- severe panel divisions or restrained state insignia.

## Motion / animation
- shield-forward stance;
- short bashes;
- guarded thrusts;
- slow, authoritative movement.

## Must not
- paladin language;
- holy-knight styling;
- comically oversized shield larger than the body;
- baseline glowing magical-barrier visuals.

# Convoy Rift Hound

## Core visual identity
Fast Black Host-employed war-beast / rift-touched predator used in convoy interception.

## Silhouette
- low, fast quadruped;
- closer to a warped hunting hound / wolf-hound than a feline;
- lean forward body;
- aggressive head silhouette;
- long enough to feel dangerous rather than compact or cute.

## Anatomy
- four-legged;
- muscular but not enormous;
- elongated shoulders / forelimb emphasis for lunging;
- narrow waist;
- heavy neck and jaw;
- tail reduced, ragged, or held tensely rather than elegant.

## Head
- elongated predatory skull;
- oversized jaw slightly beyond natural proportion;
- visible teeth;
- intense, wrong-looking eyes;
- subtle Ruin fissuring or dark tearing around mouth/eyes allowed.

## Surface / hide
- short dark hide/fur;
- patchy in places;
- real beast first, wrong second;
- scarred restraint marks, collar abrasions, or old war-harness wear are appropriate;
- Ruin veining/cracking must remain restrained.

## Palette
- black-brown / charcoal hide;
- dull ash gray or bruised undertones;
- faint Ruin-crimson or dim violet-red in eyes/fissures if used;
- never neon.

## Signature read
- deployed war-beast, not wild forest animal;
- must plausibly have been transported and handled by Black Host forces;
- subtle collar, restraint ring, or broken control-harness language is appropriate.

## Motion / animation
- low rapid lunges;
- snapping bite;
- abrupt/skittering acceleration;
- attack-dog energy taken into dangerous fantasy territory.

## Must not
- undead dog;
- goo beast;
- pure demon wolf;
- cute fantasy wolf;
- boss-scale giant proportions.

# Ruin Vanguard Pursuer — concealed Seyrik presentation

## Authority relationship
Underlying identity:
> **Seyrik**

Seyrik's current repository character master remains the authority for his actual face, build, armor identity, palette, and signature weapon. Chapter 0 changes only **presentation/concealment**, not his true appearance.

## Core visual identity
Terrifying elite Ruin Vanguard whose exact identity is obscured, but who should retrospectively feel unmistakably consistent with Seyrik.

## Silhouette
- tall;
- broad;
- intimidating human profile;
- visibly stronger than ordinary Black Host troops;
- cloak/coat-panel plus armored bulk;
- large weapon silhouette.

## Body / proportions
- massive adult build;
- physically dominant;
- clearly beyond Cyanis/Ilyra in raw presence;
- never presented in a clean hero-reference pose during Chapter 0.

## Clothing / armor
- heavy dark armor consistent with Seyrik's current master;
- black / charcoal armor;
- deep-red underlayers;
- long coat panels / hanging armored cloth;
- severe Black Host-compatible elite styling.

## Identity concealment
Use staging rather than redesign:
- shadow;
- angle;
- damaged/obscuring visor or partial face obstruction where compatible with the master;
- high collar;
- smoke/fire contrast;
- limited clean face exposure.

The player must know this is a person, not a construct.

## Weapon
- heavy greatsword silhouette;
- oversized relative to ordinary infantry weapons;
- important retrospective clue;
- do not frame it as an unmistakable clean “Seyrik reveal” in Chapter 0.

## Palette
- black / deep charcoal;
- dark red;
- cold metallic edge highlights;
- oppressive and heavy.

## Signature read
Must communicate:
- Black Host elite;
- personally dangerous;
- huge build;
- dark armor;
- red underlayers;
- greatsword silhouette;
- later visual continuity with Seyrik.

## Motion / animation
- controlled;
- efficient;
- overwhelming;
- minimal wasted motion;
- no frantic aggression.

## Must not
- clean full Seyrik reveal;
- brightly exposed blond hair as an obvious identifier;
- clear hero-lit presentation of his signature scars;
- theatrical anime-villain posing.

# Convoy War-Sorcerer

## Core visual identity
Black Host battlefield caster attached to the convoy-interception force.

## Silhouette
- human caster;
- slimmer than Raider/Shieldbearer;
- upright and composed;
- robe/coat-panel or military-mantle shape;
- a compact casting implement should aid immediate role recognition.

## Body / proportions
- adult;
- disciplined battlefield mage;
- neither frail nor melee-built.

## Clothing / armor
- Black Host military-caster hybrid;
- layered dark robes or coat over structured armor pieces;
- lighter chest/shoulder armor than line troops;
- sleeves/gloves suited to spellcasting;
- belts, seals, pouches, or catalyst fixtures.

## Head / face
- hood, high collar, military hood-helm, or restrained visor/circlet;
- face may be partially visible;
- cold, task-focused expression;
- not flamboyant sorcerer-king presentation.

## Casting language
- geometric;
- controlled;
- military;
- dark-red / dim ruin-magenta / pale colorless energy;
- precise battlefield effects rather than chaotic wild magic.

## Weapon / focus
Preferred:
> **compact military war-rod / focus staff**

A catalyst gauntlet or similar issued focus may support the design, but the unit should not read as an unarmed generic mage.

## Palette
- charcoal;
- black;
- muted crimson;
- dim pale Ruin/colorless light;
- slightly greater arcane glow than line troops.

## Signature read
- combat officer / battle mage;
- organized army member;
- war-sorcerer, not cultist or necromancer.

## Motion / animation
- deliberate aiming gestures;
- composed spell release;
- ritualized Rift Lance preparation;
- no wild flailing.

## Must not
- wizard hat;
- extravagant fantasy robes;
- skeletal necromancer language;
- evil-priest confusion.

# Riftmaw

## Core visual identity
Large Black Host war-beast boss: a heavy rift predator more extreme than the Hounds and visually suitable for the Chapter-0 climax.

## Silhouette
- large low quadrupedal beast;
- broad front half;
- enormous maw is the defining feature;
- heavier and more monstrous than a Convoy Rift Hound;
- still fundamentally beast-based.

## Anatomy
- heavy quadruped;
- oversized shoulders and ribcage;
- powerful forelimbs;
- shorter but powerful hindquarters;
- neck compressed into the torso;
- disproportionately enlarged head/jaw;
- body built visually for frontal assault.

## Head / maw
- massive split or overwide jaw;
- layered teeth;
- jawline may imply rift tearing / impossible bite depth;
- small intense eyes;
- animal-adjacent face, never humanoid.

## Surface / materials
- thick hide rather than fur;
- rugged scarred skin;
- restrained Ruin tearing/cracks/energy seams around the mouth or chest;
- heavy control harness, chain points, plated restraint pieces, or war fittings may show Black Host handling.

## Palette
- dark charcoal-brown / blackened hide;
- bruised fleshy undertones;
- dim red-violet rift glow deep inside maw fissures/wounds if used;
- restraint metal in black iron.

## Signature read
- visually related to the Rift Hound family without being a simple enlarged Hound;
- deployed military beast, not random cave monster;
- the **maw dominates the identity**.

If the mouth does not dominate the silhouette/read:
> the design has drifted.

## Motion / animation
- crushing lunges;
- head-whip bite;
- chest/maw-originating Rift Pulse;
- planted, violent body language.

## Must not
- dragon;
- insect monster;
- slime beast;
- generic demon;
- elegant chimera.
