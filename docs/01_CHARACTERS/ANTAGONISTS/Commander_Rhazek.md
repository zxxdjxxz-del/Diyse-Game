## Core
Rhazek is a seasoned commander born inside the Black Host imperial system, not an abducted child parallel to Seyrik.

He is disciplined, controlled, observant, pragmatic, patient, professionally proud, quietly intimidating, dryly funny, and competitive.

He is genuinely skilled at:
- logistics;
- fortification;
- morale;
- retreat;
- reserves;
- engineering;
- casualty management.

## Moral architecture
Central belief:
> order can carry moral responsibility for people who serve it.

Moral failure:
> he judges systems by function long before he judges them by consent.

He does not hide behind "just following orders."
He chooses, interprets, improves and owns decisions.

His deterioration is not abandonment of principles.
He creates exceptions until the principles mean their opposite.
