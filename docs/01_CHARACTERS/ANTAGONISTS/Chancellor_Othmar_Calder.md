## Core
Othmar is a brilliant institutional caretaker whose deepest fear is **discontinuity**:
- political vacuum;
- contradictory lawful orders;
- succession failure;
- unclear legitimacy;
- emergencies where nobody can prove who has the right to decide.

Healthy belief:
> civilization survives through continuity, procedure, redundancy, competent stewardship, and institutions stronger than individuals.

Corruption:
> this institution must continue → this institution must continue in the form I believe is safe → lawful authority that can interrupt continuity becomes a threat.

Central contradiction:
> he spends his life trying to make institutions stronger than individuals, then makes himself indispensable to the institution.

## Background
Educated, non-aristocratic civil-service family.
His professional formation comes through:
- records;

- authentication;

He has known Mirena since youth.
Pride/familiarity can become paternalism when the adult heir's present judgment is filtered through his memory of the vulnerable thirteen-year-old.
