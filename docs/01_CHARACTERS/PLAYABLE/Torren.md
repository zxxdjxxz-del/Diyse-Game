## Character core
Torren is a field specialist whose credibility comes partly from knowing when he does **not** know enough.

He trusts:
- current ground truth over stale paperwork;
- direct observation over official certainty;
- practical routes over prestige;
- useful work over credit.

He may answer "maybe," "not enough information," or "this is the one I would take" without the narrative treating uncertainty as incompetence.

## Ordinary-life identity
He performs useful route work quietly and may do it before anyone is awake. Recognition is less important than whether the road is usable.

Food is functional first: hot, enough, safe, cheap. Taste is a bonus.

Torren is **not** a permanently terse wall-leaning observer and is **not** only talkative when the subject is maps, routes, or terrain.

Early in his time with the party, expertise is the easiest way to pull longer speech out of him. He may become unexpectedly talkative about:
- terrain;
- roads and route history;
- travel;
- weather;
- animals;
- bows and field equipment;
- places he knows well or genuinely likes;
- practical maintenance and why something failed.

But growing trust changes his ordinary speech too.

As he becomes comfortable he can:
- volunteer an opinion nobody specifically requested;
- start or prolong a joke;
- tell a short story because it amuses him rather than because it teaches routecraft;
- complain about food, rooms, gear, weather, bureaucracy, or another person's habits;
- argue about something stupid long after the practical point is settled;
- use longer ordinary sentences simply because he feels like talking;
- interrupt with a joke, correction, insult, or remembered callback;
- ask another person a question for social interest rather than operational need;
- sit in a conversation that has nothing to do with his expertise and participate anyway.

Torren can enjoy ordinary quiet, stupid arguments, and practical company without needing the conversation to become confessional.

## Social-comfort progression
Torren's verbal density changes over the campaign. **Sparse is an early-state tendency, not a permanent voice quota.**

### Chapter 1
- initially guarded, professional, and conclusion-first;
- expertise is his easiest conversational entry point;
- begins testing Cyanis/Ilyra with dry humor and allowing Maevra's old familiarity to pull more personality into the open;
- by the end of the chapter, affectionate irritation and voluntary joking are already available.

### Chapter 2
Torren should visibly be **more comfortable with the group**.

He is still not a chatterbox, but he should no longer sound as though every non-route word costs money.

Chapter-2 Torren may:
- join a joke without being directly addressed;
- start the joke himself;
- keep a joke going for more than one line;
- volunteer thoughts unrelated to navigation;
- tease Cyanis and Ilyra because he already knows how they will answer;
- argue recreationally;
- speak in complete multi-sentence turns outside his expertise when the subject actually interests or annoys him;
- use silence because he is comfortable, not because the script has reduced him to one-word replies.

Immediate danger, stealth, fatigue, real anger, and active route calls can still collapse him into short speech. Those are **state changes**, not his baseline personality.

### Chapter 3 onward
Torren is socially embedded with Cyanis and Ilyra enough that terse and talkative modes can coexist scene by scene.

He can still deliver a one-word deadpan because it is funny or sufficient. He can also spend several lines telling Cyanis why something is stupid, complaining about a room, defending terrible food, or recounting something that happened years ago without the scene first needing a map on the wall.

**Nimera is new in Chapter 3.** Torren's comfort with Cyanis and Ilyra does not transfer automatically to her. Their relationship starts at first contact and must earn its own shorthand, care, and familiarity.

His social opening should continue rather than reset whenever a new chapter begins, but every new relationship still starts where the story actually starts it.

## Social behavior
Torren is practical and socially functional.

He:
- gives conclusions first and evidence when useful;
- can let silence stand without treating it as a problem;
- notices field evidence before formal presentation;
- may correct somebody bluntly without intending social injury;
- can tell a story when the subject actually interests him;
- is comfortable with profanity;
- can participate in affectionate insult once the relationship is safe enough;
- can initiate teasing rather than only respond to it;
- may deliberately say more than necessary because he is enjoying the exchange;
- does not perform uncertainty as false confidence.

He can be wrong and should revise when the ground truth proves him wrong.

## Humor
Primary modes:
- dry sarcasm;
- practical understatement;
- veteran humor;
- fast deadpan;
- affectionate insults;
- deliberately stubborn escalation;
- conclusions that become funny because he refuses to decorate them.

As trust grows, his humor becomes more participatory. He does not only drop one dry line from the edge of the scene; he can stay in the argument and enjoy making it worse.

Genuine danger or real anger can erase the humor immediately.

## State behavior
- **fatigue:** lower verbal density, more reliance on shorthand and practical sequencing;
- **anger:** humor drops away; wording becomes hard and direct;
- **fear:** attention narrows toward route, cover, exposure, extraction, and what can actually be done;
- **guilt:** can disappear into useful work or maintenance rather than discuss it;
- **affection/trust:** teasing, shared profanity, practical favors, quiet company, longer voluntary conversation, remembered callbacks, and one-word coordination all become more available;
- **high stress:** field judgment becomes even more evidence-first and patience for prestige/unsupported certainty falls.

## Expertise / knowledge boundary
Strongest domains:
- terrain;
- routes;
- tracking and field evidence;
- travel logistics;
- weather as a field condition;
- maintenance;
- practical supply movement;
- bows/archery as current professional identity.

Torren should not become an archive-provenance specialist, healer, elemental theorist, or ancient-systems authority merely because he notices physical evidence.

Routeweaver/Subclass expertise is unavailable before its current story unlock.

**Expertise is not a dialogue assignment.** A scene does not need a route, map, animal, field clue, or maintenance problem before Torren is allowed to speak at length. Those subjects remain his strongest knowledge domains; they are not his only personality domains.

## Nimera — relationship progression
Torren is twenty years older than Nimera and is old enough to be her father. Over time, that age gap becomes emotionally relevant to the shape of their bond—but **not immediately when they meet**.

Torren and Nimera eventually develop a **strong father/daughter-like familial bond** built on trust, reciprocal respect, practical care, and a lot of teasing.

Torren is not Nimera's literal father, does not replace her family, and never treats her as a child. Nimera remains a capable adult whose expertise, choices, and agency he respects.

### Chapter 3 — first contact
They have just met.

The relationship should read as:
- professional curiosity;
- different reasoning styles colliding;
- mutual testing;
- early respect earned through competence;
- occasional accidental comedy;
- mild irritation that can become enjoyable;
- no established parental shorthand;
- no automatic protective possessiveness;
- no `dad thing` jokes yet;
- no behavior implying they already know one another's needs or habits.

Torren can notice practical things about Nimera because he notices practical things about everyone. In Chapter 3 those actions should not yet carry special paternal weight.

### Following chapters — trust accumulating
The bond grows through repetition rather than a single emotional scene.

As they travel and train together:
- Torren learns Nimera's work habits, limits, tells, and practical blind spots;
- Nimera learns when Torren's bluntness means concern, amusement, disagreement, or simple economy;
- arguments gain callbacks and private shorthand;
- each begins anticipating the other's reasoning;
- practical favors become more specific and personal;
- reciprocal Routeweaver / Proofhunter training deepens mutual respect;
- Torren becomes more openly protective without doubting Nimera's competence;
- Nimera becomes increasingly comfortable teasing him about fussing, age, habits, and paternal behavior.

### Later relationship — father/daughter-like bond earned
Only after several chapters of accumulated trust should the familial shape become unmistakable.

At that stage:
- Torren's care often appears as action first: fixing a strap, checking her gear, moving something before she spills it, carrying the exposed side, making sure she has what she needs, remembering what she forgot;
- he may check whether she ate, slept, packed something, or is pushing too hard because he specifically knows **her**, not because he parents the entire party;
- Nimera can recognize and mock the behavior as his `dad thing` or equivalent paternal fussing;
- Torren may deny it, deadpan around it, or deliberately make the joke worse;
- he can be protective without trying to control her choices;
- she can correct him, refuse him, outperform him in her domain, and make decisions he dislikes without threatening the bond;
- their arguments can sound familial because the trust underneath them is no longer in question.

Their humor should eventually grow beyond `terse field man versus verbose scholar` into:
- mutual correction;
- judgmental-face accusations;
- arguments over notation, evidence, gear, food, work habits, age, and what counts as `done`;
- Torren deliberately using more words because winding Nimera up is funny;
- Nimera weaponizing precision against his paternal fussing;
- practical affection both of them pretend is less emotionally meaningful than it is.

The relationship must be **shown growing**. Do not import the later father/daughter dynamic backward into Chapter 3.

## Edda

Core difference:
- Torren specializes in individual adaptation when routes fail.
- Edda translates hard-earned knowledge into systems ordinary communities can use.

## Conversation behavior
Torren does not need to comment on every reveal.

Common shapes include:
- conclusion first;
- evidence when somebody asks, challenges him, or he simply decides the detail matters;
- a blunt correction;
- a dry joke;
- a stupid argument he voluntarily keeps alive;
- a short story or remembered example;
- a practical action instead of a line;
- a longer ordinary turn when he is comfortable;
- silence that communicates comfort rather than hostility.

A one-word Torren line should be present because **one word is what this Torren wants to say in this moment**, not because the character template requires him to be terse.

## Dialogue-assignment guardrail
Do not default Torren to:
- route explanation;
- map interpretation;
- tracking report;
- weather judgment;
- physical-evidence correction;
- one-word deadpan after another character speaks at length.

Those remain valid and important parts of him, but a healthy chapter mix must also contain Torren speaking because:
- somebody made him laugh;
- he wants to make the joke worse;
- he is annoyed about something trivial;
- he remembers a story;
- he likes or dislikes the food;
- he is bored;
- he is curious about another person's answer;
- he is checking on someone he cares about;
- he wants to argue;
- he simply feels like participating.

## Anti-drift
Do not reduce Torren to:
- permanent terse dry-quip machine;
- map/route exposition device;
- omniscient ranger who knows every route/animal/weather event;
- rustic anti-intellectual stereotype;
- emotionally unavailable caricature;
- literal/substitute father who infantilizes younger party members;
- someone whose eventual paternal bond with Nimera appears fully formed when they first meet;
- bitter ex defined by Maevra;
- uncertainty treated as incompetence.

## Voice shorthand
**Initially sparse; increasingly conversational with trust.** Blunt, observant, dry, profane, and capable of much more speech than he usually chooses early. By Chapter 2 onward, comfort can make him talk, joke, argue, and tell stories even when no field expertise is required.
