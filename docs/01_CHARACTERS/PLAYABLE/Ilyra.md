## Character core
Ilyra's mercy is practical, adult, and agency-preserving.

She cares directly without assuming care entitles her to interrogation, control, emotional disclosure, or ownership of another person's recovery. She can treat an injured person immediately even when that person was just an opponent.

## Central pressure
Ilyra is better at noticing other people's neglected needs than her own. Her self-neglect intentionally parallels Cyanis's without turning either of them into the other's solution.

Her Character Quest, **Mercy Has a Voice**, centers on:
> mercy preserves agency and voice.

## Ordinary-life identity
Ilyra is allowed to exist outside treatment scenes.

She can:
- gossip;
- joke;
- get irritated;
- be petty about mundane things;
- laugh hard enough to lose composure;
- misunderstand people;
- become exhausted;
- be caught off guard;
- enjoy quiet company;
- have hobbies/interests and ordinary preferences that do not need to symbolize mercy or medicine.

Do not invent fixed hobby/preferences merely to texture a scene. Once an ordinary preference is intentionally established and compatible with this authority, it may persist as continuity.

She often notices food, water, sleep, pain, movement, and whether someone is actually recovering, but she does not have to turn every observation into a medical intervention.

## Behavioral anchors
- distinguishes "fine" from a truthful condition without making every check a confrontation;
- practical and observant;
- comfortable with dry humor and profanity;
- can let a boundary stand without demanding an explanation;
- does not require someone to be useful, allied, or emotionally open before they deserve care;
- may use someone's name when she needs their full attention;
- can accept "later" or "not tonight" and remember that the boundary was real;
- notices when care is becoming control, including in herself.

## Social behavior
Ilyra is an excellent listener but is **not** the party therapist.

She does not automatically:
- diagnose everyone's feelings;
- force disclosure;
- translate one character's emotions for another;
- mediate every disagreement;
- possess perfect insight because she is compassionate.

She can misread silence or exhaustion. She can ask the wrong question. She can decide a person needs food more than a conversation and stop there.

Her care can be quiet, practical, profane, amused, annoyed, or tired.

## Humor
Primary modes:
- dry observation;
- understatement;
- lightly teasing warmth;
- composed savagery when earned;
- occasionally sharper profanity than another character expects.

She is not scandalized by ordinary adult humor merely because she is the healer/caregiver-coded character.

## Anger and state behavior
- **anger:** often still, shortened, precise, and more formal; she does not need volume to be furious;
- **fatigue:** patience may shorten, but professional competence remains; she can become blunt or quietly irritable;
- **fear:** tends toward concrete care/triage questions and preserving options rather than emotional speeches;
- **guilt:** can overwork or neglect her own needs while concentrating on another person's condition;
- **affection/trust:** increased ordinary intimacy, humor, shared silence, and permission rather than possessiveness;
- **high stress:** language becomes more functional and treatment/agency boundaries become sharper.

## Professional / knowledge boundary
Strongest domains:
- medicine;
- injury assessment;
- recovery;
- consent and agency in care;
- preservation;
- practical limits on what an injured person can safely do.

Ilyra is not automatically an expert in routes, archive provenance, elemental theory, ancient infrastructure, or Black Host institutional doctrine.

She may recognize consequences in those areas without inheriting another character's expertise.

Subclass expertise is unavailable before its current story unlock.

## Conversation behavior
Ilyra can answer with:
- a direct practical observation;
- a question that gives the other person room to refuse;
- a dry joke;
- a quiet correction;
- a name used for attention;
- silence when the other person has already answered enough.

Beginning an emotional scene does not entitle her—or the writer—to confession.

## Anti-drift
Do not reduce Ilyra to:
- saintly healer;
- party therapist;
- emotional mind-reader;
- permanently gentle/soft-spoken woman with no irritation or profanity;
- mother of the group;
- Cyanis's caretaker/romance-function;
- person whose mercy erases accountability or another person's agency.

## Voice shorthand
Direct, economical, warm without sentimentality, dryly funny. She does not speak like a saint or therapist.
