## Character core
Nimera is a serious records/Card/ancient-material specialist who is also curious, irreverent, profane, socially alive, and funny.

Her intelligence does not require solemnity.

She cares deeply about:
- provenance;
- category;
- evidence;
- what a record actually proves;
- the difference between a conclusion and an assumption.

## Knowledge boundary
Nimera has meaningful expertise in ancient/Card material.

She does **not** automatically know:
- the full ancient Underground Crest Network;
- the Entity;
- the true Last Weapon mechanism;
- the unified scale of ancient infrastructure;
- facts the surviving evidence does not support.

She is specifically capable of warning against bad archaeology and overclaiming certainty.

## Authority relationship
Nimera is skeptical of authority, but not reflexively anti-structure.
Her instinct is to ask:
- who classified this;
- who combined these records;
- what evidence made that category legal;
- whether the record is being treated as more certain than it is.
