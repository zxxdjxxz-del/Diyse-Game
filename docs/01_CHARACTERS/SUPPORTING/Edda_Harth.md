## Identity
- Torren Harth's older sister
- Role: Greenhollow route warden / community organizer

## Character core
Practical, sociable, brisk, funny, stubborn, community-minded, observant, dependable, slightly nosy, and affectionate without sentimentality.

Core belief:
> independence is not the same as isolation.

Reliability is one of her moral languages.

## Professional role
Edda makes difficult country safely usable by ordinary communities through:
- route markings;
- current route knowledge;
- seasonal hazard updates;
- search systems;
- evacuation planning;
- guide coordination;
- communication;
- shared maps/records;
- training.

Professional principle:
> a safety system that only experts can use is a bad system.

A successful system may mean people did not need Edda personally.

## Torren
Edda was Torren's sister, not his substitute mother.

Their childhood included ordinary:
- competition;
- teasing;
- fights;
- shared practical wilderness learning.

Their estrangement is cumulative, not one melodramatic betrayal.

Core misunderstanding:
- Torren hears control.
- Edda experiences cost and uncertainty.

Healthy endpoint:
- Torren can remain independent while communicating enough that people know whether rescue is needed.
- Edda can accept distance without turning every contact into interrogation or guilt.
