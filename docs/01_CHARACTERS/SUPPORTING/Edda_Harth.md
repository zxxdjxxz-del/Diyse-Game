## Identity
- Torren Harth's older sister
- Role: Greenhollow route warden / community organizer

## Character core
Practical, sociable, brisk, funny, stubborn, community-minded, observant, dependable, slightly nosy, and affectionate without sentimentality.

Core belief:
> independence is not the same as isolation.

Reliability is one of her moral languages.

Central flaw:
> she can mistake dependability for goodness and distance for rejection.

Deep fear:
> somebody disappears and nobody realizes soon enough to bring them home.

## Professional role
Edda makes difficult country safely usable by ordinary communities through:
- route markings;
- current route knowledge;
- seasonal hazard updates;
- missing-person searches;
- evacuation planning;
- isolated-settlement communication;
- guide coordination;
- local travel logistics;
- shared maps/records;
- training.

Professional principle:
> a safety system that only experts can use is a bad system.

Her authority is relational rather than royal or military. People follow her because her information is good and she repeatedly shows up.

A successful system may mean people did not need Edda personally.

Personal contradiction:
> she believes healthy systems should not depend on one indispensable person while emotionally still finding security in being needed and remembered.

## Adult life outside Torren
Edda's life continued fully without Torren.

She has:
- a real home;
- a local reputation;
- coworkers;
- friends;
- trainees;
- grudges;
- games;
- gossip;
- communal meals;
- dancing;
- practical cooking;
- social drinking;
- romance/sexual adulthood;

These are part of her life, not evidence she has been "waiting" for Torren to return.

## Torren
Edda was Torren's sister, not his substitute mother.

Their childhood included ordinary:
- competition;
- teasing;
- fights;
- shared practical wilderness learning.

Both learned from a practical community rather than mystical family talent.

Their professional difference matters:
- Torren excels at individual adaptation when established routes fail.
- Edda translates expert knowledge into systems ordinary people can use.

Their estrangement is cumulative, not one melodramatic betrayal.

Torren repeatedly assumes his competence makes silence harmless; Edda repeatedly makes rare contact angry enough that reaching out becomes unpleasant.

Core misunderstanding:
- Torren hears control.
- Edda experiences cost and uncertainty.

Healthy endpoint:
- Torren can remain independent while communicating enough that people know whether rescue is needed.
- Edda can accept distance without turning every contact into interrogation or guilt.

## Voice and acting
Speaks plainly, quickly, conversationally, and socially; no exaggerated rustic dialect.

Field instructions become extremely concise and unambiguous. She hates vague time estimates because ambiguity can become an emergency.

Humor is social, storytelling-driven, teasing, and willing to improve a story's delivery while keeping the important parts true.

Worry appears as tracking absences. Fear drives her to organize people. Deep hurt can make the usually social Edda unexpectedly quiet.

Mistakes get correction; patterns get fury.

Edda shorthand:
> Torren learned how to disappear safely. Edda learned why disappearing is never entirely private.

Emotional rule:
> she insults Torren most easily when she feels safest that he is still there.
