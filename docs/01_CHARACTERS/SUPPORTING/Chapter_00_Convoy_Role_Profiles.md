## `convoy_superior_officer`

### Identity boundary
- unnamed ranking convoy officer in Cyanis's immediate Chapter-0 command context;
- a Crown convoy professional, not a permanent party member;
- exact rank/title beyond the current story function is not fixed here;
- no canonical personal name is established.

### Core characterization
The officer must read as **competent, pressured, and professionally responsible**, not as an obstacle written to make Cyanis look smarter.

Current behavioral locks:
- takes Cyanis's abnormal-road observation seriously before the opening ambush;
- remains capable of making decisions under incomplete information;
- can issue orders decisively without pretending certainty;
- after the ambush, reasonably considers pursuing the withdrawing Black Host before they can regroup;
- listens when Cyanis gives concrete evidence that pursuit would expose wounded people, split too few defenders, surrender control of the wreckage/recovery wagon, and feed troops into a suspiciously favorable north cut;
- changes the order when Cyanis's alternative is operationally better;
- may still correct Cyanis on military process, phrasing, or how a subordinate presents a refusal;
- later authorizes bounded recovery/search work rather than reckless pursuit;
- handles the recovered artifact conservatively after its unexplained behavior;
- participates in the decision that Brackenwall is the next artifact-protocol/regrouping destination.

### Voice / social behavior
- concise professional speech under pressure;
- asks for actionable facts, numbers only when actually available, route state, who can move, and what can be defended;
- does not deliver villainous hierarchy speeches;
- does not become foolish merely so Cyanis can defy them;
- can show fatigue, irritation, relief, concern, and restrained dry humor when the crisis allows it;
- understands that civilians, wounded personnel, wagons, escorts, and recovered cargo are all parts of the same operational problem;
- does not possess author-level knowledge of the Black Host's underlying scout intelligence or the artifact's true nature.

### Relationship language with Cyanis
- professional superior/subordinate relationship;
- trusts Cyanis's field judgment enough to hear him out, but does not treat him as infallible;
- disagreement can be sharp without becoming personal betrayal;
- approval is more likely to appear as responsibility, delegated trust, or a changed order than praise speeches;
- correcting Cyanis on process is compatible with accepting his substantive argument.

### Relationship language with Ilyra
- recognizes Blue Warden medical authority where patient safety/treatment is concerned;
- may negotiate tactical constraints with her rather than automatically yielding every operational decision;
- expects concrete medical constraints and acts on them when credible;
- does not treat Ilyra as Cyanis's subordinate or attachment.

### Knowledge firewall
The officer does **not** know in Chapter 0:
- the true reason the Black Host targeted the convoy;
- the underlying scout intelligence about the excavation/cargo;
- Prime / Might / Last Sentinel;
- confirmed bearer status;
- ancient Last Weapon / Entity truth;
- the concealed Ruin Vanguard Pursuer's identity as Seyrik.

### Runtime / persistence rule
Use as a **profile-only scene participant**. Chapter-0 scene memory may inform later Chapter-0 appearances within the same authored sequence when explicitly passed as scene history, but no separate long-term persistent character service is required or implied.

### Runtime / persistence rule
