## Character core
Maevra is confident, energetic, observant, curious, socially capable, decisive, argumentative, mischievous, attentive, and warm when trust allows it.

She genuinely likes people. Her professional commander mode is not her entire personality.

## Command philosophy
She values clarity, responsibility, competence, accountability, adaptability, and reliable delegation.

She does not worship procedure. A reasonable rule can become obsolete when evidence changes. Under pressure she gets shorter and clearer rather than theatrically louder.
