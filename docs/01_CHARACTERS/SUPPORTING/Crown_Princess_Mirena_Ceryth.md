## Identity
- Age: **31**
- Eldest child of Queen Lysara Ceryth and the late Prince-Consort Cassian Marrowen
- Legal heir under **absolute primogeniture**
- Younger siblings: Alaric and Nalia
- Has spent approximately **18 years** as the reigning monarch's legal heir
- Explicitly **wants to become Queen**
- Experienced public servant with substantial regional/Borderlands experience

## Character core
Charismatic, socially confident, witty, observant, mischievous, politically impatient, stubborn, emotionally perceptive, mildly vain, and competitive.

Governing question:
> **Is power answerable?**

She is not an unwilling-heir archetype. Her desire for the Crown does not mean unquestioning defense of existing royal practice.

Central contradiction:
> she believes institutions should survive scrutiny while she herself was trained from adolescence in controlled presentation.

Deep political fear:
> becoming a beloved symbol who changes nothing.

Private fear:
> people loving the Crown more than Mirena.

## Public and private self
Mirena's public self is genuine, but edited. It is not a fake mask.

- **Public Mirena:** cleaner, slower, more ceremonial, highly aware that words carry institutional force.
- **Private Mirena:** quicker, funnier, more profane, more physically relaxed, and much more willing to be silly.

She has spent years being watched carefully, so presentation is both pleasure and political language rather than simple vanity.

## Ordinary-life texture
Mirena genuinely likes:
- people;
- conversation;
- disagreement;
- clothes and presentation;
- gossip;
- riding;
- dancing;
- food;
- competition and winning.

She remembers people and small details. She enjoys being beautiful without needing beauty to become a moral flaw or a disguise for insecurity.

She can waste time, tease, be petty, laugh, become competitive over something stupid, and occupy furniture badly in private. Royal status does not erase mundane adulthood.

## Upbringing and family logic
Before age 13, Mirena had an actual childhood rather than functioning as a miniature stateswoman.

Lysara's accession made succession immediate and changed how adults watched her. A core lesson became:
> feel whatever you feel; know where it is safe to show it.

- **Lysara** — mother, model of the job, and eventual adult political equal. Their disagreements concern pace, method, risk, consequence, transparency, and institutional memory rather than a simple young-reformer/old-conservative split.
- **Cassian** — strongly influenced Mirena's direct regional/stewardship instincts. She can understand why he made the choice that killed him and remain angry that he did not come home.
- **Alaric** — genuine sibling friendship and political equality in different lanes; never a succession rival manufactured by outsiders.
- **Nalia** — affection plus hard debate over immediate human need, precedent, durable authority, and how much of oneself care is allowed to consume.

Her regional experience is legitimate preparation and public service, not a rebellious princess tourism fantasy. She knows royal visits can be staged and learns to compare reports, revisit places, and ask unexpected questions.

## Political philosophy
Fundamental principle:
> power is legitimate only while it remains answerable.

She favors:
- practical transparency with legitimate secrecy categories;
- bad news traveling upward;
- competence over pedigree;
- regional feedback;
- procurement/reporting reform;
- institutions that do not depend on direct royal access.

She dislikes being handled or having bad news withheld.

Flaws:
- can mistake caution for fear;
- can mistake decisiveness for progress;
- can underestimate the coercive force of charisma;
- may assume that once truth is visible, the right people will naturally act.

Growth direction:
> build institutions capable of acting on truth rather than relying on personal presence or charisma.

## Voice and acting
Mirena speaks like an educated adult who has been listened to carefully for eighteen years: precise and contemporary, with contractions rather than faux-archaic court speech.

She asks excellent questions and uses **I** for decisions she personally made instead of hiding behind "the Crown."

- anger can become formal and surgical;
- personal fury is less polished;
- grief is strongest in simple language;
- private humor can be quick, profane, competitive, and silly.

Shorthand:
> Mirena speaks precisely because she knows words have consequences; she jokes because she occasionally wants them not to.
