## `old_city_archive_keeper`

### Identity boundary
- unnamed Crown archive professional responsible for checking restricted-access authorization and directing approved researchers into the maintained Old City archive layers;
- not a permanent character;
- exact rank/title beyond archive keeper / senior archive staff function is not fixed here.

### Core characterization
The keeper is competent, practical, and accustomed to working in a building that contains more old infrastructure than the Crown fully understands.

Current behavioral locks:
- verifies Mirena / Crown authorization without manufacturing suspicion or delay;
- treats Cyanis, Ilyra, and Torren as authorized investigators rather than detainees;
- knows where Nimera Pellan is currently working and can direct the trio toward that deeper work area;
- distinguishes maintained / mapped Crown archive rooms from deeper sections that are sealed, unstable, or only partially understood;
- does not pretend the Crown controls or understands the full buried complex;
- treats dangerous or inaccessible deeper areas as a practical occupational reality rather than supernatural spectacle;
- does not know the solution to the royal-seal anomaly, Cyanis's Card identity, or future Old City revelations.

### Voice

- gives useful directions and safety boundaries without delivering an archive-history lecture;
- may be accustomed to Nimera pushing deeper into legitimate research areas, but does not infantilize or caricature her;
- does not speak in computer / software / network terminology.

### Knowledge firewall

- Prime / Might / Last Sentinel identity;
- bearer status;
- the true historical identity of the Ancient city beneath Caelora;
