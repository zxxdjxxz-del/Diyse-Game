## `brackenwall_custody_officer`

### Identity boundary
- unnamed Yahtrean/Crown professional responsible for formal intake/custody handling of recovered anomalous material at Brackenwall;
- not a permanent character;
- exact rank/title beyond custody/intake function is not fixed here.

### Core characterization
The officer is competent, procedural, and reasonably cautious.

Current behavioral locks:
- treats the sealed recovered artifact as unusual but initially attempts to apply ordinary artifact-custody procedure;
- listens to Cyanis Dovaren and Ilyra Amarin's eyewitness account without pretending that eyewitness uncertainty is a failure;
- does not know the artifact's true identity;
- attempts a normal physical transfer/separation because current procedure reasonably calls for custody intake;
- stops immediately when the transfer produces another abnormal response rather than repeatedly provoking the phenomenon;
- escalates the matter to Maevra Solmar / higher command once normal procedure is no longer appropriate;
- accepts that standard custody is not safe or evidentially justified under the new behavior.

### Voice
- concise, workmanlike, not pompous;
- asks what happened, what changed, what was directly observed, and what has already been tried;
- distinguishes procedure from certainty;
- may be dry or tired, but is not comic bureaucracy.

### Knowledge firewall
The officer does not know:
- Prime / Might / Last Sentinel identity;
- bearer status;
- ancient Last Weapon / Entity truth;
- why the artifact reacts near Cyanis;
- that later ancient systems may respond to it.

Profile-only. No long-term persistent Person Agent or cross-chapter memory is implied.
