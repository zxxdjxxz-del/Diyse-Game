Exact equipment/service mechanics live in their owning system domains; exact visual authority lives in `14_ART_AND_VISUALS`.

## Identity
- Full name: **Kessara Durnan**
- Age: **22**
- Height: approximately **5'0"**
- Origin/craft tradition: **Emberforge**
- Role: runesmith, field engineer, structural/mechanical problem-solver
- Party status: major recurring **nonplayable** supporting character

The retired surname-derived workshop label and retired named family/workshop branch remain superseded; restoring the surname does **not** restore those old workshop/family structures.

## Character core
Kessara is brilliant, exuberant, fiery, physically expressive, mischievous, affectionate, impatient, competitive, profane, sexually humorous, practical, emotionally transparent, and genuinely silly.

She is a short **adult woman**. Height must never be used to infantilize her dialogue, sexuality, authority, body language, or staging.

She is one of Diyse's most openly comedic personalities, but she is not comic relief.

## Firecracker identity
Kessara has high emotional ignition.

She:
- laughs hard;
- argues hard;
- gets excited quickly;
- gets angry quickly;
- usually lets minor irritation burn off quickly;
- changes the conversational temperature of a room even when she is not trying to dominate it.

Her temper is not cruelty.

She is usually forgiving of:
- honest mistakes;
- ignorance honestly admitted;
- failed experiments;
- sincere "I don't know" answers.

She reacts much more strongly to:
- dishonesty;
- negligence;
- unsafe workmanship;
- blame-shifting;
- arrogant incompetence;
- convenient refusal to investigate warning signs.

## Core emotional contradiction
Kessara instinctively believes:
> if I can understand how a system works, I can improve it.

Machines reward this belief. People, institutions, grief, consent, and moral responsibility do not always behave like machines.

Her first expression of care is often to:
- fix;
- reinforce;
- modify;
- solve;
- provide options.

Her growth includes learning that some problems require:
- patience;
- permission;
- presence;
- acceptance;
- leaving another person's chosen state alone.

## Vulnerability and praise
Kessara believes she hides her feelings better than she does. Excitement, irritation, embarrassment, fascination, worry, jealousy, and delight are usually readable in her face and body.

When ashamed or emotionally cornered:
1. she may joke harder first;
2. the situation becomes genuinely serious;
3. the jokes stop;
4. her language becomes much simpler.

Casual praise about appearance or competence is easy for her to take. Specific praise from someone whose judgment she deeply respects—especially praise about responsible design, maintainability, or good engineering judgment—can genuinely embarrass her.

Deep fear:
> being brilliant enough to build things she is not yet wise enough to judge.

## Ordinary-life texture
Kessara likes people, especially people who are:
- curious;
- capable;
- funny;
- willing to admit what they do not know;
- willing to get their hands dirty.

She loves the process of making things:
- prototypes;
- repair;
- fabrication;
- the exact moment a mechanism starts working;
- useful information produced by a failed prototype.

Other compatible ordinary-life traits:
- likes dancing and is physically confident off duty;
- likes strong, committed flavors;
- can forget food while working until hunger turns into irritation;
- appreciates jewelry, ornament, engraving, and beautiful craftsmanship;
- believes decoration is compatible with excellent construction but never excuses bad construction;
- is comfortable in her adult body and knows she is attractive without every scene becoming sexual;
- can flirt recreationally without that becoming automatic romantic or sexual intent.

## Humor, profanity, and silliness
Kessara's humor is central ordinary-life texture.

She enjoys:
- dirty jokes;
- sexual innuendo;
- double entendres;
- shameless crude observations;
- technical filth;
- playful insults;
- taking an obvious setup someone accidentally gives her;
- fake technical terms;
- ridiculous names for objects;
- committed bits;
- dramatic arguments with machinery;
- bad noises;
- playful exaggeration;
- laughing at her own joke when it genuinely gets her.

Her profanity is frequent and natural in workshop/soldierly contexts. Casual Kessara may swear as punctuation. Emergency Kessara often becomes cleaner, stiller, and more precise.

Dirty humor must not consume every conversation. It stays funny because it is responsive to context, relationship-specific, and mixed with technical enthusiasm, ordinary observations, arguments, care, and silence.

She notices boundaries. Once she understands that somebody is genuinely uncomfortable with a type of joke, she normally stops rather than arguing that they should tolerate it.

Her jokes do not grant anyone permission to treat her disrespectfully.

## Expertise
Strongest domains:
- runesmithing;
- structural/mechanical systems;
- materials;
- fabrication;
- repair;
- maintenance logic;
- field engineering;
- practical failure analysis.

Her authority is earned through mundane craft work, not effortless prodigy omniscience. Compatible apprenticeship texture includes:
- cleaning tools;
- sorting parts;
- measuring;
- filing;
- maintenance;
- copying plans;
- carrying components;
- checking stress points;
- watching experienced craftspeople;
- supervised real work before independent responsibility.

Kessara respects theory and formal knowledge. She is workshop-first, not anti-academic.

Around unfamiliar machinery she can investigate by:
- tracing interfaces and loads;
- inferring maintenance logic;
- identifying later repairs;
- testing assumptions;
- distinguishing active from passive structure;
- comparing what should happen with what actually happened.

She does **not** possess universal ancient knowledge.

Not automatically expert in:
- politics;
- medicine;
- military strategy;
- ancient history;
- Card theory;
- every form of magic;
- every Ruin phenomenon.

## Workspace behavior
Her workspaces can be an **organized mess**, not random chaos. Component piles, partially disassembled systems, notes, tools, and in-progress work may encode information she understands.

"Helpful" cleaning can destroy that organization.

If a workstation does not fit the worker, Kessara's instinct is to fix the workstation rather than treat the worker's body as the defect.

## Maker accountability
Her family/craft responsibility arc centers on the question:
> what does a maker owe the world after the work leaves their hands?

Current authority preserves the ethical structure while leaving retired workshop/family-branch structures superseded.

The arc rejects both extremes:
- "makers are responsible for anything anyone ever does with a tool";
- "responsibility ends the instant technically correct work leaves the shop."

Compatible principles:
- warnings matter;
- provenance matters;
- maintainability matters;
- repairability matters;
- hidden risk matters;
- intermediaries do not automatically erase responsibility;
- deliberately refusing to investigate accumulating warning signs can become chosen incuriosity;
- removing endorsement from harmful work must not erase evidence of who made it;
- changed practice matters more than declaring oneself purified.

Kessara's mature engineering should leave other people more capable after the expert leaves: document, teach, delegate, simplify where appropriate, and allow other competent craftspeople to alter or improve the work.

## Relationship languages
Kessara must not use one generic joke/personality setting with everyone.

- **Cyanis** — competitive comedic equal and trusted coordinator. She likes that he can admit ignorance, return fire quickly, and defer to real specialist knowledge; she can bluntly tell him somebody else can take a risk he reflexively volunteers for.
- **Ilyra** — initially a tempting composure-breaking target, later a quietly safe person. Kessara tries to make her laugh; Ilyra can recognize when Kessara is joking too hard.
- **Torren** — practical field respect plus relentless harassment. His refusal to patronize her and his maintenance/terrain judgment win trust; his veteran immunity to dirty jokes can irritate and delight her.
- **Maevra** — competent authority Kessara genuinely respects, which makes cracking Maevra's composure a competitive sport. Institutional backing for a legitimate Kessara safety judgment matters deeply.
- **Nimera** — dangerous intellectual/comedic co-conspirator. Nimera branches conceptually while Kessara builds physically; their profanity and curiosity can escalate each other without making them duplicates.
- **Vaelira** — friendly technical rival. Formal/theoretical precision meets workshop/field maintainability; neither is anti-intellectual and neither wins every disagreement.
- **Seyrik** — varied dirty banter develops alongside real consent/repair respect. Kessara's jokes do not make him a permanent straight man; he learns her language and eventually returns it. Their relationship is not automatically romantic.
- **Talia** — initial exhaustion can become genuine affection; Talia can return filthy deadpan humor and is not required to match Kessara's energy.
- **Edda** — natural infrastructure friendship and dangerous profanity; both care whether systems remain usable by ordinary people after the specialist leaves.
- **Mirena/Lysara/Alaric/Nalia** — relationship-specific adult humor, specialist respect, and disagreement around accountability, structure, maintainability, and human consequences; royal status does not convert Kessara into a court-jester role.

## Kessara / Seyrik humor boundary
A Ruin-themed dirty joke may function as an early signature beat in their social language, but it is **not** a catchphrase and should not be repeated mechanically.

Progression may move from:
- early literal/dry Seyrik responses;
- to Seyrik recognizing setups;
- to pre-emption/refusal;
- to Seyrik deliberately returning the language and occasionally catching Kessara flat-footed.

The point is learned shared language and belonging, not "Seyrik learned sex jokes."

Serious relationship content also includes repair and consent: Kessara asks before changing another person's equipment and must not assume restoration means erasing visible history or deciding what another person's restored state should mean.

## Voice and physical acting
Kessara's dialogue is fast, conversational, profane, concrete, and workshop-shaped.

- excited Kessara talks faster and may begin physically solving a problem before the explanation is finished;
- angry Kessara can become loud and colorful;
- emergency Kessara becomes direct, still, and extremely clear;
- hurt Kessara may initially joke more, then less;
- an unadorned simple sentence from Kessara can carry unusual emotional weight;
- technical explanation should usually begin with function, danger, or consequence—why the information matters now;
- she knows when she is boring people often enough to joke about it, though sometimes she simply disagrees that the topic is boring.

She speaks with her whole body: leaning, pointing, squatting beside machinery, sitting on workbenches, bracing against something, using tools while talking, and reacting visibly before a punch line lands.

Her laugh can range from a quick grin to full-body laughter that temporarily prevents speech.

## Presence rule
Whenever a rewrite considers adding Kessara, ask:
> **Why is Kessara here?**

A good answer comes from:
- work;
- expertise;
- relationship;
- home-base life;
- her own arc.

"Because Kessara is funny" is not enough.

She changes conversational energy but does not receive mandatory participation in every group scene. Silence, absence, side conversations, and other characters' uncontested specialist space remain necessary.

## Anti-drift
Do not reduce Kessara to:
- generic sarcastic mechanic;
- grumpy woman who loves tools;
- walking exposition;
- Maevra with a wrench;
- Nimera duplicate;
- purely sexual joke delivery device;
- comic relief who follows every mission;
- answer to every ancient machine;
- unofficial seventh permanent party member;
- child-coded character because of height/youth/exuberance.

Do not domesticate or remove her dirty humor merely to make serious material seem more respectable. Comedy is part of her humanity.
