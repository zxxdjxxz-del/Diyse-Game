## 1. Pair Legacy rule

Cyanis and Vaelira receive **two physical Legacy-tier equipment items**, one belonging to each native Base tradition.

Synthesis does not create these items. It grants the reciprocal partner the legal Legacy-tier permission established by the universal Synthesis architecture.

The pair mapping is:

| Native tradition | Legacy item | Equipment family | Reciprocal Synthesis wearer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crest Knight | **Stillpoint Aegis** | Shield | **Vaelira** after purchasing Unified Spectrum / Synthesis |
| Green Arcanist | **Living Prism** | Focus | **Cyanis** after purchasing Unified Crest / Synthesis |

The native owner remains naturally compatible with the item's equipment family under the native tradition. The reciprocal partner's cross-tradition Legacy legality requires that character's purchased Synthesis node.

Legacy permission does not bypass normal Weapon / Secondary geometry. A Shield or Focus still occupies Secondary normally and cannot coexist with a two-slot configuration that already consumes Secondary.

No Ability or Ultimate gains a weapon or Secondary-equipment requirement from either Legacy item.

## 2. Crest Knight Legacy — Stillpoint Aegis

**Stillpoint Aegis** is the Crest Knight Legacy-tier Shield.

### Identity

The name carries the Cyanis/Vaelira resolution thesis without merely repeating the scene title: the shield represents the point that must hold so everything around it is free to adapt.

It is a defensive Crest Knight masterwork, not a magical requirement for Crest Knight techniques and not an alternate class mechanic.

### Visual direction

- compact high-grade Crest Knight shield rather than an oversized tower shield;
- ivory / pale stone-ceramic structure with dark gunmetal reinforcement;
- restrained antique-gold Crest channels;
- a ruby-centered fixed-point motif consistent with Might/Crest presentation;
- the central geometry should read as deliberately stable and exact rather than ornate for ornament's sake.

When Vaelira equips it, her normal Axiomblade / Elements effects remain her own. The Shield does not recolor all of her magic or turn her into a Crest Knight visually.

### Mechanical-stat boundary

This pass locks **item identity, family, name, eligibility mapping, and acquisition presentation only**.

Exact Defense / Spirit / other numerical stats and any final item passive remain for the dedicated production item/equipment audit. Do not invent a new gauge, free action, automatic Crest Attunement, or Ability-gating effect merely to make the Legacy feel special.

## 3. Green Arcanist Legacy — Living Prism

**Living Prism** is the Green Arcanist Legacy-tier Focus.

### Identity

The Focus represents controlled variation: a stable instrument that can refract changing elemental expression without demanding that every condition remain the same.

It is a Green Arcanist masterwork and does not replace Prism Cycle, Imprints, Elemental Fields, Formal Equivalence, or Unified Spectrum.

### Visual direction

- refined handheld / floating Focus scale consistent with the existing Focus equipment family;
- emerald-dominant crystalline core with ivory and antique-gold structure;
- subtle multi-element refraction across facets while the central core remains visually coherent;
- no new seventh-element color, no Black Host visual language, and no Prime-like summon presentation.

When Cyanis equips it, Crest effects remain Crest effects. The Focus does not convert Crest Knight or Crest Arcanist abilities into Green Arcanist spells.

### Mechanical-stat boundary

Exact Magic / Spirit / other numerical stats and any final item passive remain for the production item/equipment audit.

Do not give Living Prism an automatic Imprint, automatic Composite Reaction, extra Field, second Trait, or weapon-gated Ability interaction.

## 4. Acquisition presentation — Cresthaven secured equipment release

The two items are acquired through a **deterministic Cresthaven equipment pickup during the Chapter 12 pre-point-of-no-return return / preparation window**.

Production rules:
- reuse an existing Cresthaven armory / secured-equipment interior or equivalent established hub staging;
- no new dungeon, Hunt, boss, crafting recipe, currency, material grind, or random drop;
- the items are physical equipment that already exists in the world; Synthesis does not conjure them;
- exact historical provenance text remains for the later item/lore-description pass rather than inventing unsupported history here;
- the pair-resolution scene **What Holds, What Changes** still gives no item reward and is not rewritten into an equipment handoff.

### Player-facing sequence

1. The late-Chapter-11 pair resolution has already occurred as mandatory continuity.
2. During the Chapter 12 Cresthaven preparation window, **Stillpoint Aegis** and **Living Prism** become available as deterministic secured-equipment pickups.
3. The player may physically obtain the items even if one or both characters have not yet purchased Synthesis.
4. If reciprocal Legacy eligibility is incomplete, the equipment UI clearly shows the relevant Synthesis / Legacy requirement rather than silently hiding or deleting the item.
5. Once the character purchases Synthesis and otherwise satisfies equipment legality, the reciprocal Legacy becomes equippable immediately.

This keeps **acquisition** and **eligibility** separate, exactly as required by the universal Synthesis model.

## 5. Why Secondary-slot Legacies are preferred for this pair

Using a Shield and Focus instead of forcing two new capstone weapons preserves the pair's open-equipment philosophy.

- Cyanis can continue choosing Sword, dual Sword, or Arcane Staff expressions while the Focus offers a Legacy Secondary option where slot geometry allows.
- Vaelira can continue choosing Sword or Arcane Staff expressions while the Shield offers a Legacy defensive Secondary option where slot geometry allows.
- Neither Legacy determines whether an Ability can be used.
- The pair's completion reward expands late-game build choice instead of declaring one weapon family the mandatory "correct" endgame loadout.

This is especially important after the global rule that **Abilities and Ultimates never require a particular weapon**.
