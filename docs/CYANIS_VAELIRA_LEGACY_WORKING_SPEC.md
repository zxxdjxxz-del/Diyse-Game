## 1. Pair Legacy rule

Cyanis and Vaelira have two physical Legacy-tier equipment items, one belonging to each native Base tradition.

| Native tradition | Legacy item | Equipment family | Reciprocal Synthesis wearer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crest Knight | **Stillpoint Aegis** | Shield | **Vaelira** after Unified Spectrum / Synthesis |
| Green Arcanist | **Living Prism** | Focus | **Cyanis** after Unified Crest / Synthesis |

The native owner remains naturally compatible with the item's equipment family. Reciprocal legality requires the reciprocal character's purchased Synthesis.

A Shield or Focus occupies Secondary normally. Legacy permission never bypasses slot geometry and never creates an Ability/Ultimate equipment requirement.

## 2. Stillpoint Aegis — Crest Knight Legacy Shield

Identity: the fixed point that must hold so surrounding structure may adapt.

Visual direction:
- compact high-grade Crest Knight shield;
- ivory / pale stone-ceramic with dark gunmetal reinforcement;
- restrained antique-gold Crest channels;
- ruby-centered fixed-point motif;
- stable exact geometry rather than ornamental excess.

When Vaelira equips it, her Axiomblade/Elements expression remains her own.

Exact final Defense/Spirit/stat values and any passive remain for the production item/equipment audit. Do not add automatic Crest Attunement, free actions, gauges, or Ability gating.

## 4. Audit103 Character-Quest Component mapping — locked

- Cyanis's Character Quest **The Weight of the Crest** grants the unique Legacy Component that completes **Stillpoint Aegis**.
- Vaelira's Character Quest **The Sky No One Chose** grants the unique Legacy Component that completes **Living Prism**.

The Legacy Component is not the final equipment item. The physical masterwork already exists in secured Cresthaven inventory; the Character Quest provides the unique missing keyed/core component needed to complete/restore it for use.

This is a fixed authored one-to-one completion step, not crafting.

---

## 5. Chapter-12 Cresthaven secured release — final flow

During the Chapter 12 pre-point-of-no-return Cresthaven preparation window:

For each item independently:
1. the native owner's Character Quest must be complete and its Legacy Component earned;
2. the matching secured Legacy becomes ready for completion/release;
3. the component is integrated/registered in a short authored interaction;
4. the component leaves Key Items and the completed Legacy enters normal inventory;
5. the native owner may equip it under normal family/slot legality;
6. the reciprocal partner may equip it only after purchasing the relevant Synthesis and satisfying normal legality.

If the Character Quest is incomplete, the item remains secured/incomplete and is not placed in inventory as a dead shell. Because Character Quests remain available until the point of no return, the player may complete the quest and return to claim the item.

**What Holds, What Changes** remains item-reward-free.

No dungeon, Hunt, boss, recipe, material grind, currency fee, random drop, or general crafting system is added.

## 6. Build-choice boundary

Using a Shield and Focus preserves open equipment choice:
- Cyanis may continue Sword/dual-Sword/Arcane-Staff expressions while Living Prism provides a Secondary option where legal;
- Vaelira may continue Sword/Arcane-Staff expressions while Stillpoint Aegis provides a defensive Secondary option where legal.

Neither Legacy determines whether an Ability can be used.
