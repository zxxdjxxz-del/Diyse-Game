## Current status

**Guard remains a valid defensive state.**

Guard is **not** a harmful status.

The permanent player command list contains **Defend**.

Current global authority does not establish a new universal numeric Guard formula in this migration file; individual Guard/Defend implementations must use their already-authored current rules.

Do not invent a replacement percentage or hidden posture meter merely to make this file look complete.

## Action-order interaction

Defend is selected when the acting character's normal Speed-ordered turn arrives.

When Defend resolves:
- the authored defensive state begins at that point in the round;
- it does not retroactively reduce or alter attacks that already resolved earlier in the round;
- it has no separate universal priority band ahead of faster ordinary actions;
- its exact strength and duration remain governed by the current authored Defend/Guard rule rather than by a new global percentage invented here.

Therefore a faster enemy may act before a slower character has the opportunity to choose and establish Defend.

Any other established Guard state created by an authored Ability, enemy action, equipment effect, or encounter mechanic resolves according to that action/state's current rule.

## Prepared defensive/reaction states

Prepared reactions such as explicitly authored intercepts/counters are established when their preparing action resolves on that actor's turn.

They may respond to later eligible actions according to their authored trigger/duration, but they do not retroactively apply to actions that resolved before the Prepared state was established.

## Firewall

Do not substitute or reintroduce:
- Brace;
- Barrier;
- global Break/Stagger meter;
- hidden stability/posture gauges.

## Interception

Interception is not a free universal reaction system.

Where an encounter/effect supports interception or redirection, it must come from an explicitly established authored state/effect rather than being assumed for every defender.
