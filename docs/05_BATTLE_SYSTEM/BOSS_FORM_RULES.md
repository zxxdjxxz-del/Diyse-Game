# Diyse — Boss Form Route

**Status:** ROUTING SURFACE  
**Authority:** `BATTLE_SYSTEM_MASTER.md` §§20 and 24

Bosses use the same TURN / EXECUTION language as ordinary combatants unless an encounter explicitly authors an exception.

Current global boundaries:

- no blanket boss immunity to Delay or Interrupt;
- stronger/signature actions may be authored Delay-only or Uninterruptible;
- standard boss Delay displacement defaults to 50% of ordinary standard Delay unless a phase is explicitly Normal/Resistant/Immune;
- same-bar phase changes do **not** restore spent Prime identities;
- genuine fresh-HP bodies/forms do **not** restore spent Prime identities or cancel/shorten an active post-Prime lockout.

Encounter-specific fresh-body HP/state behavior remains owned by the encounter that uses it. Do not create a second global battle flow here.
