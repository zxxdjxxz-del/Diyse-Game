## Standard elements

Exactly four:
1. Fire
2. Ice
3. Lightning
4. Earth

No Water or Wind exists as a current standard combat element.

## Linked status associations

Where explicitly authored:
- Fire → Burn
- Ice → Freeze
- Lightning → Stun
- Earth → Staggered

An elemental attack does **not** automatically inflict its linked status.

Every harmful-status rider must be explicitly authored.

## Ruin

Ruin is:
- a special magical affinity/school;
- not a fifth standard element.

Its thematic harmful-status association is Bleed, but Bleed is not exclusive to Ruin.

## Elemental direct-damage multipliers

Current named-combat affinity levels:
- Weak — **125%**
- Neutral — **100%**
- Resistant — **80%**
- Strongly Resistant — **60%**
- Immune — **0%**

No generic absorption, reflection, or elemental healing is implied.

## Status application affinity modifier

For linked status application under the compatible Audit115 resolver:
- Weak — **+10 percentage points**
- Neutral — **0**
- Resist — **−10 percentage points**
- Immune — linked application blocked

This is distinct from elemental direct-damage multiplication.

## Chapter 4 firewall

Chapter 4 research/regulation uses exactly:
- Fire
- Ice
- Lightning
- Earth

Wind and Water are removed and their old functions are **not reassigned**.

The Seventh Reaction is emergent four-element behavior, not:
- a seventh element;
- a fifth/sixth hidden channel;
- a reusable global player reaction subsystem.
