# Diyse — Defend / Ward Route

**Status:** ROUTING SURFACE  
**Authority:** `BATTLE_SYSTEM_MASTER.md` §§3 and 11

Current defensive baseline:

- **Defend:** Immediate Execution / Fast Return / 0 MP;
- Defend lasts until the user's next TURN begins;
- Defend reduces incoming direct physical and magical damage by **40%**;
- **Ward:** ×0.75 incoming direct physical/magical damage for 3 affected TURNs;
- Defend and Ward stack multiplicatively.

Neither reduces indirect fixed/percentage damage unless an owning effect explicitly says otherwise.
