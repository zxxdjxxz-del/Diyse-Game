# Diyse — Legacy Component and Secured-Release Reconciliation — 2026-08-22

**Status:** APPROVED RECONCILIATION — READY FOR AUDIT104 PROMOTION  
**Parent authority:** v1.88 / Audit103 plus the approved post-Audit103 reciprocal class/Synthesis/Legacy package.  
**Purpose:** reconcile Audit103's six Character-Quest Legacy Component rewards with the newly approved six named Legacy equipment items and the shared Chapter-12 Cresthaven secured-equipment release.

The standing approval rule applies: material advanced under the current “Let's continue” sequence is approved unless later explicitly revised.

---

## 1. Core reconciliation rule

Audit103's **Legacy Component** rewards are retained exactly as meaningful Character-Quest rewards.

The later six named Legacy items are also retained.

The relationship is now:

**Character Quest → recover/earn the character's unique Legacy Component → Chapter-12 Cresthaven secured release → component is integrated into the corresponding existing Legacy masterwork → final Legacy equipment becomes claimable/equippable under normal legality.**

This is a fixed authored one-to-one completion step, **not a crafting system**.

The physical Legacy masterwork already exists in the secured Cresthaven inventory. The Character Quest provides the unique missing keyed/core component required to restore/complete that specific item for use. Synthesis neither creates the item nor creates the component.

## 3. Why this preserves both authorities

Audit103 remains meaningful because:
- every Character Quest still has a unique mechanical reward;
- the reward is necessary for that character's native Legacy item to become usable;
- completing a Character Quest therefore materially advances late-game build access;
- Character Quests remain optional and do not gate mandatory story completion.

The later Legacy package remains meaningful because:
- all six final Legacy names, equipment families, slot geometry, visual identities, and reciprocal Synthesis mappings remain unchanged;
- all six physical masterworks still exist independently in the world;
- the shared Cresthaven release remains the production-efficient final item handoff point;
- Synthesis still grants **reciprocal wearer eligibility**, not item creation.

---

## 4. Chapter-12 Cresthaven secured-equipment release — final flow

During the **Chapter 12 pre-point-of-no-return Cresthaven preparation window**, the secured-equipment area contains the six Legacy masterworks.

For each Legacy independently:

1. The corresponding Character Quest must have been completed, awarding its Legacy Component.
2. Returning to the secured-equipment release point presents the matching Legacy as **ready for completion/release**.
3. The component is physically integrated into / registered with that Legacy masterwork in a short authored interaction.
4. The component is removed from Key Items and the completed named Legacy equipment item is added to inventory.
5. The native owner may equip it immediately if normal slot/equipment legality permits.
6. The reciprocal partner may equip it only after purchasing that character's approved Synthesis node and satisfying normal equipment legality.

If the Character Quest is incomplete:
- the Legacy remains visibly/explicitly **secured and incomplete** rather than disappearing from world logic;
- it is not added to normal inventory as a dead unusable shell;
- the UI may indicate that the corresponding Legacy Component has not yet been recovered, but should not spoil quest narrative details.

Because Audit103 keeps Character Quests available until the Chapter-12 point of no return, the player may leave Cresthaven, complete an unfinished Character Quest, return, and claim that Legacy before committing to the finale.

---

## 5. Native versus reciprocal legality

### Native owner
The native owner does **not** need Synthesis to equip their own native Legacy once:
- the Character Quest Component has been earned;
- the item has been completed/released at Cresthaven;
- ordinary equipment-family and slot legality are satisfied.

### Reciprocal partner
The reciprocal partner needs:
- the final physical Legacy item to have been completed/released through the native owner's Character Quest Component;
- that reciprocal partner's own purchased **Synthesis** node;
- ordinary equipment-family and slot legality.

Reciprocal mapping remains:
- Vaelira may use **Stillpoint Aegis** after **Unified Spectrum**.
- Cyanis may use **Living Prism** after **Unified Crest**.
- Seyrik may use **Mercy's Boundary** after **Tempered Ruin**.
- Ilyra may use **Purposebound** after **Mercy Unbroken**.
- Nimera may use **Known Ground** after **Living Proof**.
- Torren may use **Decisive Record** after **Measured Passage**.

Synthesis never substitutes for the missing Character Quest Component.

## 6. Optional-content consequence — approved

Because Character Quests are optional, Legacy equipment is also **optional endgame equipment**.

This is intentional.

No Legacy item may be required for:
- mandatory Chapter-12 story completion;
- Story Prime use;
- Final Severance;
- required lore comprehension;
- mandatory boss victory assumptions;
- accessing the ending.

Legacy items are high-end optional build rewards for players who complete Character Quests and the relevant Synthesis progression.

---

## 7. No crafting-system rule

The fixed Legacy completion interaction must not expand into a general crafting system.

Specifically:
- no recipes;
- no material lists;
- no upgrade tiers;
- no random outcomes;
- no currency fee;
- no forge minigame;
- no repeatable component economy;
- no interchangeable Legacy parts.

Each Character Quest grants exactly one authored unique component for exactly one authored Legacy masterwork.

The Cresthaven interaction is better understood as **restoration/completion/release of secured equipment**, not crafting.

---

## 8. Presentation boundaries

- Reuse the same Cresthaven secured-equipment / armory staging for all six items.
- Keep the interaction short; the emotional payoff belongs to the Character Quest and the late-Chapter-11 resolution beats, not to a long inventory ceremony.
- Exact component names may remain unstated/generic until the item-description pass unless already locked in an individual Character Quest script.
- Do not add six new bespoke NPCs or six unique cinematics.
- One reusable interaction pattern with item-specific VFX/icon text is sufficient.

The three mandatory late-Chapter-11 Synthesis-resolution scenes remain **item-reward-free**.

## 9. Final reconciled acquisition chain

For a native Legacy:

**Character Quest completion**  
→ **Legacy Component earned**  
→ **Chapter-12 Cresthaven secured Legacy completed/released**  
→ **native owner may equip under normal legality**.

For reciprocal use:

**same completed physical Legacy**  
+ **reciprocal character Synthesis purchased**  
→ **reciprocal partner may equip under normal legality**.

This closes the only direct conflict between Audit103 Character-Quest rewards and the post-Audit103 Legacy acquisition presentation.
