**Status:** APPROVED / CURRENTLY RECONCILED

**Current timing overlay:** Audit107 / Audit109 / Audit113

The standing approval rule applies: material advanced under the current “Let's continue” sequence is approved unless later explicitly revised.

---

## 1. Core reconciliation rule

Audit103's **Legacy Component** rewards are retained exactly as meaningful Character-Quest rewards.

The six named Legacy items are also retained.

Current relationship:

**Character Quest → recover/earn the character's unique Legacy Component → Cresthaven secured Legacy completion/release while world return remains available → final Legacy equipment becomes claimable/equippable under normal legality.**

This is a fixed authored one-to-one completion step, **not a crafting system**.

The physical Legacy masterwork already exists in the secured Cresthaven inventory. The Character Quest provides the unique missing keyed/core component required to restore/complete that specific item for use. Synthesis neither creates the item nor creates the component.

## 4. Current secured-equipment release timing — Chapter 13 / pre-Last-Shelter threshold

The older phrase **“Chapter-12 Cresthaven secured-equipment release”** is stale pre-insertion numbering and is superseded.

Current rule:

The secured-equipment area at **Cresthaven** remains available through the **Chapter-13 pre-Last-Shelter returnable period**.

For each Legacy independently:

1. The corresponding Character Quest must have been completed, awarding its Legacy Component.
2. Returning to the secured-equipment release point presents the matching Legacy as ready for completion/release.
3. The component is physically integrated into / registered with that Legacy masterwork in a short authored interaction.
4. The component is removed from Key Items and the completed named Legacy equipment item is added to inventory.
5. The native owner may equip it immediately if normal slot/equipment legality permits.
6. The reciprocal partner may equip it only after purchasing that character's approved Synthesis node and satisfying normal equipment legality.

If the Character Quest is incomplete:
- the Legacy remains visibly/explicitly secured and incomplete;
- it is not added to normal inventory as a dead unusable shell;
- the UI may indicate that the matching Legacy Component has not yet been recovered without spoiling quest narrative details.

### Availability / point-of-no-return rule

Character Quests and eligible Legacy claims remain available while the player can still return to the world.

Under Audit109:
- entering **Chapter 13** does **not** itself close this access;
- the player may advance through the early final domain and still return before the true irreversible threshold;
- **Last Shelter → Reactor Galleries** is the true point of no return.

Therefore the final cutoff for unfinished eligible Character Quests / secured Legacy completion is the explicit Last-Shelter commitment, not Chapter-13 launch.

Exact implementation of the return route remains a production detail, but it must preserve this access rule.

## 5. Native versus reciprocal legality

### Native owner
The native owner does **not** need Synthesis to equip their own native Legacy once:
- the Character Quest Component has been earned;
- the item has been completed/released at Cresthaven;
- ordinary equipment-family and slot legality are satisfied.

### Reciprocal partner
The reciprocal partner needs:
- the final physical Legacy item completed/released through the native owner's Character Quest Component;
- that reciprocal partner's own purchased **Synthesis** node;
- ordinary equipment-family and slot legality.

Reciprocal mapping remains:
- Vaelira may use **Stillpoint Aegis** after **Unified Spectrum**.
- Cyanis may use **Living Prism** after **Unified Crest**.
- Seyrik may use **Mercy's Boundary** after **Tempered Ruin**.
- Ilyra may use **Purposebound** after **Mercy Unbroken**.
- Nimera may use **Known Ground** after **Living Proof**.
- Torren may use **Decisive Record** after **Measured Passage**.

Synthesis never substitutes for the missing Character Quest Component.

No Legacy item may be required for:
- mandatory **Chapter-13** story completion;
- Story Prime use;
- Final Severance;
- required lore comprehension;
- mandatory boss victory assumptions;
- accessing the ending.

Legacy items remain high-end optional build rewards.

## 7. No crafting-system rule

The fixed Legacy completion interaction must not expand into a general crafting system.

No recipes, material lists, upgrade tiers, random outcomes, currency fee, forge minigame, repeatable component economy, or interchangeable Legacy parts are created.

Each Character Quest grants exactly one authored unique component for exactly one authored Legacy masterwork.

The three mandatory **late-Chapter-12** Synthesis-resolution scenes remain item-reward-free.
