Music cannot reveal story information earlier than the story does.

Examples:

## Last Sentinel
Ch0:
- no confirmed Last Sentinel/Prime identity.

Ch3:
- Last Sentinel identified/recovered.

Ch4:
- first verified modern Prime manifestation.

A music cue should not unmistakably label Ch0 as "Last Sentinel's theme" in a way that defeats the intended reveal unless the use is deliberately subtle and approved.

## Entity
The modern world does not begin with accepted knowledge of the Entity.

Do not use:
- explicit named Entity-song metadata in player-facing interfaces;
- an unmistakable final-boss leitmotif presented as known historical culture

before the reveal unless deliberately approved as non-diegetic foreshadowing.

## Black Host / Vaelkor
Vaelkor remains a responsible human antagonist.
Music must not imply:
- he is possessed;
- he is secretly worshiping the Entity;
- his boss form is an Entity transformation.

## Final Severance
Modern solution is:
HOLD → DISTINGUISH → MAP → PRESERVE → CONTAIN → END.

Do not sonically stage it as the ancient six simply repeating the Last Weapon's convergence sequence.
