# Diyse — Diegetic Music Boundary

**Status:** ACTIVE DIEGETIC-MUSIC BOUNDARY  
**Parent authority:** `../AUDIO_AUTHORITY_MASTER.md`

Diegetic music means music physically performed/heard inside the world.

Current whole-project canon does not yet lock:
- regional folk traditions;
- tavern instrumentation;
- ceremonial repertoires;
- military songs;
- court music;
- ancient playback devices;
- Black Host propaganda music;
- hymns;
- work songs.

Older research explored several such ideas, but the later whole-project music reset means they are not automatically current.

## Ancient Diyse
Authorial history allows Ancient Diyseans to have possessed technologically advanced:
- instruments;
- amplification;
- recording;
- processing;
- studio technology.

This worldbuilding compatibility does **not** by itself canonize a particular ancient genre or exact real-world equipment list for the final game.

## Final rule
If future story/dialogue explicitly requires an in-world song/performance:
> promote that piece through normal music change control before treating its musical style as cultural canon.
