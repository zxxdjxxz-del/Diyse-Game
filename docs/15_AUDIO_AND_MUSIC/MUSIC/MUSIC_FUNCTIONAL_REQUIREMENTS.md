# Diyse — Music Functional Requirements

**Status:** ACTIVE MUSIC FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS  
**Parent authority:** `../AUDIO_AUTHORITY_MASTER.md`

These are **functional production requirements**, not a locked compositional style.

Final music should support the current game structure:

## Exploration
Need a strategy for:
- persistent hubs;
- routes;
- dungeons/facilities;
- optional-major sites;
- late-war territory;
- final domain.

The solution may use:
- unique cues;
- shared regional cues;
- arrangements;
- stems;
- ambience-driven silence;

but the exact model is OPEN.

## Combat
Music system must account for:
- ordinary encounters;
- mandatory bosses;
- same-bar escalation;
- genuine fresh-form transitions;
- Regional Hunts;
- Major Hunts;
- Prime manifestations;
- final two-form boss.

A fresh form does not automatically require a totally separate track.
A same-bar phase does not automatically require a new track either.

## Story
Music must be able to:
- enter/exit cleanly around dialogue;
- support quiet Character-Life scenes;
- avoid overpowering line-heavy scenes;
- preserve silence when silence is dramatically stronger.

## Finale
The final operation has distinct states:
- Deepest City;
- Archive reveal;
- Last Shelter;
- Reactor Galleries;
- Reconstituted Entity;
- Last Command;
- Final Severance;
- aftermath.

The exact cue plan remains OPEN.

## Android
Streaming/memory choices must support the Android production target.
