## Recorded voice-over
Status:
> **OPEN**

Current dialogue canon is text/scene authority, not a recorded-performance mandate.

Do not assume:
- full voice acting;
- partial voice acting;
- combat barks;
- voiced Character Quests;
- voiced story-only scenes.

## If voice is later approved
Voice performance must preserve:
- current exact dialogue wording;
- character age;
- established personality;
- relationship progression;
- pronunciation authority;
- no improvised lore that breaks knowledge firewalls.

## Text timing
Music/SFX must not make dialogue unreadable.

A future voice system would need:
- interruption policy;
- skip/advance behavior;
- subtitle timing;
- voice ducking/mix rules.

All remain OPEN.

## Nonverbal vocalizations
Efforts, grunts, breaths, pain reactions, combat exertions are also not currently locked as a production requirement.
