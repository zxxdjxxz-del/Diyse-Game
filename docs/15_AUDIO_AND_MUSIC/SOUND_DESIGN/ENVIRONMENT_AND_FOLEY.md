# Diyse — Environment / Foley Requirements

**Status:** ACTIVE AUDIO REQUIREMENTS  
**Parent authority:** `../AUDIO_AUTHORITY_MASTER.md`

## Environment families requiring a sound plan
- settlements/hubs;
- forest/road travel;
- mountains/snow;
- waterworks/wet spaces;
- Black Host industrial/military facilities;
- Ancient Diysean ruins;
- underground Ancient cities;
- reactors/Crest infrastructure;
- final deep domain.

## Production economy
Use layered looping ambience and selective one-shots to imply:
- offscreen work;
- crowds;
- machinery;
- wildlife;
- weather;
- distant combat;
- structural scale.

Avoid requiring large runtime simulations merely to generate ambience.

## Material Foley
Current visual language implies useful material families:
- stone;
- wood;
- metal;
- armor;
- cloth/leather;
- snow/ice;
- wet surfaces;
- vegetation;
- machinery.

Exact Foley library remains OPEN.

## Physical water/wind
Water and wind are still normal environmental sounds.

Their existence does not restore Water/Wind as combat elements.
