## Standard Card
Should read as:
- deliberate magical technique selection;
- stronger/more specialized than an ordinary Ability where appropriate;
- reusable action;
- not a physical trading-card shuffle/deckbuilder loop unless a specific effect calls for it.

No universal:
- draw;
- discard;
- shuffle;
- hand-fan

sound language is required.

## Prime invocation
Must read as a major state change.

### Recovered Story Prime
- invocation;
- one signature action;
- dismissal in the same ordinary round.

### Awakened Prime
- invocation;
- party suspension;
- three directly controlled Prime rounds;
- dismissal;
- return to party.

## Cooldown/availability
The UI may use subtle audio to signal:
- invocation unavailable;
- Prime ready;
- fresh-form refresh;

but exact cues are OPEN.

## Final Severance
Each Story Prime role should remain distinguishable enough that the six-step causal sequence is understandable.

Do not collapse the entire sequence into one undifferentiated impact.
