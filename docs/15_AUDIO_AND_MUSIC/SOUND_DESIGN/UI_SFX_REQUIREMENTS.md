- move/select;
- confirm;
- cancel/back;
- invalid action;
- target select;
- round confirm;
- menu open/close;
- equip/unequip;
- Card equip;
- Prime equip;
- item use;
- buy;
- sell;
- G change;
- quest update;
- Hunt unlock;
- save success;
- load success;
- load failure;
- warning/point-of-no-return confirmation.

- Synthesis unlock;

- Concordant rank-up;
- Prime XP level-up.
