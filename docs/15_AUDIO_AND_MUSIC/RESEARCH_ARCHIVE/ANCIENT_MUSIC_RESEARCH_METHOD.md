The Ancient Music appendix established several workflow rules that remain useful as **research methodology**, even while the soundtrack itself is open.

## Evidence discipline
- arrangement/mix claims should be grounded in actual analysis;
- spectrum alone must not be used to invent exact instrument models;
- failed stem separation must not later be described as successful;
- measured source behavior should be distinguished from subjective claims.

## Source verification
Literal sample work should preserve:
- source identity;
- license;
- local binary integrity;
- pitch mapping;
- dynamic tiers;
- repeated-note variation;
- native decode details.

## Variation
Dynamic-tier variation and repeated-note/round-robin variation are separate dimensions.

Do not flatten all source recordings into one average event merely for convenience.

## Prototype discipline
Temporary:
- convenience timbres;
- modeled substitutes;
- test processors;
- generated prototypes

must remain labeled as development states.

A useful prototype is not automatically a canon instrument or final cue.
