# Diyse — Equipment Economy Handoff

**Status:** ACTIVE ITEMS / EQUIPMENT AUTHORITY  
**Authority:** current items/equipment-domain owner plus later explicit approved corrections.  


This domain owns **what an item/equipment piece is**.

The following are canonical responsibilities of:
`12_ECONOMY_AND_REWARDS`

- purchase price
- sell value
- shop stock
- vendor chapter timing
- player-facing **G** economy
- service fees
- reward-value budgeting
- treasure-value pacing

Do not keep a second editable price table inside `08_ITEMS_AND_EQUIPMENT`.

The item/equipment files may state:
- reward-only
- guaranteed
- protected cache
- first-acquisition source
- unavailable for normal shop sale

when that status is intrinsic to the item's acquisition identity.
