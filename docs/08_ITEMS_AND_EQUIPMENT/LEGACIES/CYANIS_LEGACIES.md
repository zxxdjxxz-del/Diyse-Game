## Move or I Move You. — Forced Opening
Current capstone: +10pp applicable Defense/Spirit penetration on eligible Sword damage.

Against an enemy in an eligible ordinary **Guard/removable defensive state**, the qualifying damaging Sword action gains a further offensive opening under the current normalized no-Barrier/no-Brace rules. Protected boss states are never bypassed. The Trait must not resurrect Barrier, Brace, or a Break meter.

## That Was Dumb. — Bad Choice
Once per round after the wearer receives eligible single-target hostile direct damage:
- store one retaliation setup;
- next eligible damaging action before end of following round gains **+15% final damage** and **+10pp applicable Defense/Spirit penetration**.
One pending setup; refreshes rather than stacks; consumed by the qualifying action.
No free counterattack.
