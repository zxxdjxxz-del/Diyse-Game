## Should've Moved. — Predicted Line
Capstone:
- **+10 Base Hit** to eligible Great Bow offense.

Against **Hunter's Measure**:
- eligible damaging Great Bow actions gain **+10% final damage** and **+10pp Critical Chance**.
If the measured enemy has not yet acted this round, the current stronger timing window becomes:
- **+15% final damage**
- **+15pp Critical Chance**.
No hidden action reading or extra turn.

## Figured You'd Come This Way. — Prepared Ground
Once per round, when wearer resolves the first qualifying single-target action against an enemy that has not acted this round:
- wearer takes **15% less eligible direct damage from that enemy** through the current round.
Only one enemy can be covered by this Trait per round.
No dodge guarantee, retarget, extra action, or interception.
