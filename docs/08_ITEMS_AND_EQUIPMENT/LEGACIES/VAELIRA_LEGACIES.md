## There's Your Problem. — Correct Answer
Capstone:
- eligible elemental damaging actions made with the Arcane Staff gain **+10pp Spirit penetration**.

When the action hits an enemy's **actual elemental weakness**:
- **+15% final damage**
- **+10pp harmful-status application chance** for an already-authored matching linked-status rider.

No status is invented.
No weakness is revealed.
No bonus against neutral/resisted/immune results.

## That Saves Me the Trouble. — Borrowed Answer
Once per round after wearer takes eligible direct elemental damage >0:
- store that same element through end of following round.

Eligible damaging Abilities of that element:
- cost **20% less MP**, minimum 1;
- deal **+12% final damage**.

Only one element stored; later qualifying hit replaces it.
Does not grant access to an element.
Does not reduce Standard Card or Prime MP.

## Oh, I Can Use That. — Useful Pressure
Always:
- **10% less eligible direct elemental damage**.

First direct elemental damage each round:
- eligible elemental damaging actions gain **+15% final damage** through end of following round.
The boost is broader elemental pressure, not a hidden elemental mark.
Max one trigger/round; refreshes, does not stack.


## Donor legality
The Legacy Trait travels with the actual item and must function for any legal donor receiver.
No universal off-owner penalty exists.
