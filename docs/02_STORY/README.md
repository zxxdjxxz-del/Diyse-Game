## Global chapter-end cleanup rule
For **Chapters 0–12**, every chapter ends with a player-controlled **cleanup period before the next chapter begins**.

During that cleanup period:
- the current chapter is still active;
- the player may complete whatever Hunts, side quests, Character-Life scenes, permitted returns/backtracking, shopping, equipment work, formation changes, saving, healing, and preparation are legitimately available at that story state;
- cleanup does not override story geography, temporary closures, or point-of-no-return restrictions;
- the next chapter does **not** start automatically after the final mandatory scene, rest, boss, or hub arrival;
- the player must deliberately choose an explicit advance-story interaction such as **Begin Chapter N**, **Depart**, **Start Operation**, or the chapter-appropriate equivalent;
- any first event belonging to the next chapter occurs only after that choice.

This global rule supersedes older automatic chapter-to-chapter transitions in historical scripts or summaries.
