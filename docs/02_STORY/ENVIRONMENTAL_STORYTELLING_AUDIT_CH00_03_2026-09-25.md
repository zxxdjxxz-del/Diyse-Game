# DIYSE — Environmental Storytelling Audit — Chapters 0–3

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Status:** PASS WITH TARGETED LOCKS ADDED

## Purpose

Verify that Chapters 0–3 communicate story through spaces, damage, objects, NPC activity, layered construction, ecology, and changing area state rather than relying on dialogue alone.

This audit does not add new mysteries or new lore answers. It formalizes what the player should be able to infer visually/through exploration before or alongside character interpretation.

## Global rule

Environmental storytelling should usually operate in three layers:

1. **Primary read** — the player can understand the broad situation from the space itself.
2. **Secondary evidence** — optional or closer observation adds texture/context without becoming a lore dump.
3. **State change** — after a major event, the area should visibly reflect what happened.

Do not turn every prop into an inspect prompt. Do not use environmental props to bypass reveal firewalls.

## Chapter 0 — The Broken Convoy

**Overall:** GOOD, previously under-specified.

Strong existing environmental functions:
- intact working convoy before disaster;
- wreck-field geography;
- trapped/wounded survivor placement;
- suspiciously usable northern cut;
- improvised triage transformation;
- broken Card recovery casing;
- survivor departure / quiet overnight state.

New lock added to ensure:
- the convoy's before/after contrast is visually legible;
- the north-cut clue remains physical evidence rather than proof of motive;
- triage visibly reorganizes the damaged space;
- the aftermath visibly restores order without restoring the convoy;
- the overnight quiet is earned by area-state change.

**Risk to avoid:** hiding future Prime/Seyrik/Black-Host-mastermind answers in wreckage props.

**Risk to avoid:** environmental clues identifying the Wayfinder's second marking system, Face-network function, or missing Caelora center.

## Chapter 2 — The Drowned Oath

- Sunken Archive preservation architecture and flooding;
- Ancient passage intersecting later Bastion construction;

New lock adds specificity for:

- Bastion military operation being visible before Rhazek explains anything;

**Risk to avoid:** reintroducing the retired poisoned-water story, replacement map mural, destination ledger, exact prisoner totals, or transfer manifests.

## Chapter 3 — The Old City and Last Sentinel

**Overall:** VERY STRONG, CLOSE TO CHAPTER-1 LEVEL.

Strong existing environmental functions:
- modern Caelora layered over older construction;
- active Crown archive gradually becoming less controlled / more Ancient;
- recent-reader trail reconstructed from disturbed research materials;
- Hall of Seals / Sealwright physical evidence;
- Westways recordbook discovered in genuine archival context;
- Cresthaven fort visibly built over something vastly larger;
- command-oriented lower tower base;
- headquarters visibly becoming usable during cleanup.

New lock strengthens:
- Crown competence without pretending it fully understands the Old City;
- non-digital physical research traces;
- distinction between evidence of repeated seal-copy attempts and proof of success;
- rough hand-copy visual contrast between recordbook map and true Wayfinder;
- Cresthaven's reuse history before the Warden encounter;
- headquarters transformation without magically restoring the entire fort.

**Risk to avoid:** environmental depiction of what a Prime Card looks like, successful royal-seal duplication, Calder identification, Last Sentinel meaning, Wayfinder second-system explanation, or all-six-Faces network truth.

# Cross-chapter progression

Environmental storytelling should escalate with the player's understanding:

**Chapter 0**
> immediate physical aftermath and human survival

**Chapter 1**
> occupation, excavation, wilderness ecology, first large Ancient spatial evidence

**Chapter 2**
> layered infrastructure, reuse across eras, human captivity/logistics, active enemy institution

**Chapter 3**
> evidence reconstruction, archival provenance, civilization layering, ancient/modern reuse at city scale

This progression should be preserved into Chapter 4 rather than resetting every area to generic scenery.

No dialogue rewrite is required solely because of this pass.
