## Starting Chapter 13
Deliberate final-operation launch from Cresthaven.

But:
> **starting Chapter 13 is not irreversible.**

The player may progress through:
- The Deepest City;
- Deep City;
- Last Weapon Archive;
- Last Weapon Archon;
- Last Shelter;

while retaining a supported route/system to unfinished eligible world content.

## True PONR
> **Last Shelter → Reactor Galleries**

Before crossing:
- explicit warning;
- final save/checkpoint;
- final loadout;
- final chance to return.

After crossing:
- ordinary world return disabled;
- unfinished optional content inaccessible;
- finale proceeds continuously.

## Superseded

- Vaelkor defeat automatically begins finale;
- Chapter-13 launch is irreversible;
- Cresthaven briefing is the true PONR.
