# Chapter 3 — Beat 12 → Beat 13 Royal Meeting Transition Lock

**Status:** ACTIVE CONTINUITY / TRANSITION CORRECTION  
**Applies to:** end of Beat 12 and opening of Beat 13  
**Supersedes:** any wording that requires the player to manually traverse back out of the Old City after the seal-copying chamber.

## Locked transition
After the party finishes examining the sealwright / seal-copying room, the player is given a clear choice to **leave the room / conclude the current investigation**.

Once the player chooses to leave:
- the Old City field section ends immediately;
- there is **no playable climb back through the Archive**;
- there is no additional mandatory traversal, encounter stretch, exit puzzle, or separate return beat;
- do not require the player to walk back through Beats 5–12 locations;
- do not insert an extra exterior Caelora arrival scene.

The next authored scene begins **upstairs in Caelora in a royal meeting with Queen Lysara Ceryth and Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth**.

## Meeting state
Mandatory participants at the opening of Beat 13:
- **Queen Lysara Ceryth**;
- **Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth**;
- **Cyanis**;
- **Ilyra**;
- **Torren**;
- **Nimera**.

**Maevra** may re-enter physically here as established by the Chapter-3 party-state continuity: she has remained in Caelora with Mirena during the Old City field investigation and this is the natural point for the two investigative tracks to reunite.

## Purpose
The transition should feel like a clean JRPG handoff:
> **player finishes the room → chooses to leave → cut → royal debrief upstairs.**

The player does not need to watch or play the physical return journey because nothing new is gained by repeating already-cleared spaces.

## Beat-13 opening function
The royal meeting is where the party now reports and compares:
- the First Command Warden's scan and shutdown messages;
- **PREVIOUS ERROR**;
- **LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED**;
- Cyanis's stable deep Ruby Card state;
- the evidence that someone had been practicing copies of Ancient seals, older Yahtrean seals, and royal-document seals;
- repeated attempts to reproduce the **Crest of Yahtrea / royal seal**;
- evidence that very powerful magic had been used in those attempts;
- the fact that nothing yet proves anyone has ever successfully reproduced the royal seal's genuine magical authority response;
- the earlier northward route clue already established before Beat 12.

The meeting must distinguish what was directly observed from what remains theory.

## Reveal limits
This transition does not itself reveal:
- the identity of the recent reader / seal practitioner;
- who created the suspect Card transport orders;
- Calder's hidden role;
- whether the Queen's physical seal was accessed;
- whether the royal seal's genuine magical response can actually be duplicated;
- the exact method used to produce the suspect orders;
- definitive Prime / Might / Last Sentinel synthesis unless Beat 13 establishes it under its own authority.
