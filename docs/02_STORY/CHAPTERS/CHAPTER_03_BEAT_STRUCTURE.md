1. **Caelora Gate / Arrival** — normal lawful arrival; ancient-capital existence is already known.

3. **The Impossible Orders** — Lysara did not authorize the Card transport although her genuine magical authority answers on the orders.
4. **The Seal That Wasn't Used** — physical seal remains secure/functional; Mirena directs the field investigation toward the Lower Archives.
5. **Lower Archives Entrance / Nimera Joins** — Mirena escorts the trio through the active Upper Archives to the Lower Archives entrance; Nimera is found working and joins permanently.
6. **Lower Archives Investigation** — Lower Archives → Buried Collections → Hall of Seals; recent-reader and historical seal evidence deepen the mystery without solving it.
7. **Deep Archives / Memory Construct** — Ancient physical structure dominates; Six-Face material appears; Memory Construct is the archive boss; no Last Sentinel/Ruby event.
8. **Inner Collections / Findings** — ~500-year-old Yahtrean Westways recordbook + Wayfinder-derived regional copy; later seal-reproduction workspace of unknown original Ancient purpose.
9. **Return to Mirena / Cresthaven Identified** — Mirena identifies the giant tower's modern location as Cresthaven; rest is offscreen only.
10. **Cresthaven / Tower Base / Authority Construct** — normal abandoned fort + short tower-base dungeon; Authority Construct gives PREVIOUS ERROR then LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED; only after it is inert does the Card stabilize deep Ruby.
11. **Cresthaven Headquarters / Ivorybridge Decision** — headquarters and cleanup open; party chooses Ivorybridge as the next practical northern investigation point because routes continue north beyond the copied map.
