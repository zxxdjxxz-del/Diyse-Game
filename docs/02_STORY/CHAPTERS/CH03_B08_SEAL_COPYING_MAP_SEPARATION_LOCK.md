# Chapter 3 — Beat 8 Seal-Reproduction / Map Separation Lock

**Status:** CURRENT EXPLICIT USER CORRECTION — ACTIVE AUTHORITY  
**Applies to:** Beat 8 — Inner Collections / Findings

## Core separation

Beat 8 contains **two separate discoveries**:

1. **Inner Collections / Westways recordbook**
   - approximately 500-year-old Yahtrean recordbook about the Westways;
   - contains a hand-copied regional map derived from a much older Ancient Diysean Wayfinder;
   - copy is less precise than the actual Wayfinder but intact across the copied region;
   - covers Westways + central Yahtrea only;
   - restores the Caelora-area center missing from the damaged physical Wayfinder;
   - shows a very large tower north of the city;
   - several largest routes converge there and continue north off the copied map;
   - the map does not show where those routes ultimately lead.

2. **Later seal-reproduction workspace**
   - occupies Ancient construction, but its original Diysean purpose is unknown;
   - later users adapted it for seal/Crest-related magical work;
   - contains repeated copying/reproduction practice and evidence of powerful unknown magic;
   - no successful duplicate Royal Magic Seal is established.

## Map rule

The seal-reproduction workspace itself does not provide the Cresthaven lead and does not contain a new northern-route display.

The geographic lead comes from the separate Westways recordbook discovery in the Inner Collections.

## Seal interpretation boundary

- reproducing the visible Crest is not the same as reproducing genuine magical royal authority;
- nothing proves any copied seal successfully reproduced Lysara's authority;
- practitioner remains unidentified;
- practitioner/recent reader connection remains unproven;
- Calder remains unrevealed;
- the seal thread dead-ends in Caelora for now.

The seal-reproduction evidence does **not** point to Cresthaven or Ivorybridge.
