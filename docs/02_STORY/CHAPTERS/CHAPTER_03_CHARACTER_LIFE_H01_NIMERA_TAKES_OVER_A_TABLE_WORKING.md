## Scene purpose
H01 is a light Cresthaven cleanup Character-Life scene about **Nimera beginning to occupy the headquarters as if she actually intends to stay**.

Its main jobs are:
1. show Nimera claiming the records / map-table space through ordinary work habits rather than a formal declaration that she belongs;
2. let the player see the new four-person party in a low-stakes setting after the Old City and Cresthaven handoff;
3. preserve the established comedy premise that Nimera's work expands across available surfaces almost immediately;
4. bring Cyanis and Torren into the room already halfway through one of their casually vicious arguments;
5. preserve the two exact user-locked opening insult lines;
6. make it obvious through tone / behavior that Cyanis and Torren's exchange is familiar, reciprocal, and safe rather than genuine hostility;
7. let Nimera discover that she **loves their dynamic** and is not intimidated by either of them;
8. keep the scene domestic / funny / character-driven rather than turning it into another Card, seal, Last Sentinel, or Cresthaven-lore discussion;
9. finish with Nimera having effectively won the table dispute without anyone needing to formally concede it.

H01 is:
> **Nimera spreads out → Cyanis and Torren arrive fighting about nonsense → locked insult exchange → they discover the table has been conquered → Nimera joins the social chaos immediately → the table is hers now, apparently.**

## Availability / trigger
H01 is available during the **Chapter-3 Cresthaven cleanup window**, after:
- Beat 15's headquarters handoff is complete;
- Mirena and Maevra have returned to Caelora;
- player control has returned to the normal resident party of **Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren + Nimera**.

Recommended trigger:
- player enters / interacts with the **records / map room** or the main investigation table after the cleanup window opens.

The scene should not auto-fire so aggressively that it interrupts the player's first chance to orient themselves in Cresthaven.

It can be one of the first optional Character-Life scenes the player chooses to see.

## Location
Use Cresthaven's newly reopened **records / maps / investigation room**.

The room should still visibly be mid-restoration:
- one solid central table has been cleared and made usable;
- mismatched chairs / stools;
- old Crown shelves partly restocked;
- fresh paper / ink / lamps among older dusty fittings;
- map hooks / wall boards being reused;
- crates or rolled records not yet fully sorted;
- workers have left just enough open surface to imply the table is meant to be communal.

That last point is important, because Nimera immediately treats "communal" as "available."

---

## Starting image — Nimera has already expanded
The scene should begin with **Nimera already at work**, not with her asking permission to use the room.

She has started with one reasonable work area and then expanded outward.

Possible visual spread:
- her book open near one elbow;
- several copied Old City notes;
- loose sheets with Card observations;
- route / seal notes that are safe to keep here;
- labeled scraps / bookmarks;
- one or more Conduits or ordinary Cardweaver tools set down temporarily;
- reference texts borrowed from the material Mirena allowed them to bring / copy;
- a drink or food item occupying the last suspiciously convenient empty spot;
- neatly organized stacks that are completely logical to Nimera and increasingly hostile to anyone else attempting to use the table.

The joke should not be that Nimera is sloppy.

She is **highly organized**.

The problem is that her definition of a reasonable workspace has already grown to include most of the table.

### Nimera's attitude
She is comfortable, focused, and entirely unembarrassed.

If someone later accuses her of taking over the room, her genuine position can structurally be:
- she is using the available work surface;
- every pile has a purpose;
- there was unused space;
- there is technically still some unused space;
- no one specified a maximum territorial footprint.

Do not make her cute / helpless about it.

She knows exactly what she has done.

---

## Cyanis and Torren enter already arguing
Cyanis and Torren should be heard / encountered entering **already halfway through an argument**.

The audience does not need the full origin of the argument.

In fact, it is funnier if the immediate subject is trivial / stupid / wildly disproportionate to the verbal violence.

Examples of acceptable argument origins for the later Dialogue Engine:
- whether Torren's ghillie cape sheds plant matter everywhere;
- whether Cyanis's armor makes him incapable of moving quietly indoors;
- who tracked mud farther into the fort;
- whether a particular piece of gear is ugly;
- whether one of them stole / moved something mundane;
- some completely unimportant tactical disagreement that has already devolved.

The exact setup is open.

### Exact locked opening exchange
The following two lines are the **exact preserved dialogue anchors** and must remain verbatim:

**Cyanis:** `I bet you use that cape to sneak up on the goats you fuck.`

**Torren:** `You look like a walking dick in armor.`

These are the first locked spoken anchors of H01.

Do not sanitize them.
Do not paraphrase them.
Do not reverse the order.
Do not assign them to different speakers.

### Tone lock
The performance / staging must make clear:
- neither man is actually escalating toward violence;
- neither is wounded by the exchange;
- the reply speed suggests long practice;
- this is affectionate competitive hostility between people who trust one another;
- they can move on from the insult instantly.

Avoid writing nearby characters as if they need to mediate a genuine conflict.

---

## Nimera's reaction — immediate delight
Nimera should hear / witness the exchange and realize very quickly that this is simply **how these two talk to each other**.

Her reaction should not be timid shock.

She can:
- laugh unexpectedly;
- look up with immediate interest;
- ask how long this has been going on;
- judge / score one of the insults;
- encourage a continuation;
- contribute a dry observation that proves she understands the rules almost immediately;
- become visibly more interested in the argument than either Cyanis or Torren expected.

The structural point is:
> **Nimera loves their dynamic.**

This is useful because it shows she is not entering the party as the fragile new scholar everyone must tiptoe around.

She can enjoy profanity, nonsense, and adult social roughness.

---

## The actual conflict — the table
The insult exchange should lead naturally into the discovery that Nimera has taken over most of the room's main table.

One of Cyanis / Torren came in expecting to use the table, set something down, look at a map, or simply pass through and notices there is effectively nowhere left to put anything.

This becomes the scene's real low-stakes conflict.

### Key comedy principle
Nimera should be able to defend every individual item placement rationally.

The absurdity comes from accumulation.

Structurally:
1. Cyanis or Torren points out that she has taken over the table.
2. Nimera disputes the wording rather than the physical fact.
3. She identifies remaining open surface area as evidence that the table is not fully occupied.
4. Someone tests that claim by trying to use the alleged free space.
5. Nimera immediately explains why that specific free space is needed for something else.
6. The others realize the table has, in practice, already become Nimera's research territory.

Do not turn this into a mean fight over whether she is allowed to work there.

Nobody genuinely wants to evict her.

The scene is about everyone discovering that **Nimera has nested**.

---

## Cyanis function
Cyanis should:
- arrive inside the Torren argument at full speed;
- deliver the locked goat line exactly;
- shift from insulting Torren to noticing the table problem without emotional whiplash because the argument was never serious;
- challenge Nimera's claim that she has not taken over the table;
- be practical enough to recognize that the work itself matters;
- lose ground because every attempt to establish communal territory is met by Nimera's perfectly reasonable explanation for another stack;
- not become authoritarian about "his" headquarters.

Potential underlying character beat:
- Cyanis is accustomed to carrying too much himself;
- seeing Nimera independently build a real working station means someone else is now taking ownership of part of the investigation burden.

Do not state that sentiment directly unless later dialogue earns it.

The scene should remain funny.

---

## Torren function
Torren should:
- answer Cyanis's locked insult immediately with the exact `walking dick in armor` line;
- be fully comfortable in the argument;
- notice physical-space absurdities quickly;
- potentially side with Nimera simply because it annoys Cyanis, then realize she has also consumed whatever surface he wanted;
- respect that her organization has an internal logic even while calling the situation ridiculous;
- contribute dry, minimal lines rather than trying to win every exchange through volume.

Torren's observational skill should stay ordinary / physical here.

No Perception lore, no mystical read, no field mechanics discussion.

---

## Nimera function
Nimera is the real center of H01.

She should:
- be actively doing useful work before the others enter;
- have expanded across the table through competence, not carelessness;
- immediately understand that Cyanis / Torren's aggression is social play;
- enjoy it rather than retreat from it;
- be willing to swear / jab back if the Dialogue Engine naturally produces it;
- defend the table with precise language and irritatingly sound logic;
- treat the room like somewhere she expects to keep working tomorrow.

That final behavior is the quiet emotional function of the scene.

She has joined permanently, but H01 is one of the first moments where she **acts like she belongs in the shared base without asking permission every five minutes**.

Do not sentimentalize this into a speech about family or belonging.

The fact that she has colonized the furniture is enough.

## Scene rhythm
Recommended structural rhythm:
1. player triggers H01 in the records / map room;
2. Nimera is already working across a suspicious amount of table space;
3. Cyanis and Torren approach / enter already in a ridiculous argument;
4. exact locked Cyanis insult;
5. exact locked Torren reply;
6. Nimera reacts with immediate delight rather than alarm;
7. one of the men attempts to use / set something on the main table;
8. discovery that Nimera has effectively occupied it;
9. escalating but low-stakes argument over whether this counts as "taking over";
10. Nimera defends each pile / tool / open patch with precise logic;
11. Cyanis and Torren realize there is no practical route to reclaiming meaningful space;
12. Nimera either joins their banter directly or scores / comments on it in a way that establishes she is comfortable with them;
13. scene ends on a clean comedy button showing the territorial expansion will probably get worse, not better;
14. return to cleanup gameplay.

Do not drag the scene after the joke lands.

## Ending state
By the end of H01:
- Nimera has effectively established the main records / investigation table as her working territory while still technically sharing it;
- Cyanis and Torren's vicious-but-safe banter has been demonstrated in front of Nimera;
- Nimera clearly enjoys their relationship dynamic;
- the three of them are more socially comfortable with one another;
- no major plot revelation occurs;
- no one formally assigns Nimera the space — she simply keeps it;
- Cresthaven feels more inhabited by the permanent party than it did before the scene.

### Preferred final feeling
The player should leave thinking something like:
> **Nimera has been here five minutes and somehow already owns the table. Cyanis and Torren are idiots. This party is beginning to feel lived-in.**
