## Beat purpose
Beat 15 is the physical arrival at Cresthaven, the headquarters handoff, and the opening of the Chapter-3 cleanup window.

It should:
1. begin after the player chooses **Leave for Cresthaven** the next morning in Caelora;
2. skip the ordinary road journey and open **outside Cresthaven**;
3. reveal that **Mirena and Maevra are already there**;
4. show a **full practical work crew** actively cleaning, clearing, repairing, and reopening the abandoned fort;
5. introduce Cresthaven visually as an abandoned forest Crown fort built over / into older Diysean structure;
6. let Mirena explain that the party may use Cresthaven as their **headquarters while they are helping her investigate**;
7. establish enough functional spaces for Cresthaven to operate as a real hub immediately;
8. keep Mirena and Maevra important to the handoff without making either woman a permanent resident / traveling party member;
9. have Mirena and Maevra return to Caelora after the initial setup / handoff;
10. leave the resident permanent party as **Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren + Nimera**;
11. open the Chapter-3 cleanup content and preserve the deliberate **Begin Chapter 4** boundary.

Beat 15 is:
> **arrival outside Cresthaven → active restoration already underway → Mirena offers headquarters → handoff → Mirena and Maevra return to Caelora → cleanup opens.**

Exact dialogue remains deferred to the Agent-Brain rehearsal-first Dialogue Engine.

## First sight — Cresthaven is already being reopened
The party should not arrive to a completely untouched ruin.

Mirena kept her word and organized the support movement separately.

When the permanent four arrive, they see:
- Crown wagons / supply carts;
- workers carrying timber, tools, lamps, rope, buckets, bedding, food stores, and medical supplies;
- teams hauling broken furniture / debris out of usable rooms;
- people sweeping, scraping moss, clearing collapsed clutter, opening shutters, testing doors, and checking roofs;
- a small number of guards / practical Crown personnel protecting the work site as appropriate;
- marked-off unsafe areas that workers have been told not to enter;
- temporary work tables / supply piles outside or in the outer yard;
- Mirena already directing the overall effort;
- Maevra helping coordinate the practical side of the cleanup.

The tone is:
> **an abandoned fort being rapidly made useful again by people who know how to establish a field base.**

Not:
- a massive army occupation;
- a festival;
- a magically restored castle;
- a pristine ready-made headquarters.

---

## Why Mirena can be there first
Cresthaven is an abandoned Crown site, not a completely unknown lost temple.

Mirena already knows:
- where the modern fort is;
- how to reach it through Crown records / roads;
- enough about its abandoned state to send workers and supplies;
- that the immediate task is basic reopening rather than conquering an unknown enemy stronghold.

Her work group travels separately with organized transport, tools, wagons, and Crown authority.

The permanent party's travel is skipped on-screen; exact arrival order / travel minutes are not a story puzzle.

The important story state is simply:
> **Mirena and Maevra reached Cresthaven with the workers before the party's arrival scene begins.**

## Cresthaven visual identity
Cresthaven should now receive its first full location reveal.

Current required identity:
> **an abandoned forest Crown fort / former outpost built over and into much older Diysean remains.**

Visible Crown layer may include:
- weathered defensive walls;
- old gatehouse / entrance works;
- timber and stone repairs from different eras;
- a central administrative / keep structure;
- barracks / quarters;
- supply rooms;
- a treatment room or room that can be converted to one;
- map / records space;
- yard / staging space;
- old watch positions;
- forest growth reclaiming unused edges.

Visible older Diysean layer may include:
- foundation blocks too large / precise for the Crown construction above;
- sealed lower arches;
- fitted stone seams beneath later walls;
- older stairs or buried courses partially exposed by damage;
- strangely durable stone beneath rotted Crown additions;
- pre-existing geometry that the Crown fort clearly reused rather than created.

### Mystery boundary
The party may immediately recognize:
- the Crown built on something older;
- the old layer is likely Diysean;
- Mirena's identification of the Crest-marked location was probably correct.

They do **not** yet know:
- why the exact Yahtrean Crest appears on the Ancient map at this location;
- the full Diysean purpose of Cresthaven;
- whether the ancient site had the same name;
- what lies farther north;
- how Cresthaven connects to the royal-seal culprit.

## Mirena's headquarters offer
Mirena should explain the practical reason for reopening the fort.

She asked the party to help her continue investigating.

They now need somewhere to:
- work outside the normal Caeloran bureaucracy;
- keep maps / copied records / field notes together;
- rest between investigations;
- store supplies;
- receive controlled Crown messages / updates;
- prepare for travel north / west / back to Caelora;
- examine new evidence without putting every discovery immediately through broad official channels.

Therefore Mirena tells the party that:
> **while they are helping her investigate what is happening, they can use Cresthaven as their headquarters.**

This should feel like a practical trust decision, not a ceremonial grant of property.

### What this is not
Mirena is not:
- giving them permanent ownership of a Crown fort;
- creating a new noble estate;
- naming Cyanis commander of a royal garrison;
- secretly imprisoning them there;
- assigning them a military unit.

It is an operational base for the investigation.

---

- helping workers decide what must be cleared first;
- checking that sleeping / supply / treatment spaces are actually usable;
- coordinating practical security;
- making sure unsafe lower areas are marked rather than casually opened;
- teasing / reacting naturally once the party arrives;
- helping explain what has already been reopened.

She remains:
- fully non-playable;
- not part of the permanent traveling four;
- not a default Cresthaven resident after the handoff.

---

## Minimum headquarters functions opened during the scene
By the end of the initial handoff, workers should have established or be finishing the minimum spaces required for cleanup gameplay.

Required functional categories:
- **rest / sleeping space**;
- **supply storage / party preparation area**;
- **records / maps / investigation table space**;
- **basic medical / treatment space**;
- **usable entrance / departure route**;
- enough light / heat / sanitation / cleared floor space for the fort to function as a hub.

These do not need to be luxurious.

The visual charm should be that Cresthaven is still visibly half-restored:
- workers remain active;
- some doors remain sealed;
- some rooms remain dusty / unsafe;
- old furniture is mismatched;
- supplies are still being sorted;
- the older structure is visible beneath the practical cleanup.

The base can improve later if desired, but it is immediately usable.
