## Ivorybridge direction

The roughly 500-year-old Yahtrean Westways recordbook contains a rougher but intact regional copy of Ancient Diysean Wayfinder information.

The copy shows:
- Westways + central Yahtrea;
- complete ancient Caelora cluster;
- giant tower at the location now identified as Cresthaven;
- large routes converging on the tower and continuing north off the copied map.

After physically confirming the tower site at Cresthaven, the party chooses **Ivorybridge** as the next sensible modern place to investigate northward.

This is a hypothesis:
- no Ancient map labels Ivorybridge;
- no Warden message names Ivorybridge;
- nobody claims the Ancient routes definitely pass through it.
