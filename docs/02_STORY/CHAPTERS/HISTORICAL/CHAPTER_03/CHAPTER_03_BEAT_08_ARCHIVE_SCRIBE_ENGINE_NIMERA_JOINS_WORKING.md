# Chapter 3 — Beat 8 — Archive Scribe Engine / Nimera Joins

**Status:** CURRENT WORKING BEAT PACKET — DEVELOPED

## Beat purpose
Beat 8 turns Nimera from **expert accompanying the trio** into the fourth permanent combat party member through action, competence, and an explicit choice to continue.

It should:
1. pay off Beat 7's reactivation of the deeper Archive;
2. introduce the first mandatory hostile deeper-Archive machine response;
3. stage the **Archive Scribe Engine** as an Ancient archive/security mechanism, not as a villain with personality or a chapter boss;
4. give Nimera a natural reason to enter combat immediately rather than stand outside the fight as an escort NPC;
5. reveal her base-class combat identity as **Cardweaver**, using **Conduits** and Card-based magical technique under existing combat-system authority;
6. establish the first functional four-character battle group: Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren + Nimera;
7. let the group win without implying the whole Old City is now hostile or fully awakened;
8. allow Nimera to perform a more serious post-encounter examination of Cyanis's Card and the permitted royal-seal evidence;
9. have her refuse premature Prime / Might / Last Sentinel conclusions;
10. establish that deeper Archive records / mechanisms may contain the comparative evidence they need;
11. have Nimera explicitly choose to continue with the group;
12. make that choice the permanent recruitment point;
13. hand directly into Beat 9's deeper four-person exploration and Six-Face markings.

Beat 8 is **combat introduction + earned recruitment**, not a major lore-answer scene.

## Starting state
- the Old City field group is Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren;
- Maevra remains in Caelora with Mirena working the royal-seal / Crown-record investigation;
- Nimera has been freed from the Ancient barrier and is moving with the trio provisionally;
- Beat 7 proved that one Ancient barrier could be released by correctly satisfying its current response pattern;
- this did **not** prove that Cyanis possesses Ancient authority or that modern Crest work is identical to Diysean technology;
- Nimera has completed only a first bounded close examination of Cyanis's Card;
- she considers the Card meaningfully abnormal but has not classified it as Prime, Might, or Last Sentinel;
- opening the barrier restored / exposed a deeper active archive route;
- Ancient channels / tracks / fixtures farther inside have begun moving or responding;
- the deeper Archive has not yet launched a mandatory hostile response;
- Nimera is not yet a permanent party member.

## Location identity — active archive infrastructure
Beat 8 should occur just beyond the reopened barrier, where the environment stops feeling like a Crown work area and begins feeling like **old archive machinery that still believes it has a job**.

Useful environmental elements:
- record-transfer tracks;
- fitted wall channels;
- rotating or sliding archive frames;
- narrow mechanical arms / inscription tools;
- storage rings / indexed compartments;
- old response plates that react to movement or access state;
- suspended or recessed machinery that had looked dormant until the route was reopened;
- far less modern Crown modification than in Beats 5–6.

The space should communicate that this is not simply a ruin full of monsters.

It was an operating archive system, and parts of that system can still transition between passive and active states.

## Why the Scribe Engine activates
The activation should follow directly from Beat 7 rather than feel random.

Recommended causal logic:
- reopening the barrier reconnects a route / circuit / access path that had been isolated;
- the deeper archive registers a changed access condition;
- an old Scribe Engine resumes an archival / verification / containment function;
- the current human presence fails whatever state or classification it expects;
- the machine begins physically enforcing that condition.

The party does **not** need to know the machine's original exact purpose.

They may infer that it was designed to:
- transfer records;
- index or inscribe material;
- restrict access to protected archive functions;
- respond when a route changes state.

Do not let Nimera confidently identify the full historical function from one encounter.

## What the Archive Scribe Engine is
The Scribe Engine should read as a **story-significant Ancient machine encounter**, not the First Command Warden in miniature.

It may be:
- a mobile archive construct;
- a fixed machine that deploys defensive components;
- a central transcription/indexing body with integrated security limbs;
- a small coordinated set of mechanisms treated mechanically as one encounter under battle authority.

### Presentation rule
The machine does not need:
- a human voice;
- villain dialogue;
- anger;
- a personal grudge;
- a melodramatic awakening cinematic;
- lore exposition during combat.

Its behavior should look procedural.

It is dangerous because its old function is incompatible with the party's current presence, not because it becomes evil.

## Encounter scale
This encounter should **not** carry the chapter's major-boss weight.

Current structural intent:
- one contained mandatory story encounter;
- no multi-form transformation requirement;
- no fresh-HP second form;
- no large post-fight destruction sequence;
- no unique world-level reward;
- no implication that defeating it conquers the Old City.

The later **First Command Warden** remains the chapter's major Ancient authority confrontation.

Exact HP/stat budgets, action tables, drops, rewards, and encounter-validation numbers remain under combat / encounter authority rather than this story packet.

### Chapter-3 status rollout
Chapter 3 is the planned introduction window for **Stun**.

If encounter design needs a natural first story-facing carrier, the Scribe Engine may use a clearly telegraphed Stun-capable archive/security action. This is optional placement guidance, not a locked numeric/status-action specification.

## Nimera enters combat by choice
When the machine turns hostile / begins enforcement, Nimera does not:
- retreat behind the trio;
- need to be ordered to fight;
- reveal that she secretly cannot defend herself;
- wait for a formal recruitment scene.

She assesses the situation and joins the formation because:
- she is in the danger zone too;
- she understands the machinery better than the others do;
- she is combat-capable;
- continuing to observe while others fight for her would be irrational.

This should be the player's first direct proof that she is not merely a scholar NPC.

## First four-person battle state
The combat party for the encounter becomes:
- **Cyanis**;
- **Ilyra**;
- **Torren**;
- **Nimera**.

This is the first time Diyse reaches the locked ordinary combat-party size of **4** using permanent party members.

### Party-management rule
Because exactly four permanent combat members exist at this moment, do not force a reserve/swap-party tutorial here.

Nimera simply fills the fourth combat slot.

Future reserve management becomes relevant only when later permanent members exceed the active-party cap.

## Nimera's combat identity — Cardweaver
The fight should introduce the **shape** of Nimera's Cardweaver identity without turning the encounter into a complete class tutorial.

Mandatory identity cues:
- **Conduit** equipment / weapon language rather than swords, bows, or staffs;
- deliberate use of Cards as part of her combat technique;
- flexible magical problem solving rather than a single-element mage identity;
- visible comfort selecting an effect appropriate to the situation;
- a fighting style that reads as practiced, not improvised because the protagonists arrived.

### What this first battle should communicate
The player should understand:
> **Nimera studies Cards because she actually uses them.**

Her scholarship and combat identity are connected.

The exact starting kit, unlocked abilities, equipped Standard Cards, MP costs, damage values, and class progression remain under class/combat authority.

### No future-class leakage
Do not expose her **Sixfold Knight** Subclass identity here.

That later class remains locked to the appropriate Accord progression.

## Combat roles without over-scripting
Story staging can support broad teamwork:

### Cyanis
- holds pressure / protects openings appropriate to his current Crest Knight toolkit;
- reacts quickly to the machine changing targeting or movement state;
- does not become the universal machine solver.

### Ilyra
- manages survivability / positioning / protection under her current Blue Warden identity;
- can call attention to a telegraphed dangerous action;
- does not replace Nimera as the magical analyst.

### Torren
- reads motion, targeting, physical weak points, tracks, or moving components;
- can identify when a mechanism is about to reposition based on visible evidence;
- remains evidence-first rather than magically predicting attacks.

### Nimera
- recognizes practical response behavior during the encounter;
- uses her Cardweaver kit to exploit / counter what she can actually observe;
- may give one or two useful tactical calls, but does not narrate the battle continuously;
- should feel immediately useful without trivializing the encounter.

## The machine can teach without explaining itself
The encounter may reveal limited facts through behavior:
- some mechanisms still classify access / presence;
- deeper archive systems can react to changed route states;
- active machines can distinguish between passive record infrastructure and enforcement behavior;
- the Old City contains working systems beyond anything the upper Crown archive controls.

It does **not** prove:
- a universal Diysean security hierarchy;
- that all machines obey the same permissions;
- that Cyanis has or lacks an Ancient credential;
- that the Card controls the Archive;
- that the machine was activated specifically by Cyanis.

## Victory state
When the party defeats / disables the Scribe Engine:
- the local enforcement behavior stops;
- the route is no longer immediately threatened by that machine;
- nearby infrastructure may settle into a quieter active state;
- the mechanism does not need to explode or erase itself;
- recoverable physical / inscription / response evidence can remain.

This is important because Nimera should have something to inspect afterward.

### No broad deactivation
Defeating one Scribe Engine does not shut down the deeper Old City.

If anything, the encounter confirms that the deeper layers contain additional still-functional systems.

## Post-fight inspection — separate observation from conclusion
After immediate safety is established, Nimera should inspect:
- the disabled Scribe Engine / its surviving response structures;
- Cyanis's Card more carefully;
- the authorized royal-seal comparison material;
- any nearby deeper-archive markings that are visible without stealing Beat 9's Six-Face discovery.

This is the first moment she has both:
1. direct access to Cyanis's abnormal Card;
2. a freshly observed Ancient responsive mechanism for comparison.

### What she can reasonably say
Nimera may establish that:
- Cyanis's Card is not behaving like an ordinary inert Card;
- its geometry / response state appears organized rather than random damage or simple contamination;
- the changes described across prior chapters represent real state changes worth tracking;
- the Queen-seal anomaly and the Card problem both involve **meaningful magical response under disputed / unclear conditions**;
- deeper archive material concerned with classification, authority, or Card systems is now a rational place to look.

### What she must refuse to say
She should explicitly avoid turning limited evidence into a grand answer.

She cannot yet prove:
- **Prime** classification;
- **Might** Face;
- **Last Sentinel** identity;
- that Cyanis is its bearer / owner;
- that the Card caused the Scribe Engine activation;
- that the Card and royal seal use the same magical system;
- that the Queen's seal descends directly from Diysean authority technology;
- who invoked the Queen's seal;
- what `/PREVIOUS ERROR/` will later mean.

Her willingness to say **not enough evidence** remains part of her characterization.

## Why deeper exploration becomes justified
After the encounter, the group has a stronger reason to move farther in rather than immediately returning to Mirena.

They now know:
- a deeper active archive layer exists;
- its systems contain responsive mechanisms beyond modern Crown practice;
- Nimera believes surviving classification / authority / Card material may exist farther inside;
- the current evidence still does not answer the Card or royal-seal questions;
- the route they have just opened is active and accessible now.

This is enough for a bounded continuation of the investigation.

It is **not** permission to wander the entire buried city without limits.

## Nimera's recruitment decision
The permanent join should happen after the fight and immediate evidence review, when Nimera has enough information to make an adult choice.

Her reasons are practical and personal:
- the Card is a genuinely unresolved problem directly within her expertise;
- the deeper Archive has begun exposing systems she has spent years trying to understand from partial evidence;
- the royal-seal anomaly gives the investigation broader stakes than a private academic curiosity;
- the trio has already demonstrated that they can contribute useful observations rather than treating her as a portable answer machine;
- the next evidence is physically deeper in the Archive, not back at her desk.

### Choice rule
Nimera **chooses** to continue.

Do not frame recruitment as:
- Mirena having permanently assigned her without consultation;
- Cyanis ordering her along;
- gratitude debt because the trio freed her;
- the Card magically compelling her;
- a prophecy / destiny statement;
- “we need a fourth fighter” as the narrative reason.

The game-system result is recruitment; the character reason is that the investigation has become her work too.

## Permanent party state
Once Nimera makes that choice:
> **Nimera Pellan permanently joins the party.**

Permanent combat-capable roster becomes:
- Cyanis;
- Ilyra;
- Torren;
- Nimera.

Active combat party is all four.

Maevra remains in Caelora with Mirena and remains non-playable.

### Recruitment presentation
A standard JRPG recruitment confirmation / party-join UI may appear after the authored choice if implementation wants it.

Avoid interrupting her first battle with a join card before she has actually made the decision.

## Equipment / class continuity
At recruitment:
- Nimera's Base Class is **Cardweaver**;
- ordinary equipment identity includes **Conduits**;
- she remains associated visually/mechanically with Cards;
- she does not unlock Sixfold Knight early;
- her exact join gear, ability loadout, Standard Card loadout, stats, and level are governed by current progression / class / item authorities.
