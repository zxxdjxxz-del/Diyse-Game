## Beat purpose
Beat 7 gives Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren, and Nimera their first real piece of cooperative problem solving.

It should:
1. resolve the Ancient barrier established across Beats 5–6;
2. let each present character contribute a materially different kind of observation;
3. use Cyanis's Crest training as a useful **analogy / pattern-recognition tool** without proving modern Crests and Diysean systems are identical;
4. use Nimera's mechanism scholarship to translate observation into a controlled test;
5. establish that the barrier is responsive to an arrangement / condition rather than to brute force or ordinary modern controls;
6. release Nimera from the enclosed work section without making Cyanis a secret Ancient credential;
7. let Nimera finally examine Cyanis's Card at close range in a bounded first pass;
8. open the route into a deeper active archive layer;
9. end with the environment changing enough to set up Beat 8's hostile Archive Scribe Engine response.

Beat 7 is the first moment where the group learns:
> **Old Diysean systems can sometimes be reasoned with even when their original purpose is unknown.**

That does **not** mean they understand the system as a whole.

## Starting state
- the Old City field group is Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren;
- Maevra remains in Caelora with Mirena working the royal-seal / Crown-record investigation;
- Nimera remains physically enclosed in her deeper work section by an Ancient responsive barrier;
- she is safe enough to communicate and has already ruled out ordinary Crown controls and several obvious non-destructive approaches;
- Torren can inspect the barrier's exterior structure;
- Nimera can inspect response features from the interior side;
- Cyanis has noticed that some relationships among lines / nodes / divisions feel broadly familiar from modern Crest-work principles;
- Ilyra has pushed the group toward the least destructive test rather than forcing the barrier;
- Cyanis still carries the abnormal Card;
- Nimera knows why Mirena sent the trio but has not yet completed a direct physical examination of the Card;
- nobody has identified the Card as Prime, Might, or Last Sentinel;
- nobody has established that Cyanis has Ancient authority.

## Core design rule — resemblance is not lineage
Cyanis's contribution must be useful without collapsing one of Chapter 3's larger mysteries.

He may recognize principles such as:
- balanced relationships rather than isolated marks;
- a line becoming meaningful only when completed across multiple points;
- paired / mirrored geometry;
- deliberate gaps functioning as part of a larger pattern rather than damage;
- directional flow / return paths resembling the way modern Crest-work distributes or closes magical structure.

He must **not** conclude:
- “This is a Crest.”
- “Ancients invented our Crests.”
- “I know this system.”
- “It recognizes me.”
- “I have permission.”

The correct Cyanis-level observation is closer to:
> **I don't know what this is, but I know what kind of relationship I'm looking at.**

Nimera should immediately understand the distinction.

## The barrier as a mechanism
The barrier should have a readable implementation logic rather than requiring an arbitrary password.

Recommended structural model:
- the barrier is integrated into the wall / floor / frame;
- several response plates or channels exist on both sides;
- when the closure triggered, part of the pattern shifted / withdrew / misaligned;
- the mechanism now holds a stable closed condition because its response circuit is incomplete or being interpreted as incomplete;
- inside and outside observers can each see only part of that relationship.

This naturally explains why:
- Nimera could not solve it alone from inside;
- the trio could not solve it merely by reaching the exterior controls;
- cooperation across the barrier matters.

### No biometric key
Do not make the system require:
- Cyanis's blood;
- his Card touching the wall;
- his Crest mark as a credential;
- a royal seal;
- a hidden character-specific password;
- an Ancient voice naming him.

The successful solution should be reproducible in principle by another sufficiently observant / knowledgeable group.

## Phase 1 — compare both sides
Nimera and Torren first establish what is physically different between the interior and exterior views.

Torren can contribute:
- which seams actually move;
- which plates show fresh motion / wear;
- which apparent markings are structural rather than decorative;
- where mechanical tension is being held;
- whether a supposed “broken” line is actually disappearing behind another element.

Nimera can contribute:
- which interior nodes react when pressure / proximity / harmless stimulus is applied;
- which responses are linked;
- which apparent dead points are actually waiting on another condition;
- what sequence she already tested and therefore what **not** to repeat.

This avoids making either one omniscient.

## Phase 2 — Cyanis recognizes the relationship
Once the observations are laid out, Cyanis notices that the problem resembles something familiar in **structure**, not design.

The useful insight:
- everyone has been treating individual nodes as separate controls;
- the geometry behaves more like one distributed working;
- the missing / inactive section may need to be completed from both sides at once or in a balanced relationship;
- what looks like a dead segment may be the return path that tells the mechanism the condition is complete.

Nimera should not simply accept this because Cyanis says so.

She checks it against what the barrier actually did.

If the observed responses fit, she treats the idea as a testable hypothesis.

### Character texture
This is a good early point for Nimera to appreciate that Cyanis has real technical literacy without immediately respecting every conclusion he makes.

Likewise Cyanis should recognize quickly that Nimera does not need the concept simplified once she has the premise.

Their cooperation should feel efficient, not destiny-coded.

## Phase 3 — least-destructive test
Ilyra's contribution becomes concrete here.

Before anyone commits to a larger manipulation, she pushes for:
- one small response change;
- no structural force;
- no Card exposure;
- clear retreat if the mechanism begins moving unpredictably;
- Nimera staying out of the immediate path of the barrier from the inside.

The group tests one portion of the proposed relationship.

Mandatory result:
- the barrier gives a **controlled partial response** that matches the hypothesis;
- this is the first evidence they are reading the mechanism correctly;
- it does not instantly fling open because Cyanis touched something.

The partial response should be enough to make the eventual solution feel earned.

## Phase 4 — coordinated release
Nimera and Cyanis coordinate the actual operation across both sides.

Possible implementation:
- Nimera holds / activates one interior response relationship;
- Cyanis reproduces / completes the corresponding exterior relationship based on the pattern he recognized;
- Torren calls the physical movement / confirms which segments are actually releasing;
- Ilyra keeps the safe zone clear and stops them if the structure begins behaving outside the predicted pattern.

The exact button / plate / magical-input implementation can be determined later, but the dramatic logic should remain:
> **Nimera understands the mechanism's behavior. Cyanis recognizes the structural relationship. Torren verifies the physical system. Ilyra keeps the experiment controlled.**

The barrier then retracts / unlocks / folds back into its surrounding structure in a deliberate Ancient motion.

### No triumphal proof
The barrier opening does not prove why the mechanism accepted the completed condition.

The group has learned how to satisfy **this one barrier's current state**.

They have not acquired universal Diysean access.

## Immediate aftermath — Nimera is free
Nimera exits the enclosed work area under her own power.

Avoid rescue staging such as:
- Cyanis catching her as she falls;
- emotional gratitude for “saving” her;
- the others treating her as a civilian who now needs escorting out.

A more fitting response is practical:
- she checks the mechanism from the newly accessible side;
- confirms what changed;
- immediately wants to compare the successful configuration against her notes;
- then turns to the actual reason the trio came to find her.

This preserves her agency and keeps the barrier sequence as collaboration rather than damsel rescue.

## First direct examination of Cyanis's Card
Now that they occupy the same space, Nimera gets a bounded close look at the recovered Card.

This is **not** the full identification scene.

She may observe:
- its current physical / magical color state;
- unusual geometric organization;
- that some parts of its pattern appear coherent while others remain difficult to classify;
- that its prior responses described by Cyanis are meaningful enough to justify deeper study;
- that its behavior is unlike an ordinary inert Card in ways she can articulate cautiously.

She may ask Cyanis to:
- hold it normally;
- describe exactly when specific visible changes occurred;
- distinguish what happened on contact, proximity, stress, battle, or environmental change if those observations are already canonically established.

### Handling boundary
Do not require Nimera to snatch the Card or ignore Cyanis's consent.

If she physically handles it, Cyanis knowingly allows the examination and the scene does not establish that only he can touch it.

### Classification firewall
Nimera still does **not** identify:
- Prime;
- Might;
- Last Sentinel;
- a confirmed bearer;
- recovery state.

She can explicitly say there is not enough evidence yet.

That restraint is part of her competence.

## Connection to the royal-seal question
The barrier solution may sharpen Nimera's interest in **response conditions**.

A useful conceptual connection:
- the barrier answered when a condition was correctly satisfied;
- the Queen's seal apparently answered on documents despite the Crown saying the expected condition—Lysara's authorization—was absent;
- therefore “what condition did the system actually recognize?” is a useful investigative question.

This is an analogy, not proof of shared technology.

Do not let anyone conclude that the barrier and royal seal operate on the same magical system.

## The route beyond Nimera's work area
Opening the barrier should expose / restore access to a deeper continuation that was previously unavailable or functionally blocked.

The route should visibly differ from the archive workspace:
- more intact Ancient surfaces;
- less Crown adaptation;
- active response lines / mechanisms where appropriate;
- old storage / transcription / record-transfer architecture;
- stronger evidence that the group is crossing from “archive used by modern scholars” into “archive infrastructure still doing something.”

Nimera recognizes that this deeper layer could contain better comparative evidence for both the Card and responsive-authority questions.

She has a reason to continue at least provisionally before permanent recruitment is formalized.

## Nimera's temporary movement state
At the end of Beat 7, Nimera is physically moving with the trio into the next section, but this should **not yet be treated as the permanent-party recruitment moment**.

Structural state:
- she is accompanying them because the deeper area is relevant to her research and their shared investigation;
- she is capable of defending herself, but her combat identity has not yet been demonstrated to the player;
- Beat 8's hostile Scribe Engine response provides the natural moment where she enters combat;
- permanent recruitment follows from her explicit choice after that encounter and immediate investigation.

This preserves the distinction between:
1. freed scholar walking with the group;
2. combat participant;
3. permanent party member.

## Beat ending — the archive wakes
As the four move beyond the reopened barrier, the Ancient environment should begin responding more actively.

Possible signs:
- old channels illuminate or shift;
- a dormant track activates;
- sealed record-transfer fixtures reorient;
- a distant mechanism begins moving;
- something deeper has registered the newly opened route / changed state.

Do not begin the full hostile encounter until Beat 8.

The ending should communicate:
> **Opening the barrier did more than free Nimera. It reconnected the group to an active part of the Archive.**

Objective shifts toward investigating the newly accessible layer.

Handoff → **Beat 8 — Archive Scribe Engine / Nimera Joins**.
