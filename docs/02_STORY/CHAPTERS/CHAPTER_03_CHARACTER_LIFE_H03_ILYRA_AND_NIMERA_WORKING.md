## Scene purpose
H03 is a small Cresthaven relationship scene between **Ilyra and Nimera**.

Its job is to:
1. give Ilyra and Nimera their first dedicated quiet Character-Life beat after Nimera joins permanently;
2. use Cresthaven's newly reopened treatment / medical space as a natural setting rather than inventing a crisis;
3. show Ilyra's care through practical action, boundaries, and consent rather than emotional interrogation;
4. show Nimera as capable, direct, and willing to accept help when the help respects her agency;
5. establish a dry, precise humor rhythm between them;
6. let trust grow through ordinary treatment rather than confession;
7. avoid new main-story revelations, Card classification, royal-seal answers, or Cresthaven-history answers.

The scene is:
> **minor injury / strain → Ilyra notices → asks permission → precise treatment → dry teasing → quiet mutual respect → back to ordinary life.**

It is not therapy and it is not a trauma-disclosure scene.

Use Cresthaven's newly reopened **treatment / medical room** during the Chapter-3 cleanup window.

The room should still feel recently restored:
- basic cot / treatment bench;

- boiled / clean water or equivalent supplies;
- simple field medicines;
- a small cabinet or crate of Crown-provided medical stock;
- one or two surfaces still mismatched or improvised because the fort is only partly reopened;
- workers may be audible elsewhere in Cresthaven, but they are not part of this scene.

The space should be functional, not luxurious.

## Physical reason for the scene
Nimera has a **minor but legitimate hand / wrist / forearm problem** accumulated from the Old City sequence and the Warden fight.

Keep it ordinary and non-dramatic.

Possible presentation:
- a shallow scrape / split skin across the hand or knuckles;
- mild swelling around the wrist;
- forearm soreness from repeated Conduit use / gripping / bracing during combat;
- an old quick field wrap that is functional but needs to be redone properly;
- stiffness that becomes obvious when she tries to write, sort notes, or manipulate small objects.

### Important limits
This is not:
- a serious wound;
- a broken bone;
- magical corruption;
- a Card-related injury;
- a hidden illness;
- an excuse for melodrama.

Nimera is not helpless. She has continued functioning because the injury is manageable.

The scene exists because **manageable does not mean untreated**.

## Opening state
Nimera is either:
- already in the treatment space looking for supplies;
- or brought there after Ilyra notices the way she is using / protecting one hand.

Nimera's initial attitude should be practical:
- she knows it hurts;
- she knows it is minor;
- she has not been pretending she is dying;
- she simply considered other tasks more urgent.

Ilyra does not scold her like a child.

She assesses the situation as another adult who has seen people work through minor injuries and knows when it is worth fixing the problem before it becomes worse.

---

## Consent is the core relationship action
The central Ilyra beat is simple:
> **she asks before touching Nimera.**

That applies even though:
- the injury is obvious;
- the treatment is routine;
- Ilyra is competent;
- Nimera came into the medical space voluntarily.

Ilyra should ask / confirm before things such as:
- taking Nimera's hand;
- removing an existing wrap;
- pressing around the sore area;
- moving the wrist through a range of motion;
- cleaning a scrape;
- applying a new wrap.

Do not turn every tiny movement into a legal disclaimer.

The point is a natural pattern:
> **Ilyra explains what she intends to do, gives Nimera the choice, then does it once Nimera agrees.**

### Why it matters
The scene should not stop to explain this as a moral lesson.

Its meaning comes from behavior.

Nimera notices that Ilyra does not assume access to her body merely because she is helping.

That matters to Nimera without requiring Nimera to disclose a traumatic reason why it matters.

---

## Nimera's response
Nimera should respond like herself:
- direct;
- observant;
- not embarrassed by basic medical care;
- willing to correct inaccurate assumptions;
- willing to say exactly where something hurts;
- willing to tell Ilyra if pressure is too much / too little;
- curious about what Ilyra is checking;
- potentially amused by how deliberately Ilyra asks permission.

She should not become shy, fragile, or suddenly confessional.

Her trust is visible because:
- she answers honestly;
- she lets Ilyra examine the injury;
- she does not feel the need to wrest control back from the process;
- she allows the treatment to finish.

---

## Precision humor
The humor should come from **two precise people becoming increasingly precise with each other**.

Good structural territory:
- Ilyra asks where it hurts; Nimera gives an almost absurdly exact answer;
- Ilyra asks for a simpler version; Nimera makes the answer only slightly simpler;
- Ilyra explains how much she is going to move the wrist; Nimera corrects the implied angle / direction;
- Nimera critiques the tension of the wrap with technical specificity;
- Ilyra dryly points out that if Nimera can argue about bandage tension at that level, the hand is probably still attached;
- Nimera asks whether that is an official diagnostic standard;
- Ilyra can say something equally dry in response.

Do not write the exact jokes yet.

The Agent-Brain dialogue pass should generate the actual exchange.

### Humor boundary
The comedy should never mock:
- consent;
- pain;
- weakness;
- trauma;
- either woman's competence.

They are teasing because both understand the situation is low stakes.

---

## Ilyra's care style
Ilyra's care should be practical rather than maternal or therapeutic.

She can:
- clean / rewrap the hand;
- test motion carefully;
- tell Nimera what movements to avoid for a short while;
- recommend changing the wrap later;
- leave additional supplies within reach;
- tell Nimera what signs would mean she actually needs to stop using the hand.

She should not:
- demand Nimera explain her childhood;
- ask what she is "really feeling";
- interpret the injury as a metaphor;
- insist Nimera admit she needs people;
- make a speech about friendship.

The care itself is enough.

---

## Nimera's competence remains intact
The scene must preserve that Nimera is highly capable.

She may already have:
- cleaned the injury once;
- wrapped it enough to keep working;
- correctly identified that it was not a fracture;
- avoided obviously harmful use.

Ilyra's contribution is that she is better positioned / trained / practiced at proper field treatment and can do a cleaner assessment and wrap than Nimera can easily do one-handed.

This avoids the dynamic:
> "smart scholar cannot perform basic self-care until competent adult rescues her."

Instead the dynamic is:
> **Nimera managed it. Ilyra can make it better. Nimera lets her.**

---

## Quiet trust beat
The scene should contain one small moment where the joking drops naturally.

Not a confession.

Possible structural form:
- Ilyra pauses before a more uncomfortable part of the examination and asks again;
- Nimera answers immediately and gives permission;
- Ilyra does exactly what she said she would do;
- nothing bad or dramatic happens;
- Nimera relaxes slightly / stops watching Ilyra's hands so closely / returns attention to the conversation.

The scene does not need to call attention to this.

The audience can understand it.

That is the emotional center of H03.

---

## Relationship outcome
By the end of H03:
- Ilyra and Nimera are not suddenly best friends;
- they understand each other's rhythms better;
- Nimera knows Ilyra will help without taking control away from her;
- Ilyra knows Nimera will state what she needs clearly if given room to do so;
- their shared dry precision becomes a viable future humor / friendship rhythm;
- medical / care interactions between them can feel more natural later without repeating this exact lesson.

The scene should leave a sense of:
> **quiet, adult trust beginning to settle in.**

## Recommended scene rhythm
1. Nimera is using / looking for supplies or Ilyra notices the minor hand / wrist problem.
2. Short practical exchange establishes that the injury is minor but worth treating.
3. Ilyra offers to examine it.
4. Ilyra explicitly asks before touching / moving Nimera's hand.
5. Nimera agrees.
6. Examination begins.
7. Precision humor develops around pain description / movement / wrap tension / technical specificity.
8. Ilyra identifies the practical treatment needed.
9. Brief quiet consent / trust beat before a mildly uncomfortable step.
10. Ilyra cleans / rewraps / stabilizes the injury.
11. Nimera receives concise aftercare instructions and likely argues with at least one overly cautious part of them.
12. Ilyra gives ground where reasonable and holds firm where needed.
13. Nimera accepts the final result.
14. Scene ends on a small dry exchange or ordinary action rather than an emotional speech.

---

## Ending image / state
The scene should end small.

Good structural endpoint:
- Nimera flexes / tests the newly wrapped hand within Ilyra's allowed range;
- it is noticeably more comfortable / secure;
- Ilyra puts away the remaining supplies or leaves an extra wrap nearby;
- Nimera goes back to whatever she intended to do, with one practical limitation she has agreed to respect;
- neither woman announces that they have become closer.

The audience should simply feel that they have.
