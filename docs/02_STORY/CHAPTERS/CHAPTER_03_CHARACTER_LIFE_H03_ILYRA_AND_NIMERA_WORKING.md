## Current direction
H03 is simply a **fun Character-Life scene between Ilyra and Nimera being themselves together at Cresthaven**.

The previous mandatory treatment-room / minor-injury / consent-centered structure is retired.

Do not force the scene to begin from:
- an injury;
- medical treatment;
- a lesson about consent;
- a trust exercise;
- trauma disclosure;
- a plot problem;
- a lore discussion.

The Agent-Brain system should be allowed to find a natural low-stakes situation that these two women would actually fall into during the Chapter-3 Cresthaven cleanup window.

---

## Scene purpose
The scene only needs to accomplish a few things:
- give **Ilyra and Nimera** dedicated time together outside combat and investigation pressure;
- let their actual personalities determine what they talk about, argue about, laugh at, or do;
- establish their emerging chemistry through behavior rather than a predetermined relationship lesson;
- give Nimera more life inside the permanent party after joining in Chapter 3;
- let Ilyra be an adult woman with humor, opinions, irritation, curiosity, profanity, warmth, and ordinary interests rather than reducing her to the party caretaker;
- be entertaining enough to justify being optional Character-Life content.

There is no required emotional revelation.

If the scene ends with nothing more profound than the two of them having enjoyed each other's company, that is a successful H03.

---

## Agent-Brain freedom
When H03 is generated, the Ilyra and Nimera agents should be given the current Cresthaven state and allowed to find the premise organically.

Possible low-stakes material may emerge from things such as:
- something one of them is doing around Cresthaven;
- an object, book, meal, room, piece of equipment, habit, or annoyance;
- Nimera's curiosity colliding with Ilyra's dry practicality;
- Ilyra discovering something unexpectedly funny about Nimera;
- Nimera discovering something unexpectedly funny about Ilyra;
- them disagreeing about something completely unimportant;
- one of them asking the other a normal personal question;
- casual teasing;
- shared boredom;
- an accidental competition;
- a mundane Cresthaven problem that becomes funnier than it deserves to be.

These are possibilities, **not required beats**.

The agents may find something better.

## Character guardrails
### Ilyra
Keep her current identity:
- mature and socially capable;
- practical;
- merciful without becoming saintly;
- capable of dry humor, teasing, swearing, irritation, and playfulness;
- not a therapist;
- not automatically the responsible mother of every room;
- comfortable allowing silence when nothing needs to be said.

### Nimera
Keep her current identity:
- highly intelligent and technically precise;
- curious;
- blunt without being socially nonfunctional;
- capable of swearing, joking, teasing, and enjoying absurdity;
- not helpless;
- not an emotionless exposition machine;
- willing to revise her opinion when evidence changes;
- still young enough at 22 to have energy, preferences, petty opinions, and fun.

### Their chemistry
Do not decide in advance that their relationship must be about care, mentorship, or vulnerability.

Let the interaction reveal what is actually enjoyable about putting these two personalities together.

They may be:
- surprisingly compatible;
- mildly competitive;
- dryly antagonistic about something trivial;
- mutually curious;
- amused by each other;
- some mixture of the above.

The Agent-Brain rehearsal should determine the best version.

## Dialogue-engine handoff
The Agent-Brain rehearsal-first pipeline owns the actual scene discovery.

Generation brief:
> Put Ilyra and Nimera together during ordinary downtime at Cresthaven. Let them behave like themselves. Find a low-stakes premise that gives them something genuinely fun to react to. Do not force injury, treatment, trauma, consent instruction, lore, or an emotional breakthrough. Let humor, disagreement, curiosity, silence, profanity, affection, or absurdity arise naturally from the two character brains. Once the scene has found its point and the interaction lands, get out.

Dialogue rules:
- adult, natural JRPG / anime timing;
- no obligation for every thought to be spoken;
- silence and physical behavior count;
- no forced sentimental ending;
- no predetermined moral;
- no historical H03 wording is exact authority;
- the resulting dialogue remains working / rehearsal-first until explicitly approved by the user.

H03 succeeds if, after watching it, the player feels:

Nothing more complicated is required.
