**Purpose:** open Chapter 4 with a grounded local problem, establish the restored fort's ecological consequence, and move the party into the field without a recap briefing.

### Opening trigger
- automatic opening the morning after the player starts Chapter 4;
- Torren urgently wakes Cyanis because a very large creature crossed or approached the occupied grounds;
- Torren has already begun checking the physical disturbance;
- Ilyra and Nimera are gathering / joining the response;
- no worker or resident is missing;
- no one is confirmed badly hurt;
- no Black Host, Ancient-system, Prime, or Last Sentinel theory is raised at the wake-up.

### Disturbed grounds

Mandatory physical evidence:

- its path leads toward the forest;
- movement/marking reads as territorial rather than a deliberate attack on people;
- older range signs exist;
- Cresthaven restoration/reoccupation has disturbed or blocked part of its habitual range.

### Handoff
Objective changes directly to:
> **Follow the Elder Thornhide's trail**

No additional headquarters briefing occurs.
