# Regional Hunts #1–#3 — Chapter Cleanup Access Lock

**Status:** ACTIVE STORY / ACCESS AUTHORITY  
**Applies to:** Regional Hunt #1 — Cistern Devourer; Regional Hunt #2 — Transfer Executioner; Regional Hunt #3 — Archive Judgment Engine

## Purpose
The first three Regional Hunts are **optional combat / exploration content**, not mini-story quests.

Story authority does **not** need to build a separate multi-scene narrative around each Hunt.

The required authored story layer is only:
1. establish the physical entrance / return point during or before the chapter;
2. during that chapter's end-of-chapter cleanup window, make it explicit that the Hunt route is now available;
3. make sure the player knows where to go;
4. allow the player to enter the additional Hunt area / branch;
5. area exploration leads to the Hunt boss;
6. boss victory provides the Hunt rewards under reward / item authority.

The basic model is:
> **cleanup unlock / access notice → known entrance → additional area / branch → Hunt boss → rewards**

Do not add story complexity merely because the Hunt is optional content.

---

## Dialogue rule
Hunts #1–#3 should use **minimal dialogue**.

The unlock may be communicated through:
- a short natural NPC update when one already exists;
- a cleanup interaction;
- a Hunt / quest notification;
- a map / route marker;
- or a combination of those methods.

Do not create a long briefing, investigation subplot, boss lore monologue, or post-boss debrief unless later explicit story authority requires one.

Inside the Hunt area, dialogue can be sparse or absent. Characters may make brief natural observations if needed, but the Hunt does not require authored story scenes between the entrance and the boss.

# Regional Hunt #1 — Cistern Devourer

## Chapter timing
Available during the **Chapter 1 cleanup window** after the Wayfinder Junction material has been resolved and the chapter's cleanup state is active.

## Entrance / location
The Hunt entrance is in:
> **southern Briar Passage**

This is the previously established Briar-side cistern / lower-access point.

The physical location may be encountered / established before it becomes useful.

Information gained at **Wayfinder Junction** is what makes the earlier southern-Briar access meaningful / returnable in cleanup.

### Player-facing cleanup requirement
During Chapter 1 cleanup, the game must make clear that:
- the southern Briar Passage cistern access is now a valid return destination;
- the Hunt route is now open / usable;
- the player knows where on the regional route to return.

The exact mechanism by which the entrance becomes usable does not require a separate story scene unless implementation needs one.

### Hunt flow
> southern Briar Passage entrance → additional cistern Hunt area → **Cistern Devourer** → rewards

No separate Hunt storyline is required.

# Regional Hunt #2 — Transfer Executioner

## Chapter timing
Available during the **Chapter 2 cleanup window** after the Old Bastion story has been resolved and Dunmere cleanup is active.

## Entrance / location
The Hunt entrance is the previously established:
> **sealed side-branch door in the Old Waterworks**

The party physically sees this door during mandatory Chapter 2, but it cannot be opened at that time.

### Player-facing cleanup requirement
During Chapter 2 cleanup, the game must explicitly communicate that:
- the previously sealed Old Waterworks branch is now accessible / open;
- this is the same door the party encountered during the mandatory Waterworks route;
- the player can return through the Old Waterworks to reach the newly available Hunt branch.

The notification can come through a brief Dunmere / Waterworks update, a cleanup interaction, Hunt notification, or route marker. Exact dialogue is not required at story-structure level.

Do not turn the opening of the branch into a new Chapter 2 mystery or quest chain.

### Hunt flow
> Old Waterworks sealed-door return point → additional Hunt branch → **Transfer Executioner** → rewards

No separate Hunt storyline is required.

# Regional Hunt #3 — Archive Judgment Engine

## Chapter timing
Available during the **Chapter 3 Cresthaven cleanup window**.

## Entrance / location
The Hunt entrance is:
> **the newly accessible side passage / branch in the Old City Archives beneath Caelora**

## Player-facing cleanup requirement
This Hunt already has an explicit natural unlock in Beat 15:
- Mirena tells the party that continuing Crown work in the Old City Archives has uncovered / opened a previously inaccessible passage;
- she tells them where it is and that they may return to investigate it;
- Mirena does not know what waits inside and does not name the Archive Judgment Engine in-world.

This is sufficient story setup.

No additional Hunt investigation narrative is needed after the party returns to the Archives.

### Hunt flow
> Old City Archive new passage → additional Archive Hunt branch → **Archive Judgment Engine** → rewards

No separate Hunt storyline is required.

---

## Area-design rule
Each Hunt should still have enough physical space to feel like **additional optional content**, not a boss sitting directly behind a door.

Area design may determine:
- room / path count;
- side paths;
- treasure placement;
- environmental obstacles;
- ordinary encounters;
- shortcuts;
- visual identity;
- boss arena approach.

Those are area / encounter-design questions, not additional story beats.

---

## Reward rule
Exact Hunt rewards remain under item / equipment / reward-placement authority.

Do not prematurely lock:
- exact equipment;
- exact item names;
- exact materials;
- exact currency amounts;
- exact treasure-table contents;

until the current item / equipment taxonomy and reward-placement sequencing permits it.

The only current structural requirement is:
> **the player receives worthwhile optional-Hunt rewards after defeating the boss.**

---

## Story firewall
Regional Hunts #1–#3 should not become alternate sources for major mandatory story revelations.

In particular, Hunt #3 must not solve:
- recent-reader identity;
- seal-copying practitioner identity;
- Calder's hidden role;
- the royal-seal mechanism / culprit;
- `PREVIOUS ERROR`;
- Last Sentinel recovery / awakening;
- why the Yahtrean Crest appears on Ancient material;
- Cresthaven's complete ancient purpose;
- the ultimate northern-route destination.

Likewise Hunts #1 and #2 should not introduce mandatory lore that a player would miss by skipping the Hunt.

Optional environmental flavor is allowed, but **main-story comprehension must never depend on completing these Hunts**.

---

## Lock summary
For Regional Hunts #1–#3, the production requirement is intentionally simple:

> **Establish the entrance. At the chapter-end cleanup window, tell / show the player that it is now open and where it is. Let the player explore the additional area, defeat the boss, and collect the rewards.**

Do not build separate Hunt story arcs unless explicitly requested later.
