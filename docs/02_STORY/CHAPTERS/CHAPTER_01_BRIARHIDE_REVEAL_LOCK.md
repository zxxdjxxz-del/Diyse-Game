# Chapter 1 — Briarhide Reveal Lock

**Status:** CURRENT EXPLICIT USER LOCK  
**Domain:** Chapter 1 story/reveal timing  
**Overrides:** any older Chapter-1 wording that places a clear Thornhide sighting earlier in Southern Briar.

## Hard rule

> **The player does not clearly see the Thornhide until the party is about to fight it.**

Beat 12 may establish that a large Briarhide is active in Southern Briar through restrained indirect information, such as the already-approved single track and Torren's identification of it.

Beat 12 must **not** include:
- a clear on-field Stalker sighting;
- the Briarhide stepping onto the route and then leaving;
- a stalking/circling sequence;
- a pre-boss visual encounter separated from the actual boss confrontation;
- dialogue that assumes the party has already seen the Stalker.

## Beat transition

Beat 12 carries the party through Southern Briar toward the final stretch before the Junction and ends without revealing the animal itself.

Beat 13 begins at the actual boss approach. The Thornhide is first clearly shown there, immediately before the authored boss encounter.

The reveal should be concise:
1. Stalker appears / blocks the usable route;
2. short character reaction if needed;
3. Maevra remains outside combat;
4. boss combat begins.

Do not create a separate cinematic pursuit or second pre-fight encounter.

## Creature identity

The Thornhide remains exactly what current Chapter-1 authority establishes:
- a naturally occurring Briar Passage wild beast;
- exceptionally strong;
- not corrupted, controlled, armored, mechanically altered, or suffering from a special condition;
- defeated through an ordinary authored boss fight.
