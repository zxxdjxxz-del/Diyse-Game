# Chapter 1 — Maevra Broken Arm Lock

**Status:** CURRENT EXPLICIT USER LOCK  
**Domain:** Chapter 0 aftermath / Chapter 1 character state  
**Overrides:** any older Chapter-1 presentation that leaves Maevra's noncombat travel state unexplained or depicts her as physically battle-ready.

## Hard continuity

> **Maevra Solmar's arm was broken during the Broken Convoy attack before Chapter 1 begins.**

The exact fracture moment does not need a new playable Chapter-0 beat. It occurred during the broader convoy attack/recovery crisis before the Chapter-1 Brackenwall opening.

By the beginning of Chapter 1:
- the arm has already been set;
- Maevra is wearing a practical field splint;
- the injury remains painful and materially limits combat use of the arm;
- this is the immediate in-world reason Maevra travels with the group but does not fight during Chapter 1;
- the global Maevra non-playability lock remains unchanged. The injury is a story-state explanation for Chapter 1, not a temporary guest-party rule.

Do **not** invent left/right laterality unless separately locked.

## Ilyra / healing-magic limit

Ilyra may maintain the injury in ordinary Warden/medical ways:
- checking the arm;
- changing or re-securing the splint;
- making sure swelling/pressure has not made the fit unsafe;
- using restrained healing/Grace magic to ease pain and make the injury more tolerable.

Hard limit:

> **Healing magic can ease Maevra's broken arm, but it cannot mend the broken bone itself.**

Do not turn this into a lecture about the magic system. One natural line or the visible persistence of the splint is enough to establish the limit.

## Chapter-1 presentation

### Beat 1 — Brackenwall
Maevra's first Chapter-1 appearance should establish the injury clearly and economically:
- she arrives already splinted;
- dialogue can identify that it happened during the convoy attack;
- Ilyra can state that she will change/check the splint before departure;
- Maevra can make clear that she is traveling but not fighting.

### Beat 2 — Briar Passage
Do not re-explain the injury. The splint remains part of Maevra's visible Chapter-1 state whenever she appears in a triggered scene. Her portrait-dialogue route guidance remains fully valid.

### Beat 3 — Greenhollow / Torren
Torren may naturally notice the splint because this is his first Chapter-1 meeting with Maevra. A short exchange is enough. Do not turn it into a medical scene.

### Later Chapter 1
The splint remains through Hollow Watch, Southern Briar, the Junction, and Junction camp unless later authority explicitly changes recovery timing.

The injury should not be verbally re-established every scene. Once Beat 1 makes it clear, the visible splint and Maevra's continued noncombat state carry continuity.

## Character-Life use

The Junction-camp Character-Life scene involving Ilyra and Maevra is an appropriate place for Ilyra to **change the splint and use a small amount of magic to ease the pain** while the characters talk about something ordinary.

The medical action should create the situation, not swallow the scene. The characters do not need to discuss Maevra's injury at length; it is an ordinary ongoing problem they are living with.
