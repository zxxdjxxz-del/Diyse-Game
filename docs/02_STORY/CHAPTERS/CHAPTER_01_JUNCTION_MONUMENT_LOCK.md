# Chapter 1 — The Junction / Hidden Monument Lock

**Status:** CURRENT EXPLICIT USER DIRECTION  
**Domain:** Chapter 1 story / Torren knowledge / mural-to-Junction continuity

## Hard correction

The location previously spoken of in Chapter-1 dialogue as `Wayfinder` is called **the Junction** by Torren and the party at this stage.

Torren knows the place as an **ancient crossroads** on the modern southern route network. He has been through / around the crossroads before and knows where it is, but he has **never seen the actual ancient cartographic monument hidden there**.

## Hollow Watch mural

The protected Hollow Watch mural must show:
- recognizable regional geography;
- Hollow Watch's own hill;
- the ancient crossroads farther south that Torren recognizes as **the Junction**;
- a **large, clearly important monument positioned at those crossroads**;
- the monument is visually distinct from the multiple deep tunnel entrances shown elsewhere in the mural;
- no crowd is shown descending beneath the monument and it is not presented as another tunnel entrance.

Torren recognizes the **location / crossroads**, not the monument itself.

He must not say he has seen the monument before, must not recognize its exact shape from firsthand experience, and must not call it `Wayfinder` in Chapter-1 dialogue before the later discovery establishes what the hidden stone actually is.

## Arrival at the Junction

When the party later reaches the Junction:
- the ancient crossroads are familiar to Torren as a place / route feature;
- the important monument seen in the mural is not immediately obvious in the present-day landscape;
- its stone surface is heavily covered by **vines and moss**;
- the party physically pulls / clears the vegetation away;
- only then do they reveal the preserved **map / cartographic surface** on the monument;
- this is the first time Torren actually sees the monument's map surface.

The reveal should feel like the mural told them **something important exists at the crossroads**, while the present-day vegetation explains why Torren and ordinary travelers never realized what was there.

## Knowledge rule

Torren's valid Chapter-1 knowledge is:
- where the Junction is;
- that it is an old / ancient crossroads;
- how the modern route passes through or near it;
- local road and terrain conditions.

Torren does **not** know before the vegetation is cleared:
- the hidden monument's map surface;
- its ancient cartographic function;
- its technical grammar;
- why the mural emphasizes it;
- how it relates to the wider Diysean route system.

## Naming

In current Chapter-1 character dialogue, use **the Junction** for the place.

`Wayfinder` may remain an internal / legacy production identifier where changing filenames or IDs would be disruptive, but it must not imply that Torren or the party already know the hidden monument by that name or have previously seen its exposed cartographic stone.
