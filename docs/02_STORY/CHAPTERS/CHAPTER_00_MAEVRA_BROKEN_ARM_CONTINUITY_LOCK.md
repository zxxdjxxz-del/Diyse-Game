# Chapter 0 / Chapter 1 — Maevra Broken-Arm Continuity Lock

**Status:** CURRENT HARD CONTINUITY LOCK  
**Scope:** Broken Convoy attack → Chapter 1  
**Applies to:** Chapter 0 story/dialogue, Chapter 1 story/dialogue, Maevra Solmar character continuity

## Hard continuity

- **Maevra Solmar's arm is broken during the Broken Convoy attack before Chapter 1 begins.**
- The injury occurs during the wider convoy crisis, outside Cyanis's immediate required Chapter-0 playable/dialogue sequence.
- Chapter 0 does **not** need to add a new mandatory Maevra scene merely to show the fracture happening.
- Maevra is not added to Cyanis's Chapter-0 combat party because of this continuity.
- By the time Maevra appears in Chapter 1, the arm is already in a practical field splint.
- The fracture is the immediate in-world reason Maevra can continue traveling/commanding but remains **noncombat** through Chapter 1.
- Ilyra can change/re-secure the splint and use restrained Warden/healing magic to reduce pain and surrounding strain.
- **Magic cannot mend the broken bone.** The fracture must heal normally over time.
- Do not repeatedly explain the injury after it is established. The visible splint and Maevra's noncombat state carry most of the continuity.
- The injured arm side remains unspecified until explicitly locked.

## Chapter-0 presentation rule

The injury is writer-facing continuity during the Broken Convoy sequence. If Maevra is visible anywhere in wider convoy/background staging, do not create a bespoke injury cinematic, dialogue detour, or combat beat solely to explain the fracture. The current mandatory Chapter-0 focus remains Cyanis, the convoy crisis, Ilyra's introduction, the two Card flares, and the final defense.

## Chapter-1 establishment

Chapter 1 Beat 1 is the first required spoken establishment:
- Maevra arrives already splinted;
- Cyanis notices the arm;
- Maevra states that it is broken;
- Ilyra connects it to the convoy and plans to change/re-secure the splint;
- Maevra makes clear that she is traveling but not fighting.

Torren may naturally notice the splint when he first sees Maevra in Greenhollow, but the story should not turn the injury into a repeated exposition topic.
