## Core lock

The **Authority Construct is a large Diysean construct** encountered at the end of the short Ancient tower-base dungeon beneath/within Cresthaven.

When the party enters the Authority Chamber:
1. the Authority Construct activates;
2. it visibly **assesses the entire party**;
3. the assessment is part of its Ancient magical function;
4. the result is unacceptable/insufficient under an unknown old condition;
5. the Authority Construct attacks.

The party does not yet know exactly what it is looking for, what standard it applies, or which member/object/state caused its conclusion.

No digital scan/UI/terminal language.
