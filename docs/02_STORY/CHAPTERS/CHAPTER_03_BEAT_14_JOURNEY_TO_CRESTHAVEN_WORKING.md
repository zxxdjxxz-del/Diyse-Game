## Beat purpose
Beat 14 is no longer a playable journey to Cresthaven.

It should:
1. begin immediately after Mirena identifies the Crest-marked northern location as probably **Cresthaven**;
2. have Mirena tell the party to **rest for the night and leave the next morning**;
3. establish that Mirena will follow separately with Maevra and a Crown support / work group later the next day;
4. give Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren, and Nimera a real **rest / decompression scene** after the Old City sequence;
5. avoid turning that rest scene into another exposition dump or investigation meeting;
6. move to the next morning with the party restored and able to prepare in Caelora;
7. let the player decide when they are ready to leave town;
8. make the **leave-for-Cresthaven choice** the end of Beat 14;
9. skip the road journey itself;
10. hand directly to Beat 15, which opens **outside Cresthaven** after the travel cut.

Beat 14 is:
> **Mirena gives the plan → party rests → overnight decompression → next-morning preparation → player chooses to leave Caelora.**

Exact dialogue remains deferred to the Agent-Brain rehearsal-first Dialogue Engine.

## Mirena's plan — do not leave tonight
Mirena should explicitly stop the party from turning around and marching north immediately after the debrief.

Her practical direction is:
- they have already spent a long stretch in the Old City;
- they need food, sleep, treatment, equipment checks, and a clear head;
- **rest tonight**;
- leave for Cresthaven **the next morning** when they are ready;
- Mirena will organize the Crown-side movement separately;
- she will bring Maevra and a proper support / work team to Cresthaven **later the next day**.

This is not a military order barked at them.

It is Mirena acting sensibly after asking them to take on a dangerous investigation for her.

### Party-state rule

- Cyanis;
- Ilyra;
- Torren;
- Nimera.

Mirena and Maevra do not become temporary combat members.

## The rest scene
Beat 14 should contain a genuine authored rest scene before the next-morning departure state.

The exact lodging can remain implementation-flexible unless later location authority locks it. It may be:
- Crown-provided guest rooms;
- a secure inn / lodging already available to the party;
- another ordinary Caeloran place suitable for an overnight stay.

Do not turn the location itself into a new lore beat.

### Scene purpose
The rest scene should let the party:
- eat;
- clean up after the Old City;
- tend ordinary injuries / exhaustion;
- settle gear;
- decompress from the Warden fight and seal-copying discovery;
- exist together now that Nimera is genuinely part of the traveling group.

The scene may acknowledge:
- the Warden's final `LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED` message;
- the deep-Ruby Card state;
- Mirena's concern;
- Cresthaven as tomorrow's destination.

But it should **not** attempt to solve any of those things.

### Tone
This is one of the first chances in the chapter to let the new four-person party breathe.

Use:
- natural fatigue;
- practical care;
- small jokes / irritation / banter where organic;
- ordinary eating / cleaning / getting ready for sleep;
- silence where silence is better.

Do not force:
- a giant emotional confession;
- everyone taking turns summarizing their feelings;
- another technical explanation from Nimera;
- another Mirena briefing;
- a Card manifestation;
- a surprise attack simply because the party stopped moving.

### Dialogue rule
Use the global contract:
> Talk like people. React like anime characters. Time jokes like a comedy. Structure important scenes like a great RPG. Remember they are living through a war. And occasionally let them argue about absolutely nothing.

Exact dialogue belongs to the later Agent-Brain pass.
