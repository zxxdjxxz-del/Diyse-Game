She:
- tells the party where it is;
- says it leads into a deeper/previously inaccessible Archive branch;
- does not know exactly what is inside;
- thinks it is worth investigating because of what the party already found.

Mirena does not tell the party that an Archive Judgment Engine specifically waits inside.

The Hunt name may appear through the game's Hunt/quest interface after the conversation as implementation requires.
