# Chapter 3 — Cresthaven Archive Hunt Unlock Lock

**Status:** ACTIVE CHAPTER-3 STORY / CLEANUP CORRECTION  
**Applies to:** Beat 15 — Cresthaven Headquarters / Cleanup Window  
**Cleanup content affected:** Regional Hunt #3 — Archive Judgment Engine

## Purpose
Regional Hunt #3 must have an explicit in-story unlock rather than simply appearing in the cleanup menu after Cresthaven is established.

The unlock information comes directly from **Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth during the Cresthaven headquarters handoff**.

---

## Mandatory unlock scene beat
During Beat 15, after Mirena has explained that the party may use Cresthaven as headquarters while helping her investigate, and before Mirena and Maevra return to Caelora, Mirena gives the party one additional investigation update.

She tells them that the Crown's continuing work in the **Old City Archives beneath Caelora** has uncovered / made accessible a **previously unavailable passage**.

The important player-facing information is:
- this passage was **not accessible during the party's mandatory Old City investigation**;
- Crown archivists / workers only discovered or opened access to it afterward while continuing to inspect and stabilize the Archive;
- the route leads into a deeper / previously inaccessible Archive branch;
- Mirena does **not** know exactly what is inside;
- because of everything the party has already found in the Old City, she thinks the passage is worth investigating;
- she tells the party they may return to Caelora and inspect it when they are ready.

This conversation is the **canonical story unlock** for:
> **Regional Hunt #3 — Archive Judgment Engine**

---

## Mirena knowledge boundary
Mirena does **not** tell the party:
- that an `Archive Judgment Engine` specifically waits inside;
- what the boss mechanics are;
- what reward is inside;
- that the passage contains another mandatory Chapter-3 revelation.

She only knows that a **new Archive passage / branch has become accessible** and that it is potentially relevant and dangerous.

The Hunt name may appear through the game's Hunt / quest interface after the conversation as implementation requires, but Mirena should not speak as though she already knows the identity of the encounter at the end of the branch.

---

## Timing inside Beat 15
Recommended placement:
1. party arrives at Cresthaven;
2. Mirena and Maevra are already present with the full work crew;
3. headquarters spaces / cleanup work are shown;
4. Mirena tells the party they may use Cresthaven as headquarters while helping her investigate;
5. Mirena gives the **Old City Archive passage update**;
6. **Regional Hunt #3 unlocks**;
7. remaining practical handoff is completed;
8. Mirena and Maevra return to Caelora;
9. player regains control in the Chapter-3 Cresthaven cleanup window.

The Hunt therefore becomes available at the start of cleanup, not during the mandatory Old City run.

---

## Return-to-Archive rule
After the unlock:
- the playable party is **Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren + Nimera**;
- the player may return to the Old City Archives in Caelora through lawful post-story access;
- the new passage leads to the side branch used for Regional Hunt #3;
- Mirena and Maevra do not accompany the party as combatants or default travelers.

---

## Reveal firewall
The newly opened passage / Hunt must not reveal:
- Calder's hidden transport role;
- the identity of the recent reader;
- the identity of the seal-copying practitioner;
- whether those two are the same person;
- the culprit behind the suspect royal-seal orders;
- the exact mechanism behind the genuine royal magical response;
- whether a duplicate royal seal succeeded;
- the meaning of `PREVIOUS ERROR`;
- the full meaning / recovery of Last Sentinel;
- why the exact Yahtrean Crest appears on Ancient material;
- a mandatory new Chapter-3 main-story answer.

The Archive Judgment Engine remains **cleanup Hunt content**, not an extension of the mandatory Old City story climax.

---

## Lock summary
At Cresthaven, **Mirena explicitly informs the party that a newly accessible passage has been found in the Old City Archives**.

That information is the in-world signal that:
> **Regional Hunt #3 — Archive Judgment Engine is now open.**
