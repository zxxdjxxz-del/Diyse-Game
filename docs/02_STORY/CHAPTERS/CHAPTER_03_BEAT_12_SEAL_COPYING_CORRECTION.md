The practice material should tell a story through physical evidence.

This makes the room feel like somebody was learning / refining the craft rather than randomly stamping symbols.

The party should be able to see that the person was working toward the royal seal deliberately.

## Physical copying versus magical reproduction
Beat 12 must clearly separate two different achievements.

### Copying the visible seal
A sufficiently skilled engraver / scribe / sealwright can potentially reproduce:
- the shape;
- proportions;
- linework;
- physical impression;
- visual appearance of the Crest of Yahtrea.

That is difficult craft, but not the central impossibility.

### Reproducing the royal magical response
The Queen's genuine royal seal carries a recognized magical authority response.

Reproducing **that** is another matter entirely.

Current Beat-12 conclusion:
- doing so would require **extremely powerful magic**;
- the copied attempts in the chamber show evidence that substantial magical force was being used;
- some attempts may show partial magical residue or failed binding work;
- none of the evidence proves that a copied seal ever reproduced the genuine royal authority response;
- the party / Nimera knows of **no established historical case** in which anyone successfully reproduced the Queen's / Yahtrean royal seal's true magical response through a duplicate.

The distinction is critical:
> **Someone was practicing how to copy the royal seal. That does not prove they succeeded in copying its magic.**

## Relationship to Queen Lysara's impossible orders
This discovery should **increase** the mystery rather than solve it.

The party already knows:
- the suspect transport orders carry the genuine magical response associated with Queen Lysara's personal royal seal;
- Lysara did not authorize them;
- the actual seal remained in Crown custody with no obvious theft-and-return explanation.

Beat 12 adds:
- someone has been practicing how to copy the visible royal seal;
- someone has been attempting magical work around those copies;
- reproducing the true royal response appears to require tremendous magical power;
- there is still no evidence that anyone has historically achieved a perfect magical duplicate.

Therefore the correct conclusion is **not**:
> “We found how the orders were forged.”

It is:
> **“Someone has been trying to learn how to reproduce the royal seal, and we still do not know how the suspect orders carry the genuine response.”**

That keeps both major possibilities alive:
- some unknown access / use involving Lysara's actual seal;
- some extraordinary unknown magical method capable of making a copy answer like the real thing.

Neither is proven.
