## Beat 1 — Dunmere / Missing Travelers — legacy S012 REOPENED

**Purpose**
- establish Dunmere as a functioning town being isolated by Black Host activity;
- establish the kidnappings as the Chapter-2 inciting problem;
- establish the Red Transfer Bastion as the immediate obstacle and objective;
- give the party a covert route into the Bastion.

**Starting state**
- party arrives in Dunmere from the Wayfinder / southern-Briar side.
- combat party: Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren.
- Maevra may travel with them but is never a combat participant.
- the larger journey still points toward **Caelora**.

**Required events / discoveries**
- Dunmere is functioning, but travel out of town has become dangerous.
- people leaving on the **Caelora road** have been disappearing.
- locals understand that the **Black Host is kidnapping travelers**, not that people are simply being lost to wilderness danger.
- the direct road toward **Greenhollow** is also unsafe because the Black Host now occupies the **Red Transfer Bastion**, an old fort off / controlling that road.
- a direct assault or normal road approach to the Bastion is judged too dangerous.
- the party meets / is briefed by the **Dunmere town elder**.
- the elder knows an old, little-used **waterworks route that secretly connects into the Bastion's lower structure**.
- the route lets the party bypass the dangerous road approach and enter the Black Host position from below / within.
- the party agrees to use the waterworks route to stop the kidnappings and break the Bastion's control of the roads.

**Character functions**
- **Torren:** evaluates the roads, fort position, and viability of the hidden approach.
- **Cyanis:** commits to acting on the disappearances and threat to travelers.
- **Ilyra:** keeps the missing people / captives as the human priority rather than treating the Bastion only as a military target.
- **Maevra:** reads the Bastion strategically and supports the operation without becoming playable.
- **Town elder:** provides local route/history knowledge the party could not reasonably know on its own.

**End state / knowledge change**
- the Black Host is kidnapping people leaving Dunmere toward Caelora.
- the Red Transfer Bastion is the seized old fort making the Greenhollow road unsafe.
- the Bastion is the immediate Chapter-2 target.
- the party has a covert entry route through the waterworks.
