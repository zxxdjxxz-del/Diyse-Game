- Commandable combat party: **Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren**.
- Chapter 2 is a sustained development chapter for this permanent trio.
- **Maevra remains fully non-playable and non-combat, but physically travels with the group throughout Chapter 2.**
- Maevra remains present through the Dunmere return / inn cleanup period and must not disappear merely because she is not in battle.

- Dunmere was already the party's intended next destination before the Wayfinder discovery.
- Wayfinder established that the modern route they were already following overlaps an older Diysean route alignment.
- The party carries physical copies of useful Wayfinder information.
- People leaving Dunmere toward **Caelora** have been disappearing because the **Black Host is kidnapping travelers**.
- The direct **Greenhollow–Dunmere road** is unsafe because the Black Host occupies the **Red Transfer Bastion**, an old fort positioned to control that corridor.
- The Red Transfer Bastion is the Chapter-2 Bastion; it is not a separate fort/location.
- The Chapter-2 finale breaks the Bastion's local control and reopens direct Greenhollow ↔ Dunmere travel.
- Dunmere is the Chapter-2 cleanup hub.

## Beat 1 — Path Outside Dunmere

**Purpose**
- open Chapter 2 cleanly after the explicit chapter-start confirmation;
- establish immediate continuity from Chapter 1 without replaying the Wayfinder camp departure;
- place the party at Dunmere's threshold with the Ancient-route discovery still fresh but not dominating the scene.

**Starting state**
- the player has chosen to continue toward Dunmere from the Chapter-1 camp and confirmed **Start Chapter 2**;
- Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren, and Maevra are already on the road immediately outside Dunmere;
- combat party: Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren;
- Maevra is present but non-combat;
- the group still carries physical copies of relevant Wayfinder information;
- Caelora remains the larger journey objective.

**Mandatory events**
- Dunmere is visible/near at hand and reached through ordinary short approach gameplay;
- Torren's role as guide remains natural but does not require another long travel sequence;
- no new major Ancient revelation occurs before entering town.

**End state**
- party reaches Dunmere.

**Gameplay handoff**
→ Beat 2 — Dunmere Arrival / Missing Travelers

## Beat 2 — Dunmere Arrival / Missing Travelers

**Purpose**
- establish Dunmere as a functioning town under increasing travel pressure;
- make the missing travelers the immediate human problem;
- identify the Red Transfer Bastion as the military obstacle controlling the corridor.

**Mandatory events / discoveries**
- Dunmere is functioning, not ruined or poisoned;
- people leaving Dunmere toward Caelora have been disappearing;
- local knowledge supports that the Black Host is kidnapping travelers rather than people simply vanishing to wilderness danger;
- the direct Greenhollow–Dunmere road is unsafe because the Black Host has seized the Red Transfer Bastion;
- the larger Caelora journey is temporarily subordinated to the immediate rescue/road problem.

**End state**
- the party knows the kidnappings are active and the Bastion controls the dangerous corridor.

**Gameplay handoff**
→ Beat 3 — Dunmere Elder / Hidden Approach

**Reveal firewall**
Do not reveal Seyrik's identity, later Chapter-3 answers, or the full ancient-route system.

## Beat 3 — Dunmere Elder / Hidden Approach

**Purpose**
- reject a wasteful direct assault;
- give the party local knowledge it could not reasonably possess;
- establish the covert waterworks approach.

**Mandatory events**
- the party meets/is briefed by the Dunmere town elder;
- a normal road assault on the Bastion is judged too dangerous or tactically poor;
- the elder knows an old, little-used waterworks route connected toward the Bastion's lower structure;
- Torren evaluates the route as plausible enough to attempt;
- the party commits to using the waterworks to infiltrate and reach the source of the kidnappings.

**End state**

**Gameplay handoff**
→ Beat 4 — Old Waterworks

**Mandatory progression**

**Purpose**

**Mandatory events**
- the party enters the Sunken Archive;
- surviving preservation architecture, flooded passages, old route structures, defenses/hazards, and archive-scale construction establish that this is not simply part of the waterworks;
- the party connects it broadly to the Diysean structures encountered at Hollow Watch/Wayfinder without yet understanding the whole system.

**End state**

**Gameplay handoff**
→ Beat 6 — Western Map Mural

## Beat 6 — Western Map Mural

**Purpose**
- deliver Chapter 2's major Ancient cartographic payoff;
- reveal what the Wayfinder could not show;
- establish that this is one complete section of a much larger map rather than a damaged fragment.

**Locked mural rules**
- the mural itself is **complete and intact enough to read as a coherent map**;
- it shows the **western half** of a much larger Diysean map/network;
- it is obviously only one section because route lines, geographic alignments, and/or network markings **continue past the physical edges of the mural**;
- the missing information is outside this western section, not lost because the wall is broken;
- this map provides broader context that was absent from the Wayfinder monument;
- the Hollow Watch / Wayfinder / Dunmere corridor can be recognized within the western network;
- the route system continues toward the location occupied by **modern Caelora**;
- at modern Caelora's location, the map depicts a **very large unfamiliar ancient city**, visibly larger than the modern capital;
- the western map does not identify Cresthaven or explain the ancient city's full relationship to modern Caelora;
- some ancient alignments may not fit present terrain cleanly, but the party does not yet know whether this reflects changed geography, underground/non-surface routes, or both;
- the group makes practical copies/records of useful information.

**End state / knowledge change**
- the party now has the western half of the old route picture;
- they know the Wayfinder was only one local piece of a much larger system;
- they know the western network continues beyond the edges of this map and toward the Caelora location;
- the identity/purpose of the huge ancient city and the missing continuation remain unresolved.

**Gameplay handoff**
→ Beat 7 — Archive Depths

**Reveal firewall**
Do not reveal the complete route network, Cresthaven, Last Sentinel, buried-Crest truth, Entity, or why the ancient landscape differs from the modern one.

## Beat 10 — Prisoner Galleries / First Contact

**Purpose**
- turn the kidnapping problem into direct human contact;
- establish the prisoner population as the people being caught in the corridor conflict rather than an anonymous rescue counter;
- reveal through prisoner experience that Red Transfer Bastion functions as a **holding/transit site** before captives are taken elsewhere.

**Locked prisoner composition**
The people currently held in the Prisoner Galleries include a mix of:
- **travelers** taken on the regional roads;
- **injured soldiers** captured or detained by the Black Host;
- **Greenhollow residents**;
- **Dunmere residents**.

Other captives were held here before the current group and have already been transferred elsewhere.

**Mandatory events / knowledge**
- the party enters the Prisoner Galleries unexpectedly from the old secret passage;
- living captives are held across usable holding areas/bays rather than presented as one anonymous objective crowd;
- the prisoners may be startled or cautious at first, but once the party's rescue intent is clear they **do not refuse needed help, treatment, or rescue**;
- **Ilyra still asks consent before examining or treating conscious prisoners**, and assistance is willingly accepted rather than becoming a refusal conflict;
- prisoners explain from direct experience that this Bastion is being used as a **holding area before the Host takes captives away to another location**;
- they know previous prisoners have already been transferred out;
- they **do not know where those people were taken** or the larger purpose of the transfer system;
- the prisoners' immediate fear is practical: they do not believe they can safely escape while the Black Host still controls the Bastion above them;
- the party begins opening/clearing accessible holding areas without turning the scene into collect-all-prisoners gameplay.

**End state**
- living prisoners are found and local control of part of the galleries is broken;
- the prisoners are willing to cooperate with rescue efforts but believe the Bastion must first be defeated/secured enough for an escape to succeed.

**Gameplay handoff**
→ Beat 11 — Prisoner Safe Area / Exit Problem

## Beat 11 — Prisoner Safe Area / Exit Problem

**Purpose**
- create a defensible temporary position for the rescued captives;
- establish why the party cannot simply evacuate everyone back through the route they entered;
- convert the prisoners' fear into a concrete tactical requirement: **break the Black Host's control of the Bastion before attempting the escape**.

**Mandatory events**
- the prisoners gather in a defensible lower safe area while the party prepares to move against the upper Bastion;
- the people inside retain control of their own interior door/bar; the party does not lock them in or take control away from them;
- the secret Archive/waterworks route is judged unsuitable for moving a mixed group that includes injured soldiers, injured/weakened civilians, and ordinary travelers;
- prisoners make clear that trying to flee through an unsecured fort while the Host still controls the Bastion would likely mean recapture, attack, or abandonment of people who cannot move quickly;
- the practical exit toward the surface/direct Greenhollow–Dunmere road is controlled by the Bastion's active command layer;
- the party decides to push upward, defeat/break the Bastion's local Black Host control, seize/open the usable exit route, and then return for the prisoners.

**End state**
- prisoners have a temporary safe position and are waiting for the party to make escape genuinely possible;
- the party's next objective is the Bastion command layer and exit controls.

**Gameplay handoff**
→ Beat 12 — Bastion Command Ascent
