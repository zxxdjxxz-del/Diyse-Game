# Diyse — Card Casing Transition Lock

**Status:** CURRENT EXPLICIT USER DIRECTION  
**Domain:** Chapter 0 → Chapter 1 Card physical-state continuity  
**Authority:** supersedes older references that keep the recovered Card sealed in its casing after the Chapter-0 final boss.

## Hard continuity lock

- Through the early and middle portions of Chapter 0, the recovered Card is transported inside its protective recovery casing / sealed cargo context as already established.
- During **P06 — Riftmaw + Convoy War-Sorcerer / Final Broken Convoy Confrontation**, the **casing breaks**.
- The **Card itself survives intact**.
- By the end of P06, the old protective casing is no longer a usable container.
- From the post-boss portion of Chapter 0 onward, Cyanis / the traveling group are **carrying the Card itself**, not carrying a sealed casing containing it.
- Do not introduce a replacement casing merely to restore the old presentation.
- The Card may remain inert when no scripted response is occurring, but "inert" no longer means "sealed in the casing."

## P06 timing boundary

The casing must break **during the boss fight**. The exact blow, visual beat, or causal mechanism of the break is not locked here unless separately authored and approved. Do not imply that the Card is damaged just because the casing is destroyed.

## P07 / Chapter-0 cleanup correction

Any older wording after P06 such as:
- "sealed Card";
- "sealed artifact";
- "sealed casing";
- "the casing remains dark";

is stale where it describes the Card's current physical state.

After P06, use direct Card language instead:
- "the Card";
- "recovered Card";
- "Card remains inert";
- "Cyanis carries the Card";

The party still does not gain forbidden future knowledge merely because the Card is physically visible.

## Chapter 1 correction

Chapter 1 begins with Cyanis carrying the **Card itself**.

At Brackenwall:
- custody personnel inspect/receive the Card directly;
- the attempted separation/transfer moves the **Card itself** away from Cyanis;
- the abnormal proximity-linked response still occurs;
- no replacement casing is involved;
- no casing knock, casing seam-light, or sealed-container behavior should appear.

This correction applies to Chapter-1 story specs, dialogue drafts, cumulative manuscripts, and future staging.

## Reveal firewall

Breaking the casing does **not** reveal:
- Prime identity;
- Might identity;
- Last Sentinel;
- bearer/ownership truth;
- ancient authorization logic;
- Last Weapon truth;
- Entity truth.

The physical Card can be visible while its deeper identity and function remain unknown.
