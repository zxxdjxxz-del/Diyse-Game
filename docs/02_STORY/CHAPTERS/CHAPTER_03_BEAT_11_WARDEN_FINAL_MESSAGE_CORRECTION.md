**Status:** CURRENT EXPLICIT USER CORRECTION — ACTIVE

## Locked sequence
The **First Command Warden itself** delivers the final message during its shutdown sequence.

The required order is:
1. the party defeats the First Command Warden in combat;
2. the Warden remains barely active for a final failure / shutdown moment;
3. it communicates:
   - **PREVIOUS ERROR**
   - then **LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED**;
4. **LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED is the Warden's final message**;
5. immediately after that final message, the Warden shuts down completely / becomes inert;
6. only after the Warden has gone inert does Cyanis's Card respond and settle into its stable **deep Ruby** state.

## Presentation rule
The final message must belong to the Warden.

Do **not** present `LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED` as:
- lettering newly revealed on a wall after the Warden is already dormant;
- a chamber inscription unrelated to the Warden's final act;
- a separate archive message;
- a terminal / screen / system log.

The Warden may communicate the words through fantasy-appropriate means tied to its own body / wardcraft, for example:
- fading engraved letters across its seal plates;
- magical inscription lighting on its armor as power leaves it;
- a final ancient utterance / resonant magical voice if dialogue staging chooses that later;
- letters appearing in the Warden's own carved or inlaid surfaces during shutdown.

The exact audiovisual implementation belongs to the later staging/dialogue pass, but the **source and timing are locked**:
> **The Warden communicates the message, and `LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED` is the final thing it says before it shuts down.**
