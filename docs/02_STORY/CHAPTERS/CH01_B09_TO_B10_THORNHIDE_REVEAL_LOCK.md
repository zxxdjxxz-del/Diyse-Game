# Chapter 1 — Thornhide Stalker Reveal Lock

**Status:** CURRENT EXPLICIT USER LOCK  
**Domain:** Chapter 1 story/reveal timing  
**Overrides:** any older Chapter-1 wording that places a clear Thornhide sighting earlier in Southern Briar.

## Hard rule

> **The player does not clearly see the Thornhide Stalker until the party is about to fight it.**

Beat 9 may establish that a large **Thornhide** is active in Southern Briar through restrained indirect information, such as the already-approved single track and Torren's species identification. The specific boss identity is **Thornhide Stalker**.

Beat 9 must **not** include:
- a clear on-field Thornhide Stalker sighting;
- the Thornhide stepping onto the route and then leaving;
- a stalking/circling sequence;
- a pre-boss visual encounter separated from the actual boss confrontation;
- dialogue that assumes the party has already seen the Thornhide Stalker.

## Beat transition

Beat 9 carries the party through Southern Briar toward the final stretch before the Junction and ends without revealing the animal itself.

Beat 10 begins at the actual boss approach. The **Thornhide Stalker** is first clearly shown there, immediately before the authored boss encounter.

The reveal should be concise:
1. Thornhide Stalker appears / blocks the usable route;
2. short character reaction if needed;
3. Maevra remains outside combat;
4. boss combat begins.

Do not create a separate cinematic pursuit or second pre-fight encounter.

## Creature identity

The Thornhide Stalker remains exactly what current Chapter-1 authority establishes:
- an exceptionally large member of the naturally occurring Briar Passage **Thornhide** species;
- exceptionally strong;
- not corrupted, controlled, armored, mechanically altered, or suffering from a special condition;
- defeated through an ordinary authored boss fight.
