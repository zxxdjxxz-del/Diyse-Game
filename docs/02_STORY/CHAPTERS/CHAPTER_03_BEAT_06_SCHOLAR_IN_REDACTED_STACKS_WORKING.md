# Chapter 3 — Beat 6 — Scholar in Redacted Stacks

**Status:** CURRENT WORKING BEAT PACKET — DEVELOPED

## Beat purpose
Beat 6 introduces **Nimera Pellan as a person before she becomes a party member**.

It should:
1. pay off Mirena's explicit Old City expert lead;
2. reveal Nimera inside the enclosed deeper archive work section without framing her as helpless;
3. establish her working personality through what she is doing while trapped: observing, testing, cataloguing, arguing with the problem, and continuing useful work;
4. establish her as intelligent, precise, blunt, curious, and comfortable with profanity without turning the introduction into a comedy sketch;
5. let the trio explain why Mirena sent them: the abnormal Card plus controlled evidence from the Queen-seal investigation;
6. establish that the Ancient barrier is genuinely beyond normal Crown archive controls;
7. let Nimera contribute useful observations about the barrier without solving it alone;
8. end with the four characters sharing the same immediate problem and ready to work together in Beat 7.

Beat 6 is **character introduction + problem definition**.

It is not yet:
- the full barrier solution;
- Nimera's permanent recruitment;
- her combat introduction;
- a Prime / Might / Last Sentinel reveal;
- a complete examination of Cyanis's Card.

## Starting state
- Cyanis, Ilyra, and Torren have entered the Old City archives lawfully under Crown authorization;
- **Maevra has remained in Caelora with Mirena** to continue the royal-seal / records investigation;
- the field group is therefore the permanent trio: Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren;
- Cyanis still physically carries the recovered Card;
- the trio has controlled copies / technical evidence Mirena authorized them to show Nimera;
- the Queen's actual personal seal remains secured in the palace;
- Beat 5 established that the Crown meaningfully uses the upper Old City but does not master the whole buried complex;
- the trio has followed archive staff directions into a deeper work route;
- they have reached the correct section where Nimera was working;
- an integrated Ancient security barrier has unexpectedly closed across / around the work area;
- modern archive controls do not simply reopen it;
- signs indicate Nimera is inside the enclosed section.

## Location identity — the redacted stacks
Nimera's work area should feel more specialized and less public than the upper archive.

Useful environmental elements:
- restricted shelves / sealed record containers;
- partially catalogued Ancient material;
- modern labels attached to objects / compartments whose original function is not fully understood;
- portable lamps and temporary work surfaces rather than fully built-in modern infrastructure;
- old wall recesses / mechanical tracks passing behind or through the archive setup;
- Crown redaction / restriction practices applied to modern records where appropriate;
- visibly older structural layers continuing beyond what archivists have adapted.

The title **Scholar in Redacted Stacks** should read as a place where sensitive / incomplete material is being worked through, not as a magical censorship gimmick.

## First contact with Nimera
The trio should not discover Nimera unconscious, injured, panicking, or passively waiting to be saved.

They find evidence of active work first:
- materials have been reorganized around the closure;
- notes / diagrams / measurements are still being made;
- Nimera may be using the enforced pause to continue cataloguing or testing what she can reach;
- she has already tried several reasonable non-destructive approaches to the barrier;
- she has concluded that normal Crown access methods are irrelevant to the mechanism currently holding.

When contact is made, her immediate concern is the problem in front of her, not performing gratitude.

### Introduction tone
Nimera can be irritated by:
- the barrier;
- the timing;
- a mechanism changing state while she was working;
- people asking an imprecise question when she needs a precise one.

She should still read as socially functional and fully capable of cooperating.

Do not make her:
- an antisocial caricature;
- unable to communicate except through technical jargon;
- mysteriously omniscient about Ancient systems;
- childlike because she is the youngest permanent party member;
- helpless because she is temporarily enclosed.

## What Nimera knows about the barrier
Nimera has had enough time to observe it from inside.

She can reasonably establish:
- it is integrated into the Ancient structure rather than a modern Crown security door;
- the closure was triggered by some change in the old system, not by a known archive lock command;
- modern controls are not addressing the mechanism that actually closed it;
- the barrier appears to be responsive rather than simply jammed;
- there are visible / detectable relationships among plates, lines, joints, or response points;
- forcing it physically would risk damaging the structure, records, or route without guaranteeing release.

She cannot yet establish:
- the original purpose of the barrier;
- the exact rule that triggered it;
- that it recognizes Cyanis;
- that modern Crest principles are directly descended from this system;
- that Cyanis has Ancient authority;
- how to release it by herself.

## The trio identifies itself / why they are here
The group does not need a long formal introduction.

The functional information Nimera needs:
- Mirena sent them specifically to find **Nimera Pellan**;
- Cyanis is carrying an abnormal Card that has shown unexplained responsive behavior;
- the Crown is also investigating documents that carry Queen Lysara's genuine magical authority response despite her denial of authorization;
- they have controlled evidence / comparison notes rather than the Queen's physical seal;
- Mirena wants Nimera's expertise because both problems involve magical response under uncertain conditions, not because anyone has proven the Card and seal share a cause.

### Information discipline
Do not dump every Chapter-1/2 event on Nimera in this scene.

She needs enough context to understand:
1. why they came;
2. why Cyanis's Card matters;
3. why the seal-response evidence matters;
4. why they are not simply leaving and returning later.

Full technical examination can wait until the immediate barrier problem is resolved.

## Nimera's reaction to the Card
Nimera should become genuinely interested when she learns what Cyanis is carrying, but Beat 6 should not let that interest erase the barrier situation.

Allowed:
- she asks concise observational questions about color / geometry / when it changed / what conditions seemed to coincide with response;
- she wants to see it properly once they are in the same space;
- she distinguishes what Cyanis actually observed from what people inferred;
- she is interested enough that the audience understands why Mirena chose her.

Not allowed yet:
- Prime classification;
- Might identification;
- Last Sentinel;
- bearer declaration;
- direct physical handling if the barrier prevents it;
- a complete Card theory.

## Nimera's reaction to the seal anomaly
The controlled seal evidence should also matter to her.

Her first-pass reaction can be narrower and more useful than a solution:
- if Crown authentication is correct, the issue is not somebody drawing the Queen's Crest correctly;
- some recognized magical authority response occurred under conditions the Crown says should not have produced it;
- custody evidence eliminates the easiest story but not every physical-access possibility;
- the useful question is what **conditions cause a responsive authority object to answer**, not merely who signed the paper.

She does not identify a culprit or mechanism.

This establishes why she is valuable without solving Beat 12 early.

## Character dynamics in the introduction
### Cyanis
- is the person carrying the object Nimera most wants to examine;
- should answer what he actually observed without trying to impress the scholar;
- may appreciate her refusal to overclaim;
- does not solve the barrier yet.

### Ilyra
- keeps the interaction grounded in safety and consent;
- can notice whether Nimera is actually injured / distressed rather than assuming enclosure means emergency;
- supports non-destructive problem solving;
- helps prevent the scene from becoming two specialists talking past everyone else.

### Torren
- reads the physical setup from the accessible side;
- can compare wear, recent movement, barrier geometry, and practical routes;
- provides exterior observations Nimera cannot make from inside;
- remains comfortable saying when he does not know what a mechanism means.

### Nimera
- contributes interior observations and archive-specific context;
- communicates precisely enough to collaborate;
- is curious about the trio because they have brought two genuinely strange problems to her workplace;
- should feel like someone who was already doing important work before the protagonists arrived.

### Maevra / Mirena
- both remain in Caelora during this beat;
- they do not communicate constant live instructions into the dungeon;
- their absence should be normal and purposeful, not treated as abandonment.

## Why the trio does not simply leave
Once they confirm Nimera is safe enough to communicate, the party still has good reasons to solve the barrier now:
- Nimera is the expert they specifically came to consult;
- she cannot leave her enclosed work section through normal Crown controls;
- the barrier has changed state unexpectedly and may affect access to deeper archive areas;
- forcing the issue later would not make the mechanism easier to understand;
- Nimera herself has useful observations that make a careful joint attempt reasonable.

This is problem solving, not a dramatic rescue obligation.

## No combat requirement
Beat 6 remains noncombat.

The first mandatory hostile machine response is still reserved for Beat 8, after Nimera is physically free and can enter combat by choice.

Do not add:
- enemies attacking while she is trapped;
- a timed oxygen / flooding / crushing hazard;
- a countdown that makes the barrier puzzle feel arbitrary;
- a rescue boss.

The tension comes from the Old City doing something the modern archive does not control.

## Beat ending — a solvable shared problem
By the end of Beat 6, the four have pooled enough observation to know the barrier is not simply a dead lock.

The ending should establish:
- Nimera has identified several Ancient response features / nodes from inside;
- Torren can see physical relationships from the outside she cannot;
- Cyanis is beginning to notice that some structural / geometric relationships feel oddly familiar in the broad sense of modern Crest-work principles;
- nobody yet states that those systems are the same thing;
- Ilyra reinforces that they should test the least destructive interpretation first.

The actual cooperative release belongs to Beat 7.

### Hard handoff rule
Beat 6 should stop **before** Cyanis and Nimera successfully operate the mechanism.

Beat 7 begins with them testing the relationship and learning whether it actually changes the barrier.

## Gameplay / authored-scene rhythm
Recommended Beat-6 rhythm:
1. trio reaches the enclosed work section;
2. signs establish Nimera is active inside rather than helpless;
3. first contact / concise introductions;
4. Nimera explains what she has already ruled out about the barrier;
5. trio explains Mirena's Card / seal reason for seeking her;
6. Nimera asks precise bounded questions about the Card and seal anomaly;
7. party and Nimera compare inside/outside observations of the Ancient barrier;
8. several obvious destructive / modern-control approaches are explicitly rejected;
9. shared pattern begins to emerge without being solved;
10. objective becomes **Open the Ancient barrier**;
11. handoff to Beat 7.

## Reveal firewall
Do **not** reveal in Beat 6:
- Prime classification;
- Might identification;
- Last Sentinel wording / identity;
- stable deep Ruby Card state;
- Prime manifestation / recovery;
- confirmed bearer status for Cyanis;
- proof that modern Crests derive from Diysean systems;
- proof that Cyanis has Ancient authorization;
- the ancient seal-imbuing mechanism;
- the age / lineage of the Queen's present seal;
- who used / invoked Lysara's seal;
- Calder's hidden transport role;
- Cresthaven;
- the northern route destination;
- Nimera's permanent recruitment.

## Beat-6 knowledge checkpoint
By the end:
- the trio has formally met **Nimera Pellan**, the expert Mirena sent them to find;
- Nimera is established as capable, precise, curious, blunt, and actively working despite being enclosed;
- she is not injured or helpless simply because the Ancient barrier has trapped her work section;
- the field group remains Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren; Maevra remains in Caelora with Mirena;
- Nimera understands the bounded reason the trio came: abnormal Card + impossible royal-seal response;
- she has not yet completed a direct physical examination of Cyanis's Card;
- she agrees the seal problem concerns genuine magical response conditions rather than mere visual forgery, without identifying a mechanism or culprit;
- the barrier is confirmed as an Ancient responsive mechanism beyond normal Crown controls;
- Nimera has observations from inside and the trio has complementary observations from outside;
- Cyanis has only begun to notice a broad structural resemblance to principles familiar from Crest work;
- no proof of shared origin / authority has been established;
- the barrier remains closed;
- no combat has occurred;
- the immediate next task is to cooperatively open the Ancient barrier.

Handoff → **Beat 7 — Ancient Barrier / First Cooperation**.
