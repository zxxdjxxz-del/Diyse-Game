## Beat purpose
Beat 13 is no longer `Four Answers, Not One` and is no longer a formal royal debrief with Queen Lysara present.

It should:
1. begin immediately after the player chooses to leave the Beat-12 sealwright chamber;
2. cut directly upstairs in Caelora to a private meeting with **Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth**;
3. keep **Queen Lysara out of this scene**;
4. let Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren, and Nimera explain what they found in the Old City in a coherent sequence;
5. allow Maevra to be physically present again if useful, because she remained in Caelora with Mirena during the field investigation;
6. make Mirena understand that the threat is no longer just forged paperwork or one strange Card;
7. establish Mirena's working concern that **someone with meaningful access and unusually high magical power is behind at least part of what is happening**;
8. preserve uncertainty about the culprit, exact method, and whether the seal-copying practitioner actually succeeded;
9. have Mirena actively recruit / ask the party to continue helping her investigate;
10. return attention to the **existing copied Ancient map / mural evidence** already carried by the party;
11. have the party show Mirena the map with the exact **Yahtrean Crest** marked on the northern site;
12. let Mirena be visibly confused by the Crest appearing on Ancient material but tentatively recognize the location as **Cresthaven**;
13. make Cresthaven the practical next lead and hand into Beat 14.

Beat 13 is:
> **private report to Mirena → threat becomes personal and political → Mirena recruits the party → old map returns → Cresthaven lead.**

It is not a Prime-classification lecture and it is not a Queen-led audience scene.

Exact dialogue remains deferred to the Agent-Brain rehearsal-first Dialogue Engine.

## What the party reports
The party should explain the discoveries in the order that makes sense to Mirena rather than reciting every room they crossed.

### 1. The buried library research
They found old Yahtrean and Ancient Diysean books / manuscripts concerning:
- inactive / dormant Cards;
- unusual Card light, including green-and-gold descriptions;
- older royal-seal traditions;
- the Diysean ruins in the Westways from which Cyanis's Card had been transported.

They also found strong signs that **someone had recently been studying many of the same subjects**.

### 2. The Hall of Seals
They followed that research trail into old seal collections and learned that:
- the Yahtrean royal seal is not just a visible mark;
- older working royal seals were treated as magically significant physical objects;
- the magical response attached to the royal seal is distinct from simply reproducing the Crest visually;
- the Yahtrean tradition appears to have roots in much older magical seal practice.

### 3. The First Command Warden
The party reports the major event accurately:
- the large Diysean Warden activated;
- it scanned / assessed the entire party;
- it rejected the result and attacked;
- after defeat it began shutting down;
- it communicated **PREVIOUS ERROR**;
- its final communication before going inert was **LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED**;
- only after the Warden shut down did Cyanis's Card settle from unstable green-and-gold behavior into a stable deep Ruby state.

No one in this scene has to force a complete Prime / Might / Last Sentinel classification.

`Last Sentinel` remains highly significant, but the scene's dramatic purpose is not to turn it into a four-part technical answer structure.

### 4. The seal-copying chamber
This is the part that most alarms Mirena.

The party explains that someone had been practicing reproducing seals in the old sealwright room.

The progression included:
- Ancient Diysean seal forms;
- older Yahtrean seals;
- seals found on royal documents;
- increasingly accurate attempts at the **Crest of Yahtrea**, the royal seal itself.

The party should be clear about the evidence boundary:
- copying the visible Crest is possible;
- reproducing the **genuine royal magical authority response** is another matter entirely;
- the later attempts show signs that very powerful magic was being used;
- they found no proof that any copied seal ever successfully reproduced the true royal response;
- they know of no established historical case proving that anyone has ever successfully duplicated the genuine Yahtrean royal seal response.

This is intent / practice evidence, not proof of success.

## Mirena's conclusion — access plus power
Mirena should not instantly name a culprit.

Her concern is more grounded and more dangerous:
> **whoever is behind this has access to things ordinary people should not be able to reach, and somebody involved has serious magical power.**

The evidence supporting her concern includes:
- the original Card transport orders used a genuine royal magical response even though Lysara never authorized them;
- someone had enough access or knowledge to study restricted / obscure Card and royal-seal material;
- someone reached deep Old City collections and the sealwright chamber before the party;
- someone deliberately practiced royal-seal reproduction;
- some of those attempts consumed / left evidence of unusually powerful magic.

### Important limit
Mirena does **not** know:
- whether one person did all of this;
- whether the recent reader and seal practitioner are the same person;
- whether the practitioner successfully reproduced the royal response;
- whether the suspect transport orders were made with a duplicate seal;
- whether the actual royal seal was accessed;
- whether the culprit is Calder;
- why the Card was being moved toward Caelora.

Her conclusion is a threat assessment, not a solved conspiracy.

---

## Mirena is worried
Mirena's worry should be allowed to matter emotionally without making her panicked or helpless.

Why this worries her:
- this involves the Crown's own authority;
- the person or group behind it may have access inside Yahtrean institutions;
- whoever practiced the magical side is not an ordinary forger;
- the Card was being moved under orders the Queen never issued;
- the same investigation keeps crossing old Diysean material, royal authority, and a dormant abnormal Card;
- Mirena cannot safely assume every senior official or record channel is clean.

She should remain competent and decisive.

The worry pushes her to narrow the circle and take direct responsibility for the next step.

---

## Mirena recruits the party
Mirena should **actively ask the party to help her continue the investigation**.

This is not:
- formal conscription;
- Queen's order;
- punishment;
- custodial supervision;
- an excuse to keep Cyanis under watch.

Mirena's reasoning is practical:
- the party has already survived the Westways convoy disaster and followed the Card trail farther than anyone else;
- Cyanis is carrying the Card and has firsthand knowledge of its changes;
- Torren has the route / map evidence and is reliable about physical observations;
- Ilyra is careful about evidence and risk;
- Nimera now has direct knowledge of the Card and the Old City scholarship;
- they are already inside the problem whether anyone likes it or not;
- Mirena needs people she can speak to directly rather than widening the investigation through normal channels.

The party should choose to continue.

Exact acceptance dialogue comes later through the Agent-Brain pass.

## The old map returns
After the immediate report and Mirena's request for help, the party returns to the earlier map evidence.

This is **not a new map from Beat 12**.

Use the existing copied Ancient map / mural material already carried forward from Chapter 2.

Key visible evidence remains:
- the huge Ancient city corresponding to modern Caelora;
- a separate northern location / structure marked with the **exact Yahtrean Crest**.

The party shows this copy to Mirena directly.

### Why Mirena is confused
The presence of the Yahtrean Crest on Ancient material should genuinely bother her.

Her reaction should structurally be:
- the mark is unmistakably Yahtrean;
- the source is far older than Yahtrea should be;
- those two facts should not fit together cleanly;
- she does not invent an explanation on the spot.

This preserves the mystery of why the modern Crest appears on the Ancient map.

---

## Mirena recognizes the location — probably Cresthaven
Mirena studies the position / surrounding geography / route relationship rather than identifying the site from the Crest alone.

She should not sound omniscient.

A good confidence level is:
> **she thinks she knows where that marked location is.**

Her tentative identification:
> **Cresthaven.**

### Why she can make the connection
Without adding a new map discovery, Mirena can compare:
- the copied Ancient geography;
- modern Crown maps / old regional records available upstairs;
- the relative position north of Caelora;
- surviving terrain relationships;
- the known location of Cresthaven.

The fit is strong enough to justify going there, even if the deeper historical meaning is unknown.

### What this does NOT explain
Identifying the location as Cresthaven does not explain:
- why an Ancient map carries the modern Yahtrean Crest there;
- whether Cresthaven itself predates Yahtrea;
- whether the Crest meant the same thing in the Ancient era;
- why the Card / seal investigation intersects with that site;
- who has been researching / practicing seal copies;
- what waits there now.

Cresthaven becomes the next lead, not the answer.
