## Beat purpose
Beat 13 is the controlled Crown debrief immediately after the player chooses to leave the seal-copying chamber.

It should:
1. begin **upstairs in Caelora**, with no playable climb / backtrack out of the Old City;
2. put the field party directly in a restricted meeting with **Queen Lysara Ceryth and Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth**;
3. bring **Maevra** physically back into the story after her parallel Crown-side investigation with Mirena;
4. consolidate what Beats 9–12 actually established about the Card, the First Command Warden, the recent researcher / seal-copying practitioner, and Queen Lysara's royal seal;
5. give the Card several bounded identifications without turning those identifications into a complete explanation;
6. establish with confidence that the **Card itself — not Cyanis — is the thing designated Last Sentinel**;
7. identify the Card as a **Prime** associated with the Face of **Might**;
8. keep the Card unrecovered / unmanifested / unusable;

11. preserve Calder's hidden role and avoid giving him mandatory access to the full sensitive debrief;
12. reintroduce the existing **northward route clue** as the party's next geographic lead without discovering a new map in Beat 13;
13. hand into Beat 14, where Crown records / modern geography can identify the next usable destination.

# The four bounded answers
The title **Four Answers, Not One** should describe how the debrief converts a pile of strange evidence into several precise answers without pretending those answers solve the entire mystery.

## Answer 1 — this is a Prime
Nimera can now classify Cyanis's Card as a **Prime** with strong confidence.

This conclusion should come from combined evidence rather than color alone:
- the Card's persistent abnormal behavior;
- dormant / reactivation precedent found in the buried collections;
- its now-stable internal geometry after the Warden encounter;
- comparison against preserved Card scholarship;
- the scale / structural complexity of the Card relative to ordinary Standard Cards.

### Important distinction
This means:
> **Prime is the Card's classification.**

It does **not** mean:
- the Prime is Recovered;
- it is Awakened;
- it can be equipped / invoked;
- it can replace the party in combat;
- Cyanis has gained a new Prime command.

The Card is still unavailable as a usable Prime.

---

## Answer 2 — its Face is Might
The stable form gives Nimera enough evidence to associate the Card with **Might**.

This should be based on comparison with the six-Face Card material found in the buried collections and the now-stable marks / relationships on the Card.

### Hard Face lock
The current canonical six Faces remain:
- Might;
- Elements;
- Grace;
- Resource;
- Perception;
- Ruin.

The conclusion is:
> **Might is the Card's Face.**

Do not frame this as:
- Cyanis personally being assigned the Face of Might;
- the Warden declaring Cyanis a Might user;
- a personality test;
- proof that every Prime maps cleanly to every piece of modern Face scholarship.

It is the Card being classified, not Cyanis being categorized.

---

## Answer 3 — Last Sentinel is the Card's designation
The Warden's final communication now matters in context.

The sequence was:
1. Warden scans the entire party;
2. Warden rejects / attacks;
3. party defeats Warden;
4. Warden begins shutdown;
5. **PREVIOUS ERROR**;
6. **LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED**;
7. Warden becomes inert;
8. only then does Cyanis's Card settle into stable deep Ruby.

Taken alone, that sequence was suggestive but not enough.

Combined with the Card's newly stable Prime / Might structure, the party can now conclude with strong confidence:
> **Last Sentinel is the designation / identity attached to the Card.**

### Card, not Cyanis
This is an important correction point.

The conclusion is **not**:
> Cyanis is the Last Sentinel.

The conclusion is:
> **the Prime Card is Last Sentinel.**

Cyanis is currently carrying it.

Why it is with him remains unanswered.

---

## Answer 4 — the royal-seal copies are attempts, not proof of success
The sealwright room gives the fourth bounded answer.

The party can now say with confidence:
- someone deliberately studied and practiced reproducing seals;
- the practice sequence moved from Ancient Diysean forms into Yahtrean royal-document seals and then repeated copies of the **Crest of Yahtrea**;
- the **Crest of Yahtrea is the royal seal**;
- substantial magical force was used in some of the later attempts;
- visual copying of the Crest and reproduction of the Queen's genuine magical authority are different problems.

What they **cannot** say is just as important:
- they cannot prove the practitioner ever succeeded;
- they cannot prove any practice copy produced the genuine royal response;
- they cannot prove the practitioner authored the suspect orders;
- they cannot name the practitioner;
- they cannot establish a known historical precedent for successfully duplicating the true royal response.

So the fourth answer is:
> **someone was trying to reproduce the royal seal — but success remains unproven.**

That does not solve Lysara's mystery.

It makes it more dangerous.

## The Queen's seal — what the debrief now establishes
Queen Lysara should be allowed to understand the threat clearly without suddenly possessing technical answers.

By this point the group knows:
- the suspicious orders carry her genuine magical authority response;
- she did not knowingly authorize them;
- the physical seal has no simple documented disappearance explaining the event;
- old Yahtrean tradition treated the royal seal as a magically active physical object;
- someone recently researched that tradition;
- someone practiced reproducing increasingly accurate royal-seal forms;
- powerful magic was used in those attempts;
- no one present can prove a duplicate ever reproduced the real response.

### Security implication
The Crown now has reason to treat the problem as more than forged paperwork.

The threat is:
> **someone is investigating — and may have been attempting — a way to reproduce or counterfeit not merely the royal image, but the authority behind it.**

### Do not over-solve
Do not let anyone conclude:
- therefore the suspect orders came from a copied seal;
- therefore Calder made the copy;
- therefore the practice room belonged to one specific named person;
- therefore the Queen's physical seal was never involved;
- therefore remote seal magic is possible.

All of those remain open.
