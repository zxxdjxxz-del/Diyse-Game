# Chapter 3 — Beat 11 — First Command Warden Scan Correction

**Status:** CURRENT EXPLICIT USER CORRECTION — ACTIVE  
**Scope:** Beat 11 — First Command Warden  
**Supersedes:** any Beat-11 wording that says the Warden does not scan / assess the party before combat.

## Core lock
The **First Command Warden is a large Diysean construct**.

When the party enters the protected chamber:
1. the Warden activates;
2. it visibly **scans / assesses the party**;
3. the scan is part of the Warden's ancient magical function;
4. the result is unacceptable / insufficient under whatever old condition it is enforcing;
5. the Warden then attacks.

The party does not yet know exactly what the Warden is looking for, what standard it is comparing them against, or which member / object triggered its conclusion.

## Presentation
The scan should feel like **ancient Diysean magical craft**, not modern technology.

Appropriate presentation may include:
- seal marks or engraved lines across the Warden's body lighting in sequence;
- magical light passing over the party;
- ward-rings in the chamber brightening as the Warden assesses them;
- the Warden turning / focusing deliberately on different party members;
- carved apertures, gemstones, sigils, or inlaid magical lenses activating;
- the Card reacting subtly if later staging authority wants that, without identifying its nature yet.

Avoid making the presentation feel like a modern computer UI, but **do not remove the fact that the Warden is scanning the party**.

## Meaning boundary
The scan does **not** yet prove:
- that Cyanis has Ancient authorization;
- that Cyanis is the Last Sentinel;
- that the Card is the Last Sentinel;
- that the Card is a Prime;
- that the Card belongs to Might;
- that the Warden recognizes any party member by personal identity;
- that the recent reader passed the same scan;
- what the Warden's original approval criteria were.

The exact interpretation of the Warden's assessment remains unresolved.

## Final-message sequence remains locked
After combat:
1. the Warden is defeated and begins shutting down;
2. it communicates **PREVIOUS ERROR**;
3. its final communication is **LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED**;
4. the Warden then goes fully inert;
5. only after that does Cyanis's Card stabilize into its deep Ruby state.

## Active continuity statement
> **Large Diysean Warden activates → scans the party → rejects / attacks → party defeats it → PREVIOUS ERROR → LAST SENTINEL CONFIRMED → Warden shuts down → Card stabilizes deep Ruby.**
