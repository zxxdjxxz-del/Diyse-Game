# Lean Story Beats

## Beat 1 — Caelora / Containment — legacy S017 REOPENED FOR LEAN MIGRATION

**Purpose**
- bring the party to Caelora without portraying the capital or Crown system as secretly villainous by default;
- establish that the party is under lawful **containment / custodial review**, not arrest;
- reveal a genuine procedural defect in an otherwise authentic custody order;
- establish Mirena and Maevra as competent lawful authority actors who investigate the defect rather than either blindly enforcing or recklessly voiding the process;
- launch the Chapter-3 order-chain investigation.

**Starting state**
- party reaches functioning **Caelora** after Chapter 2.
- permanent combat-capable trio: Cyanis + Ilyra + Torren.
- Maevra travels / acts as commander and authority figure but is never playable.
- Cyanis still carries / remains directly connected to the recovered sealed Card under current knowledge limits.
- the party came to Caelora specifically because the Card requires higher-level investigation / authority.

**Required events / discoveries**
- Caelora is busy, functional, and governed; it is not presented as sinister, abandoned, or under siege.
- Crown officials are already expecting the party and present a formal custody / containment order.
- the order restricts leaving Caelora pending review but does **not** amount to arrest or punitive detention.
- ordinary rights / functions remain available within the lawful limits: medical access, correspondence, and normal city movement except where secure-area rules apply.
- refusing the lawful containment could become grounds for arrest, but the party does not manufacture a confrontation by trying to flee.
- the Card is not casually seized, opened, or directly examined during this beat; no supernatural reaction occurs here.

**The procedural defect**
- the custody chain, seals, witnesses, and downstream authority are authentic.
- Maevra identifies that the order is missing the **initiating declaration / lawful beginning that should precede the custody chain**.
- checking the registered original produces the same defect; this is not simply a lost page or bad copy.
- the order therefore appears to be a real lawful chain with a missing or impossible beginning, rather than an obvious forgery.
- nobody can yet explain whether the initiating authority existed and was omitted, was bypassed, or whether the chain was somehow constructed from valid pieces without a valid initiating act.

**Mirena's role**
- **Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth** enters the formal review and independently recognizes the same defect.
- she does **not** simply void the entire containment order, because the rest of the order and custody chain are genuinely lawful.
- she keeps containment in place while preventing arbitrary expansion of restrictions.
- she explicitly prevents the Card from being seized merely because the order is being questioned.
- she authorizes Maevra to trace the actual chain.
- when that investigation requires movement beyond the normal containment area, Mirena issues a lawful **limited-purpose investigative movement writ** rather than pretending the restriction does not exist.

**Character functions**
- **Maevra:** recognizes the procedural defect, distinguishes authentic authority from missing initiation, and leads the chain investigation.
- **Mirena:** preserves lawful process while actively investigating its apparent corruption / impossibility.
- **Cyanis:** asks practical questions and accepts uncertainty without turning the scene into rebellion against legitimate procedure.
- **Ilyra:** tests what containment actually restricts and verifies that it is not punitive custody.
- **Torren:** remains alert to the practical implications of being expected / contained without over-interpreting the evidence.
- **Crown personnel:** competent officials acting under an apparently valid order, not disposable antagonists.

**End state / knowledge change**
- the party is lawfully contained in Caelora but has authorized investigative movement.
- the custody order is authentic in its downstream components but lacks the initiating declaration that should make the process legally coherent.
- Maevra and Mirena are jointly treating the defect as a real institutional problem.
- the next objective is to **trace the order chain and determine how an apparently lawful order can exist without a lawful beginning**.
