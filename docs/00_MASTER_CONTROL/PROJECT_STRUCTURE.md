# Diyse — Current Project Structure

## 00_MASTER_CONTROL
Cross-domain authority, terminology, open-work index, source hierarchy and current project status.

## 01_CHARACTERS
Playable/supporting/antagonist identities, relationships, chronology and character-specific boundaries.

## 02_STORY
Mandatory story structure, chapter spine, arcs, reveals, recruitment, point of no return and ending.

## 03_DIALOGUE
Exact approved spoken dialogue, dialogue authoring status, voices and line-level continuity.

## 04_WORLD_AND_LORE
Regions, locations, map authority, factions, modern/ancient history and authorial lore truth.

## 05_BATTLE_SYSTEM
Global combat formulas, round rules, targeting, statuses, elements, crits, Guard and boss-form rules.

## 06_CLASSES_AND_ABILITIES
Classes, Abilities, Ultimates, Traits, MP, Class Levels, Masteries and donor equipment eligibility.

## 07_CARDS
Six Faces, Standard Cards, Story Primes, Major-Hunt Primes, loadouts, costs and Prime runtime.

## 08_ITEMS_AND_EQUIPMENT
Consumables, ordinary equipment, Relics, Legacies, Forge Components and project materials.

## 09_ENEMIES_AND_ENCOUNTERS
Enemy rosters, formations, raw stats, bosses, Elites, Regional Hunts, Major Hunts and support objects.

## 10_PROGRESSION_AND_EXP
Player EXP, Level curve, campaign level spine, CEXP model, encounter planning and optional progression.

## 11_QUESTS
Character Quests, ordinary Side Quests, Hunt access/presentation and optional-content cutoff.

## 12_ECONOMY_AND_REWARDS
G denomination, prices, shop progression, ordinary-equipment repurchase and reward/economy boundaries.

## 13_UI_AND_IMPLEMENTATION
UI/runtime requirements, save-state boundaries, current Godot proof status and implementation debt.

## 14_ART_AND_VISUALS
Exact visual authorities, HD-2D production grammar, environment language, map handoff and VFX.

Current exact character masters are stored outside the docs tree under:
`asset_sources/characters/current/`

Their production authority/index lives at:
`docs/14_ART_AND_VISUALS/PRODUCTION/CHARACTERS/README.md`

## 15_AUDIO_AND_MUSIC
Current OPEN music authority, sound-design requirements, research archive and audio implementation boundary.

## 16_BALANCE_AND_TESTING
Closed/open balance layers, cross-system verification, regression planning and release gates.

## asset_sources
Source/reference production inputs separated by provenance and storage rules. These are not automatically runtime assets.

Current repository-backed character source masters:
`asset_sources/characters/current/`

## 90_WORKING
Only currently unresolved/reopened work. Drafts here must be promoted into the owning numbered domain when approved.

## 99_ARCHIVE
Historical migration/source material. Nothing here is current merely because it exists.
