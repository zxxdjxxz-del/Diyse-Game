# CHAPTER 11 — FORWARD HUB AND FINAL CLEANUP WINDOW

**Status:** ACCEPTED / LOCKED  
**Scope:** bounded Chapter 11 progression/hub authority  
**Date:** August 19, 2026

This file preserves the user's accepted Chapter 11 progression changes. It does not rewrite unrelated Chapter 11 boss, antagonist, combat, or story authority.

## 1. Dedicated Chapter 11 Forward Hub

Chapter 11 includes a dedicated temporary **Forward Hub** established in captured Black Host territory.

The Forward Hub is a military staging base inside the Chapter 11 campaign space. It is not a replacement for Cresthaven.

## 2. Cresthaven remains the primary headquarters

**Cresthaven remains the primary full-service headquarters.**

The Chapter 11 Forward Hub provides only essential field functionality appropriate to an active military staging base.

Do not silently duplicate the full Cresthaven service stack at the Forward Hub.

## 3. Permanent Chapter 11 two-way fast travel

Once the Forward Hub is secured, Chapter 11 unlocks permanent two-way fast travel between:

**Cresthaven ↔ Chapter 11 Forward Hub**

This connection remains active for the rest of Chapter 11.

The player may move between Cresthaven and the Forward Hub without replaying the earlier Black Mountains approach zones.

## 4. Vaelkor defeat does not force Chapter 12

Defeating **Emperor Vaelkor Draeven** at the end of Chapter 11 does **not** immediately force the player into Chapter 12.

Vaelkor's defeat opens a final Chapter 11 cleanup / preparation window.

## 5. Final cleanup / preparation window

During the post-Vaelkor Chapter 11 cleanup window:

- Cresthaven ↔ Chapter 11 Forward Hub fast travel remains active;
- the player may complete any still-available Hunts;
- the player may complete any still-available optional bosses;
- the player may complete still-available Character-Life scenes;
- the player may continue eligible exploration;
- the player may collect still-available equipment;
- the player may collect still-available Cards;
- the player may perform available upgrades;
- the player may finish other unfinished optional content that remains legally available before the finale commitment.

This is the final preparation window before Chapter 12.

## 6. Chapter 12 launch is deliberate

Chapter 12 begins only when the player deliberately chooses to **launch the final operation**.

There is no automatic Chapter 11 → Chapter 12 transition immediately after Vaelkor's defeat.

## 7. True point of no return

The deliberate launch of Chapter 12 is the **true final point of no return**.

The game must communicate that the player is choosing to leave the Chapter 11 cleanup/preparation state and begin the final operation.

## 8. Compatibility with inherited late-game authority

This lock preserves all compatible inherited Chapter 11 and Chapter 12 authority, including:

- Chapter 11 remains the Black Mountains campaign;
- Marshal Varkesh's Chapter 11 return/capture authority remains unchanged;
- the Throne of Emperor Vaelkor authority remains unchanged;
- Emperor Vaelkor remains a responsible human antagonist whose final escalation and defeat remain governed by existing authority;
- Chapter 12 remains the final Ancient/subterranean operation and final Entity confrontation domain;
- the Black Mountains campaign and Chapter 12 final domain are not thereby converted into ordinary WORLD_AFTER free-roam/tourist destinations.

## 9. Supersession

Any older wording that implies one of the following is superseded:

- Chapter 11 has no temporary Forward Hub;
- the player must replay earlier Chapter 11 approach zones to return to Cresthaven;
- defeating Vaelkor automatically starts Chapter 12;
- there is no final cleanup/preparation window after Vaelkor;
- the final point of no return occurs before the player's deliberate Chapter 12 launch.
