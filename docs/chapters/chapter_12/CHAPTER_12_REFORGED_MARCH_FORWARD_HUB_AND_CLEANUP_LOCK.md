# CHAPTER 12 — THE REFORGED MARCH — FORWARD HUB AND FINAL CLEANUP WINDOW

**Status:** ACCEPTED / LOCKED  
**Scope:** current Chapter-12 Black Host campaign, Vhalmarch Forward Hub, Vaelkor aftermath, cleanup window  
**Original pre-insertion acceptance:** CH11-A001 / CH11-A002 under Audit84/Audit89  
**Current reconciliation:** Audit107 / Audit109 / Audit111 / Audit113

This file is the current-facing projection of the former Chapter-11 campaign material after insertion of Chapter 10 — The Last Blank.

## 1. Current chapter identity

**Chapter 12 — The Reforged March** is the final Black Host war/campaign chapter.

Current macro route:

**Westguard-side campaign corridor → The Blackspine crossing → Draevensreach → Vhalmarch → Vorathen → The Veiled Citadel**

Use current terminology:
- **Black Host Territory**, not Blackstone as region name;
- **The Blackspine**, not Black Mountains;
- **Westguard**, not Westreach;
- **Vhalmarch** = proper name of the temporary Forward Hub.

## 2. Varkesh and Forward Hub timing

Varkesh controls the defensive withdrawal and fallback network through the first major phase of Chapter 12.

The party/coalition progressively strips away his retreat options, defeats him, and **captures him alive**.

**Only after Varkesh is defeated/captured** is his secured former position converted into the temporary Forward Hub at **Vhalmarch**.

This preserves the original Audit89 timing lock under the correct current chapter number.

## 3. Cresthaven ↔ Vhalmarch travel

Once Vhalmarch is secured, permanent two-way travel opens:

**Cresthaven ↔ Vhalmarch**

It remains available through:
- the remainder of Chapter 12;
- the post-Vaelkor cleanup/preparation state;
- any compatible pre-Last-Shelter returnable endgame access defined by Audit109.

Cresthaven remains the primary full-service headquarters.

Vhalmarch remains a field military staging base with essential services only.

The player does not replay earlier Chapter-12 approach zones simply to return between Cresthaven and Vhalmarch.

## 4. Deeper Chapter-12 campaign

After Varkesh is removed and Vhalmarch is secured, the coalition pushes deeper through Black Host Territory.

The party discovers evidence that Black Host forces have been:
- excavating ancient Diysean infrastructure;
- removing/cataloguing ancient machinery;
- attaching Black Host systems to ancient components;
- experimenting with Crest-related technology;
- attempting to make ancient systems respond to modern controls.

The Black Host has not successfully rebuilt the ancient Last Weapon.

This material preserves the inherited strategic justification for the campaign while remaining subordinate to later Chapter-13 truth.

## 5. Chapter-12 Elite / Hunt separation

Elites, Regional Hunts, and mandatory story bosses remain separate categories.

### Conventional Elite
Chapter 12 retains the dedicated high-ranking conventional Black Host Elite role inherited from Audit89.

- `Sovereign's First Warlord` remains retired.
- `Lord-Marshal Kharvek` remains only a working replacement name unless separately finalized.
- the Elite is optional and does not gate mandatory progression.

### Regional Hunt #11 — Throne of Emperor Vaelkor

**Regional Hunt #11** occurs in current **Chapter 12**.

It remains a corrupted ancient Diysean construct appropriated/modified by the Black Host, preserving:
1. Sealed Throne
2. Walking Throne

It is optional and remains available during the post-Vaelkor cleanup window if unfinished.

The Hunt number remains **#11**; Hunt numbering is independent of chapter numbering.

## 6. Vaelkor climax

Vaelkor remains a responsible human antagonist.

He receives a genuine opportunity to surrender after the coalition breaks his military position and refuses.

Mandatory boss progression remains:
1. **Emperor of the Reforged Host**
2. **Sovereign Panoply Unbound**

Vaelkor remains consciously himself and morally responsible throughout. The transformation is not possession and does not absolve him.

When Vaelkor falls, corruption and machinery directly tied to him fail, while deeper activity continues. This proves he was not the deepest cause.

## 7. Post-Vaelkor cleanup/preparation window

Vaelkor's defeat does **not** automatically begin Chapter 13.

A broad cleanup/preparation state opens.

During it:
- Cresthaven ↔ Vhalmarch travel remains available;
- still-available Hunts may be completed;
- the Chapter-12 Elite and Regional Hunt #11 remain available if unfinished;
- still-available Character Quests/scenes and eligible exploration remain accessible;
- equipment, Cards, upgrades, and other eligible optional content may still be completed.

The final-operation briefing remains at Cresthaven with **Queen Lysara Ceryth and Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth physically present** under inherited authority.

## 8. Chapter-13 launch is deliberate, but not the true PONR

The player deliberately chooses when to begin **Chapter 13** after the Chapter-12 cleanup state.

However, Audit109 supersedes the old rule that chapter launch itself is the irreversible point of no return.

Current rule:

- Chapter 13 may begin and the player may advance through the early final domain while world return remains supported.
- **Last Shelter** is the final returnable preparation point.
- the true irreversible threshold is **Last Shelter → Reactor Galleries**.

Do not restore old wording that calls the deliberate Chapter-12/13 launch the true final PONR.

## 9. Late Synthesis timing

The three mandatory reciprocal-pair resolution beats now occur in **late Chapter 12**:

- Cyanis ⇄ Vaelira — **What Holds, What Changes** — Cresthaven.
- Ilyra ⇄ Seyrik — **Keep Them Alive** — Vhalmarch recovery/treatment area.
- Torren ⇄ Nimera — **Enough to Move** — Vhalmarch operations/map area.

They remain item-reward-free authored story gates for Synthesis.
