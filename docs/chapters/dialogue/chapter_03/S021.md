## S021_B001 — Cresthaven, next morning

The following morning, the party reaches Cresthaven using Torren's copied route and Crown directions. The abandoned Crownhold outpost is already active: Crown wagons unload supplies, workers clear and inspect rooms, clerks carry record boxes inside, medical staff establish a temporary treatment space, and a small security detail checks the perimeter. Older Diysean structure remains visible beneath and around the Crown construction.

**TORREN:** Abandoned.

**CYANIS:** Apparently we're using the flexible definition.

**ILYRA:** Someone got here early.

**MAEVRA:** Mirena.

They find Mirena directing the setup near the main structure.

**MIRENA:** You're late.

**MAEVRA:** You told us morning.

**MIRENA:** Then I'm early.

**TORREN:** By a lot.

**CYANIS:** You reopened it.

**MIRENA:** Enough of it.

**MIRENA:** Until we know why that command system pointed here, you need somewhere to work that isn't a borrowed room in Caelora.

**MAEVRA:** Temporary headquarters.

**MIRENA:** Working headquarters. Temporary until we understand what the hell is going on.

Mirena gestures toward the crews already making the outpost usable.

**MIRENA:** Records, medical, supplies, rest, security, and somewhere to plan the next move. Nothing more permanent than the facts justify.

**NIMERA:** I like her.

**CYANIS:** Careful. She'll hear you.

**MIRENA:** I did.

**CYANIS:** Last Sentinel.

**NIMERA:** No as in don't fucking do that. The Card is Last Sentinel. What that means for you is not answered by the label.

**ILYRA:** Sentinel of what?

**NIMERA:** Don't know.

**TORREN:** Last because?

**NIMERA:** Don't know.

**MAEVRA:** Previous error?

**NIMERA:** Don't know.

**NIMERA:** Yes. Prime. Might. Last Sentinel. And the fourth answer is that we still do not know what Last Sentinel ultimately means.

**LYSARA:** And that is tomorrow's, unless it becomes today's without permission.
