## S018_B001 — Judicial Causeway

The group crosses into a more administrative part of Caelora: tall civic buildings, record offices, couriers, lawyers, clerks, covered bridges.

**TORREN:** Lot of buildings for telling people what they can do.

**MAEVRA:** Mostly they're for recording what people already did.

**TORREN:** That's better?

**MAEVRA:** Some days.

**CYANIS:** Today?

**MAEVRA:** No.

## S018_B002 — Mirena's writ works

At the first Causeway checkpoint, an Order Clerk checks Mirena's movement writ, Maevra's identity, and the party.

**ORDER CLERK:** Valid.

**CYANIS:** Good start.

The clerk stamps the ledger, then pauses over another entry.

**ORDER CLERK:** Wait.

**MAEVRA:** What?

## S018_B003 — A second order

**ORDER CLERK:** This was registered seventeen minutes ago.

He pulls a newly filed seizure writ. Maevra reads.

**ILYRA:** What does it say?

**MAEVRA:** The Card is to be removed from my custody.

**CYANIS:** To whom?

**MAEVRA:** Judicial Evidence.

**TORREN:** That bad?

**MAEVRA:** Not by itself.

She keeps reading.

**MAEVRA:** This is.

## S018_B004 — Her authorization

**MAEVRA:** It says I authorized the transfer.

**CYANIS:** Did you?

Maevra looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Required question.

**MAEVRA:** No.

**ILYRA:** Forgery?

**MAEVRA:** Seal's real.

**ORDER CLERK:** Registry agrees.

**TORREN:** Somebody stole your seal?

**MAEVRA:** Not this one.

## S018_B005 — Impossible timing

**MAEVRA:** Look at the time.

**CYANIS:** That was before we reached Caelora.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**ILYRA:** Could you have authorized it remotely?

**MAEVRA:** Not through this process.

**ORDER CLERK:** This authorization requires physical witness.

**TORREN:** Where?

**ORDER CLERK:** Here.

**TORREN:** You have a busy morning I don't know about, Solmar?

**MAEVRA:** Apparently.

## S018_B006 — The title problem

**CYANIS:** So the seal's real.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** Witness record's real.

**ORDER CLERK:** Authenticated.

**CYANIS:** And she wasn't here.

**MAEVRA:** Correct.

**CYANIS:** That's a problem.

**MAEVRA:** That's an order that should not exist.

## S018_B007 — The clerk tries to freeze it

**ORDER CLERK:** I'm freezing the transfer.

Before he can mark it, an Ivory Watch detail approaches with the same seizure order.

**IVORY OFFICER:** Commander Solmar.

**MAEVRA:** Officer.

**IVORY OFFICER:** We're here for the recovered Card.

**MAEVRA:** You're not taking it.

## S018_B008 — Two legitimate orders

The officer presents the seizure writ. Maevra presents Mirena's investigative writ.

**IVORY OFFICER:** Mine postdates yours.

**MAEVRA:** Mine was issued by the Crown Princess.

**IVORY OFFICER:** And this transfer carries your authorization.

**MAEVRA:** Which I did not give.

**IVORY OFFICER:** Commander, I have an authenticated order.

**MAEVRA:** So do I.

## S018_B009 — The problem cannot be argued away

**ILYRA:** Your order contains a physically impossible witness entry.

**IVORY OFFICER:** I don't have authority to void it.

**CYANIS:** Do you have authority to pause it?

**IVORY OFFICER:** Not after physical transfer has been ordered.

**MAEVRA:** Then we're not solving this at the gate.

## S018_B010 — First confrontation

**IVORY OFFICER:** Commander. Release the case.

**MAEVRA:** No.

**IVORY OFFICER:** Then I am required to secure it.

**MAEVRA:** I understand.

**TORREN:** We're really doing this?

**MAEVRA:** Nonlethal.

**CYANIS:** Obviously.

**AUTHORED ENCOUNTER #1 — IVORY WATCH DETAIL. Restraint-oriented; no lethal victory requirement; lawful personnel drop no personal loot.**

## S018_B011 — After first battle

The final officer is disarmed and held. Cyanis backs away immediately when resistance ends.

**CYANIS:** Done.

**ILYRA:** Anyone hurt?

One officer has a bad wrist. Ilyra kneels beside him.

**IVORY OFFICER:** You just fought us.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

She checks the wrist.

**ILYRA:** Don't move that.

**IVORY OFFICER:** Those facts aren't related?

**ILYRA:** No.

## S018_B012 — Maevra does not humiliate them

Maevra returns the officer's dropped sidearm hilt-first.

**MAEVRA:** You had a sealed order.

**IVORY OFFICER:** Still do.

**MAEVRA:** I know.

## S018_B013 — The copies differ

**CYANIS:** Commander.

**MAEVRA:** What?

Cyanis shows her two paper copies.

**CYANIS:** These aren't the same.

The clerk compares them.

## S018_B014 — Altered sequence

**MAEVRA:** Witness block.

**ORDER CLERK:** Different sequence number.

**ILYRA:** Which one is valid?

**ORDER CLERK:** Both.

**CYANIS:** I don't think I'm going to like that answer.

## S018_B015 — Real pieces, wrong assembly

**ORDER CLERK:** The first page was registered through Judicial Evidence.

**ORDER CLERK:** The authorization page came through military custody.

**MAEVRA:** Both authentic systems.

**ORDER CLERK:** Yes.

**MAEVRA:** But they weren't issued together.

**ORDER CLERK:** No.

**TORREN:** Somebody stitched them together.

**MAEVRA:** Looks that way.

## S018_B016 — Not a simple forgery

**CYANIS:** So somebody forged an order.

**MAEVRA:** Not exactly.

**MAEVRA:** They didn't forge the pieces.

She holds up the pages.

**MAEVRA:** They used real pieces to make something false.

## S018_B017 — Provenance

**ORDER CLERK:** The merged copy passed through Central Adjudication.

**MAEVRA:** Who signed release?

The clerk goes quiet.

**CYANIS:** That's encouraging.

**ORDER CLERK:** Ivory Adjudicator Sereth.

**MAEVRA:** Where?

The clerk points farther down the Causeway.

## S018_B018 — Walking dialogue

Player control resumes briefly. No random encounters.

**TORREN:** You know him?

**MAEVRA:** By reputation.

**TORREN:** Good?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**TORREN:** That's inconvenient.

**MAEVRA:** Very.

**CYANIS:** You two sound disappointed.

**TORREN:** Bad people are easier.

**CYANIS:** Usually.

**ILYRA:** And usually less dangerous than competent people acting on bad information.

Nobody jokes after that.

## S018_B019 — Central Adjudication

The Ivory Adjudicator is already waiting, formal royal armor and poleblade grounded beside him. Two restraint officers stand back.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Commander Solmar.

**MAEVRA:** Adjudicator.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** You've resisted lawful seizure.

**MAEVRA:** I've resisted a false order.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Authenticated order.

**MAEVRA:** False order.

## S018_B020 — Neither side is stupid

He hands her the ledger.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Find me the false seal.

**MAEVRA:** There isn't one.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** False witness.

**MAEVRA:** Witness exists.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Invalid authority.

**MAEVRA:** Both authorities are valid.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Then I have a lawful order.

**MAEVRA:** No.

## S018_B021 — Prove it

**CYANIS:** We found two pages issued separately and combined afterward.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Prove it.

Cyanis gives him the copies. The Adjudicator reads.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** This proves sequence discrepancy.

**MAEVRA:** I know it is contradictory.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Belief is not the standard.

**ILYRA:** Neither is pretending.

The room stills.

## S018_B022 — Why he cannot simply stand down

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** I believe you.

**ILYRA:** Then stop the seizure.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** I can't.

**ILYRA:** Why?

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Because belief is not a legal finding.

## S018_B023 — Procedure protects procedure

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** If I ignore an authenticated seizure order because the subject tells me it is false, I destroy the same process you're asking me to protect.

**MAEVRA:** Agreed.

**CYANIS:** That's not helping us.

**MAEVRA:** It isn't supposed to.

## S018_B024 — The review loop

**MAEVRA:** Open the declaration chain.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Restricted evidence.

**MAEVRA:** Then authorize review.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** I cannot while seizure is contested.

**CYANIS:** So we can't prove the order is false until you seize the Card.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Under current procedure—

**CYANIS:** And once you seize it, we're obeying the order we're trying to prove is false.

**TORREN:** That's fucked.

## S018_B025 — The order protects itself

**ILYRA:** The procedure is blocking its own review.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** The order was protecting itself.

The Adjudicator looks again at the ledger. His certainty weakens, but his duty does not disappear.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Stand down and surrender the case.

**MAEVRA:** No.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Then I have to enforce the order.

**MAEVRA:** I know.

## S018_B026 — Second confrontation

The Adjudicator lifts his poleblade into a restraint stance.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** No lethal force.

**MAEVRA:** Agreed.

**CYANIS:** Strangest polite conversation I've had all week.

**TORREN:** Week's young.

**AUTHORED ENCOUNTER #2 — IVORY ADJUDICATOR. Expose the Order Ledger / Declaration Seal contradiction; do not delete player command categories.**

## S018_B027 — Evidence exposed

At the authored contradiction threshold, the Ledger's sealed declaration section opens and pages separate. The Adjudicator stops immediately.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Hold.

Combat ends. Everyone lowers weapons.

## S018_B028 — The pages cannot belong together

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** This declaration predates the seizure request.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** And this authorization was copied from another custody action.

**ILYRA:** Different case?

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Different year.

**TORREN:** Well.

**CYANIS:** That's worse.

## S018_B029 — Enforcement suspended

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Seizure suspended.

**MAEVRA:** Suspended?

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Until formal invalidation.

Maevra considers pushing and does not.

**MAEVRA:** Good enough for now.

## S018_B030 — Lawful officers remain lawful people

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Your enforcement was lawful under the record you were given.

**IVORY OFFICER:** Didn't feel very lawful.

**MAEVRA:** That's why records matter.

**IVORY OFFICER:** Apparently not enough.

**MAEVRA:** No.

## S018_B031 — Where did the assembly happen?

**CYANIS:** Where did those pages get joined?

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Not here.

He checks an older, partly suppressed stamp.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Source record came through an archived judicial repository.

**MAEVRA:** Which one?

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Old City records. Suppressed stacks.

## S018_B032 — Suppressed does not mean dead

**TORREN:** Suppressed means closed?

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Removed from ordinary record circulation.

**CYANIS:** But apparently still good enough to issue orders.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Apparently.

## S018_B033 — Maevra begins retreating into command

**MAEVRA:** I need the complete ledger, copies of every affected order, issuance sequence, witness chain, seal registry, transfer history—

**TORREN:** Solmar.

She looks at him.

**TORREN:** One thing at a time.

**MAEVRA:** I know.

**TORREN:** All right.

He leaves it there. The first deliberate “Maevra” remains protected for H02.

## S018_B034 — Practical next step

**CYANIS:** First thing?

**MAEVRA:** We find out what record they used.

**ILYRA:** Then?

**MAEVRA:** Then we find out how many orders were built from it.

**CYANIS:** Good.

## S018_B035 — Access warrant

The Ivory Adjudicator signs a physical access warrant.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** This grants entry to the Suppressed Archive under investigative authority.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** If someone challenges it—

**CYANIS:** Please don't say we have another authenticated order.

The Adjudicator almost smiles.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** This one has a beginning.

**CYANIS:** We're improving.

## S018_B036 — Exit

The party turns toward the Old City. The Ivory personnel stay behind to audit the false order rather than becoming continuing enemies.

**ILYRA:** You all right?

**MAEVRA:** No.

**ILYRA:** All right.

A few steps later.

**MAEVRA:** Ask me later.

**ILYRA:** I will.

At the secured descent, the access writ is checked.

**IVORY ADJUDICATOR:** Enter the Suppressed Archives.

Cut to S019.
