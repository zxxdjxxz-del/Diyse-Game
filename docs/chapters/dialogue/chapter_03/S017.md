> **Location: Caelora • No random combat • Cyanis / Ilyra / Torren / Maevra; Mirena enters during formal review**

## S017_B001 — Caelora

Caelora is functioning. Not under siege. Not abandoned. Not sinister. The party enters through a busy southern approach: carts, Crown couriers, market traffic, soldiers changing watch, workers repairing stonework.

**TORREN:** Busy.

**CYANIS:** Capital.

**TORREN:** I've seen capitals.

**CYANIS:** Then I'm out of explanations.

**ILYRA:** You could try not explaining something.

**CYANIS:** Seems extreme.

Maevra almost smiles. Then she notices the waiting officers ahead, and her expression changes.

## S017_B002 — They are expected

A Crown officer and two guards wait beside an official inspection post. No weapons raised.

**CROWN OFFICER:** Commander Solmar.

**MAEVRA:** Officer.

The officer looks at Cyanis, Ilyra and Torren.

**CROWN OFFICER:** Crest Knight Dovaren. Warden Amarin. Harth.

**TORREN:** They knew which line to put us in.

**CYANIS:** Being remembered?

**TORREN:** Being expected.

**MAEVRA:** He's right.

## S017_B003 — The order

The officer produces a sealed physical writ: wax, stamped authority, witnessed copy. Maevra takes it and reads.

**CROWN OFFICER:** By custodial authority, the recovered Card and all persons directly involved in its transport are to remain within Crown supervision pending review.

**CYANIS:** Remain where?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Caelora.

**CYANIS:** That's a large answer.

**MAEVRA:** Dovaren.

**CYANIS:** It is.

**CROWN OFFICER:** Your movements inside the authorized district are not restricted beyond listed secure areas.

**ILYRA:** And leaving the city?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Restricted pending review.

## S017_B004 — Containment, not arrest

**ILYRA:** Are we under arrest?

**CROWN OFFICER:** No.

**ILYRA:** Detained?

A slight pause.

**CROWN OFFICER:** Contained under custodial authority.

**TORREN:** That's a prettier word.

The officer looks at him.

**TORREN:** Still asking.

## S017_B005 — Ilyra tests the restrictions

**ILYRA:** Medical access?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Unrestricted.

**ILYRA:** Private treatment?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Yes.

**ILYRA:** Personal correspondence?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Not restricted by this order.

**ILYRA:** Weapons?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Peace-bound only in the palace district. Standard city law.

Ilyra nods. Those are real answers.

**ILYRA:** So this isn't punitive custody.

**CROWN OFFICER:** No.

**ILYRA:** Good.

## S017_B006 — Cyanis asks what refusal means

**CYANIS:** What happens if we leave anyway?

**CROWN OFFICER:** The containment becomes defiance of lawful Crown custody.

**CYANIS:** And then?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Then you may be arrested.

**CYANIS:** There it is.

**MAEVRA:** You asked.

**CYANIS:** I did.

No attempt to leave. No dramatic refusal.

## S017_B007 — The Card

The officer indicates the secured transport case.

**CROWN OFFICER:** The recovered Card remains sealed.

**CYANIS:** Has anyone opened the case?

**CROWN OFFICER:** No.

**MAEVRA:** Transferred custody?

**CROWN OFFICER:** No.

**MAEVRA:** Examined it directly?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Only external seal verification.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

Cyanis looks at the case. No response. No Ruby. No Might identification. Nothing supernatural happens.

## S017_B008 — Maevra finds the problem

Maevra reads the order again, then turns back to the beginning.

**MAEVRA:** Under whose authority?

**CROWN OFFICER:** The order came through Judicial Authority.

**MAEVRA:** I can read. Who initiated it?

The officer looks back down at the document.

**MAEVRA:** Custodial chain is authenticated. Witnesses are valid. Seal is valid. Transfer language is valid.

She taps the first section.

**MAEVRA:** There should be something before this.

**CROWN OFFICER:** No, Commander. It wasn't included in the copy.

**MAEVRA:** Then get me the original.

## S017_B009 — The missing declaration

The officer signals a clerk.

**CROWN OFFICER:** Pull the registered original.

**CLERK:** Yes, sir.

**CYANIS:** Important omission?

**MAEVRA:** Very.

**ILYRA:** Clerical?

**MAEVRA:** Possible.

**CYANIS:** And if it isn't?

**MAEVRA:** Then we find out.

## S017_B010 — The officer remains competent

**CYANIS:** You're not going to tell us to stop asking questions?

**CROWN OFFICER:** If Commander Solmar found a defect in a custody order carrying my name, I'd prefer to know before I enforce it.

**CYANIS:** I like him.

**MAEVRA:** Don't make it strange.

**CYANIS:** Wasn't planning to.

**TORREN:** You usually aren't.

**CYANIS:** That sounded personal.

## S017_B011 — The registered original

The clerk returns with the registered original and ledger. Maevra checks both, longer this time.

**MAEVRA:** Same defect.

**CROWN OFFICER:** That's the registered original.

**MAEVRA:** I know.

**ILYRA:** So nobody lost a page.

**MAEVRA:** No.

**CYANIS:** Can an order exist without whoever ordered it?

**MAEVRA:** It shouldn't.

## S017_B012 — What they know and do not know

**TORREN:** Forged?

**MAEVRA:** Not from what I'm looking at.

**ILYRA:** Then what?

**MAEVRA:** I don't know yet.

**CYANIS:** All right.

Maevra glances at him. He is not disappointed by the answer.

**CYANIS:** What do we do when you don't know?

**MAEVRA:** We check the chain.

## S017_B013 — Mirena arrives for formal review

Crown Princess Mirena Ceryth enters without ceremonial procession. The officer bows; the party follows appropriately. Mirena looks at the open writ.

**MIRENA:** I was told we had a custody problem.

**MAEVRA:** We have a custody order.

**MIRENA:** Those are usually the same sentence.

**MAEVRA:** Not today.

Mirena holds out her hand.

## S017_B014 — Mirena reads it

Mirena reads quickly, then returns to one section.

**MIRENA:** Where's the declaration?

**MAEVRA:** Exactly.

**MIRENA:** Registered original?

**CROWN OFFICER:** Same defect, Your Highness.

**MIRENA:** Wonderful.

Cyanis looks at her. Mirena notices.

**MIRENA:** That was not sincere.

**CYANIS:** I suspected.

## S017_B015 — Mirena does not simply void everything

**MIRENA:** Is the order otherwise lawful?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**MIRENA:** And the custody chain?

**MAEVRA:** Authenticated.

**MIRENA:** Then I'm not voiding it because one required element is missing.

**CYANIS:** Wasn't going to ask.

Mirena looks at him.

**CYANIS:** I was thinking about it.

**MIRENA:** Better.

## S017_B016 — Accountability

**MIRENA:** Containment remains in effect while we establish whether the initiating authority exists and was omitted—or whether someone managed to create a lawful chain with no lawful beginning.

**MIRENA:** No expansion of restrictions without my office's review.

**CROWN OFFICER:** Yes, Your Highness.

**MIRENA:** No seizure of the Card.

**CROWN OFFICER:** Understood.

**MIRENA:** And nobody decides these people are fugitives because they asked why they're being contained.

**CROWN OFFICER:** They won't.

**MIRENA:** Good.

## S017_B017 — Maevra's authority

**MIRENA:** Can you trace the chain?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

Maevra corrects herself.

**MAEVRA:** I can trace what exists.

**MIRENA:** Do that.

**CYANIS:** We're going with her.

Not a question. Mirena looks at him.

**MIRENA:** You are contained, not decorative.

**CYANIS:** Good distinction.

## S017_B018 — Ilyra's final condition

**ILYRA:** One more.

**MIRENA:** Go ahead.

**ILYRA:** If following this chain puts us outside the authorized district?

Mirena considers it.

**MIRENA:** Issue an investigative movement writ under my authority.

**MAEVRA:** Limited purpose.

**MIRENA:** Obviously.

Maevra gives her the smallest approving look. Mirena catches it.

**MIRENA:** Don't look so surprised, Commander.

**MAEVRA:** I wasn't.

**MIRENA:** Of course not.

## S017_B019 — New writ

The new writ is sealed. Maevra checks it before anyone moves.

**CYANIS:** That one have a beginning?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** Promising.

**TORREN:** Low standard.

**CYANIS:** Bad day for standards.

**ILYRA:** Please don't make that our motto.

## S017_B020 — Exit

The group heads toward the Judicial Causeway. Behind them, Caelora keeps functioning: people work, carts move, guards change shifts. The system appears legitimate, everyone involved is behaving reasonably, and the order still has no lawful beginning.

**MAEVRA:** If the process is compromised—

**CYANIS:** Then we find out.

Torren glances toward a food stall they are passing.

**TORREN:** Food before constitutional crisis.

**ILYRA:** Finally, a policy I support.
