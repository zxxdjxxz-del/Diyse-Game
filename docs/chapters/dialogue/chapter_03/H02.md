## H02_B001

Small Cresthaven records/work room after the authority crisis. Table, two chairs, lamp, water, copied orders and custody records. Maevra is rereading the same packet. Torren appears in the doorway.

**TORREN:** You missed it.

**MAEVRA:** What?

**TORREN:** Whatever changed since the last time you read that.

**MAEVRA:** Nothing changed.

**TORREN:** Then you didn't miss it.

## H02_B002

Maevra turns a page anyway.

**MAEVRA:** I know what it says.

**TORREN:** I know.

**MAEVRA:** Apparently not well enough.

**TORREN:** You've read it four times since I got here.

**MAEVRA:** You've been here thirty seconds.

**TORREN:** Exactly.

## H02_B003

**MAEVRA:** The order passed seal, witness, custody and emergency authority.

**TORREN:** And still shouldn't have existed.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**TORREN:** Reading it a fifth time won't make that less true.

**MAEVRA:** I know.

She reads another line.

## H02_B004

**TORREN:** Solmar.

**MAEVRA:** Don't.

**TORREN:** Didn't say anything.

**MAEVRA:** You were about to.

**TORREN:** Was going to ask if you wanted water.

Maevra looks at the untouched cup beside her.

**MAEVRA:** That's irritating.

**TORREN:** Usually is.

## H02_B005

He sits without taking the papers from her.

**TORREN:** Remember the weather report from Veycross?

**MAEVRA:** Which one?

**TORREN:** Old one. Patrol came in after three days of rain. Whole report said “weather: wet.”

**MAEVRA:** That was a terrible report.

**TORREN:** Accurate.

**MAEVRA:** Barely.

**TORREN:** Still accurate.

## H02_B006

**MAEVRA:** This isn't weather.

**TORREN:** No.

**MAEVRA:** If the procedure can assemble an order out of valid pieces—

**TORREN:** Then you find where it broke.

**MAEVRA:** Before someone else gets hurt.

**TORREN:** Yes.

## H02_B007

Maevra's left arm shifts; she hides the soreness by moving another document. Torren notices.

**TORREN:** Left arm.

**MAEVRA:** Fine.

**TORREN:** Shoulder too.

**MAEVRA:** Harth.

**TORREN:** Attached to you.

**MAEVRA:** Still is.

**TORREN:** Good.

## H02_B008

**MAEVRA:** I don't need you deciding when I'm done.

**TORREN:** I'm not.

**MAEVRA:** Sounds like it.

**TORREN:** I'm telling you you checked the same seal four times.

Beat.

**TORREN:** Maevra.

She stops.

## H02_B009

**MAEVRA:** Good.

**TORREN:** Good?

**MAEVRA:** You said it like a name.

**TORREN:** It is one.

**MAEVRA:** You know what I mean.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

He does not use it again to win the conversation.

## H02_B010

**TORREN:** River moved once outside Bellmarsh. Map stayed right for six years because nobody wanted to redraw it.

**MAEVRA:** The river was not wrong.

**TORREN:** Exactly.

**MAEVRA:** You're comparing Crown procedure to a moved river.

**TORREN:** I'm comparing the map to the map.

Maevra exhales, almost a laugh.

## H02_B011

**MAEVRA:** Five minutes.

**TORREN:** Didn't ask for five.

**MAEVRA:** That's what you're getting.

She finally puts the papers down. He slides the water toward her and leaves the silence alone.

## H02_B012

After a while, Maevra picks the packet back up, but not to reread it. She stacks it for morning.

**MAEVRA:** Don't disappear before morning.

**TORREN:** I'll be here.

End.
