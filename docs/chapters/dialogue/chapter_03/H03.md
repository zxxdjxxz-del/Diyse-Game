## H03_B001

Cresthaven temporary Medical space. Nimera enters with one hand wrapped badly in a strip of cloth.

**ILYRA:** What happened?

**NIMERA:** Nothing.

**ILYRA:** That's bleeding.

Nimera looks down.

**NIMERA:** Well, shit.

**ILYRA:** Sit.

**NIMERA:** It's fine.

**ILYRA:** Then sitting shouldn't hurt it.

## H03_B002

Nimera sits. Ilyra reaches for the wrap, then pauses.

**ILYRA:** Can I?

**NIMERA:** No.

Ilyra stops immediately.

**ILYRA:** All right.

Nimera looks at her, surprised by the lack of argument.

**NIMERA:** That's it?

**ILYRA:** You said no.

## H03_B003

Beat. Nimera flexes the injured hand and winces.

**NIMERA:** Fine. Yes.

**ILYRA:** Different answer.

**NIMERA:** Don't enjoy that too much.

**ILYRA:** I wasn't planning to.

## H03_B004

Ilyra unwraps the terrible improvised bandage.

**ILYRA:** This is awful.

**ILYRA:** It was six seconds old.

**NIMERA:** Exactly.

## H03_B005

## H03_B006

**NIMERA:** I am bleeding near the archive.

**ILYRA:** Much better.

**NIMERA:** Precision matters.

## H03_B008

Ilyra finishes the clean wrap.

**ILYRA:** Bread?

**NIMERA:** What?

**NIMERA:** I was saving it.

**ILYRA:** From whom?

**ILYRA:** Poorly.
