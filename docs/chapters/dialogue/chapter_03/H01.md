## H01_B001

Cresthaven, temporary Archive/Common work area. One long table. Cyanis has a cup at one end; Torren is checking a strap at the other. Nimera arrives with the Living Index Tablet, three paper bundles, two slate fragments, a cup, a loose pencil, and an object neither man recognizes.

**TORREN:** No.

**NIMERA:** I haven't done anything.

**TORREN:** You were about to.

## H01_B002

Nimera puts one paper bundle down anyway.

**CYANIS:** That's not empty.

**NIMERA:** Temporarily.

**TORREN:** That's not what that word means.

**NIMERA:** It can be aspirational.

## H01_B003

Another bundle appears. Torren moves his strap two inches toward himself.

**TORREN:** You have your own table.

**NIMERA:** I have half a table.

**CYANIS:** This is also half a table.

**NIMERA:** Correct.

**CYANIS:** I see the problem.

**TORREN:** Do you?

**CYANIS:** Not mine.

## H01_B004

**NIMERA:** You've known me for an hour.

**TORREN:** Didn't take that long.

**NIMERA:** That's insulting.

**TORREN:** Accurate.

**NIMERA:** Worse.

## H01_B005

Maevra passes the doorway, sees the table, and stops.

**MAEVRA:** There was more table a minute ago.

**NIMERA:** Temporarily.

**MAEVRA:** I've adapted.

She keeps walking before Nimera can answer.

## H01_B006

Nimera shifts a cup onto Cyanis's side.

**CYANIS:** That's mine now?

**NIMERA:** It's neutral territory.

**CYANIS:** On my hand?

**NIMERA:** Border dispute.

**TORREN:** Lose the border.

## H01_B007

**TORREN:** There's room for people. There's no room for more things.

**NIMERA:** People are things.

**CYANIS:** Dangerous opening.

**NIMERA:** Fine. People are spatially expensive objects with opinions.

**TORREN:** Better.

**CYANIS:** Is it?

## H01_B008

Nimera finally sits, then immediately stands to reach across Torren for a record slip.

**TORREN:** Sit down or take the other table.

**NIMERA:** Those are not equivalent options.

**TORREN:** They are to me.

**NIMERA:** I dislike how simple your world is.

**TORREN:** Works.

## H01_B009

Cyanis looks at the slowly vanishing table surface.

**CYANIS:** How much of this are you actively using?

**NIMERA:** Define actively.

**CYANIS:** Touching before tomorrow.

**NIMERA:** Hostile definition.

## H01_B010

A clerk enters with another folder, stops, and looks at Nimera's occupation of the table.

**CLERK:** Pellan?

**NIMERA:** Yes?

**CLERK:** You're only assigned half that table.

Nimera opens her mouth.

**NIMERA:** Technically—

**CYANIS:** No.

**TORREN:** No.

Nimera looks between them, offended and delighted.

**NIMERA:** Fuck both of you.

End.
