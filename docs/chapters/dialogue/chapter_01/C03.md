> **Chapter 1 — Brackenwall and the Wayfinder**

**APPROVED / LOCKED**

## PRODUCTION

Very cheap 2.5D.

One sheltered roadside/rest background.

Existing fire.

One pot.

Three bowls.

Bread.

Ladle.

Water.

Reusable sitting/eating/stirring poses.

No cooking montage.

No elaborate food animation.

No Maevra required for this scene.

## B001 — DINNER

Evening.

Torren is stirring something in a pot.

Cyanis walks over.

Looks into it.

Keeps looking.

**CYANIS:** What is that?

**TORREN:** Dinner.

**CYANIS:** I understood the category.

Torren stirs once.

**TORREN:** Then why'd you ask?

Cyanis looks into the pot again.

**CYANIS:** Hoping for specifics.

**TORREN:** Beans.

Beat.

**TORREN:** Meat.

Another beat.

**TORREN:** Other things.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Several things have surrendered.

Torren points the ladle at him.

**TORREN:** You eating?

**CYANIS:** Obviously.

## B002 — STANDARDS

Ilyra sits down with her bowl.

Cyanis tastes his.

Stops.

Looks at Torren.

Torren is already eating.

**CYANIS:** You made this on purpose.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** Interesting.

**TORREN:** Hot.

Cyanis waits.

**TORREN:** Enough.

Ilyra looks over.

**TORREN:** Safe.

Another bite.

**TORREN:** Cheap.

**CYANIS:** Those are your standards?

**TORREN:** Those are the jobs.

**ILYRA:** Taste isn't a job?

**TORREN:** Bonus.

Ilyra tastes hers again.

Thinks.

**ILYRA:** I don't think it's bad.

Cyanis turns toward her.

**CYANIS:** You don't have to protect him.

Torren looks at Ilyra.

**TORREN:** Don't help me.

**ILYRA:** I wasn't—

**TORREN:** Makes him worse.

**CYANIS:** I'm sitting right here.

**ILYRA:** I noticed.

## B003 — THE BREAD

Cyanis tears off a piece of bread.

Takes a bite.

Immediately looks offended.

**CYANIS:** Jesus Christ.

Torren looks up.

**TORREN:** What?

Cyanis holds up the bread.

**CYANIS:** This is terrible.

**TORREN:** Didn't make it.

**CYANIS:** You served it.

**TORREN:** Was bread when I bought it.

Ilyra takes a piece.

Tests it with her fingers.

**ILYRA:** It's very hard.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**ILYRA:** How old is it?

**TORREN:** Don't know.

**CYANIS:** Could kill somebody with this.

**TORREN:** Then don't waste it.

Cyanis looks at the bread.

Then at Torren.

**CYANIS:** That's not a defense.

**TORREN:** Didn't say it was.

Ilyra dips hers into the bowl.

Waits.

Takes a bite.

**ILYRA:** There.

Cyanis watches her.

**ILYRA:** It's fine if you soak it.

Torren points at her with his spoon.

**TORREN:** See?

**CYANIS:** You said not to help you.

**TORREN:** Changed my mind.

Ilyra realizes she has somehow made the argument worse.

**ILYRA:** I'm done participating.

## B004 — SECOND BOWL

A little later.

Cyanis holds out his empty bowl.

Torren looks at it.

Then at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** Thought it was bad.

**CYANIS:** It is.

Torren does not move.

Cyanis keeps holding the bowl out.

**CYANIS:** There's still food.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** And I'm still hungry.

Torren ladles him another serving.

**TORREN:** Strong endorsement.

**CYANIS:** Don't get excited.

Ilyra looks at Cyanis's second bowl.

**ILYRA:** You ate all of the first one.

**CYANIS:** I contain contradictions.

**TORREN:** Mostly food, apparently.

Cyanis starts eating.

## B005 — WHY SO MUCH

Ilyra looks at the pot.

There is still a lot left.

**ILYRA:** You always make this much?

**TORREN:** Usually.

**ILYRA:** For yourself?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

Cyanis looks at the pot.

**CYANIS:** Why?

Torren shrugs.

**TORREN:** When you're cooking for six, you make a meal.

He takes another bite.

**TORREN:** Cooking for one, you make enough not to do it again tomorrow.

Ilyra nods slowly.

**ILYRA:** That's actually sensible.

Torren looks at her.

**TORREN:** Thank you.

**CYANIS:** Don't encourage him.

Ilyra looks between them.

**ILYRA:** I can't agree with either of you.

**CYANIS:** Why not?

**ILYRA:** Because whichever one I agree with becomes unbearable.

**TORREN:** Fair.

**CYANIS:** Cowardice.

Ilyra keeps eating.

## B006 — AFTER DINNER

The argument runs out.

Not because anything meaningful happened.

They're just eating.

Cyanis finishes most of his second bowl.

Ilyra soaks another piece of the terrible bread.

Torren finishes his food.

A few seconds pass.

Nobody speaks.

The practical reason for sitting together is basically over.

Nobody gets up.

Torren reaches over and moves the pot away from the hottest part of the fire.

Then he takes out a blunt.

He leans toward the coals and catches the end from an ember.

A small glow.

Cheap looping smoke.

He sits back and takes a pull.

Nobody comments on it.

Conversation does not turn toward the smoking.

More quiet.

Comfortable enough.

No one comments on that either.

## B007 — TOMORROW

Cyanis looks at the little food left in his bowl.

**CYANIS:** You know what would've helped?

Torren doesn't look up.

**TORREN:** Salt?

Cyanis pauses.

**CYANIS:** Actually, yes.

Torren nods.

**TORREN:** Tomorrow night, you cook.

Cyanis immediately:

**CYANIS:** I withdraw my complaint.

**TORREN:** Too late.

Ilyra laughs into her bowl.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** You could help me.

**ILYRA:** I already established the danger of that.

Torren reaches for the pot with his free hand, blunt still between two fingers.

Cyanis looks at Ilyra.

**CYANIS:** How bad do you think tomorrow is going to be?

**ILYRA:** Depends.

**CYANIS:** On?

**ILYRA:** Whether he makes you use the bread.

Cyanis looks at the remaining loaf.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** Fuck.

Ilyra starts laughing again.

Torren quietly smiles and takes another pull.

Fade.
