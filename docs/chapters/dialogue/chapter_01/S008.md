## B001 — HOLLOW WATCH

The party reaches the service entrance.

Hollow Watch is larger up close.

A later Yahtrean watchtower has been built over much older stonework. Roots run through collapsed retaining walls.

The service gate hangs open.

A chain has been cut.

Maevra crouches beside it.

**MAEVRA:** Recent.

Cyanis looks at the mud.

**CYANIS:** Tracks coming out?

Maevra follows them with her eyes.

**MAEVRA:** Some.

**CYANIS:** Walking?

She points to a dropped tool and a long drag through the mud.

**MAEVRA:** No.

Ilyra checks a blood mark beside the entrance.

**ILYRA:** Not much blood.

**CYANIS:** Good.

Ilyra stands.

**ILYRA:** Not the same as safe.

Cyanis draws his sword.

**CYANIS:** No.

Maevra looks through the open gate.

**MAEVRA:** Let's go.

Control returns.

## EXPLORATION 1 — OUTER WATCH

A moderate random-battle area, not a hallway and not a huge dungeon floor.

Expected first-pass pressure: roughly 2–3 random encounters, depending on encounter rolls and side exploration.

Cheap 2.5D:

**• one outer-watch environment kit;**

**• broken tower rooms;**

**• short exterior wall section;**

**• one lower stair;**

**• one small optional dead end;**

**• changed doors, rubble and lighting rather than bespoke rooms.**

The player gets enough uninterrupted space to actually use the Cyanis / Ilyra / Maevra party.

Hollow Watch Sentries establish the construct ecology.

No long tutorial.

## SHORT POST-BATTLE BEAT

After an early encounter, one disabled Sentry remains partially active.

It reaches toward a recess in the wall.

A small light flashes.

Then the construct goes still.

Ilyra notices.

**ILYRA:** It signaled.

Maevra looks at the recess.

**MAEVRA:** Assume it worked.

Cyanis looks farther into the Watch.

**CYANIS:** Great.

**MAEVRA:** Move.

Control returns immediately.

## B002 — LOWER ACCESS

The main route reaches a broken interior span.

A later Yahtrean control lever has been bolted onto much older green-gold machinery beneath it.

No one gives a history lesson.

Cyanis looks at the lever.

Then at the gap.

**CYANIS:** Think it still works?

Maevra looks at the machinery.

**MAEVRA:** Try it.

Cyanis pulls it.

The bridge shifts into place.

Simple environment-state swap.

Something much older beneath the floor lights briefly.

Ilyra watches it.

**ILYRA:** That looks familiar.

Cyanis looks down.

**CYANIS:** Close.

Maevra is already crossing.

**MAEVRA:** Keep moving.

No lore conversation.

## B003 — BLACK HOST WORKSITE

A small safe investigation pocket.

No random encounter inside.

Cheap props:

broken digging tools,

charcoal,

wax sheets,

one cracked pry bar,

sections of wall that have been deliberately scrubbed clean.

Maevra picks up the pry bar.

**MAEVRA:** They weren't defending the tower.

Cyanis looks at the exposed wall.

**CYANIS:** Searching.

Ilyra finds blood on a discarded cloth.

**ILYRA:** And something interrupted them.

Cyanis looks at branching grooves in the stone.

**CYANIS:** Writing?

Maevra compares one of the wax rubbings to the wall.

**MAEVRA:** Maybe not.

The lines connect across the rubbing.

Cyanis looks at the pattern.

**CYANIS:** Routes?

Ilyra studies it.

**ILYRA:** Could be.

Cyanis nods.

No argument over certainty.

Maevra puts the rubbing away.

**MAEVRA:** Whatever they thought it was, they wanted the rest.

## EXPLORATION 2 — INNER WATCH

Another moderate combat/exploration stretch.

Again, not huge.

Expected first-pass pressure: roughly 2–3 random encounters.

The same core Hollow Watch assets are recomposed into:

**• lower corridors;**

**• one circular defense room;**

**• short raised gallery;**

**• damaged gate-control section.**

Combat formations become slightly more complex.

The player begins seeing Sentries interacting with Ballista support fixtures, so the boss mechanic is learned through ordinary play rather than explained before the fight.

After the first formation using that relationship:

**MAEVRA:** Small one marked the shot.

Cyanis looks at the Ballista fixture.

**CYANIS:** Then kill the small one first.

**ILYRA:** Or interrupt it.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** Also acceptable.

Control resumes.

## B004 — LOWER JUNCTION

The party reaches a circular chamber.

Six damaged channels meet in the stone floor.

Nobody knows what the six channels represent.

A worn relief shows:

adults,

children,

handcarts,

animals,

and injured people on litters

moving through an open gate.

Ilyra looks at the relief.

**ILYRA:** Civilians.

Cyanis looks at the floor channels.

**CYANIS:** Evacuation route?

Maevra studies both directions depicted in the relief.

**MAEVRA:** Maybe.

She points.

**MAEVRA:** Looks like people moved both ways.

Ilyra looks at the carved litters.

**ILYRA:** Whatever this was, they expected injured people.

Silence for a moment.

Cyanis looks back toward the Black Host worksite.

**CYANIS:** And they dug through half the fucking tower looking for it.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

She looks toward the deeper gate.

**MAEVRA:** Which means we're not done.

## B005 — INNER GATE

The gate ahead has already been forced.

Recent Black Host damage exposes older machinery inside it.

The route beyond leads directly to a much larger chamber.

Random encounters shut off here.

A recovery/save point is available before the boss.

No camp scene.

No pre-boss speech.

Cyanis looks through the opening.

**CYANIS:** That's big.

Maevra looks past him.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** Just checking.

They enter.

## BOSS — HOLLOW WATCH CASTELLAN

The Castellan is integrated directly into the fortress wall.

It initially looks more like part of the architecture than a walking creature.

Cheap 2.5D boss production:

**• one boss chamber;**

**• fortress-state model/pose;**

**• Ballista and Watch Seal support already built into the arena;**

**• short threshold cut-in;**

**• Walking-state swap;**

**• lighting/dust change;**

**• no transformation movie.**

Maevra takes position.

**MAEVRA:** Positions.

Cyanis sees the smaller support hardware.

**CYANIS:** Same setup as the others.

**MAEVRA:** Then use it.

Battle begins.

## STATE A — FORTRESS

One HP bar.

The Castellan uses:

**• Ballista support;**

**• Watch Seal support;**

**• direct fortress attacks.**

Support pieces are finite.

If destroyed, they stay destroyed.

No infinite respawning support.

## SAME-BAR THRESHOLD

At the authored threshold, the Castellan tears itself free from the wall.

Short battle pause.

Dust.

Environment-state change.

No HP refill.

No free threshold attack.

Permanent damage to destroyed support remains permanent.

The Castellan lands in its Walking state.

Cyanis looks up.

**CYANIS:** Of course it walks.

**MAEVRA:** Move.

Battle resumes immediately.

## STATE B — WALKING

The same health bar continues.

The Castellan becomes more directly aggressive because it is no longer anchored to the fortress.

Previously destroyed support remains gone.

The boss falls on the same HP bar.

## B006 — AFTERMATH

The Castellan collapses.

Dust settles.

Nobody immediately talks over it.

Ilyra checks Cyanis quickly.

Then notices Maevra favoring one arm.

**ILYRA:** Arm.

Maevra moves it once.

**MAEVRA:** Fine.

Ilyra looks at her.

**ILYRA:** Then later.

Maevra nods.

That's it.

No medical argument.

Ilyra moves on.

## B007 — THE ROUTE PLATE

The Castellan's collapse has exposed a protected stone plate behind it.

Same boss chamber.

One wall-state swap.

No new room.

Branching route lines cover the plate.

One surviving line runs southeast.

Recent Black Host charcoal traces the same branch.

Maevra compares it with the rubbing from upstairs.

**MAEVRA:** Same branch.

Cyanis looks at the route.

**CYANIS:** Greenhollow?

**MAEVRA:** That region.

**ILYRA:** What's there?

**MAEVRA:** Villages. Forestry routes. Old crossings.

Cyanis looks at the Black Host charcoal.

**CYANIS:** Nothing they should care this much about.

**MAEVRA:** Apparently they disagree.

Ilyra looks at the route plate.

**ILYRA:** Then Greenhollow.

Maevra nods.

No one identifies:

a Face,

a Prime,

a giant underground Crest,

a hidden Network,

or what the six channels ultimately represent.

They only know someone was studying routes and followed one toward Greenhollow.

At the surface:

**MAEVRA:** Brackenwall. Report. Resupply.

Ilyra starts walking.

**ILYRA:** Then Greenhollow. After food.

Cyanis opens his mouth.

Maevra looks at him.

**MAEVRA:** Food.

Cyanis closes his mouth.

**CYANIS:** Fine.
