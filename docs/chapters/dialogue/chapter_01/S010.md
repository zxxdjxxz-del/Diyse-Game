The maintained Greenhollow road gives way to older route stone.

Across the ridge, Wayfinder Junction appears between the trees occasionally.

**TORREN:** Old one moved.

Maevra looks at it.

**MAEVRA:** Third?

**TORREN:** Third.

Ilyra looks at the disturbed ground.

**ILYRA:** How recent?

**TORREN:** Day or two.

**MAEVRA:** Host?

The mud.

## EXPLORATION 1 — UPPER PASSAGE

A moderate hostile field stretch.

Not a corridor, but not a giant open zone either.

Cheap 2.5D uses one Briar Passage forest/stone-road kit with:

**• old route stones;**

**• overgrown sections;**

**• one higher shelf;**

**• shallow drainage;**

**• one short side loop;**

**• one optional reward pocket;**

**• Wayfinder Junction periodically visible across the ridge.**

Normal wilderness random encounters begin after the short entry buffer.

There is no forced 'first four-person tutorial fight.'

The player simply gets to use Torren normally as the fourth party member.

A direct route should still give enough room for several ordinary fights to happen naturally; exploration may produce more.

## B003 — FALSE TRAIL

A safe environmental pocket interrupts encounter pressure.

A route marker points toward what looks like a usable trail.

Torren stops.

No QTE.

No damage.

## B005 — ROUTE SPLIT

The party reaches a broad overlook.

Encounter pressure shuts off.

Two routes are physically readable.

Upper shelf

Shorter.

Higher.

Recently damaged.

Narrow enough that extracting an injured person would be difficult.

Lower causeway

Longer.

Wider.

Fresh boot traffic.

More cover.

More possible exits.

Rain is approaching.

Torren studies both.

Maevra waits.

**MAEVRA:** Which?

**TORREN:** Don't know yet.

Cyanis looks at both routes.

**CYANIS:** What do we know?

Torren points up.

**TORREN:** Upper's faster.

He studies the remaining shelf.

**TORREN:** Rock under it is probably sound.

**MAEVRA:** Probably.

Torren looks at her.

**TORREN:** Yes.

No apology.

Ilyra studies the narrow section.

**ILYRA:** If somebody gets hurt on the far side, can we bring them back through that?

Torren looks again.

Longer this time.

**TORREN:** No.

Ilyra nods.

That's information, not victory.

Maevra looks at the sky.

**MAEVRA:** Rain?

**TORREN:** Makes the shelf worse.

Cyanis looks toward the lower causeway.

**CYANIS:** Tracks?

**TORREN:** Boots.

Beat.

**TORREN:** Can't identify them from here.

**CYANIS:** Can you scout both?

Torren looks at the sky.

Then the routes.

**TORREN:** Not properly.

Maevra doesn't tell him to try harder.

**MAEVRA:** How much time would you want?

**TORREN:** Hour.

Maevra looks east.

**MAEVRA:** We don't have it.

Torren knows.

He doesn't like it.

Nobody fills the silence for him.

## B006 — THE ROUTE HE WOULD TAKE

Torren looks at the upper shelf again.

**TORREN:** Upper's faster.

He looks toward Ilyra.

**TORREN:** She's right about getting somebody back out.

Then the lower route.

**TORREN:** Lower gives us more room.

Beat.

**TORREN:** More ways out if the tracks are hostile.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Which would you take?

**TORREN:** I don't know enough.

**CYANIS:** I know.

Torren looks at him.

Cyanis doesn't change the question.

**CYANIS:** Which would you take?

**TORREN:** Lower.

**CYANIS:** Why?

**TORREN:** Because armed people are a problem I understand.

**TORREN:** I can't promise the route.

Beat.

**TORREN:** This is the one I would take.

Cyanis nods.

**CYANIS:** Good enough.

That matters.

Maevra looks at the lower path.

**MAEVRA:** Lower route.

Control returns.

The party does not get a fake player choice after the authored decision.

## EXPLORATION 2 — LOWER CAUSEWAY

This is the larger of S010's two playable stretches, but still moderate rather than huge.

The same environment family is recomposed into:

**• lower stone road;**

**• wooded embankment;**

**• an old drainage crossing;**

**• one short branch/loop;**

**• a wider ruined route section;**

**• the final old timber-and-stone crossing.**

Random encounters resume naturally.

No quota.

The direct path should usually expose the player to a few ordinary fights, while side exploration can add more.

One branch can contain a tougher optional formation or useful reward.

No bespoke mini-dungeon.

As the player progresses, evidence becomes clearer without stopping for dialogue every time:

cut brush,

boot marks,

broken load strap,

black-red residue,

heavy drag marks.

Eventually Cyanis looks down at a clear print.

**CYANIS:** Host?

Torren crouches.

This time there is enough evidence.

**TORREN:** Probably.

Maevra nods.

Nobody says, See, you were right.

The evidence changed.

So did the answer.

## B007 — THE CROSSING

Torren checks the remaining structure.

**CYANIS:** Meaning?

**MAEVRA:** Drop the span.

**TORREN:** Greenhollow uses this crossing when the river road floods.

**ILYRA:** For carts too?

**TORREN:** Carts. Litters.

Beat.
