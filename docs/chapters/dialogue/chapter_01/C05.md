## PRODUCTION

Brackenwall, evening.

Small supply/medical work area.

Cheap 2.5D:

**• one table;**

**• Ilyra's medical pouch and loose supplies;**

**• Maevra's reports;**

**• cups;**

**• existing sit/sort/read/stand poses;**

**• portraits and expression changes.**

Maevra's arm is still sore from Hollow Watch.

No lore.

No Hunt material.

No relationship speech.

No gameplay reward.

The scene is not two women managing an irresponsible man. The joke only works because Ilyra and Maevra are doing essentially the same shit themselves.

## B001 — RESTING

Ilyra is checking medical supplies.

Maevra is reading a report.

**ILYRA:** You're supposed to be resting.

**MAEVRA:** I am sitting down.

**ILYRA:** That's not the same thing.

Maevra looks at Ilyra's half-emptied medical pouch.

**MAEVRA:** Neither is reorganizing that.

**ILYRA:** We're not discussing me.

**MAEVRA:** Convenient.

## B002 — FINE

Ilyra's attention drops to Maevra's arm.

Maevra catches the look.

**MAEVRA:** Fine.

Ilyra keeps looking.

Beat.

**MAEVRA:** Sore.

**ILYRA:** Better.

**MAEVRA:** I preferred fine.

**ILYRA:** I know.

Ilyra goes back to the pouch.

Maevra goes back to the report.

Neither stops working.

## B003 — DOVAREN

A little while passes.

**ILYRA:** Does Dovaren ever stop moving?

**MAEVRA:** No.

Ilyra looks up.

**ILYRA:** That was fast.

**MAEVRA:** I've researched the subject.

**ILYRA:** Results conclusive?

**MAEVRA:** Depressingly.

## B004 — TEN MINUTES

Maevra turns a page.

**MAEVRA:** I told him ten minutes this morning.

**ILYRA:** I know.

**MAEVRA:** He took that to mean he needed to walk twice as fast.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

Maevra looks at her.

**MAEVRA:** You didn't warn me.

Ilyra continues sorting supplies.

**ILYRA:** I thought it would be funny.

Maevra stares at her.

Beat.

**MAEVRA:** It was a little funny.

Ilyra smiles.

## B005 — FOOD

**ILYRA:** He does it with food too.

**MAEVRA:** Refuses?

**ILYRA:** Not exactly.

Ilyra closes one compartment.

Opens another.

**ILYRA:** He treats eating as something he'll get around to once every other living person has been accounted for.

**MAEVRA:** Ah.

Ilyra looks at Maevra's report.

**ILYRA:** You know the type.

Maevra very deliberately looks back down at the page.

## B006 — STAY FOCUSED

**ILYRA:** Oh, don't do that.

**MAEVRA:** Do what?

**ILYRA:** Pretend I wasn't also talking about you.

**MAEVRA:** We're complaining about Dovaren.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

**MAEVRA:** Stay focused.

Ilyra gives her a look.

**ILYRA:** Commander.

Maevra catches the mock obedience immediately.

**MAEVRA:** I heard that.

**ILYRA:** Good.

That is where their friendship starts showing without either of them announcing it.

## B007 — QUESTIONS

**ILYRA:** He asks questions at inconvenient times.

**MAEVRA:** Constantly.

**ILYRA:** Not stupid questions.

**MAEVRA:** No.

**ILYRA:** That would be easier.

**MAEVRA:** Awful.

**ILYRA:** It is when you're busy.

Maevra thinks.

**MAEVRA:** Useful, though.

Ilyra looks at her.

**ILYRA:** We're complaining.

**MAEVRA:** Sorry.

## B008 — FINE, AGAIN

Maevra sets one report aside.

**MAEVRA:** Your turn.

**ILYRA:** He says he's fine.

**MAEVRA:** That's it?

**ILYRA:** No. That's the opening move.

Ilyra imitates the exchange without doing a Cyanis voice.

**ILYRA:** Does your shoulder hurt?

Beat.

**ILYRA:** Torren took the harder side of that last fight.

Maevra understands immediately.

## B009 — LOGISTICS

**MAEVRA:** What if you ask again?

**ILYRA:** He becomes very interested in logistics.

**MAEVRA:** Of course he does.

**ILYRA:** Water. Supplies. Distance. Watches. Anything with a number.

Maevra folds her report slightly.

**MAEVRA:** Have you tried threatening him?

Ilyra looks over.

**ILYRA:** Medically?

**MAEVRA:** I wasn't going to specify.

Ilyra laughs.

## B010 — CAUGHT

Cyanis enters carrying gear.

Maevra sees him first.

**MAEVRA:** Dovaren.

**CYANIS:** Commander.

Ilyra looks over.

**ILYRA:** Have you eaten?

Cyanis keeps walking.

**CYANIS:** Recently.

**MAEVRA:** That is not an answer.

**CYANIS:** It was intended as one.

## B011 — EAT

Maevra does not even look up from the report.

**MAEVRA:** Eat.

At exactly the same time:

**ILYRA:** Eat.

Cyanis stops.

Looks between them.

**CYANIS:** Is this coordinated?

**MAEVRA:** No.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

Ilyra looks at Maevra.

Maevra looks at Ilyra.

**ILYRA:** Apparently we're still refining the procedure.

## B012 — FOOD

**CYANIS:** I see.

**MAEVRA:** Food.

**CYANIS:** I was going—

**ILYRA:** Cyanis.

He stops.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** Fine.

He puts the gear down.

## B013 — AT LENGTH

Cyanis looks at them.

Then the reports.

Then the medical supplies.

**CYANIS:** Were you talking about me?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**ILYRA:** At length.

**CYANIS:** Anything flattering?

Silence.

Cyanis waits.

Neither woman helps him.

**CYANIS:** That's hurtful.

## B014 — USEFUL

Maevra finally puts the report down.

**MAEVRA:** You ask useful questions.

**ILYRA:** At terrible times.

Cyanis points at Maevra.

**CYANIS:** There it is.

**ILYRA:** You notice when other people need something.

**MAEVRA:** And become mysteriously stupid when the other person is you.

Cyanis looks between them.

**CYANIS:** That feels less like complaining and more like an ambush.

**MAEVRA:** Eat.

## B015 — HYPOCRITES

Cyanis starts toward the food.

Then stops.

Slowly looks back.

At Maevra's reports.

At Ilyra's half-repacked medical kit.

A smile starts.

Both women see it.

Immediately:

**ILYRA:** Don't.

**CYANIS:** You're both working.

**MAEVRA:** Dovaren.

**CYANIS:** While telling me to stop working.

**ILYRA:** Not the same thing.

Cyanis nods solemnly.

**CYANIS:** Of course not.

He picks his gear back up.

**CYANIS:** Wonderful conversation. Very educational.

## B016 — EVERYBODY STOPS

**MAEVRA:** Put that down.

**CYANIS:** Why?

**MAEVRA:** Because now you're being difficult on purpose.

Cyanis considers that.

**CYANIS:** Fair.

He puts the gear back down.

Ilyra closes one section of the medical pouch.

**ILYRA:** Food.

Cyanis looks at both women.

Neither one has moved.

**CYANIS:** Both of you coming?

Silence.

Cyanis waits.

**CYANIS:** Thought so.

Ilyra looks at Maevra.

Maevra looks at the report.

Then deliberately leaves it on the table.

Ilyra closes the medical pouch completely.

No speech.

They just stop.

## B017 — I HATE HIM

Cyanis heads toward the doorway.

Maevra watches him.

**MAEVRA:** I hate him.

Ilyra stands.

**ILYRA:** No, you don't.

Maevra exhales.

**MAEVRA:** I know.

From the doorway:

**CYANIS:** I can still hear you.

Maevra does not miss a beat.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

## B018 — DINNER

They stand.

Ilyra takes the closed medical pouch but does not reopen it.

Maevra leaves the report behind.

Cyanis waits instead of walking off without them.

**MAEVRA:** If the food's gone—

**CYANIS:** Torren cooked.

Maevra relaxes.

**MAEVRA:** There's some left.

**CYANIS:** Probably.

Ilyra looks at him.

**ILYRA:** You've been spending too much time with Torren.

Cyanis considers it.

**CYANIS:** Probably.

They leave together.

Cut.

**END C05.**
