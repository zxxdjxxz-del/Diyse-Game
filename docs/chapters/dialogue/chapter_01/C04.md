> **Chapter 1 — Brackenwall and the Wayfinder**

**APPROVED / LOCKED**

## PRODUCTION

Brackenwall, late evening.

One small sheltered route room/table.

Cheap 2.5D:

**• Torren’s battered personal map;**

**• Crown-issued route map;**

**• charcoal;**

**• map weights;**

**• cup;**

**• Torren’s bow nearby;**

**• existing sit/stand/point/write poses.**

No special handwriting animation.

No lore reveal.

No Hunt progression.

No mandatory story information.

Just Cyanis bothering Torren while Torren works.

## B001 — CURIOUS

Torren is correcting the official route map against his own battered one.

Cyanis stands behind him.

Torren keeps writing.

**TORREN:** You need something?

**CYANIS:** No.

**TORREN:** Then why are you standing over me?

**CYANIS:** Curious.

**TORREN:** Dangerous habit.

**CYANIS:** I’ve heard.

Cyanis pulls over a chair.

## B002 — FIXING IT

Cyanis studies the map.

**CYANIS:** What are you doing?

**TORREN:** Fixing it.

**CYANIS:** The map?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** That sounds arrogant.

Torren glances at him.

**TORREN:** River moved.

**CYANIS:** Ah.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** Then the river’s arrogant.

**TORREN:** I’ll tell it.

## B003 — EVIDENCE

Torren crosses out part of a road.

**CYANIS:** You killed a road.

**TORREN:** Road’s already gone.

**CYANIS:** According to?

**TORREN:** Me.

Cyanis smiles.

**CYANIS:** We’re back to arrogant.

**TORREN:** I walked it yesterday.

**CYANIS:** Evidence ruins everything.

Torren keeps writing.

## B004 — PROBABLY

Torren points at the terrain.

**TORREN:** Bank collapsed here. Water’s cutting farther south now.

He indicates another line.

**TORREN:** This’ll probably be mud after two good rains.

**CYANIS:** Probably.

Torren looks at him.

**CYANIS:** I’m learning your language.

Torren goes back to the map.

## B005 — WAGON

Cyanis points to another route.

**CYANIS:** Would you take this one?

**TORREN:** On foot?

**CYANIS:** Sure.

**TORREN:** Maybe.

**CYANIS:** Horse?

**TORREN:** No.

**CYANIS:** Wagon?

Torren laughs once.

**TORREN:** Fuck no.

**CYANIS:** Excellent. First definitive answer of the evening.

## B006 — OLD WHORE

Cyanis leans farther over Torren’s map.

A handwritten note catches his eye.

He squints.

**CYANIS:** Old whore.

Torren looks up instantly.

**TORREN:** Bitch.

Cyanis freezes.

Half a second.

Then he realizes Torren thought Cyanis had just casually called him an old whore.

Cyanis completely loses it.

Torren stares at him.

**TORREN:** What?

Cyanis can barely point at the map.

Torren looks down.

Reads the actual note.

Beat.

**TORREN:** That does not say “old whore.”

Cyanis looks at the handwriting again.

**CYANIS:** Yes the fuck it does.

**TORREN:** No, it doesn’t.

**CYANIS:** Read it.

Torren reads the actual note quickly.

Cyanis looks at the map.

Then at him.

**CYANIS:** Bullshit.

**TORREN:** Learn to read.

**CYANIS:** Learn to write.

## B007 — RETALIATION

Torren takes the map a little farther away from Cyanis.

**TORREN:** Oh, fuck you.

**CYANIS:** You called me a bitch.

**TORREN:** You called me an old whore.

**CYANIS:** I was reading the map!

**TORREN:** Then learn to fucking read.

Cyanis is still laughing.

## B008 — AGAIN

Cyanis wipes his eyes.

**CYANIS:** Didn’t know you had that in you.

**TORREN:** Immediate retaliation.

Beat.

**TORREN:** You thought I’d sit here and let you call me an old whore?

Cyanis laughs again.

**TORREN:** Stop.

**CYANIS:** I’m trying.

**TORREN:** Try harder.

**CYANIS:** Don’t say old whore again.

Torren stops.

Looks Cyanis directly in the eye.

**TORREN:** Old whore.

Cyanis folds forward laughing.

Torren holds it for another second.

Then he finally starts laughing too.

Actually laughing.

No portrait flourish.

No friendship-increases beat.

They’re just being idiots.

## B009 — THE ACTUAL MAP

Eventually they settle.

Torren goes back to work.

Cyanis remains beside him.

**CYANIS:** All right.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** Show me what I’m actually looking at.

Torren pulls the map closer between them.

**TORREN:** This line’s the official road.

**CYANIS:** Currently alive?

**TORREN:** Mostly.

**CYANIS:** Good start.

## B010 — WHAT A ROAD IS

Torren traces several smaller marks.

**TORREN:** People move. Road’s just where enough of them keep doing it.

**CYANIS:** That’s annoyingly sensible.

**TORREN:** I get that a lot.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Do you?

**TORREN:** No.

## B011 — UGLY MAP

Cyanis compares Torren’s map with the Crown-issued one.

**CYANIS:** Yours is uglier.

**TORREN:** Mine’s useful.

**CYANIS:** The Crown cartographers would be wounded.

**TORREN:** They can come walk the fucking road.

Cyanis points at him.

**CYANIS:** There he is.

**TORREN:** Who?

**CYANIS:** The man who called me a bitch ten minutes after dinner.

**TORREN:** You called me—

Torren stops himself.

**TORREN:** No.

Cyanis grins.

**CYANIS:** Coward.

## B012 — I DON’T KNOW

Torren indicates an uncertain mark.

**TORREN:** This one’s old.

**CYANIS:** How old?

**TORREN:** Eight months since I checked it.

**CYANIS:** So?

**TORREN:** So I don’t know.

**CYANIS:** Okay.

Torren pauses.

Looks at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** You expected me to argue.

**CYANIS:** Most people hear “I don’t know” and ask again louder.

**TORREN:** Would that help?

**CYANIS:** No.

**TORREN:** Then that sounds exhausting.

Cyanis nods.

No speech about trust.

They just keep going.

## B013 — GREENHOLLOW’S BRIDGE

Cyanis recognizes a mark.

**CYANIS:** That’s our bridge.

**TORREN:** Greenhollow’s bridge.

**CYANIS:** Right.

Cyanis reads Torren’s annotation.

**CYANIS:** Standing. Damaged. Civilian flood route.

**TORREN:** That’s what it is.

**CYANIS:** Good.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

Nothing about Torren saving it.

Nothing self-congratulatory.

The map records what the bridge is for.

## B014 — HELPING

Cyanis reaches toward the charcoal.

Torren catches the movement immediately.

**TORREN:** No.

**CYANIS:** I was helping.

**TORREN:** You were reaching.

**CYANIS:** First stage of helping.

**TORREN:** Not with my map.

**CYANIS:** Afraid I’ll improve the handwriting?

Torren looks at him.

**TORREN:** Touch the map and I’ll break your fucking fingers.

Cyanis smiles.

**CYANIS:** See, now we’re friends.

**TORREN:** That’s your standard?

Cyanis considers it.

**CYANIS:** Apparently.

Torren shakes his head.

Starts folding the map.

## B015 — ONE MORE TIME

Cyanis watches him carefully.

**CYANIS:** What’s that one say?

Torren does not look up.

**TORREN:** Don’t start.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** Old—

Torren looks up.

Cyanis is already grinning.

**TORREN:** You little—

Cyanis laughs and starts toward the door.

**CYANIS:** Good night, Torren.

**TORREN:** Fuck off, Cyanis.

Cyanis does not turn around.

**CYANIS:** Bitch.

Torren stares after him.

Beat.

Then he laughs despite himself.

Cut.
