## B001 — GREENHOLLOW

Greenhollow is a small working route community.

Cheap 2.5D: one compact village kit centered around the route board, bridge approach, a few workshops/houses, and trail exits. Most village life is reusable background loops.

A carpenter replaces rotten bridge boards.

Wet route notices hang beside older ones.

A courier argues with somebody over which road is still usable.

At the public route board, a ranger in a ghillie cape has crossed a line through one Crown road.

Beside it:

**BANK GONE**

Maevra stops.

**MAEVRA:** Harth.

The ranger keeps writing.

**TORREN:** Solmar.

Cyanis glances at Ilyra.

Ilyra notices.

Says nothing.

Torren finishes the mark.

Maevra steps closer.

**MAEVRA:** You crossed out a Crown route.

**TORREN:** River crossed it out first.

**MAEVRA:** The report says passable.

**TORREN:** Report's four days old.

Maevra looks at the map.

Then back at him.

No smile.

No hostility either.

Torren notices the bruising on her arm.

**TORREN:** Left arm.

**MAEVRA:** Fine.

Torren nods.

Accepts it immediately.

Cyanis notices that too.

Still says nothing.

## B002 — THE MAP

Torren finally looks at Cyanis and Ilyra.

**TORREN:** Crest Knight?

**CYANIS:** Cyanis.

**TORREN:** Torren.

**ILYRA:** Ilyra.

Maevra taps the map.

**MAEVRA:** We need Briar Passage.

**TORREN:** Not like that.

**CYANIS:** What's wrong with it?

**TORREN:** Bridge here is gone.

**TORREN:** North shelf turns to shit after rain.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** Usable?

Torren looks at him.

**TORREN:** Different question.

Cyanis nods.

**CYANIS:** All right.

No one demands false certainty from him.

## B003 — HIS MARKS

Maevra notices several charcoal marks on smaller side routes.

They do not match the official route-office notation.

**MAEVRA:** These yours?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**MAEVRA:** How many have you checked?

**TORREN:** This week?

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**TORREN:** Seven.

A woman working at the route board speaks without looking up.

This is EDDA HARTH, Greenhollow's route warden.

**EDDA:** Eight.

Torren looks at her.

**TORREN:** Seven.

**EDDA:** You did Mill Cut yesterday.

**TORREN:** Wasn't checking it.

**EDDA:** You replaced two markers.

**TORREN:** They were wrong.

Edda looks at Maevra.

**EDDA:** Mae, it was eight.

Torren gives her a look.

**TORREN:** Don't.

**EDDA:** What?

**TORREN:** You know what.

Edda absolutely does.

She goes back to the board.

Maevra almost smiles.

Doesn't give him the satisfaction.

Edda pins another notice.

**EDDA:** He does half of them before anybody wakes up.

Torren gives her a look.

**EDDA:** And doesn't sign shit.

**TORREN:** People need the road, not my name.

Cyanis looks over the quiet corrections.

Every one directs traffic around a damaged or unsafe section.

He doesn't compliment Torren.

He doesn't need to.

Short exchange off to the side.

Torren turns immediately.

**MAEVRA:** Edda.

Edda looks at her.

**EDDA:** Cart went over near Birch Trail.

Ilyra is already checking her kit.

**ILYRA:** How many?

**EDDA:** Two.

Beat.

**EDDA:** One's leg is bad.

Torren points at the map.

**TORREN:** I can reach them from here.

**MAEVRA:** How long?

**TORREN:** Hour.

Ilyra looks at the terrain.

**ILYRA:** And bring someone back with a serious leg injury?

Torren checks it again.

**TORREN:** No.

No defensiveness.

He points somewhere else.

**TORREN:** Birch Trail.

Edda grimaces.

**EDDA:** Briarhide's been charging people down there.

Cyanis looks at Torren.

**CYANIS:** Hunter?

**TORREN:** Don't know.

**CYANIS:** Useful.

**TORREN:** Need to see it first.

Maevra looks at Edda.

**MAEVRA:** Anything else?

**EDDA:** Mud. Bad footing. Probably two places Harth will tell you aren't roads anymore.

**TORREN:** Three.

Edda doesn't even look at him.

**EDDA:** Three.

The urgency begins here.

Optional Greenhollow wandering is temporarily suspended until the civilians are recovered. The town remains available afterward.

**TORREN:** No.

**TORREN:** Looks like.

**CYANIS:** Recently?

**TORREN:** Maybe.

## AUTHORED ENCOUNTER — BRIARHIDE STALKER

The Briarhide enters the route.

It is frightened, wounded, and unnaturally aggressive.

No villain-monster entrance.

No roar cinematic.

Cheap 2.5D encounter introduction: forest battle state, animal model enters, visible embedded Black Host component/barb.

Torren spots it.

**TORREN:** There.

Cyanis follows his line of sight.

A Black Host fitting is buried into the Briarhide's flank.

**CYANIS:** That supposed to be there?

**TORREN:** Fuck no.

The embedded irritant/component becomes a valid encounter objective.

**ENCOUNTER RULE**

This is nonlethal.

The player's goal is to disable/remove the embedded Black Host component while surviving the Briarhide's attacks.

Torren identifies the ecological problem.

The player party performs the solution.

Torren does not secretly shoot the barb out or receive an NPC combat turn.

No QTE.

No bespoke capture system.

Normal battle rules handle targeting the component.

When the component is removed:

The Briarhide recoils.

Stops.

Cyanis has an opening to attack.

He doesn't.

The animal backs away.

Then disappears into the trees.

Battle ends.

No victory pose.

Torren looks at Cyanis.

Cyanis sheathes his weapon.

Torren says nothing.
