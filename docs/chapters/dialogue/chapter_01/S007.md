## B001 — BRACKENWALL

Morning.

Cheap 2.5D: one main Brackenwall hub kit with a gate street, repair/supply court and artifact yard. Medical/quartermaster activity is mostly background dressing and reused NPC loops.

Brackenwall is busy, not devastated.

Workers replace damaged wall timber.

A wagon is being repaired near the gate.

Soldiers and civilians move through the same streets.

The broken shape of Hollow Watch is visible on the wooded ridge east of town.

Cyanis and Ilyra enter beside the sealed artifact wagon.

**CYANIS:** You said the road would improve.

**ILYRA:** I said it would flatten.

A supply cart hits a rut hard enough to rattle everything on it.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** That's a shitty distinction.

**ILYRA:** It's an accurate one.

A SERGEANT approaches.

**SERGEANT:** Convoy recovery?

Cyanis looks at what remains of the wagon.

**CYANIS:** What's left of it.

The Sergeant checks the seal.

Doesn't touch it.

**SERGEANT:** Artifact yard. Second court.

**ILYRA:** Who's receiving it?

**SERGEANT:** Custody detail.

**CYANIS:** That sounds comforting.

**SERGEANT:** Wasn't meant to.

The Sergeant moves on.

## B002 — CUSTODY

The sealed casing is brought into the outer artifact yard.

A CUSTODY OFFICER checks the seal.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Broken since recovery?

**CYANIS:** No.

**ILYRA:** It reacted after we sealed it.

The Officer looks at her.

Ilyra indicates Cyanis.

**ILYRA:** So did he.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** You could make that sound less fucking ominous.

**ILYRA:** Could.

The Officer records it.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Standard procedure is inner-ward inspection.

He points toward a marked line on the floor.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** You stay outside until containment clears.

Cyanis shrugs.

**CYANIS:** Fine.

**ILYRA:** I'm staying with him.

The Officer looks between them.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Fine.

No argument.

No authority performance.

The transfer crew takes the casing.

They move it toward the inner ward.

One step.

Two.

Then—

A thin green-and-gold geometric pattern appears beneath the casing.

Not a creature.

Not a voice.

Not a manifestation.

Simple animated linework across the existing floor texture.

It extends toward Cyanis.

Cyanis catches the edge of the table.

**CYANIS:** Stop.

**ILYRA:** Stop.

The crew immediately freezes.

Ilyra moves beside him.

**ILYRA:** Eyes.

He looks at her.

**ILYRA:** Pressure?

**CYANIS:** Behind them.

**ILYRA:** Pain?

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

The Custody Officer looks from Cyanis to the geometry.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Back it up.

The crew moves the casing back.

The geometric line retracts.

No explosion.

No giant magical cinematic.

It simply disappears as the distance increases.

Cyanis exhales.

Ilyra watches him.

**ILYRA:** Better?

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

The Officer stares at the floor.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** That's new.

**CYANIS:** Been a productive couple of days.

## B003 — MAEVRA

Maevra enters the yard with two soldiers.

No hero entrance.

No dramatic camera orbit.

The guards straighten because they know who she is.

She has a transfer writ in hand.

She sees:

the stopped casing,

the crew,

Cyanis,

Ilyra,

the fading last trace on the floor.

**MAEVRA:** Who authorized the transfer?

The Custody Officer answers immediately.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** I did. Standard sealed-artifact custody.

Maevra looks at the original marked transfer path.

Then at Cyanis.

Then back to the Officer.

**MAEVRA:** Reasonable.

She folds the writ.

**MAEVRA:** Now obsolete.

The Officer nods once.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Agreed.

That is the whole authority conflict.

Nobody gets humiliated for following a procedure that was reasonable before the facts changed.

Maevra turns to Ilyra.

**MAEVRA:** What happened?

**ILYRA:** Same pressure response as before.

**MAEVRA:** Worse?

**ILYRA:** Faster.

Maevra looks at Cyanis.

**MAEVRA:** How bad?

**CYANIS:** Manageable.

Ilyra looks at him.

Cyanis sees it.

**CYANIS:** Don't.

**ILYRA:** Didn't say anything.

**CYANIS:** Your face did.

Maevra catches the exchange.

Does not interfere.

She turns back to the Custody Officer.

**MAEVRA:** Keep it in the outer yard.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** The writ requires secure custody.

**MAEVRA:** It has secure custody.

Beat.

**MAEVRA:** It doesn't require us to repeat something we now know hurts him.

The Officer considers that.

**CUSTODY OFFICER:** Outer yard. Double watch.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

She looks at the crew.

**MAEVRA:** Nobody tests the distance again.

## B004 — HOLLOW WATCH

A SCOUT enters with a message tube.

Mud up the legs.

Out of breath, but not collapsing theatrically.

**SCOUT:** Commander.

Maevra takes the report.

**MAEVRA:** East patrol?

**SCOUT:** Hollow Watch.

That gets everyone's attention.

**SCOUT:** Fresh tracks around the ruin. Heavy boots. Red fittings.

**CYANIS:** Black Host?

**SCOUT:** Looks like it.

Ilyra looks at the mud and blood on the Scout's shoulder.

**ILYRA:** Who got hit?

The Scout touches the dressing.

**SCOUT:** Me.

**ILYRA:** By what?

**SCOUT:** Stone.

Cyanis looks toward Hollow Watch.

**CYANIS:** Somebody threw a rock at you?

**SCOUT:** No.

Beat.

**SCOUT:** Something inside moved.

He hands Maevra a rubbing.

Branching geometric lines.

Not identical to what just appeared in the yard.

Close enough to be uncomfortable.

Ilyra looks at it.

Then at the floor.

**ILYRA:** Not the same.

**CYANIS:** Close.

Maevra studies the rubbing.

**MAEVRA:** Close is enough to inspect.

No one identifies the pattern as a Face.

No First Champion.

No Prime terminology.

No sudden lore revelation.

## B005 — THE HANDOFF

Maevra returns the rubbing to its tube.

**MAEVRA:** The casing stays here.

To the Scout:

**MAEVRA:** Get fed. Have somebody who knows the lower approach report to the east gate.

**SCOUT:** Yes, Commander.

He leaves.

Maevra turns to Cyanis and Ilyra.

**MAEVRA:** You're coming with me.

**CYANIS:** I assumed that wasn't optional.

**MAEVRA:** Correct.

Ilyra folds her arms.

**ILYRA:** Why us?

Maevra points toward Cyanis.

**MAEVRA:** Because you know what happened when that thing moved.

Then to Ilyra:

**MAEVRA:** And you know what it did to him.

**ILYRA:** If it happens again, we stop.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

No argument.

Cyanis looks toward Hollow Watch.

**CYANIS:** When?

**MAEVRA:** East gate. Ten minutes.

She walks away.

Cyanis watches her go.

**CYANIS:** I like how everybody keeps giving me ten minutes.

**ILYRA:** You keep surviving them.

**CYANIS:** So far.
