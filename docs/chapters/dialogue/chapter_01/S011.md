## B001 — THE CROSSROADS

The party comes out of Briar Passage.

The forest opens.

Ahead is a huge weathered crossroads partly swallowed by the landscape.

There is no building.

Several Ancient Diysean roads once converged here.

Some survive as recognizable stretches of stone.

Others disappear beneath trees, later roads, collapsed slopes, or ground that has shifted enough that following them visually becomes impossible.

Modern Yahtrean paths cut across the old routes without respecting them.

At the center sits the Wayfinder.

A broad stone-and-metal cartographic monument built low into the crossroads itself.

Large enough that the party has to walk around it.

Roots cover sections.

Earth fills others.

One side has been recently uncovered.

Black Host tools and scraps remain nearby.

Torren stops.

**TORREN:** There.

Cyanis looks at the structure.

**CYANIS:** That's the Wayfinder?

Torren studies it.

**TORREN:** I'd assume so.

Maevra looks at the disturbed earth.

**MAEVRA:** They did too.

Fresh pry marks.

Charcoal.

Wax scraps.

Black-red residue.

But no Black Host troops remain.

**MAEVRA:** Spread out.

The player can inspect:

**• the Wayfinder;**

The party does not comment on everything.

## B002 — A ROAD TO NOWHERE

Torren follows one of the Ancient roads away from the Wayfinder.

After a short distance it vanishes beneath forest and earth.

Cyanis follows.

**CYANIS:** Where'd it go?

**TORREN:** Under there.

**CYANIS:** I mean where was it going?

Torren looks across the modern terrain.

**TORREN:** Don't know.

Cyanis looks at the substantial road beneath their feet.

**CYANIS:** That's a lot of road to nowhere.

**TORREN:** It didn't go nowhere when they built it.

Ilyra is examining another stretch where a modern embankment covers most of the Ancient stone.

**ILYRA:** The ground changed this much?

**TORREN:** Ground. Rivers. Roads. People.

He looks back toward the Wayfinder.

**TORREN:** Long enough, everything moves.

Maevra:

**MAEVRA:** Except apparently that.

They return to the map.

## B003 — READING THE LAND

They clear only enough growth to expose useful sections.

No excavation montage.

A few simple before/after environment states.

The Wayfinder is not a modern overhead map.

Its representation is strange but deliberate:

ridge shapes,

watercourses,

route lines,

junction marks,

smaller service branches,

terrain notation,

and repeated familiar markings.

Torren studies it.

Long enough that nobody interrupts him.

Then:

**TORREN:** That's the river.

Cyanis leans over.

**CYANIS:** Which one?

Torren points toward the actual landscape.

**TORREN:** Ours.

Cyanis looks from the river in the distance back to the Wayfinder.

**CYANIS:** Oh.

Ilyra crouches on the opposite side.

**ILYRA:** Then this is the ridge.

Torren walks around.

Looks.

**TORREN:** Probably.

**ILYRA:** Probably?

**TORREN:** It's old.

Maevra:

**MAEVRA:** How old?

Torren gives her a look.

**TORREN:** Old enough that the river's in the wrong fucking place.

Maevra looks toward the real river.

Fair enough.

## B004 — HOLLOW WATCH

Maevra removes the rubbing recovered from Hollow Watch.

Places it beside one exposed section of the Wayfinder.

She shifts it once.

Twice.

Torren reaches over.

Turns it.

**MAEVRA:** I had it.

**TORREN:** You had it upside down.

**MAEVRA:** I did not.

Torren points.

**TORREN:** River.

Maevra looks.

Beat.

**MAEVRA:** Fine.

Cyanis wisely says nothing.

Part of the Hollow Watch route geometry now continues cleanly into the Wayfinder.

Torren traces it.

**TORREN:** That's Greenhollow.

He continues southeast.

Stops.

Looks toward the real landscape.

**TORREN:** Then this should be river country.

Ilyra:

**ILYRA:** Dunmere?

Torren takes his time.

**TORREN:** Could be.

Maevra follows Black Host charcoal laid over the same ancient route.

**MAEVRA:** They believed it was worth following.

**CYANIS:** Good enough for me.

Torren looks at him.

**CYANIS:** I said for me.

**TORREN:** Better.

## B005 — THE FACES

Ilyra notices a familiar mark near one of the route clusters.

**ILYRA:** Grace.

Nobody reacts to the name.

They all know it.

Cyanis finds another.

**CYANIS:** Might.

Torren circles around.

**TORREN:** Acuity here.

Maevra:

**MAEVRA:** Ruin.

The other familiar Face markings appear elsewhere across the surviving map:

Elements. Change.

Nothing about the six Faces themselves is a discovery.

What is strange is their placement.

Grace appears beside several entirely different geographical features.

Acuity appears around route branches that do not obviously have anything in common.

Ruin appears more than once.

Might is not simply “the Might road.”

Cyanis looks around the map.

**CYANIS:** Why are Faces on a route map?

Torren looks at the surrounding notation.

**TORREN:** Don't know.

Ilyra points between two Grace markings.

**ILYRA:** They're labeling something.

Maevra:

**MAEVRA:** Or instructions.

Torren studies one cluster.

**TORREN:** Could be either.

They leave it unresolved.

The new information is not what the Faces are.

It is that Ancient Diyseans apparently used the familiar six-Face system in a technical cartographic language the modern party does not understand.

## B006 — CYANIS'S CARD

Cyanis takes out the sealed Card.

Its Might association is already recognizable.

That is not news.

He compares its geometry with the Might notation on the Wayfinder.

**CYANIS:** Same Face.

Ilyra looks closer.

**ILYRA:** Not the same pattern.

Torren studies both.

**TORREN:** No.

Maevra:

**MAEVRA:** But related.

Cyanis looks at the Card.

**CYANIS:** Helpful.

**TORREN:** You wanted answers?

**CYANIS:** Would've been nice.

He puts it away.

No magical reaction.

No Prime identification.

No Last Sentinel.

No implication that they have just learned Cyanis belongs to Might.

They already understand what Might is.

They do not understand what this particular Ancient relationship means.

## B007 — WATER

Ilyra finds another repeated piece of notation near several waterways.

Unlike the Face marks, this one is unfamiliar.

**ILYRA:** This keeps showing up near water.

Torren comes over.

Looks.

Another occurrence.

Then another.

**TORREN:** Could be storage.

**ILYRA:** Or control.

Torren:

**TORREN:** Could be.

Maevra studies the Black Host marks on the southeast route.

**MAEVRA:** Their movement continues through the same country.

Ilyra looks at the water notation again.

**ILYRA:** Toward Dunmere.

**CYANIS:** That's enough coincidence for me.

**TORREN:** Coincidence doesn't have a quota.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** You know what I mean.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

## B008 — THE OLD CUT

Torren follows one smaller branch away from the primary southeast line.

Stops.

Backtracks.

Looks from the Wayfinder toward the modern landscape.

**TORREN:** Wait.

Maevra comes over.

**MAEVRA:** What?

Torren points to the old branch.

**TORREN:** This road isn't there anymore.

**CYANIS:** We established that happens.

**TORREN:** No. I know this ground.

He traces the branch.

**TORREN:** Modern road moved west.

Ilyra points at the water notation beside the old cut.

**ILYRA:** And that?

Torren looks.

**TORREN:** Cistern, maybe.

Maevra:

**MAEVRA:** Can you find the old entrance?

Torren looks toward the present-day road.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

## B009 — THE BROKEN EDGE

Torren continues around the Wayfinder.

One edge is badly damaged.

Several mapped features simply terminate at the break.

Not because the depicted routes ended there.

Because the physical map itself is incomplete.

Torren crouches.

Runs a finger along one broken route line.

Cyanis:

**CYANIS:** What?

**TORREN:** Nothing.

Cyanis waits.

Torren looks again.

**TORREN:** No. Something.

He points.

**TORREN:** Line keeps going.

**CYANIS:** Into what?

Torren taps the missing section.

**TORREN:** That'd be the problem.

Ilyra comes over.

**ILYRA:** Part of the Wayfinder is missing?

**TORREN:** Or this was only part of it.

Maevra looks at the broken edge.

**MAEVRA:** Can you tell which?

**TORREN:** No.

That's where the mystery stays.

Nobody suggests another map exists.

Nobody predicts Chapter 3.

Nobody says they need to find the other pieces.

**TORREN:** Solmar.

Do not engage.

## B012 — LEAVING

Late afternoon.

The party leaves Wayfinder Junction.

No final encounter.

No antagonist watching.

No collapse behind them.

No giant reveal.

Torren stops briefly and looks back at the crossroads.

Then at one of the copied map sections.

He adjusts its orientation.

Puts it away.

**CYANIS:** Harth.

**CYANIS:** Still heading east?

**TORREN:** Looks that way.

Cyanis nods.

They continue.
