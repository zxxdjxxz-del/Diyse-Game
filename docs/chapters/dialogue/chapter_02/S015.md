## B001 — THE BASTION WORKS

The view from S014 holds for a moment before control returns.

The Red Transfer Bastion is not a ruined fortress. It is operating.

Transfers move through separate controlled lanes. Black Host soldiers change posts. Wounded personnel are carried toward a marked medical route. Supply carts move behind reinforced barriers.

Nobody is standing around waiting for the protagonists.

Cheap 2.5D: one large Bastion exterior/interior kit, background soldier/porter loops, opening/closing gate states, transfer carts, lighting, banners and mechanical movement. Scale comes from the environment rather than expensive character animation.

Torren studies the routes below.

**TORREN:** Three entrances.

**MAEVRA:** Which one gets us to the gate control?

Torren points toward the center structure.

**TORREN:** That one.

**CYANIS:** Naturally.

**ILYRA:** What's wrong with the other two?

**TORREN:** One goes to barracks.

He points again.

**TORREN:** One goes somewhere with enough guards that I don't give a shit what's inside.

Cyanis looks.

**CYANIS:** Reasonable.

Maevra checks the transfer route.

**MAEVRA:** Center, then.

Control returns.

## EXPLORATION 1 — OUTER BASTION

A moderate Black Host combat area. Not huge. Expected first-pass pressure: roughly 2–3 random encounters before the next authored pocket.

The player moves through one exterior transfer platform, a reinforced connecting hall, a logistics junction, and a short upper walkway. Same Bastion kit reused throughout.

Enemy formations are disciplined rather than monstrous chaos. Soldiers use cover positions, support units and established Black Host combat roles. The place feels dangerous because people know what they are doing.

## OPTIONAL SIDE ROOM

A supply station can be looted after combat. Useful reward, no mandatory story information.

**CYANIS:** What?

**ILYRA:** Nothing.

She watches another wounded soldier being moved.

**ILYRA:** They're treating them.

Maevra looks.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**ILYRA:** Properly.

Torren watches the route rather than the patients.

**TORREN:** Still a prison.

Ilyra looks at him.

**ILYRA:** I know.

They move. No speech about moral complexity. The image is allowed to exist.

## EXPLORATION 2 — TRANSFER CENTER

The next moderate stretch moves closer to the actual transfer machinery. Expected first-pass pressure: roughly 2–3 random encounters.

The party sees evidence of a system designed for repeated use: numbered holding lanes, medical screening points, transfer manifests, restraint storage, guard rotation stations, maintained mechanical controls.

No torture-room spectacle. The horror is administrative.

A transfer lane crosses beneath the player on a lower level. Empty at the moment. Cyanis looks down.

**CYANIS:** How long has this been here?

**MAEVRA:** Don't know.

**TORREN:** Long enough to have procedures.

That is all.

## B003 — COMMAND TRAFFIC

The party reaches an upper corridor outside the Bastion command center. Voices carry through the open chamber before anyone sees the party.

A BLACK HOST OFFICER is giving a report. Commander Rhazek stands at a central operations table. Not on a throne. Not posing.

**OFFICER:** Western bridge collapsed.

**RHAZEK:** Personnel?

**OFFICER:** Seven confirmed dead. Eleven missing. Six wounded recovered.

**RHAZEK:** Destination for the wounded?

**OFFICER:** Second medical station.

**RHAZEK:** Good.

He points to the map.

**RHAZEK:** Move the reserve off the lower spine. Route Three.

The officer hesitates.

**OFFICER:** That yields the transfer center.

**RHAZEK:** Yes.

Beat.

**RHAZEK:** You have the order.

**OFFICER:** Yes, Commander.

Another soldier steps forward with a slate.

**SECOND OFFICER:** South line lost.

Rhazek does not look up.

**RHAZEK:** Define lost.

**SECOND OFFICER:** No signal. No response from the post.

Rhazek looks at him now.

**RHAZEK:** Then the line is abandoned. Nine are missing.

He hands the slate back.

**RHAZEK:** Do not promote them to dead because your report is shorter that way.

**SECOND OFFICER:** Understood.

The officer leaves. Only then does Rhazek look toward the entrance. At the party. He knew they were there. No dramatic turn.

**RHAZEK:** Commander Solmar.

His eyes move.

**RHAZEK:** Dovaren.

**RHAZEK:** Amarin.

Then Torren.

**RHAZEK:** Harth.

Maevra studies him.

**MAEVRA:** Commander Rhazek.

**RHAZEK:** You read the reports.

**MAEVRA:** I require them.

**RHAZEK:** So do I.

His attention shifts briefly to the tactical display. Then back.

**RHAZEK:** You destroyed three exterior positions.

**CYANIS:** You got one wrong.

Rhazek looks at him.

**RHAZEK:** Four.

Beat.

**RHAZEK:** Your western bypass is gone too.

Torren glances at Maevra. Maevra looks toward the map.

**MAEVRA:** Confirm.

Rhazek gestures to one of the remaining officers. The officer checks.

**OFFICER:** Confirmed.

Maevra looks back at Rhazek.

**MAEVRA:** Thank you.

**RHAZEK:** Both of you.

Cyanis looks at Maevra.

**CYANIS:** Did we just help each other?

**MAEVRA:** Don't get excited.

Rhazek waits. Not amused. Not offended. Simply finished with the interruption.

## B004 — THE GATE

Maevra steps toward the operations table.

**MAEVRA:** Open the prisoner extraction gate.

**RHAZEK:** No.

No speech. Maevra’s jaw tightens.

**MAEVRA:** There are injured civilians in those galleries.

**RHAZEK:** I know.

Ilyra looks at him.

**ILYRA:** Then you know they need to leave.

**RHAZEK:** They are not scheduled for release.

**ILYRA:** Scheduled.

Rhazek looks at her.

**ILYRA:** Do they get a say?

**RHAZEK:** They were given one.

**ILYRA:** And when they said no?

**RHAZEK:** The transfer proceeded.

Ilyra’s expression hardens.

**ILYRA:** Then they weren't given a choice.

**RHAZEK:** They were given information and an opportunity to comply.

**ILYRA:** That's not consent.

**RHAZEK:** No.

No excuse follows. That answer lands harder because he knows exactly what the word means.

Cyanis studies him.

**CYANIS:** And you're fine with that.

**RHAZEK:** I authorized it.

**CYANIS:** Wasn't what I asked.

**RHAZEK:** Yes.

Silence.

## B005 — WHAT HE OWNS

Ilyra looks toward the medical route visible through the command-room opening.

**ILYRA:** Your wounded are getting treatment.

**RHAZEK:** Yes.

**ILYRA:** What about the people you've detained?

**RHAZEK:** No detainee under my authority is denied necessary medical treatment.

**ILYRA:** Good.

Beat.

**ILYRA:** You should do that.

Another beat.

**ILYRA:** It doesn't buy the rest.

**RHAZEK:** I didn't offer it as payment.

Ilyra watches him. She expected resistance. Not agreement.

**ILYRA:** Then we're clear.

**RHAZEK:** We were already clear.

Maevra steps closer to the table.

**MAEVRA:** Open the gate.

**RHAZEK:** No.

**MAEVRA:** Then we're taking the control.

**RHAZEK:** Yes.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Yes?

**RHAZEK:** That is what you came here to do.

A Black Host officer shifts uneasily. Rhazek does not.

**RHAZEK:** You did not enter my Bastion to persuade me.

Maevra answers immediately.

**MAEVRA:** No.

**RHAZEK:** Good.

He removes his command cloak and hands it to the nearest officer. Simple pose swap. No transformation movie.

**RHAZEK:** Then we have finished talking.

## BOSS — COMMANDER RHAZEK: BASTION MASTER

## Presentation

The command chamber reconfigures into a readable battle space using the existing room. No teleport to an anonymous arena. Officers withdraw behind defensive barriers. Rhazek uses Bastion Master, his Chapter 2 combat state. No Reforged Commander. No Bastion Devourer. No later catastrophic/unarmored form.

## ONE HP BAR

One continuous health bar. The encounter has two authored behavior states on that same bar.

## STATE A — BASTION MASTER

Rhazek fights as a commander using the functioning Bastion around him. Finite support only. Support does not respawn indefinitely.

One shield/support detachment.

One ranged support position.

Limited command-linked environmental pressure.

The battle should make the player feel that Rhazek is using prepared infrastructure, not magically summoning endless soldiers. When support is exhausted, it stays exhausted. Rhazek remains personally dangerous. No constant battle quips. Functional barks only.

**RHAZEK:** Left line.

or:

**RHAZEK:** Hold.

or:

**RHAZEK:** Move.

Short. Command-shaped.

## SAME-BAR THRESHOLD — RUIN / ARMOR ESCALATION

At the authored threshold, Rhazek takes a hard hit. He remains standing. Brief battle pause. The Bastion’s Ruin channels respond through his armor. Dark-purple energy threads more visibly through the black/crimson biomechanical plates. Armor closes/reinforces around vulnerable sections.

Cheap 2.5D: illustrated cut-in + armor-state swap + chamber lighting response. No long transformation. No new health bar. No free attack during the threshold.

Torren sees the change.

**TORREN:** That's new.

Maevra does not take her eyes off Rhazek.

**MAEVRA:** No.

Beat.

**MAEVRA:** That's deliberate.

Rhazek looks at her.

**RHAZEK:** Yes.

Battle resumes.

## STATE B — BASTION MASTER: RUIN ESCALATION

More direct personal pressure. Less reliance on remaining support. Armor becomes harder to break through. Ruin-enhanced attacks become more prominent. Still recognizably the same combat state, not a new form. When the HP bar reaches zero, Rhazek is defeated.

## B006 — AFTER RHAZEK

Rhazek drops to one knee. Not unconscious. His armor is damaged but nowhere near the later catastrophic state. Cyanis starts toward him. Maevra raises one hand. Not now. Torren is already looking at the command controls.

**TORREN:** Found it.

Ilyra remains watching Rhazek.

**ILYRA:** Can you stand?

Rhazek looks at her.

**RHAZEK:** Yes.

He tries. Gets up. Barely. Ilyra does not offer a hand.

**ILYRA:** Fine.

Rhazek looks toward the operations table.

**RHAZEK:** The extraction control is there.

Cyanis looks at him.

**CYANIS:** You're helping now?

**RHAZEK:** No.

Rhazek straightens as much as the damaged armor allows.

**RHAZEK:** You won access to it.

Cyanis gives him a long look.

**CYANIS:** You're a strange fucking man.

**RHAZEK:** I've been called worse.

**CYANIS:** I'm sure.

Torren reaches the controls.

**TORREN:** Solmar.

Maevra joins him.

**TORREN:** Gate control. Transfer spine. Causeway.

Maevra checks the indicators.

**MAEVRA:** Can we open all of it?

Torren studies the system.

**TORREN:** Not all at once.

Rhazek answers from behind them.

**RHAZEK:** You don't want to.

Torren turns.

**TORREN:** Why?

**RHAZEK:** You'll expose the gallery route before you have a column moving.

Maevra studies the display. Then realizes he is right.

**MAEVRA:** We open the return route first.

Torren nods.

**TORREN:** Then the Causeway when they're moving.

Cyanis looks back at Rhazek.

**CYANIS:** Still not helping?

**RHAZEK:** No.

**CYANIS:** Right.

## B007 — WITHDRAWAL

A distant alarm changes tone. Rhazek recognizes it. So does Maevra.

**MAEVRA:** Reserve movement.

**RHAZEK:** Yes.

Cyanis shifts toward him again. Rhazek’s remaining officers appear at the far exit. Not charging. Covering a withdrawal.

Maevra looks at the controls. Then at Rhazek. She has a choice between chasing him and opening the route. It is not treated as a player dialogue choice. She turns to Torren.

**MAEVRA:** Open it.

Rhazek watches her make the decision. Then withdraws with his remaining personnel.

No final villain threat. No “next time.” No mysterious promise. He leaves because remaining here no longer serves his objective.

The Bastion remains active around them, but its local command structure is broken. Torren works the control. A heavy gate mechanism engages somewhere far below.

**TORREN:** Gallery route opening.

Maevra looks toward the return corridor.

**MAEVRA:** Move.

## SHORT RETURN STRETCH — BACK TO THE PRISONERS

Control returns. This is not another full dungeon. The player moves back through the now-disrupted Bastion/transfer route. The return is shorter because portions of the path are already cleared.

At most 1–2 likely random encounters from remaining Black Host forces before the party crosses back into the cleared Prisoner Galleries. No new boss. No fake Rhazek rematch.

The party reaches the safe pocket. Cyanis knocks. Three times. The bar lifts. The Prisoner Woman opens the door. Looks at their condition.

Torren looks down at himself.

**TORREN:** I've had better.

She looks past them.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Gate?

**MAEVRA:** We have it.

The woman does not cheer. She turns back inside.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** All right.

Louder:

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Get your shit.

People begin moving. Ilyra immediately steps toward those who cannot. Torren checks the route. Cyanis looks back toward the transfer spine. Maevra watches everyone preparing to leave. No victory pose.

## S015 — LOCKED CONTINUITY NOTES

The Bastion is frightening because it is functional, not because it is a torture theme park.

Rhazek is introduced commanding casualties, missing personnel, medical routing and reserves before he deals with the protagonists.

Rhazek is competent, accountable, non-possessed, ideologically intact, and owns the transfer operation and his decisions.

Proper medical treatment is real but does not morally purchase coercive custody.

Combat uses Bastion Master only, with finite support and exactly one HP bar.

The Ruin/armor change is a deliberate same-bar escalation, not another form.

No Reforged Commander, Bastion Devourer, or later catastrophically damaged/unarmored Rhazek is used in Chapter 2.

Rhazek survives and withdraws. The party prioritizes opening the evacuation route over chasing him.

Exploration remains moderate: roughly 2–3 encounters + 2–3 encounters + boss + short return.

Cheap 2.5D throughout: existing Bastion space, background loops, control/gate states, portrait acting, armor-state swap, lighting and an illustrated threshold cut-in.
