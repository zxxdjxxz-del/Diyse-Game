## B001 — TORREN

Late night. The room is dark except for low coals near the doorway. Cyanis and Ilyra are asleep.

Torren wakes. Nothing dramatic. Eyes open. He lies there for a while. Eventually:

**TORREN:** Fuck.

Quietly. He sits up. Checks the others. Still asleep.

Torren gets up, moves to the doorway and sits against the wall. He takes out a blunt and uses one of the remaining coals to light it. Small ember. A little smoke disappears through the open doorway.

He takes a slow pull. Sits there. The tension gradually leaves his shoulders. No speech. A minute passes.

## B002 — ILYRA

From behind him:

**ILYRA:** You’re going to burn your fingers doing that.

Torren doesn’t turn around.

**TORREN:** Haven’t yet.

Ilyra sits up.

**ILYRA:** That’s an incredibly stupid defense.

**TORREN:** Still a defense.

She looks toward Cyanis. Still asleep. Then back at Torren.

**ILYRA:** How long have you been up?

**TORREN:** Don’t know.

**ILYRA:** Useful.

**TORREN:** Wasn’t keeping records.

Ilyra gets up and sits near the doorway. Not right beside him. Close enough. Torren takes another pull. Ilyra looks at the blunt.

**ILYRA:** That the same shit you had three nights ago?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**ILYRA:** Smells different.

Torren looks at it.

**TORREN:** Got wet.

Ilyra stares at him.

**ILYRA:** Of course it did.

**TORREN:** Everything got fucking wet.

**ILYRA:** You had pockets.

**TORREN:** Wet pockets.

Fair. Ilyra reaches for the water. Drinks. Torren looks outside. Neither says anything for a while.

## B003 — NOT TALKING ABOUT IT

Ilyra glances sideways. Torren looks calmer now. Focused. Normal.

**ILYRA:** You going back to sleep?

**TORREN:** Eventually.

**ILYRA:** Good plan.

**TORREN:** You?

Ilyra looks back toward her bedroll.

**ILYRA:** Apparently not.

Torren nods. That’s the entire discussion. He doesn’t ask why. She doesn’t ask why. A few seconds.

**ILYRA:** You know what I hate?

**TORREN:** That’s a dangerous opening.

**ILYRA:** Wet sleeves.

Torren looks at her.

**TORREN:** That’s it?

**ILYRA:** Right now.

**TORREN:** Fair.

**ILYRA:** You?

Torren thinks. Takes another pull.

**TORREN:** People who say “could be worse.”

**ILYRA:** That’s a good one.

**TORREN:** Every time somebody says it, the world hears them.

**ILYRA:** That’s not how anything works.

**TORREN:** Prove it.

Ilyra thinks about answering. Doesn’t.

**ILYRA:** Fuck you.

Torren smiles. Barely.

## B004 — CYANIS

From the bedroll:

**CYANIS:** If you two are going to whisper, whisper louder.

Neither turns around.

**ILYRA:** Go back to sleep.

**CYANIS:** Can’t.

**TORREN:** Tragic.

Cyanis sits up. Looks at both of them by the doorway.

**CYANIS:** How long has this been happening?

**TORREN:** What?

**CYANIS:** Whatever this is.

**ILYRA:** We’re discussing sleeves.

Cyanis stares at her.

**CYANIS:** Of course you are.

He gets up and joins them. There are only two convenient places to sit. Cyanis looks around. Torren shifts his boot a few inches. That’s apparently enough. Cyanis sits on the floor.

**CYANIS:** Comfortable.

**TORREN:** Didn’t say it was.

## B005 — THREE AWAKE PEOPLE

The blunt remains in Torren’s hand. Nobody treats it as an event. Cyanis smells the smoke.

**CYANIS:** That’s what woke me up.

**ILYRA:** No, it isn’t.

**CYANIS:** You don’t know that.

**ILYRA:** You were awake before he lit it.

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** How do you know?

**ILYRA:** You sigh when you’re pretending to sleep.

Beat. Cyanis turns to Torren.

**CYANIS:** Do I?

**TORREN:** Constantly.

**CYANIS:** Both of you can go fuck yourselves.

Torren takes another pull.

**TORREN:** Good. You’re awake.

**CYANIS:** I was awake.

**TORREN:** Now you’re awake and angry.

**CYANIS:** Improvement?

**TORREN:** For you.

Ilyra drinks more water.

## B006 — STUPID SHIT

Nobody tries to sleep. Not yet. Cyanis looks outside.

**CYANIS:** Think Marr’s actually getting that timber tonight?

**TORREN:** Probably.

**ILYRA:** You told Solmar he needed all of it.

**TORREN:** He asked how much.

**CYANIS:** You said “yes.”

**TORREN:** Accurate answer.

**ILYRA:** Not remotely.

**TORREN:** They’ll bring too much.

**CYANIS:** And?

**TORREN:** Then we’ll have enough.

Cyanis considers this.

**CYANIS:** I hate that that’s defensible.

**ILYRA:** Don’t encourage him.

**TORREN:** Too late.

A pause. Cyanis notices Torren’s boots.

**CYANIS:** Those still wet?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** Mine too.

Ilyra looks down at hers.

**ILYRA:** Mine aren’t.

Both men look at her.

**ILYRA:** What?

**CYANIS:** Nothing.

**TORREN:** Nothing.

**ILYRA:** You’re both idiots.

**TORREN:** Dry-footed privilege.

Ilyra laughs before she can stop herself. Immediately covers her mouth so she doesn’t wake anyone else nearby. Cyanis grins.

**ILYRA:** Shut up.

**CYANIS:** Didn’t say anything.

**ILYRA:** Your face did.

Beat. Torren looks at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** It does that.

## B007 — QUIETER

The conversation dies naturally. Not sadly. They just run out of things worth saying. Torren rests his forearms on his knees. The blunt burns lower. Cyanis leans back against the wall. Ilyra pulls part of the blanket from her bedroll around her shoulders. Nobody goes back to bed.

After a while:

**CYANIS:** What time is it?

Torren looks outside.

**TORREN:** Late.

**CYANIS:** Helpful.

**TORREN:** Getting lighter.

Ilyra looks toward the doorway. He’s right. Very faint dawn.

**ILYRA:** We should sleep.

Nobody moves. Beat.

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

Nobody moves. Torren takes one final pull. Puts the end out against the ash beside the coals and saves what remains rather than throwing it away. Cheap prop-state change. Silence again.

## B008 — MORNING

Gradual light change. No montage. No one discusses the dreams. No one asks if the others are okay.

Cyanis eventually lets his head rest back against the wall. Ilyra’s eyes close while she’s still sitting up. Torren looks out through the doorway. Calm. Focused. A little tired. But fine.

He glances at the other two. Then back outside. Hold. Fade.

> **END C07.**
