## B001 — THE GLOVE

Later. Same Dunmere staging area from S016. Most activity has moved offscreen. A lantern sits on a rough table.

Torren is seated, sorting a short length of cord and a few pieces of damaged gear. One chair beside him is empty. Cyanis stands nearby looking through his things.

**CYANIS:** You seen my glove?

**TORREN:** Which one?

**CYANIS:** My glove.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** Left.

Torren looks at him.

**TORREN:** Your other left.

Cyanis looks down. The glove is tucked under his opposite arm. Beat.

**CYANIS:** Fuck you.

Torren goes back to what he was doing. Ilyra arrives carrying a cup. She looks at the empty chair. Then at the other two. Nobody says anything about it. She sits. That was her seat.

## B002 — STRING WITH CONFIDENCE

Ilyra looks at what Torren is working on.

**ILYRA:** What happened to that?

**TORREN:** Nothing.

The cord is visibly frayed. Ilyra looks at him.

**TORREN:** Nothing important.

Cyanis picks up the loose end.

**CYANIS:** This is rope?

**TORREN:** Cord.

Cyanis turns it over.

**CYANIS:** It’s string with confidence.

Torren looks up.

**TORREN:** Fuck you.

Ilyra takes a drink.

**ILYRA:** He has a point.

**TORREN:** I liked you better an hour ago.

**ILYRA:** No, you didn’t.

Torren considers that.

**TORREN:** No.

## B003 — ORGANIZED

The table is covered with gear that has somehow divided itself into three small piles. Ilyra looks over it.

**ILYRA:** This feels organized.

Torren does not look up.

**TORREN:** It isn’t.

Cyanis pushes one item from his pile into Torren’s. Torren pushes it back.

**CYANIS:** Manageable.

Ilyra looks at Cyanis. Then Torren.

**ILYRA:** Your faces did.

Cyanis looks at Torren.

**CYANIS:** What did my face do?

**TORREN:** Complained.

**CYANIS:** I didn’t say anything.

**TORREN:** Didn’t need to.

Ilyra drinks again.

## B004 — WEST PERIMETER

Torren reaches for another piece of cord.

**TORREN:** West perimeter needs moving before dark.

**CYANIS:** Already did.

Torren stops. Looks at him.

**TORREN:** You finally learned something.

**CYANIS:** You showed me once.

**TORREN:** Twice.

**CYANIS:** Once sober.

Ilyra looks at Torren. Torren points at Cyanis.

**TORREN:** That is not how that happened.

**CYANIS:** That’s how I remember it.

**TORREN:** Your memory’s shit.

**CYANIS:** Convenient, though.

Ilyra looks between them.

**ILYRA:** Do I want the other version?

**CYANIS:** No.

**TORREN:** No.

Ilyra nods.

**ILYRA:** Good.

## B005 — KNIFE

Torren reaches across the table without looking.

**TORREN:** Knife.

Cyanis looks over the scattered gear.

**CYANIS:** Which knife?

Torren finally looks at him.

**TORREN:** My knife.

Ilyra points beside Torren’s elbow.

**ILYRA:** There.

Torren looks down. His knife is directly beside his hand. Cyanis smiles. Torren picks it up.

**TORREN:** Don’t.

**CYANIS:** I haven’t said anything.

**TORREN:** I know.

Cyanis watches him trim the frayed cord. Then:

**CYANIS:** We could probably—

**ILYRA / TORREN:** No.

Cyanis looks between them.

**CYANIS:** You don’t even know what I was going to say.

Torren keeps cutting.

**TORREN:** Yes, I do.

Ilyra nods.

**ILYRA:** So do I.

**CYANIS:** That’s fucking insulting.

Neither answers.

## B006 — STOP

Torren ties off the repair. Then immediately reaches for the next damaged piece. Ilyra puts her hand on it first. Torren looks at her. She moves it to the far side of the table. No speech.

Torren reaches for another. Cyanis takes that one and puts it beside the first. Torren looks at him.

**TORREN:** Seriously?

Cyanis leans back.

**CYANIS:** Apparently.

Torren looks at Ilyra. She takes another drink.

**ILYRA:** You’re done.

**TORREN:** I’m sitting down.

**ILYRA:** You can work sitting down.

**TORREN:** Exactly.

Ilyra takes the knife from him. Sets it down out of reach. Torren stares at her. She stares back. Beat. Torren gives up. Not dramatically. He just leans back. Cyanis does the same.

## B007 — STILL ROPE

A few seconds pass. Torren looks at the repaired cord on the table.

**TORREN:** Still rope.

Cyanis turns toward him.

**CYANIS:** Get out.

Ilyra looks at Cyanis.

**ILYRA:** He’s sitting down.

**CYANIS:** Then he can get out sitting down.

Torren settles farther into the chair.

**TORREN:** Finally, a workable plan.

Silence. Nobody leaves. The lantern burns. Offscreen, somebody drops something and swears. Cyanis glances toward the sound. Doesn’t get up. Ilyra takes another drink. Torren closes his eyes for a moment.

Nobody asks if anyone is all right. Nobody needs to fill the silence. Hold several seconds. Fade.
