## B001 — LOWERED STONE THROAT

The party descends from Dunmere into the Archive. Water drips through seams. Pale preservation channels still function in sections of wall. The ramp ahead disappears into black water.

**CYANIS:** It kept the water out.

**ILYRA:** Not all of it.

**MAEVRA:** Then stay where the stone is still doing its job.

Torren checks the submerged floor.

**TORREN:** The floor drops ahead.

**CYANIS:** How far?

**TORREN:** Far enough.

Maevra takes out the rope.

**MAEVRA:** Rope.

Control returns.

## EXPLORATION 1 — FLOODED STACKS

A moderate first dungeon stretch. The player moves through partially submerged archive stacks, narrow raised walkways, and two connected preservation rooms. The same core environment kit is reused with different shelving arrangements and water levels.

Expected first-pass pressure: roughly 2–3 random encounters, depending on movement and encounter rolls.

Enemies begin simple enough for the player to learn the Archive ecology before combinations get harder.

One short optional side route contains a chest and a collapsed records alcove. No mandatory lore.

Midway through the area, Torren secures the rope over a broken section.

**TORREN:** Gets deeper.

**CYANIS:** Of course it does.

Torren tests the next slab. A crack. Everyone stops.

**TORREN:** Mostly solid.

**CYANIS:** Mostly?

**TORREN:** You want certainty, go back upstairs.

**CYANIS:** Fair assumption.

They cross. A little later, the route forks.

**TORREN:** Two ways.

**MAEVRA:** Which one's dry?

**TORREN:** Neither.

**MAEVRA:** Which one's less wet?

Torren points.

**TORREN:** Left.

**CYANIS:** Excellent system.

**TORREN:** You're welcome.

Control remains with the player. The left route leads toward the powered preservation wing.

## B002 — MEMORY SCRIBE CHAMBER

A small powered records room interrupts the traversal. Not a long cutscene. A Memory Scribe activates as the party crosses the central floor.

## AUTHORED MEMORY SCRIBE ENCOUNTER

The Scribe reacts only after an eligible action actually completes.

It does not read menu selections or predicted commands.

Once an action is recorded, it reproduces a weaker functional version.

If Ilyra was actually copied:

**ILYRA:** It copied me.

If another actor was recorded, use that actor-specific equivalent or:

**PARTY ACTOR:** It copied that.

The fight ends. Maevra looks at the inactive machine.

**MAEVRA:** When?

Torren wipes water from an arrow.

**TORREN:** After you moved.

**MAEVRA:** So it sees the action first.

**CYANIS:** Fair assumption.

Maevra looks toward the next corridor.

**MAEVRA:** Then change what you give it.

Torren retrieves the rest of his arrows.

**TORREN:** I was planning to.

**CYANIS:** Sure.

**TORREN:** Fuck off.

They move.

## EXPLORATION 2 — PRESERVATION / TRANSFER WING

Another moderate stretch rather than an immediate story room.

Expected first-pass pressure: roughly 2–3 random encounters.

Memory Scribes may now appear in normal formations, paired with simpler Archive enemies so the player gets to apply what the authored encounter just taught them.

The environment slowly changes. Ancient shelving remains, but newer repairs begin appearing: metal brackets, replaced hinges, fresh scrape marks, and sections where silt has recently been disturbed. Nobody stops to explain every clue. The player sees the pattern forming.

One optional side room has supplies or a chest.

Another contains damaged archive shelving and nothing important.

Toward the end of the stretch, the party reaches an old water-control room.

## B003 — WHAT CAME THROUGH HERE

Torren stops.

**TORREN:** Hold up.

Everyone does. Tracks cross the wet floor. Some are normal footsteps. Others are uneven. One set drags on one side. Another becomes two parallel scrape marks.

Ilyra crouches.

**ILYRA:** These aren't patrol tracks.

**MAEVRA:** No.

Torren follows the marks toward a sealed service gate.

**TORREN:** They came through there.

Cyanis looks at the floor.

**CYANIS:** Walking?

Ilyra studies it.

**ILYRA:** Some of them.

She points to the deeper drag marks.

**ILYRA:** Not all.

Silence. Maevra tries the old release. Nothing.

**MAEVRA:** Can we open it?

Torren checks the mechanism.

**TORREN:** Not from here.

**CYANIS:** Other way?

Torren looks toward the next flooded passage.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

Cyanis follows his gaze.

**CYANIS:** Of course it's underwater.

**TORREN:** Not all of it.

Ilyra looks at him. Torren realizes what he just said.

**TORREN:** Don't.

She almost smiles. They move.

## EXPLORATION 3 — CURRENT GALLERIES

This is the longest stretch in S013, but still moderate, not oversized.

Expected first-pass pressure: roughly 3–4 random encounters before the recovery pocket.

The water is deeper in places, alternating between raised archive paths, knee-deep lanes, one section with a stronger lateral current, and a dry upper shelf that provides an optional treasure route.

No precision platforming. Traversal is simple positioning, ramps, stairs, and route choice.

Enemy formations now mix previously introduced Archive enemies more aggressively.

A Drowned Archive Maw can appear as a tougher regular/elite encounter here rather than requiring a separate story scene.

After one particularly rough encounter, Cyanis braces against the wall. Ilyra sees it.

**ILYRA:** How bad?

**CYANIS:** I'm functional.

**ILYRA:** That wasn't the question.

**CYANIS:** It was the answer I had.

She steps closer.

**ILYRA:** Anything broken?

**CYANIS:** No.

**ILYRA:** Dizzy?

**CYANIS:** No.

**ILYRA:** Sick?

**CYANIS:** No.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** Wet.

**ILYRA:** Tragic.

**CYANIS:** Thank you.

They keep moving. No bespoke medical animation.

## OPTIONAL ELITE ROOM

A side branch contains the Archive Duplicant. It is optional. It may reward equipment/materials, but no mandatory story information is hidden behind it. The main route bypasses the room entirely.

## RECOVERY POCKET

Before the Leviathan basin, the party reaches a dry preservation alcove. This is a clear safe zone. Save/recovery/menu access as appropriate to the game’s established systems. No full camp scene.

Short ambient exchange only if the player remains in the area. Cyanis wrings water from one glove.

**CYANIS:** I'm throwing these away.

**TORREN:** No, you're not.

**CYANIS:** They're soaked.

**TORREN:** They're gloves.

**CYANIS:** Wet gloves.

**TORREN:** Still gloves.

**MAEVRA:** I'm begging both of you to shut up.

Ilyra sits for a moment.

**ILYRA:** Seconded.

Silence. Then control returns fully.

## B004 — LEVIATHAN BASIN

The Archive opens into a large preservation basin. A records platform stands beyond the water. No portrait dialogue at first.

Something moves beneath the surface. Torren stops.

**CYANIS:** That's large.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**MAEVRA:** Can we avoid it?

**TORREN:** Not if we're going there.

**ILYRA:** We're going there.

## BOSS — ARCHIVE LEVIATHAN

One HP bar. Two authored behavior states. No refill.

Cheap 2.5D presentation: the battle arena uses the same basin environment.

Leviathan body remains primarily a large readable 3D/battle-space threat.

Short illustrated cut-in for the threshold.

Water level/current and lighting shift, then battle resumes immediately.

## STATE A

The Leviathan uses the Archive's recorded-pattern behavior. Completed eligible actions may be recorded and answered by weaker functional equivalents. The state is visible to the player.

## SAME-BAR THRESHOLD

The Leviathan dives. Water pulls toward the basin center. Short visual beat. It resurfaces in the altered arena state. No damage from the transition itself. No second HP bar.

If a Recorded Pattern actually persists through the threshold:

**CYANIS:** Same one?

**TORREN:** Yes.

If none persists, the exchange does not happen.

## STATE B

The Leviathan becomes more physically aggressive. Higher direct pressure. Broader undertow/water control. Recorded patterns decay rather than building forever. The boss falls on the same HP bar.

## B005 — THIRTY-ONE

The water settles. No victory pose. The records platform unlocks. The party crosses. Maevra checks the available exits while Ilyra examines records that are visibly newer than the surrounding Archive material.

**ILYRA:** These aren't ancient.

Maevra comes over. She reads.

**MAEVRA:** These are transfers.

She scans the entries.

**MAEVRA:** Thirty-one.

**CYANIS:** Thirty-one what?

**MAEVRA:** People.

Silence. Ilyra takes another sheet. Dates. Condition marks. Routing codes.

**ILYRA:** Where were they sent?

Maevra traces the route.

**MAEVRA:** Deeper.

Torren looks toward the service gate.

**TORREN:** Same direction as the tracks.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

Cyanis looks back at the page.

**CYANIS:** Thirty-one.

**MAEVRA:** Recorded.

**ILYRA:** Meaning that's what this record accounts for.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

**TORREN:** Not everyone who came through.

**MAEVRA:** No.

Nobody tries to make the number more certain than it is.

Cyanis looks at the gate.

**CYANIS:** Open it.

## SHORT POST-BOSS TRAVERSAL — SERVICE ROUTE

The gate opens from this side. The next stretch is deliberately short. No giant fourth dungeon section.

The player gets a few minutes of movement through a drier service corridor.

The architecture begins looking increasingly used: newer braces, recent footprints, replaced door hardware, scrapes on the walls.

A small random encounter window can remain active at the start of this route—likely no more than one encounter on a normal first pass.

Then the player crosses an authored threshold. Random encounters shut off.

A barred structure becomes visible ahead. Movement behind it. A faint sound. Everyone stops.

A cough. Human. Cyanis looks at Torren.

**CYANIS:** Harth.

**TORREN:** I know.

No joke. They move forward. Another cough.
