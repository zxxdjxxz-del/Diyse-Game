## B001 — BEYOND THE BARS

The service corridor from S013 reaches a barred gallery. The cough comes again.

**TORREN:** Someone's in there.

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

Maevra checks the corridor behind them.

**MAEVRA:** Open it.

The release on this side still works. Simple gate-state change. No cinematic. The bars retract far enough to pass. They enter.

The gallery is not one giant prison room. It is a series of small holding bays connected by a central corridor. Some doors stand open. Some do not. A woman sits against one wall with a bloodied sleeve wrapped around her forearm. A man farther back is trying to help another prisoner stand. Someone else watches the party without moving.

The woman sees Maevra's armor.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** No.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Stay there.

She looks at Cyanis. Then Torren. Then Ilyra.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Who the fuck are you?

**MAEVRA:** Commander Maevra Solmar. Yahtrean Army.

The woman's expression does not improve.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** That's still a commander.

Beat. Maevra nods.

**MAEVRA:** It is.

Ilyra steps forward only far enough to be seen clearly.

**ILYRA:** I'm a healer.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** You're armed.

**ILYRA:** Also true.

A few prisoners look at one another.

**ILYRA:** I'm not touching anyone who doesn't want me to.

The woman studies her. Then points toward the man struggling to stand.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** His knee.

Ilyra looks at him.

**ILYRA:** Can I look?

**INJURED MAN:** Look.

He points at her.

**INJURED MAN:** Don't fucking twist it.

**ILYRA:** Wasn't planning to.

She kneels.

## B002 — NOT A HEADCOUNT

Cheap 2.5D staging: one gallery room, several foreground prisoners, a few reusable seated/standing NPC poses, additional silhouettes through open bays. No crowd animation.

While Ilyra works, Cyanis checks the nearest holding doors. Torren looks farther down the corridor.

**CYANIS:** These locks are newer than the walls.

**TORREN:** Same as upstairs.

Maevra looks around.

**MAEVRA:** How long have you been here?

The Prisoner Woman shrugs.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Which time?

Maevra looks at her.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** They move us.

She points down the gallery.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Room to room. Sometimes farther.

**MAEVRA:** Where?

Fair. Maevra lets it go.

Cyanis returns.

Torren gives him a look.

**CYANIS:** Doors, Harth.

**TORREN:** I know.

Torren points.

**TORREN:** Six on this section. More around the turn.

Maevra looks toward the prisoners.

**MAEVRA:** We're opening them.

The Prisoner Woman stands.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Then open them.

## EXPLORATION 1 — GALLERY CLEARANCE

The party moves through a moderate section of the Prisoner Galleries. Not huge. Expected first-pass pressure: roughly 2–3 random encounters.

The player clears connected gallery corridors while opening accessible holding areas. This is not “collect all prisoners” gameplay. Individual people become visible as sections are made safe.

Environmental variation stays cheap:

same gallery kit,

different door states,

broken lamps,

newer Black Host braces,

abandoned carts,

benches and restraint fixtures,

one flooded lower passage.

A few freed prisoners remain where they are rather than automatically following the party. One refuses to move at all.

## B003 — WHERE DO THEY GO?

The party reaches a wider junction. A service room sits off the main gallery. Stone walls. One heavy door. A manual bar on the inside.

Torren checks it.

**TORREN:** This could hold.

The Prisoner Woman arrives behind them with two others. She looks inside.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** One door.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Vent?

Torren points high on the wall.

**TORREN:** There.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Other entrance?

**TORREN:** Not unless somebody starts digging.

She nods.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Better than the cells.

Maevra looks at her.

**MAEVRA:** We can put everyone who wants to wait here.

The woman catches the wording.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Who wants to.

**MAEVRA:** Yes.

A beat. The woman looks at the interior bar.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** We keep the door.

**MAEVRA:** You keep the door.

Torren checks the locking bar.

**TORREN:** Once it's down, nobody gets in without you lifting it.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Good.

Cyanis looks down the corridor they came from.

**CYANIS:** And if we come back running?

She looks at him.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Knock.

**CYANIS:** Fair.

## B004 — WHO CAN MOVE

The service room becomes the defensible safe pocket. No random encounters can trigger inside it or immediately outside once secured. Ilyra triages people who choose to enter. No montage. A few short exchanges carry it.

She addresses an older man.

**ILYRA:** Can you walk?

**OLDER PRISONER:** Yes.

He tries to rise. Nearly falls. Ilyra catches his elbow only after he reaches for her.

**ILYRA:** Still yes?

**OLDER PRISONER:** Fuck you.

**ILYRA:** I'll put that down as maybe.

He laughs despite himself.

Nearby, the injured man from earlier looks at his braced knee.

**INJURED MAN:** I can walk.

**ILYRA:** Not on that.

**INJURED MAN:** I didn't ask.

Ilyra looks at him.

**ILYRA:** No.

He looks back. A beat.

**INJURED MAN:** Fine.

No lecture. Torren brings over an old maintenance stool.

**INJURED MAN:** What the hell am I supposed to do with that?

**TORREN:** Sit on it.

**INJURED MAN:** I gathered that.

**TORREN:** Then we're making excellent progress.

The man sits.

## B005 — THE ROUTE OUT

Maevra examines the old service map beside the door. Torren joins her.

**MAEVRA:** This returns to the Archive.

**TORREN:** Not anymore.

He traces a damaged section.

**TORREN:** Collapsed here.

Another route.

**TORREN:** This one goes up.

The Prisoner Woman looks over.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Locked.

Torren turns.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Tried it.

**CYANIS:** What's on the other side?

**PRISONER WOMAN:** More guards.

Helpful.

**MAEVRA:** Anything else?

The woman thinks.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** They call it the transfer line.

Maevra looks at Torren. He is already following the map.

**TORREN:** Runs west.

Cyanis looks over his shoulder.

**CYANIS:** Toward what?

Torren follows the markings. Stops.

**TORREN:** Something bigger.

The Prisoner Woman answers from behind him.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Bastion.

Maevra turns.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** That's what they call it.

Silence.

**CYANIS:** Can we get these people out without going through it?

Torren studies the damaged map longer. Then:

**TORREN:** Not from here.

Maevra looks at him.

**MAEVRA:** Certain?

**TORREN:** Unless you want to take injured people back through the flooded Archive, across a broken floor, and up Dunmere's waterworks.

The injured man raises a hand from across the room.

**INJURED MAN:** I'd rather not.

Torren looks over.

**TORREN:** There you go.

The transfer spine is another moderate random-battle area, around 2–3 likely encounters.

This section is more actively used than the Archive:

Same basic gallery/service assets, changed dressing and lighting. No expensive new dungeon kit. Black Host formations become more common.

## B006 — THE TRANSFER GATE

The party reaches a broad mechanical gate. Torren checks the controls. Cyanis examines the newer reinforcement around the frame.

**CYANIS:** This isn't opening from here.

**TORREN:** No.

**MAEVRA:** Can we break it?

Cyanis looks at the size of the gate. Then at her.

**CYANIS:** Eventually.

**TORREN:** And bring half the fucking ceiling down.

**CYANIS:** I said eventually.

Ilyra looks through a narrow opening in the gate. Beyond it: a maintained route, more recent structures, movement in the distance.

**ILYRA:** They're using the other side.

Maevra looks at the control housing.

**MAEVRA:** Where is this controlled?

Torren follows a bundle of mechanical linkages through the wall.

**TORREN:** West.

Maevra already knows.

**MAEVRA:** The Bastion.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

A sound from behind. Metal. Fast. Torren turns. A Black Host patrol enters the junction from the side passage.

**MAEVRA:** Torren!

Immediate beat.

**MAEVRA:** Harth.

Torren ducks the strike. No one reacts to the name. No portrait hold. No music sting. The fight continues.

**MAEVRA:** We come back first.

**PRISONER WOMAN:** Good.
