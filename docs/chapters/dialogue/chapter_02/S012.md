Playable cast: Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren • Temporary allied presence: Commander Maevra Solmar

## B001 — Arrival

2.5D staging: One reusable Dunmere street/water-channel space. Red cloth tied around several pump handles. Workers carry sealed barrels. A healer works from a folding table. Background NPC loops continue while dialogue plays.

The party enters with Maevra. Watermaster Marr approaches from the channel.

**WATERMASTER:** Commander Solmar.

**MAEVRA:** What happened?

**WATERMASTER:** First sickness yesterday afternoon. Three districts by morning.

**ILYRA:** Symptoms?

**WATERMASTER:** Vomiting. Fever. Tremors. A few couldn’t stand.

**ILYRA:** Deaths?

**WATERMASTER:** Two.

Ilyra looks toward the nearest closed pump.

**ILYRA:** Show me.

The Watermaster turns.

**WATERMASTER:** This way.

Playable movement resumes.

## B002 — Three Wells

They stop beside an opened maintenance junction. Torren looks from the pipework to the three affected districts visible beyond the channel.

**TORREN:** Those share the lower feed.

**WATERMASTER:** All three.

**CYANIS:** Anything outside it?

**WATERMASTER:** Not yet.

Ilyra checks a stained filter removed from the line.

**ILYRA:** When did you shut the wells?

**WATERMASTER:** As soon as we made the connection.

**TORREN:** The wells or the feed?

The Watermaster looks at him.

**WATERMASTER:** The wells.

**TORREN:** Feed’s still running.

**WATERMASTER:** It supplies Eastbank.

**ILYRA:** Then Eastbank is still exposed.

**WATERMASTER:** We don’t know that.

**ILYRA:** No. We don’t.

She rises.

**ILYRA:** That’s the problem.

Maevra looks to the Watermaster.

**MAEVRA:** How much reserve water does Eastbank have?

**WATERMASTER:** Half a day. Less if people panic.

**CYANIS:** They already know something’s wrong.

**WATERMASTER:** Knowing something’s wrong and knowing the taps are dry aren’t the same thing.

Torren keeps examining the junction.

**TORREN:** He’s right.

Ilyra looks at him.

**TORREN:** Doesn’t mean we leave it open.

Beat.

**ILYRA:** No.

## B003 — Trace the Line

Playable investigation through the same waterworks space and one adjoining maintenance lane. Only three required interactions.

At the first junction, Torren checks the flow markers.

**TORREN:** This line’s older than the street above it.

**WATERMASTER:** Most of Dunmere is.

**TORREN:** Who maintains it?

**WATERMASTER:** My crews.

**TORREN:** All of it?

The Watermaster hesitates.

**WATERMASTER:** Everything on our maps.

Torren looks at him.

**TORREN:** That’s not all of it, is it?

**WATERMASTER:** No.

At the second point, Cyanis examines a regulator housing.

**CYANIS:** This was opened.

**MAEVRA:** Recently?

**CYANIS:** Recently enough.

**WATERMASTER:** My people would’ve logged it.

**CYANIS:** Did they?

The Watermaster already knows the answer.

**WATERMASTER:** No.

At the third point, Ilyra examines residue on the inside face of a filter. She turns it toward the others.

**ILYRA:** This didn’t come in with the river.

**MAEVRA:** How do you know?

**ILYRA:** Outside face is clean.

Torren crouches beside her.

**TORREN:** So it entered after filtration.

**ILYRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** Somebody put it there.

No one answers immediately. The Watermaster looks toward the town.

**WATERMASTER:** Fuck.

## B004 — Lower Feed

They enter the lower-feed control chamber. Cheap 2.5D: one valve wheel, one pressure gauge, one gate-state change, water sound decreasing after closure. No bespoke machinery cinematic.

The Watermaster stands at the main valve.

**WATERMASTER:** If I close this, Eastbank loses pressure.

**ILYRA:** If you don’t, you may keep poisoning them.

**WATERMASTER:** I know.

Silence. He puts both hands on the wheel.

**WATERMASTER:** Help me.

Torren steps in first. Cyanis takes the opposite side. They turn the valve. The gate closes. The water noise drops. The Watermaster checks the gauge.

**WATERMASTER:** The lower feed is shut.

Maevra turns to one of the workers at the door.

**MAEVRA:** No water from the lower feed. Every public station.

**WORKER:** Yes, Commander.

The worker leaves.

**ILYRA:** And don’t tell them the rest is safe.

The Watermaster looks at her.

**ILYRA:** Tell them the lower feed is shut. Nothing more.

**WATERMASTER:** Understood.

## B005 — The Wrong Door

With the flow stopped, Torren notices water dripping beneath a maintenance door on the lower level.

**TORREN:** That supposed to be there?

The Watermaster looks down.

**WATERMASTER:** No.

They approach. The door is old stone and timber. The lock is not. Cyanis crouches beside it.

**CYANIS:** New lock.

**WATERMASTER:** Not ours.

**MAEVRA:** What’s behind it?

**WATERMASTER:** Nothing we’re using.

Torren looks at him.

**WATERMASTER:** Old service works. Sealed before I took the post.

**TORREN:** Apparently not.

Ilyra looks at the dark residue collected beneath the threshold.

**ILYRA:** Same material.

The Watermaster’s face changes.

**WATERMASTER:** You’re sure?

**ILYRA:** Yes.

Cyanis stands.

**CYANIS:** Then we need it open.

The Watermaster hands him the key ring.

**WATERMASTER:** None of those will fit.

Cyanis tries the lock once. They don’t. He looks at Maevra. Maevra nods. Cyanis breaks the lock. Simple strike. Metal drops. The door opens onto a narrow descending stair. Old pale lights remain active below. Nobody speaks for a moment.

**WATERMASTER:** I’ve never seen that.

Torren takes the lantern from the wall.

**TORREN:** Believe you.

## B006 — Before Going Below

They step back onto the upper landing long enough to see workers changing the markers on the pumps. A call carries across the channel.

**WORKER:** Lower feed’s closed!

A second voice farther away repeats it. Ilyra watches a mother take a cup from a healer, hesitate, then hand it to her sick child. Cyanis sees it too.

**CYANIS:** They’re not going to trust the water for a while.

**ILYRA:** They shouldn’t.

**CYANIS:** Even after it’s clean.

**ILYRA:** Trust doesn’t work that fast.

Torren returns from checking the stair.

**TORREN:** Route’s passable.

**MAEVRA:** How far?

**TORREN:** Can’t tell yet.

The Watermaster looks at Maevra.

**WATERMASTER:** You find whoever did this—

He stops.

**WATERMASTER:** No.

Beat.

**WATERMASTER:** Find out how they did it.

**MAEVRA:** We will.

She looks back toward the workers.

**MAEVRA:** Keep the lower feed closed.

**WATERMASTER:** Wasn’t planning to open the fucking thing.

Maevra gives him the smallest nod.

**MAEVRA:** Good.

## B007 — Old Infrastructure

The party descends. Playable transition. Cheap 2.5D environmental shift: modern brick and timber gradually give way to much older stone. The same corridor kit can be reused deeper in S013 with altered lighting, water level, and archive dressing.

Torren runs the lantern across a wall seam.

**CYANIS:** Recognize it?

**TORREN:** No.

**CYANIS:** That’s reassuring.

**TORREN:** Didn’t say it was dangerous.

**CYANIS:** You didn’t say it wasn’t.

**TORREN:** There you go.

Ilyra stops at another patch of dark residue.

**ILYRA:** Here too.

Maevra looks down the corridor.

**MAEVRA:** Then we’re on the right route.

Far ahead, water echoes through a much larger space. The party continues.

## B008 — Sunken Archive

The corridor opens onto a flooded chamber. No dialogue immediately. Let the environment have the reveal: old archive structures, partly submerged shelving, preservation channels still emitting pale light, black water covering the lower floor. Five-second clean world shot. No portrait UI.

**CYANIS:** That’s not part of the waterworks.

**TORREN:** No.

Ilyra crouches near the edge.

**ILYRA:** Residue continues down.

Maevra looks over the flooded route.

**MAEVRA:** Can we cross?

Torren studies it.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**CYANIS:** That confident?

**TORREN:** No.

Cyanis looks at him. Torren starts down.

**TORREN:** Come on.

Maevra follows.

**MAEVRA:** Stay on Harth’s route.

Ilyra steps after them. Cyanis takes one last look back toward Dunmere above, then follows.
