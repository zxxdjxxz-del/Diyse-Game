## C09_B001 — TWO IN THE MORNING

**IVORYBRIDGE — LODGING COMMON ROOM — VERY LATE**

The building is quiet.

One low lamp remains lit.

Nimera is sitting at a table in her night clothes with several sheets of copied notation spread around her.

She is eating something cold directly from the plate.

Vaelira comes downstairs carrying her notebook.

She stops when she sees Nimera.

Nimera looks up.

They stare at each other.

**NIMERA:** No.

**VAELIRA:** I didn't say anything.

**NIMERA:** You're about to.

**VAELIRA:** You're eating in the dark.

**NIMERA:** Lamp.

**VAELIRA:** Nearly dark.

**NIMERA:** Two in the morning.

Vaelira considers that.

**VAELIRA:** Fair.

She walks to the table.

Nimera points at the empty chairs.

**NIMERA:** Plenty of room to be awake somewhere else.

Vaelira sits opposite her.

**NIMERA:** Of course.

---

## C09_B002 — RELATIONSHIP MARKS

Vaelira opens her notebook.

Nimera continues eating.

Several seconds pass.

Vaelira:

**VAELIRA:** I hate “relationship marks.”

Nimera slowly lowers her fork.

**NIMERA:** What?

**VAELIRA:** Your name for the Wayfinder notation.

**NIMERA:** You've been thinking about that?

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**NIMERA:** Since when?

Vaelira thinks.

**VAELIRA:** Ivorybridge.

**NIMERA:** That was hours ago.

**VAELIRA:** I know.

Nimera stares at her.

**NIMERA:** That's fucking incredible.

**VAELIRA:** It's imprecise.

**NIMERA:** It's provisional.

**VAELIRA:** Provisional language can still be precise.

**NIMERA:** Then what do you call it?

Vaelira opens to a page.

**VAELIRA:** Relative positional syntax.

Silence.

Nimera's face goes completely blank.

**NIMERA:** Absolutely fucking not.

**VAELIRA:** Why?

**NIMERA:** Because people have to say it.

**VAELIRA:** That isn't a technical objection.

**NIMERA:** It's the most important technical objection.

---

## C09_B003 — WORDS DO THINGS

Vaelira turns the notebook toward her.

**VAELIRA:** “Relationship” implies a connection between the sites themselves.

**NIMERA:** There is a connection.

**VAELIRA:** In the notation.

**NIMERA:** Which describes the sites.

**VAELIRA:** Relative to one another.

**NIMERA:** That's what a relationship is.

**VAELIRA:** Not necessarily.

**NIMERA:** Oh, fuck off.

Vaelira almost smiles.

**VAELIRA:** If you give something the wrong name early enough, people start asking questions that assume the name is correct.

Nimera points her fork at her.

**NIMERA:** That's true.

Vaelira looks pleased for half a second.

**NIMERA:** Your name still sucks.

The pleasure disappears.

**VAELIRA:** It's accurate.

**NIMERA:** It's six syllables too accurate.

**VAELIRA:** That's not how syllables work.

**NIMERA:** I know.

Beat.

**NIMERA:** That's how bad it is.

---

## C09_B004 — NIMERA'S NOTES

Vaelira looks down at the papers in front of Nimera.

Arrows.

Crossed-out words.

Tiny diagrams.

A sentence with three different replacements above it.

**VAELIRA:** What is that?

Nimera looks down.

**NIMERA:** Notes.

**VAELIRA:** I can see that.

**NIMERA:** Then excellent work.

Vaelira picks up one sheet.

**VAELIRA:** You crossed this out.

**NIMERA:** Yes.

**VAELIRA:** Then wrote it again.

**NIMERA:** Slightly differently.

**VAELIRA:** Then crossed that out.

**NIMERA:** It was still wrong.

**VAELIRA:** And then you wrote the first version again.

Nimera takes the page back.

**NIMERA:** Turns out I was too hard on her.

**VAELIRA:** Her?

**NIMERA:** First sentence.

Vaelira looks at Nimera.

**NIMERA:** She was doing her best.

Vaelira laughs once despite herself.

**VAELIRA:** Your notes are terrible.

**NIMERA:** My notes work.

**VAELIRA:** Nobody else could read these.

**NIMERA:** Nobody else is invited.

Vaelira looks at her own notebook.

Every line is straight.

Every heading is clear.

Every correction is cleanly marked.

Nimera notices.

**NIMERA:** Oh, yours are disgusting.

**VAELIRA:** They're legible.

**NIMERA:** Exactly.

---

## C09_B005 — DON'T CLEAN THAT UP

Nimera reaches across before Vaelira can close the notebook.

**NIMERA:** Wait.

Vaelira lets her look.

There is a calculation from the Annex.

A clean corrected version beneath it.

Nimera points at an earlier crossed-out assumption.

**NIMERA:** Why'd you cross that out?

**VAELIRA:** Because it was wrong.

**NIMERA:** I can see that.

**VAELIRA:** Then why are you asking?

**NIMERA:** Because that was the thing you thought before you knew it was wrong.

Vaelira looks at the page.

**NIMERA:** That's useful.

**VAELIRA:** I kept it.

**NIMERA:** Barely.

The line is still visible.

**VAELIRA:** How visible does a mistake need to be?

**NIMERA:** Depends how much damage it can do twice.

Vaelira glances at her.

Nimera is looking at the page, not at Vaelira.

No dramatic pause.

No consolation.

Vaelira uncrosses nothing.

Instead, she writes a short note beside the old assumption.

**VAELIRA:** Better?

Nimera leans over.

**NIMERA:** “Initial boundary assumption.”

Beat.

**NIMERA:** Boring.

Vaelira looks at her.

**NIMERA:** But yes.

---

## C09_B006 — YOUR TURN

Vaelira points at Nimera's papers.

**VAELIRA:** Fine.

**NIMERA:** What?

**VAELIRA:** My turn.

She pulls one of Nimera's pages closer.

**VAELIRA:** What's this?

**NIMERA:** I don't know.

**VAELIRA:** You wrote it.

**NIMERA:** I know.

**VAELIRA:** You don't know what your own notation means?

**NIMERA:** I know what I was trying to mean.

**VAELIRA:** That's worse.

**NIMERA:** No, it isn't.

**VAELIRA:** Nimera.

**NIMERA:** Vaelira.

Vaelira points at a small mark beside one diagram.

**VAELIRA:** This one.

**NIMERA:** That's “probably important.”

Vaelira stares.

**VAELIRA:** That's not notation.

**NIMERA:** It is now.

**VAELIRA:** What distinguishes it from “definitely important”?

Nimera points to another symbol.

Vaelira looks.

**VAELIRA:** That's just two of them.

**NIMERA:** Exactly.

Vaelira puts the page down.

**VAELIRA:** I hate this.

**NIMERA:** You're learning.

---

## C09_B007 — A BETTER BAD NAME

They work quietly for a while.

Not together exactly.

At the same table.

Every so often one reaches across to point at something on the other's page.

Eventually Vaelira speaks.

**VAELIRA:** Relative markers.

Nimera looks up.

**NIMERA:** What?

**VAELIRA:** Instead of relative positional syntax.

Nimera thinks.

**NIMERA:** Better.

Vaelira waits.

**NIMERA:** Still worse than relationship marks.

**VAELIRA:** Why?

**NIMERA:** Because we don't actually know what kind of relation they're marking.

Vaelira pauses.

That lands.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**NIMERA:** So until we do—

**VAELIRA:** We shouldn't pretend we do.

Nimera smiles.

**NIMERA:** Look at you.

Vaelira points at her.

**VAELIRA:** Don't.

**NIMERA:** Didn't say anything.

**VAELIRA:** Your face did.

Nimera looks delighted.

**NIMERA:** Oh, that's staying.

---

## C09_B008 — TWO FIFTY-SEVEN

Another long stretch.

Nimera yawns hard enough to stop pretending she isn't tired.

Vaelira notices.

**VAELIRA:** What time is it?

Nimera looks toward the wall clock / local timepiece.

**NIMERA:** Two fifty-seven.

Vaelira freezes.

**VAELIRA:** We've been sitting here for nearly an hour?

**NIMERA:** Longer.

**VAELIRA:** Why didn't you say anything?

**NIMERA:** About time passing?

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**NIMERA:** I assumed you'd modeled it.

Vaelira looks at her.

Nimera starts laughing before Vaelira can answer.

**VAELIRA:** That wasn't even good.

**NIMERA:** It's three in the fucking morning. Standards are down.

Vaelira closes her notebook.

**VAELIRA:** I'm going to bed.

**NIMERA:** Good.

Vaelira stands.

Takes two steps.

Stops.

Turns back.

**VAELIRA:** Relationship marks is still bad.

**NIMERA:** Go to sleep.

**VAELIRA:** It is.

**NIMERA:** Good night, Vaelira.

Vaelira starts upstairs.

**VAELIRA:** Relative markers.

**NIMERA:** Fuck off.

From the stairs:

**VAELIRA:** More precise.

Nimera throws a crumpled scrap of paper toward her.

It misses by several feet.

Vaelira looks at where it lands.

Then back at Nimera.

**VAELIRA:** Terrible accuracy.

**NIMERA:** GO TO BED.

Vaelira disappears upstairs laughing.

Nimera shakes her head, smiling despite herself.

She looks at Vaelira's proposed term written in the margin of her notes.

Nimera crosses it out.

Underneath, she writes:

**RELATIONSHIP MARKS**

Then, after a second:

**(PROVISIONAL)**

She closes the papers.
