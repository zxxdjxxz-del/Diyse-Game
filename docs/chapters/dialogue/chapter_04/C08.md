## C08_B001 — STOPPING HERE

**FIELD CAMP — EVENING**

A small fire burns beside the road.

Nothing about the place suggests people normally camp here.

The party has simply found a reasonably flat patch of ground before dark and stopped.

Packs are open.

Bedrolls are still bundled.

Dinner is nearly finished.

Torren stands at the edge of the firelight looking over the surrounding ground.

Vaelira watches him for a moment.

**VAELIRA:** What are you looking for?

**TORREN:** Problems.

**VAELIRA:** Specifically?

Torren points toward one side of the clearing.

**TORREN:** Ground dips there.

Then toward the road.

**TORREN:** Too close.

He looks toward a cluster of brush.

**TORREN:** Can't see through that.

Vaelira looks around again.

**VAELIRA:** So this is a bad campsite.

**TORREN:** No.

Beat.

**TORREN:** It's a campsite.

Vaelira waits.

**TORREN:** They all have something wrong with them.

Nimera, eating:

**NIMERA:** That's reassuring.

**TORREN:** You want reassuring, sleep indoors.

**NIMERA:** I frequently do.

---

## C08_B002 — NO ONE ASSIGNED ANYTHING

Vaelira finishes eating.

Cyanis reaches for the cooking pot.

Ilyra is already gathering bowls.

Nimera starts clearing a place for her bedroll.

Torren comes back toward the fire.

Vaelira looks between them.

**VAELIRA:** Who decided who was doing that?

Cyanis looks down at the pot in his hands.

**CYANIS:** Doing what?

**VAELIRA:** Any of it.

Ilyra looks at her.

**VAELIRA:** You took the bowls.

She points at Cyanis.

**VAELIRA:** He took the pot.

Then Nimera.

**VAELIRA:** She's—

Nimera is kicking a stone away from where she intends to sleep.

**NIMERA:** Improving property values.

Vaelira ignores that.

**VAELIRA:** Nobody assigned anything.

**ILYRA:** No.

**VAELIRA:** Then how do you know who's doing what?

Ilyra shrugs.

**ILYRA:** Something needs doing.

Cyanis:

**CYANIS:** Someone does it.

Vaelira looks at Torren.

**VAELIRA:** That's your system?

**TORREN:** More or less.

**VAELIRA:** That's barely a system.

**NIMERA:** You've been here one dinner and already understand us.

---

## C08_B003 — WHERE DO I SLEEP?

Vaelira picks up her bedroll.

She looks at the unfamiliar ground.

**VAELIRA:** Is there somewhere I shouldn't put this?

Torren turns and actually looks.

He walks a few steps.

Presses one boot into the ground.

Looks toward the fire.

Then toward the road.

**TORREN:** Here should be fine.

Vaelira looks at the spot.

**VAELIRA:** Why?

**TORREN:** Level enough.

He points.

**TORREN:** You're not downhill from the fire.

Another point.

**TORREN:** Not blocking anybody getting out.

Vaelira studies the ground.

**VAELIRA:** That's it?

**TORREN:** For now.

**VAELIRA:** For now?

**TORREN:** Might find out something sucks about it later.

Vaelira stares.

**TORREN:** Then you move.

Nimera:

**NIMERA:** Science.

Vaelira looks at her.

**NIMERA:** Field testing.

**VAELIRA:** I hate you a little.

**NIMERA:** That's fast.

---

## C08_B004 — WATCH

Vaelira lays out her bedroll.

**VAELIRA:** What's the watch rotation?

Torren sits near his pack.

**TORREN:** Haven't decided yet.

**VAELIRA:** We stopped here without deciding who's keeping watch?

**TORREN:** We stopped here about forty minutes ago.

**VAELIRA:** Exactly.

**TORREN:** Had other things to do.

Vaelira looks toward the darkness.

**VAELIRA:** Like dinner.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**VAELIRA:** Dinner outranked perimeter security.

**TORREN:** Hungry people make stupid mistakes.

Nimera points her spoon at him.

**NIMERA:** Finally, doctrine I support.

Torren looks around the camp.

**TORREN:** I'll take first.

**ILYRA:** I can take second.

Torren nods.

Cyanis:

**CYANIS:** Wake me after.

**NIMERA:** And I'll take the comfortable one where everyone's already proven we aren't being murdered.

**CYANIS:** Brave.

**NIMERA:** Strategic.

Vaelira looks between them.

**VAELIRA:** You just made that up.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**VAELIRA:** Right now.

**TORREN:** That's usually when we need it.

---

## C08_B005 — AFTER DINNER

The meal is finished.

Conversation loses whatever shape it had.

Nobody announces that dinner is over.

It simply is.

Ilyra finishes putting the cookware aside.

Cyanis settles against his pack.

Nimera is fighting with one boot.

Torren crouches beside the fire.

He takes a short charred twig from the edge of the coals.

One end glows orange.

He uses the ember to light a blunt, draws until it catches, then drops the twig back into the fire.

No one comments.

Torren moves a little farther from the others, settles where he can see the road and surrounding dark, and smokes quietly.

Vaelira notices.

Not because it is important.

Because nearly everything is new to her right now.

She looks away.

---

## C08_B006 — NOTHING

Vaelira takes out her notebook.

Nimera finally gets her boot off.

Silence settles for a while.

Fire.

Night insects.

Occasional movement of gear.

Vaelira looks around.

**VAELIRA:** What happens now?

Nimera looks at her.

**NIMERA:** What do you mean?

**VAELIRA:** After dinner.

**NIMERA:** This.

**VAELIRA:** What is “this”?

Nimera looks around.

**NIMERA:** Nothing.

**VAELIRA:** Nothing?

**NIMERA:** Yeah.

**VAELIRA:** You deliberately schedule nothing?

**NIMERA:** We don't schedule it.

**VAELIRA:** Then how do you know when you're doing it?

Nimera stares at her.

**NIMERA:** Vaelira.

Beat.

**NIMERA:** We're doing it right now.

Vaelira looks down at her open notebook.

Then around the camp.

Cyanis has his eyes closed.

Ilyra is quietly fixing a loose strap.

Torren is watching the unfamiliar road.

Nobody is waiting for instructions.

Nobody is discussing tomorrow yet.

Vaelira slowly closes the notebook.

**VAELIRA:** This feels inefficient.

Cyanis, without opening his eyes:

**CYANIS:** Give it a chance.

---

## C08_B007 — LEARNING THE IMPORTANT RULES

Vaelira lies down.

A root presses immediately into her back.

She sits back up.

Nimera looks over.

**NIMERA:** Problem?

**VAELIRA:** Root.

Nimera nods solemnly.

**NIMERA:** They'll get you.

Vaelira drags the bedroll several inches to the side.

Lies down again.

Pause.

**VAELIRA:** Better.

From the edge of camp:

**TORREN:** Told you we might find something wrong with it.

Vaelira turns her head toward him.

**VAELIRA:** You sound pleased.

**TORREN:** Little bit.

**VAELIRA:** Asshole.

Torren gives a quiet laugh.

Nimera looks delighted.

**NIMERA:** Oh, she's settling in.

**VAELIRA:** Don't make this significant.

**NIMERA:** Wouldn't dream of it.

A few seconds pass.

**VAELIRA:** If I need something?

Ilyra:

**ILYRA:** Ask.

**VAELIRA:** Anyone?

**ILYRA:** Whoever's awake.

Vaelira considers this.

**VAELIRA:** There really isn't a procedure for anything here.

Torren:

**TORREN:** There's procedures.

Beat.

**TORREN:** Just not for sleeping.

Vaelira pulls the blanket up.

**VAELIRA:** Good.

Pause.

**VAELIRA:** I was beginning to worry.

Nimera laughs softly.

The fire settles lower.

Torren remains awake at the edge of the light, smoking and watching a road none of them knows particularly well.

The others gradually go quiet.

Vaelira does too.

**END C08 — VAELIRA LEARNS CAMP**
