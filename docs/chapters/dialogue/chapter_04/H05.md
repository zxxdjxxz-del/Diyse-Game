## H05_B001 — HER TABLE, APPARENTLY

**CRESTHAVEN — WORKROOM — LATE AFTERNOON**

Vaelira is standing at the table she acquired approximately one hour ago.

Torren's route notes have been pushed to one side.

Nimera's copied symbols occupy another.

Vaelira has somehow created a clean working area in the middle.

She is labeling a storage box.

Cyanis walks through carrying a stack of supplies.

Vaelira looks at him.

Then the supplies.

Then him again.

**VAELIRA:** Those don't belong here.

Cyanis stops.

Looks down.

**CYANIS:** Strong opening.

**VAELIRA:** Medical storage is across the hall.

**CYANIS:** These aren't medical.

Vaelira reads the top label.

**VAELIRA:** Bandages.

**CYANIS:** Also rope.

Vaelira looks at him.

**CYANIS:** Mixed box.

**VAELIRA:** Why?

**CYANIS:** Because someone put rope in the bandage box.

**VAELIRA:** Who?

Cyanis thinks.

**CYANIS:** That's a dangerous question around here.

Vaelira points across the hall.

**VAELIRA:** Take it away.

**CYANIS:** Yes, ma'am.

He turns.

**VAELIRA:** Cyanis.

He stops.

Different tone.

---

## H05_B002 — I SHOULD APOLOGIZE

Cyanis sets the box down.

**CYANIS:** What?

Vaelira takes a moment longer than usual.

**VAELIRA:** I owe all of you an apology.

Cyanis looks genuinely confused.

**CYANIS:** For the box?

Vaelira gives him a look.

**CYANIS:** Worth checking.

**VAELIRA:** The Annex.

Beat.

Cyanis leans one hand on the box.

**CYANIS:** What about it?

**VAELIRA:** We went there because of me.

**CYANIS:** Yes.

**VAELIRA:** I brought you into a system I thought I understood.

**CYANIS:** Yes.

**VAELIRA:** We ended up fighting through half the lower facility.

**CYANIS:** Felt like more.

**VAELIRA:** Cyanis.

**CYANIS:** Sorry.

Vaelira folds her arms.

**VAELIRA:** Someone was nearly killed.

**CYANIS:** I know.

**VAELIRA:** You could have been.

Cyanis waits.

**VAELIRA:** All of you.

Another beat.

**VAELIRA:** So. I'm sorry.

---

## H05_B003 — FOR WHAT?

Cyanis looks at her.

**CYANIS:** For what?

Vaelira's expression tightens.

**VAELIRA:** I just told you.

**CYANIS:** You listed things that happened.

**VAELIRA:** Because I was wrong.

**CYANIS:** About the boundary.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** You already said that.

**VAELIRA:** That doesn't make it disappear.

**CYANIS:** Didn't say it did.

Vaelira exhales through her nose.

**VAELIRA:** I am attempting to apologize to you.

**CYANIS:** Badly.

Her head snaps toward him.

**VAELIRA:** Excuse me?

**CYANIS:** Too broad.

Vaelira stares.

**VAELIRA:** My apology is insufficiently specific.

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

**VAELIRA:** You're grading my apology.

**CYANIS:** Apparently.

**VAELIRA:** I may kill you.

**CYANIS:** That one you'd probably need to apologize for.

Vaelira looks away before he can see the smile.

He sees it anyway.

---

## H05_B004 — THE PART THAT WAS HERS

**VAELIRA:** Fine.

She turns back.

**VAELIRA:** I chose a boundary that excluded relevant effects.

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

**VAELIRA:** I trusted the model longer than I should have because it continued producing accurate predictions.

**CYANIS:** Maybe.

Vaelira frowns.

**VAELIRA:** Maybe?

**CYANIS:** You had bad information that still looked good.

**VAELIRA:** I chose what information counted.

**CYANIS:** There.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** That's yours.

Vaelira nods once.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** You didn't classify twelve days of return variance by yourself.

**VAELIRA:** No.

**CYANIS:** You didn't approve every exposure procedure.

**VAELIRA:** No.

**CYANIS:** You didn't make us follow you downstairs.

Vaelira looks at him.

**VAELIRA:** I asked you to come.

**CYANIS:** And we came.

**VAELIRA:** Because you trusted me.

**CYANIS:** Yeah.

No hesitation.

Vaelira goes quiet.

**CYANIS:** That wasn't a mistake.

**VAELIRA:** The Annex suggests otherwise.

**CYANIS:** No.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** Trusting you doesn't mean you have to be right every time.

---

## H05_B005 — THAT SOUNDS TERRIBLE

Vaelira stares at him.

**VAELIRA:** That sounds terrible.

**CYANIS:** What does?

**VAELIRA:** “You don't have to be right.”

**CYANIS:** Why?

**VAELIRA:** Because being wrong can kill people.

**CYANIS:** So can waiting until you're certain.

Vaelira says nothing.

**CYANIS:** So can doing nothing.

Still nothing.

**CYANIS:** So can me being wrong.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** I know.

He shrugs.

**CYANIS:** That's why there are five of us.

Vaelira looks toward the rest of Cresthaven.

**VAELIRA:** Four before me.

**CYANIS:** Five now.

She catches that.

Cyanis does not make anything of it.

---

## H05_B006 — DON'T DO THAT

Vaelira turns a pencil between her fingers.

**VAELIRA:** I still feel like I should say I'm sorry.

**CYANIS:** Then say something you're sorry for.

**VAELIRA:** I—

Stops.

Thinks.

**VAELIRA:** I am sorry I dismissed the return variance as instrument loss without forcing a broader review.

Cyanis nods.

**CYANIS:** All right.

Vaelira waits.

**VAELIRA:** That's it?

**CYANIS:** What do you want me to do?

**VAELIRA:** I don't know.

**CYANIS:** Forgive you?

Vaelira immediately grimaces.

**VAELIRA:** No.

**CYANIS:** Good.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** That would be strange.

**CYANIS:** Very.

**VAELIRA:** You weren't the person harmed by that decision.

**CYANIS:** Exactly.

Vaelira looks at him.

That lands harder than reassurance would have.

**CYANIS:** Don't apologize to me for somebody else's injury so I can tell you it's okay.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** That's not mine to give you.

Vaelira nods slowly.

**VAELIRA:** No.

---

## H05_B007 — WHAT SHE CAN DO

Silence.

Workers cross the background carrying lumber and supplies.

Cresthaven keeps being built around them.

Vaelira looks down at her notes.

**VAELIRA:** The Annex is changing the exposure protocol.

**CYANIS:** I know.

**VAELIRA:** Return variance is primary now.

**CYANIS:** You told us.

**VAELIRA:** Independent review can't use the same boundary assumptions.

**CYANIS:** Also told us.

Vaelira looks annoyed.

**VAELIRA:** Then why did you let me continue?

**CYANIS:** You seemed committed.

She almost laughs.

**VAELIRA:** Asshole.

**CYANIS:** There she is.

Vaelira sets the pencil down.

**VAELIRA:** So that's it.

**CYANIS:** What?

**VAELIRA:** Find what was mine.

Fix what I can.

Don't claim the rest.

**CYANIS:** Sounds about right.

**VAELIRA:** That's irritatingly simple.

**CYANIS:** Most useful things are.

Vaelira looks at him.

**VAELIRA:** That is obviously false.

**CYANIS:** Yeah, probably.

---

## H05_B008 — THE ACTUAL APOLOGY

Cyanis picks the mixed supply box back up.

**VAELIRA:** Wait.

He stops.

Vaelira points at the table.

**VAELIRA:** Your breakfast wrapper.

Cyanis looks.

The wrapper from earlier is still sitting among the papers.

**CYANIS:** Could be anyone's.

**VAELIRA:** Cyanis.

**CYANIS:** No witnesses.

**VAELIRA:** I watched you eat it.

**CYANIS:** Circumstantial.

Vaelira picks up the wrapper.

**VAELIRA:** Take it.

Cyanis makes no move.

Vaelira throws it.

This time it hits him in the chest.

He looks down.

Then at her.

Vaelira smiles.

**VAELIRA:** Improved accuracy.

Cyanis picks up the wrapper.

**CYANIS:** I'm sorry.

Vaelira points at him.

**VAELIRA:** That apology I accept.

Cyanis laughs and finally carries the box out.

Vaelira returns to the table.

She looks at the unfinished boundary in her notebook.

Doesn't close it.

Doesn't erase it.

She goes back to work.
