# 7. CROWN PROTOTYPE — COMBAT IDENTITY

## Core concept

A **single-target combat prototype built around repeated attack correction**.

It is not an elemental mini-Crucible.

It does not repeat S024's six-element gimmick.

Its gameplay identity is:

**miss → adjust → reacquire → strike repeatedly**

This naturally supports the Relentless Flurry reward without claiming the defeated enemy somehow creates the Card.

## Structure

- **One enemy**
- **One HP bar**
- **No transformation**
- **No summoned support units**
- **No mandatory character**
- **No Prime requirement**
- **No scripted round gimmick**
- **No immunity puzzle**
- Stronger than surrounding Chapter 4 enemies but clearly below a Major Hunt.

### Retained technical baseline
- Lv. 11
- HP: 1,505
- EXP: 280
- CEXP: 50
- Auren: 120
- Historical tuning reference: approximately 4–7 rounds depending on build/party

Those numbers remain balance targets rather than narrative scripting.

# 8. MOVESET

## CALIBRATION STRIKE
Single-target physical attack.

Basic pressure move.

Clean readable animation using the Prototype's normal attack rig.

## TARGET ACQUISITION
The Crown Prototype marks one party member.

Until its next major sequence:
- increased Accuracy against that target;
- target is visibly identified through an inexpensive reticle/status icon.

No unavoidable damage.

Player can defend, protect, heal, debuff, or simply race it.

## RELENTLESS SEQUENCE
Three or four physical hits.

Normally prioritizes the acquired target, but later hits may redirect if that target is incapacitated.

This is the boss's signature attack pattern.

It **does not award or activate Relentless Flurry during battle**.

The similarity is thematic only.

## CORRECTIVE STEP
If one of the Prototype's attacks misses or is evaded, it gains a temporary Accuracy increase.

The machine is visibly correcting for failure.

This keeps high-evasion parties useful without allowing permanent trivialization.

The correction expires normally.

## BREAKLINE
Heavy single-target physical blow.

Moderate chance to lower Defense for a short duration.

Used less frequently than its multi-hit sequence.

Provides a reason to respond rather than simply heal through repeated small hits.
