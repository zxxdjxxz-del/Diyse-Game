## B001 — Regulation Core

Old infrastructure modern Annex has built around. Six large elemental chambers around central regulator shaft, with modern instruments/bridges/railings on old stone/metal.

Vaelira stops at first landing.

**NIMERA:** Bad?

**VAELIRA:** Familiar.

**TORREN:** That's bad?

**VAELIRA:** Right now? Yes.

**CYANIS:** Because?

Vaelira points down.

**VAELIRA:** Those six chambers are the model.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** Not a representation of it.

She looks at machinery.

**VAELIRA:** The actual thing we've been measuring.

**NIMERA:** And the spillway?

Vaelira points farther down beneath six chambers.

**VAELIRA:** Under it.

**TORREN:** Of course it is.

**ILYRA:** You sound personally offended.

**TORREN:** Stairs.

## B002 — Six answers

Local inspection safe pocket. Six readings stabilize.

**VAELIRA:** Fire.

**VAELIRA:** Ice.

**VAELIRA:** Lightning. Wind. Earth. Water.

All six settle.

**NIMERA:** Same problem?

**VAELIRA:** Worse.

**CYANIS:** Why?

**VAELIRA:** Because they're right.

**TORREN:** Again.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

A seventh return-pressure number rises after the six settle.

**NIMERA:** There.

**VAELIRA:** Part of it.

**NIMERA:** Part?

Vaelira checks more instruments.

**VAELIRA:** It's not staying here.

**ILYRA:** Where is it going?

Vaelira looks down shaft.

**VAELIRA:** Central balance.

### PLAYER CONTROL — CHAMBER LEVEL
Six chambers; changing activation alters environmental state with cheap lighting/VFX/audio. Random encounters outside safe refuges.

## B003 — The thing between them

Vaelira lays original diagram beside live reading.

**NIMERA:** There it is again.

**CYANIS:** Vaelira?

Vaelira points at six channels.

**VAELIRA:** I kept asking what each element did to the others.

**NIMERA:** That's the model.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

Vaelira points at central regulator.

**VAELIRA:** I never asked what the system does when all six succeed.

Silence.

**TORREN:** It dumps something.

**VAELIRA:** More than that.

She studies the pattern.

**VAELIRA:** Fire isn't doing this.

Points.

**VAELIRA:** Neither is Ice. Lightning. Wind. Earth. Water.

**NIMERA:** It's what happens between them.

**VAELIRA:** After them.

Nimera looks at her.

Vaelira corrects herself.

**VAELIRA:** Both.

**CYANIS:** So not a seventh element.

**VAELIRA:** No.

**VAELIRA:** A seventh reaction.

Beat.

**NIMERA:** The system reacting to being balanced.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

## B004 — Too much of a good answer

**ILYRA:** Why is it dangerous now?

**VAELIRA:** Because it accumulates.

**NIMERA:** Slowly.

**VAELIRA:** Until it doesn't.

**TORREN:** That's usually how breaking works.

Vaelira looks at him.

**TORREN:** Roads. Bridges. Bow limbs.

Beat.

**TORREN:** People.

Ilyra glances at him. He doesn't elaborate.

**VAELIRA:** Every correction pushes a small amount somewhere the model wasn't watching.

**CYANIS:** And we kept congratulating ourselves because the six numbers got better.

**VAELIRA:** I did.

**CYANIS:** We.

Vaelira looks at him.

**CYANIS:** There are a lot of people working upstairs.

**NIMERA:** Doesn't make the model useless.

**VAELIRA:** No.

**NIMERA:** Makes the boundary useless.

**VAELIRA:** Incomplete.

**NIMERA:** That's a nicer word.

**VAELIRA:** It's also more accurate.

**NIMERA:** Fine.

## B005 — It has been correcting itself

Safe landing overlooking central chamber. Mechanism moves/reconfigures.

**VAELIRA:** It's compensating.

**NIMERA:** For what?

**VAELIRA:** Us.

**CYANIS:** We haven't touched anything.

**VAELIRA:** We interrupted the exposure wing.

**ILYRA:** Stabilized the researcher.

**VAELIRA:** And staff shut down the compromised branch.

**NIMERA:** So the load moved again.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

Three chamber feeds light; three go dark.

**TORREN:** That looks intentional.

**VAELIRA:** It is.

**CYANIS:** Can you stop it?

Vaelira works controls. Nothing.

**VAELIRA:** Not from here.

Mechanism locks into combat configuration.

**VAELIRA:** We're going to have to break it.

**TORREN:** Finally.

**NIMERA:** You've been waiting all day to hear that.

**TORREN:** Little bit.

### PLAYER CONTROL — FINAL CORE RUNWAY

### MANDATORY BOSS — SIXFOLD CRUCIBLE

#### FORM I — DIVIDED SIXFOLD CRUCIBLE
- Six elemental chambers around central engine.
- Only three chambers active/targetable at once; others dormant until rotation.
- Central core legitimate target.
- Player may rush core or spend actions dismantling chambers.
- Destroyed chambers stay destroyed.
- Six identities: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Earth, Water.
- What survives becomes part of Form II.

#### FORM I DEFEAT / GENUINE TRANSFORMATION
Core reaches zero. No damage spills forward. Battle UI withdraws for transformation staging.

Vaelira sees it.

**VAELIRA:** Back.

Party withdraws within existing stage.

Surviving chamber lights feed inward; central silhouette changes with prepared state swap.

**NIMERA:** It's combining them.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**TORREN:** We just killed it.

**VAELIRA:** We killed the divided system.

New body forms.

**CYANIS:** Different thing?

**VAELIRA:** Different problem.

#### FORM II — UNIFIED ELEMENTAL BODY
Fresh full HP / fresh authored MP.

Inherited traits only from Form-I chambers still intact.
Examples:
- Fire: stronger Burning/spreading pressure
- Ice: stronger Slow/Freeze pressure
- Lightning: fast/high-accuracy magic
- Wind: Speed/area pressure
- Earth: defense/grounding pressure
- Water: healing/steam/Soaked-style pressure

Destroyed chamber traits stay gone. No Prime refresh from transformation.

## B006 — The answer they made

Form II reaches zero. Machine collapses to dormant regulator instead of exploding. Six chamber indicators come back very low and stable; central return near zero.

**NIMERA:** Say it.

**VAELIRA:** What?

**NIMERA:** You already have the sentence.

Vaelira looks at dormant core.

**VAELIRA:** The seventh reaction wasn't a failure of any one element.

Cyanis waits.

**VAELIRA:** It was the consequence of making all six behave as though the boundary around them was the whole system.

**TORREN:** So every time you fixed it—

**VAELIRA:** We moved the cost.

**ILYRA:** Onto people who weren't in the equation.

Vaelira's expression changes.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

## B007 — Not stupidity

**VAELIRA:** I should have seen it.

**NIMERA:** Maybe.

Vaelira looks at her.

**NIMERA:** That's not the same as saying you could have known it from what you measured.

**VAELIRA:** I chose what to measure.

**NIMERA:** Yes.

Beat.

**NIMERA:** That's the part you own.

Cyanis:

**CYANIS:** And the people who approved it?

Vaelira looks upward.

**VAELIRA:** Own theirs.

**ILYRA:** Good.

**TORREN:** So what now?

**VAELIRA:** We stop calling transferred cost acceptable variance.

**NIMERA:** That's a start.

**VAELIRA:** No.

She looks at her.

**VAELIRA:** That's the minimum.

**NIMERA:** Better.
