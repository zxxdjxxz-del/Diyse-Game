## B002 — They didn't erase it

Vaelira stands before a public technical board. Yesterday's six-channel model is still posted, with new sheets around it:
- RETURN VARIANCE
- MULTI-CHANNEL EXPOSURE
- BOUNDARY ASSUMPTIONS
- SEVENTH REACTION — PROVISIONAL

A junior researcher pins up a correction.

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** I changed the third return table.

**VAELIRA:** Why?

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** The baseline was wrong.

Vaelira reads it.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** This is better.

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** That's it?

**VAELIRA:** Did you want punishment?

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** No.

**VAELIRA:** Good.

She points at board.

**VAELIRA:** Next time, write why you changed the baseline.

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** I thought that part was obvious.

**VAELIRA:** It is to you.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** That's why you need to write it.

Junior nods, starts to leave.

**VAELIRA:** And don't take the old table down.

Junior stops.

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** It's wrong.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

Vaelira looks at both versions.

**VAELIRA:** Leave it.

Junior understands enough.

Nimera has been nearby.

**NIMERA:** You kept the bad one.

**VAELIRA:** Incorrect isn't useless.

**NIMERA:** Sometimes it is.

Vaelira looks at her.

**VAELIRA:** Frequently.

Nimera smiles.

**NIMERA:** Good. I was worried you'd become inspirational.

Vaelira goes flat.

**VAELIRA:** Horrifying.

## B003 — What changes when nobody has to pretend

Nimera looks at revised board.

**NIMERA:** Think they'll actually challenge you now?

**VAELIRA:** Some of them.

**NIMERA:** That's optimistic.

**VAELIRA:** It wasn't meant to be.

Nimera laughs.

Vaelira opens notebook. Larger six-channel diagram, boundary still unfinished.

**NIMERA:** Still haven't closed it.

**VAELIRA:** I don't know where it belongs.

**NIMERA:** Yesterday that would've bothered you more.

Vaelira thinks.

**VAELIRA:** Yesterday I would have drawn one.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** That would have been worse.

Nimera nods.

**NIMERA:** Probably.

**VAELIRA:** Probably?

**NIMERA:** I'm learning from you.

Vaelira narrows eyes.

Nimera starts walking.

**NIMERA:** Terrible feeling, isn't it?

## B004 — The party is waiting

Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren near eastern bridge. Torren has route map, Cyanis food, Ilyra coffee/local equivalent.

**NIMERA:** She's coming.

**TORREN:** Didn't ask.

**NIMERA:** You looked.

**TORREN:** At the road.

**NIMERA:** Of course you did.

Vaelira joins.

Cyanis holds out wrapped breakfast.

**CYANIS:** Eat.

Vaelira looks at it and him.

**VAELIRA:** Is this a group rule?

**ILYRA:** Increasingly.

**TORREN:** He's usually the problem.

**CYANIS:** Betrayal before breakfast.

**ILYRA:** You've already eaten.

Cyanis looks at his own food.

**CYANIS:** Strong evidence.

Vaelira takes breakfast.

## B005 — Wrong road

Torren folds route map.

**VAELIRA:** Cresthaven is west from here.

**TORREN:** Mostly.

**VAELIRA:** Mostly?

Torren points.

**TORREN:** Main road goes west.

Then another line.

**TORREN:** We don't.

**VAELIRA:** Why?

**TORREN:** Washout.

**VAELIRA:** The Crown route sheet says it's clear.

**TORREN:** Crown route sheet's three weeks old.

Vaelira looks west, then at Torren.

**VAELIRA:** So I was wrong.

Torren shrugs.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

Beat.

**TORREN:** Happens.

Vaelira looks at him.

**TORREN:** South road's better.

He starts walking.

Nimera sees Vaelira pause.

**NIMERA:** You all right?

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** That was strangely painless.

Nimera grins.

**NIMERA:** Give him time.

From ahead:

**TORREN:** I can hear you.

**NIMERA:** Good.

## B006 — Why she stays

Cyanis falls into step beside Vaelira.

**CYANIS:** You don't have to come with us today.

**VAELIRA:** I know.

**CYANIS:** Annex probably has six months of work now.

**VAELIRA:** More.

**CYANIS:** That's what I figured.

Vaelira eats.

**VAELIRA:** They'll do it.

**CYANIS:** Sure?

**VAELIRA:** No.

Cyanis nods.

**CYANIS:** Good enough.

**VAELIRA:** That's an appalling standard.

**CYANIS:** Works surprisingly often.

A few steps.

**VAELIRA:** I want to see the other sites.

**CYANIS:** The maps?

**VAELIRA:** The maps. The systems. Whatever else you've been finding.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** I spent years studying one structure until I knew exactly what questions to ask it.

She glances back.

**VAELIRA:** Apparently I'd like some new structures.

**CYANIS:** We've been very good at finding inconvenient ones.

**VAELIRA:** I noticed.

Cyanis smiles.

**CYANIS:** Then you're hired.

**VAELIRA:** I already joined you.

**CYANIS:** Then the paperwork's excellent.

Vaelira stares.

**VAELIRA:** There is no paperwork.

**CYANIS:** Even better.

## B007 — One condition

Vaelira slows.

**VAELIRA:** Cyanis.

He looks back.

**VAELIRA:** If I do that again—

She tries another sentence.

**VAELIRA:** If I'm certain about something—

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** And you think the question is wrong—

**CYANIS:** I'll ask.

**VAELIRA:** Even if I'm sure.

**CYANIS:** Especially if you're sure.

Nimera ahead:

**NIMERA:** I volunteer too.

Vaelira looks at her back.

**VAELIRA:** I assumed you would.

**NIMERA:** Excellent.

**TORREN:** Sounds exhausting.

**ILYRA:** You'll survive.

**TORREN:** Probably.

Vaelira catches the word and looks at him. Torren doesn't notice, or pretends not to.

## B008 — The opposite condition

Ilyra falls into step on Vaelira's other side.

**ILYRA:** Goes both ways.

**VAELIRA:** What does?

**ILYRA:** If they're wrong.

She points at Cyanis and Torren.

**ILYRA:** Tell them.

Cyanis without turning:

**CYANIS:** Seems hostile.

**ILYRA:** You'll survive.

**TORREN:** Probably.

**NIMERA:** I'm starting a list.

**CYANIS:** Of what?

**NIMERA:** Things you're wrong about.

**CYANIS:** Small notebook?

**NIMERA:** Fuck you.

Vaelira laughs — genuinely.

Everyone looks at her.

**VAELIRA:** What?

**CYANIS:** Nothing.

Vaelira narrows eyes.

**VAELIRA:** That wasn't nothing.

**NIMERA:** See? She's learning.
