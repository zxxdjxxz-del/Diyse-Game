## B002 — Whose failure?

Senior Annex staff around main table. Vaelira enters; conversation stops.

**SENIOR RESEARCHER:** We heard what happened.

**VAELIRA:** Which part?

**SENIOR RESEARCHER:** The Crucible.

Vaelira puts return records on table.

**VAELIRA:** Then you haven't heard enough.

**VAELIRA:** The six-channel predictions were correct.

**RESEARCHER:** We know.

**VAELIRA:** That's the problem.

She points to outer-return figures.

**VAELIRA:** We defined success as stability inside these six channels.

Then spill readings.

**VAELIRA:** We treated everything outside them as secondary variance.

**SENIOR RESEARCHER:** Your model defined the boundary.

Vaelira looks at them.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**VAELIRA:** I chose that boundary.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** That part is mine.

Then another log.

**VAELIRA:** This isn't.

**VAELIRA:** Return variance exceeded normal drift twelve days ago.

**RESEARCHER:** It was logged.

**VAELIRA:** As noise.

**RESEARCHER:** Because the primary cycle stayed predictive.

**VAELIRA:** I know why you did it.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** You still did it.

## B003 — Don't fix one person

**RESEARCHER:** Then what are you saying?

**VAELIRA:** That the model boundary was wrong.

She points at herself.

**VAELIRA:** Mine.

Then records.

**VAELIRA:** That abnormal returns were repeatedly accepted because the six primary readings looked good.

Points toward staff.

**VAELIRA:** Ours.

Then sealed lower level.

**VAELIRA:** That exposure thresholds were evaluated one element at a time when the researcher was receiving alternating exposure.

Ilyra hardens.

**VAELIRA:** Also ours.

**SENIOR RESEARCHER:** You're spreading responsibility.

Vaelira narrows her eyes.

**VAELIRA:** No.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** I'm locating it.

Nimera likes that sentence.

**VAELIRA:** If you call this my failure and remove me, you'll feel decisive.

She taps records.

**VAELIRA:** And every procedure that let it happen will still be sitting here tomorrow.

Cyanis says nothing.

## B004 — The director owns something

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** I approved the exposure protocol.

Vaelira looks at them.

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** And your boundary definition.

**RESEARCHER:** Director—

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** No.

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** You designed it.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** I authorized it.

Vaelira nods.

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** We all kept using it because it kept giving us correct answers.

**NIMERA:** Correct answers to one question.

Everyone looks at her.

**NIMERA:** Sorry.

Beat.

**NIMERA:** Actually, no. That's the whole fucking problem.

**VAELIRA:** It is.

Director looks at model.

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** Then we change the question.

## B005 — What changes now

Concrete decisions, not vague “do better.”

**VAELIRA:** Exposure work stops.

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** Agreed.

**VAELIRA:** All six-element alternating exposure.

**ILYRA:** All exposure involving more than one channel until you've checked interaction.

Vaelira considers.

**VAELIRA:** Better.

She writes it.

**VAELIRA:** Lower spill galleries stay closed except medical and stabilization crews.

**ANNEX DIRECTOR:** Yes.

**VAELIRA:** Return variance gets promoted to a primary measurement.

**RESEARCHER:** That changes every baseline.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**RESEARCHER:** Weeks of work.

Vaelira looks at them.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

Researcher exhales.

**RESEARCHER:** Fine.

Vaelira writes again.

**VAELIRA:** No model gets independently checked only by people using the same boundary assumptions.

Cyanis looks at her.

**VAELIRA:** Don't.

**CYANIS:** Didn't say anything.

**VAELIRA:** Your face did.

Torren turns away to hide grin. Nimera sees him.

**TORREN:** Didn't say worse.

## B007 — The injured researcher

**ILYRA:** One more thing.
