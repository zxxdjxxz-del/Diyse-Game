## B001 — Six are fine

**SIXFOLD ANNEX — PRIMARY RESEARCH FLOOR**

Researchers are moving equipment, shutting resistant shutters, and arguing with instruments.

Vaelira heads to the main regulator.

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** Vaelira—

**VAELIRA:** What changed?

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** Nothing changed.

Vaelira stops.

**VAELIRA:** Something outside just answered the primary cycle.

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** The six channels are stable.

**VAELIRA:** I know.

Six readings: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Earth, Water — all normal.

**NIMERA:** That's what we just saw outside.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** So those are fine.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

Torren looks toward sealed outer galleries.

**TORREN:** Something isn't.

**VAELIRA:** Correct.

Vaelira asks for raw return readings.

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** Those aren't part of the primary set.

**VAELIRA:** I know.

Beat.

**VAELIRA:** That's why I want them.

## B002 — The missing number

A second instrument display appears.

**VAELIRA:** Input.

Then distribution.

**VAELIRA:** Distribution.

Then return.

**VAELIRA:** Return.

**CYANIS:** And?

Nimera leans closer.

**NIMERA:** Those don't add up.

**VAELIRA:** No.

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** Instrument loss.

Vaelira looks at them.

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** That's how we've been logging it.

**VAELIRA:** How long?

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** It's always had some variance.

**VAELIRA:** How long has it been this large?

They check.

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** Twelve days.

Vaelira goes still.

**VAELIRA:** And nobody told me?

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** Your model was still predicting the main cycle.

**NIMERA:** Which it was.

No accusation, just fact.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** If it isn't coming back here—

**TORREN:** It's going somewhere.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

## B003 — Outside the boundary

Vaelira traces her model: entry, regulation, return.

Cyanis points beyond the edge.

**CYANIS:** What's here?

**VAELIRA:** Outside the model.

**CYANIS:** Not outside the building.

Vaelira looks at him.

**VAELIRA:** No.

**NIMERA:** Oh, fuck.

**ILYRA:** What?

Nimera points to the edge of the paper.

**NIMERA:** The equation stops.

Then the Annex around them.

**NIMERA:** Reality doesn't.

Torren asks what is beyond the return. Old galleries, spill channels, earlier test rooms.

**VAELIRA:** Staff?

Hesitation.

**ILYRA:** How many?

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** Usually four or five.

**ILYRA:** Usually?

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** There's only one down there now.

**VAELIRA:** Who?

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** Senior exposure study.

Beat.

**ANNEX RESEARCHER:** They stopped answering the relay.

Vaelira closes her notebook.

**VAELIRA:** Open the lower access.
