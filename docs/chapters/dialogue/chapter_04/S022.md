## S022_B001 — Ivorybridge

**2.5D staging:** Ivorybridge should immediately read differently from Caelora and Cresthaven. It is a working research settlement built around old infrastructure rather than a grand capital or military outpost: elevated stone walks, narrow water channels, workshops, instrument frames, small academic buildings, and pieces of much older construction incorporated into ordinary modern use.

No ominous music cue. No one is panicking.

The party enters from the western approach.

**TORREN:** Name's accurate.

**NIMERA:** That's your review?

**TORREN:** Bridge. Ivory-colored stone.

**NIMERA:** Riveting.

**CYANIS:** Give him time. He hasn't evaluated the road yet.

Torren looks down.

**TORREN:** Road's shit.

**ILYRA:** There it is.

Nimera has the three-map packet under one arm.

**NIMERA:** We need whoever handles old technical records.

**TORREN:** You say that like every town keeps one behind the bar.

**NIMERA:** Good towns do.

**CYANIS:** What do bad towns keep behind the bar?

**TORREN:** Better liquor.

Ilyra keeps walking.

**ILYRA:** Both of you, please survive until lunch.

## S022_B002 — Asking the wrong person

They stop near a municipal work station where two technicians are checking flow marks on a public channel.

Nimera approaches one.

**NIMERA:** Sixfold Annex?

The technician looks up.

**TECHNICIAN:** What about it?

**NIMERA:** Who handles historical routing records?

**TECHNICIAN:** Annex staff.

**NIMERA:** Yes. Which one?

**TECHNICIAN:** Depends what you broke.

**CYANIS:** Nothing yet.

The technician gives him a look.

**CYANIS:** Important word, yet.

**ILYRA:** Ignore him.

Nimera opens the packet just enough to reveal part of the Wayfinder map.

The technician's expression changes.

Not fear.

Recognition that this is above their pay grade.

**TECHNICIAN:** You want Serren.

**NIMERA:** First name?

**TECHNICIAN:** Vaelira.

Torren glances at Nimera.

**TORREN:** That easy.

**NIMERA:** I hate when you're right accidentally.

**TORREN:** Wasn't accidental.

**NIMERA:** I hate that more.

## S022_B003 — First sight of Vaelira

They find Vaelira in a small open-sided regulation workspace overlooking one of Ivorybridge's controlled channels.

She is not posing over ancient artifacts.

She is working.

Several calibrated glass vessels sit in a rack. A modern regulation frame feeds controlled elemental charge through three test chambers.

A junior researcher is writing down readings.

Vaelira watches the third chamber.

**VAELIRA:** Again.

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** Same load?

**VAELIRA:** Same load. Increase discharge delay by half a count.

The researcher adjusts it.

The test runs.

First chamber: stable.

Second: stable.

Third: a brief surge, then settles exactly where Vaelira expected.

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** Point-eight.

**VAELIRA:** Point-seven-nine if your instrument isn't lying to you.

The junior checks again.

**JUNIOR RESEARCHER:** Point-seven-nine.

Vaelira finally notices the group waiting.

**VAELIRA:** If you're here to complain about the south channel, it's not mine.

**CYANIS:** We weren't.

**VAELIRA:** Excellent.

She looks at Torren.

Then Ilyra.

Then Cyanis.

Then stops on Nimera, who is very obviously carrying old maps.

**VAELIRA:** You're not here about the south channel.

**NIMERA:** Very good.

**VAELIRA:** Thank you.

Beat.

**NIMERA:** I wasn't complimenting you.

**VAELIRA:** Then that was rude.

Cyanis smiles.

## S022_B004 — The maps

They move to a worktable.

Nimera lays down the first map.

Then the **Wayfinder map**.

Then Torren's **post-Warden copy**.

Vaelira studies all three without touching them at first.

**VAELIRA:** Where did you get these?

**CYANIS:** Different places.

**VAELIRA:** I gathered that.

**NIMERA:** This one from a Wayfinder site.

She indicates the second.

Then Torren's.

**NIMERA:** This one was copied from a routing display in an Ancient command room.

Vaelira looks at the rough sketch.

**VAELIRA:** Copied by whom?

Torren raises a hand slightly.

Vaelira looks from him to the drawing.

**VAELIRA:** You kept scale surprisingly well.

**TORREN:** Surprisingly?

**VAELIRA:** I don't know you.

**TORREN:** Fair.

Nimera taps the map.

**NIMERA:** He reads terrain. I read the constructed geometry.

Vaelira looks between them.

**VAELIRA:** And together you got here.

**TORREN:** Eventually.

**NIMERA:** Quickly.

**TORREN:** Eventually.

**NIMERA:** It was one morning.

**TORREN:** Long morning.

## S022_B005 — Vaelira sees the relationship

Vaelira pulls a blank sheet toward herself and begins making small alignment marks.

Not copying the maps.

Testing relationships.

She checks one repeated figure against another.

Then a second.

Then stops.

**VAELIRA:** These aren't fragments of one map.

Nimera's attention sharpens.

**NIMERA:** No.

**VAELIRA:** They're independent maps using the same positional grammar.

Torren points toward his copy.

**TORREN:** That's what we got.

**VAELIRA:** You got that they connect.

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**VAELIRA:** I'm saying why.

Torren considers that.

**TORREN:** Better answer.

Nimera looks at him.

**NIMERA:** You can admit that?

**TORREN:** Didn't hurt.

Vaelira traces one repeated relationship with the tip of a stylus.

**VAELIRA:** This isn't just directional.

**NIMERA:** Regulation?

Vaelira looks at her.

**VAELIRA:** Possibly.

**NIMERA:** That's a cowardly word.

**VAELIRA:** It's an accurate word.

Nimera smiles.

**NIMERA:** Better.

## S022_B006 — What Ivorybridge has

Vaelira opens a cabinet and removes a modern transcription sheet.

It contains a partial geometric pattern taken from the Sixfold Annex.

She places it beside the three maps.

There is an obvious family resemblance.

Not an exact match.

**ILYRA:** That's why we were led here.

**VAELIRA:** Apparently.

**CYANIS:** You know what it does?

**VAELIRA:** Some of it.

**NIMERA:** Define some.

**VAELIRA:** The Annex contains a regulation system. It responds predictably to controlled elemental input.

**TORREN:** Ancient?

**VAELIRA:** Older than Ivorybridge.

**TORREN:** That's not what I asked.

Vaelira looks at him.

**VAELIRA:** Ancient Diysean, yes.

Torren nods once.

**VAELIRA:** We don't understand its original purpose.

**NIMERA:** Good.

Vaelira raises an eyebrow.

**NIMERA:** Means you're less likely to lie to me by accident.

**VAELIRA:** I can lie deliberately if you'd prefer.

**CYANIS:** You'll fit in beautifully.

## S022_B007 — The brilliant answer

Vaelira brings them to a contained demonstration rig connected to a small Annex-derived regulator component.

**VAELIRA:** This part we understand.

Nimera immediately looks skeptical.

**VAELIRA:** Enough to demonstrate.

That satisfies her slightly more.

Vaelira points to three receiving cells.

**VAELIRA:** Equal input into all three produces unequal discharge.

**ILYRA:** Why?

**VAELIRA:** Because the system isn't balancing energy.

She adjusts one control.

**VAELIRA:** It's balancing consequence.

A beat.

Cyanis watches her.

**CYANIS:** That's a large word.

**VAELIRA:** It's the correct one.

She indicates the first cell.

**VAELIRA:** Increase load here.

The junior researcher does.

The second and third cells change automatically.

One absorbs more.

The other reduces output.

The whole system stabilizes.

**TORREN:** It moved the burden.

**VAELIRA:** Exactly.

Nimera leans closer.

**NIMERA:** Without changing total input.

**VAELIRA:** Exactly.

Nimera looks at her.

Genuinely impressed now.

**NIMERA:** That's fucking clever.

**VAELIRA:** Thank you.

This time Nimera did mean it.

## S022_B008 — Vaelira predicts it

Vaelira resets the system.

**VAELIRA:** Watch the third cell.

She increases the first load again, but changes the timing.

**VAELIRA:** It will rise, hold for two counts, then drop below baseline.

The test begins.

Rise.

Hold.

One.

Two.

Drop.

Exactly.

Torren's eyes narrow slightly.

**TORREN:** Again.

Vaelira looks at him.

**TORREN:** Same thing.

She repeats it.

Same result.

**ILYRA:** How many times have you tested this?

**VAELIRA:** Enough.

**NIMERA:** That's not a number.

**VAELIRA:** One hundred eighty-seven controlled repetitions under this configuration.

Nimera pauses.

**NIMERA:** Fine.

**CYANIS:** She brought a number.

**NIMERA:** I respect a number.

## S022_B009 — Cyanis's question

Vaelira records the result.

Cyanis is still looking at the apparatus.

**CYANIS:** Who checks your model?

Vaelira looks up.

No offense taken.

**VAELIRA:** I do.

Cyanis waits.

**VAELIRA:** The Annex staff does.

He still waits.

Vaelira studies him now.

**VAELIRA:** You have another question.

**CYANIS:** Who checks theirs?

A small silence.

The junior researcher stops writing.

Vaelira does not become defensive.

She thinks.

**VAELIRA:** Each other.

**CYANIS:** Same assumptions?

**VAELIRA:** Mostly.

**CYANIS:** Same instruments?

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**CYANIS:** Same definition of success?

That one lands.

Vaelira's expression changes slightly.

Not because she has been defeated.

Because it is a legitimate question she has not been asking in that form.

**VAELIRA:** Usually.

**CYANIS:** Then they're checking the answer.

Beat.

**CYANIS:** I'm asking who checks the question.

Nimera looks from Cyanis to Vaelira.

Torren quietly does the same.

Vaelira looks back at the regulator.

**VAELIRA:** That's irritating.

**CYANIS:** I've been told.

Torren immediately:

**TORREN:** Frequently.

## S022_B010 — The anomaly

Vaelira returns to the three maps.

Now she compares the pattern from the Annex against them differently.

Not as location.

As behavior.

Her pace changes.

**VAELIRA:** Wait.

Nimera comes beside her.

**NIMERA:** What?

Vaelira points to a repeated break in the geometry.

**VAELIRA:** I treated this as a boundary marker.

**NIMERA:** Why?

**VAELIRA:** Because every local example behaves like one.

**TORREN:** Local.

Vaelira looks at him.

**TORREN:** Your word.

She looks back at the maps.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

Nimera follows the sequence across all three.

**NIMERA:** If it isn't a boundary—

**VAELIRA:** Then it's a transfer point.

Silence.

**ILYRA:** Transfer of what?

Vaelira doesn't answer immediately.

**VAELIRA:** I don't know.

That matters.

She does not bluff.

## S022_B011 — Sixfold Annex

Vaelira gathers her notes.

**VAELIRA:** I need to see the primary regulator.

**CYANIS:** At the Annex.

**VAELIRA:** Yes.

**TORREN:** How far?

**VAELIRA:** Less than an hour if nobody insists on taking the scenic route.

Torren looks offended.

**TORREN:** Scenic routes are usually better roads.

**NIMERA:** There he is.

Vaelira looks at the four of them.

**VAELIRA:** Are you coming?

Nimera picks up the maps.

**NIMERA:** Obviously.

**CYANIS:** We traveled all the way here to bother you.

**VAELIRA:** Then I'd hate for the effort to be wasted.

Ilyra starts toward the door.

**ILYRA:** Food first?

Everyone stops.

Torren immediately:

**TORREN:** Yes.

Nimera looks at him.

**NIMERA:** That was fast.

**TORREN:** Important question.

## S022_B012 — After lunch

**2.5D staging:** Short time cut. We do **not** turn lunch into another full mandatory scene.

Exterior Ivorybridge. The group meets Vaelira near the route out of town.

Vaelira has a field bag over one shoulder, her staff in hand, and several notes tucked under one arm.

Torren looks at the bag.

Then at Vaelira.

Then at the bag again.

**VAELIRA:** What?

**TORREN:** Nothing.

Vaelira gives him a flat look.

**VAELIRA:** Bullshit.

Nimera’s head snaps toward her.

Tiny beat.

Then a grin.

**NIMERA:** Oh, good.

Vaelira looks at her.

**VAELIRA:** What?

**NIMERA:** Nothing.

Same flat look back.

**VAELIRA:** Bullshit.

Cyanis immediately turns away, trying not to laugh.

Ilyra closes her eyes for half a second.

**ILYRA:** We’ve known her an hour.

**TORREN:** Long hour.

Vaelira points at Torren with two fingers.

**VAELIRA:** You. What was wrong with the bag?

**TORREN:** Nothing wrong with it.

**VAELIRA:** Then why did you look at it like it insulted your mother?

**TORREN:** Thought you’d bring more.

**VAELIRA:** I’m going to a research annex, not invading a country.

Torren shrugs.

**TORREN:** Never know how the day’s going.

Vaelira stares at him.

**VAELIRA:** Is that how you pack?

**TORREN:** Yeah.

**VAELIRA:** That explains a lot.

**TORREN:** You don't know enough about me for that sentence.

**NIMERA:** I do.

**TORREN:** Nobody asked you.

They start walking.

## S022_B013 — Leaving Ivorybridge

The safe settlement state ends at the edge of town.

The road toward the Annex follows pale retaining walls and narrow controlled waterways. Old stonework appears underneath newer construction in places.

Vaelira walks ahead for a few steps, checking a small field instrument.

Torren notices.

**TORREN:** You checking the road?

**VAELIRA:** No.

**TORREN:** Shame. Needs help.

Vaelira glances down.

**VAELIRA:** You really do this.

**NIMERA:** Constantly.

**TORREN:** Road matters.

**VAELIRA:** I didn’t say it didn’t.

**TORREN:** Good.

A beat.

**VAELIRA:** It’s still ugly.

Torren looks at her.

Small surprised pause.

Then:

**TORREN:** Yeah.

Nimera looks between them with exaggerated disgust.

**NIMERA:** Jesus Christ, now there’s two of you.

Cyanis leans slightly toward Ilyra.

**CYANIS:** Should we separate them?

**ILYRA:** I want to see what happens.

Torren hears her.

**TORREN:** Rude.

**ILYRA:** Completely.

## S022_B014 — Six counts

They reach the first old regulation marker outside Ivorybridge.

Vaelira abruptly stops.

Portrait shift: focused.

One hand comes up.

Everybody stops with her.

No argument. They’ve been traveling together long enough to recognize **that** tone even if Vaelira is new.

**VAELIRA:** Six.

Nimera looks at her.

**NIMERA:** Six what?

**VAELIRA:** Five.

Nimera’s portrait goes instantly irritated.

**NIMERA:** Oh, fuck you.

**VAELIRA:** Four.

Torren is already scanning the road.

Cyanis takes one step back toward Ilyra without looking away from the marker.

**VAELIRA:** Three.

A faint glow begins beneath the old stonework ahead.

**ILYRA:** Ah.

**VAELIRA:** Two.

Torren sees it now.

**TORREN:** Back.

Everyone is already back.

**VAELIRA:** One.

A sharp band of elemental light races across the road between two buried markers.

**WHUMM.**

Quick white-frame flash.

Small camera kick.

Nimera’s hair and coat settle from the displaced air.

She stares at Vaelira.

Vaelira lowers her hand.

**VAELIRA:** Done.

Long beat.

**NIMERA:** You could’ve said what was going to happen.

**VAELIRA:** You stopped.

**NIMERA:** Because you started counting like a fucking executioner.

Cyanis bites the inside of his cheek.

Torren looks away.

Ilyra is visibly trying not to smile.

Vaelira considers this.

**VAELIRA:** Fair.

Nimera wasn’t expecting that.

Her angry portrait drops for half a beat.

**NIMERA:** ...All right.

Vaelira starts walking again.

**VAELIRA:** It cycles every eleven minutes.

Torren falls in beside her.

**TORREN:** That one exact?

**VAELIRA:** Ten minutes, fifty-six seconds on average.

Torren nods.

**TORREN:** Exact enough.

Nimera throws both hands slightly outward behind them.

**NIMERA:** Oh, now *he* likes the numbers.

**TORREN:** Useful number.

**NIMERA:** Fuck you, Harth.

Torren keeps walking.

**TORREN:** There she is.
