## B001 — Three maps

**CRESTHAVEN — ARCHIVE / WORKROOM — MORNING**

Cresthaven is noisy with workers. Inside the temporary Archive, Cyanis, Ilyra, Torren, and Nimera have taken over a large table.

On the table:
- Hollow Watch rubbing;
- surviving Wayfinder copies;
- Torren's post-Warden routing sketch;
- current Crown map of the Crownhold.

Torren moves his sketch slightly left.

Nimera moves it back.

Torren moves it left again.

**NIMERA:** Why?

**TORREN:** River.

**NIMERA:** That's not an answer.

Torren points at the Wayfinder copy.

**TORREN:** River used to be here.

Then the modern map.

**TORREN:** Now it's here.

**NIMERA:** I know rivers move.

**TORREN:** Then quit putting it back.

Nimera's portrait goes flat.

**NIMERA:** I was aligning the notation.

**TORREN:** And I'm aligning the ground.

Cyanis watches.

**CYANIS:** I think this is going well.

**ILYRA:** Don't encourage them.

**TORREN:** Nobody's arguing.

Nimera slowly turns toward him.

**NIMERA:** You moved my fucking map three times.

**TORREN:** Because it was wrong three times.

Nimera opens her mouth, stops, looks down at the modern river, then back at Torren.

**NIMERA:** ...All right.

Torren gives the smallest nod imaginable.

**NIMERA:** Don't enjoy that.

**TORREN:** Wasn't going to.

He absolutely was.
