# Audit112 — Chapter 10: The Last Blank — controlling

## Core role / runtime

- Formal title: **Chapter 10 — The Last Blank**.
- Placement: after Chapter 9 Larkspire / Crownfall / Rhazek and before Chapter 11 Crown Engine.
- Target first-clear runtime: approximately **55–65 minutes**, i.e. about one hour.
- This is a tight mandatory investigation / expedition chapter, not another large war campaign.
- Item drops, treasure, Standard-Card allocation, EXP/CEXP, equipment rewards, and economy tuning remain deferred to the separate item/progression pass.

## Mirena's long-running records investigation

- Since Chapter 3, **Crown Princess Mirena has continued reviewing Crown records for other strange orders / provenance irregularities**.
- Most records have mundane explanations.
- One older eastern research authorization remains unresolved.
- The accessible Crown chain contains:
  1. authentic authorization to research an area east of / beyond **Cerythvale**;
  2. later authentic notation that the research was **completed**.
- The meaningful middle is absent: no field findings, excavation summary, recovered-material inventory, or useful explanation of what was discovered.
- Mirena does **not** know Eastern Wayfinder exists, does not know Calder/Prime research was involved, and does not know the site contains the final eastern map evidence.

## Chapter-10 route / discovery

Narrative movement:

**Caelora / Mirena lead → Cerythvale → eastern forest / old route → old Crown research area → discovery of Eastern Wayfinder**

Important correction:
- The physical endpoint is Eastern Wayfinder, but **the party does not know it is there when it leaves Cerythvale**.
- Older shorthand `Cerythvale → Eastern Wayfinder` remains geographically true but must not be interpreted as prior character knowledge.
- The final master design map may display Eastern Wayfinder for production authority, but discovery-gated in-game presentation must not treat it as a known party destination before Chapter 10.

## Eastern Wayfinder / final map completion

- The party discovers a second Wayfinder unexpectedly while following Mirena's old-order lead.
- Torren recognizes its relationship to Wayfinder Junction.
- Eastern Wayfinder preserves the **final missing eastern survey / cartographic evidence**.
- The physical map mystery closes **before** the deeper excavation and before the mandatory boss.
- No later chapter should add another missing physical-geography piece.

Current cartographic progression:
1. Ch1 Hollow Watch rubbing / route evidence.
2. Ch1 Wayfinder Junction material / copies.
3. Ch3 Torren post-Warden routing sketch.
4. Ch4 Vaelira / Reaction Annex related transcription/chart.
5. Ch5 Deepforge works survey after Deepforge Colossus / sovereign-maintenance sequence.
6. Ch8 **Westguard** related survey/map; older `Westreach` references mean Westguard.
7. Ch10 Eastern Wayfinder final eastern survey.

Map completion establishes **where the physical relationships fit together**, not the true purpose of the larger Ancient system.

Knowledge firewall remains intact around:
- Underground Crest Network;
- full Crown Engine meaning;
- Custodian truth;
- Entity implications;
- final Ancient catastrophe interpretation;
- full Prime meaning.

## Old Crown excavation pivot

After the map is completed, the party returns to Mirena's original question and discovers clear evidence that the historical Crown researchers conducted a **substantial excavation beneath / beyond Eastern Wayfinder**.

The site contains modern-era Crown cutting/clearing, supports, hauling/staging traces, field marks, and abandoned local research remnants.

This scale of work is absent from the accessible central Crown record.

The party does **not** find a complete final expedition report underground. The missing-middle problem is real.

Evidence is reconstructed from local remnants such as working notes, tags, partial manifests, copied inscriptions, rough indexes, duplicate routing slips, and locally posted instructions.

Do not use a convenient Calder confession / villain diary / complete surviving final report.

The exact reason the accessible central archive lacks the middle documentation remains open. Do not silently decide whether Calder personally suppressed it, redirected it, later archival handling fragmented it, or another cause applies.

The exact trigger that made the old expedition begin excavating below Eastern Wayfinder is also intentionally **not required**.

## Calder's Prime research

- Calder's historical work proves he was investigating **Primes, Ancient sites, and related Crown/Diysean records for years before the present story**.
- **No Prime had ever been successfully activated before the first modern Prime activation in the game's present era.**
- Therefore Calder's pre-activation research is documentary / archaeological, not based on observed historical Prime users or successful prior activations.
- His older questions concern what Primes were, why records distinguished them from ordinary Cards, why Prime references recur around certain Ancient sites/administrative material, and whether relevant surviving Cards exist.
- After the first modern activation, his archival/research behavior may become more urgent and targeted, but his complete philosophical interpretation belongs to Chapter 11.

## Chapter 0 payoff

- Before Chapter 0, Calder's Prime research program already had a **standing recovery/reporting directive**.
- A recovered Diysean Card matching the research criteria was to be reported and transported to **Caelora** for examination.
- The recovered Card aboard the Chapter-0 convoy triggered that authority.
- Therefore **Calder is the reason the recovered Card was ordered to Caelora and the reason the convoy Cyanis was assigned to protect existed**.

Limits:
- Calder did not choose Cyanis personally.
- Calder did not arrange Cyanis's assignment specifically.
- Calder did not cause the Chapter-0 attack/ambush.
- Calder did not know the Card would successfully activate a Prime.
- Calder did not orchestrate every downstream event.

The Chapter-0 transfer itself was **lawful**.

## Chapter 3 payoff

Chapter 3 remains unchanged in what the party knew at the time:
- the composite seizure order used genuine but unrelated authorities;
- timing and record-family contradictions proved those records should not naturally meet;
- Chapter 3 proved **how** the false order was assembled while leaving **who and why** unresolved.

Chapter 10 closes the provenance chain:
- Nimera finds the two genuine source authorities in Calder's older research material, still separate with their proper dates/purposes;
- Calder's project deliberately brought both authorities into the same body of work;
- surviving return/routing evidence links them into the restricted/suppressed archival path later encountered in Chapter 3;
- Mirena verifies the final capital-side authorization/provenance chain when the party returns.

By the end of Chapter 10 it is definitive:

**Othmar Calder was behind the Chapter-3 composite seizure order.**

Preserve the distinction:
- Prime research = not inherently criminal.
- Chapter-0 transfer = lawful.
- Eastern Wayfinder excavation = secretive / incompletely preserved in the accessible record, but not automatically the central crime merely because it occurred.
- Chapter-3 composite seizure = deliberate abuse of genuine authority: **real pieces, wrong assembly**.

## End of Calder's excavation

- The old Crown excavation reaches a protected Ancient threshold below Eastern Wayfinder and stops.
- A locally surviving operational/shutdown instruction establishes that lower access became unsafe, personnel were withdrawn, recovered material was removed/secured, further access was prohibited, and **Calder approved suspension/termination of the lower attempt**.
- At this earlier point Calder did not order ordinary research crews to keep dying against the sealed area.
- The later central `research completed` notation is technically compatible with the operation being closed without the accessible archive preserving its true scale.
- The party already has enough evidence to return to Mirena but consciously chooses to go farther than Calder's team did.

Function:

- guards the registry threshold, not the final map.

Encounter architecture:
- one continuous HP bar;
- no adds;
- no transformation / second form;
- no boss-only meter, puzzle, timeline system, or archive minigame;
- normal supported combat language: heavy direct attacks, defensive/barrier behavior, ordinary resistance/configuration changes, focused enforcement pressure, broad denial pressure, increased low-HP cadence.

Exact move names, stats, weaknesses, drops, EXP/CEXP, and reward package remain open for later combat/item/progression work.

No additional mandatory miniboss is currently required.

## Buried Registry

Beyond Registry Warden is a compact untouched Ancient administrative space: the **Buried Registry**.

Calder's expedition never reached it.

Function:
- regional custody / authority / responsibility registry physically paired with the Wayfinder geography above;
- records locations together with custody, jurisdiction, responsibility, protected holdings, and transfers/amendments;
- not a digital control room or database;
- not the full Underground Crest Network reveal;
- not Crown Engine itself.

Character ownership:
- Torren recognizes geographic correspondences.
- Nimera recognizes custody/authority/transfer organization.
- Vaelira recognizes the shared relational/spatial structure.

Core conclusion:

**Eastern Wayfinder's cartographic geography and the Buried Registry's administrative/custody records were deliberately related parts of a larger Ancient arrangement.**

Prime references:
- some Registry records use the Ancient Prime classification;
- this proves only that Prime-class Cards were distinct enough to receive formal administrative/custody treatment;
- it does not provide prior successful activations, Prime-wielder histories, complete powers, or an explanation of why modern activation is possible.

The Registry may show responsibility/custody being transferred between offices or bearers as a restrained seed, but Chapter 10 does not deliver the full philosophical answer to Calder's later Living Anchor argument.

Both the completed map and Registry administration disproportionately converge toward **Caelora in Yahtrenhold**.

The party may conclude that the area occupied by modern Caelora was unusually important to the Ancient arrangement, but not yet why.

Post-boss Registry exploration is short, quiet, and encounter-free.
