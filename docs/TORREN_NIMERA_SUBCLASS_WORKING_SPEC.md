# Diyse — Torren ← Nimera Subclass Working Spec

**Status:** APPROVED CLASS-REWORK IDENTITY / PRESERVE-FIRST PACKAGE — CL13 EXPANSION STILL INCOMPLETE  
**Parent trackers:** `docs/CLASS_REWORK_MASTER_TRACKER.md`, `docs/CLASS_CEXP_WORKING_MODEL.md`, `docs/CLASS_MASTERY_WORKING_MODEL.md`  
**Authority boundary:** v1.84 / Audit99 plus newer explicit class-rework decisions. On August 21, 2026 the user clarified that the class-rework proposals advanced under their repeated “Let’s continue” responses are approved. This file records the approved Torren ← Nimera material that has actually been designed. It does not invent the still-missing CL11 expansion or final rebalance.

## 1. Approved identity

- **Subclass name: Routeweaver.**
- **Character/class pairing:** **Torren — War Archer / Routeweaver**.
- Routeweaver is preserve-first rather than a wholesale redesign.
- Torren learns Nimera's actual **Cardweaver / Conduit** tradition, not a fake Torren-only imitation.
- Routeweaver remains a **hybrid physical/magical field-control and selective Card-support** subclass, not a copy of Nimera's Base support kit.

## 2. Approved donor-equipment progression

| Subclass CL | Equipment result |
|---:|---|
| **CL1** | Gain legal use of Nimera's basic / one-slot **Conduits**. |
| **CL3** | Gain legal use of **Cardweaver armor**. |
| **CL5** | Gain legal use of **two-handed Conduits** plus **Focus** where the equipped Conduit leaves Secondary free. |
| **CL10** | Equipment Mastery becomes eligible; buying Subclass Mastery Node 4 grants access to the Cardweaver donor **Relic**. |
| Synthesis | Later paired Legacy access under the final Synthesis rule. |

Torren's native Great Bow permissions remain persistent under the open-equipment rule; cross-training expands his legal loadouts rather than replacing War Archer equipment.

## 3. Approved preserve-first ability spine

The following existing Routeweaver identities are retained as the subclass's authored spine:
- **Throughline**
- **Clear Route**
- **Crossroads**
- **Covered Crossing**
- Trait: **Field Weaving**
- Ultimate: **Open the Way**

These names/mechanical identities are approved preserve-first material. Exact CL13 rebasing and final numerical balance are still to be recovered/reconciled before promotion.

## 4. Approved design boundaries

- Routeweaver must use Nimera's actual Conduit concept and legal donor equipment.
- Torren may retain selective **Standard Card** interaction, but Routeweaver must not become a second Cardweaver.
- The subclass should preserve Torren's practical battlefield judgment and route-control identity while expressing Nimera-derived field manipulation.
- No new personal gauge/resource is introduced.
- Existing open-equipment and persistent-unlocked-ability rules remain in force.
- Ability formulas and hit patterns are authored per ability; they do not automatically inherit the equipped weapon's ordinary Attack pattern.

## 5. Still-open work

The user's blanket approval applies to the Routeweaver material that was actually proposed under the “Let’s continue” sequence. The following items were not yet fully designed and therefore remain open rather than silently invented:
- the required **fifth normal ability at CL11**;
- exact CL1/4/7/9 numerical rebalance under the CL13 subclass curve;
- exact three-rank **Field Weaving** CL1/6/12 wording after recovery of the prior authored mechanic;
- final **Open the Way** CL13 numerical package;
- Routeweaver selected-subclass stat package if not already fixed by older controlling authority;
- exact Subclass Mastery effects other than the global **Equipment Mastery at CL10**;
- Torren/Nimera Synthesis interaction.

Nothing in this open-work list reopens the approved **Routeweaver** name, donor relationship, equipment architecture, or preserve-first identity unless the user explicitly revises them.
