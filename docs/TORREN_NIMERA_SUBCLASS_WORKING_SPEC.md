This file records the approved Torren ← Nimera package under the final Routeweaver identity.

## 1. Approved identity

- **Subclass name: Routeweaver.**
- **Character/class pairing:** **Torren — War Archer / Routeweaver**.
- Torren learns Nimera's actual **Cardweaver / Conduit** tradition and translates it into practical battlefield routing, hybrid pressure, Field control, and selective Standard Card support.
- Routeweaver is not a second Cardweaver and introduces no new personal gauge/resource.

## 2. Approved donor-equipment progression

| Subclass CL | Equipment result |
|---:|---|
| **CL1** | Gain legal use of Nimera's basic / one-slot **Conduits**. |
| **CL3** | Gain legal use of **Cardweaver armor**. |
| **CL5** | Gain legal use of **two-handed Conduits** plus **Focus** where the equipped Conduit leaves Secondary free. |
| **CL10** | Equipment Mastery becomes eligible; buying Subclass Mastery Node 4 grants access to the Cardweaver donor **Relic**. |
| Synthesis | Later paired Legacy access under the final Synthesis rule. |

Torren's native Great Bow permissions remain persistent under the open-equipment rule.

## 3. Approved selected-Subclass stat package

- MP +8%
- Attack +6%
- Magic +6%
- Accuracy +6%
- Defense -6%

Intent: Routeweaver preserves Torren's martial reliability while giving him enough Magic/MP to use Nimera-derived Conduit and Field techniques without becoming a full caster.

## 4. Approved weapon/loadout rule

Routeweaver abilities are **weapon-independent once learned** unless a specific ability says otherwise.

- Damaging Routeweaver abilities use their own authored hit pattern and formula.
- They remain legal with either Torren's native **Great Bow** or a learned **Conduit** loadout.
- No hidden Great-Bow damage bonus or Conduit spell bonus is added inside the abilities; equipment stats create the build difference naturally.
- Great Bow naturally favors Attack/Accuracy; Conduit + Focus naturally favors Magic and mixed utility.

All damaging Routeweaver abilities below use one authored hit and **50% Attack / 50% Magic Hybrid scaling**.

## 5. Approved Trait — Field Weaving

### Rank I — CL1
After Torren uses a **Standard Card**, his next damaging Routeweaver ability before the end of the following round gains **+10% Power**.

### Rank II — CL6
After Torren uses any Routeweaver ability, his next Standard Card before the end of the following round gains **+10 Base Hit / application reliability** where relevant.

### Rank III — CL12
Torren-authored Routeweaver Fields last **+1 round**, up to their authored maximum.

These temporary sequencing states are not a player-facing gauge and do not create extra actions.

## 6. Five normal abilities

### CL1 — Throughline
**7 MP; one enemy; 160 Hybrid Power; one hit; 50% Attack / 50% Magic.**

- Gains **+10 Base Hit** against a target with **Hunter's Measure**.
- Does not apply or consume Hunter's Measure.
- May receive Field Weaving Rank-I Power when properly sequenced after a Standard Card.

Purpose: the simplest Routeweaver attack — Torren identifies the clean line through a target and follows it, whether the strike is expressed through a Great Bow or a Conduit.

### CL4 — Clear Route
**10 MP; one conscious ally.**

- Removes **1 ordinary harmful status**.
- Grants **+10 Speed for 2 rounds**.
- Does not heal and does not create a barrier.

Purpose: Torren applies Cardweaver-derived battlefield correction in a practical way: remove what is obstructing an ally and get them moving again.

### CL7 — Crossroads
**16 MP; battlefield; 3-round Routeweaver Field.**

Choose one mode when used:

**Forward Route**
- all conscious allies gain **+10 Base Hit**;
- all conscious allies gain **+10 Speed**.

**Covered Route**
- all conscious allies gain **+15 Total Defense**.

Only one Torren-authored **Route Field** may be active at a time. Casting another Route Field replaces his previous Route Field. Crossroads has an authored maximum duration of **5 rounds** after extensions.

Purpose: Torren does not reproduce Nimera's information engine; he turns her field-manipulation lesson into a veteran's choice of the safest or fastest line through a fight.

### CL9 — Covered Crossing
**15 MP; all conscious allies.**

- Allies gain **15% direct-damage reduction through the end of the following round**.
- If one of Torren's Route Fields is active, extend that Field by **1 round**, up to its authored maximum of 5 rounds.
- Does not intercept, redirect enemy actions, or arm a Prepared effect.

Purpose: this is Torren covering the party while they move through the line he has already established.

### CL11 — Forced Passage
**18 MP; one enemy; 260 Hybrid Power; one hit; 50% Attack / 50% Magic.**

- **30% penetration** against the applicable Defense and Spirit contributions before the Hybrid result is combined.
- Gains **+15 Base Hit** against Hunter's Measure.
- After damage, removes **1 removable hostile Field** if one is present.
- Does not apply or consume Hunter's Measure.

Purpose: the advanced Routeweaver attack. When no clean route exists, Torren makes one through the target and the battlefield structure around it.

## 7. Approved Ultimate — Open the Way

**CL13; battlefield; 3-round Route Field.** Ultimate cost follows the final global Ultimate convention.

While active:
- all conscious allies gain **+15 Speed**;
- all conscious allies gain **+10 Base Hit**;
- all conscious allies take **10% less direct damage**;
- Standard Cards used by Torren gain **+10 Base Hit / application reliability** where relevant.

Open the Way deals no direct damage. It replaces any existing Torren Route Field and has an authored maximum duration of **5 rounds** after legal extensions. Field Weaving Rank III increases its initial duration from 3 to 4 rounds when Routeweaver is selected.

Purpose: Torren's subclass conclusion is not a larger spell. He uses Nimera's fieldcraft to create the route everyone else can exploit.

## 8. Approved Subclass Mastery

| Node | Eligibility | Approved effect |

| **Subclass 2 — Cleared Ground** | CL5 | Clear Route additionally grants **+10 Total Defense through the end of the following round**. |
| **Subclass 3 — Secured Passage** | CL7 | Crossroads Forward Route improves **+10 → +15 Base Hit**; Covered Route improves **+15 → +20 Total Defense**; Covered Crossing direct-damage reduction improves **15% → 20%**. |

Each costs 1 MP under the inherited nine-point Mastery economy.

## 9. Approved pair interaction with Nimera / Truthshot

Hunter's Measure remains shared between Torren and Nimera.

- Throughline and Forced Passage can exploit Measure applied by either character.
- Routeweaver does not create a duplicate mark.
- Diysean Appraisal remains Nimera's information-reveal action and does not become an `Appraised` status.
- Indexed remains separate and Card-specific.

The pair therefore has two reciprocal languages without collapsing them together: **Measure** for quarry precision, and **Fields/Cards** for route control.

## 10. Approved role boundary

- Routeweaver is a hybrid controller/support attacker, not a healer and not a second Cardweaver.
- Standard Card support remains selective and reliability-focused rather than granting extra Card slots, extra Card actions, or a separate draw/deck system.
- Crossroads/Open the Way use the existing Field concept rather than a new battlefield-resource system.
- All abilities obey the existing one-action-per-round and Prepared-effect rules.
