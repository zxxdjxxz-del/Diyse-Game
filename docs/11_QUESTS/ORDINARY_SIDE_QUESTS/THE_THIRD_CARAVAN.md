## Route
> Greenhollow → Recovery Depot → Old Supply Cut → Failed Handoff → Temporary Shelter → Settlement Approach → Ashford

These are local recovery-route spaces, not permanent master-map pins.

## Premise
Talia is coordinating a recovery line between Greenhollow and Ashford.

Two caravans arrived.
The third is overdue.

The real question is not just:
> where is the caravan?

It is:
> **where did the recovery system fail?**

## Objective flow
### 1. Recovery Depot
Verify departure records.

The third caravan left later because a wagon required wheel repair.
The revised departure time exists on the depot copy.

### 2. Old Supply Cut
Trace:
- wheel ruts;
- route marker;
- repaired wagon's distinctive track.

Result:
> all wagons reached the cut.

### 3. Failed Handoff
Evidence shows:
- receiving crew had the **original** departure time;
- they waited through the expected window;
- followed standing protocol when the caravan did not arrive;
- the delayed caravan later found no receiving crew.

Failure:
> **the revised departure time did not propagate to the handoff copy.**

No sabotage.
No cowardice.
No villain.

### 4. Fallback Procedure
Follow the posted fallback route rather than search randomly.

### 5. Temporary Shelter
Find the third caravan:
> **everyone alive**

Problems:
- repaired wheel now unsafe for final leg;
- supplies redistributed;
- civilians unwilling to risk moving until route is confirmed.

### 6. Stabilize Final Leg
Three compact interactions:
- rebalance load;
- leave/mark damaged wagon for proper later repair;
- confirm settlement approach.

No crafting minigame or escort-health system.

### 7. Settlement Approach / Ashford
Short player-led route segment; caravan movement may use fade/reposition rather than slow multi-wagon escort AI.

At destination, account for everyone—including a person who chooses not to continue to the originally planned destination.

Core:
> recovery means keeping people alive and informed enough to decide where they go next.
