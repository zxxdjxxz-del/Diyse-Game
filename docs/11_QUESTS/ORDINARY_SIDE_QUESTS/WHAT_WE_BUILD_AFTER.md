## Premise / investigation
Mirena asks the party to inspect what the area now actually needs rather than defaulting to prewar restoration.

The authored investigation should gather current use/need from the existing area:
- movement/transport and public access;
- relief/recovery use;
- clinic/work/service needs where represented;
- what temporary arrangements have become genuinely valuable;
- which emergency structures are unsafe/inadequate for permanent civic life.

Residents/users do not all want the same future.

## Resolution
Mirena rejects both:
- forced restoration/dismantling to the old prewar state;
- sentimental preservation of emergency infrastructure exactly as it is.

She authorizes:
- formal survey;
- permanent civic conversion of the viable area;
- durable access/services rather than emergency-grade arrangements;
- public planning that recognizes how residents actually use the space now.

Compatible broader outcome:
- the area can develop toward recognized ordinary civic/ward function;
- the eventual neighborhood/ward name is **not chosen by Mirena**.

The people who live/use the place will decide its name later.
