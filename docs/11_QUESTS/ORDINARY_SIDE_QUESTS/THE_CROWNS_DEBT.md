## Current continuity
Mirena does not randomly stumble over the problem.

She is already:
> **reviewing old Crown orders, authorizations and administrative records**

because the main story has repeatedly shown that inherited paperwork can still matter.

She finds valid Ashford wartime requisition entries marked:
> **AUTHORIZED — UNPAID**

The occupation interrupted settlement of the claims.

## Playable scope
Three representative requisition claims are verified through:
- surviving Crown/military records;
- corroborating witnesses;
- physical evidence;
- reasonable valuation.

Current completion package includes:
- grain debt settled;
- serviceable wagon replacement;
- surviving tools returned and/or missing value compensated;
- outstanding-claims notice posted.

The notice signals that other valid claims may be reviewed administratively; it does not create an infinite procedural quest system.

## Principle
Officials are not default corrupt villains.
Claimants are not default scammers.

Mirena rejects:
> **no paperwork, no payment**

when war itself destroyed the paperwork.

Core:
> compensation is not charity; if the Crown used/destroyed civilian property in Yahtrea's defense, the Crown owes a debt.
