## Premise
Caelora has enough food, but an old emergency distribution formula allocates bread/grain according to household counts that no longer match the displaced population.

The failure is:
> a technically valid administrative model no longer matches reality.

## Area reuse
Reuse existing Chapter-3 Caelora:
- southern approach / supply traffic;
- market/public food activity;
- civic/administrative spaces.

No new city district.

## Objective flow
1. Review the provisioning discrepancy with Mirena.
2. Verify actual occupied beds/households in the affected temporary-housing population.
3. Verify current bakery throughput.
4. Verify civic storehouse reserves.
5. Compare the current physical situation against the stale allocation rule.
6. Mirena authors the correction:
   - temporary reserve increase;
   - rolling emergency headcount based on current occupancy/consumption rather than only permanent address.
7. Confirm the revised provisioning notice/ledger state.

No policy-choice minigame.
The player gathers/verifies; Mirena makes the governing decision.

## Character function
Mirena accepts criticism/evidence without royal defensiveness.
Her strength is competent attention, not performative charity.

## Revisit
- no timer;

## World-state payoff
- revised provisioning notice;
- corrected allotment records;
- smoother market/supply staging;
- shortened bread-line/background crowd state.

Arc principle:
> government must accurately see the people it governs.
