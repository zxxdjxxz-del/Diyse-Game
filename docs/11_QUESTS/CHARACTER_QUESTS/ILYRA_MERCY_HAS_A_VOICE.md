## Premise
An emergency safety rule in a reopened Blue Warden recovery sanctuary has degraded into suppressing testimony because silence is classified as safer.

Original principle:
- survivor may speak when ready;
- survivor may remain silent;
- testimony is preserved/released only with consent.

## Interactions

Different survivors may legitimately choose:
- preserve;
- release;
- destroy;
- not ready.

No single response is labeled the only correct form of healing.
