## Unlock / runtime
- after Chapter 7
- ~30 minutes
- parent hub: Cerythvale
- site: **Crestline Relay**
- local placement: west/southwest toward the populated interior

## Route
> Relay Approach → Evacuation Ring → Load Galleries → Command Core

## Premise
An old Crest-linked evacuation shelter/relay reactivates with a modern survey/recovery team trapped inside.

Its preservation logic evaluates:
- load;
- power;
- survivor capacity;
- route viability;
- acceptable losses.

## Character conflict
Cyanis tends to confuse:
> willingness to carry a burden

with:
> being the person who should always be sacrificed first.

## Exploration
Authored **Load Distribution** interactions:
- reroute Crest Load Nodes;
- visibly shift structural/system strain;
- rescue capacity improves as load is shared.

This is not:
- a hidden-number puzzle;
- a new universal load meter.

The trapped team remains alive.

## Combat
Reused ordinary identities:
- Command-Station Sentry
- Authority Lens
- Command Ring Drone

Boss:
> **Crest-Exhausted Warden**

- one HP bar;
- current name supersedes the older `Crest Load Warden`.

## Climax
The final core proposes permanently binding a Crest-compatible operator.

Cyanis initially volunteers, then rejects the premise that:
> "no one is expendable"

somehow excludes himself.

The party restores distributed pathways, rescues the trapped team, and gets Cyanis out without replacing him with another sacrificial victim.
