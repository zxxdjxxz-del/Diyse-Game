## General rule
Current eligible Character Quests, Side Quests and Hunts remain accessible after unlock while:
- their location remains reachable;
- their special prerequisites remain satisfied;
- the player has not crossed the final irreversible threshold.

## Current irreversible threshold

Starting Chapter 13 is not itself the cutoff.
