## 1. Pair thesis

Cyanis and Vaelira do not become copies of one another at Synthesis. Their final integration rewards using the already-earned Base and Subclass systems together:

- **Cyanis:** Crest Knight discipline learns to prime and receive payoff from Crest Arcanist Fields/Seals.
- **Vaelira:** Green Arcanist Imprints become direct structural inputs for Axiomblade techniques.

Neither effect creates a third class, second Trait, new gauge, extra action, or weapon requirement.

## 2. Cyanis Synthesis — Unified Crest

**Unified Crest** is Cyanis's permanent Synthesis passive after the universal Synthesis gate is met and the node is purchased.

### A. Base → Subclass bridge

**Once per round**, after Cyanis deliberately selects and resolves a **Crest Knight Ability**, if **Crest Attunement** is not already active, he gains Crest Attunement.

- This uses the existing Crest Attunement state; it does not create a second meter/state.
- It does not refresh or stack an already-active Crest Attunement.
- A later Crest Field or Crest Seal consumes Crest Attunement under its existing rules.
- Resolving the reaction portion of a previously armed Prepared counter is not a second qualifying selected Crest Knight Ability for this clause.

This allows Cyanis's Base Crest discipline to prepare his Crest Arcanist field/seal logic without requiring Arcane Lance every time.

### B. Subclass → Base bridge

When Cyanis **consumes Crest Attunement** with a legal Crest Field or Crest Seal, his next **damaging Crest Knight Ability** before the end of the following round gains **+15% Power**.

- One consumption arms one bonus.
- The bonus does not stack with itself.
- It is consumed by the next qualifying damaging Crest Knight Ability.
- It does not alter the ability's damage type, target rules, hit count, MP cost, or weapon legality.

### C. Weapon rule

Unified Crest never requires an Arcane Staff, Sword, Shield, Focus, or any other particular weapon. All referenced Abilities remain usable under the global no-Ability-weapon-requirement rule.

### Intent

Cyanis's loop becomes:

**Crest Knight discipline → Crest Attunement → Crest Field/Seal → empowered Crest Knight offense.**

The Synthesis therefore joins his protective/martial Crest identity to his late-game magical Crest control without activating both class Traits or creating a third kit.

## 3. Vaelira Synthesis — Unified Spectrum

**Unified Spectrum** is Vaelira's permanent Synthesis passive after the universal Synthesis gate is met and the node is purchased.

### A. Imprint → Axiomblade bridge

When Vaelira uses a **damaging Axiomblade Ability** against an enemy carrying one or more existing **Imprints**, she may choose one of those Imprint elements as the Ability's element.

- The chosen Imprint is **not consumed**.
- The Ability keeps its normal authored Martial/Arcane formula, Power, targeting, hit count, and other riders.
- If Prism Cycle already determines the Ability's element, Prism Cycle retains precedence; if that element matches an existing Imprint, the extension clause below may still apply.
- Formal Equivalence remains useful and is not replaced; Unified Spectrum simply gives Base-created Imprints an additional direct bridge into Axiomblade.

### B. Axiomblade → Base bridge

**Once per round**, when a damaging Axiomblade Ability hits using an element matching an existing Imprint on that target, extend that matching Imprint by **+1 round**, up to its authored maximum duration.

- The Imprint is not consumed.
- If multiple matching/eligible Imprints could qualify, the one whose element was used for the Ability is the one extended.
- The extension does not itself trigger a Composite Reaction or create a new Imprint.

### C. Weapon rule

Unified Spectrum never requires a Sword, Arcane Staff, Focus, Shield, or any other particular weapon. Axiomblade Abilities remain weapon-independent and use their approved Martial/Arcane expression rules regardless of loadout.

### Intent

Vaelira's loop becomes:

**Green Arcanist Imprint → Axiomblade elemental expression → maintain the Imprint for further Green Arcanist/Axiomblade play.**

The Synthesis rewards mastery of both halves of her kit without granting automatic Composite Reactions, extra actions, or a second active Trait.

## 4. Pair balance boundaries

- Neither Synthesis grants flat permanent stats.
- Neither grants extra MP, extra Card slots, extra equipment slots, or another ordinary action.
- Neither activates the partner's Base Trait.
- Neither removes the need to learn/equip legal equipment normally; it only prevents equipment choice from disabling Abilities.
- Cyanis's +15% Power clause is conditional on a Base → Subclass → Base sequence and is one-use.
- Vaelira's Imprint extension is capped at once per round and at the Imprint's authored maximum duration.
- No additional reciprocal-pair bonus is granted merely for fielding Cyanis and Vaelira together.
