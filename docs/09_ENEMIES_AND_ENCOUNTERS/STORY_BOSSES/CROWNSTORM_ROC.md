> **Perched Sovereign → Stormbound**

# STATE TRANSITION — STORMBOUND

At:
> **50% HP**

the Roc enters Stormbound.

Same HP bar.

On transition:
- no free attack;
- no healing;
- no Prime refresh;
- no extra action;
- Crown Perch becomes unavailable;
- action priority becomes more aggressive.

Stormbound also gains:
> **Speed +10% for the rest of the battle**

This modifies ordering only and never grants another action.
