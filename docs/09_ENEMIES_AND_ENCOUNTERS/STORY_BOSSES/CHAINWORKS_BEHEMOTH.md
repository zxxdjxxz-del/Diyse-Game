# Restraint Anchors

Exactly:
> **3 Restraint Anchors**

Each:

| HP | DEF | Spirit | EVA | SR | Direct-damage Power |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **240** | **72** | **62** | 0 | 10 | **N/A** |

Anchors:
- take no independent ordinary turn;
- deal no direct damage;
- cannot be repaired;
- remain destroyed;
- are removed as functional support once the Behemoth becomes Freed.

## Bound-state protection

Each intact Anchor grants:
> **+5 Total Defense**

Maximum:
> **+15 Total Defense**

Destroying an Anchor immediately removes its +5 contribution.

## Gate-hazard support

At least one intact Anchor is required for:
> **Bound-state Gate Crush**

If all Anchors are destroyed by one resolved action:
> Gate Crush immediately becomes unavailable.

## Early-release rule

The normal Bound → Freed transition occurs at:
> **55% HP**

But destroying:
> **2 or more Restraint Anchors**

causes the Behemoth to break free immediately after the current party action resolves.

No transition attack occurs.

## Breakout consequence

When the Behemoth becomes Freed, count Anchors that were still intact immediately before transition.

For the first **2 Freed rounds**, it gains:
> **Attack +5% per intact Anchor**

Maximum:
> **+15% Attack**

This means:
- aggressively destroying Anchors removes protection but releases the Behemoth earlier with a smaller breakout bonus;
- leaving Anchors intact keeps the Bound-state protection/hazard active longer but creates a harder natural breakout.

All Anchors cease functioning at the transition.

This is not a Break system.
