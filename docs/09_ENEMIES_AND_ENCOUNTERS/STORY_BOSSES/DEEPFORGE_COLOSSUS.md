## Preserved architecture

Deepforge Colossus remains one of Diyse's genuine fresh-body transformations:

> **Assembly Frame → Worldsmith Body**

Form I is a large maintenance/construction frame with exactly three targetable functional assemblies:
- Guard Press
- Repair Arm
- Command Loom

The player may destroy none, one, two, or all three before defeating Form I.

None is a mandatory HP gate.

Destroyed assemblies:
- remain destroyed;
- cannot be repaired;
- do not respawn;
- do not return during transformation.

Surviving functions carry into the Worldsmith Body.

There is no third form.

## Current fresh-body correction

Current global boss rule controls:
> **a genuine fresh-HP body refreshes Prime availability.**

Therefore the Worldsmith transformation:
- begins at fresh full HP;
- takes no Form-I damage spillover;
- refreshes Prime availability;
- does not restore party HP/MP;
- does not reset ordinary temporary-effect durations unless their own rule says so;
- pays no intermediate victory reward.

Historical Deepforge text that said Prime availability did not refresh is superseded.

# Forge Collapse

Forge Collapse is the Worldsmith's major visible Preparation.

Classification:
> **Protected Preparation**

It is not normally cancelable.

## Preparation round
- Worldsmith spends its selected action preparing;
- heavy support columns unlock above the battlefield;
- target area is visibly telegraphed;
- no direct damage occurs.

Power:
> **N/A on Preparation**

## Resolution
On the Worldsmith's next selected action:
- all conscious party members
- Physical / Neutral
- **300 Power per target**
- Base Hit **90**
- **25% Staggered per target**

If Command Loom survived:
> Base Hit becomes **100**.

After resolution:
> 3-round repetition lock.

Counterplay is:
- Defend;
- existing legal damage mitigation;
- dismantling Guard Press/Command Loom in Form I;
- healing/resource planning.

No Barrier mechanic is created.
