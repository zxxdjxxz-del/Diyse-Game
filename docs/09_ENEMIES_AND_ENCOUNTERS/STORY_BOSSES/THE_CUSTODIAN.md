**Chapter:** 11 — Crown Engine / Calder / Custodian / Truth

**Role:** mandatory Ancient administrative-domain encounter / truth gate  
**Status:** **WORKING PASS / RAW LINE RETAINED / POWER COMPLETE**

## Story boundary

The Custodian is not a human authoritarian antagonist.

It is:
- a surviving Ancient administrative intelligence/system;
- operating from incomplete but authentic late-Ancient records;
- enforcing an old access/containment mandate;
- capable of communication after the enforcement conflict ends.

Knowledge firewall:
> The Custodian believes the Ancient Last Weapon destroyed/neutralized the Entity.

It does **not** know that one Entity fragment survived.

Do not let this encounter reveal:
- the surviving fragment;
- the modern reconstitution chain;
- the final solution.

Those belong later.

## Battle outcome

At 0 HP:
- the party wins the enforcement conflict;
- the Custodian is **not destroyed as an information source**;
- enforcement authority yields;
- direct contact / historical explanation proceeds.

There is no protected HP floor.

This is an authored narrative survival/interface outcome, not a universal Mercy/Capture/Subdual rule.
