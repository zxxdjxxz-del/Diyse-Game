## Nonlethal protected floor

Seyrik has an encounter-specific protected disengagement threshold at:
> **20% Max HP = 800 HP**

If a party action would reduce Seyrik below 800 HP:
- damage is clamped at 800 HP;
- Seyrik's unresolved selected action for that round is canceled;
- no counterattack occurs;
- the battle ends immediately into the authored disengagement/recruitment sequence.

This is not:
- a universal Mercy command;
- a Capture system;
- a Subdual meter;
- a generic human-enemy HP floor.

It exists only for this authored encounter.
