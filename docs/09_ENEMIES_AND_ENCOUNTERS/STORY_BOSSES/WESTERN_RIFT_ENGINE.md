## Preserved architecture

Western Rift Engine remains:
> **one enemy / one continuous HP bar**

State A:
> **Engine Core**

State B:
> **Rift Incarnate**

The transition is a same-bar state change.

Therefore:
- no fresh HP;
- no damage reset;
- no intermediate reward;
- no free transition attack;
- no Prime availability refresh.

No `Spatial` damage element exists.

All direct damage in this kit uses current legal typing:
- Physical / Neutral;
- Magical / Colorless;
- Magical / Lightning.

Rift presentation does not create a fifth element.

# STATE TRANSITION — RIFT INCARNATE

At:
> **45% HP**

the Engine Core unfolds into the Rift Incarnate state.

Same HP bar.

Transition:
- no refill;
- no free attack;
- no extra ordinary action;
- no Prime refresh;
- any surviving Rift Echo remains present.

State modifiers for the remainder of battle:
- **Speed +10%**
- **Defense −10%**
- **Spirit −10%**

The Engine becomes faster and more exposed as its containment geometry fails.

Speed still never grants another ordinary action.

Core Recalibration becomes unavailable.
