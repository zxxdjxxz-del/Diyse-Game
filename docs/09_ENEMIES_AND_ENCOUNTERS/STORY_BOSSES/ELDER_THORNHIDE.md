This is not a normal kill-to-zero boss.

The encounter is authored to last:
> **exactly 4 rounds**

- Elder Thornhide remains alive;
- party damage should visibly matter but remain small relative to its combat capacity.

Round 4:
- ordinary command selection is replaced by the authored **LAST SENTINEL** prompt;
- Recovered Last Sentinel uses **Sentinel Impact — 340 Power**;
- Elder Thornhide retreats alive after the round resolves.

Because this encounter is a fixed four-round narrative tutorial for the first verified modern Prime manifestation:
> completionists do **not** finish 2–3 rounds faster here.

Their advantage is:
- lower incoming danger;
- less healing/resource use;
- easier survival through Rounds 1–3.

This is an explicit exception to the ordinary mandatory-vs-completionist duration rule.
