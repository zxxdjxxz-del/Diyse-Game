## Protected-resolution rule

HP represents:
> remaining unstable combat capacity.

At 0 HP:
- the researcher is **stabilized**, not killed;
- hostile actions cease;
- the researcher collapses alive;
- the current B08 post-encounter medical/stabilization handoff begins.

No universal Mercy, Capture, or Subdual system is created.

## Cleansing counterplay

Clearing Burn / Freeze / Stun / Staggered from party members uses the normal status-clear rules.

There is no hidden stabilization meter.

Status clearing:
- improves party action economy and survival;
- does not directly reduce Conduit HP;
- does not create bonus damage;
- does not require a particular character.

This preserves the authored cleansing/stabilization play without inventing a second combat resource.
