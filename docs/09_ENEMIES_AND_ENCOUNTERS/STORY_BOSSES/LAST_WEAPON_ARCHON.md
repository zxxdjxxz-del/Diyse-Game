# Major sequence rule

Once per battle after Weapon Protocol Unsealed begins, the Archon prioritizes one complete visible sequence across three of its selected actions:

1. **Unsealed Convergence**
2. **Compression Verdict**
3. **Weapon Discharge**

Rules:
- one ordinary Archon action per round;
- no bonus actions;
- if a legal target for a single-target step is lost, normal hostile retargeting applies;
- the sequence does not create a new damage school, gauge, or status;
- after the three steps resolve, the Archon returns to its ordinary State-B selection rules.

This sequence is instructional/thematic pressure, not a recreation of the ancient Last Weapon event.
