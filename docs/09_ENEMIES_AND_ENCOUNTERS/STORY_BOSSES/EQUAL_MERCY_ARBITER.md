# State A — Mercy Protocol

## Mercy Protocol action tax

At the beginning of each normal party round while Mercy Protocol is active:
> the Arbiter gains **Sanctuary Restraint**.

While Sanctuary Restraint is active:
> the Arbiter takes **50% less direct HP damage**.

Sanctuary Restraint ends for the rest of that round after the party completes one qualifying **care/restraint action**.

Qualifying actions:
- **Defend**
- an Ability, Card, or Item that deals **no direct enemy HP damage**

Examples include:
- healing;
- status clear;
- revival;
- legal buffs;
- other support-only effects.

Not qualifying:
- Attack;
- any Ability/Card that directly damages an enemy;
- indirect passive/reaction effects by themselves.

Rules:
- the qualifying action resolves normally;
- no extra command is added;
- no MP refund or cost increase is created;
- if the party chooses not to pay the action tax, it may still damage the boss through the 50% reduction;
- Sanctuary Restraint automatically expires at end of round and reappears at the beginning of the next party round while State A remains active.

This is:
> **an encounter-specific damage-reduction state, not Barrier.**

# Transition — Open Sanctuary

At:
> **45% HP**

Mercy Protocol ends and Open Sanctuary begins.

Transition:
- Sanctuary Restraint is removed immediately;
- it does not return;
- no HP refill;
- no free attack;
- no Prime refresh.

For the remainder of battle:
- Arbiter gains **Speed +10%**;
- Arbiter receives **Defense −10% / Spirit −10%**.

The late fight therefore becomes:
> faster and more dangerous, but more open to direct resolution.

Speed grants no additional action.

Temper Harm becomes unavailable.
