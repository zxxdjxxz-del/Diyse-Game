# Continuity Collapse

Below:
> **60% Form-II HP**

the Living Anchor may begin a major Clamp-linked Preparation if at least one Clamp remains.

## Preparation
Classification:
> **Interruptible Preparation**

The Living Anchor:
- spends its selected action preparing;
- designates one intact Living Anchor Clamp as the loaded Clamp;
- visibly routes Crown Engine pressure through that Clamp;
- deals no direct damage during Preparation.

Power:
> **N/A during Preparation**

## Counterplay
Before the Living Anchor's next selected action:
> destroy the loaded Clamp.

If the loaded Clamp reaches 0 HP:
- Continuity Collapse is canceled;
- the Living Anchor loses that prepared resolution;
- the Clamp remains destroyed.

## Resolution
If the loaded Clamp survives:
- all conscious party members
- Hybrid / Neutral
- **50% ATK / 50% MAG**
- **345 Power per target**
- Base Hit **95**
- no harmful-status rider

After successful resolution:
> the loaded Clamp burns out and is destroyed automatically.

Therefore:
- maximum successful Continuity Collapse resolutions per battle = **2**
- no infinite Preparation loop
- no respawn

After each resolved or canceled attempt:
> 2-round repetition lock before another intact Clamp can be loaded.

This is not a Break meter or Barrier mechanic.
