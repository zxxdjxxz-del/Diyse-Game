# Deepforge Colossus — Functional Assemblies

**Encounter:** Assembly Frame → Worldsmith Body  
**Status:** **POWER COMPLETE**

Exactly three Form-I assemblies:
- Guard Press
- Repair Arm
- Command Loom

They are targetable support objects, not independent enemy turns.

| Assembly | HP | DEF | Spirit | EVA | SR | Direct-damage Power |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Guard Press | **320** | 62 | 58 | 0 | 10 | **N/A** |
| Repair Arm | **300** | 58 | 60 | 0 | 10 | **N/A** |
| Command Loom | **280** | 56 | 62 | 0 | 10 | **N/A** |

## Guard Press
Enables Assembly Frame `Reinforce Chassis`:
> +15 Total Defense through end of following round.

If it survives Form I, Worldsmith receives `Reinforced Load` with the same defensive magnitude.

No Barrier is created.

## Repair Arm
Enables Assembly Frame `Field Repair`:
> 220 HP, maximum 2 successful Form-I uses.

If it survives Form I, Worldsmith receives `Self-Stabilize`:
> 250 HP, maximum 2 successful Form-II uses.

It cannot restore a destroyed assembly, recreate an inherited function, resurrect anything, or exceed Max HP.

## Command Loom
While alive in Form I:
> Assembly Frame +10 Base Hit on damaging selected actions.

If inherited by Worldsmith:
- +10 Base Hit on damaging selected actions;
- Forge Collapse Base Hit 90 → 100.

No natural Accuracy stat is created.

## Persistence
Destroyed means destroyed:
- no Repair Arm restoration;
- no Repair Frame restoration;
- no transformation respawn;
- no hidden script.

All three support bodies:
> **Power: N/A — no independent direct damage.**
