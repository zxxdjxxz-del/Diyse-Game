# Diyse — Global Regional Hunt Structure Lock

**Status:** ACTIVE GLOBAL REGIONAL-HUNT AUTHORITY  
**Applies to:** **all Regional Hunts, current and future**, unless a later explicit Hunt-specific story exception is approved.

## Core rule
Regional Hunts are primarily **optional exploration + combat + reward content**, not miniature story quests.

The default Regional Hunt structure is:
> **chapter-cleanup unlock / access notice → clearly established entrance → additional optional area / branch → Hunt boss → rewards**

A Regional Hunt does not need a separate story arc merely because it is optional content.

---

## Required story / access layer
For every Regional Hunt, story authority only needs to establish:
1. **where the Hunt entrance / return point is**;
2. **when the route becomes available**;
3. during the appropriate chapter cleanup window, **how the player learns that it is now open / usable**;
4. enough player-facing direction that the player knows where to return;
5. that entering the route leads into optional Hunt content rather than mandatory story progression.

The entrance may be:
- seen earlier while inaccessible;
- discovered during cleanup;
- opened by changed world state;
- reported by an NPC;
- marked by a route / map update;
- or surfaced through another simple in-world or interface signal.

Do not add an elaborate unlocking subplot unless that specific Hunt is explicitly approved as a story exception.

---

## Dialogue rule
Regional Hunts should use **minimal dialogue by default**.

The cleanup unlock may be communicated through:
- a short natural NPC update;
- a cleanup interaction;
- a Hunt / quest notification;
- a map or route marker;
- environmental state change;
- or a combination of these.

Inside the Hunt area:
- dialogue may be sparse or absent;
- brief natural party observations are allowed when useful;
- no mandatory long briefing is required;
- no boss monologue is required;
- no post-boss debrief is required.

Do not route Regional Hunts through the full story-dialogue production pipeline unless a particular Hunt genuinely contains authored character / story material.

---

## Area-design rule
Each Regional Hunt should still feel like **real optional content**, not simply a boss immediately behind a newly opened door.

The optional Hunt area / branch may include, as appropriate:
- traversal;
- side paths;
- treasure;
- ordinary encounters;
- environmental hazards;
- simple traversal or environmental mechanics;
- shortcuts;
- visual / atmospheric identity;
- a boss approach / arena.

Exact area size and complexity remain under map / area-design authority.

A Hunt can be short if that fits the location, but the default expectation is:
> **an additional explorable space leading naturally to the boss.**

---

## Boss rule
The Hunt boss is the primary authored payoff of the content.

Boss combat authority owns:
- stats;
- Power values;
- actions;
- status application;
- resistances / immunities;
- form structure;
- encounter-specific combat behavior;
- difficulty validation.

Story authority should not duplicate those mechanics.

---

## Reward rule
Defeating a Regional Hunt boss must provide **worthwhile optional rewards**.

Exact rewards remain under item / equipment / reward-placement authority and should not be prematurely locked before the project's reward-sequencing work is ready.

Depending on later reward authority, Hunt rewards may include appropriate combinations of:
- equipment;
- accessories;
- consumables;
- materials;
- currency;
- treasure-cache access;
- other approved progression rewards.

The structural requirement is simply:
> **optional exploration and a stronger optional boss should feel materially worthwhile to complete.**

---

## Main-story firewall
Regional Hunts are optional.

Therefore:
> **mandatory main-story comprehension must never depend on completing a Regional Hunt.**

Regional Hunts may contain:
- environmental flavor;
- local history;
- visual worldbuilding;
- nonessential context;
- optional foreshadowing that does not become required knowledge.

They should not, by default, contain:
- a mandatory identity reveal;
- the only explanation of a main-story mystery;
- a required character recruitment;
- a required chapter transition;
- an essential explanation of the central plot;
- information the mandatory story later assumes every player knows.

If a future Regional Hunt is intentionally designed to carry substantial story material, that must be treated as an **explicit exception** and authored separately.

---

## Chapter-cleanup integration rule
The preferred timing is:
1. mandatory chapter story reaches its cleanup state;
2. player receives the Hunt access / entrance update;
3. Hunt becomes available alongside other cleanup activities;
4. player may complete it or skip it;
5. chapter advancement remains separately player-controlled where the chapter structure allows.

A Regional Hunt should not normally interrupt the mandatory chapter spine before cleanup unless a specific Hunt's authority explicitly says otherwise.

---

## Current concrete applications
The existing early-game access lock:
`docs/02_STORY/CHAPTERS/REGIONAL_HUNTS_CH1_CH3_CLEANUP_ACCESS_LOCK.md`

- **Regional Hunt #1 — Cistern Devourer**;
- **Regional Hunt #2 — Transfer Executioner**;
- **Regional Hunt #3 — Archive Judgment Engine**.

Those three are examples of this global rule, not special exceptions to it.

Future Regional Hunts should inherit this global structure automatically unless explicitly revised.

---

## Global lock summary
For **all current and future Regional Hunts**, default to:

> **Establish the entrance. During the appropriate chapter cleanup, tell / show the player that the route is now open and where it is. Let them explore the additional area, defeat the Hunt boss, and collect worthwhile rewards. Keep story and dialogue minimal unless a specific Hunt is deliberately authored as an exception.**
