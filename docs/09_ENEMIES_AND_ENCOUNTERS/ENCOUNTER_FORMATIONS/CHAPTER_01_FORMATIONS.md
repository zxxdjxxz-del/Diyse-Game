# Diyse — Chapter 1 Formation Composition

**Current placement authority:** explicit Chapter-1 encounter correction, 2026-09-22.  
**Rule:** Briar Passage uses a compact natural-threat roster with formation variety; all Chapter-1 Black Host and construct ordinary encounters are confined to Hollow Watch.

## Briar Passage — first traversal

| Formation | Composition | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| **Trail Pack** | 2 Greenhollow Stalkers + 1 Thornvine Creeper | 30% |
| **Briar Mix** | 1 Briar Boar + 1 Greenhollow Stalker + 1 Thornvine Creeper | 45% |
| **Thorn Pressure** | 1 Greenhollow Stalker + 2 Thornvine Creepers | 25% |

This first traversal establishes the local ecology without exhausting its formation space.

## Hollow Watch — occupied fort / Black Host

| Formation | Composition | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| **Occupation Patrol** | 2 Black Host Raiders + 1 Black Host Crossbowman | 35% |
| **Shield Line** | 1 Black Host Raider + 1 Black Host Crossbowman + 1 Ruin Shieldbearer | 40% |
| **Crossbow Screen** | 2 Black Host Crossbowmen + 1 Ruin Shieldbearer | 25% |

No Black Host ordinary formation is used in Brackenwall or Briar Passage during Chapter 1.

## Hollow Watch — lower excavation / ancient defenses

| Formation | Composition | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| **Sentry Pair** | 2 Hollow Watch Sentries | 30% |
| **Watch Line** | 2 Hollow Watch Sentries + 1 Hollow Watch Ballista | 45% |
| **Crossfire Post** | 1 Hollow Watch Sentry + 2 Hollow Watch Ballistae | 25% |

The lower-section pool deliberately stays compact: the Sentry/Ballista relationship provides the mechanical variety rather than additional construct identities.

## Southern Briar Passage

| Formation | Composition | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| **Predator Trail** | 2 Greenhollow Stalkers + 1 Briar Boar | 30% |
| **Briar Pressure** | 1 Briar Boar + 1 Greenhollow Stalker + 2 Thornvine Creepers | 45% |
| **Heavy Woods** | 2 Briar Boars + 1 Greenhollow Stalker + 1 Thornvine Creeper | 25% |

Southern Briar reuses the same three natural-threat identities in heavier and more predator-forward combinations. The roster does not need additional Chapter-1 wildlife solely to create encounter variety.
