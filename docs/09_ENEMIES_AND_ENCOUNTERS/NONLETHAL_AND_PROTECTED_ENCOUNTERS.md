**Migration baseline:** `Diyse_CURRENT_WORKING_TRACKER_CONSOLIDATED_2026-08-27_v85.md`

Diyse does not use a universal:
- Subdual gauge;
- Capture meter;
- nonlethal weapon mode;
- Mercy command.

## Authored nonlethal encounter
When an encounter is explicitly classified **NONLETHAL / PROTECTED**, its HP represents:
- remaining combat capacity;
- ability/willingness to continue;
- safe restraint/stabilization threshold;

not necessarily biological death.

Reaching the authored resolution threshold produces the specified:
- subdual;
- withdrawal;
- stabilization;
- rescue;
- capture;
- disconnect;
- surrender;

rather than inventing a lethal outcome.

## Victim/lawful boundary
Lawful personnel, coerced people, prisoners, patients, harmed researchers, compelled workers, and other victim-class humans are not ordinary farmable loot bodies merely because combat occurs.

## Bespoke capture/objective encounters
A bespoke encounter may override ordinary 0-HP resolution with visible objective rules, such as:
- Varkesh Retreat Beacons;
- Calder/Crown Engine support devices;
- Continuity/Reconciliation requirements;
- Last Sentinel's authored Elder Briarhide nonlethal override.

No universal capture mechanic is inferred from these encounters.
