# Diyse — Nonlethal and Protected Encounter Rules

**Status:** ACTIVE OUTCOME / STORY-RESOLUTION AUTHORITY — EXACT COMBAT KITS REBUILD PENDING

An authored nonlethal/protected encounter uses its story-defined defeat/resolution outcome instead of assuming that 0 HP means death.

This is encounter-specific. It does not create a universal Mercy/Subdual command.

## Story-first resolution rule

For every protected/nonlethal encounter:
- narrative outcome is owned by the current story/encounter authority;
- no enemy kit may silently convert the outcome into a lethal one;
- no universal protected HP floor or generic capture mechanic is inferred unless that encounter explicitly defines it.

## Rebuild boundary

Exact combat duration, HP floors, thresholds, actions, status packages, Potencies, timing, and objective mechanics are open for the enemy-kit redesign unless separately re-authored under the current battle system.
