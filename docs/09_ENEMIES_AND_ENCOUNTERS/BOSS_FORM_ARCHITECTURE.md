# Diyse — Boss Form Architecture

**Status:** ACTIVE FORM-STRUCTURE AUTHORITY / COMBAT NUMBERS REVALIDATION PENDING

## Same-bar state change

A same-bar state change:

- keeps the existing HP body;
- does not refill HP;
- does not restore spent Prime identities;
- does not create a free TURN or action unless explicitly authored;
- remains part of the same battle continuity.

Current same-bar identities include:

- Furnace Tyrant — heat escalation
- Crownstorm Roc — Perched Sovereign → Stormbound
- Chainworks Behemoth — Bound → Freed
- Warden of the Nameless / Revision Arbiter — Open Revision
- Western Rift Engine — Engine Core → Rift Incarnate
- Equal Mercy Arbiter — Open Sanctuary
- Registry Warden — Closed Registry → Open Registry
- The Custodian — Administrative Closure → Open Reconciliation
- Last Weapon Archon — Archive Authority → Weapon Protocol Unsealed
- Concordance Guardian — Six Faces → Open Concordance
- Worldscar Leviathan — Prismatic Confluence
- The Unfinished World — WORLDFRAME → WORLDHEART EXPOSED → FINAL CONSTRUCTION

## Genuine fresh body

A genuine fresh-body transition:

- starts a separately authored HP body;
- does not transfer excess damage by default;
- does not restore party HP/MP;
- does not restore spent Prime identities;
- does not imply an additional later form.

Current fresh-body identities include:

- Regulation Crucible → The Seventh Reaction
- Deepforge Colossus — Assembly Frame → Worldsmith Body
- Matron Zevraya → Perfected War Mother
- Marshal Varkesh → Rift Conqueror
- Commander Rhazek → Bastion Devourer
- Calder → Crown-Bound Living Anchor
- Emperor Vaelkor → Sovereign Panoply Unbound
- Reconstituted Entity → The Last Command
- Crownless Siege Marshal → Crownless War Engine
- Sealed Throne → Walking Throne

## Persistent structural effects

An encounter may explicitly carry destroyed supports/functions or other authored state across a fresh-body transition. Such carryover must be named by the encounter rather than inferred globally.

## Revalidation boundary

Exact thresholds, HP values, stat modifiers, supports, action kits, status packages, timing, and transition-trigger mechanics are owned by each encounter and remain subject to the current enemy/balance rebuild.

The final boss remains exactly:

> **Reconstituted Entity → The Last Command**

At The Last Command reaching 0 HP, combat ends and Final Severance begins; there is no third combat body.
