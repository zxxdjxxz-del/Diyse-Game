## Same-bar state change
Behavior/state changes while retaining the same HP bar:
- no fresh HP;
- no Prime restoration.

Examples:
- Hollow Watch Castellan Fortress → Walking
- Furnace Tyrant High-Heat escalation
- Crownstorm Roc Perched Sovereign → Stormbound
- Chainworks Behemoth Bound → Freed
- Revision Arbiter Open Revision
- Western Rift Engine Engine Core → Rift Incarnate
- Equal Mercy Arbiter Open Sanctuary
- Custodian reconciliation states
- Concordance Guardian Six Faces → Open Concordance
- Worldscar Leviathan Prismatic Confluence
- The Unfinished World WORLDFRAME → WORLDHEART EXPOSED → FINAL CONSTRUCTION

## Genuine fresh body
A separately authored physical/narrative body with fresh HP:
- starts a new HP body;
- does **not** restore spent Prime identities;
- does not imply a third form.

Fresh-body replacement and Prime restoration are separate systems. Only an explicitly authored valid rest/restoration effect can restore a spent Prime.

Current important examples:
- Regulation Crucible → The Seventh Reaction
- Deepforge Colossus Assembly Frame → Worldsmith Body
- Matron Zevraya → Perfected War Mother
- Marshal Varkesh → Rift Conqueror in Chapter 8
- Rhazek → Bastion Devourer in Chapter 9
- Calder → Crown-Bound Living Anchor
- Vaelkor → Sovereign Panoply Unbound
- Reconstituted Entity → The Last Command
- Crownless Siege Marshal → Crownless War Engine
- Sealed Throne → Walking Throne

## No invented component HP
A targetable support object/assembly can exist without this migration inventing an unsupported HP pool.

If exact component HP is not recovered, leave it unresolved rather than deriving it from boss HP.
