### Targeting Signal
> **Power: N/A — no direct damage**

Classification:
> **encounter-specific Interruptible Preparation**

The Sentry visibly designates one party member for a linked Hollow Watch Ballista.

Rules:
- Targeting Signal consumes the Sentry's selected action.
- The preparation lasts through the remainder of that round.
- Any successful direct-damage hit against the preparing Sentry cancels the signal.
- Defeating the Sentry cancels it.
- If preparation survives to the next beginning-round, the linked Ballista may lock **Marked Heavy Bolt** onto that target.
- The completed signal is consumed by that Ballista shot.
- A Sentry cannot maintain more than one signal.

This is a formation relationship, not a harmful status or new universal command.
