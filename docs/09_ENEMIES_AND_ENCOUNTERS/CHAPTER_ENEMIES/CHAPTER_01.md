## Enemy identity notes

### Thicket Stalker
Fast natural predator identity retained.

### Vine Creeper
Creeping multi-headed vine mass identity retained.

### Bullhog
Current identity replaces **Briar Boar**.
Visual identity: very large muscular warthog with large bull horns; heavy hog-like middle/face rather than a conventional boar silhouette.

### Needlewing
Small flying dragonfly/scorpion cross; more scorpion-like; long pointed stinger; red/yellow body; bright green eyes.

### Burrowclaw
Current identity replaces **Rootmaw**.
Broad combat-role placeholder only: mobile / ambush-oriented physical threat.
Exact stats/actions are intentionally deferred.

### Barkling
Current identity replaces **Rubbleback**.
Broad combat-role placeholder only: sturdier frontline / bruiser threat.
Exact stats/actions are intentionally deferred.

### Construct
Current basic Hollow Watch ancient-defense identity.
Replaces the old Chapter-1 Watch Sentry / Watch Ballista / Watch Captain Frame spread.

### Shield Construct
Defensive/heavier Construct variant.
Fixed authored stronger fight, not a miniboss.

### Thornhide
Current Chapter-1 final boss.
- exceptionally large native beast;
- werebear-like body;
- dense porcupine-like quills;
- red eyes are natural;
- no Black Host equipment or involvement;
- no corruption;
- no machinery;
- no purification/nonlethal objective;
- normal lethal boss victory.
