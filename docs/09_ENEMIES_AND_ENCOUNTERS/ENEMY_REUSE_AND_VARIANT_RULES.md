## Area-first ecology
Enemy identities are assigned to areas/ecologies first, not spread uniformly to satisfy a chapter-wide species quota.

There is:
- no minimum species quota per area;
- no requirement for one new enemy in every content beat;
- no requirement for one Elite per numbered chapter.

## Carryover
Earlier enemies may return later when:
- ecology fits;
- faction presence fits;
- story context fits;
- their existing role remains legible.

Prefer meaningful carryover over a renamed duplicate created only to look new.

## Stronger variants
A stronger returning form needs a meaningful difference such as:
- equipment;
- targeting;
- formation role;
- defense;
- support behavior;
- status pressure;
- action timing;
- visible presentation.

HP/stat inflation alone is not enough to justify a new identity.

## Asset reuse
Shared:
- rig;
- skeleton;
- animation;
- VFX;
- shader/material;
- silhouette foundation

does not automatically mean same lore identity.

Likewise distinct lore identities may share production foundations.

## Chapter 10 special reuse
Current Chapter 10 deliberately requires **0 new ordinary enemy identities**.
It reuses eight established enemies across:
- eastern forest;
- Eastern Wayfinder;
- Old Crown excavation / Buried Registry.

Do not invent `Eastern Judgment Frame`, `Greater Authority Lens`, `Registry Ring Drone`, or similar renamed clones merely to hide reuse.

## Old 160-count note
Audit93 closed a **160-combat-identity production basis** under the pre-insertion chapter structure, excluding some support-only identities and Major Hunts.

This migration preserves that as production provenance but does not use it as a simplistic runtime count. Current chapter reindex, reused Chapter-10 identities, support objects, and Major Hunts are tracked by role instead.
