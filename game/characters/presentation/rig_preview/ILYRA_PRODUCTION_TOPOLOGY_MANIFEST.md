# Ilyra Stage 7 — Production-Topology Transition

Stage 7 is the first topology-oriented bridge from rig proof toward a production character. It keeps the exact 65-bone UAL core but replaces the Stage 6 collection of small proof surfaces with broader deformation-aware character surfaces.

## New weighted surfaces
- continuous upper-chest-to-hip Warden shell
- shoulder transition weights into `clavicle_l/r` and lightly into `upperarm_l/r`
- lower shell weights from pelvis toward the matching thigh
- fitted upper-arm sleeves with clavicle → upper arm → lower arm transition
- continuous front/side hip drape
- long pelvis/thigh-weighted front tabard
- broad back-hair mass across Head → neck → upper/mid spine
- shoulder-aware cape whose outer top edge blends into the matching clavicle

Wardrod, shield, light bracers, greaves, clasp, and other hard pieces remain rigid intentionally.

## Stress sequence
T-pose → Idle → Walk → Jog → Crouch → Climb → Roll → Shield Dash → Warden Cast → Knockback.

## Controls
- `SPACE` next animation
- `P` pause/resume
- `V` character / UAL body
- `L` Stage 7 topology / Stage 6 comparison
- `K` Stage 6 multi-bone / Stage 5 rigid comparison when Stage 7 is off
- `B` key deformation-bone markers
- `Q / E` rotate
- `T` automatic turntable
- `R` restart
- `ESC` quit

## Topology budget
The Stage 7 comparison geometry is intentionally lightweight rather than final sculpt topology. The standalone proof uses about **533 generated soft-surface vertices / 834 triangles**, excluding the underlying UAL-skinned body and retained hard/detail geometry.

## Authority boundary
This is not Ilyra's final production model or appearance canon. Her approved cleaned B00 remains authoritative for exact face, vivid jade eyes, mature/confident expression, windswept blonde hair, slightly athletic natural-waist proportions, fitted pale-blue/white Warden clothing, restrained ornament, cape, utility gear, Wardrod, shield, colors and final silhouette.

Stage 7 validates topology layout and deformation ownership. Exact sculpting, UVs, textures, facial topology, final hair geometry, cloth thickness and production secondary-motion systems remain later work.
